# Solar-Punk Principles

> *Solar-punk is not an aesthetic. It is a commitment — to regeneration, sovereignty, and a future worth inheriting.*

---

## The Core Principles

### 1. Regeneration Over Extraction
We build systems that give back more than they take. Soil, energy, water, attention, money — all managed regeneratively.

### 2. Sovereignty First
We own our data, our land, our energy, our education, and our food supply. Dependency on centralized systems is minimized by design.

### 3. Technology as Tool, Not Master
AI, robotics, and automation serve human flourishing. When they don't, we remove them.

### 4. Decentralization by Default
Where possible, infrastructure is local, distributed, and resilient. The grid fails — our life doesn't.

### 5. Abundance Mindset
Solar-punk rejects scarcity thinking. The sun gives freely. Water cycles. Land produces. We design for abundance.

### 6. Aesthetic Integrity
The way we build should be beautiful. Homes, tools, systems — all designed with care for the human experience.

### 7. Open Knowledge
What we learn, we share. The Codex is designed to become a public resource for sovereign families everywhere.

### 8. Generational Stewardship
We are borrowing this world from our children's children. Every decision asks: *will this serve 2076?*
