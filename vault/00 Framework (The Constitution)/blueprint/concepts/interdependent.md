# Interdependent

## Concept
A design principle and possible brand/framework name. Systems — and people — that are stronger *because* of their connections, not despite them.

## Tech Context
- **Distributed systems**: services that cannot function in isolation; failure/growth in one ripples to others
- **Dependency graphs**: explicit, mapped relationships between components (circular = bad, explicit = healthy)
- **Complex adaptive systems**: nodes that co-evolve; emergent behavior arises from relationships, not individual parts
- **Mesh networks / Web of Trust**: nodes gain capability FROM their connections

## The Key Distinction
- **Dependent** = relies on, weaker without
- **Independent** = self-sufficient, isolated
- **Interdependent** = gains strength FROM the relationship; the connection itself is the asset

## OneMind OS Angle
The entire OneMind architecture IS interdependent by design:
- SO/HP/LE/GE are not isolated pillars — they feed each other
- Agents, NATS, Zeus, and the vault form an interdependent mesh
- The CODE× loop only works because each step depends on and amplifies the others
