## What It Does

Life management app combining habits, todos, planner, timeline, and personal metrics tracking. Positions itself as "The Life OS" — the same language OneMind uses.

**Tagline:** "Chaotic life? Tired of making promises you don't keep? Too many apps and subscriptions? It's time to simplify everything."

## Weaknesses ❌

- **Minimal AI** — not AI-native, no agents
- **No PKM** — habits/tasks only, not knowledge management
- **Cloud-only** — no self-hosting
- **No automation** — manual workflows only
- **Limited integrations** — small ecosystem
- **No IoT** — digital only
- **No agent autonomy** — passive tracking tool
- **Younger product** — less mature than established players

## Competitive Position

Benji targets the **mass-market "life OS" space** — people who want one app for all life management but don't need technical depth or AI.

**Key differentiator from OneMind:**

- Benji = consumer-friendly, habit-focused, simple
- OneMind = technical, AI-native, expansive

**Relationship to OneMind:**

- Benji proves market demand for "Life OS" products
- Benji shows the importance of cross-platform mobile apps
- OneMind goes deeper (AI, agents, PKM) but is less accessible

**Choose Benji if:** You want simple habit/life tracking without technical setup
**Choose OneMind if:** You want AI agents, knowledge management, and self-hosting

---

## What OneMind Can Learn From Benji

1. **Cross-platform apps** — Benji's native apps on every platform is compelling
2. **Timeline view** — retrospective on personal change is powerful
3. **Family sharing** — making life OS collaborative
4. **Community features** — accountability beyond solo use
5. **Simple pricing** — $8-10/mo is accessible
