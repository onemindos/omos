## Competitive Position

Josh.ai occupies the **ultra-luxury smart home** niche. It's not a life OS competitor — it's a specialized voice control layer for high-end homes.

**Relationship to OneMind:**

- Josh.ai and Home Assistant (which OneMind uses) compete in smart home control
- Josh.ai targets luxury/professional install; Home Assistant targets DIY/technical users
- OneMind provides the brain/coordination layer; Josh.ai provides premium voice UX

**If you have $10K+ for home automation:** Josh.ai offers unmatched polish and integration
**If you want a DIY life OS:** OneMind + Home Assistant is more flexible and free
