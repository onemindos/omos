Comprehensive Guide to Red Hat Summit Topics

  1. AI/ML (Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning): Technologies that enable machines to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions with minimal human intervention.
  1. App, Data, and Process Integration: Combining different applications, databases, and business processes to ensure they work together seamlessly.
  1. Application Development: The process of creating a computer program or a set of programs to perform different tasks that a business requires.
  1. AWS (Amazon Web Services): A comprehensive cloud platform by Amazon that provides a variety of services such as computing power, database storage, and content delivery services.
  1. Business Automation: The use of technology to automate complex business processes to increase efficiency and reduce costs.
  1. Compliance: Ensuring that a company's practices are in accordance with established laws, regulations, guidelines, and specifications.
  1. Data Management & Analytics: The practices of collecting, keeping, and using data securely, efficiently, and cost-effectively, along with analyzing data to extract insights.
  1. DevOps: A set of practices that combines software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops) to shorten the systems development life cycle and provide continuous delivery.
  1. Edge: Refers to computing that’s done at or near the source of data generation, instead of relying on the cloud at one of a dozen data centers to do all the work.
  1. Infrastructure: The organizational structures needed for the operation of a society or enterprise, or the services and facilities necessary for an economy to function.
  1. IT Automation: The use of software to create repeatable instructions and processes to replace or reduce human interaction with IT systems.
  1. IT Management: The discipline whereby all of the information technology resources of a firm are managed in accordance with its needs and priorities.
  1. IT Strategy: The comprehensive plan that outlines how technology should be used to meet IT and business goals.
  1. Microservices: A style of software architecture that structures an application as a collection of loosely coupled services.
  1. Multicloud: The use of multiple cloud computing and storage services in a single network architecture.
  1. Open Organization: An organization that is open to the ideas of their employees, partners, and customers and is transparent in its operations.
  1. Open Source Communities: Groups of individuals who collaborate on developing and supporting open-source software.
  1. SAP: A multinational corporation that makes enterprise software to manage business operations and customer relations.
  1. Software-defined Storage: An approach to data storage in which the programming that controls storage-related tasks is decoupled from the physical storage hardware.
  1. Analytics: The systematic computational analysis of data or statistics, often used for the discovery, interpretation, and communication of meaningful patterns in data.
  1. Application Delivery: The process of making a software application available to end users.
  1. Application Platforms: The underlying hardware or software for a system that acts as a foundation for applications.
  1. Azure: A cloud computing service created by Microsoft for building, testing, deploying, and managing applications and services through Microsoft-managed data centers.
  1. CI/CD (Continuous Integration/Continuous Delivery): CI/CD are methods to frequently deliver apps to customers by introducing automation into the stages of app development.
  1. Containers: Lightweight, executable units of software in which application code is packaged, along with its libraries and dependencies, in common ways so that it can be run anywhere, whether it be on desktop, traditional IT, or the cloud.
  1. Deployment: The process through which applications, software updates, and patches are delivered and installed on a computer system or server.
  1. Diversity & Inclusion: Involves creating an environment that values and utilizes the contributions of individuals with diverse backgrounds, experiences, and ideas.
  1. Hybrid Cloud: A cloud computing environment that uses a mix of on-premises, private cloud, and third-party, public cloud services with orchestration between the platforms.
  1. Internet of Things (IoT): A network of physical objects that are embedded with sensors, software, and other technologies for the purpose of connecting and exchanging data with other devices and systems over the internet.
  1. IT Culture: The business culture that shapes and influences the development and implementation of technology within an organization.
  1. IT Processes: The series of actions or steps taken in order to achieve a particular end in information technology environments.
  1. Kubernetes: An open-source system for automating deployment, scaling, and management of containerized applications.
  1. Migration: The process of moving data, applications, or other business elements to a cloud computing environment.
  1. Networking/NFV (Network Functions Virtualization): Networking involves connecting computers and other electronic devices to share resources. NFV is an initiative to virtualize the network services traditionally run on proprietary, dedicated hardware.
  1. Open Source**: Software for which the original source code is made freely available and may be redistributed and modified.
  1. Operator: Refers to a person or technical staff managing servers or other equipment.
  1. Security: The practice of protecting systems, networks, and programs from digital attacks.
  1. Virtualization: The creation of a virtual version of something, such as a server, a desktop, a storage device, network resources, or an operating system where the framework divides the resource into one or more execution environments.
