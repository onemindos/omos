# Udemy

> World's largest online course marketplace. Buy courses once, access forever. Best for practical skills — coding, design, business, marketing.

---

## Overview

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **URL** | https://www.udemy.com |
| **My Courses** | https://www.udemy.com/home/my-courses |
| **Focus** | Tech (coding, data, AI), business, design, personal development |
| **Format** | Video-based, self-paced, projects, Q&A |
| **Cost** | ~$10-20/course on sale (sales run constantly); Udemy Business subscription |

## Stack Use (One Mind)

- **Practical skills** — hands-on coding, tools, and frameworks
- Buy once, reference forever — good for courses with large codebases or projects
- Complements DeepLearning.AI (which is theory-heavy) with more hands-on depth
- Best purchased on sale (never pay full price)
