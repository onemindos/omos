## Programming Principles

### Hypertrophy
- 8–12 rep range, 3–4 sets
- Progressive overload each session
- 60–90 sec rest between sets

### Strength
- 1–5 rep range, 4–6 sets
- 3–5 min rest between heavy sets
- Periodization cycles (4–6 weeks)

### Functional / Combat Conditioning
- Explosive movements: power cleans, snatches, med ball
- Carries: farmer walk, sandbag, atlas stone
- Anti-rotation: pallof press, plank variations
