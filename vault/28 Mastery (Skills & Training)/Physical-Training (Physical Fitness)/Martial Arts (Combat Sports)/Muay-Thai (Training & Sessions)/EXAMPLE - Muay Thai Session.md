## Technical Work

### Technique 1: Lead Leg Teep
**Reps/Rounds:** 3 rounds × 20 reps per leg
**Quality:** 8/10
**What went well:**
Hip extension improved significantly from last week. Better balance on standing leg, able to snap back quickly.

**What needs work:**
Still telegraphing the kick slightly - need to minimize shoulder movement before the kick. Timing needs work when opponent is advancing.

**Coach feedback:**
"Good power generation from the hips. Focus on chambering the knee higher before extension."

---

### Technique 2: Rear Teep to Mid-Section
**Reps/Rounds:** 3 rounds × 15 reps per side
**Quality:** 7/10
**What went well:**
Good distance management. Successfully used it to create space when partner was pressuring.

**What needs work:**
Sometimes dropping my rear hand when kicking. Need to keep guard up consistently.

**Coach feedback:**
"Excellent use for creating distance. Remember - hands stay high always."

---

### Technique 3: Catch and Counter Roundhouse
**Reps/Rounds:** 4 rounds × 12 catches per side
**Quality:** 6/10
**What went well:**
Timing improved. Successfully caught 80% of kicks thrown at moderate speed.

**What needs work:**
Transitioning from catch to counter sweep is slow. Need to be more explosive. Sometimes catching with wrong hand position.

**Coach feedback:**
"Good improvement! Now focus on immediately off-balancing opponent after the catch - don't wait."

## Sparring/Live Training

**Type:** Light
**Rounds:** 3 rounds × 3 minutes
**Partner(s):** Jake (similar weight class)

### Round 1
**Strategy:**
Work on teeps to control distance and prevent him from entering boxing range.

**What worked:**
Lead teep worked excellently to interrupt his rhythm. Caught two of his roundhouse kicks.

**What didn't work:**
Got caught with a body shot when I dropped my hand after a teep. Need to be more mindful.

**Key moments:**
Successfully executed teep-cross-hook combination that I've been drilling. Felt natural.

---

### Round 2
**Strategy:**
Focus on defense - practice catching kicks and moving off the centerline.

**What worked:**
Lateral movement was good. Avoided most of his combinations by moving to his open side.

**What didn't work:**
Got swept when I tried to catch a kick - my base wasn't solid enough.

**Key moments:**
Caught his roundhouse and successfully swept him! First time getting that in sparring.

---

### Round 3
**Strategy:**
Mix offense and defense, try to implement everything from today's drilling.

**What worked:**
More fluid combinations. Teep-jab-cross worked well. Slip and counter with elbow landed clean.

**What didn't work:**
Got caught with a low kick because I wasn't checking properly - too focused on upper body.

**Key moments:**
Best round of the three. Felt like techniques are becoming more instinctive. Coach gave thumbs up from sideline.
