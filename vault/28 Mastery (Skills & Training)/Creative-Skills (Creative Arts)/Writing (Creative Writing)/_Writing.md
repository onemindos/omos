## Training Principles

1. **Write Daily** - Consistency builds skill faster than anything
2. **Read Widely** - Input fuels output
3. **Edit Ruthlessly** - First drafts are just starting points
4. **Seek Feedback** - Outside perspective accelerates growth
5. **Finish Projects** - Completion teaches more than perfection

---

## Mastery Levels for Writing

- **Novice** - Learning basics, struggling with structure
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can complete pieces with guidance
- **Competent** - Produces clear, coherent work independently
- **Proficient** - Developed voice, fluid expression
- **Expert** - Mastery across genres, published/recognized work
