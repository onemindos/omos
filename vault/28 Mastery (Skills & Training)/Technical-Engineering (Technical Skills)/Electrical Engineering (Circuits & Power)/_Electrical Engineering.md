## Training Principles

1. **Start Simple** - Master basics before complex circuits
2. **Breadboard First** - Prototype before committing to PCB
3. **Measure Everything** - Multimeter is your friend
4. **Understand Safety** - Electricity can be dangerous
5. **Read Datasheets** - Component specs matter

---

## Mastery Levels

- **Novice** - Learning basic circuit theory and components
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can build simple circuits with guidance
- **Competent** - Designs functional electronics independently
- **Proficient** - PCB design, debugging complex systems
- **Expert** - Advanced systems, innovative solutions
