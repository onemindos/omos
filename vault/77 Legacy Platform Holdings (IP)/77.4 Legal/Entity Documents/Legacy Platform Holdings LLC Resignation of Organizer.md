Resignation of Organizer

for

**Legacy Platform Holdings LLC**

The undersigned, being the Organizer of **Legacy Platform Holdings
LLC**, herein the "Company", a limited liability company in the state of
Wyoming, does hereby adopt the following resolutions and takes the
following action by written consent in lieu of a meeting.

the duties of Organizer and relinquishes all further duties to the
Members of the Company; and

**RESOLVED,** that simultaneous with the Organizer's transfer of all
further duties to the Members, the said Organizer resigns such office
effective this date; and

**RESOLVED,** that the following named persons shall constitute the
initial Members of **Legacy Platform Holdings LLC**:
