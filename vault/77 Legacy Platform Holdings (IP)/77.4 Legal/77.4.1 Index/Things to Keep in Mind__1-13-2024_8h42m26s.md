**RE: LLC Maintenance and Operation**

Dear Client:

This letter is intended to provide you with a checklist that you should
follow for each LLC so that you can maximize all benefits an LLC can
provide.

Since one of the major reasons for forming your LLC was to provide you
with the asset protection that is so necessary in these litigious times,
I want to emphasize some activities that you must be sure to follow:

1.  **Properly Title Your Property:** Any asset you wish to protect must
    > be properly titled in the name of your LLC. Property not titled in
    > the name of your LLC will not be protected from creditors.

2.  **Obtain Endorsement for Title Insurance:** Transferring real estate
    > to your LLC may result in a loss of title insurance coverage. As a
    > precautionary measure, we recommend that you obtain an endorsement
    > to your title insurance policy that names your LLC as an
    > additional insured. There may be a nominal cost to secure an
    > endorsement, but it is an expense that is worth spending.

3.  **Obtain Endorsement for Property and Casualty Insurance:** When
    > transferring real property into the name of your LLC, it will be
    > important that the property and casualty insurance policy be
    > modified to reflect the name of the LLC as an additional insured.
    > Most insurance companies do not charge any additional premium to
    > add an LLC as an additional insured. It is important that the
    > insurance company is made aware of the transfer and all
    > requirements for maintaining the policy are complied with.

4.  **Treat Your LLC as a Separate Entity for all Purposes:** There is a
    > theory in creditor protection known as the "alter ego" theory. It
    > means that if you fail to respect the separateness of your LLC, it
    > will be ignored by the courts because it has been ignored by you
    > and therefore, a successful litigant will be able to take the
    > LLC's property because you failed to treat it in the formal
    > fashion the courts demand.

5.  **Use the LLC's Federal Employer Identification Number:** All
    > financial business conducted by the LLC should be done using the
    > FEIN. None of your financial or business assets should be reported
    > under your personal taxpayer number. This is as important to
    > maintaining the asset protection features of the LLC as with
    > titling all of your business and financial assets in the name of
    > the LLC.

> Open a bank account in the name of the LLC, using your Federal Tax
> Identification Number. **All income and expenses must go through this
> account**. It is preferable to have a separate bank account for each
> subsidiary. If you choose to use one bank account, please make sure to
> keep detailed records that track the income and expenses for each
> subsidiary.

6.  **Treat all Members Equally:** If the managing members decide that a
    > distribution of profits is warranted, all members must receive
    > their proportionate share of the distributions. If you control the
    > finances of the LLC as though it is merely an extension of your
    > pocketbook, the courts will do likewise and the powerful asset
    > protection features will be lost.

7.  **Income Tax Reporting:** Because all your financial activities are
    > to be operated in the name of the LLC, it must file its own income
    > tax return. If you have subsidiary LLC's, they will not need to
    > file a separate return, only the master will file. If the LLC has
    > more than one Member, then it is treated as a partnership for
    > federal income tax purposes and files a Form 1065. Your accountant
    > or other tax preparer can assist you in filing the return. Each
    > member of the LLC will receive a Form K-1 that includes that
    > member's share of the financial activity of the LLC to report on
    > their individual income tax returns.

8.  **LLC Meetings:** I advise my clients that an annual meeting, with
    > minutes similar to what would be required for a corporation, are
    > strongly advisable. Please keep minutes of what was discussed and
    > who attended the meeting, and file the copy of the minutes in your
    > LLC file. I suggest you choose a day, perhaps a birthday, or other
    > important day so that you develop a habit of having the meeting at
    > the same time each year. If any member is not able to attend, they
    > should be given a copy of the minutes to sign and return to the
    > managing member.

9.  **No Personal Expenditures from LLC Funds:** Under no circumstances,
    > unless from a distribution approved by a vote of the members, may
    > LLC funds be used for personal purposes not related to the
    > operations of the entity itself. This is essential to maintaining
    > the separate stature of the entity and avoiding a loss of asset
    > protection.

10. **File Annual Reports:** Our filing service will notify you of
    > required annual maintenance steps, so please keep your email and
    > phone number up-to-date. If you neglect to do so, your LLC may be
    > administratively dissolved, which will leave you with no asset
    > protection, so please be sure to promptly file your reports.

If you have any questions that I can answer, please let us know.
