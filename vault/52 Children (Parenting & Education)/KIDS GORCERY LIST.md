**Whole Grains**

-   **Whole wheat bread**

-   **Whole wheat tortillas:** good for wraps, quesadillas, burritos,
    rolled up with peanut butter and banana, etc.

-   **Whole-grain pasta**

-   **Whole grain rice**

**Refrigerated Items**

-   **Eggs:** hard-boiled for salads, in freezable breakfast sandwiches,
    scrambled for \"breakfast for dinner\" nights

-   **Cheese**: cheese sticks for snacks; sliced cheese to go with
    crackers and salami for lunchbox; fresh mozzarella balls for dinner,
    cheddar cheese for everything

-   **Yogurt**: Greek varieties including squeeze yogurt
    or single-serving vanilla, which pairs well with granola

-   **Hummus**

-   **Orange juice**

-   **Milk**

**Frozen Foods**

-   **Frozen fruits**: for smoothies

-   **Whole wheat waffles or pancakes**: for easy breakfast or lunch

-   **Sweet potato fries**: sometimes it\'s easier just to buy the
    frozen ones

-   **Veggie burgers**

-   **Ice cream!** For when the kids are extra well behaved treat
    them---and yourself---with some ice cream!

**\
Vegetables**

-   **Carrots**: great as a snack with hummus or roasted with maple
    syrup for dinner

-   **Red pepper**: this is a dinner staple - both kids eat it raw

-   **Broccoli**: can be used in stir-fry\'s, in mac-in-cheese, or
    dipped in dressing for a snack

-   **Cauliflower**

-   **Tomatoes**

-   **Cucumbers**

-   **Avocados**: Taco Tuesday - enough said

-   **Winter squash**: roasted or mashed with maple syrup

-   **Sweet potatoes**: mashed or baked into fries

-   **Fresh greens**:

    -   Spinach

    -   Kale

    -   Arugula

    -   Lettuces

**Fruits**

-   **Apples**: great for snacks, in salads, as a side with dinner, and
    so much more

-   **Bananas**: adds nutrition to cereal, smoothies, pancakes or just
    as is

-   **Grapes:** great for grab-n-go, hiking, and picnics

-   **Berries:** pairs well with yogurt or homemade whipped cream as a
    healthier dessert

-   **Melon:** buy pre-cut fresh melons for easy lunch or snack option

-   **Clementines:** a perfect snack and easy to peel for young kids

-   **Mandarins**

**Healthy Snacks**

-   **Peanut butter & jelly**

-   **Cereal**: limit added sugar intake by avoiding anything that looks
    like a rainbow of colors

-   **Granola**: Loose Granola and/or Granola Bars

-   **Pretzels**

-   **Popcorn**

-   **Wheat** **crackers**

-   **Tortilla chips**

-   **Salsa**

-   **Nuts**

-   **Raisin**

-   **Apple Sauce**
