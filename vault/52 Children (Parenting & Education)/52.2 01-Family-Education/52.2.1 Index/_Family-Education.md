# Family-Education — Family Learning

> *"A learning family is a growing family."*

---

## Purpose

Family-Education is the **family education center**. This area focuses on family learning systems, children's education, and building a culture of growth together.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Education Planning** — School choices, curriculum, learning paths
- **Family Learning** — Shared reading, discussions, learning projects
- **Child Development** — Developmental milestones, growth tracking
- **Homeschool Resources** — If applicable, curriculum, materials
- **Enrichment** — Classes, activities, experiences

---

## Education Philosophy

| Principle | Application |
|-----------|-------------|
| Curiosity First | Foster questions, not just answers |
| Learn by Doing | Hands-on, experiential learning |
| Model Learning | Parents as active learners |
| Individual Paths | Honor each child's interests |
| Real World | Connect learning to life |

---

## Learning Domains

- Academic (reading, math, science)
- Creative (art, music, making)
- Physical (sports, movement, play)
- Social (cooperation, communication)
- Practical (life skills, responsibility)
