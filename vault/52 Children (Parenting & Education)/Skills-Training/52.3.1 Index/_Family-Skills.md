# Family-Skills — Family Development

> *"Skills transfer across generations."*

---

## Purpose

Family-Skills is the **family skill development center**. This area focuses on teaching life skills, family activities, and building capabilities that transfer across generations.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Life Skills** — Cooking, cleaning, maintenance, self-care
- **Family Activities** — Hobbies, sports, crafts together
- **Skill Transfer** — Teaching what you know
- **Rites of Passage** — Age-appropriate challenges, growth markers
- **Character Development** — Discipline, responsibility, integrity

---

## Skill Categories

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Domestic | Cooking, cleaning, organizing |
| Practical | Repairs, maintenance, building |
| Financial | Money management, earning |
| Social | Communication, conflict resolution |
| Outdoor | Nature skills, survival basics |

---

## Development Approach

1. Model the behavior
2. Explain the reasoning
3. Practice together
4. Support independence
5. Celebrate mastery

---

## Age-Appropriate Progression

| Age | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| 2-5 | Basic self-care, simple chores |
| 6-9 | Expanded responsibilities, money basics |
| 10-13 | Complex tasks, decision making |
| 14-17 | Independence, life management |
| 18+ | Full adult capability |
