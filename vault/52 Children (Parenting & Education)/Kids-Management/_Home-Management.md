# Home-Management — Primary Residence

> *"Home is where the foundation is built."*

---

## Purpose

Home-Management is the **household management center**. This area covers the primary residence—maintenance, organization, improvements, and creating a functional living space.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Maintenance** — Repair logs, service schedules, vendor contacts
- **Organization** — Room-by-room organization, storage systems
- **Improvements** — Renovation projects, upgrades, wishlist
- **Utilities** — Service providers, accounts, bills
- **Inventory** — Major items, warranties, documentation
