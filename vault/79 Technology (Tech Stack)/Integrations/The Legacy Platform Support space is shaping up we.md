# The Legacy Platform Support space is shaping up well! The manuals are more organized and targeted, but there’s still an opportunity to streamline further and ensure the content is both comprehensive and easy to navigate.

The **Legacy Platform Support** space is shaping up well! The manuals are more organized and targeted, but there’s still an opportunity to streamline further and ensure the content is both comprehensive and easy to navigate.

🧠 **Assessment of Current Manuals:**

1.**Customer Follow-Up Procedures** (0 Articles)

2.**Billing Support** (23 Articles)

3.**Technical Support** (15 Articles)

4.**Sales and Product Support** (14 Articles)

5.**General Customer Support** (10 Articles)

6.**Knowledge Base Setup and Maintenance** (10 Articles)

7.**Ticket and Escalation Management** (10 Articles)

8.**Zoho Desk** (1 Article)

9.**Email Support - Desk** (0 Articles)

10.**Chat Support - Sales IQ** (0 Articles)

11.**Customer Success** (1 Article)

🚦 **Opportunities to Improve:**

✅ **What to Keep & Consolidate:**

1.**Billing Support**, **Technical Support**, and **Sales and Product Support** should remain as standalone manuals since they already have a significant number of articles.

2.**General Customer Support** is valuable but could be merged with **Customer Success** and **Customer Follow-Up Procedures** into a more comprehensive manual.

📂 **Manuals to Consolidate:**

**1. Customer Support & Success Manual:**

- **Chapters:**
- *General Customer Support* (10 Articles)
- *Customer Success* (1 Article)
- *Customer Follow-Up Procedures* (Move any relevant content here)
- *Client Communication Guidelines* (How to handle calls, emails, chats)

**2. Support Tools & Ticket Management Manual:**

- **Purpose:** Consolidate **Zoho Desk**, **Email Support - Desk**, **Chat Support - Sales IQ**, and **Ticket and Escalation Management**.
- **Chapters:**
- *Zoho Desk Setup & Usage* (Include the existing article)
- *Ticket and Escalation Management* (10 Articles)
- *Email Support Processes* (Previously “Email Support - Desk”)
- *Chat Support Processes* (Previously “Chat Support - Sales IQ”)
- *Knowledge Base Setup and Maintenance* (10 Articles)

🗂 **Proposed New Structure:**

**Manuals:**

1.**Billing Support** (23 Articles)

2.**Technical Support** (15 Articles)

3.**Sales and Product Support** (14 Articles)

4.**Customer Support & Success** (Consolidated Manual)

5.**Support Tools & Ticket Management** (Consolidated Manual)

**Why This Setup?**

- **Reduces Manual Count:** From 11 manuals down to 5, making it easier for the support team to find relevant information.
- **Streamlined Processes:** All ticketing, escalation, and tool-related processes in a single manual.
- **Clarity in Roles:** Customer-facing support is clearly separated from technical and billing support.
- **Supports Training Programs:** Easier to create targeted training courses using fewer, more robust manuals.

🚀 **Next Steps:**

1.**Migrate Articles:** Move the relevant articles to the new, consolidated manuals.

2.**Archive or Remove Empty Manuals:** Delete manuals with 0 articles to avoid confusion.

3.**Update Training Courses:** Ensure the changes reflect in any courses or onboarding materials linked to these manuals.

4.**Set Permissions:** Assign specific manuals to relevant roles to avoid information overload.
