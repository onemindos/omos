# Given your integration with Zoho One for managing sales, marketing, operations, finance, and HR, I recommend the following streamlined approach to fully align ClickUp with Zoho’s centralized capabilities. This structure maximizes efficiency while ensuring clarity across all departments and companies.

**Given your integration with Zoho One for managing sales, marketing, operations, finance, and HR, I recommend the following streamlined approach to fully align ClickUp with Zoho’s centralized capabilities. This structure maximizes efficiency while ensuring clarity across all departments and companies.**

**Proposed Integration Structure**

**1. Zoho One as the Operational Hub**

**All core departmental processes like Sales, Marketing, Finance, Operations, and Support are executed and automated within Zoho One. ClickUp will complement Zoho One by focusing on task management, project execution, and SOPs.**

**How ClickUp Complements Zoho One**

**ClickUp will handle:**

1.**Task-Level Management: Assigning and tracking tasks across teams.**

2.**SOP Management: Housing step-by-step guides for processes executed in Zoho One.**

3.**Cross-Company Collaboration: Managing cross-departmental workflows.**

4.**Integration Monitoring: Coordinating [Circle.so](http://circle.so/) and Zoho One integrations.**

**Updated ClickUp Structure**

**The structure ensures every task is linked to the relevant company, department, and process.**

**Space: Legacy Platform Management**

**Centralized for managing all workflows across companies.**

**Folders**

1.**Legacy Restore LLC**

2.**Legacy Acadacom LLC**

3.**Legacy Coaching LLC**

4.**Legacy Armor LLC**

5.**Global Operations**

**Lists Within Each Folder**

**Each folder houses the following department-based lists, with tasks reflecting processes managed in Zoho One:**

1.**Operations: SOP management, process documentation.**

2.**Marketing: Campaign planning and execution coordination.**

3.**Sales: Lead assignments, pipeline tracking.**

4.**Support: Linking Zoho Desk tickets to task completion.**

5.**Finance: Expense tracking, payment reconciliations.**

**Example Custom Fields in ClickUp**

**To tie tasks back to companies and Zoho One workflows:**

- **Company: Legacy Restore, Legacy Acadacom, etc.**
- **Zoho Tool: CRM, Campaigns, Desk, Projects.**
- **Priority: High, Medium, Low.**
- **Status: To Do, In Progress, Completed.**
- **Integration Status: Synced with Zoho, Pending.**

**Example Workflows by Department**

**Sales**

- **Zoho CRM: Manage pipelines and lead assignments.**
- **ClickUp Task:**
- **Task: “Follow up with leads from webinar.”**
- **List: Sales (Global Operations)**
- **Custom Fields: CRM Lead Source, Company: Legacy Acadacom.**

**Marketing**

- **Zoho Campaigns: Create email campaigns and automate sequences.**
- **ClickUp Task:**
- **Task: “Approve email content for January launch.”**
- **List: Marketing (Legacy Restore LLC)**
- **Custom Fields: Zoho Campaign ID, Target Audience.**

**Support**

- **Zoho Desk: Handle tickets and resolve member issues.**
- **ClickUp Task:**
- **Task: “Resolve ticket #1257: [Circle.so](http://circle.so/) access issue.”**
- **List: Support (Global Operations)**
- **Custom Fields: Zoho Desk Ticket ID, Member Impact Level.**

**Finance**

- **Zoho Books: Manage invoices and expense reports.**
- **ClickUp Task:**
- **Task: “Review payroll allocations for December.”**
- **List: Finance (Global Operations)**
- **Custom Fields: Expense Type, Department Allocation.**

**Integration Recommendations**

1.**Zoho Flow: Automate task creation in ClickUp when actions are triggered in Zoho (e.g., new lead, campaign, or ticket).**

2.**ClickUp Dashboards:**

- **Create Company-Specific Dashboards for Legacy Restore, Acadacom, etc., to monitor active tasks.**
- **Create Department Dashboards to track progress across Sales, Marketing, Support.**

**Benefits of This Approach**

1.**Centralized Operations: Zoho One handles execution; ClickUp manages coordination and tracking.**

2.**Cross-Company Transparency: Tasks are grouped by company but remain accessible to relevant teams.**

3.**Scalability: Easily add new companies, departments, or workflows.**

4.**Low Maintenance: Automations reduce manual updates in ClickUp, letting you focus on growing the platform.**
