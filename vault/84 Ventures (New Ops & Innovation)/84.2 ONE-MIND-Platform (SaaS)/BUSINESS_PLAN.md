## 18. Trademark & Legal Strategy

### The Challenge

"One Mind" is already trademarked by One Mind (onemind.org), a mental health nonprofit with $11.3M revenue, partnerships with Bank of America and Bristol Myers Squibb, and recognition from Fast Company. They operate "One Mind at Work" (140+ member organizations) and claim "ONE MIND" as their trademark.

### The Solution: ONE MIND OS

Adding "OS" (Operating System) creates sufficient trademark distinctiveness while preserving the core philosophy. This separates from mental health/wellness positioning and claims the "life operating system" category explicitly.

### Why "One Mind OS" Wins as IP

- **Category Creation**: Claims "life operating system" as the defining category
- **Immediate Differentiation**: "OS" signals technology/systems, not mental health
- **AI-Native Positioning**: Operating systems run on computers; your OS runs on human-AI partnership
- **Trademark Strength**: Sufficiently distinctive from existing "One Mind" marks
- **Marketing Power**: "What's your life OS?" becomes a movement

### Filing Action Plan

| Step | Action                                                                    | Cost           | Timeline    |
| ---- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------- | ----------- |
| 1    | Commission professional trademark clearance search                        | $500-1,500     | Week 1      |
| 2    | File Intent-to-Use application for "ONE MIND OS" in Classes 9, 35, 41, 42 | $1,500-2,500   | Week 2-3    |
| 3    | Full registration                                                         | Included above | 8-12 months |

**Total trademark budget**: $2,000-4,000
