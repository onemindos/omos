Trademark & Brand Strategy

The Challenge

\"One Mind\" faces trademark competition from One Mind (onemind.org), a
mental health nonprofit with \$11.3M revenue, partnerships with Bank of
America and Bristol Myers Squibb, and recognition from Fast Company.
They operate One Mind at Work (140+ member organizations) and claim
\"ONE MIND\" as their trademark.

The Solution: ONE MIND OS

Adding \"OS\" (Operating System) creates sufficient distinctiveness
while preserving the core philosophy. This approach separates from
mental health/wellness positioning and claims the \"life operating
system\" category explicitly.

Why \"One Mind OS\" Wins

-   **Category Creation:** Claims \"life operating system\" as the
    defining category

-   **Immediate Differentiation:** \"OS\" signals technology/systems,
    not mental health

-   **AI-Native Positioning:** Operating systems run on computers; your
    OS runs on human-AI partnership

-   **Trademark Strength:** Sufficiently distinctive from existing \"One
    Mind\" marks

-   **Marketing Power:** \"What\'s your life OS?\" becomes a movement
