# Land Search Sources

Master list of websites for finding land deals — raw land, farms, rural acreage, and investment parcels.

---

## Tier 1 — Major Land Marketplaces

The primary platforms dedicated to land listings. Largest inventory, best filters.

| Site | URL | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| **LandWatch** | [landwatch.com](https://www.landwatch.com) | Largest land marketplace — farms, ranches, rural land |
| **Land and Farm** | [landandfarm.com](https://www.landandfarm.com) | Farms, ranches, hunting land, timberland |
| **Land.com** | [land.com](https://www.land.com) | Part of the Lands of America network — premium listings |
| **LandSearch** | [landsearch.com](https://www.landsearch.com) | Aggregator pulling from MLS + land-specific sources |
| **Lands of America** | [landsofamerica.com](https://www.landsofamerica.com) | Rural land, ranches — CoStar network |
| **LandHub** | [landhub.com](https://www.landhub.com) | Cheap land, owner-financed parcels |
| **LandFlip** | [landflip.com](https://www.landflip.com) | Land auctions + listings, strong search tools |
| **FarmFlip** | [farmflip.com](https://www.farmflip.com) | Farm-focused spin-off of LandFlip |

---

## Tier 2 — General Real Estate (with Land Filters)

Mainstream platforms — filter by "Land" or "Lots" category.

| Site | URL | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Zillow** | [zillow.com](https://www.zillow.com) | Filter: Home Type → Lots/Land |
| **Realtor.com** | [realtor.com](https://www.realtor.com) | Filter: Property Type → Land |

---

## Tier 3 — Niche & Alternative Sources

Smaller platforms, direct sellers, government surplus, and community marketplaces.

| Site | URL | Focus |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Whitetail Properties** | [whitetailproperties.com](https://www.whitetailproperties.com) | Hunting land, recreational, rural — broker network |
| **Landio** | [landio.com](https://www.landio.com) | Direct land sales, owner financing |
| **LandCentral** | [landcentral.com](https://www.landcentral.com) | Budget land, easy financing |
| **Classic Country Land** | [classiccountryland.com](https://www.classiccountryland.com) | Rural land, small acreage, owner financed |
| **LoopNet** | [loopnet.com](https://www.loopnet.com) | Commercial land + larger parcels (CoStar network) |
| **GovDeals** | [govdeals.com](https://www.govdeals.com) | Government surplus land auctions — cities, counties, state |

---

## Tier 4 — Community / Peer-to-Peer

Unstructured listings — requires more digging but can surface off-market deals.

| Site | URL | Notes |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Facebook Marketplace** | [facebook.com/marketplace](https://www.facebook.com/marketplace) | Search "land for sale" + location — FSBO deals |
| **Craigslist** | [craigslist.org](https://www.craigslist.org) | Real Estate → Land section — local FSBO |

---

## Search Strategy

1. **Cast wide** — Start with Tier 1 (LandWatch, LandSearch) for volume
2. **Cross-reference** — Check Zillow/Realtor.com for MLS-listed parcels Tier 1 might miss
3. **Hunt deals** — Tier 3 (GovDeals, LandCentral) for below-market and owner-financed
4. **Go direct** — Tier 4 (Facebook, Craigslist) for FSBO off-market opportunities
5. **Set alerts** — Most Tier 1 sites support saved searches with email notifications
