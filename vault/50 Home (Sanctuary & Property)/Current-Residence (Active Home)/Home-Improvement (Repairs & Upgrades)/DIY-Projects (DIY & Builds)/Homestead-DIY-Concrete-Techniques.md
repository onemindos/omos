# Homestead DIY — Concrete Surface Techniques

> Applicable to: garage, patio, driveway, workshop floor, basement. Decorative concrete costs 2–3× more when hired out — all of these are owner-doable.

---

## The 3 Methods

### 1. Acid Stain
- **What it does**: Chemically reacts with concrete to create permanent, variegated color — marble-like appearance
- **Best for**: Patios, entryways, anywhere you want a natural stone look
- **Durability**: Permanent — color cannot be removed, only covered
- **Caution**: Toxic — wear PPE (gloves, eye protection, respirator); neutralize with baking soda + water after application
- **Tip**: Test a small hidden area first — results vary by concrete age and mix

### 2. Water-Based Stain
- **What it does**: Absorbs into surface pores and acts like a dye — no chemical reaction
- **Best for**: Indoor applications, areas requiring specific colors (wide color range available)
- **vs. acid**: Far less toxic, user-friendly, easier cleanup
- **Durability**: Good — apply a sealer coat on top to extend life

### 3. Epoxy Coating
- **What it does**: Hard, chemical-resistant surface film — durable against oil, fuel, tool drops
- **Best for**: Garage floors, workshop floors, basement — anywhere exposed to spills and wear
- **Cost**: ~$200 in materials for a 400 sq ft garage
- **Critical rule**: Surface prep is everything — floor must be clean, dry, and degreased before application. Failed epoxy = failed prep.
- **Water-based epoxy**: Lower VOC, safer for indoor use. Slightly more expensive.

---

## Scoring / Pattern Work
- **Tool**: Angle grinder with 4" grinding or cutting wheel
- **What it does**: Cuts geometric patterns into cured concrete — mimics tile, flagstone, custom grid layouts
- **Critical step**: Draw your full pattern in chalk first, live with it for a day, then cut. **Scoring is irreversible.**
- **Best results**: Score first → stain second. Stain pools in cuts = natural grout-line look.

## Delacruz Homestead Applications

| Surface | Method | Notes |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Garage/workshop floor | Epoxy | Handles oil, tools, cleaning — best ROI |
| Covered patio | Acid stain + sealer | Premium look, zero maintenance |
| Future barn/outbuilding | Water-based stain + sealer | Faster, safer on large area |
| Outdoor driveway/path | Penetrating sealer only | Film sealers peel outdoors — don't use them |
