## What is Solarpunk?

Solarpunk is an optimistic vision of the future that balances **technology, equity, and environmental care**. It rejects dystopia and imagines a world where:

- Humanity, nature, and technology coexist harmoniously
- Renewable energy powers everything
- Communities are self-sufficient and regenerative
- Design is beautiful, functional, and sustainable
- Technology serves people and planet, not corporations

### Core Principles

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Regenerative** | Give back more than you take |
| **Circular** | Eliminate waste—everything is reused |
| **Localized** | Decentralized, community-scale production |
| **Open Source** | Knowledge freely shared |
| **Beautiful** | Aesthetic matters—nature + art + tech |

---

## Solarpunk Design Philosophy

> "If it can't be reduced, reused, repaired, rebuilt, refurbished, refinished, resold, recycled, or composted, then it should be restricted, designed or removed from production." — Pete Seeger

### Design Guidelines

1. **Use common, locally-sourced materials** when possible
2. **Modular & repairable** — swap parts, not systems
3. **Parameterized designs** — adaptable to different contexts
4. **Accessible maintenance** — high-wear parts easy to reach
5. **Simplicity** — avoid unnecessary complexity
6. **Harmonize with nature** — integrate, don't dominate

### 💧 Water: Harvest + Cycle

| System | Function |
|--------|----------|
| **Rainwater Harvesting** | Collect from roofs → cisterns |
| **Greywater Recycling** | Sink/shower water → gardens |
| **Blackwater Treatment** | Composting toilets or biodigesters |
| **Reed Bed Filtration** | Natural water purification |
| **Drip Irrigation** | Efficient water delivery |

**The 4-Use Water Cycle (Earthship Method):**
1. Catch rainwater from roof
2. Filter for drinking/cooking
3. Use in sinks/showers (becomes greywater)
4. Greywater feeds indoor planters
5. Overflow to outdoor gardens
6. Blackwater composted or biodigested

## Solarpunk Aesthetic Elements

### Visual Language

- **Organic curves** over harsh angles
- **Green everywhere** — plants integrated into structures
- **Natural materials** — wood, stone, copper, glass
- **Art Nouveau influences** — flowing, nature-inspired forms
- **Stained glass** and colored light
- **Visible systems** — show the tech working (solar panels as design feature)

### Color Palette

| Color | Usage |
|-------|-------|
| 🌿 **Greens** | Dominant — living plants, painted accents |
| 🌅 **Warm earth tones** | Wood, clay, terracotta |
| ☀️ **Solar gold/amber** | Accents, lighting |
| 🌊 **Water blues** | Water features, glass elements |
| 🌸 **Flowering colors** | Seasonal pops from plants |

### Design Touches

- **Solar panels as art** — arrange in patterns, colorful frames
- **Water features** — visible rain chains, small ponds, fountains
- **Pollinator gardens** — butterflies, bees, hummingbirds
- **Outdoor living spaces** — covered patios, hammocks, fire pits
- **String lights** — solar-powered ambient lighting
- **Murals & mosaics** — nature-themed public art

## Integrating Your Tech Stack (Solarpunk Style)

### FarmBot → "The Garden Tender"
- **Rename in UI** to something organic
- **Enclose in living trellis** — beans/vines growing on frame
- **Solar-powered** — dedicated panel array
- **Visible but integrated** — feature, not eyesore

### Rachio → "Rain Dancer"
- Smart irrigation as **water stewardship**
- Connect to rainwater cisterns first, municipal backup
- **Weather-responsive** — work with nature, not against

### Ecowitt → "Forest Whispers"
- Sensors as **nature's voice** — listening to the land
- Deploy throughout food forest
- Beautiful weatherproof housings (copper, wood)

### Home Assistant → "The Hearth Mind"
- Central nervous system of homestead
- **Dashboard as art** — beautiful visualizations
- Transparent operation — show family the flows

### Legacy AI → "The Keeper"
- Steward of the land through technology
- Morning briefs include **garden status**
- Seasonal planning and harvest tracking
