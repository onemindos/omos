# 80 — Innovation (New Ventures Pipeline)

> *Ideas die without a system. This is the system.*

## Purpose

The innovation engine for building new things from scratch. Ideas flow through three stages: raw idea → validated → launch-ready. Once a venture launches and scales, it earns its own domain number.
