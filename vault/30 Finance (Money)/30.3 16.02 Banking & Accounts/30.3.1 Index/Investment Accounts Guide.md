# Investment Accounts Guide

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Finance, Investments-Wealth

## Overview

Comprehensive guide to the types of investment and financial accounts needed for wealth building.

---

## Investment Accounts

### 1. Employer-Sponsored Retirement (401k, 403b)
If your employer offers a retirement plan and especially if they match contributions, it's usually a good idea to contribute to this account.

### 2. Individual Retirement Account (IRA)
Different types available:
- **Traditional IRA** - Tax-deferred contributions
- **Roth IRA** - Tax-free growth and withdrawals

### 3. Taxable Brokerage Account
If you have maxed out your contributions to tax-advantaged retirement accounts and still have funds to invest, open a regular taxable brokerage account.

### 4. Specialized Investment Accounts
- **529 College Savings Plan** - Education funding
- **Coverdell Education Savings Account** - K-12 and higher education
- **SEP IRA** - Self-employed retirement
- **Solo 401(k)** - Self-employed with higher contribution limits

### 5. Robo-Advisors
Automated investing platforms for hands-off management.

---

## Banking Accounts

### 1. Checking Account
Primary account for depositing income and paying bills. Consider separate account for discretionary spending.

### 2. Savings Account
For emergency funds and short-term goals. Use "savings buckets" for different purposes:
- Emergency Fund
- Vacation Fund
- Major Purchase Fund

### 3. High-Yield Savings / Money Market
Higher interest rates for medium-term goals or liquid funds.

### 4. Business Account
Keep business and personal funds separate for clarity and tax purposes.

---

## Brokerage Account Considerations

| Reason | Details |
|--------|---------|
| **Diversification** | Different brokers offer different services and investment options |
| **Risk Mitigation** | SIPC insures up to $500,000 per brokerage |
| **Ease of Management** | Separate accounts for different strategies (long-term vs. active) |
| **Backup Access** | Secondary account for technical issues |

---

## Account Priority Order

1. ✅ Checking Account (primary)
2. ✅ High-Yield Savings (emergency fund)
3. ✅ 401(k) up to employer match
4. ✅ Roth IRA (max contribution)
5. ✅ 401(k) max out
6. ✅ Taxable Brokerage
7. ✅ Specialized accounts as needed
