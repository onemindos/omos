## Penalty Abatement Strategy

### First-Time Penalty Abatement (FTA)
**Eligibility:**
- Haven't had penalties in prior 3 years
- Filed all required returns (or filed extension)
- Paid or arranged to pay any tax due

**How to Request:**
- Call IRS: 1-800-829-1040
- Request FTA for reasonable cause
- Can remove failure-to-file and failure-to-pay penalties

### Reasonable Cause Abatement
**Valid reasons:**
- Death, serious illness, or unavoidable absence
- Fire, casualty, natural disaster
- Unable to obtain records
- Errors by IRS
- Incorrect written or oral advice from IRS
