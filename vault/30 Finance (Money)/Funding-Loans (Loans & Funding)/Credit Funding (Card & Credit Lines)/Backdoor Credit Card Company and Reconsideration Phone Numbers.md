"Backdoor" Credit Card Company and Reconsideration Phone Numbers

Want to check your application status on a credit account? Or ask for
reconsideration with different terms?

These numbers will help you get in touch with customer service reps as
well as credit analysts and underwriters at a variety of issuers. In
addition to basic customer support, they can often do things like:

-   help you find credit cards that they issue

-   find out your application status

-   tell you more about why you got denied

-   reconsider you for other credit products or terms

-   give you a higher credit limit or lower APR

-   help you use your rewards and benefits

You probably won't get the direct line to executives at these banks, but
calling these numbers might get you more help than the regular customer
service line.
