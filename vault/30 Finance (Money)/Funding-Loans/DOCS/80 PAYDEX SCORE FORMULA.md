**What is Net-30?**

It\'s a type of credit terms that offer some online vendors to their
clients. Consist of 30 days credit, then the client will have to pay the
invoice in full. These credit accounts are seen as entry-level credit
for every business looking to establish a good credit score. 

*When using net-30 accounts there are numerous factors that impact your
business credit report:*

-   **Purchase Activity** -- When you get a net-30 credit account it's
    > important to use that account frequently. If you only make one
    > purchase it will only show one payment and no continuing payment
    > history.

-   **Payment Status** -- Always pay your invoices on or ahead of the
    > due date. Paying better than terms is ideal since this can improve
    > your credit rating even more. For Dun & Bradstreet's scoring model
    > (Paydex), we want you to pay at least 7 days before the due date
    > (**this is imperative to succeed in our program**)

-   **Payment Behavior** -- It's important for the long term, to get a
    > couple of vendor accounts that you can use more consistently since
    > your payment behavior is key. It's all about ongoing purchase
    > activity so you can build more payment history.

-   **High Credit** -- You also want to work to increase your credit
    > limits with the net 30 vendors you actually use. So, once you
    > obtain a credit line and you're using your credit, request a
    > credit limit increase in six months. With higher credit limits
    > reporting on your business credit reports, it has a direct impact
    > on your credit limit recommendation.

-   **Balance Owing** -- With balance owing make sure you do not exceed
    > your credit limits or carry excessive debt across all your vendor
    > accounts. To start, we want you to have 0% usage.

-   **Length of Payment History** -- The length of your Payment History
    > will play a significant role during every credit review process.
    > After you finish our program, you might want to continue building
    > your credit history on your own for a better credit limit on your
    > business different credits.
