AUTO LOAN PLAY AND INTEREST RATE THAT WILL NEVER CONVERT. ( The Rates
are cheaper when doing auto instead of a home loan).

Breakdown:

Loan 1 was for 35k with a 5.19% APR. Loan was instantly approved within
seconds of me submitting the application from my phone. Funds were
deposited into my personal checking account the next morning. Please
note that I never spoke with anyone and no documents were ever
requested. Super Easy Money!!!

Loan 2 was for 65k with a 5.14% APR. I applied for this loan 5 months
after the first loan. (They have a 100k cap) I received an email notice
to call in prior to approval .

I was asked 3 questions. I used \$165,674 as income.

1\. Was this loan going to be used to pay off the first loan? I
responded "no"

2\. Was I planning on closing Loan 1? I responded "no"

3\. Am I able to provide 2 Proof of Income Documents (no deadline
giving)? I responded "yes" SN: Adobe Acrobat is YOUR BEST FRIEND

I uploaded the documents and completed an identity verification quiz and
BOOM! Approved. Funds deposited the next morning directly into my
account.

These are the steps to achieve the above results and takes about 5
minutes to complete. I've also done this process for 2 clients with
credit scores in the low 700 just go with at least a 715 to be on the
safe side.

Please note They will pull from either Equifax or Transunion.

1\. Go to lightstream.com (they are a SunTrust Partner, and NO you don't
have to have a Suntrust account)

2\. Select Individual Application

3\. Select Used Auto Purchase from Dealer (trust the process) Title or
Dealership info will never be requested. My loans are over a year old
and information has never been requested and there has NEVER been an
increase or change in interest.

4\. Insert Loan amount & Term (How much you are requesting and how long
you want to repay) You give them the amount of the loan you're
requesting.

5\. Select Auto Pay

6\. Insert Checking information for deposit/payments when prompted

7\. Insert your personal information (Name, address, job, & income (this
should be enough to cover current outstanding debt and new loan payment)

8\. You will also need a visa/discover card for verification purposes

9\. Make sure your report is not locked or frozen before next step

10\. Then Submit Application
