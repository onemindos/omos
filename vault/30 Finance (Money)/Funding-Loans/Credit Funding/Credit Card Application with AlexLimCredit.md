**Hey guys! Here is the guide to applying for a ton of credit cards at
once!**

The point here is to take advantage of the few minutes you have for
application processing times. This only works for cards that you get
approved for immediately online (which is most if you have good credit)

So be sure you have optimal credit when you do this. If you think you
can guarantee 1 approval, then you should be good to be approved for
most that you apply for! Example is 5+ years of credit age, and 700+
credit score.

For example: You can get past 5/24 Chase Rule (Only 5 of ANY credit
accounts in 2 years if you want to get approved for any Chase CC) by
applying to your 4th 5th and 6th card using this method, and because the
applications are approved before it hits the system, you can now get
6/24.

So, to get started\....

1\. Get either \"Hotspot Shield VPN Free Proxy\" or \"touch VPN\", or
maybe even ExpressVPN, they allow you to pick US IP addresses. Get
premium so you can even pick states which works in your benefit. Might
make sense to get either premium for when you are applying. You want to
make sure it is in your area/state because CC companies might get sus.

2\. Use one device for applying for no more than 2 cards with the same
browser. For example: Computer - 2 cards, Phone - 1 card, second
computer - 2 cards and yet another computer for another 2 cards. You can
try 4 cards per computer but make sure to use a different browser for
every 2 cards because if the system senses you are applying from the
same IP, your applications will likely get denied, so make sure to use a
new IP for every 2-4 cards.

3\. When you have the setup, applications will be submitted for at the
exact same time so the system doesn't have enough time to understand
applications have been submitted.

I know other credit guys who have applied for 14-16 cards with 95%
acceptance rate.

Applications for that many cards was done within 30 seconds.

BE SURE if you have the option, do different IPs in your STATE or at
least within the same state region. If you apply from an out of state IP
address, they will decline you, even if you have high odds of being
approved, just because it's suspicious that the application is somewhere
you don't live.

With this you also save on as many hard pulls as possible (maximum is
usually 2 but those that applied for 16 cards got only 5 hard
inquiries.)

The reason we want to apply for a ton of cards with little inquiries is
because if you apply for cards 1 by 1, by the 3rd or 4rth application,
you will be declined because they can see that you are constantly
applying for new cards and it is suspicious. You have to build history
and show loyalty and responsibility. Therefore we apply for a ton at
once in order to speeden the process of obtaining all the cards we need.

Not only can we use all those cards to MS and get the benefits, but a
lot come with bonus points for spending a certain amount in a certain
amount of time. So you are able to MS on all those cards and receives
hundreds of thousands in points or cashback.
