## SMART Goal Framework

**For each goal, ensure:**
- **S**pecific: Clear, defined objective
- **M**easurable: Quantifiable target
- **A**chievable: Realistic given resources
- **R**elevant: Aligned with values and priorities
- **T**ime-bound: Clear deadline
