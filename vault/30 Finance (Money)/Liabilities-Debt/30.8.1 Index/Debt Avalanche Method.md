# Debt Avalanche Method

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Finance, Budgeting

## Overview

The Debt Avalanche Method is a debt repayment strategy designed to minimize the total interest paid over time. It prioritizes paying off debts with the highest interest rates first, which leads to faster overall debt elimination and financial savings.

## How It Works

1. **List All Debts:**
   - Compile a list of all your debts (e.g., credit cards, personal loans)
   - Record the balance, interest rate, and minimum payment for each

2. **Order by Interest Rate:**
   - Rank your debts in descending order, starting with the highest interest rate

3. **Allocate Payments:**
   - Make minimum payments on all debts
   - Use any extra funds to pay off the debt with the highest interest rate

4. **Repeat and Redirect:**
   - Once a high-interest debt is cleared, redirect funds toward the next highest-interest debt
   - Continue until all debts are paid off

## Example

| Debt Type | Balance | Interest Rate | Minimum Payment |
|-----------|---------|---------------|-----------------|
| Credit Card A | $5,000 | 20% | $100 |
| Personal Loan | $10,000 | 10% | $200 |
| Student Loan | $15,000 | 5% | $150 |

**Strategy:**
1. Focus all extra payments on **Credit Card A** (20% interest) while making minimum payments on other debts
2. Once Credit Card A is paid off, allocate extra payments to **Personal Loan** (10% interest)
3. After the Personal Loan is cleared, target the **Student Loan** (5% interest)

## Advantages

- **Saves Money on Interest:** Minimizes total interest paid by targeting high-interest debts first
- **Faster Debt Elimination:** Achieves overall debt clearance more efficiently than other methods

## Disadvantages

- **Longer to See Progress:** Large, high-interest debts can take longer to pay off, which may affect motivation

## Debt Avalanche vs. Debt Snowball

| Method | Priority | Best For |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **Avalanche** | Highest interest rate first | Maximum savings |
| **Snowball** | Smallest balance first | Quick wins/motivation |

## Is This Method Right for You?

The Debt Avalanche Method is ideal for:
- Individuals motivated by long-term savings
- Those comfortable with delayed gratification to achieve greater financial efficiency
