Hacking The Factors Of Credit
Intro To Credit
How to Increase your score in each factor:

1) Inquiries- You can go through inquiry removal with a credit repair company. I do not recommend going through inquiry removal on accounts that are open even if the company claims they can get it off without getting the account closed.

2) Total Accounts- Open new accounts, get someone to add you as an authorized user on a positive credit account or buy Tradelines. Remember this category also includes the mix of the types of credit accounts you have (auto, mortgage, credit cards, personal loans, ect.)

3) Age of Accounts- Buy Tradelines of get family/friends to add you as an authorized user on their accounts with a long history.

4) Utilization- Bridge loans, family/friends paydowns, debt hacking (moving debt around from account to account) and debt consolidation loans.

5) Payment History- Dispute through CFPB if you have 1 derogatory mark or less and above 80% of on time payments. If your payment history is worse than that, you will likely need to hire a credit repair or sweep company.

If you are disputing yourself, you can dispute almost all late payments/derogatory marks through:

1) Direct with the bank/institution

2) through credit karma or the credit bureaus

3) On CFPB.gov (usually the best and quickest) 

You cannot dispute:

Age on your accounts (unless the company reported in error)

The number of accounts (unless the company reported in error)

Your utilization (unless it is reported in error)
