To attain results for applications:
1) Utilization below 50% on every single personal account (includes Personal Lines of Credit) 

2) No late payments in last 24 months is ideal but none in last 6 months is necessary.

3) Absolutely no derogatory marks

4) Less than 3 hard inquiries in last 6 months on every bureau

5) Above a 700 FICO 8 score

Three Reasons Why Utilization Is Important:
1. It limits you from applying for new credit as you appear high risk with a high DTI ratio.
2. You get trapped in high interest debt.
3. Your credit score takes a big hit.

Here are the strategies for utilization above 50% on your personal accounts (including personal lines of credit); 
 

1. Ask family or friends to pay down the balance right before the statement due date on the account, then pay them back through a merchant or another MS technique after the card reports a lower rate


2. Get a bridge loan from a company. Some funding companies offer to pay down your balances temporarily to get you funding but charge a very high rate (the lowest I have seen is 30% of the amount they pay down)


3. Get a debt consolidation loan. If you credit score is pretty low they will often want you to have pay stubs from your employer to verify income. This is a temporary fix as interest rates are almost always over 10%. If you get one make sure to pay it off and move balance over to a 0% business card asap. Nerdwallet.com is an excellent resource for this.


4. Move credit limits around. If you have multiple accounts with the same bank, call in and move some of the credit limit over to the high balance account to lower your utilization


5.  Balance Transfer to other accounts or a new credit card (preferably a business credit card, if possible - reminder: business credit does not report utilization). Most credit cards have this balance transfer option and is quite inexpensive (around 3%)


6. Withdrawal funds from 401 (k) - If you have an old 401k from an employer, you can actually move that to a self directed account and loan yourself the money at no expense. We can connect you with a few resources to eliminate a lot of potential fees if you need us.


7. Home Equity Line of Credit / Cash-Out Refinance - Funds from equity in current primary residence &/or other properties.


8. Lines Of Credit - We always recommend getting at least one real line of credit. They are super valuable because you can use them to pay down high interest cards immediately which will allow you to move everything over to 0% cards. If you business is not over 6 months old, it is still wise to at least go get a personal line of credit.
