**Authorized Users & Tradelines**

So.. Why do I always bring up authorized users / tradelines?   Because
it is the FASTEST way to boost your credit score! If you do not have
late payments or derogatory marks (collections, bankruptcies, etc).

Your credit score consists of 6 factors

-   **Utilization** ( Credit use / Total credit line )

-   **Total Accounts** (Mortgages, Student Loans, Car Mortgage, CC)

-   **Age of Credit History** ( Average across all total accounts )

-   **Payment History** ( % of timely payment )

-   **Hard Inquiries** ( When creditors run your credit for approval )

-   **Derogatory Marks** ( Collections, Bankruptcies, Repos, etc )

You can boost the first 3 categories of credit, instantly, once your
credit score updates on the next cycle if you were to be added to a
credit card with good standing.   How?

For an example, let's say your credit score is in the mid 600s because
your utilization is 100% at \$5,000, total accounts is 10, and age of
credit history is 2 years.

If you were to be added as AU to a credit card that has a

-   5 Years credit history

-   \$30,000 Credit limit

-   100% Payment history

You would INSTANTLY see a boost in your credit score. This is because
your

-   Average age history goes up !

-   Total Accounts goes up !

-   Utilization would go down, \$5000/\$35000 CL = 14% !

I recommend you ask friends and family for authorized users because it
could be 100% free! All they have to do is go on their banking app or
website and add you as authorized user on their credit card.   **MAKE
SURE YOU ARE ADDED TO A CARD IN GOOD STANDING!**

Even if you get added to a card that has \$30,000 CL.. If they have a
90% payment history or 50% utilization on it, it will screw you more
than it will help.

Make sure they are responsible with their credit cards and pay their
balance on time and in full, prior to statement date every month.

Tradelines are the exact same as authorized users except you have to pay
for the service. That is why I always recommend asking for AU first and
then look into tradelines. Free is always better.
