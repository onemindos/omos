# Identify your life purpose, what you should look for

How are you supposed to find your life purpose? Sometimes this does not mean your job that you do everyday, your responsibilities and your long-term goals.  This can mean the real reason that you are here and the very reason that you exist in life.  

If you believe that you do not have a purpose in life and that you are not looking for a meaning, you do not have to worry about identifying anything.  If you believe that you are looking for something to do in life then you need to work hard at getting your purpose and learning about what it means for you.  This is going to help you be a stronger and more achievable person with anything that you try to do.  

If you want to find the true purpose that you have in life then you need to let go of all the emptiness and false purposes that you hold in your life.  You need to discover what you have in your life and what is most important to you.  There are going to be so many ways that you can do this and all you really have to do is really look at who you are and where you see yourself in the future.  

Here are a few things that you should do when you are looking for a purpose in life.
* You should write down a list of what you believe you are here on earth to do. 
* Make sure that you are answering your questions honestly and put what jumps into your headfirst.  It does not have to be complicated, just what you feel.
* Continue writing things that you want to do.  When you find the one that makes you emotional and gets your excited, you have found your true purpose in life. 

It does not matter what you do in life.  To some it will make no sense at all and to others it will make perfect sense.  As long as you feel good in your decision and what you are doing, then you are following your heart.  This is what your purpose is and what makes most sense to you. You want to make sure that you are taking out all the clutter in your life.  You have to in order to understand what you are truly here to do. 

If you are really trying to find your purpose and you are not sure how to make it happen, you might want to try some counseling. This can be the best way to get involved with what is going on in your life and getting all the facts in the open.  You might want to really analyze your life and determine if you are doing something that really makes you happy.

You may have the feeling of resistance at some point in your life.  However you want to go through your feelings and make sure that you are using excuses to get out of the work.  You need to push past it all and keep doing what you want to so that you can live a happier and more successful no matter what you are trying to do. 

When it is your time to leave earth, you need to feel as if you accomplished something and your mind will be at ease.  This is what will make life worthwhile and give you great peace of mind.
