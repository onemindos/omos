# Who you can talk to about changing careers

When you think that you might want to make a career change you will want to have as much support as you can. You need to figure out what you want to do and how you need to go about making it happen. Once you have this information you will be better able to find motivation to get you on the right path to success and motivation.  

Think about the idea of a career change.  Is this something that is going to make you happy in life?  If you had all the money in the world, what would be doing with yourself and time? Would you be happy with sitting around and doing nothing?  Would you rather be working and making a good life at a career that you can be satisfied with yourself at the end o the day?  These are questions that you need to realize so that you can be better able to find the career path the makes you happier. 

You will need to evaluate your skills and the talents that you have.  What are you good at and what do you most enjoy?  Think about the career paths that allow you to do what makes you happiest.  Be open minded and creative when it comes to doing what you want to do.  Consider your financial situation and then think about what you expect to be paid to make a living.  Are you willing to lower your standards so that you can take a job that may pay less in order to be happy?  

List out everything that you will expect in your career.  You should look at a career handbook and find the courses that you think will make you happiest.  There are services out there that can help you to prepare for the career that will give you satisfaction and enjoyment.  You may also want to volunteer or organizations that are related to the job field that you have. If you want to work doing something with your hands you need to find the career that you can sit in on and learn as much as you can.  

Talk to people that is in your desired field.  You need to explain your situation and ask them for some advice.  Give them your information and let them talk to you about certain aspects in the career path that you want to follow in.  You may need to save some money to support yourself for a few months or however long you think you will need to find a job in the career that you are looking for.  It is always good to have some money saved for this type of instance in life.

There are going to be plenty of things to think about when you are trying to decide if you should switch careers or not.  You will need to have support from your family and friends so that you are better able to figure out what you want to do.  When you have someone to lean on and give you the love and support that you need there will be an easier transformation in your life. You will then be able to do what you want to so that you can be more fulfilled in your lifestyle.  

Take time to really think about what you want to do.  When you are serious about life and changing careers you will want to make sure that you are taking every opportunity that you can. Be persistent and give yourself time to take on challenges that you may think will help you with your career.  When you are thinking about doing something different in your life you will need to make sure you have all facts lying out in front of you so that you can be more goal oriented and happier.
