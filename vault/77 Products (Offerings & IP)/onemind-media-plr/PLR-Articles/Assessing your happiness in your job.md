# Assessing your happiness in your job

Are you feeling unhappy in your current career? If you are then maybe it is time to make a change.  You may find that you are no longer excited in your workday and that you need to find something else to keep you motivated.  If you are not learning anything new and doing the same thing all the time you are not going to feel stimulated and this can become very boring and depressing. 

It makes no difference how successful you are if you are not excited over your work anymore.  You have to feel good about going to work so that you can get through a day with no problems.  If you are not feeling as if you are satisfied then you may want to go out and try to find something different that will make you happier.  Your career happiness is important to your overall happiness in life. 

The path that you have decided to take may not be the one that you want to stay on. You may want to change your careers and find that happiness that you were meant to have.  You can seek out the happiness of a lifetime if you are checking into the different options that you have and all that is out there waiting for you.  

The first thing that you can do is write down all that you do at work.  You will also want to put down your strengths, weaknesses, experience, and many other things that you think are a great value to you.  You want to then mark down the areas that you believe you need to improve.  You are the one that is going to be learning and you need to continue to grow as you go.  Participate in the opportunities that are out there and then you are better able to explore with your current employer. 

Think about the things that have brought you to this point in your life. What are trying to fulfill and how do you expect to make this all happen? If you are sincere about making yourself someone big and being proud of all that you have done than you need to sit back and express how you feel and what you want to do with your time.  

Take a look at the past success that you have had.  Are you making your dreams come true? Are you at least attempting to make your dreams and goals a reality for you?  Think about this one too; are you your own person?  Are you trying to please others and not worrying about the consequences that are out there if you make the wrong choices?  Being positive and persistent are two things that are going to get you where you need to be in your life no matter what it is.  

You deserve to have your highest potential answered.  You have to have success in your life to feel confident and good about yourself.  You do not necessarily have to switch jobs but you may have to make drastic moves to get from where you are to where you want to be.  You must take this opportunity to think of yourself as someone that is very important and all the steps that you take are going to lead you to where you want to go.  

You have to be absolutely ready to make an assessment of your career and the path that you decide to go down.  You need to take everything into account and make the choices that only you can feel good about.  The responsibility of being happy is completely up to you and the career choice that you this is right for you.
