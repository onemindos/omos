To the memory of my dad, Walter Makauyo Mhlanga, a Christian soul, a wise leader of his
community, a generous and loving father, ‘a man for all seasons’, and the first person that
taught me that ‘I am my brother’s keeper’.
Through his own struggle, and the sagely (yet oft times stern) counsel of his words, he taught
me that ‘a good parent’ seeks, if not by example then by happenstance, and without knowing
how, to teach their progeny not that they are perfect or closer to perfect, but rather to be the
courageous, gracious, and prudent kind of person that: brings hope to the world; makes
things new; makes the world and those in it better for the sake of all mankind; falters and
doubts, but upon rising continues doing what he or she can - his or her best - and tries
diligently to make their best better.
Always quick to assert that he was human (and fallible) he taught his children to not just
rejoice in being able to choose to live in Christ, but also to be intelligent, enquiring, vigilant
and spiritual. It is in that spirit that I dedicate these pages to the memory of someone that
came from nothing, and as he rose, never made his purpose the accumulation of things, but
rather invested in building the people immediately around him as well as his community. He
chose to not just be someone that selfishly minds his own business, and ignores the injustice
and misery in the world, but a rather a father figure and a wise counsel to those that would,
with a sense of grace and mercy, heed his words and his best actions.

resetting your campus and altimeter in the context of a secular social identity, career or
vocation. It is about reminding you of things you probably already know but have not
consciously thought about. It may make reference to themes typical of Christian (or other)
faiths, but it does not pretend to be a guide for Christians exclusively because it is essentially
secular in its construction. The secular voice employed throughout is not intrinsically in

prejudice that it is this authors assertion that there are no ‘children of a lesser god’ if indeed

regardless of religion, race or creed. Thus may it be installed uppermost in your minds as we
read further that this text it is not intended for an exclusive audience wherever the reader
finds themes or concepts belonging to a specific faith whether explicit or implied. It is the
author’s hope that wherever its content is debated it should prove useful for anyone trying to

Chapter 1
With the parting of the clouds after each storm, and with the dawn of each day, life asks…
What is your purpose? Life asks, and each man, woman and child must answer. We either
answer with conviction or retreat to a place of refuge within, where shame is your bed, and
fear and self-loathing your blanket.
The wise man and the fool, whatever their values are confronted by a dilemma! We are, each
of us, on a journey to discover who we are. We are consumed, obsessively, by an indulgent
interest in our own drama and past! Our internal movie reels run again, and again, no matter
what show we are watching or playing in. We also care more than we would ever admit about
how other people see us, and how we are necessarily victims due to the neglect, action or
inaction of other actors… We are, despite our neuroses’, either growing toward something
greater because we choose to move toward what we desire, or we are becoming your lesser
selves because we are only focused on what ‘they’, who ‘they’ are and what ‘they’ have done
and what we ‘do not’ want. O’ let’s just be innocent a little longer.
As humanity progresses in enlightenment we gain a keener sense of the forces that influence
the universe. If this keener sense is not to feel with your emotions, then rather it is that we
may dream more vividly and have visions, and if not that then rather it may be to hear and see
and thus measure the evidence of what life is. And when we measure we may then, with your
reasoning and your imagination, appreciate the chaos, the beauty and the order. Having a
personal perspective tells us we should not fear, for in this context we dare only to be human
– as much a part of the universe as it is because of us - but we should always take care for
there is a higher purpose.
We do find comfort by shaping the world around us yet still we cannot divorce yourselves
from the ever-present sense that there is something powerful enough to hold the universe
together, yet so lithe, and to all manner of perception vaporous that it pervades all existence.
It is within us to seek it without even choosing to do so. Perhaps it yearns for us to understand
it as much as we yearn for it. At this point I am only limited by vocabulary, and it must be
obvious what is going on. I am sure an ignorant individual calling a fridge-freezer an ‘ice
box’ does not take away from the fact that he is grappling with the description of an
encounter with a fridge-freezer. Despite that deep yearning, we must engage life daily, and
we need to tool yourselves to encounter it.
Man’s encounter with a universe much larger and more complex than he can ever fully
countenance, is limited by perspective, and random episodes of bliss interrupting pain, and
more regularly, the need to forage for provision to eat and drink. There is an abundance of
evidence around you that you are not part of a closed system but rather a dynamic universe
that is in constant decay and genesis all at once. So, it may really be up to us choose to
employ your reason to understand and fashion your encounter of the universe rather than live
suspicious of existence beyond your mind.
It is obvious that life is not just a process of growing and constantly trying to attain a state of
homeostasis. We also possess a sense of yearning within that hungers for a new, and better,

Existence certainly does not excuse itself. It is axiomatic, and its laws are as exacting and

Bruce Lee in his ‘Tao of Jeet Kune Do’ says that to be more effective it is important to know

In a necessarily binary mode we engage a thought mode that may very well compare, in a
world that is ubiquitously digital and bipolar by disposition, to that of zero becoming one –
existence and not - by venturing to say that there must be that force which brings forth
primary existence and that which destroys it! When we are frustrated and feeling all but

force, but when all is calm and we feel supremely confident that we are in control we turn
from the same faith. At such times, in your arrogance, depending on circumstance and
attitude we would rather dismiss events we cannot explain as mere happenstance because
they are too logically, philosophically, economically or politically inconvenient to
investigate.
What must we do? Wretched from your dreams, shackled to a path through space and
summoned by the apparent yet demanding immediacy of now, and then now again… we
must live purposefully. We could then develop a set of values that add up to living better, if it

augment your perception, adding other dimensions to your ability to observe and experience
existence in the process of becoming your best possible incarnation knowing that there was
no competition, we were superior even in your origins to the other species and it is your
responsibility still to advance that most superior part of your being, your mind and spirit. In
such a mode one who seeks God will find him and the other who does not is sure to find that
mankind’s ability to be master of the universe is rather limited whatever your knowledge,
capability and beliefs.

I invite you to take a journey with me beyond finding your purpose. The following is an
example of how doing something simple can change your life.
Let me stray a little, from the matter at hand and tell you a story to show you how doing simple things
to take charge does have a lot of impact. I promise to make it brief. Here goes:
A friend said that she was in a relationship with a nice guy who, after some time, had turned out to be
a manipulative jackass with passive aggressive tendencies. She wanted out. It just seemed like every
time she was about to give him the boot he would suddenly change into the charming perfect lover
she met and fell in love with all those years ago. What to do?
I gave her simple advice. I asked her what she thought being in a relationship meant. If the way her
guy treated her helped her experience what she wanted in a relationship, then she should stick with
him. If there were things he was doing that she did not like, and they made her believe strongly that
she should not be in that relationship, then it was obvious. It was about her and what she wanted out
of it. It was not about him and his behaviour. It was about what she desired her life story to be, not
his actions and his character for she could only encounter him as he chose to be and not as she
wanted him to be. Of course, that was not enough. She wanted a way to tell him that she was on her
way.
The advice was simple: “The next time you see him just turn around and yell, ‘It’s not working!’ This
is if you want to work things out. If you want to end it then rather say, ‘It’s over and I’m tired!’
Anything you say after he enquires what you mean will naturally lead to the result you want, and if it
doesn’t just get out of the room.
If you try it enough times you’ll get it right. If he asks what you mean and you feel like you are getting
cold feet just yell, ‘This thing that was…US!’ The ‘was’ word sets the context for the balance of what
you will say. My advice to her would apply in a lot of other situations where there is conflict, abuse of
a party in a relationship, partnership or contract or a deteriorating relationship. Start with a directed
and impactful expression of your dissatisfaction and then follow with a temporary retreat.
Don’t explain yourselves! It is important that at least one of you have a moment to digest what you
have said – the emotion can be all too much and there is nothing wrong with that, it is not a sign of
weakness – rather it shows that you are truly invested and you don’t just care… you believe in
commitment. State what you want as respectfully as you can, then adjourn for five minutes or get a
moment of calm. Gather yourselves. After you have gathered yourselves then make sure to get your
power back by saying the rest of the ‘why’ when in control.” She got it right.
The pettier your issues with the person you are in a relationship with, the more likely it is that you are
blaming them for your shortcomings! People are often in denial about the truth of who they are in a
relationship with. Perhaps you are the party that takes the other for granted – this is hard to accept.
You may be the possessive, jealous type that is stifling your partner’s emotional and spiritual growth.
It is difficult to be honest about who you are, and what you bring to a relationship. Sometimes the
abusive party only sees the occasions of abuse perpetrated by their partner rather their own much
more frequent and much more brutal transgressions. Do not lose the plot here, while you don’t have
to lift a hand to be psychologically and emotionally violent, both leave deep emotional scarring but
physical abuse is inexcusable.

We all know exactly what we need to do to change your lives. Why don’t we? We also
happen to know a lot of other things we could do that are either easier or much more fun in
the mean-time. Most of the things we end up very busy doing have nothing to do with
revealing the greatness within nor solving your most important challenges. It is a kind of busy
body syndrome where you feel better distracting yourselves, and entertaining an indulgent
mumbo jumbo, rather than solving your problems, or getting on with your most pressing
objectives or projects. It is an emotionally convenient, but costly idling mode.
Choose to start with the first step, it is likely that the next step will follow… then the
next, and the next after that, but start with one step regardless.
Why do we constantly get bogged yet we have other traits that should see us through? We
have vision. We have capacity. We have faith, and we have courage. All these are built in
traits for survival. What we lack is a focus on which to apply these traits. When focus has a
moment of triumph over the muddle, you get an ‘Ahaa!’ moment! The kind Oprah Winfrey is
so famous for talking about – I do not remember where she got it from though.
I meet people every day that are focused and smarter than me in some way, and I do my best
to learn from them. I also meet people every day that will likely spend many years working
on goals they inherited by accident. I meet people that will spend their lives loving people
they will never truly know, or worse, committed to ideals that are not even theirs. How
different are you from cows in a meadow, or insects underfoot if you do not care to live a
purposeful life? It is not your ultimate purpose to be born, live, have sex, work, bear
offspring, have a regular 3.5 moments of utter joy a year and die in a reasonably comfortable
way!
An attitude change may be as immediate as making a decision, but changing your
circumstances takes time
In the context of career, you may want a dramatic change in your career or feel that you need
one but in most instances when your job is not a personal calling you simply should do it
well, whether you are thanked for applying yourselves or not. Giving up your income is not a
great first step to dealing with your motivational challenges. You should eat and you also

Am I going to feel inspired forever after I read this book?
Whenever we start a new project we are excited because of how accomplishing it will change
your lives. Seldom do we take to heart that even the process of accomplishing an objective
might bring about the changed self we desire such that the goal itself becomes
inconsequential. In this instance problem solved! You can move onto a new project.
I am not suggesting that if you figured out your purpose you would spring out of bed every
morning after that bright-eyed and bushy-tailed. On the contrary! It’s all about choosing,
every day, who you are going to be. Even if you feel sad some days it is a valuable and
enlightening experience to discover an apparent lack of something. The challenge however,
is to not be discovering more than three mornings in a row that upon waking you are
completely disinterested in your life. That is a symptom of either depression, repressed
anxiety or the emergence of your new self.
Depression requires medical help beyond the scope of this publication. Anxiety requires deep
introspection on the current pressures in your life and perhaps a couple of consultations with
a psychologist or therapist.
Do you suspect that your loss of focus is an indication of a new direction or purpose, but you
are not sure? I shall attempt to help you figure out your own way to figure things out.
If, however you are not entirely sure why you have lost your inner drive or motivation and
you are not decided on letting go of your chosen goal then WELCOME! This book is meant
for you and I would like to help, if you will indulge me. Straight to the matter at hand!
