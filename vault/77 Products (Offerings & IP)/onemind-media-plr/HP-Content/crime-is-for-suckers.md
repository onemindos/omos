                            Yes or No?
Where do you see yourself in the future? Do you want to be a winner? Do you want to be
successful? These questions leave no wiggle room; the answer is either yes or no. When you
have to make decisions in life, especially those that have long term effects, you must analyze
your options in the best way possible. Asking yourself closed-ended questions, questions that
require a yes or no answer, are a very efficient way to get yourself on the right path.

Getting on the wrong path can be something that happens in the blink of an eye. Only fools
dream of becoming criminals. Most children want to be doctors, police officers, astronauts,
athletes, singers, or successful in other legitimate career fields.

You probably still spend a lot more time picturing where you want to go than thinking about
where you don’t want to end up. It is necessary to think about how your life could end in failure
to avoid falling into the traps that many people fail to see until it’s too late. The title of this book
is “Crime Is for Suckers.” But what is a sucker? Well, the definition of sucker that is relevant to
this text is, “A person easily cheated or deceived.” People who commit crimes are suckers
because they cheat themselves out of having a decent life or allow others to lead them into a
worthless life of crime.

I want you to be a winner, not a sucker. I want you to know that when people say crime doesn’t
pay, they’re providing a very serious warning. I don’t want you to be surprised by the harsh
realities of life that many people before you failed to consider. But I’m not just going to tell you
what’s right and what’s wrong. I am going to show you how committing crimes can ruin your
life. I’m going to allow you to determine your future by presenting questions in this section that
require a yes or no answer. So there’s no escape. It’s up to you to decide if you want to win or
lose in this game called life.
                DO YOU WANT TO BE JOBLESS AND HOMELESS?

          Crime is for suckers because it leaves you with a criminal record. A criminal record lists

all of your arrests and convictions. For the most part, this list remains a part of your history

forever. Even if you were arrested by mistake - for example, due to mistaken identity - it will still

show up on your record. The fact that the public can access records at any time, makes

something like this very dangerous to a person’s reputation.

          Let’s say you matched the physical description of a car thief and the cops took you in

because they thought you were the suspect. That event, your arrest for auto theft, would show up

on your record. In most cases, you can get things removed from your record, if the charges are

dismissed or you’re found not guilty. But, you may have to follow a process that can require a

lawyer.

          A conviction on your record means that you either pled guilty to a crime or were found

guilty of a crime by a court. So, let’s say you get arrested for stealing candy from a baby. If you

admit to the crime, a conviction for stealing candy from a baby appears on your record. If you

don’t admit to the crime, but a jury or judge decide that the evidence says you did it, the

conviction for stealing candy from a baby appears on your record. A conviction on your record is

obviously worse than an arrest because an arrest just means the police thought you committed a

crime. A conviction means there was enough evidence to prove you committed a crime.

          Overall, a criminal record keeps track of all of the crimes you get caught committing in

your lifetime. If you never commit a crime, you won’t have one. The courts use criminal records

to determine how to punish a criminal for their next crime. For example, if a thief has been

convicted of robbery three times, the fourth time they commit a crime would lead to a longer

prison sentence than they received in the past.
       Cops and investigators use criminal records to keep track of offenders and determine if a

recent crime was committed by a suspect. For instance, to visit or move to Las Vegas, people

with criminal records have to check in with the police. When a burglary is committed, police

may investigate people in the area with a record of burglary.

       Frankly, being sentenced to 10 years in prison and left with no criminal record is better

than receiving no prison time and a criminal record for life. Yes, prison can be a vile and violent

place; but, you can change in there. The prison guards and warden don’t want to deal with people

who cause problems, they want you to be good. But on the outside, in the real world, a criminal

record makes it hard for criminals to change.

       It’s hard to turn over a new leaf, when a criminal record limits job opportunities. There

are many industries where people with records cannot apply no matter what. A person with a

record cannot work in law enforcement. Society can’t have a criminal chasing criminals; it not

only looks bad, it’s dangerous. Whether a person is a violent criminal, thief, or white collar

criminal, it is unlikely they would be allowed do any job that requires handling money. So, that

rules out a ton of jobs for many people with criminal records.

       Most jobs provide some sort of a warning on the job application or job listing such as

“applicants must pass a background check,” “no criminal record” etc. These companies are

pretty much saying, “Don’t even apply if you have a criminal record, we don’t want you here.”

Other companies may not provide the same warning, but end up being a waste of time because

they do background checks after offering you a job. So, you go through three interviews and

prepare to start a new job, only to be rejected after they “discover” your criminal history.

       No matter what type of experience or education you gain immediately after being hit with

a criminal record, most employers will not give you the benefit of the doubt. Let’s say you
commit a crime and then, four years later, you turn your life around. You get a fast food job

making minimum wage and rise as high as you can with your experience. You also earn a

bachelor’s degree with a 4.0 GPA. You then go apply for a higher paying job than your fast food

gig. You most likely will be rejected due to your recent criminal history.

       Having a criminal record can keep you at the bottom of society for life. Most employers

won’t even let you become their janitor. Regardless of your skills or knowledge, they will be too

afraid to trust you, which will make it hard for you to make money. Even if an employer saw you

as the coolest person ever, you’re a liability. This means that even if they wanted to hire you,

they can’t because compared to the applicants without a criminal history, you are more likely to

act in a manner that damages the company.

       Let’s say you have a record of theft and you steal from the company. Businesses pay

insurance to cover company losses, so whatever you steal counts as a loss. But unlike other

losses, the insurance company may not cover the loss because they could argue that the employer

should have known you would steal.

       Your criminal record could also be used against a company in lawsuits. Let’s say you

have assault on your record for getting into a fist-fight with someone. This record could be used

against your employer, if you got into a physical altercation with someone and the person

decided to sue the company. The company could lose the lawsuit and have to pay big bucks, if

the courts decide they should have known you were a threat to other employees.

       A criminal record not only limits your job opportunities; it also keeps you from obtaining

housing in certain areas. Many apartments and properties do not rent to people with criminal

records. They either notify you during the application process that they won’t rent to you or they

reject you after a background check. On the application, it may say “No criminal record,” or
sometimes “No criminal conviction within the last 20 years,” or “There is a 99 year waiting

period for all criminal convictions” etc. It would be a waste of time to apply to these places, if

you have a record, because your application would be rejected immediately. Even if you made

10 times the rent, you would not be allowed to rent the property. It would also be a waste of

money to apply to places that deny people with records. Processing fees for each place applied to

is usually between $30-50 or more.

       There are places that do not place much weight on a person’s background. However,

these places are either too expensive for the average person or located in high crime areas. A

person with a record would most likely only be able to find housing in a high crime area. This

means that having a criminal record can result in the loss of your ability to live in a safe

environment.

       By using criminal records to determine where people should live, society is basically

saying, “If you’re a criminal, you should live around other criminals.” It’s understandable that

people would be afraid to live next door to individuals who may be a danger to their property and

physical well-being. There’s no buts about it. However, the situation does make it hard for many

offenders to turn over a new leaf. They are unable to escape the environments that encourage

criminal behavior.

       For illustrative purposes, let’s say you get sent to prison for being a drug dealer. You get

out after serving your sentence and nearly every place you try to rent rejects your application.

The only place that accepts you is the neighborhood where you used to sell drugs. Because of

your criminal record, you cannot legally own a gun. But in the neighborhood, break-ins and

robberies are common and if you aren’t armed, you may be physically harmed by a thief. If you
get a gun for protection, you risk going back to prison. To truly start over, you have to leave the

neighborhood.

       You can’t live on most properties without being listed as a renter or occupant. The renter

is the person responsible for paying the rent and an occupant is a person authorized to live with

the renter. Most properties won’t allow you to be listed as an occupant, if you have a criminal

record. If you were to move in with someone else, “off the record,” that may put them at risk of

being kicked out of their apartment. Unless you have a relationship with a homeowner, a

criminal record guarantees you a spot in a high crime area or homeless shelter.

       Your progress and growth makes no difference when it comes to most property owners.

Even if you saved a family from a burning home or served two tours overseas in a combat zone,

if you have a criminal record, they don’t want you on their property.

       Your money is no good to most of them, but some of them will try to take advantage of

you. Because they see offenders as desperate for a place to live, they may overcharge you or

force you to accept conditions that a person without a record would not. For example, if a place

usually charges $750 per month for rent, they may charge a criminal $900. If landlords are

required to give 24 hours’ notice for inspections or tours, they may only give a criminal an

hour’s notice or none at all because they expect them to be “grateful.”

       Having a criminal record makes you a liability to property owners. Like employers, they

pay insurance to cover losses. For example, if a portion of an apartment building burned down,

the owner would have to pay for the damage. If their insurance covered the loss, the payment

would come from the insurer. But, if a tenant with a record of arson caused the fire, the insurance

company may not cover the damage. They could argue that the owner should have known the

renter would burn down the property. If a renter with a record were to get into a fight with
another renter, the property owner could be sued. They could lose, if the court decides they

should have known that the criminal was a threat to other tenants.

        For the most part, there is no way to hide a criminal record from the public. Once it’s out

there, it’s out there and it will follow you for life. Schools teach that it’s ok to make mistakes.

Religions teach that forgiving others is the right thing to do. But in the real world, none of that

stuff matters.

        Once you have a criminal record, you’re pretty much screwed. Your past “mistakes”

prevent you from getting jobs you’re are qualified for. Property owners do not forgive you for

your past transgressions. You will find it hard to achieve your dreams, when society is unwilling

to help you succeed because you violated the law. Why put yourself in a situation that could

leave you broke and without a place to live? Do you want to risk being jobless and homeless for

the rest of your life?
                     DO YOU WANT TO BE DISRESPECTED?

       Crime is for suckers because it makes you and your community look bad. Why does it

matter, right? Who cares what people think, right? Well, it’s important what people think

because we are all connected. You are a member of society. Whether you believe it or like it, you

depend on society to survive.

       Right now at this very moment, you are being protected by society. In every

neighborhood, there are elements of society that make it difficult for people to do harm to you.

The threat of punishment deters most people from committing crimes. You and your property

should be safe because other people don’t want to be arrested, sent to prison, or left with a

criminal record. But when people don’t care about the consequences, you and your property are

in danger. Would you consider a person who does not care about violating your rights as

trustworthy?

       When you commit a crime, you give up your right to define yourself as an individual.

Even though you believe you are a trustworthy person, if you commit robbery, people can justify

their negative views and opinions of you. They can claim you are dangerous, bad, or a horrible

person because you committed a crime in the past. When you commit a crime, you tell society

that you aren’t afraid of the consequences that result from breaking the law. If you aren’t afraid

of the consequences, then you are more likely than law-abiding citizens to act in a way that

threatens lives and property.

       Your criminal record can be used to make you look bad in any situation where someone

is against you. To get the upper hand against you, your rival could use your criminal background

to paint you as a bad person and make themselves look good. Having a criminal record makes it

hard to counter attacks against your character by other members of society.
       Not only are you a member of society, you are also a member of a community. Your

community is one of many that make up society as a whole. Right now at this very moment, your

community is being protected by the rest of society. Every community wants to be safe. No city

or town wants another city or town to bring trouble to their area.

       When you leave your community and visit another community, you represent where you

come from. Your behavior represents the teachings and values of your town, city, state, and

country. The way you act makes a great impact when not many members of your community

have visited an area. For example, if a village in Nigeria never received European visitors, it may

assume that all Europeans behaved as their first European visitors. If these first visitors raped and

pillaged the village, future European visitors would be seen as a threat to the community.

Sometimes, your behavior in your own community can shape the perception of outsiders. If the

Nigerians previously mentioned constantly heard about Europeans raping and pillaging each

other, they would expect the worse during encounters with them.

       Generally speaking, there are two types of crimes: emotion-driven crimes and profit-

driven crimes. An emotion-driven crime usually involves some sort of violence. For example,

people usually commit assault when they are angry or upset. A person who commits emotion-

driven crimes is viewed as unstable and unable to control their feelings, when necessary. If an

arrest or conviction is on your record for an emotion-driven crime, you are marked as a person

who cannot be trusted to keep a cool head in stressful situations.

       If many people from your community are marked as emotionally unstable, other

communities will be afraid of you. An example of a group of people marked as emotionally

unstable are gang members or gangsters. They are usually linked to violent crimes perpetrated

against other gangsters and innocent people. Their behavior makes no sense to the average
person, they hurt other people for stupid reasons. They destroy their own communities while

threatening neighboring communities and have no regard for their own future. They act on how

they feel; if they feel disrespected, they attack.

        Regardless of what is shown and promoted in media and entertainment, regardless of the

admiration “thugs” get from some members of society; in the real world, adults hate gangsters.

They are viewed as overgrown children because they are emotional and prone to temper tantrums

that lead to crime. Adults think they are foolish and untrustworthy.

        A profit-driven crime usually involves some sort of sale that is not approved by the

government or activity that interferes with a person’s right to their property. Many profit-driven

crimes involve providing something potentially dangerous to other members of society. For

example, selling prescription drugs without a license would be a profit-driven crime. Stealing

and using someone’s credit card would also be a profit-driven crime. In these situations, a

person’s desire to make money outweighs their respect for the law and society. If an arrest or

conviction is on your record for a profit-driven crime, you are marked as a selfish person willing

to put others at risk to get what you want.

        If many people from your community are marked as dangerously greedy, other

communities will be afraid to trust you. Communities, where residents are comfortable enough to

leave their homes and vehicles unlocked, will feel the need to secure their property when you

arrive. Men will feel for the wallets and women will pull their purses closer in your presence.

        An example of a group of people marked as dangerously greedy are Wall Street

executives. In the past, people put their faith in Wall Street firms and were ripped off. Many

people blame the corrupt practices of executives on Wall Street for the Great Recession, a

financial crisis linked to over 10,000 suicides. Because of the behavior of a few firms and
executives, individuals and companies engaged in business on Wall Street are viewed as

potential thieves by many members of society.

       To be clear, some crimes that qualify as emotion-driven could also be classified as profit-

driven and vice versa. For example, a person could commit assault for financial gain or steal

property to get even with someone. The important thing to take away from this chapter is that

people judge you and others similar to you by your actions and the actions of those similar to

you.

       People should be judged individually by the content of their character, but unfortunately

the world rarely works that way. You can argue and complain about how the world should work

all day, but that won’t change how the world works. All citizens should be treated as innocent

until proven guilty. But, individuals and communities with criminal activity as a part of their

history are often treated as guilty until proven innocent.

       For example, a person with a conviction on their record for assault would be approached

more cautiously by a police officer during a traffic stop than a person with a clean record. If

someone has attacked another person before, most officers, and people in general, would not find

it safe to assume they would not attack others again. If a person has shoplifted before, and they

are in the vicinity of a recent theft, they would have to go out of their way to prove their

innocence to law enforcement.

       Being obligated to prove you are not a threat to others is humiliating, especially when

you have no intention of doing wrong. When people assume you are going to do something

wrong because of your criminal history, they don’t respect you as an individual. Do you want to

give people the power to disrespect you?
                            DO YOU WANT TO BE WEAK?

       Crime is for suckers because it makes you and your community vulnerable. When you

have a history of being involved in crime or have a criminal record, people can use the situation

to get you in trouble; even when you haven’t done anything wrong. At the end of the day, public

opinion matters. The media, police officers, neighborhoods, juries, and judges can determine

your fate when you’re constantly on the wrong side of the law. If you are known to have been

involved in crime, that information can be used to further damage your reputation and negatively

impact your future.

       For example, let’s say you are known to engage in illegal activity or hang out with

criminals. Outsiders can use that association to make people believe you might be involved in a

crime you did not commit. The media could use the information to sensationalize your

involvement in a crime. Your history could lead to you being questioned, detained, or arrested by

police; as it may serve as probable cause to investigate you.

       Your past behavior could lead to communities rejecting you because it makes you a

threat. Members of these same communities could end up being jury members who decide your

innocence, if you are put on trial for a crime you did not commit. If you were found guilty, your

previous involvement in criminal activity could cause a judge to impose a harsh punishment on

you.

       Though, some people may honestly believe you committed a crime based on your

history, others could use your criminal background to frame or blame you for a crime they know

you didn’t commit. If you had a history of robbing banks, it wouldn’t be too hard for someone to

set you up for robbing one. A person could also just say you did a crime and if you had a record

for the same offense, that could be enough for police to investigate or arrest you.
       Why put your fate in the hands of other people? When you stay away from crime, you

keep your destiny in your own hands. Whenever people are accused of a crime, the first thing the

media and police do is look into their background. If your record is clean, more evidence is

needed to tie you to a crime. This is very important in situations where you are falsely accused of

a crime or incorrectly identified as a suspect.

       If you have a criminal record or are known to engage in illegal activity, you give the

media the power to define your character. You give the police a good reason to think you may be

responsible for other crimes. Unlike when you have a clean record, trying to prove your

innocence is an uphill battle, when you’ve been associated with criminal activity in the past.

       Being involved in crime could result in losing your position to others. For example, let’s

say you’ve worked at a company for several years and you are in line to be promoted. One night

you go out and drive while intoxicated. You are convicted of the crime, serve some jail time and

get probation. You’re also slapped with a criminal record. Later, because of your record, you are

denied the promotion and it is given to another employee. How would you feel?

       Life is competitive. We compete in a variety of ways for resources. For instance, top

students compete against each other to get the best grades because getting the best grades means

they can get into the best schools. Getting into the best schools means they can get the best jobs.

Getting the best jobs means they can get the best stuff. When you get involved in crime, you

weaken your chances of: survival, fulfilling your dreams, and getting the things you want in life.

       You also make it easy for other people to make your life difficult. In the adult world,

your reputation is very important. As humans, we all share a common history riddled with wars

and violence that have made trust a very important part of civilized societies. People must be

able to trust that you are committed to fairly participating in the systems that allow humans to
coexist peacefully. When you commit a crime, you tell society that you do not play fair. You tell

society that you are willing to hurt people to get what you want.

       Many law-abiding citizens feel it is their duty to protect other law-abiding citizens from

criminals. When you are a criminal, you are marked. People will not want you around or in a

position where you have the power to hurt others. People will not hesitate to use your history

against you. They will try their best to limit your opportunities to harm the innocent.

       When many members of a community are involved in crime, or have criminal records,

the situation can be used to limit opportunities for the area to flourish. Chances to better a

community come in many forms. Governments, private companies, organizations, and other

groups usually invest in areas where there is potential for growth.

       For example, if an area is filled with residents who commit acts of vandalism and damage

property, governments may view maintaining public buildings and structures as a waste of time.

If many residents of a neighborhood engage in theft, private companies may view placing quality

stores in the area as a waste of money. If violence is an everyday occurrence in a location, groups

that could make a difference in the lives of the poor, may be too afraid to provide a helping hand.

       Good communities want to be viewed as safe because outside investments can help an

area prosper. A government would rather fund upgrades to a neighborhood park if it was a safe

and healthy environment for children, than if it was constantly riddled with bullets. Businesses

would rather build stores, fund after-school programs, and offer college scholarships in an area

with safe streets, than one where parents feared for the lives of their children. Organizations

would feel more comfortable providing assistance to communities that could ensure the safety of

their workers, than areas where the safety of local residents was not guaranteed.
       Heavy criminal activity in a community can not only cause outsiders to be weary of

providing assistance; it can also provide malicious individuals and groups with the means to

sabotage its growth. Imagine a neighborhood where people look out for each other, they report

suspicious activity, and the most crime that takes place is a shoplifter every now and then. One

night, a member of the community is shot. The police respond by placing heavy effort into

finding the shooter, the media puts a sketch of the suspect on the news every night, officials

make statements and assure citizens that law enforcement will find the gunman, and residents

keep an eye out for the perpetrator. Within a week, the shooter is found, the neighborhood feels

safe again, and life goes back to normal.

       Now picture another neighborhood where people don’t look out for each other, they don’t

report suspicious activity, and every day a person is shot. One night, a member of this

community is shot. The police respond by attempting to put heavy effort into finding the shooter.

Witnesses don’t cooperate. Because there are other shootings that took place that night and the

night before, police resources are strained. The media ignores the incident because it happens

every day, it isn’t news. Officials can’t make any promises to residents because the amount of

crime in the area is more than they can handle. Some residents keep an eye out for the

perpetrator, but the high level of crime in the area makes them afraid to go to the police for help;

they fear getting hurt for “snitching.” Months go by and the shooter is not found. The victim’s

family gets no closure. The incident is considered just another part of everyday life in the

community.

       Now imagine if an outsider was looking to cause trouble somewhere; which community

would provide the best chance for him to get away with a crime? An outsider could commit a

crime in a community filled with chaos, or gang violence, and take comfort in the fact that the
community, media, and police would assume, based on the history of the area, that the crime was

committed by a member of the community. You give away the power to control your fate, when

you engage in criminal activity. Committing crimes makes you and your community powerless.

Do you want to be weak?
                     DO YOU WANT TO WASTE YOUR LIFE?

       Crime is for suckers because it provides no love, no loyalty, and no future. What is love?

It is wanting to be there for someone no matter what and knowing that they want to be there for

you in the same way. Love is unconditional, it exists regardless of your position in life, it is there

when you need it the most, it doesn’t abandon you during your darkest hour.

       When you surround yourself with criminals, you are a crab in a bucket with other crabs.

People who love you want to see you soar, they want to help you be the best person you can be.

Criminals just want to take advantage of you, use you for their benefit, it’s not in their best

interest to see you do better than them. Criminals only see others as opportunities, even other

criminals.

       Many people get involved in crime to be accepted by gangs and other criminal

organizations. These are groups where love is a tool used to manipulate people. For example, it

is common for gang leaders to convince members to commit crimes for the “love of the

neighborhood.” Many gang members will not admit that they want to be loved. They will say

they value the respect of other gang members and want to be accepted by them, or make some

other excuse for being in a gang. But the truth is, they want people to love them and they are

often willing to commit any crime to be loved.

       A person who truly loves you would not want you to jeopardize your future. They would

not want you to do anything that could get you sent to prison. They would not want you to risk

your future by violating the law in any way. Think about the love a good father has for his son. A

person who has the power to influence another person steers them right, if they love them.

       When you engage in illegal activity or surround yourself with criminals, you are not

loving yourself. When you love yourself, you want the best for yourself. You want to be around
people who are positive and smart. You want to be around people who have goals that promote

the well-being of society. You want to be around people who protect you and your interests, not

people who put you at risk of prison or death by encouraging you to violate the law.

        There is no honor among criminals. When you commit crimes as a member of a gang or

criminal organization, there is no guarantee your partners in crime will have your back, when

you need them the most. Even if they seem loyal, there is always a chance they will stab you in

the back. They may do it for money, a chance to take your romantic partner, an opportunity to

avoid being locked up, or other selfish reasons. To be loyal, a person must be willing to follow

rules regarding a relationship. If a person is not willing to follow the laws of society, when the

consequences for breaking those laws can put them in prison, why would they respect a

relationship with you?

        Law-abiding people will not let you drag them down with you; so they won’t keep any

promises either, if you’re a criminal. When you’re involved in crime, people will be afraid to

trust you because they’ll see you as unstable. They’ll fear that you may turn against them at any

moment. They won’t be loyal to you because they will find it hard to believe that you would be

loyal to them. For example, if you have a history of theft, a law-abiding person will be afraid to

allow you free access to their property, no matter how close of a bond you feel you share with

them.

        People will turn you in, if they suspect you committed a crime. They will not give you

you’re reading, get rid of all distractions. We live in a society where we are connected to

technology 24/7. It can be hard to get away from it even for a short period of time. You need to

be able to place your full attention in what you’re reading to truly absorb the information. So,

turn off the tv, turn off your phone, put down your headphones, and spend some quality time

with a book.

       When you’re reading, you shouldn’t have to actively try to memorize anything, it should

just happen. When you have too many distractions, you have to force yourself to concentrate,
which causes you to waste energy that could be used to retain information. Try to put yourself in

a space where you can read a 100-300 page book within a month. Then try to get that time frame

down to two weeks. If you can read this amount within a week, you’ll be more than ready for

college. If not, you can still be successful, if you make enough time to read.

       Be prepared to take general courses. In college, about 70% of the classes you’re required

to take are related to your major. The rest are basic courses that every student has to take, such as

English, math, history, and science. When researching schools, take note of the type of general

courses are required to graduate. It is a good idea to brush up on your skills in the subjects that

you may find difficult. For example, let’s say you’ve never really earned good grades in English

class. Invest in yourself and buy a book that teaches you the basics of writing and proper

grammar.

       Perhaps you even have problems with Math or Science. There are self-teaching guides

that can help you become familiar with just about any subject. Before I started school, I bought

three self-teaching guides: Basic Physics by Karl F. Kuhn, Biology by Steven D. Gerber, and

Chemistry by Clifford Houk and Richard Post. Self-teaching guides are great because you can

learn at your own pace and practice the same type of problems that you will be assigned in

school. Do not be ashamed if you need to work on your skills in a certain area. No one is good at

everything. Take the time to learn, or at least become familiar with what you need to learn, so

you can do well in school.

       Learn to manage your time wisely. If you have not mastered the art of scheduling and

using your time properly, then now is the time to get it together. When you get to college, you

will feel like there are not enough hours in the day to do everything that you need to do. Maybe

you’ll have your school work, school activities, a job, and your social life to manage. Each
obligation will seem to demand all of your time. If you want to be successful and maintain your

sanity, it is imperative that you start developing ways to properly manage your time.

       Managing your school work will be the most difficult task, but if you have a plan to

succeed as a student, everything will fall into place. To stay grounded, it is best to plan your

assignments by the month, the week, the day, and the hour. For example, let’s say you will have

four classes when you start school. For the months of September and October, use one

assignment schedule that lists each class and the assignments/exams that are due each week. The

due dates for each assignment or exam should be listed on your schedule three or more days

before they’re actually due, to give yourself enough time. Plan to use another assignment

schedule to manage your weeks. Examples of assignment schedules are available at the end of

this chapter.

       Think about your financial situation. Many people drop out of college because they did

not take the necessary steps to make sure they could get the money needed to pay for it. Do not

be one of those people. Prepare a budget that lists the amount of money you’ll need, to go to the

school you want to attend. There is financial aid to cover tuition and some expenses, if you need

it.

       But what else are you going to need money for, while in school? Many people prefer to

have a car when they go to school, so they can get around easily. Now, if you don’t have

someone paying your bills for you, how are you going to make car payments or pay insurance?

Are you going to have a job? Will it be part-time or full-time? Will your job provide enough

income to pay your bills?

       While we’re on the subject of jobs, think about how having one will to affect your study

time. Will you truly be able to have a full-time job? Would it be more feasible to work on
campus or get a work study job? Then, there is the question of housing. Will you live on

campus? If not, how will you be able to pay your rent? Will you need a roommate? Could you

live with a parent, friend, or loved one who would help you save money, until you’re done with

school?

       These are the types of questions that you really want to have answered before you start

applying for schools. Don’t make the same mistakes that many others make, when it comes to

money. Living the life of a student means limiting yourself and limiting your bills. Know what

you’ll need and what you’ll have. Try to get more, but don’t live beyond your means.

       You should always have some money to fall back on, in case the money expected from a

job doesn’t come or you have to pay unexpected expenses. Focusing on what you need to pay for

can help you make the right decisions before you get an acceptance letter. Have a serious

conversation with people close to you about what they can do to help you. If they can’t do

anything, then you will have to depend on yourself. An example of a student budget is on the

                              WHERE SHOULD YOU GO?

       A degree is a degree in the eyes of many employers. Most are more interested in the

experiences and knowledge you gained while you were in school, as opposed to where you got

your education. This is not to say that where you go doesn’t mean anything at all. Some

employers actively recruit students that attend certain institutions. That’s why it’s important to

have a good idea of the direction you want to take your career, so you can make sure you’re

going to the best school for you.

       If you have a desire to work for a certain company, or get the best training for your

occupation, you have to find a college that will help you get where you want to be. Some schools

end up costing you a whole lot more than others because of a location or even just a title. Before

we get into the types of colleges you can go to, let’s take a moment to distinguish the difference

between private and public schools.

       A major difference between private and public schools is tuition. Private schools are

funded by individuals, institutions, and organizations. Public universities are funded by state

governments, since they are usually founded with assistance from the state. As a result, public

universities are usually less expensive than private ones. Private schools often have more funds

available for scholarships and grants.

       Another difference is class sizes and degrees. Private schools are typically smaller in

size, with some having less than 2,000 students. A smaller school results in a smaller number of

degrees to choose from. Larger universities can have over ten-thousand students resulting in

more choices. Depending on your career path, this can be a very important distinguishing factor.

Smaller student populations mean smaller class sizes. Private schools can have an average of

about 15-30 students per class. Public schools can have classes with up to 50-200+ students. If
you’re in a class that big, you may find it difficult to focus and learn. Or, you could be the

opposite and thrive in crowded environments.

       Sometimes, employers view graduates of private institutions more favorably than public

school graduates. Usually, it’s because the employer attended a private school. Sometimes, it’s

because they have been influenced by what they’ve heard about private schools. At a public

school, a graduate student or adjunct professor teaching a course is common. In most private

schools, classes are taught by tenured professors and individuals with doctoral degrees.

Although, the education received in private and public universities is similar, some people

perceive graduates of private schools as having received a better education. Some of the most

revered private schools are Harvard, Yale, Princeton, and Stanford – all schools you’ve probably

heard people on television fawning over.

       It’s important to realize that a name, even one that is highly respected and revered, is still

just a name. There is nothing that says you can’t go to a public school, like the University of

Alabama or the University of Georgia, and receive the same opportunities as someone who

attended a private school. Where you get your knowledge is less important than what you do

with it. Experience also goes a long way. For example, if you brought success to a major

company for several years and the only thing that separated you from a promotion was a

bachelor’s degree, it wouldn’t matter if you went to Harvard or a Massachusetts State University.

Sometimes, you just have to pay your dues because everyone else has.

       Now that you’ve been shown the difference between private and public schools, let’s

discuss the types of institutions you can attend, starting with community colleges. A community

college is a two-year school that provides an affordable way to get started with either a

bachelor’s degree or your career. Community colleges offer general courses and career-specific
programs that lead to certification or an associate’s degree in a field or trade (i.e. automotive

mechanics, business management, cosmetology, culinary arts, welding).

       Community colleges are a cost effective way to get your education. With tuition costs at

around $3,000 a semester, most people can graduate from a community college debt-free. Going

to a two-year school can you help you save money prior to transferring to a four-year school.

Many people transfer in and out of schools during their college career. Every time they transfer,

they have to send a transcript from their old school to their new school. Many times, the classes

taken at one university do not transfer to another, which means a student could be a junior at one

school but a freshman at another. The beauty of community colleges is that almost all of the

courses count towards a bachelor’s degree at another school.

       There are a few things to consider, if you decide to attend a community college. A lot of

students aren’t very involved in activities outside of the classroom. Although not the case at

every school, a community college may not provide the same “college experience” you may

have seen in movies. Many students only take a few classes a semester and work full-time jobs,

so they don’t have a lot of time to participate in school functions or socialize after class. There

also may be a large amount of “non-traditional students,” so, there may be noticeable age gaps

between classmates.

       The workload at many community colleges is not very heavy, compared to 4 year

schools. While this can be great news, if you have other commitments, it may leave you

unprepared for challenges in the work place or at four year universities. But, if you have a plan to

get where you want to be in the future, you can succeed by making use of any accredited

institution. No matter what type of college you attend, you should be capable of learning on your

own, not dependent on the institution itself to educate you.
       There are two types of four-year schools. Although the term college is used

interchangeably with university, the two are actually slightly different. The biggest distinction is

that a college tends to offer only bachelor’s degrees, whereas a university offers both

undergraduate and graduate studies. In order to be classified as a university, schools must have a

minimum of three graduate programs available to students. Some universities are called colleges

even though they have law schools, business schools, or other graduate programs. This is usually

because of tradition and nothing else.

       Colleges tend to have more subject-specific degree programs. Colleges are usually much

smaller than universities and focus on a particular area of study, such as the Liberal Arts (history,

English, social sciences, etc). Universities are made up of multiple colleges. You can attend a

university and be a part of a specific college on campus, such as the College of Liberal Arts,

College of Engineering, etc.

       When you go to a flag-ship university, you get the full “college experience.” Your time at

a traditional university can be some of the best years of your life, and for good reason. The

atmosphere is amazing. There are beautiful people to meet, smart people to talk to, and some of

the best cafeteria food on the planet!

       It’s a place where you can learn new things about yourself and also have fun while doing

it. You will get the chance to discover many of your hidden interests because there are so many

programs, groups, and events to get students involved. There are clubs for almost anything

students are interested in. If there isn’t a club that matches your interest, then you can probably

create it yourself. If you attend a school that has a prominent athletic team, you will have the

chance to experience some incredible moments. Students at flagship universities get the chance

to show pride for their school by showing out on game days.
        There are things to consider, if you choose to go to a four-year school. There is no

question that attending a college or university will cost more than a 2-year school. Depending on

the school you attend; tuition, room and board (if you live on campus), and other expenses could

cost you over $40,000.

        Realize that if you choose to pursue a bachelor’s degree, you might have to be in school

for a while, possibly longer than four years. If you don’t enjoy waiting a long time for things to

come to you, the idea of spending four years, as opposed to two or less, getting a degree may

sound unappealing. In some cases, it may take you even longer to get your degree. If you get

lazy, you could easily become a 5th or 6th year senior. But even if it takes that long, getting a

degree would still be one of the best things you could do to improve your life.

        Beware of for-profit schools. Whenever, you choose a school, make sure it’s accredited.

There are many schools that are simply diploma mills and schools that are just outright scams.

Research the history of every school you’re interested in attending. Find out if the schools are a

part of a university system with other legitimate schools. Find out if the courses offered by a

school will help you become certified in your career field. Read reviews left by former students

to determine if a school is legitimate. If you feel like a particular school is a scam, trust your

instincts.

        When deciding where to go to school, you must have a sense of direction. Don’t be an

aimless vessel, be the captain of your future. Take control. Know where you want to go and who

you want to be. College is simply the path; you have to choose where it will take you.
                                   HOW DO YOU GET IN?

        To get into college, you have to ask for permission. Depending on the school you want to

go to, it may take only a transcript for the admissions office to determine if you can attend or it

may take a few more items. You should always review the admission requirements and try to

predict the likelihood you would be accepted to a school. You should apply to multiple schools,

to ensure you get into at least one. Applying to college is simple, if you’re prepared.

        You should make a list of every school you want to go to and label them as “Schools I

really want to go to” or “Schools I’ll accept going to, if I can’t get into the ones I really like.”

Pick at least four. At least one should be the school of your dreams, the others should be the “just

in case” schools. Apply to at least one school from each category. If time and money allows,

apply to the other schools on your list. Doing it this way will ensure you have a decent range of

choices.

        There are a few ways you can apply for admission to college. Not every college offers

each of the following application methods. Contact the schools you’re interested in to determine

their admission process. Most colleges allow you to submit an application for Early Decision,

Early Action, Regular Admission, Rolling Admission, or Late Admission. If you apply for Early

Decision, you are allowed to apply early to one school. If you get accepted, you are required to

attend the school, regardless of any other applications you may have pending.

        Early Decision is an option beneficial to high school students who are ready to apply to

college before graduation. When you apply for this type of admission, you usually have no idea

what kind of financial aid you will receive until after you are accepted to the school. If you use

this application, and find out later you won’t receive enough money to pay your tuition, you will

be in a very difficult spot.
          Applying for Early Action admission still requires you to submit your applications

before regular admission deadlines. However, you don’t have to commit to a school before

knowing how much aid you’ll receive. This gives you the ability to compare financial aid

packages before committing to a specific school. If you choose this method, you’ll have to make

sure you get all of your applications in early, which means you’ll be very busy. Definitely not a

big deal when you’re building your future. There is also a single-choice early action application

similar to the early decision one; except, you can apply to other schools, just not as early action.

In other words, you get the same benefit as regular early action, but you only get to use it for one

school.

          With regular admission, you can apply to as many schools as you want. If you are

accepted to multiple schools and you want to compare them, you may not have enough time.

Many colleges want you to give them some type of confirmation several months before the

semester begins. If you research schools before applying to them, you can make a decision

quickly, when you get acceptance letters.

          Rolling Admission is by far the most convenient because it allows you to submit your

application anytime during a period of around six months. Students are usually accepted on a

first-come, first-serve basis. Therefore, the longer you wait, the least likely you are to get

accepted. Once all student slots are filled, your application may not be considered. So, if you

decide to apply to a rolling admission school, submit your application as early as you can.

          Every now and then, people make a last minute decision to attend college. Schools

understand that, and offer a late admission process. However, if all of the spots for students are

already filled, your application may not even get looked at. You should try to avoid applying for

school at the last minute, if you can. Though, it’s better to apply late than not at all.
        The common app is one application you can use for multiple schools. It greatly reduces

the amount of time you spend filling out paperwork. If you want to apply to multiple schools,

find out if there is a common app available. You may have to customize essays for each college

you apply to; but, the overall information should be similar.

        Filling out college applications may seem stressful, but as you go through the process

you’ll realize it’s all repetition. If an essay is required, try your best to make it flawless. Your

essay should be interesting, engaging, and most importantly answer the essay question(s) or

provide the information requested. Depending on your major, you may have to do a lot of

writing. Your application will be a way for schools to test your writing skills and determine how

well you follow directions. If you have to submit an essay, get an English teacher or someone

good at writing to look over your work.

        If a letter of recommendation is required, get one from a teacher, employer, supervisor,

mentor, pastor, coach, co-worker, or someone else in your community. Get someone who can

write about your character or work ethic. Ask for the letter at least two weeks before you want to

send it. That will give the writer ample opportunity to really think about what they want to say,

and they’ll appreciate the advanced notice.

        Take the required standardized test (ACT/SAT) on time and shoot for a decent score.

Tests are offered throughout the year. Usually, the final test dates are before regular admission

applications are due. Before applying, determine which test your preferred college requires. Give

yourself enough space to retake the test, if you don’t get the score you need. There are books and

digital programs released every year that can help you prepare for the ACT/SAT. Invest in the

tools you need to succeed, so you only have to take the test once.
       Most college applications have a fee. If you apply to multiple colleges, it can end up

being pretty costly. For example, if there are ten colleges you want to attend and the fee for each

one is $20, you’ll end up spending $200 to choose or be chosen by one school. If you contact a

school directly, they can tell you how to get an application fee waiver.

       To get money for college, you must submit a FAFSA (Free Application for Federal

Student Aid). The document is used to determine your financial situation and provide your

expected family contribution (EFC) towards your college expenses. Your EFC determines what

type of financial aid you’re eligible for. If you or your family doesn’t have a lot of money, your

EFC will be 0. That would make you eligible to receive the maximum amount of financial aid

made available by the federal government. There are four different types of aid available: loans,

grants, scholarships, and work study.

       Loans are borrowed funds that have to be paid back. Federal loans are available to just

about anyone who applies for the FAFSA. There are two main types of federal loans: subsidized

and unsubsidized. The only difference between the two is that the government pays your interest

while you’re in school on subsidized loans. Two other loans, the PLUS and Perkins are also

available from the government. PLUS loans are taken out by a student’s parent or guardian, if

you’re a dependent, and are contingent upon their credit score. The Perkins loan is only available

to students who desperately need it. All schools don’t offer it. Federal loans have an added

benefit of not having to be paid back until six months after you stop going to school. They also

give you the ability to make payments based on your income.

       Private student loans are based on your credit. You can get them from banks and other

organizations. Lenders may only reduce repayment amounts, if you can’t pay on time. This

means you have to mess up your credit to repay lower amounts each month.
       Grants are a form of financial aid you don’t have to pay back. Some schools give you a

grant if you’re from the state where it’s located. Other grants, such as the Pell Grant or FSEO

(Federal Supplemental Education Opportunity) Grant, are awarded based on your financial

situation. Colleges may award grants based on student-specifics such as race, gender, military

service, or disability status. For example, the TEACH (Teacher Education Assistance for College

and Higher Education) grant is available to students who agree to teach at a school in a low-

income area, after graduation. Check with your school’s financial aid office to see what type of

grants you may qualify for and how to apply.

       Scholarships are also a form of financial aid you don’t have to pay back. You can receive

college scholarships from your school of choice, companies, or other organizations. You can be

awarded a scholarship on the basis of your academic accomplishments, athletic achievements,

race, community activities, religion, and many other factors. You should research which

scholarships you’re qualified to receive and how to apply for them.

       Work Study is a program that allows you to work on campus and earn money for school.

Different schools have different policies for federal work study. Check with your school’s

financial aid office to learn about their process. Do not wait until the last minute to apply for

work study. It is awarded on a first-come, first-served basis, and positions fill up quickly.

       Really look at the financial aid awards you are offered. You do not want to owe an arm

and a leg when you get out of school. Try to pick a college that gives you a solid education and

is easy on your pockets. Make sure that it’s a school that you could be happy attending for at

least 4 years. Visit the campus. Talk to other students who go there. Make the decision that’s best

for you by taking into account the short and long term effects of your choice.
                                HOW DO YOU SUCCEED?

        When you’re accepted to a school you want to attend, the first thing you should do is to

go to orientation. Orientation is an event that gives you a better understanding of the school.

Some schools charge a fee for the session, while others add its cost to your tuition and fees bill.

Sometimes, it’s not mandatory to attend. However, even if it isn’t required, you should go. You

always want to be prepared for what’s to come. Attending orientation will give you a little bit of

a head start.

        At the event, you can get the first glimpse of your classmates and some of the prominent

people on campus like the dean, professors, etc. You should get to know some of the right

people, not only for your career plans, but for friendship as well. Just like in high school,

hanging with the wrong crowd can affect your studies. It could cause you to drop out of school

or be there longer than you anticipated. Associating with the right people can provide the

direction and assistance you may need to succeed.

        You may be able to register for classes directly after orientation. There will be advisors

there that can help you pick the right courses. Take advantage of their presence and use them.

Ask as many questions as you can think of. You can get suggestions on what classes to take to be

best prepared for your career. You can also make sure that you don’t take an unnecessary class.

You should try to register for classes as early as possible. Sometimes they fill up quickly. You

don’t want to be forced to stay in school for an extra year because you didn’t register on time.

        You should understand course loads before you register for classes. A full-time course

load is usually at least 12 credits. The maximum credits you can take per semester is usually 16

credits. Some schools allow you to take additional credits, but only after you receive approval

from your advisor. Most schools require that you earn at least 120 credits to graduate. To finish
school in four years, you’ll need to take at least 15 credits per semester (fall/spring). Most

courses are worth 3-4 credits each.

       I highly recommend that for your first semester, you take only 12 credits. Why? For

starters, adapting to the college environment may be difficult in the beginning. You don’t have

anyone micromanaging you or telling you when to study. You may be tempted to slack a little

bit. It happens to the best of us. It’s best to take twelve credits your first semester, so you have

time to get used to your new lifestyle. You’ll have plenty of time to make up the 3 credits you’ll

need later. You can either take a class in the summer or during a semester where you have a light

load of course work. Make sure you research the courses available in the summer. Some classes

aren’t offered during the semester.

       You can usually get the recommended course sequence for your major from the college’s

website or your academic advisor. It is up to you to find this information or determine how you

will earn the credits you need to graduate. Every single major can usually be completed within a

3 to 4-year period. There are a few exceptions, but most colleges set up their programs to be

completed in less than four years. So many people have to go to school for an extra semester or

more because they did not plan ahead. Try not to be one of those people. If you have to stay in

school longer because you change your major, that’s fine. I mean, it doesn’t matter if it takes you

10 years to graduate, as long as you graduate; but, try not to waste too much time.

       After you’ve selected your courses, you should get your school supplies and books. Get

your books as soon as possible. Do not wait and see if the teacher says you need them. Go to

your college bookstore or its website several weeks ahead of the semester start date. Find out

which books you’ll need for your classes.
       Some teachers are nice enough to send you their course syllabus before school starts. The

first page of the syllabus usually lists the books you’ll need for class. You can contact your

teachers directly and ask them which books you’ll need for the semester. If you write them an

email, make it short, polite, and to the point. If you have a good teacher, you’ll receive a

response within a week.

       College books are expensive, and you will most likely not need them after you graduate.

But, you need to get them to graduate. You do not want to be in a situation where you get behind

in your school work, or you don't understand something, just because you were too cheap to get

the book. After you figure out which books you need, you may not have to buy them from the

college bookstore. There may be more affordable ways to get what you need for class.

       You can get your textbooks and other required course material from websites such as

Amazon and Chegg or used book stores. These distributors usually have the books you need for

a lower price than college bookstores. The websites offer the same books for sometimes half of

the suggested retail price. Your teachers don’t care where you get your books from. They just

want you to have them. Just make sure you get the right book. The best way to do so is by using

the book titles and ISBN numbers to search for them. You may be able to get away with getting

the previous year’s edition of a book. Usually, new editions only have minor changes. Ask your

teacher if you can get the previous year’s addition and still do well in class.

       You can buy or rent the books you need for class. When you buy the books, you own

them for life. You can use them in the future, if you need them, or sell them back to the

bookstore or elsewhere. Kind of like a car, books depreciate in value as soon as you use them or

fail to return them for a refund. Rented books are a cheaper option, but you have to return them
on time and in good condition. If you don’t return them by the rental date, or if you return them

severely damaged, you’re charged a fee.

       Electronic books, or eBooks, are usually cheaper than print books. Being able to access

your books on a computer or tablet can be a convenient option; you can quickly switch from

your assignment to your book to get your school work done. If you get eBooks, you’ll have

instant access to them after you pay, which means you don’t have to wonder when your

                                YOU HAVE THE POWER

       Many people never consider the true importance of commerce and its impact on their

daily lives. They go to work, get a paycheck, buy stuff, and repeat the cycle. Somehow, magical

forces create markets, prices, and ultimately – living standards. Unfortunately, this is especially

the case in America, where consumers are lured into the mirage created by big companies. When

buying a cell phone, most people don’t think too deeply about how their purchase impacts the

maker, distributor, and retailer of the device. Many people believe their contributions as

customers are insignificant, because there are many people who surrender their dollars to

companies.

       By not actively seeing the whole picture, you lower your self-worth as a consumer and

make your money weaker in your hands than it is in the accounts of businesses. You convince

yourself that you have no power over the polices and well-being of companies that take money

from your community. However, as a consumer, you have a lot of control over the direction a

company takes, if it wants your money. You’re a very important part of the economy. Without

you, and the millions of people who share your tastes and needs, many companies would fail.

       For example, let’s say you stop spending money at your local corner store because of an

issue or affordable option elsewhere. You convince your relatives, friends, and neighbors to also

stop spending money at the local corner store. Each of them convince people they know to make

purchases elsewhere. Now imagine this process repeating until half of the neighborhood refuses

to buy from the corner store. Sales will drop rapidly and the store owner will have to find a way

to make the community happy, if they want your dollars.

       At this point, the power of consumers is felt. If more people in the neighborhood keep

their money away from the corner store, it will have to go bankrupt or out of business. With no
demand for their products or services, a company fails. Demand is made up of people and their

communities.

        In the past, economists believed that producers created their own markets. In other words,

companies decided what types of products and services people would be able to buy and people

bought them. Nowadays, consumers have more control. Every year, companies create products

that people want, need, or “demand.” For the most part, businesses don’t offer things they want

to sell, they offer things they can sell.

        Better education and technology has made it possible for consumers as a whole to have

great power in the economy. People used to have to depend on companies to provide the right

information about products. Today, there are many groups and organizations that exist to make

sure people aren’t lied to about products. You can go online and learn about how any product is

made. You can also learn about a company’s service from thousands of people.

        The fact that you have so much power as a consumer is the reason most companies invest

heavily in marketing. Every commercial you watch is an attempt by a company to take away a

little bit of your power. You should know what you want and what you need, but companies try

to control your desires and decisions through marketing.

        Most people are heavily influenced by marketing. They don’t pay for what they need;

they buy what they’re told they need. Their tastes are created by the companies that desire their

money. For example, there are many celebrities that endorse products. The people who buy the

endorsed products usually only do so because they are “told to” by their favorite celebs. They

end up paying more for the product than they would have, if they truly wanted it, because the

hefty cost to market the product to them is added to the price tag.
          Marketing helps businesses bring in revenue from all over the world. The most successful

companies have great influence over the economy. They are able to make enough money to

promote their goods and services to millions of people. These companies create oligopolies,

markets dominated by only a few businesses. For example, there are over 100 wireless service

providers in the U.S., but your friends and family probably have cell phone plans with about

three of them.

          The top companies in oligopolies accumulate power so great that governments actually

seek their favor, often times at the expense of employees and communities. An example of an

oligopoly, on a more down to earth level, would be grocery stores in low-income neighborhoods

located near the bus line. In these areas, many people rely on public transportation to travel.

Stores closest to the bus line are positioned to get more customers than stores further away. If

there were two stores close to the bus line and six stores far away from the bus line, the two

stores could dominate the market. If the two stores properly used their advantage, the

neighborhood’s grocery market would be an oligopoly that serves the best interest of local

customers.

          Companies can use marketing to convince people that they would be better off being

worse off. If two of the stores furthest away from the bus line had millions of dollars to invest in

commercials and billboards, they could create an oligopoly that puts customers at a

disadvantage. They could market their stores as “better” than the closer options. Residents may

be convinced by the ads to use more expensive means of transportation to shop at the “better”

stores.

          Because advertising costs are added to the price tag of a good or service, customers

literally pay companies to manipulate them. If a large portion of the money earned by a company
was reinvested in the communities of customers, then marketing could be viewed as a way to

stimulate the economy. People are motivated, and have the means to work, when goods and

services make them happy, improve their life, and improve their community. Unfortunately,

many companies could care less about improving the communities that help increase their

wealth.

          Businesses can decide what types of products and services you buy, if you allow them to

control your spending. Communities suffer when they allow corporations, that do not care about

their needs, to dictate their lifestyles. When the dictators are rich and the communities are poor,

the consumers participate in self-destructive activities. They are strangled by the companies they

pay to manipulate them. They feed into a mechanism that puts them deeper in poverty.

          For a community to improve, or at least survive, it must be self-sufficient. The money

that leaves your hands must be reinvested in the area. The money-flow diagram (Figure 1) is a

visual example of how a self-sustaining community should operate. On the diagram are

households, resource markets, businesses, and product markets.

          Households are the people who live in a community. Resource markets are made up of

the members of households who are able to make money. For example, in a family of four where

there are two children under the age of 9, an unemployed father with a degree in engineering,

and a mother who works full-time as a retail store manager, the parents are members of the

resource market, while the kids are just members of the household. Businesses hire employees

from the resource market to create, promote, and sell goods & services in the product market to

households.

          In a self-sustaining economy, the community feeds itself. When the resource market is

skilled or educated, households can make and spend money. When households are prosperous,
businesses can sell more products and hire more people from the resource market. When parents

make a lot of money, they can invest in their children’s education or training to add future

workers to the resource market. The value of products increase alongside the capabilities of

workers; smarter people create better products.

       Let’s imagine that the money-flow diagram represents your body. The arrows represent

“food” (deoxygenated blood, oxygenated blood, etc.) and the boxes represent your “organs”

(heart, lungs, etc.) that need food to work. If your heart doesn’t feed your lungs by pumping

blood into the organ, your body will die. If your lungs don’t feed your heart, by providing it

oxygen-rich blood, your body dies. As a consumer, you can feed yourself or feed someone else.

If you feed someone else without feeding yourself, you die.

        Every company needs three things to conduct business: labor, capital, and land. Workers

get paid wages and salaries for their labor. The money people use to start and invest in

companies is called capital. The place where a company operates is land. The main reason

business owners invest their capital in labor and land is to make profits. They don’t go into

business to break-even; their goal is always to earn more than they spend or owe. Employees and

land-owners, on the other hand, often go to work to survive.

        Most employees earn enough to pay for their living expenses. This is usually because the

cost of living a luxurious lifestyle is high, but that’s how everyone wants to live. Many people

live by the mantra, “Spend it, if you got it.” So, most people simply live beyond their means to

try to feel like they’re at the top.

        For example, most people don’t want to live below their means in terms of housing. If a

person makes $3000 a month, they may find it more appealing to live somewhere that costs

$1900 a month than a place that costs $750. There would be obvious differences between the two

types of housing. The cheaper place would most likely have more crime, pollution, and building

issues than the more expensive one. So, the average person would probably just spend the $1900/

month to live a good life and spend the remaining $1200 on bills and leisure. Not many people

view saving money as a priority; so, no matter how much they make, they always end up

working to survive.

        No business truly owns any portion of the earth. Every company is allowed to operate in

a location by the citizens who live there. Even if a company buys land and constructs buildings,

it is still required to give back to the area where it takes up space. With that being said, land-
owners are not only the individuals or groups that rent out land, buildings, and property. The

communities that allow businesses to operate in their area are land-owners as well.

       When a company owns the land where business is conducted, the community still expects

it to pay rent in other ways. When companies do business in a community, whether that

community be a town, city, state, or nation, it is required to pay taxes to support the area that

allows it to exist. There are also other costs that the community expects the company to pay.

Residents expect the business to hire people from the community, especially when they purchase

its goods and services. They also expect the business to help the community in times of trouble

or need. For example, if a company builds a billion-dollar business in an area, and a hurricane

strikes, community members will expect some help from the company.

       Let’s say a sneaker company named “Dairy” arrives in your city. Dairy buys land in your

city to build a production facility and retail store. The company hires all of its employees from

your city, but uses legal loop-holes to avoid paying any taxes. Also, none of the profits are

reinvested locally. In this case, we have the following three money flows: salaries, rent, and

profits. The flow of salaries goes to the local community, since all of the workers live there. The

other two flows leave the community (Figure 2).

       In the short term, people in your city will be happy to have jobs. They’ll be able to buy

more stuff. Since people in your city love Dairy sneakers, the company gets back about 40% of

the money it pays workers. The quality of life increases in your community, for a while.

       In the long-run, as the years go by, your city gets dependent on the sneaker company. The

possibility of lay-offs and low sales become constant threats to the livelihood of residents. If the

company fails, the quality of life in your area falls below where it was before Dairy moved to

town. Even if the company is successful, there is always the possibility that the company might
leave the city to go elsewhere for lower production costs, better talent, or higher sales

opportunities.

       To keep jobs in the city, the local government may try to appease the company by

overlooking certain tax obligations, questionable business practices, and violations. The cost of

these benefits will be paid from the workers’ pockets instead of by the company as “rent.” If the

costs are high, residents may end up living worse than before.

                           PROTEST WITH YOUR MONEY

       Households are the most powerful force in a community. The most important resource in

an economy, labor, is provided by households. The quality of labor available can make or break

a community. A place with skilled and educated households can survive and prosper without a

lot of natural resources. A place with a lot of natural resources can fail when its workforce is lazy

or unproductive. Households buy back what they produce in the product market, paying with

wages and salaries they receive from businesses. Companies are made of people from

households. Businesses are financed by households that pay for their purchases with profits.

       When you systematically refuse to buy goods and services from a business, its profits

quickly decrease (Profit = Sales – Costs). When a company keeps losing money, it can either go

out of business or go bankrupt. If a company operates within or markets to your community, you

can influence its behavior. You can stop it from abusing its economic or social power.

Businesses behave badly when their actions hurt residents or do not help the community grow.

This is the case when a company:

       a) Doesn’t hire people from the community

       b) Discriminates against specific groups

       c) Provides bad service or products to specific groups

       d) Pollutes the environment

       e) Does not support the community during difficult times

       When at least one of these situations happen in your community, you should protest with

your money. You protest with your money by refusing to buy from a company for a justifiable

reason. You must have a reason that motivates other people to join you in protest. For example,
if you don’t shop at a store because it doesn’t hire members of the community, you must be able

to clearly communicate your stance to be heard and followed.

       It may be difficult to gain the numbers for an effective protest, if you’re the only one

aware of the bad behavior of a company. If you find yourself in such a situation, consider the

concept of six degrees of separation. In one of his short stories, Frigyes Karinthy proposed the

idea that, “everyone and everything is six or fewer steps away, by way of introduction, from any

other person in the world.” Trust the people you know to get the word out about a business

behaving badly. Meet new people, and get them to spread the word. You should gain the

numbers to start a proper protest if you’re sincere and passionate about the situation. Providing

convincing evidence of the company’s bad practices is the best way to win people over.

       You can protest by simply refusing to buy what the company sells or you could buy from

its competitor. For example, if the item or service is something you can go without, then not

buying it will not severely impact your life. If the product is something you need to survive, like

groceries, then shopping at the competitor of the store you are protesting would be a good move.

You should choose the best option for you and your community. The goal is to hit the business

where it hurts.

       You must be able to show the company that its behavior is making it lose money. To do

so, you could provide receipts of your spending at its competitor or testimony from fellow

protestors. If the company corrects its mistakes, you can stop the protest. You have the power to

decide if a company stays in business, shuts down, or goes bankrupt.

       It is possible that the community could divide into two camps: people wanting to protest

and people not wanting to protest. The latter category could consist of people working at the

boycotted company. The community should be united. The protest will not work, if the majority
of residents are against it. The majority should decide the fate of the community. Support should

be provided to residents who suffer direct losses as a result of the protest.

       If the company being protested goes bankrupt or leaves the community, lost jobs and

money flows will have to be replaced. If the community presents profitable opportunities, new

investors should quickly fill the void. If it has or can get the money required, the community can

strike out on its own and finance a new business. Either way, because of its willingness to take a

stand, your community should be able to place itself in a more profitable situation after

protesting a bad company.
                             BORROW WITH A PURPOSE

       Capital is necessary to develop economic activity. Capital is the foundation of a business.

It could be in the form of skills, talent, knowledge, money, natural resources, property,

technology, etc. In the past, people used the barter system in commerce. Blacksmiths would use

their skills and knowledge of the trade as capital to start businesses. Farmers would use their land

and animals to start businesses. Both groups would originally start out creating items to provide

for their family or local areas. They would quickly realize that they could get more stuff by

exchanging the stuff they make for stuff from other creators. Pretty simple, huh?

       Nowadays, the main source of capital is money. Often times, you can’t start a business

without having enough money. Most businesses don’t start out actually having the money they

need to open up shop. They depend on loans or outside investments. Companies pay a price for

the money they borrow called interest. When interest rates are high, borrowing money is

expensive and the costs are usually passed on to consumers by making products more expensive.

       The Federal Reserve works to control interest rates. It does so to provide a stable

monetary system. When it wants to stimulate the economy, it acts to drive down interest rates,

which makes getting loans cheaper. When loans are cheaper, more individuals and organizations

try to get capital to fund or grow businesses. This can lead to increased employment and

economic growth.

       A community needs access to convenient sources of credit to be financially self-sufficient

(Figure 4). Residents should have access to a cooperative bank, such as a Credit Union. These

banks give communities the power to establish interest rates favorable to the area. Cooperative

banks can help a community grow, when the Federal Reserve institutes policies not in the area’s

best interest. For example, Credit Unions tend to offer higher savings interest rates and lower
loan interest rates than big banks. This means, when you deposit money in your neighborhood’s

cooperative bank, you’ll earn more money than if you were to put the money in a commercial

bank. It also means that money is cheaper to borrow from a cooperative. These institutions are

usually better for communities because their growth depends on the success of the community;

whereas commercial banks can pop up in any place, at any time.

       When you use credit as a consumer, you should try to only borrow from sources that

reinvest the interest in your community. Interest is a money flow; it is similar to the salary and

wages flow on the money-flow diagram. It is what lenders earn for allowing you to use their

money. The money you borrow and the money you pay to borrow money should not leave your

community.

       You should never use credit to survive, it should always be used to invest in something

profitable. If you don’t have the money you need to buy something you simply want, you don’t

deserve it. If you just want a fancy car to look nice, and by the time you get done paying for it

you break-even or lose money, then taking out a loan for the purchase is a foolish decision.
       However, if getting a loan for the fancy car leads to profits after you pay the car off, then

it’s a good decision. For example, let’s say there’s a car you want and it costs $30000. You get a

car loan which will result in you paying $45000 for the car at the end of four years. During that

time frame, if you don’t use the car to bring in at least $15,000 in profits, all you’ve done is slave

away to look fly and feel good. Now to some people, looking fly and feeling good is the ultimate

accomplishment in life.

       However, to be of use to yourself and your community, you must act with purpose. If the

interest you pay goes back to your community, you simply do a disservice to yourself, when you

do not profit from borrowing money. If the interest you pay does nothing to bring you profits and

also leaves your community, you do a disservice to yourself and your community. It doesn’t

matter if you use credit cards or take out loans, you should always use the money you borrow to

build your community.

       Be wary of lenders in general. Often times, it is more profitable for them if you are

unable to repay them. That’s why it’s important to never become dependent on credit for

survival. In your haste to get your hands on cash, you may not read the fine print. Though, there

are consumer groups that try to look out for citizens, there are also lawyers on the payroll of

lenders. These lawyers spend 40 hours a week looking for ways to legally screw people. Always

make sure you know the true cost of the money you borrow.
                             SUPPORT YOUR NEIGHBORS

       Being self-sufficient doesn’t mean isolating yourself. Every community has to manage

external relationships or risk being overpowered by outside forces. This is even truer when

talking about small communities. In the past, communities developed relationships based on their

specialized skills. For example, if one village was good at winemaking while its neighbor had

large flocks of sheep, they would build a relationship based on the trade of these goods. A barter

system fueled by the exchange of wine for mutton and fleece would bond the two communities.

Everything else, such as a joint defense force to protect both towns from outside invaders, would

be an extension of the local economy.

       For a mutually-beneficial relationship to exist between communities, one has to be self-

sufficient. If your community is dependent on outsiders, there is little it can contribute to another

area. You can’t teach someone how to walk, when you’re still crawling. A self-sufficient

community can lead by example; a dependent community must follow. When a community is

self-sufficient, it has nothing to lose in providing assistance to its neighbor(s).

       So, how do you know if your community is self-sufficient? A self-sufficient community

has enough resources to avoid being completely dependent on government funds, large

corporations, and commercial institutions. The local economy is less affected by economic

downturns than the national economy. For example, during the Great Recession, a lot of people

lost their homes. Even though many of the people claimed to be “homeowners,” they were

actually tenants paying rent to banks that were basically their landlords. These “homeowners”

viewed as a group could be considered an example of a dependent community. A collection of

people who own their homes outright could be an example of a self-sufficient community.
       A self-sufficient community has the means to support residents in need, if the

government can’t. People can get jobs or assistance funded by the efforts and contributions of

community members. If large corporations decide to abandon the area, local businesses can

absorb part of the negative effects. The community is prepared for natural disasters and can

react quickly to unexpected events as a result of its strong internal economic relationships.

       For a community to be self-sufficient, its money flows must be internalized. It must have

an internal financial market that operates without high dependence on external funds. It is one

thing to get a little start-up money from outside sources, but eternal dependence on the providers

of capital or goods is a problem. An area must be able to feed and save itself to properly stand on

its own.

       It is very important for self-sufficient communities to “share the wealth,” rather than

ignore situations in struggling areas nearby. The well-being of your neighbors can be measured

by the productivity of their local economies. Areas supported by money-flows that don’t travel to

your local economy are “external markets.” When self-sufficient, external markets do not

significantly impact your community.

       When external markets struggle, they put the stability of your community at risk. When

there are no jobs in one area, many people leave and look for places where opportunities are

available. Competition for jobs increases, when there are many applicants. Because people

coming from poorer areas are desperate for work, they are willing to take lower pay. Their

desperation can lead to lower wages in the community. Though, the situation can be beneficial to

businesses in the area, it can lower the quality of life. It can also cause strife between existing

residents and new ones.
       Impoverished communities are more likely to breed criminals than self-sufficient

communities. When some people are in poverty, they feel like they have nothing to lose. This

can lead to the commission of crimes not only within their own community, but also in more

prosperous areas. If one town has high unemployment rates, while the neighboring town has

mansions, the prestigious area will be targeted by criminals. The level of crime in an area is

linked to the success of its economy. Ignoring the poverty of your neighbors can lead to a spill-

over effect that lowers your community’s quality of life.

       Helping your neighbors succeed can increase profits in your community. The easiest way

to expand a business is to reach out to nearby areas. By doing so, you save a lot of money. You

don’t have to spend much time learning about the local culture, economic figures, sales forecasts,

business environment, or competition because you’ll already have a lot of information about

your neighbors, because they’re your neighbors.

       Cooperating with your neighbors could be mutually beneficial. It could provide

opportunities for the struggling area and ways to lower the cost of goods sold by businesses in

your community. For example, let’s say your community sells peanut butter. Companies order

crushed peanuts from a different state, because they don’t have access to machinery that can

crush peanuts. Meanwhile, a nearby community sells almond milk and uses the same type of

machine to crush almonds as needed to crush peanuts. The economy of your neighbor goes

through some hard times. Your community decides to do business with them by buying some of

their inventory to sell almond milk locally. Because they appreciate the support, your neighbor

agrees to produce crush peanuts for your community at a price 50% less than the current cost.

Both areas prosper through the relationship.

revenue to help support the entire area. All communities in an area are responsible for paying

taxes to help the government build and maintain schools, roads, etc. If a community is

struggling, it is not able to pay its fair share. When one area can’t pay, it increases the burden on

neighboring communities.
