another in the immediate future.

of people who value flexibility or creativity above almost everything

that if anyone is going to tell them what to do, it may as well be

         Several years ago I was discussing ways to build the new

business of a client named Pete. I asked him a standard question:

“What is your vision for the business, say, in five years?”
         “I want to have 2,000 locations around the world,” he

replied without missing a beat.

put his company in the top .5% of all franchises.”

to accomplish that vision.

answer revolves around creative vision. I don’t mean “creative

visualization,” whereby you sit on a yoga mat with legs crossed and

imagine yourself in a plush office overlooking New York’s Fifth

Avenue to make it all come true. I mean developing a precise

vision of how you, the entrepreneur, will bring your company into

probably the most fun I’ve ever had working for someone else. In

       Goals are important because of what you, your business,

have if you hadn’t “reached for the stars.”

                         Pricing Pitfalls
