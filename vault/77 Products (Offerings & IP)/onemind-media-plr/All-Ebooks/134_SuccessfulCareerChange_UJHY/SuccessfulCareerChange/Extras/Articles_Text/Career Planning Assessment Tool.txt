Career Planning Assessment Tool 

If you are just beginning the planning phases of your career, you can benefit greatly from a few assessment tools made available to you both online as well as at career development centers.  The last thing you want to do is get heavily involved in a job that you hate before you have really taken the time to see if that career is right for you.  That’s why a huge part of career planning involves assessment tools that can match you with a job that matches your personality, your abilities, and your talents.

One career planning assessment tool you can use can be found many places online and for free.  Career assessment tests are available all over the Internet.  You will be asked a variety of questions that have to do with various job tasks.  Questions could be worded in this way.  

Would you rather:

Write a business plan
Participate in a brainstorming session
Formulate a project budget

You will then be asked to choose the option that most interests you as well as the option that least interests you.  Based on the responses you give to these questions, the program will then present you with a list of possible careers that will fit best with your interests.  You might be surprised what you will find with this career planning assessment tool because some of the careers you will probably know you have already been interested in and others you may have never considered.

Another great assessment tool for career planning is to take a personality test.  You can also find these online for free in many places as well.  You will answer some questions – just like with a career assessment test – and find out what type of personality you have.  If the test finds that you are generally an outgoing person with an overwhelming need to take care of others, you might be able to choose a career that will best utilize those strengths such as social work or guidance counseling.

The career planning assessment tools that are at your disposable are available in many places.  Of course, the Internet is a great place to begin, but you might also want to go to your local community college or a local university to find the tools you need.  Check and see if a local employment office offers any of these career planning assessment tools as well.  You may be surprised at the resources that are available to you if you take the time to look.  After all, this is your career we’re talking about – you might as well know that it is the right career for you.
