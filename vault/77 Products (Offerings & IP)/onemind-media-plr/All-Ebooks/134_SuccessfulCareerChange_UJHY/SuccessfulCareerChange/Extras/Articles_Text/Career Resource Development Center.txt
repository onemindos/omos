Career Resource Development Center

There is a wonderful place online that can be a great tool in making a career change or choosing a career – the Career Resource Development Center.  The website is located at www.crdc.info and provides many, many tools for the person who needs advice and guidance in their career selection.  They have been in business for over forty years.  The Career Resource Development Center has carried out its mission by providing pre-employment training and needed services to the underserved population since 1966.

Physically, the Career Resource Development Center is located in San Francisco where they conduct classes and seminars for people interested in career development and who want to take full advantage of the center’s resources.  However, their website is a great place to go to as well.  They are a non-profit, private, licensed post-secondary institution that provides services to a unique part of America’s population - immigrants, refugees, displaced workers, and other under-represented parts of the population.

The Career Resource Development Center began when the founders noticed a need for education for immigrants and people for whom English is not their first language.  The Center began providing classes to immigrants and refugees from other countries, but their business quickly expanded into displaced workers and groups such as single mothers with little to no job experience and divorced women who had never worked before.

The classes provided by the Career Resource Development Center include hospitality service training, computer and business skills training, art school, and citizenship classes designed to help immigrants become United States citizens.  They also offer English as a second language courses to help non-English speaking people become more fluent at the language of America.

A majority of their graduates work in the hospitality and service industry, but the Career Resource Development Center also provides support services that can help them advance in their jobs and become more integrated into American life.  Many people who have graduated from the Career Resource Center have realized career success just because this valuable tool was made available to them.

For some people, the idea of having a valuable and fulfilling career seems out of reach and just a pipe dream.  This is especially true for people new to the country.  The Career Resource Center fills an ever-widening gap in the employment sector for people who thought they could never have a career.  Their resources have made the dreams of hundreds of people come true and put them into careers that make them productive members of society and increase their self-esteem along the way!
