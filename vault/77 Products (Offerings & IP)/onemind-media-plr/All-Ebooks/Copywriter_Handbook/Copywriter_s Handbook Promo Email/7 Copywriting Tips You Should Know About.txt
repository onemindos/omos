SUBJECT: 7 Copywriting Tips You Should Know About

Hey [[firstname]],
Copywriting is the most essential skill of all marketers. It is basically your ability to turn words into gold.
Today, I’m going to share with you 7 copywriting tips which will help you increase the conversions of your sales copy.

1) Start your copy with bold, eye catching headlines.
2) Always try to trigger emotions of your readers. Now, its all about emotional selling points.
3) Always use “You” in your sales copy instead of “I”.
4) Only use visual graphics where relevant and when necessary
5) Align all text to the left, it improves read through rate
6) Every now and then, throw in a couple of sub headlines to catch the attention of “scrollers”
7) Handle objects by using FAQS, Post Scripts and testimonials

In short, these tips will help you boost your conversions and improve readership. Remember, continuous effort and practice in copywriting will help you to write faster and better, so keep practicing your writing!
