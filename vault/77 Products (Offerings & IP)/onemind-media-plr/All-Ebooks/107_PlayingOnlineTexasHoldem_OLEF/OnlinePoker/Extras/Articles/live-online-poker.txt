What To Expect In A Live Online Poker Event

For many poker beginners, this card game can be very hard to learn. Many online poker sites give new users free download software programs that can teach them the poker game. There is even a poker school in some online poker sites that give out the tips on how to improve the users game.

Soon enough, after learning all the basic skills of poker, you will have to go to a live online poker event. What is a live online poker? Well, as you are only a trainee, if you have been playing alone in front of the computer then you are playing an offline poker game.

A live online poker event is where you log on to your online poker room and join thousands of other poker players in tournaments hosted by the website. Why is it called a live online poker event? It is live because you are playing against other people from different places in real time.

What is exciting about a live online poker game is you can actually put your knowledge and skills to the test. Betting a computer is one thing, especially if it is pre-programmed. Trying to outwit another human being in a live online poker event, you now have a competition.

There are different types of live online poker tournaments that you can join in. Over 60,000 players try to beat each other in these live online poker rooms. Finding the live online poker tournament that matches your current budget and skill level could helo you eran more winning.

The first live online poker tournament type is a freeroll tournament. This is a multi-table tournament where more than 10 participants can play. Multi-table means that you can play on different tables without having to tear yourself off an ongoing play. Freeroll in freeroll tournament means there are no entry fees needed to play in this live online poker game.

The second live online poker tournament type is the guaranteed tournament. Guaranteed Tournaments are also multi-table tournaments played with more than 10 participants. Guaranteed means there is a guaranteed prize pool amount, regardless the number of entrants in the game. 

Unlike the freeroll, guaranteed tournaments require a buy-in and an entry fee. This type of live online poker game has a guaranteed amount for the prize pool, which is always bigger than the 10 combined entrants value. This makes sure that the prize is big even if only a few entered the live online poker game.

Some guaranteed tournaments provides for "rebuys" and "add-ons" of the game chips.. This means that if you lose all of your chips, you can "re-buy” your entry into the live online poker tournament and continue to play. An add-on is offered at the first break. Add-ons give those who have a short stack a chance to "top up" their chips at the same original buy-in amount. Some tournaments have "double add-on" which will give you twice as many chips as the starting chip count, but for the same cost of an original buy-in.

Another kind of live online poker tournament is a freezeout tournament. It means there are no “rebuys,” so once you lose all your chips; you are out of the game.

The fourth live online poker tournament available to users is the sit and go tournament. Sit and Go tournaments are played on a single table, but it can also be played as a multi-table, depending on what the participants agree upon.

Sit and Go live online poker tournaments have specific buy-in and entry fee amount. This will form the prize pool money. At the end of the game, this is split up among the top three ranked winners and can follow a payout schedule in claiming the pool amount.

There will be times when you will encounter some problems, like being confused by the rules of the game. Some online poker rooms provide telephone support that you can call and ask about some clarifications.

Whatever live online poker tournament you play on, just remember that you are still a novice. Just feel your way into the game and everything will be fall into place. Who knows? With constant practice, you just might be the next World Series Poker Champion!
