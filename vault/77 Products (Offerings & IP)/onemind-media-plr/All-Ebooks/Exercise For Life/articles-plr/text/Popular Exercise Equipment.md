# Popular Exercise Equipment

The market for exercise equipment is huge. There is such a variety of equipment to choose from that making a choice can be a difficult decision.  Below we will discuss some of the popular exercise equipment available today. 
The fitness of stability ball is a popular choice because for one reason the price is right. For less than $20 you can pick up a large sized ball. Another reason is that the stability ball is versatile and it is not as easy to use as you might think.  You should try performing sit ups on the ball!
The ball can make exercises like sit ups and push ups less stressful on your joints. Allowing you to actually perform these exercises for once! If you have trouble doing an exercise like a squat then you can try placing the stability ball behind your back in the lower back region while standing against a wall. Then stand with your feet about shoulder width apart and push your bottom towards the wall while going into a squat.  If you knees turn outwards place a rolled up towel in between your knees and concentrate on squeezing your knees together as you go down. 
Many stability balls come with a DVD or a workout poster so you know exactly what exercises to perform with your new ball.
