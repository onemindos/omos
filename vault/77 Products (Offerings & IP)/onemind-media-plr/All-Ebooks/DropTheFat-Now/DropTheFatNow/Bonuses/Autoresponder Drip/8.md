Subject:

Need some motivation today?

Body:

You know what I find to be the best motivation?

Hearing stories about successful weight loss
journeys. There is something very powerful
about hearing about success from someone
who was in the same position as you.

Equally motivating for me is hearing stories 
from people who are battling weight loss.  It
is nice to know you aren't alone in your struggle.

That is why I subscribe to some weight loss blogs.

Make sure to check these sites out and bookmark
any of them that you find especially motivating.
