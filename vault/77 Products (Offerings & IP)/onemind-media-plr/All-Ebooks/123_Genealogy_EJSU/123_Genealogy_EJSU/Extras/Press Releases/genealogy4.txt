Genealogy is a growing interest in many public and private fields.  For many individuals, it has even become a serious quest. Marion, Iowa (PRWeb) February 6, 2007 – Genealogy has been practiced for thousands of years and yet, it was only in the 1970s when it enjoyed a resurgence.
