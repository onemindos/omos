# No Fees Involved: Where to Find Free Sites for your Genealogy Search

Genealogy is the study of one's family history. It is a documentation of important facts about your ancestors. You are to explore on the date of their births, the places they lived, the persons they married, and the children they had. Most significantly, you will be familiarized on what generation you belong on your family tree.  

Oral history is the initial and most common step undertaken by anyone who wants to research about his family lineage. However there are times that interviews with your relatives seem insufficient. With this regard, you ought to seek more information aside from oral history. 

These days, there are several free ways to search "who's who" in your family lineage. 

The internet ranks first. It is being widely utilized for genealogical search. There are paid and free websites in order for you to embark on your genealogical search. For free genealogy search websites, becoming a member is recommended. By doing so, you will be able to enjoy privileges intended for members only. Additionally, you may opt to subscribe to an online newsletter. Online genealogy newsletter contains stories to inspire you with your undertaking. It also contains tips and guidelines to help you with your research. 

Joining a genealogy chat room is another way for you to learn more about your family lineage. Although, the chances may seem arbitrary for you to find your ancestors, there is a high possibility for you to gain insights and strategies to help you with your empire through your chat mates. 

There are also message boards in the internet which focuses in genealogy. You can obtain ample of genealogic information on message boards. 

Internet-aside, there are still other locations which you can search for free such as government agencies or institutions.  

Public libraries are free to anyone who wishes to seek data and facts. Public libraries are home for different reading materials where many announcements are being publicized such as obituaries and marriages. The said pieces of information can help you gather facts about your family history drastically.  

History of your locality can also be found at most public libraries. Such reading elements can help you picture how your ancestors lived during their days. These days, you can now enjoy visiting some libraries in the comfort of your home through the internet. Some libraries have their own websites.  

Government agencies are also helpful sites to visit to help you with genealogy data gathering. There are agencies which can help you with your data gathering which will cost you no charges. Important documents can be gathered in government agencies such as records of birth, marriage, and death. Legal records such as immigration, naturalization, and law-related cases may be obtained from such agencies. Additionally, the said agencies can also guide you to locate your ancestors from military and land records.  

The National Archives or NARA is a good institution to locate genealogical information.  

Money is not everything. Some people may readily dole out money for genealogy research. However for those who are on a budget, then you can temporarily settle with the above-mentioned sites to help you with your undertaking.
