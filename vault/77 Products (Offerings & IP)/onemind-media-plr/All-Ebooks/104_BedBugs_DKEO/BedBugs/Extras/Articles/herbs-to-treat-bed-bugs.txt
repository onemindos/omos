Bitten By The Bug<br><br>

A good night’s sleep might become next to impossible if one is constantly being bitten by bed bugs.<br><br> 

Bed bugs have been a problem for many centuries dating as far back as medieval times and classical Greek, as these have been documented in various writings. Sleeping on an infested bed is one thing but being bitten is another. While a bed bug bite is painless and has an extremely low chance of transmitting diseases, people with such problems are mostly ashamed about their dilemma.<br><br>

Many believe that a bedbug infestation is detrimental to a relationship and can cause psychological strain. Outside of mental anguish, bed bugs can cause physical harm. The question now is how do you treat bed bugs and their bites.<br><br>

Just bite it <br><br>

Bed bug bites initially appear as large read wheals that eventually become a red mark. The mark can last for as lost as five days. The mark is also made in orderly way unlike the random pattern of mosquito bites.<br><br>

Characteristics of bed bug bites<br><br>

-       Irritation, inflammation and destruction of the local tissue<br><br>
-       Allergic reactions that can happen immediately or delayed<br><br>
-       Orderly pattern of marks<br><br>

Bed bugs bites cannot transmit harmful diseases and their medical significance is only limited to itching and inflammation. The immediate treatment for the affected part is by washing it with soap and water. Scratching is strongly advised, as this will surely lead to infection and possibly require more advanced medical aid. Treating the area with ice or possibly special herbs is also recommended, as these will help speed up the healing process.<br><br>
