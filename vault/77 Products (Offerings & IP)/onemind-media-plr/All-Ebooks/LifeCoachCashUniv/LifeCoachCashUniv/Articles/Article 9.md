# Life Coaching Online Courses and Certifications

If you have found an interest in becoming a life coach and know what particular area you want to study, then your next step is finding the right courses for you that will give you the education, skills, and certification that you need to become a life coach.  Although it is not required to have a certification or training to become a life coach it certainly helps when it comes to getting clients that believe in your credibility as well as makes you more trustworthy and the right person for the job.  There are tons of online courses available and will give you the knowledge and skills that you need to know to become a life coach.  Let's take a look at the top online courses available today.

The Institute For Life Coach Training - This is one of the top online programs that you can enroll in to become a life coach.  When you first sign up for the program you have two different paths you can choose to go through, the credentialing path or the specialities path.  There is a little bit of a difference between the two and should be looked at closely when deciding on which path to go with. The credentialing path is geared more towards getting the credentialing aspect of becoming a life coach at a quicker rate and covers the general aspects of being a life coach  The specialities path is geared more towards individuals that want to specifically deal with one area of life coaching such as career life coaching, relationship life coaching, wellness coaching, etc.  For whatever path you choose you are sure to get the utmost training and the certifications you need to show your potential clients that you are the right person for them to be able to get their life back in order.

Coach Training Alliance - This is another top rated course that you can take completely online to earn your life coach certification and helpful skills all in one place.  They offer live teleclasses for people interested in becoming a life coach and also offer programs and courses for those that are interested in learning or specializing in a particular area of life such as personal coaching, leadership coaching, success coaching, spiritual coaching, etc.  Although there programs can be a bit pricey, they do have easy payment plans that can be made where it makes it a little easier to pay for the entire course of training.  They also offer additional training material that you can learn on your own spare time.  With all the many options available with this online course you simply can't go wrong when it comes to gaining the credentialing you need to become a life coach through the help from coach training alliance.

Holistic Learning Center - This is also a very popular online course available for people considering becoming a life coach.  There are a two different ways that you can choose to take your online course, interactive coaching courses and independent study coaching courses.  When you choose the interactive coaching courses you will go through a total of 3 different courses before you can get your life coaching certification, however this is all done by teleclasses where you can actually interact with a life coach.  When you choose to do the independent study coaching courses they of course are courses that you can take at your own pace with all of the necessary materials you need to learn the skills needed on your own. They also offer an "A La Carte" Life Coaching Consultants program which allows already certified life coaches to gain extra training.  They also have helpful tips and suggestions on how to get business swarming your way and all from the comfort of your own home.  It is clear to see why this has become one of the top rated life coaching courses to take part in!

So make sure that if you are deciding to become a successful life coach that you get all the training and credentialing you need through one of these highly rated online courses.  You will not only gain skill and knowledge but will be looked at more frequently by potential clients seeking someone who really knows their stuff.  You can do just that by gaining the training from one of these fully accredited life coaching online courses.    .
