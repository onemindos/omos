Miracles can be created as you increase your energy.  The struggle to keep your thoughts positive comes from a lack of energy.  The more energy you have the higher your thought patterns the faster your ability to manifest, the synchronicity you will experience.  

When your energy is low you can struggle to manifest what you want until you turn blue but your success rate will be quite slow or completely unsuccessful.  The most amazing secrets are so natural and so free and not hidden yet we miss them.
