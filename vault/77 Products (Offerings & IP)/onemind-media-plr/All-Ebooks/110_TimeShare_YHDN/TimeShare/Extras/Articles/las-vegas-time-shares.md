# Enjoying your vacation within and around the Las Vegas Strip

Time share resorts in Las Vegas offer fabulous amenities available especially to owners of Las Vegas time shares. You can stay in an exclusive resort around or right on the very strip of Las Vegas for unbeatably low prices. 

Here are some of the Las Vegas resorts that offer time shares:

The Carriage House's location is one block off the Las Vegas Strip. It is in between the MGM Grand Hotel and The Alladin Hotel. As owners of Las Vegas time shares purchased from The Carriage House, you can enjoy an outdoor pool that is heated according to your discretion. There are well-lighted tennis and basketball courts as well for the sports enthusiasts. 

You can have the choice between having a kitchenette or a full kitchen equipped with microwave oven and coffee maker. The rooms are also complete with hair dryer, and iron and ironing board for your grooming needs. The hotel offers to owners of Las Vegas time shares complimentary airport transportation to ensure your hassle free ride from your flight to the hotel's doorstep.

The Cliffs at Peace Canyon offers to owners of Las Vegas time shares a peaceful yet elegant vacation abode that serves a convenient shuttle transportation system that goes around several times a day. It brings you to the different sightseeing locations and golf courses nearby.

In Club de Soleil, purchasing Las Vegas time shares means you are offered lush landscaping and serene mountainous background. This is a French-Mediterranean themed resort that is located on West Tropicana Avenue, a couple of miles from the famous Las Vegas Strip. 

Las Vegas time shares of Convention Center Drive Hotel & Casino located only half a block from the Strip. It has a collection of motion picture memorabilia collected by Debbie Reynolds that is why this resort was formerly known as Debbie Reynolds Resort Club. The panoramic view of Las Vegas is an incomparable feature of every suite - something owners of Las Vegas time shares can look forward to during their stay.

Desert Paradise Resort is homey. It offers personal service and the ambiance of home which aside from the regular amenities within the units offers services that of a fitness center, heated pool, spa, concierge and business center as well as discount tickets for shows. 

Epic Resorts Clubs offers luxurious resort accommodations. Aside from the Las Vegas resort, you can also exchange your Las Vegas time shares to its other resorts in Daytona Beach, Florida and Hilton Head Island, South Carolina. 

Fairfield Grand Desert offers 

Las Vegas time shares that allow guests to enjoy its amenities while being less than a mile away from the Strip. Right in the heart of Las Vegas, you can indulge in the relaxation brought about by the sumptuous suites of the resort to buyers of Las Vegas time shares. 

Grandview at Las Vegas gives time share owners the right location to enjoy everything Las Vegas has to offer with its brand new Tower II that opened early this year. 

Hilton Grand Vacation Club is just steps away from the fun and frolic of the Las Vegas Strip while still enjoy tranquility in its exclusive suites furnished with king size bed, cable TV, DVD player, kitchenette and wet bar with microwave oven, fridge and coffee maker. You can choose between HGVC Las Vegas or HGVC at the Flamingo.

The Jockey Club is right on the Strip where the style of Southern California comes to life bringing a feeling of home to its visitors including those who are enjoying their Las Vegas time shares.

La Quinta Inn brings the quiet of a non-gaming hotel. This is particularly loved by families who own Las Vegas time shares who wish to enjoy the fun of the place yet return home with much needed peace.

Lady Luck Resort Casino has 24-hour hotel casino. Gaming is offered here along with full service restaurants and room service to your one or two bedroom suites with soaking tubs and kitchen. 

Leisure Resorts is only two blocks away from the Strip where casinos are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. It lives up to the expectations of people owning Las Vegas time shares in enjoying their stay in the city.

Other time share resorts in Las Vegas include Diamond International, Lady Luck, Monarch Grand Vacations, Olympian Palms, Peppermill, Polo Towers, Ramada Grand Flamingo, Royal Hotel & Casino, Sahara Safari Club, Shell Vacations Club, Silverleafs Resort, Summer Bay Resort, Tahiti, Westgate and Worldmark. 

Along with the amenities you can enjoy within the walls of your resort, or in night clubs, shopping malls and casinos, you may also explore the Red Rock Canyon, Hoover Dam, the Death Valley, and the Valley of Fire during the day.
