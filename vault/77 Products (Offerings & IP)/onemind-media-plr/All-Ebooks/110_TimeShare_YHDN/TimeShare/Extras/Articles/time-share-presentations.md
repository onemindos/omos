# Timeshare presentations: How to act in one

Timeshare presentations are now another form of advertisement and marketing for companies offering timeshare properties. In timeshare presentations, opportunities to buy timeshare are shown to people who might be interested in investing in this segment of the travel industry. This is the new method of luring customers as well as venues for the latter to know what might be offered. 

Inquiries regarding timeshare presentations can be done by consumers directly by calling the timeshare resorts for any visitor promotions. There are lists of resorts from RCI and II membership catalogs. You can also browse in the internet to find the potential resorts you would like to visit soon on a vacation holiday. If you happen to visit a resort or resort cities, you can acquire more information at the help desks or virtual help desks usually found at the lobby area of the hotel. 

In agreeing to timeshare presentations, be wise about it. Haggle if you must. These people who offer timeshare at presentations are given discounts and the more people they connive to come to a presentation means more money and discounts for them. Try to get as much as you can and have the concrete guarantee that you will be getting everything that you have agreed upon.

You may qualify to join a timeshare presentation by adhering to the requirements of the activity. Usually, an ID and a credit card are requested to be presented prior to your admittance. You should be 25 to 30 and above. Most of these timeshare presentations would require you to bring your spouse if you are married. 

If you are single and you wish to bring your girlfriend or boyfriend, make sure to go for separate single packages. This way you will be able to receive two premiums, meaning more benefits as well. If they force you to do otherwise, stand firm. The decision is always your to make. 

But if during the timeshare presentation, you do not like what is being offered and want to leave, you can tell them sincerely. While encouraging you to join, they will usually promise you 90 minutes of your time or less, if the time is up, you may say that you have made your end of the bargain. You can tell them that you do not wish to be badgered around when you already gave your precious time. Or you tell them the truth or make excuses why you do not like what they are offering, tell them that you have been there in that resort and do not wish to come back or that you have a brother in the area and you can stay with him. 

Remember that if you are not satisfied, they have no choice or right whatsoever in forcing you to stay. 

During the timeshare presentation, write everything down!
Whatever you do and agree upon after a timeshare presentation, put it into writing. This way everything is legitimate and well documented. Write other details as well that will help you in the decision making process.

Make sure to evaluate what you have written as well. They may offer to give you free breakfast but is it fit for a king? They say the room is equipped with a TV set. Know if it is black or white, or color and whether is has cable channels. Know exactly what you are getting. By doing this, you will know if everything you paid for is worth your money.

After the timeshare presentation, if you like a specific timeshare offered, take your time in deciding. Get the number of the person or company to be contacted for the purchase as well as the cancellation fax number. This way if you get the contract and read something in it that you do not like, you automatically have the manager's number to call for the necessary negotiation or cancellation. 

Be safe and have a disclaimer in signing a timeshare contract. It should state that the contract will be cancelled within seven days without penalty or charge if the benefits and services are not truly available or misrepresented, your money should be refunded within a week's time. A charge of $50 a day will be paid to you if the exact money you paid for the timeshare is not returned or credited within a ten-day period after notification. 

Make sure to include in the disclaimer that this specific contract supersedes all other contracts in this transaction. By taking this necessary precaution, you are rest assured that you are on the safe side. And if you wish to cancel your contract, do not wait any longer and have it officially cancelled. 

After the timeshare presentation, if someone insists that you leave an amount of money for deposit, don't. You can simply say that you did not bring any cash with you except your credit card.

Timeshare presentations are good venues to find the right timeshare offer you want to take. But the best thing to do is to go to several timeshare presentations for more offers you might want to buy for your dream vacation.
