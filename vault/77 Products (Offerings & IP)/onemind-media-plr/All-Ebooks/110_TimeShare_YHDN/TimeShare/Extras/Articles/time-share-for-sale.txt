Owners of these time shares get bored easily if their every vacation is at the same resort.

Other Options for Time Share Owners

Aside from advertising a time share for sale, time share owners also have the option to do the following with their time shares:

1. Use the time share. 

Time share owners can opt to use their time share instead of selling or renting it. Time shares are real properties with deeds that transpire in a week's time. When using the time share, the owner can have the following type of ownerships:

a. The fixed week ownership, wherein owners have a fixed week as schedule for availment of the time share.  
	
b. The floating ownership, wherein owners have an option within a defined season such as the summer season for what particular week to use for their time share. 

c. The rotating ownership, wherein owners have equal opportunities in availing a particular week that they prefer. In this type of ownership, the time share interval is rotated each year to each time share owner. So, if you were currently assigned with week 11 as your time share interval for this year, expect that you would be assigned week 12 for next year and so on. 

2. Rent out the time share.

Time share owners can rent out the time share then post the time shares for sale. Renting out the time shares would make it still available to the owner in case he decides to use his time shares for vacation. 

3. Use the time share as a gift.

Time share owners can give the time shares as gifts to family and close relatives and friends. Instead of posting his time shares for sale, he can provide happiness to those close to him just transferring usage of the time share.

4. Exchange the time share internally.

Over the years resort developers have banded together under one umbrella group. This paved the way for time share owners to exchange their time shares with others as long as the time share resort belongs under the umbrella group.

Instead of posting the time shares for sale due to the redundant vacation, they can exchange it for other exotic or new vacation resorts that they have not yet seen. 

5. Exchange the time share externally. 
 
Time share owners can also opt to exchange their time shares to other time share resorts external to the umbrella group. These resorts number to more than a thousand.

Needless to say, time share owners have many options available to them regarding their time shares. They can opt to post their time shares for sale but they should always note the options that are available to them.
