Ways to Clear Acne Naturally and Give Yourself “Sweet” SkinAlmost everyone loves to eat sweets, and that’s alright in moderation.  Sweet things, however, are not only good to eat.  For those who suffer from acne breakouts, there are a few sweet things that can help naturally clear your acne and give yourself that clear skin you’ve dreamed of.
Here are a few of them:
• Honey— Honey is an antibacterial, and can disinfect and help heal blemishes.  You can use honey as a mask on your face a few times a week to help clear acne.  It is gentle on your skin, even if you have the most sensitive skin.  Just rub the honey on your entire face.  Let it sit until it’s totally dry, and then peel it off like you would any commercial mask.• Brown sugar—Two or three times a month, you can use brown sugar scrubs to help with your acne.  You should use it when you take a shower and your skin is damp.  The shower will cause your pores to open up.  Use brown sugar and scrub the acne areas.  It helps with acne and helps keep your skin smooth.  • Mint—Mint can be soothing for your skin.  It contains menthol, which is an anti-inflammatory.  The anti-inflammatory will help take away that red your skin shows when you have acne.  There are two ways to use it, either use the juice of mint tea leaves or mint oil.  Put it on your face for approximately 15 minutes and rinse off.  Since it’s an anti-inflammatory, it won’t hurt your skin, so you can use it as often as you choose to.• Cinnamon and honey mixture—Take one teaspoon of honey and one teaspoon of cinnamon and mix them together.  Put it on your face and let it sit for about 15 minutes.  Rinse your face thoroughly with warm water and pat dry.  Cinnamon is good for the skin because it has properties such as antiviral, antifungal, and antibacterial.  Honey heals soothes, because it is a natural antiseptic.  Honey also is a hygroscopic, so it can absorb moisture.There you have it—several sweet solutions to having that clear, acne-free skin you want.
