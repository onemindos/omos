Ten ways to love yourself

If you do not respect yourself, nobody will. This goes the same, if you
don't love yourself, how are you going to get anybody to love you. So
here are a ways that you can learn to love yourself.

1.  Embrace your past. Sure, you've had a few heartbreaks in your past.
    But whatever is in the past, leave it in the past. Learn that the
    past has made you the person that you are today. Do not regret the
    past because there is nothing you can do to change the past. What
    you can change is the present.

2.  Let go of your mistakes. Stop going through or thinking about your
    mistakes. It wouldn't do you any good. Learn from your past mistakes
    and move on. What you can't change about your past mistakes, you can
    learn to avoid in the future.

3.  Acknowledge yourself. Sure, people don't like a show off, but it is
    okay to admit once in a while that you are an amazing person and you
    deserve to be loved. Know your self worth!

4.  Reward yourself. Once in a while, treat yourself with a little
    treat. A relaxing massage or perhaps a day at the spa. Give your
    body some love by giving it the best care available!

5.  Do something you are passionate about! Have you always been
    passionate about something? For example, you love cooking and are
    always looking up recipes on the internet. Enroll yourself in a
    culinary class and awe your family with your amazing culinary
    skills.

6.  Take care of your body! Take up physical exercises. Exercising gets
    the endorphins pumping. Get a great workout and feel great all the
    time.

7.  Do away with the negative criticism. Instead of saying I am
    worthless; tell yourself, that I am worth fighting for. Negative
    criticisms are always destructive.

8.  Throw away bad apples. Get away from the people who are always
    putting you down. They are the kind of people who always have some
    negative input for you. Why do you need them for? You don't need
    them to feel good about yourself.

9.  Be a boy scout. A boy scout's motto is to do a good deed every day.
    Do something nice for someone every day. Or you could volunteer at a
    center or a homeless shelter. Once you see how badly people have it,
    you will learn to appreciate how good life can be.

10. Be adventurous. Life is all about experiencing new stuff. Learn a
    new instrument. Get your diving license. Most of all, live life for
    yourself!
