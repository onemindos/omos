# Outsourcing How to avoid doing it all yourself

Outsourcing: How to avoid doing it all yourself

Are you a small time business owner or an entrepreneur? Do you have too much on your plate right now and are unable to focus on the core points of your business? A strategy to this may be to outsource your work. Since it is impossible for you to do every single thing, outsourcing is the next best thing. So here are a few steps on how to outsource your business.

Pick an area you need help in.
You have to pick a function in your company that needs help. For example, you can increase your marketing function by hiring another company to do it for you.

Do your research.
Now you have chosen the area that you need help in, do you research to identify companies that can provide that services. You may ask around from other companies and see which service provider they engage.

Engage in talk
Once you’ve got a list of service providers down. Interview those companies and their customers on the quality and performance of the service. Review their portfolio and past experiences. If there is anything that you are concerned about, voice your concerns. You need to get it all out before you engage in their services.

Be specific 
When meeting with them, list down what you expect from them and how you want your work done. Be specific about the schedule. Once the service provider got all the information down, they will come up with a proposal and the price they have to offer.

Choose wisely
Don’t go for the cheapest. Choose your service provider based on the experience that they have to offer. You want your money’s worth. 

Get it in writing
Remember to get your agreement in writing and now you can proceed!
