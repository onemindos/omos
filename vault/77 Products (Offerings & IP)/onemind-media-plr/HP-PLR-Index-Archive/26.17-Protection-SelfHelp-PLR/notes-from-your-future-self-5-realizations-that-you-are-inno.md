Preface

Are you always curious to discover more about yourself?


Do your previous experiences and current perspectives
contribute to an understanding of how to navigate life?

- Your interest and self-discovery of this topic is already a
sign to appreciate where you are on your path.
- The journey has many routes, with no ‘end’ in sight.
You can control – or at least be aware of – each step you
take and how you respond to each moment that is created
by you and for you.

This ebook reminds you about the unique aspects of
yourself. Although everyone is equal in many ways, your
experience is distinct from those around you. You’ll
always dream big but you have to start somewhere and
focus on one move at a time. As you look deeper at the
life you want, more situations and people appear that will
support you, challenge you (in a learning path), and
empower you.


Trust the process, good luck, and have fun!
- Yev Marusenko, Ph.D.
Chapter 1

Do you have moments in your life
when you realize that you’re different
than your peers?

Having awareness that something about you is unique, or that
you don’t fit in, is a necessary moment that will be a
CATALYST later in life.
At some point you make a decision to be on a certain path -
finally with a good feeling that “you’re onto something”. No one
else has ever had the exact experiences as you did to take those
steps. You’ll be comforted by the reminder that there was a
reason why you were different than everybody else - now it all
makes sense.
Chapter 2

Do you feel empowered by
collaboration?


Developing partnerships and connections is intuitive for you. A
competitive mind-set certainly could have “winners”, and even
the “losers” have an opportunity to learn and grow, but the
process for this approach is fear-based and unfulfilling once the
best competitor reaches the “top”.
Mutual relationships just have a better overall sense about them.
Chapter 3
Have you had any success before –
either large or small?


List your accomplishments. Anything. An award, an honor, or
simply a ‘congratulations’, something where another person
acknowledged you or you felt accomplished. There may be
many items, or even if only one, it shows that you can create
value and be recognized for it.
When you doubt yourself, think back to this point for
encouragement. You have been successful (even if minor) and
CAN succeed.
Chapter 4
Are you passionate about creating your
life and the type of world that you want
to live in?

Well-being for ALL seems like a great deal. Win-win scenario.
This broad goal at the back of your mind is honorable. Even if
you forget about it, your actions every moment and every day
have a solid foundation that s contributing to a greater mission.
Chapter 5
Do you ever notice that you are an
“observer” in the room?
Has this happened to you?: Everyone is discussing something.
People are interacting. You are engaged in the conversations.
Suddenly your mind breaks away and you are no longer aware
of the topic. Rather than being aware of “what” everyone is
talking about, you realize the nature of the event itself.
These are early signs that you’re a thinker and conceptualizer at
a deep level. You see the details and are aware of the bigger
picture.

Chapter 6
Do you always want to build a “bridge”
between two different sides?

#6 is a bonus – it stands on its own and overlaps with all of the
other points. Each experience is connected to each other.
Integration is the platform for acceptance and understanding.
Your previous studies and work has likely been in a position that
is a blend of multiple types of roles within your department or
occupation. If both sides respect and appreciate each other, the
communication is more open and there is less chance of error in
the long-term. Find those links and unite them.

    NOTES FROM YOUR FUTURE SELF -
          5 Realizations That YOU ARE
               Innovative + Integrative

Are you busy? Always have stuff to do that demands your
attention. Your time. Your effort.


There must be occasions when you have a feeling – even
if rarely – that it could be possible to live a dream life!?


Well, you DON’T necessarily need more time or effort. You
do need a

PERSPECTIVE SHIFT.


Imagine yourself in the future – late part of life – you had
a     decent      journey,      accomplishments,          some
fun...WAIT...let’s try this again. Create an optimistic
version that is almost impossible to achieve (the one
others say is ‘unrealistic’), the potential you that pursued
passions and changed the world. You’re surrounded by
people truly embracing you, your current heroes and
inspired friends.


Have a conversation with your future self:

Start by actively contributing to your passion mission
with only

ONE MINUTE PER DAY, EVERY DAY.

A thought, email, conversation - anything - just let the
universe know.


You are: unique, collaborative, can-do-driven, self-aware,
and integrative. Your passion will grow and amplify.
