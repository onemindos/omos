**7 essentials in Spiritual Mind Healing**

In spiritual healing you become conscious of the truth that your life is
the Life of God, or the Great Spirit. We can realise the essential truth
in our lifes by recognizing, understanding, and appreciating the logic
of a primal Harmony. When we perceive the reality of wholeness, then
Truth takes over our lives. To understand this, one must incorporate
into the mind what is known as the seven essentials of spiritual mind
healing. These are pre-exquisite if the spiritual mind treatment. They
are as follow:

#### 1.  The recognition of a personal union with the Divine Spirit.

You must realise this as it is what you have come from and where you
function and exist.

#### 2. There can be no compromises, no negotiations with evil.

Evil means denial of good. It is the negative and destructive use of
thoughts, feelings, and emotions that must be dissolved.

#### 3. Face up to your shortcomings.

Your shortcomings are the illogical and irrational experiences of states
of consciousness and feeling. Do not brush them aside. Face them. Take
time to observe yourself in relationship to everyone and everything.

#### 4. Don\'t fight the negatives or evil, Realize Truth.

By knowing the Truth, you get rid of mental entanglements and blocks
such as anger, resentment, grudges, jealousies, etc.

** 5. Practice continued self-examination.**

Observe your mood swings and states of thought and feeling. These are
the basics of awakening self awareness.

#### 6. Don\'t look outside for the source of your problems.

Do not play the blame game, instead look within yourself for causes of
outer distress. Realize and understand that there are no real enemies in
your outer world. As you neutralize the cause, you will get rid of the
disordered effects.

** 7. Control your negative feelings**

Recognise that internal negatives (anger, grudge, jealousy etc.) are
simply inversions of good. Learn to face and control your fears and
anger. Then perceive the Truth in which you recognize and affirm the
good. Then you will understand that good comes from the great Source of
all Life within you.
