## Spiritual Meditation Techniques

One of the most common techniques used in spiritual meditation is the
use of [breathing
meditation](http://howtomeditate.me/Meditation_For_Beginners/Breathing_Meditation.html).
To use this technique, simply sit in a comfortable meditation position
(many practitioners prefer to use the lotus position, in which the legs
are crossed and the back is held straight). Then, close your eyes, and
slowly breathe in as deeply as possible.

As you inhale, visualize divine energy flowing toward you and permeating
your physical body. Hold the breath for a few seconds and then exhale
slowly; imagine all of your stress and mundane thoughts of your daily
life released from you with your breath. Repeating this [meditation
technique](http://howtomeditate.me/Meditation_Techniques/) will allow
you to open up to divine energy, and greatly improve the benefits you
receive from your meditation practice.

Reciting a chant or prayer during spiritual meditation will also serve
to strengthen your connection to the divine. The rhythmic, repetitive
nature of short chants and prayers allows your mind to drift into a
meditative state, and improves your ability to access the divine wisdom.
