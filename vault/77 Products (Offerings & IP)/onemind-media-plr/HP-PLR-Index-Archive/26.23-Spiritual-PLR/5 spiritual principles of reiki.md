**5 spiritual principals of reiki**

Reiki as a spiritual method for enlightenment. Reiki healing includes
physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual healing. Those who are
familiar with the concepts of energy healing using Reiki methods is also
most likely aware of the 5 Spiritual Principles of Reiki. These are
meant to be lived (in our thoughts, words and deeds), not just noted or
recited or remembered. By living these principles you will find your
life settling into a comfortable alignment with the universe around you,
and possibilities are endless. The five principles of reiki is as
follow:

1.  **Just for today, I will not be angry.**

Anger towards oneself or at the whole world creates serious blockages in
one\'s energy. It is the most complex inner enemy. Reiki is an excellent
tool to remove anger blockages which have accumulated in the body over
years, but it cannot remove the residue of current anger that you put
into yourself daily. Letting go of that anger brings peace into the
mind.

2.  **Just for today, I will not worry.**

While anger deals with past and present events, worry deals with future
ones. Although worrying at times is not always a negative phenomena,
endless worries may create unnecessary stress and poison one's mind,
body and soul. While anger requires a focused Reiki treatment to remove
obstacles, worry requires the energy to be spread throughout the entire
body. Letting go of worry brings healing into the body.

3.  **Just for today, I will be grateful.**

Be grateful from the bottom of your heart. Inner intention is the
important element in this principle. Simple things as being thankful,
and practice forgiveness and gratitude can improve others life and make
them happy. Being thankful brings joy into the spirit.

4.  **Just for today, I will do my work honestly.**

Support yourself and your family respectably, without doing any harm
onto others. Earn a respectable living, live a life of honour. Working
honestly brings abundance into the soul.

5.  **Just for today, I will be kind to every living thing.**

Honour your parents, teachers, elders and even friends. Being kind
brings love into the will.
