# 10e. Understanding Body Language for Avoiding Rejection

Understanding Body Language for Avoiding Rejection
More than 70% of our daily communications are non-verbal through body language. In fact, our inner thoughts are reflected by our gestures without us even realising it. What is the implication to you as a network marketer? 
The answer: You need to learn how to read your prospect’s body language because it would help you to gauge how well your prospect is receiving your proposals.
What are the key body languages to look out for when you are talking to your prospects?

1. To gauge the overall mood of your prospects.
 Direct eye contact indicates that your prospect is interested. Smiling eyes and relaxed eye brows indicate that they are comfortable listening to you. The opposite indicates that they are not interested. If you pick up small nodding gestures, it shows they understand. On the other hand, if they show even tiny shaking movement, you might want to explain the points that you just made. 

2. To gauge their openness
An open posture indicates that your prospects are open to your ideas and are willing to listen more. An open posture is made up of body leaning forward and no arms-crossing. The opposite would make up a closed posture, i.e. hunched shoulder and crossed arms. A prospect is more willing to give you information when they speak to you with an open palm. When you see this signs, you might want to change your approach to the issue you are just discussing.

3. To gauge their response
People fidget and drum their fingers when they are agitated and feel bored. At this point, you might want to take a break or change your topic. Your prospect is evaluating your proposal when he starts to touch his chin. It is likely to be a negative evaluation when they touch their nose and scratch it. On the other hand, a prospect shows positive evaluation by touching the forehead, rubbing and scratching their eye brows. When your prospect is ready to sign the deal, they are more likely to lean forward and might play around with their pen, as if saying “Where is the agreement?”
	
In summary, training yourself to understand body language and respond to it appropriately can bring you far in the network marketing business. Do note that the skills takes time to develop so be patient and be consistent!
