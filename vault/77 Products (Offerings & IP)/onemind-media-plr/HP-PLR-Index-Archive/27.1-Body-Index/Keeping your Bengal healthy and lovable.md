# Keeping your Bengal healthy and lovable

Keeping your Bengal healthy and lovable 
Undoubtedly, Bengal cats are considered to be a great companion and wonderful pet. They are well known for their outgoing and playful nature. They are affectionate, gentle and nice with all other pets in the home. It is essential for you to take good care of Bengals if you want to keep them as loving companion. If you have other pets in the home, make sure to create personal space for your Bengal kitten before bringing it to home. It has some majestic features of both domestic cat and Asian leopard. It has bit wild nature but its physical attributes are just like other traditional cats but with bit small size. 
Bengals sometimes produce weird sound only to ensure its own territory. You can give dry food to your Bengal kitten during first 2 to 3 weeks. You can easily find right food for kitten or cat from the market. When it comes to vaccines, your Bengal needs FVRCP vaccinations depending upon the age. Vaccinations for Calicivirus, Rhino and Panleukopenia are essential for Bengals less than 1 year old. There is no need to give vaccine for Rabies to Bengals. Also, most people do not allow their Bengal cat to roam outside freely. They are attractive and adorable which simply means can be easily stolen. 
In case, if you do not have any knowledge about the vaccines, it is better to consult with an expert veterinarian. It is essential to give good amount of space to your Bengal so that they can play in the house comfortably. Also, they love playing in water and if you can play with them, they can easily get familiar with you in very short time. Actually, they need exercise in a day and it is good to take your Bengal for a short walk. So, try to gather more and more information about other ways to raise Bengals to make them feel good.
