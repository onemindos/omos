# What is hypnotherapy

What is hypnotherapy?

When you mention hypnosis, the first thing that your mind conjures up is a magician hypnotizing an audience with the swinging of a pendulum. However, what you don’t know is that hypnosis is a totally natural state of mind. When you are under hypnosis, you will be able to relax. So what exactly is hypnotherapy? Hypnotherapy is some simple hypnotic relaxation techniques which you can use to attain the state of hypnosis. With hypnotherapy, you can visualise yourself becoming healthier, happier, and more confident you.

Hypnotherapy is a useful tool that you can use to get into your totally natural state of mind. Once you are in that mind frame, you will be able to clear your mind and answer all the internal questions that you have. Such as what do you want your life to be? What kind of changes will you need to make for this to happen? These kinds of questions are difficult to answer when you are feeling frustrated or stressed but once you are in that state of mind, it becomes clear to you.

Secondly, hypnotherapy can also be used to assist emotional and psychological disorders. The hypnotherapist will often try to access the client’s subconscious mind. This requires the client to be in a relaxed state and uses the client’s own imagination. This may vary a wide range of techniques such as story telling, metaphor, symbolism or direct suggestions for beneficial change. The most important thing however is that the client should feel comfortable and at ease with their therapist. Trust and mutual confidence will further enhance the healing process. 

In reality, hypnotherapy is not only to assist people who are experiencing emotional and physiological disorder; virtually everybody can go for hypnotherapy. It can help you to kick off unwanted addiction, or stress problems, or anything that you are concerned about. It could also be used as an adjunct to counselling techniques. It can be used to help in resolving relationship difficulties too.

So, throw out your misconception about hypnotherapy. Try hypnotherapy today to unlock your true inner potential!
