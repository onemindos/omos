# Debunking the myth about hypnotherapy

Debunking the myth about hypnotherapy!

From the very start, hypnotherapy has been surrounded by a layer of mystery. Mention hypnotherapy and you get visions of people acting like ducks or chickens, all because they were told to do so when they were hypnotized! However, that is not what hypnotherapy is about. So let us look at the few myths of hypnotherapy and why they are untrue!

You lose control
Losing control is the most classic myth of all time. It is a myth perpetuated by countless of cartoons or television shows. Firstly, you have to remember that when you are put in a state of hypnosis, you don’t lose control! In fact, it is the total opposite. You are in total control of yourself and you are able to come out of the state of hypnosis anytime you want!

You start revealing your innermost secret
As we said before, you will be in total control of yourself. Therefore you may choose not to reveal your deepest, darkest secret unless you choose to do so.

Can they ask you to do something against your will?
This scenario is not possible. You are in total control of your body. Your subconscious will protect you and will not accept any suggestion that is harmful to you. So don’t worry!

They can make me change my personality
Remember your subconscious mind is awake and alert. You will never accept any suggestions that are made to you unless it fits completely with your core beliefs and values.

It is not safe!
Of course it is safe. Undergoing hypnosis is putting your mind back into its natural state. It gives you the opportunity to access your subconscious and unlock your inner potential.

In conclusion, forget about all the dangerous things that they have said about hypnotherapy! The most important thing to know is that you are in the driver’s seat and nothing goes on without you having a say in it!
