             The Goal Setting Basics


There are millions of words written on how to set
and achieve your Goals, which are practiced by
millions of people as well. However; surprisingly
most of the goals created are far from being
fulfilled. Here are some goal setting basics that
will help you frame your goals and increase your
chance of success.



1) Your goals should be yours.

This doesn’t mean that your goals cannot be
similar to that of others. It just means that the
desire to achieve such a goal should originate
from yourself, where you provide your own
original approach and unique touch to the
nature of the goal. Many people acquire goals
that are based on the expectations of others.


                                                    2
Such acquired goals are temporary by nature
and you cannot stick around to see the results,
why is that? Simply due to the fact that you are
addressing the goals which are derived from
others rather than yourself. Remember that
setting an original goal, truly means that you
should earn fulfillment and happiness as you go
about accomplishing it.

Practice setting goals that are originally yours. If
it does not originate from you, then what is the
point? Accomplishing them will not give you that
sense of achievement and fulfillment.



2) Your goals should be inspirational.

To set a goal you should be determined to do
something, and that sense of determination is a
product of inspiration. Your passion should set
the frame of work for the goals that you want to
achieve. It must be something that will drive you



                                                       3
to strive harder and see through the difficulties
that might come across the process of achieving
your goals.

Passion is that something which gives you that
extra push, for that extra mile which might be all
what you need to achieve that goal. It keeps you
on track. It strengthens your concentration and
motivates you. It is passion that inspires you and
gets you up and running towards your goals.



3) Your goals should be harmonious in nature.

As mentioned earlier, your goals should be
original. In addition, there should be a sense of
harmony in your set of goals, with no
contradictions between your objectives rather a
smooth blend.



4) Your Goals must be realistic in nature.



                                                    4
The goals that you set should be doable,
achievable and measureable and could easily fit
in a realistic time frame. For example: losing 2lbs
of fat a week is achievable and measurable, while
losing 20lbs of fat a week is far from being
realistic which in turn will leave you frustrated
and disappointed. Keeping in mind that realistic
goals are relative in nature. What does that
mean? Going back to the fat loss example; some
might consider that losing 2lbs a fat a week as a
simple goal while others view it as a difficult one,
it depends on your capabilities and the effort
that you are willing to put, after which your goal
could be tracked by a timeframe.

Furthermore, your goals should take into
account surrounding aspects before setting them
such as environmental factors, physical factors,
relationships and emotional aspects, financial
factors, ethical, spiritual, educational and mental
aspects. Which should help you adjust or



                                                     5
visualize how ideal and realistic is your goal. This
way you’ll increase the odds of actually
achieving your goals rather than getting
disappointed.



5) Make your goals specific.

Your goals should be specific in nature, rather
than simply highlighting a broad target with no
sense of actual measurement. For example: If
your goal is to lose fat, how would you be able to
measure that goal? A more specific goal would be
losing 2lbs of fat in a period of 2 weeks.



6) Your goals should be flexible.

Several individuals miss the chance to achieve
their goals as result of narrowing their vision or
concentration which is form of a tunnel view,
which might make you lose potential
opportunities as you go about achieving your


                                                     6
target. Recognize better goals, better
methodology, and better opportunities as you go
about achieving your main target.




7) Visualize your goals.

Visualizing your goals will help you a lot and will
provide you with the motivation that you need.
Imagining your goals makes them more realistic
and believable, thus energizing you and
inspiring you to strive harder to achieve them.



8) You should affirm your goals.

You must believe in yourself and that you can
actually achieve your goal. Keep in mind that if
you don’t believe that you can make it, then


                                                      7
most probably you won’t. Without that passion
and drive, success is hard to reach. You must be
that someone to speak about your goal.



9) Have a timeframe for your goals.

It is in time and space where everything exists.
Set specific period of time for the goals to be
achieved. Track your progress as you go, and
adjust accordingly but never keep your goals as a
loose end.




10) You must have a list of all the goals that you
want to achieve.

It has been proven through studies that people
who jot down their goals have higher ability of
accomplishing their tasks rather than those who
only list such goals in their minds. People who



                                                     8
don’t have a list of their goals are more likely to
withdraw from the achievement process. But still
there are some individuals who manage to set
and achieve their goals successfully, even
without writing them down. Do not pretend that
you are one of those. It is much better to write
them down since it is not guaranteed that you
are still going to remember all the goals that you
have set after a long period of time.

                STOP FANTASIZING


Sometimes, it seems that you are living in a
world of fantasy. Entertainment is what people
use in order to fantasize and escape from the
happenings in the real world. Fortunately, some
gain gratification from this form of
entertainment.

Someone needs to have an unbelievably
responsive mind to be able to observe that
he/she is always eliminating or coming up with
something, which is the act of fantasizing.

This act of fantasizing every now and then can
lead to a corrupt mind which is always in a clash
and unruly. Whenever you fantasize something,
there can be a great chance that you are going to
lust after it. Thus, it is pushing you to do
inappropriate activities.




                                                 10
Living in a world of fantasy will not let you have a
chance to see the real world as it is. If you dig
more into it, you will realize that fear and greed
are somewhere in the mix. These two factors are
habitually the reasons behind how most people
interact with life.

It is important to know what the root causes are,
then you will know how to address that conflict
correctly.

Fantasizing is illusive and far from reality, so
stop fantasizing and start achieving the real goal
that you have in this world for the real
satisfaction and fulfillment lying ahead of you.

                                                   12

a better person. Such transformation will open

In addition, being a goal-oriented individual will
equip you with the ability to think long term. It is

Before reaching your desired goals in life, setting

The things that you are doing nowadays in order
to develop yourself will surely determine who

have in the future. This is something that you
should acquire as you go along the process of
attaining your goals.

Now that you know something about the goal
achievement process and its significance on
one’s life, it is the time for you to start planning

            Avoid the Slippery Slope


Setting a goal can be the push that you need to
get started and become more efficient, but keep
an eye for a Hiccup that could sneak along the
way.

Let’s take healthy eating habits for an example.
Consider that you have set a daily amount of
food intake and you are planning to stick to your
goal, but unexpectedly your peers brought you to
a restaurant one day. Which in turn results in a
situation where you are having a meal in
restaurant, rather than a healthy one prepared
by you at home. Then inside the restaurant, you
decide to have some appetizers and while at it
you get yourself the dessert as well. By the end of
it you will realize that you have already
surpassed your daily intake and your goal has
slipped. The Slippery Slope could be triggered by




                                                   18
an incident that will lead to a cycle of slips that
will pull you away from sticking to your goal.

This gives you the picture that sometimes as you
go along the process of achieving your goal,
things happen and unexpected circumstances
take place, yet it’s up to you to throw the towel or
stick to your target.

The “Slippery Slope” is not just a momentary
lapse or a sudden lack of self-control. Instead, it
is greatly reduce the odds of meeting your goals.

The Slippery Slope applies to diet, shopping,
money, behavior and other areas which you have
set your limitations. Where you will do your best
to stick to your goal for the first few days and as
you progress something comes along the way to
knock you off track.

In order to avoid this, it is important to think
long term rather than short term when it comes
to achieving your Goals. A helpful approach is to



                                                      19
think of your goal as something you are
acquiring day in and day out and every day that
passes by is one step closer to your goal.




                SHIFTING FOCUS


Have you set yourself on a goal achieving
journey and somehow ended up further away
from your goal? Any idea how did that happen?
Or why did it happen?

Perhaps the main reason for those failures is that
you have continuously shifted your focus to


                                                  20
other things that are of short term interest
compared to your long term goal. How do you
prevent that from happening? How do you lock
down that bulls eye mark? The following tips will
help you get things in line.

     Be creative and critical while setting your
      goal achieving plan. Break down your plan
      into chunks and procedures that you can
      commit to on a short term basis and
      eventually leading to your long term goal.
      The more detailed your breakdown the
      clearer the overall picture.



     There is always room for improvement. Do
      not follow an “All or Nothing” approach. It
      is favorable to improve one thing at a time
      rather than fix everything all at once.




                                                    21
   Take time to reflect. Think about the
    reasons of why you want to obtain an
    objective. Realizing this aspect will take you
    one step closer towards your goal. Make
    sure that you really know what you want to
    achieve.



   Write them down in anywhere. If you have
    cards or a notebook, list your entire goals.
    This will serve as timely reminder for you
    and will make you more consistent in your
    approach.




   Have a well-defined schedule towards your
    goal. When and what kind of activities you
    are going to employ. The time, the place
    and the type of situations that you are going
    to utilize in the process. Set alarms and


                                                   22
    reminders to prevent yourself from
    forgetting your targets.



   If you have missed or skipped a goal, spare
    some time to go back and assess what went
    wrong and where you left off. Better late
    than never approach serves you well in this
    scenario.




   Motivate yourself. Design a way to keep
    yourself motivated as you go about
    achieving your goals. It can be through
    listing the things that you have
    accomplished or rewarding yourself after
    every achievement. Some kind of reward
    system will serve you well and keep your
    motivation intact as you progress.




                                                23
The above tips can be helpful to jump start the
process. Stick to them day in and day out and
eventually they will become a habit rather than a
Chore.




              LIMITING BEHAVIOR




                                                  24
As you go about your goals you might get caught
up in the process and in turn developing certain
approaches and behaviors which are
counterproductive in nature. The following are
some behavioral limitations that you might
encounter on your journey:

1-You should have one goal at time only. Having
two goals at the same time, will confuse you and
upon concentrating on one will make it difficult
for you to achieve the other one.

2-Dealing with the risks and goals, researchers
have found out that people who are given more
difficult goals increase their risk strategies to
develop their performances.

3-Another limitation is the tunnel vision. This is
when individuals excessively focus on some
aspects of their goals, leaving behind other
aspects of their life and career.
