Here’s a list summarizing each home testing service along with their website links:
1. LetsGetChecked
• Focus: General health tests (e.g., thyroid, cholesterol, STDs, and more).
• Pricing: $69 to $349.
• Results Time: 2-5 business days.
• Pros: 24/7 customer support, international availability, board-certified physicians reviewing tests.
• Cons: Sample collection issues reported, no insurance accepted.
• Website: LetsGetChecked  
2. Everlywell
• Focus: Broad testing, including food sensitivity, hormone, and general health tests.
• Pricing: $49.99 to $200+.
• Results Time: Around 5 business days.
• Pros: Clean interface, membership option, wide availability.
• Cons: Limited availability in certain states, no immediate physician consultations for positive results.
• Website: Everlywell  
3. myLAB Box
• Focus: STD testing, wellness, and broader health checks.
• Pricing: $32 to $499.
• Results Time: 1-5 business days.
• Pros: Physician consultations for positive results, broad test selection, available to ages 14+.
• Cons: US-only service, some tests are expensive.
• Website: myLAB Box
