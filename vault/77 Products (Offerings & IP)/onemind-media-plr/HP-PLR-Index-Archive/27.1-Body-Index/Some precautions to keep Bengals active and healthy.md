# Some precautions to keep Bengals active and healthy

Some precautions to keep Bengals active and healthy
Bengal cats are adorable, beautiful with pelted coat in them which needs minimum grooming. Weekly brushing is enough to keep their fur healthy, shiny and soft. Two to three times bath in a week is essential for Bengals as it can remove all the loose or unhealthy hairs from the skin. Also, front nails must be clipped at least once in a week else it may damage your furniture or clothing. Bengal cats need daily exercise and if you want your cat to be healthy, it is good to bring some cat toys in the home. There are different kinds of cat toys available in the market but Bengals enjoy interactive toys. 
In order to raise cats in a right manner, you need to introduce it with all family members during the first meeting. Bengal cat is bit shy in nature and by doing this all cat can become familiar with all members. If you are bringing a kitten into a home, it is essential to keep good care at least for six months. Especially in case of Bengal cats, make sure not to allow them to roam freely outside. They are adorable and beautiful creatures which mean more chances of being stolen.
Raising a cat is not at all difficult task but there are few things to consider for the overall security of cats. It is essential to give personal space to your cat and leave your cat to that place at the time when you bring it in home.
When it comes to cat food, there are different varieties available in the market and you can choose depending upon your choice. Bengal cats have quite sensitive digestive system and thus you need to take special care while giving them food. Human food and other pet’s food can be dangerous for Bengals. So, gather as many knowledge as you can in order to raise Bengals in a good way.
