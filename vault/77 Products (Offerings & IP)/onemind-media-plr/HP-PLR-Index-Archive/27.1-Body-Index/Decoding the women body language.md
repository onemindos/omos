# Decoding the women body language

Decoding the women body language

There is an overgeneralization these days that the actions of the women are difficult to understand. However, reading the women body language is definitely easy with our 5 easy tips. So read on!

Physical contact
Is she touching your arm in any manner? This is always good sign. This indicates that she is interested in you and she is taking any opportunity she can to be able to have physical contact with you. Unless she is striking you and asking you to leave her alone, then it means exactly what she is saying! That is one signal that means you should just do what she says because she is not interested.

Her lips
Is she biting her lips? It’s alright. She may be nervous and it may be a habit of hers when she is nervous. Or she may be subtly flirting with you! If she wets her lips often, it may be a desire for you to pay attention to her lips! So she is definitely interested!

Her eyes
If she maintains eye contact with you, this is a good sign!  This indicates that she is interested in what you have to say and definitely interested in you. Observe her pupils too. If they are dilated, it may be a sign that she is interested in you. Although, do be aware that other factors can influence the dilation of the pupils such as brightness of the room and alcohol!

Her hands and arms
Is she sitting back with her arms crossed together? She may be closed and not receptive towards you. It may be from a habit but more often than not it indicates that she is not interested. So take a step back and continue talking, if she seems uninterested in what you have to say. Just step out. Plenty of other fishes in the sea! 

Her fingers
Is she playing with her hair or is she running her fingers through her hair? She may be seeking your attention with all these little acts of preening of hers. If she is fidgeting around, it may be a sign of discomfort or nervousness. So try being kind to her and she will receive you more warmly for trying to calm her down. 

So men, take heart. Take note of the body language tips and flirt your way to success!
