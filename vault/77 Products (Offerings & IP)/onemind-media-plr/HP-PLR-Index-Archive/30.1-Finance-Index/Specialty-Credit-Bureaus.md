# Specialty Credit Bureau/agency

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Finance

**ChexSystems**

**Purpose**: Tracks banking history, particularly issues like overdrafts, closed accounts, or bounced checks. Often used by banks to determine eligibility for opening checking/savings accounts.

**Relevance**: If you’ve been denied a bank account, it’s likely due to your ChexSystems report.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [ChexSystems Consumer Assistance](https://www.chexsystems.com/).
- Request a free annual report under the **Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA)**.
- Dispute inaccuracies online or via mail.

**Tips**: Review your report before applying for a new bank account. Check for fraudulent activity.

**Innovis**

**Purpose**: Provides alternative credit data to lenders, such as address history and credit behavior not included in the “big three” bureaus.

**Relevance**: Innovis may impact credit decisions, as some lenders use their reports in addition to Experian, TransUnion, and Equifax.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [Innovis Consumer Services](https://www.innovis.com/).
- Request a free report through the website or by calling 1-800-540-2505.

**Tips**: Include Innovis in your credit monitoring routine. They may also list accounts not shown on traditional credit reports.

**SageStream (now LexisNexis Risk Solutions)**

**Purpose**: Provides alternative credit scores and data used by lenders, particularly for auto loans and credit cards.

**Relevance**: If you’re denied for credit, SageStream might have been used to evaluate your credit risk.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [LexisNexis Consumer Center](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/).
- Request your personal credit report and freeze your SageStream report if necessary.

**Tips**: SageStream data can sometimes have errors, so it’s worth reviewing.

**Clarity Services**

**Purpose**: Tracks subprime lending data, such as payday loans, installment loans, and auto loans.

**Relevance**: If you’ve used payday or alternative lenders, your history will appear here.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [Clarity Services](https://www.clarityservices.com/).
- Call 1-866-390-3118 or submit an online request for a report.

**Tips**: Monitoring this report helps you understand how subprime lenders view you.

**CoreLogic Credco**

**Purpose**: Collects mortgage, rental, and property tax data. Used by mortgage lenders, landlords, and utility companies.

**Relevance**: If you’re applying for a mortgage or rental property, this report is crucial.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [CoreLogic Consumer Affairs](https://www.corelogic.com/).
- Request your report by calling 1-877-532-8778.

**Tips**: Verify mortgage and rental payment accuracy to avoid issues with future housing

applications.

**TeleCheck**

**Purpose**: Tracks check-writing history to prevent fraud and check-bouncing issues. Retailers use it to verify checks.

**Relevance**: If a retailer denies your check, your TeleCheck report might have a negative mark.

**How to Access**:

- Call TeleCheck at 1-800-366-2425 or visit [TeleCheck Services](https://www.telecheck.com/).
- Request a free report or dispute inaccuracies.

**Tips**: Always dispute fraudulent activity promptly.

**Early Warning Services (EWS)**

**Purpose**: Tracks banking and checking account activity, similar to ChexSystems but broader. Often used by banks.

**Relevance**: If you’re denied a checking account, EWS might have contributed to the decision.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [Early Warning Services](https://www.earlywarning.com/).
- Call 1-800-325-7775 to request a free report.

**Tips**: Use EWS in conjunction with ChexSystems for a complete banking history review.

**FactorTrust**

**Purpose**: Tracks alternative credit data, focusing on short-term loans, payday loans, and rent-to-own transactions.

**Relevance**: Lenders use FactorTrust to evaluate subprime credit behavior.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [FactorTrust Consumer Services](https://www.transunion.com/factortrust).
- Request a report online or by mail.

**Tips**: Dispute inaccuracies that may affect loan eligibility.

**MicroBilt**

**Purpose**: Provides credit data to lenders, leasing companies, and collections agencies.

**Relevance**: If you’ve used nontraditional lending or leasing, your activity might appear here.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [MicroBilt](https://www.microbilt.com/).
- Call 1-800-884-4747 to request a report.

**Tips**: Include MicroBilt in credit monitoring to ensure accurate reporting.

**PRBC (Pay Rent, Build Credit)**

**Purpose**: Tracks rental and utility payments to help build credit.

**Relevance**: Helps consumers with limited credit histories establish a credit profile.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [PRBC](https://www.prbc.com/).
- Create an account and link your rent or utility payments to report them.

**Tips**: Use PRBC to build positive credit if you lack traditional credit accounts.

**National Consumer Telecom and Utilities Exchange (NCTUE)**

**Purpose**: Tracks telecom, pay TV, and utility account history.

**Relevance**: Used by service providers to determine deposit requirements or payment terms.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [NCTUE Consumer Reports](https://www.nctue.com/).
- Call 1-866-349-5185 to request a report.

**Tips**: Pay utility bills on time to maintain a good record.

**ID Analytics**

**Purpose**: Tracks credit behavior and fraud risk. Often used for identity verification.

**Relevance**: Lenders and insurers use this report to assess fraud risk.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [ID Analytics](https://www.idanalytics.com/).
- Contact the consumer services team for your report.

**Tips**: Use this report to detect fraud or identity theft early.

**Tenant Screening Bureaus**

**Purpose**: Tracks rental payment history and eviction records.

**Relevance**: Landlords use these reports during tenant screening.

**How to Access**:

- Request reports from bureaus like [CoreLogic Rental Property Solutions](https://www.corelogic.com/) or [Experian RentBureau](https://www.experian.com/rentbureau).

**Tips**: Ensure any eviction or rent payment information is accurate.

**Insurance Information Exchange (C.L.U.E.)**

**Purpose**: Tracks insurance claims history for auto and home insurance.

**Relevance**: Insurers use C.L.U.E. reports to determine rates and coverage.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [LexisNexis C.L.U.E. Reports](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/).
- Request a free report or dispute inaccuracies.

**Tips**: Review your claims history before shopping for insurance.

**MIB Group, Inc.**

**Purpose**: Maintains a database of medical information used by insurance companies to assess risk and eligibility for life, health, disability, and long-term care insurance.

**Relevance**: If you’ve applied for insurance, MIB may have a record of medical conditions you’ve disclosed.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [MIB’s Consumer Center](https://www.mib.com/request_your_record.html).
- Request a free copy of your MIB Consumer File online or by calling 1-866-692-6901.

**Tips**: Review your MIB report to ensure medical information is accurate, as it can impact

insurance applications.

**Milliman IntelliScript**

**Purpose**: Provides prescription history reports to insurance companies to assist in underwriting life and health insurance policies.

**Relevance**: Your prescription history can influence insurance decisions.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [Milliman’s Consumer Information](https://www.rxhistories.com/consumer-inquiries/).
- Request your prescription history report by following the instructions provided.

**Tips**: Ensure your prescription information is correct to avoid issues with insurance underwriting.

**The Work Number (Equifax Workforce Solutions)**

**Purpose**: Provides employment and income verification services to lenders, employers, and government agencies.

**Relevance**: Used during loan applications, job screenings, and eligibility determinations for government programs.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [The Work Number](https://www.theworknumber.com/).
- Use the “Employee” section to request your Employment Data Report (EDR) or call 1-866-604-6570.

**Tips**: Regularly check your employment data to ensure accuracy, especially before applying for loans or jobs.

**CoreLogic Teletrack**

**Purpose**: Provides credit and risk assessment information, focusing on non-traditional credit providers like payday lenders and rent-to-own businesses.

**Relevance**: If you’ve engaged with alternative financial services, your information may be included here.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [CoreLogic Teletrack](https://www.corelogic.com/solutions/teletrack/).
- Call 1-877-309-5226 to request your consumer report.

**Tips**: Review this report to understand how alternative credit activities impact your financial profile.

**MicroBilt Corporation**

**Purpose**: Offers credit and background screening services, including alternative credit data.

**Relevance**: Used by lenders and employers to assess creditworthiness and reliability.

**How to Access**:

- Visit [MicroBilt](https://www.microbilt.com/).
- Contact their customer service at 1-800-884-4747 to request your report.

**Tips**: Ensure the information is accurate, especially if seeking employment or credit from companies using MicroBilt services.
