Practical Goal-Setting

Below are some pointers that must be taken into consideration when setting goals and achieving them.

*Attitude plays an extremely large role in setting and achieving your goals. You should ask yourself if any part of you or your mind is holding you back from completing your goals? Is there are any part of your actions that is being an obstruction or puts your plans into disorder? If you do have problems in these areas then the immediate thing to do is to tackle this problem. 

*Careers are made by excellent time management practice. Failing in a career is often credited to bad time management. 

*Education is key in achieving your goals. If your goals require you to have a certain type of degree or need a certain specialization or require a certain ability to be developed, make plans to get the proper education.

*Your family should never be left out of your plans. 

*Personal finances also play a key role in achieving your goals. Set a realistic goal on how much you actually desire to earn. You also should be able to generate plans or stages by which you will be able to attain your earning potential.

*Determining your physical capability should be one of your priorities. Physical limits can however be conquered with appropriate planning.

*As the saying goes -'All work and no play makes Jack a dull boy', or something to that effect. Giving yourself a little time for pleasure ought to be incorporated into your plans.

To begin achieving your lifetime goals, lay down a twenty-five year plan, then break it down further to five year plans. Further, break it down another time to one-year plans, and then six month plans then monthly plans, then weekly, then daily.

Then create a things-to-do list for the day.
