**[What guys really wants: Deciphering non-verbal signs]{.ul}**

Ever wonder if a guy is giving you a signal? It\'s not easy to decipher
the mind of the modern male, but learning to decode what his nonverbal
communication truly means is an invaluable asset in the game of love. So
in order to be sure you\'re reading him right, look out for the
following 10 signs.

**1. Leaning into Love:** 

If a guy is interested in you, you\'ll find that his body will lean
forward toward yours. This move can be either very subtle or extremely
\"in your face\" (literally!). It\'s his way of letting you know he\'d
like to get even closer. Once his interest is piqued, you\'ll both find
it hard to pull away!\
 

**2. Keeping It Even Closer:** 

A vital aspect of the physical nature of romance is reciprocity.
Translation: meeting his advances with your own. This not only signals
your own interest, but also serves to keep his.

**3. Look into his eyes**

If he\'s interested in you, he\'ll focus on you with those piercing
glances and *hold* it. Return his romantic gaze with a quiet smile and
let him know that you are interested. He\'ll be at your side in no time.

 4. **Touching Is a Good Thing** 

If a guy is interested in you, then he\'ll want to be near you. He\'ll
also want to take every opportunity to touch you. Maybe it\'s your arm,
your leg, your knee --- it doesn\'t matter, as long as his presence is
physical and affectionate. It\'s his way of letting you know he likes
you.\
 

**5. Funny Meeting You Here** 

He shows up at all your favourite hangout places. This may not be
accidental after all. He may be signalling that he\'s trying to connect
with you in the most casual of ways. Take this as a positive sign and
make the most of your next encounter.

 

**6. Listen Closely**

 For a man to shut up and really listen to someone other than himself,
you know it must be love (or at least a strong attraction).

**7. Funny Lady**

Told a very bad joke but he laughs at it anyway? Men are very in touch
with their sense of humour, so if he\'s sending some hearty laughter
your way, it\'s probably because he\'s interested.

 

**8.Nervousness** 

Look for signs like unexplained laughter, sweaty palms and fidgeting.
Guys always want to be in control of their emotions but if he has
trouble doing that around you, it\'s most likely because you make him
nervous and excited. Don\'t take it for granted; help him to relax, and
he\'ll thank you by being a great guy you can depend on.
