# Understanding the behavior of your Bengal cat

Understanding the behavior of your Bengal cat
Do you have Bengal cat in your house and are confused due to their unpredicted behavior? It is not your fault but it is their real behavior. Bengal cats are of little wild nature but they need love and attention to become complete domestic. If you have little knowledge about how to take good care of Bengal cat, it is essential for you to understand the behavior of Bengals in order to keep them lovingly in house. You need to spend quality time with your new pet if you really want to get familiar with it. They need attention and lacking it may results in emotional stress in cats.
Space is another thing which you need to consider as they require more space to settle comfortably. Make sure to make their space before bringing them home. The fact is they require their own personal space where they can stay comfortably. It is more important if you have any other pet in your house. Bengal cats are natural hunters and if you have any fish in the house, it can become prey of your Bengal any time. You need to give special care to Bengal in order to make them more loving and calm. Also, you it is essential not to allow them to roam outside freely. They are cute, good looking and adorable pet. Thus, chances of getting stolen are more, so try keeping them inside. 
Bengal cats also need regular grooming just like other pets but grooming is much easier for them. They are very active, much more as compared to traditional cats. Thus, it is important to give them a regular walk and other exercises to keep it healthy. It is essential to keep a permanent bowl of water near the house of your Bengal cat. So, by following above few things, you can easily understand the behavior of your Bengal in very short time.
