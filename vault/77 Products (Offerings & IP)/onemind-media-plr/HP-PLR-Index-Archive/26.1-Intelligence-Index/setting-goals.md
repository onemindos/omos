# setting-goals

                                      Setting goals

Why
In this story I will tell you why setting goals will significantly improve your rate of
achievement, and have power over that which you would like to achieve. No longer will
dreams be deception.
Once you get the hang of it, and others see your accomplishments you will earn the
respect of others. Finally you will stop being the loser who doesn’t get what he wants.
Imagine yourself achieving your first goal. Learning a new language? Losing an amount of
weight? You did it! Not another failed attempt. But another accomplishment!

What
Setting goals is how you communicate what you want to achieve from your conscious mind
to your subconscious. By setting goals you create amazing potential.
This method works because we use 4 ways that give the right touch to your goals.
Positivity, the present moment, make it specific and getting the message across. How
many times did negative ideas really work out? Or only feeling the past? That’s because
those things can not influence goals the way you’d like to.
-We’ll discuss how we can give positivity to your goals.
-We’ll talk about the present moment and why the past is the past.
-Making it specific.
-getting the message across will be the final steps.

How
The positive form
Goals require positivity. A positive influence, full of love, can be the determining factor
for achieving your goal or not. This might be hard for some people. Instead of “I don’t
want to be fat”, change it to, “I want an amazing body”. Your subconscious mind is a bit
tricky. By constantly thinking, “I don’t want to be fat”, your mind will think “I want to be
fat”. The message will go through and it will be on its way to achieving being fat.
If you find it hard to set positivity, I want you to do this. The following days, think of
EVERY situation as a positive situation. Not just the goals you want, but EVERYTHING must
be positive. (Not only will it help your goals, your overall life will improve). Positivity is
the way to go! If you have trouble with this. Write a P on your hand. So every time you
look at your hand, you see the P for positivity. Not only read it, but acknowledge it.
The present moment
Something else that can mess up your subconscious mind, is that the subconscious cannot
think of time. The subconscious is not smart, yet very powerful. It has no idea of the past
or future. It lives in the now.
It will work towards achieving the goal you’ve set. So by saying, “I want an amazing body”.
It will keep on working towards wanting that. Instead of saying “I want an amazing body,
transfer it to “I have an amazing body”. Now the subconscious will work towards having
instead of wanting.
Specifics
Like I said, your subconscious isn’t smart. Make your goal as specific as possible! It needs
to know as much as possible! What you want to achieve, how to achieve it and when to
achieve it. Specifically as possible.
“It is the 20th of June 2014 and I have an amazing body by working out every day”.
This way it makes it easier for the subconscious and conscious to communicate.
The message across
Congratulations! You know have a goal. Let’s get it into the subconscious.
Getting it in the subconscious isn’t difficult if you have done it a couple of times. Use as
many of your senses as possible! Write your goal out, read it, say it so that you can hear it,
and feel how you will feel like if you already have it. Imagine the moment in your head,
first person view, and feel all the emotions that come with it. The subconscious will rule.
Do it as much as possible. You can change the goal however you like.

What if
Everything is possible if you apply the information correctly. Play with it, experience it,
achieve your goals to the fullest! Apply them right! Get them in the subconscious! You’ll be
amazed of its power. Life is in YOUR hands.
You can use it anywhere you like. Anytime you like. Set a goal now. Set easy goals,
difficult goals. You know how to do them!
