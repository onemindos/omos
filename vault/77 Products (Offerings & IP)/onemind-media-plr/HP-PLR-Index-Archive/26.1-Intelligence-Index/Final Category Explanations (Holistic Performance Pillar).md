### These are the 7 fundamental self-mastery domains that make up your personal operating system under Holistic Performance.

### 🧬 **Performance**

> _Your body and biology as an engine._

> Tracks physical output, recovery, readiness, and internal regulation.

> Includes:

- Fitness, sleep, energy, HRV, nutrition
- Mental clarity, mood, biometrics
- Tracking daily performance indicators

---

### 🎯 **Development**

> _Skill growth through action and repetition._

> Where you build new disciplines: tactical, creative, mental, martial.

> Includes:

- Chess, coding, piano, language, airsoft, boxing, public speaking
- Performance challenges and mastery sprints

---

### 💰 **Finance**

> _Personal money control, growth, and financial intelligence._

> Your full personal finance ecosystem.

> Includes:

- Budgeting, emergency funds, savings
- Credit building, debt tracking, financial literacy
- Personal investing (crypto, ETFs, stocks)

---

### ⚖️ **Sovereignty**

> _Identity, protection, and legal readiness._

> Everything that protects your rights, identity, and freedom.

> Includes:

- Insurance, POAs, legal docs, licenses
- Estate prep (basic)
- Identity protection tools and digital security

---

### 🌿 **Lifeflow**

> _The way you live, express, and experience joy._

> This is your rhythm of life. Personal rituals, entertainment, aesthetics.

> Includes:

- Travel, exploration, gear, style, grooming
- Entertainment, social outings, creative play
- Recovery rituals and reset weekends

---

### 🤝 **Connection**

> _The quality and frequency of your relationships._

> Intentional management of your social, romantic, and family networks.

> Includes:

- 1:1 rituals, events, outreach
- Relationship scorecards
- Community and circle building

---

### 🎓 **Education**

> _Ongoing learning, skill stacking, and knowledge integration._

> Where you track how you’re expanding your mind.

> Includes:

- Books, podcasts, courses, note capture
- Skill tree development
- Certification tracking
