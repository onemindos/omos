# Caring your Bengal to make them complete domestic

Caring your Bengal to make them complete domestic
Bengal cats are adorable to cat lovers due to their active and domestic behavior. They have around 12 percent of wild behavior but taking good care of Bengal cats can make them completely domestic. If someone is planning to bring Bengal cat at home, it is essential to give them good space in home where your kitty can roam in comfortably. They are fond of playing with water and you can easily become friend by playing with them for some time. Just make sure to create additional space as their permanent home where they can comfortably sit and eat. They need their own personal space especially during the first few days and make sure to complete this requirement to make them complete domestic.
Do not make mistake of locking Bengal inside your home as they may damage your costly furniture as they easily become victim of emotional stress. Bengal cats make an unfamiliar or very unique sound just to tell own dominance. It is more common if you have any other pet in the house. Bengal cats also enjoy walking outside and it is better to take them away for a walk. They have bit wild nature and thus they can run suddenly after a bird or any living being. It is a cat which is best suited to those who have experience of keeping cat in the home. 
There are different kinds of activities Bengal cats do in the home and that is why sometimes they become restless. It is true that Bengal cats are demanding, quirky and entertaining. Interestingly, in very short time they will become worst enemy of all the rodents available in your house. You are going to have lot of entertainment if you are planning to bring a Bengal cat in home. Despite from good looks, they show off their affection for owner. So, by doing all the above few things you can easily make your Bengal a complete domestic.
