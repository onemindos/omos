                                          Introduction

Hello, my name is Rita. I'm from Russia. And I want to introduce my new notebook. It is not so
big and consists of only 11 notes. But it will be enough to start looking upon things in a bold
new way. Here you will find different examples and wonderful stories about how my wishes
came true, and also some my thoughts on this theme. This notebook will be useful for those
who want to change their life for the better. After reading you will find that you can attract
absolutely everything into your life and that any desire can become a reality.
                  A Trip to Bali Island with the Help of The Law of Attraction

I really wanted to go to Bali Island, but I had money only for a plan ticket (it was half of the
cost of the trip). And that’s when everything started. I wasn’t going to use any techniques to
specially attract my wish. It all happened so as it happened and only later I understood what
was really going on and how my wish came true. Though, of course, you can call it accident,
coincidence and luck.

The plan ticket was bought immediately. And this is a very important moment, because now
there was no turning back. The airplane was supposed to fly through 3 months. That’s how
much time I had to raise enough money. I counted all my future salary for this time, all my
money that I already had, and it turned out that I didn’t have still 30 thousand (rubles). I
didn’t have any source of income except my official job. And I convulsively began to guess,
how I can get this money. In fact it turned out that I had to earn the additional 10 thousand
(rubles) a month during the next 3 months. I tried to make up different ideas. Among them
were: to do any custom drawings for students, to carry out the course projects, to do
mathematic sums. In all, these were the same things that I did before in University. But then,
I’ve given it some thought, and I’ve understood that I will not going to get very far with such
methods. Moreover, I needed a lot of time to practice it and to get some skills in this matter.
But I didn’t have so much free time on my hands. I was free only in the evenings. So,
everything was sad. And this is where I got a genius idea. And it was really genius!
I will earn more money on my official job than anywhere else until I have enough skills on this
“anywhere else”.

But why should I learn a new skill, if I already have an advanced one – it is a skill that I use on
my current job. It means that if I compare my current skill to any new skill in the same period
of time (for example in my free evening) then it will be obvious that I will get more money on
my current direction. But on the other hand I wonder how I can get more money on my
current skill, if it isn’t used anywhere except my job. Of course, sometimes we get a signing
bonus, but it isn’t controlled by you in all. So, in general, I couldn’t think of anything logical
and working on 100 per cent. I just gave it up and decided that I will borrow money from my
brother before leaving. So, that’s all. A question of money didn’t bother me anymore. And I
got rid of convulsions that lasted several days. And what do you think? In about two weeks my
boss called me and told: “There is one project. We have never done this before, but I will hire
an expert on this topic and you will do it together with him. Do you want to do this?”

And I didn’t even think about it, I just agreed and that’s all. I also remember that when I
started to deal with this project, it was so strange and hard. And I also thought: Why would I
do that? I could just sit, drink tea, discuss the news and look through the window, as usual...
But then the job went well, I dealt with all of this and even the excitement wakes up in me. I
was really interested to make it all. I looked at the different examples for the project, read a lot
of literature, communicated with people, spent a lot of late nights on the job, sometimes even
came home in the morning, slept 3-4 hours and ran back. So, one month has passed. The
main work under the project was finished. And then we solved just some separate questions.
And so, when it was the end of the first month, my boss suddenly came to me and said that I
made a good job and, therefore, he will give me a signing bonus amount of 10 thousand
(rubles)… That was the news! And since this project was still in progress, I was getting such
bonus even further. It means that in sum I got the additional 10 thousand (rubles) for 3
months: I got exactly as much as I needed for the trip…

So, it was how invisible and seemed to be casually my wish came true and my genus idea was
confirmed. And I suspect that all of this happened because I have complied with all main rules
for making my wish come true, even without knowing it.

Firstly, I’ve made the first step – I’ve bought the ticket and there was no turning back. (And
something tells me that if I just wanted to get 30 thousand for something uncertain, then
nothing would happen. The law of attraction wouldn’t work out). Secondly, I have found the
alternative solution - to borrow money from my brother. Thirdly, I have accepted the offer - I
have agreed to make a new project, although initially there was no talk about any signing
bonus. And I also want to say that if I specially sat and invented how I can get this money
(even if it were fantastic thoughts), I would never guess exactly this path in all. And I think that
it also can be taken as a rule – don’t try to guess the solution yourself, because it will be the
most ugly, terrible and untruth solution from all possible variants. It will better if you make
your job, and the Law of Attraction makes its.
