Stub out that fag before it stubs out your life

There are many disadvantages to smoking. So let us lay down all the hard
facts of what being addicted to smoking could get you.

Smoking causes many bad physical effects. It strains your heart and
blood vessels. It hardens the blood vessel and causes it to constrict.
This reduces blood flow. The heart will then have to pump harder to
supply oxygenated blood to the whole body. This may result in a heart
attack if your heart gives out. The carbon monoxide in the smoke
deprives your body of oxygen. Over time, your airways start to swell up,
letting less air into the lung. Furthermore smoking causes chronic
obstructive pulmonary disease. It is irreversible lung damage. The next
time you won't be puffing on your cigarettes but will need the use of
inhalers just to help you with you breathing.

Smoking also causes detrimental mental effects to the smoker. It may be
hard to believe but most compulsive smokers are otherwise considered as
smoking addicts. You could get addicted to it and your body will be
constantly craving for your nicotine fix just to get you through a
normal day. When you don't smoke, you get nervous and fidgety. You also
wouldn't be able to concentrate in your job so your productivity at work
will decrease. When trying to quit, there will also be withdrawal
symptoms. You will be easily agitated and sometimes depressed. So why
would you want to start something so detrimental to your health?

What are the social effects of smoking? Smoking does not make you cooler
or endear you to more people. You don't make friends more easily when
you are a smoker. When you smoke, you are just limiting yourself to a
group of people who smoke only. Look at restaurants, it is divided into
a smoking and non-smoking section. Furthermore, smoking also causes bad
breath and stained yellow teeth. What is so attractive about an
individual who is puffing like a chimney? In addition, smoking ages you
and you look so much older than people of your own age.

In conclusion, smoking is detrimental not only to your health but also
to other aspects of your life. So stub out that fag before it stubs out
your life.
