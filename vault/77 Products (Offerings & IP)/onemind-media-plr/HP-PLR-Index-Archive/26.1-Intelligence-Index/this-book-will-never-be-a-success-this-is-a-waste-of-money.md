                 Why i wrote this book

This is for all of you, who have been stuck in destructive

thinking patterns over the years. You are conscious that

this type of thinking is not helping you to be who you

were met to be! God wants each of us to sore high like

the eagles and in order to do that, we must be very

careful of the words that come out of our mouths! Are

those words uplifting people around you or are they

destroying them? Are your words of victory or defeat?

Are you proud of who you have become?

This little book will surely make you question some of

your thinking process. It might be exactly what you

need! You know that little push, to make you want to

start thinking on a higher level. Ask God to help you on
                                                           5
this journey. I pray you become the light that shines the

brightest!




                                                            6
                         Chapter 1


                     Be aware of it


I can’t. I won’t. It will never work. I’ll fail. What was I

thinking?

Those are all words that have at one point come out of

your mouths, am I right? Yes, I know I am!

What people don’t seemed to realise is that negative

words, are so powerful, and they take root in your heart

and form barriers, doubts and fears.

Those powerful destructive words will eventually,

without even you realising it, become those fears and

doubts. You will quickly built your own barriers all


                                                              7
around you, leaving all the great possibilities outside

those big walls of yours!

Is it working for you? I’ll ask again “Is it working for

you?”

You know it’s not but still, you’ve been feeling stuck for

years in this destructive thinking pattern. You need to

look at yourself in the mirror, and be honest in

recognising that you have indeed been stuck in that

negative pattern for way too long! In Matthew 12:34,

Jesus says “For the mouth speaks, what the heart is full

off”.

I suggest you ask yourself those questions:




                                                           8
1. Why have you allowed yourself to stay in that

   pattern?

2. What contributed to you starting to have that type

   of destructive pattern?

3. What are you so afraid of?

4. Do you believe that you have the right to be

   happy?

5. Do you believe God can help you? Would you at

   least let Him try?

6. Would you like to change your perception of

   life?




                                                    9
7. Are you willing to do the work and not find

   excuses?

8. What are you grateful for? And why?

9. What if this week was your last week? Would

   you be content with what you have achieved and

   how you got there?




You need to take time to answer those questions!

Don’t find excuses, do not try to convince yourself

this is not important and that you have to run again to

do some important task just to push that away! If you

are not happy about where you are right now, starting

by answering those questions could help you to push

                                                      10
you in the right direction. We all need to face the

truth one way or another. It’s not worth it to put your

head in the sand, hoping it will all go away when you

know, it doesn’t. The 30 minutes it will take you to

answer those questions will allow you to get to know

yourself better. Which is probably something you

haven’t done in a while! Do you even know what

you like anymore? Do you even have dreams

anymore?

See, I told you, this was a crucial step!




                                                      11
                         Chapter 2


        Pay attention to your surroundings


Now that you are aware of those patterns, you need to

create new healthy ones. Let me add that your entourage

and your surroundings play a key role on your attitude or

your behavior.

If you live in a home where everything is scattered on

the floor, nothing has its place, you trip on toys or shoes

each time you get in the house, you have piles of dishes

in the sink, you have more dirty clothes than clean ones,

that’s absolutely not going to help you change your

attitude or thoughts.



                                                         12
If you have created yourself a depressing area, you will

surely become over time, very frustrated and have

tendencies to become angry faster, plus your attitude will

be very far from being positive!

But you have to recognise that YOU have created this

and luckily there’s a way out! Take responsibility!

It’s not your parent’s fault, your sister’s fault, or your

spouse’s fault. You made decisions and you have to deal

with them now. Those who are not working, let them go

and start making new efficient and constructive ones!

We talked about your home but there’s also the question

of who you are hanging out with! What kind of people




                                                             13
have you let into your life? What are their attitudes and

behaviors like?

If you think about it long enough, surely you’ll see the

pattern here! You are probably not the only one with the

destructive pattern. I’m certain some of your family

members and some of your friends are too, on that same

path. If most of the time, your conversations are geared

towards complaining about everything and anything, you

are definitely not surrounded with positive role models!

You have to make a very important and hard decision. If

you want to give yourself a chance of having a better life

and of stopping the destructive thinking pattern, you

need to stop surrounding yourself with people who do

just that! You should be inspired to better yourself by
                                                           14
your friends and family members, certainly not to

continue on being negative, which never brings anything

constructive or positive.

Hate doesn’t bring love, so why think that being negative

would bring positive changes in your life?

I can remember a time when I was so frustrated that

darkness was my middle name! I was full of anger

towards my mother who was a very violent person and

always complaining about what I could have done better.




                                                      15
                         Chapter 3


          Where is the pain coming from?


I was angry about all those times I had to go through

sexual harassment or sexual assaults, which happened

too many times! I was angry about not having control on

my life and the only way I knew how to express this, was

through anger, bitterness and frustrations!

Did it work for me? I taught it did for a while, until a

good friend of mine, told me that he could no longer deal

with my destructive attitude, and if I wanted us to remain

friends, I would have to get help and fast!

It kind of shook me up, since I really appreciated the

company of this man! And thank God, I followed his
                                                           16
advice because I am absolutely certain we would not be

married today if I had not! You never know who God

will put on your path. And if you have not done your

homework or least tried, you might very well, miss the

opportunity of your life!

Of course, just the fact that I had decided to do

something about my lack of control, did not mean all

was suddenly perfect! I actually had to work extremely

hard at it and for many years, but it got easier over time

since I was able to get the tools needed to go forward

which helped me to stop going backwards!

It’s much easier to always repeat our behaviors since

they are well known to us and we feel safe around them.


                                                         17
They act as friends but we just don’t realise they are not

the kind of friends we need in our lives!

There is no simpler way to get out of a bad behavior and

get back the control of your life: You need to work hard

and long but most of all, you need to persevere when the

hard times will arrive because they will and it can make

things even harder for a while. That’s where the

perseverance comes in handy. No one said that life

would be like a bed of roses. God tells us, He will be

with us when we walk through the valley of the shadow

of death. He did not say, we will never go through those

valleys, He explicitly said, He would be there when we

do. So, strap yourself tightly and walk with you head



                                                         18
high. You can do it. You need to tell yourself again and

again, that you will and you can.




                                                       19
                        Chapter 4


      Do you believe that you can be happy?


I remember that my psychologist had asked me that

complicated question: “Are you happy?”

I wasn’t able to answer. I tried to create in my mind the

answer I was supposed to say but the “yes” never came. I

was trying hard to explain to him, how not being happy

wasn’t that bad. That it was the norm for many people

and we can continue with our lives even if we are not in

a state of happiness. What a sad answer! Only a very sad

person might think of an answer like that and actually

believe that it makes sense. But it doesn’t.



                                                         20
God did not create us to be miserable people. He put us

on this earth with the intention of us being happy. And to

be happy, we need to follow some simple rules. Be

accountable for our lives and the consequences of our

decisions. If we always find someone to blame for every

bad thing that happens in our lives, we have missed the

boat!

Yes, of course, some things happen and it was not due to

our own doing, and it’s pretty difficult to go through the

havoc that someone else as created. We’ve all been

through those situations. But whether it is our fault or

not, the goal is to react properly to that bad situation.

God wants us to act and react with grace and with total

faith in Him. We might not understand what is going on
                                                            21
but we need to stay still, breathe, think, pray on it and

wait for a while before diving in that mess that we could

probably make a lot worse if we are too quick to react.

Don’t start saying that you’ll never be happy, that

happiness is only for others. Please! Stop it and focus

your wondering mind. You need to be the boss of your

thoughts. Don’t let the enemy play with you like a cat

does a mouse. Come on, you are an adult and you were

created with intelligence, quick wit, and the capability of

learning from a bad situation in order to make something

good out of it. Aren’t you tired of being miserable?

Wouldn’t you rather be brave in the face of adversity?

Feeling brave can be learned! You learned how to



                                                            22
always diminish yourself but now it’s time to go at it

another way!

Buy inspirational and motivational books. Go to

seminars that encourages you to better yourself. Go to

your nearest church and listen carefully to the weekly

teachings. Teach yourself on how to be happy.

Everything can be taught and learn. Reprogram your

attitude, and thinking patterns. Stay away for a while

from those negative influences, you know, those friends

who can just complain about everything all the time even

if it’s a sunny day and they won the lottery…it will never

be enough! Surround your house with positive

reinforcement items: for example, a nice frame with a

positive message that you would put on your night stand.
                                                         23
Little sticky notes on your fridge with a list of positive

actions to take for the day or week. Positive TV viewing

only. No more depressing dramas or documentaries.

Stick to comedies and uplifting movies. Learn something

new at your school, like a writing class or jewelry

making class or learn how to dance!




                                                             24
                         Chapter 5


               It’s easier than you think


You need to embrace your soon to be “new you”.

Visualize yourself and do this many times per day. If you

want the new dress, aren’t you going to imagine yourself

parading in it every second of the day? Exactly! You

understand where I’m going with that.

Consider this journey, the greatest adventure you will

ever be on. Except, the results and the journey itself, will

be much more gratifying and fulfilling then a simple trip

to Colorado. It will be life changing and for the better.

When you start to change, little by little, you will at first

notice it in your face. Yes, the expression in your eyes
                                                            25
will be different as well as the tightness of your skin.

You see, the muscles in your face will be more relaxed

thus your skin will not be the same. You can’t look

refreshed and calm if you keep being negative and are

always complaining! Did you think your mom looked

amazing when she was screaming at you to take your

clothes off the floor? Her looking great was the last thing

on your mind!

Joking aside, all your body is affected by your thinking

pattern. Everything. It can do some major changes inside

and outside your body. The more you are consumed by

hatred and anger, the more you will hurt. You will

probably have some back problems, stomach problems,



                                                           26
big headaches, trouble with your digestion and even

some heart palpitations just to name a few.

Everything is related. Our bodies can’t properly function

if we don’t treat them right. If we put major stress on

them by being depressed and aggressive, our bodies will

eventually fight back but you will not appreciate “how”

it will be done. All those symptoms listed above will just

go into overdrive and you will end up very sick or even

worse, at the emergency room. If you have not yet gone

that way, do what you can to avoid that trip! It’s not

worth it. I suggest, you start your journey by analyzing

your eating habits. How and what you eat plays a huge

role on your attitude and your willingness to thrive on

positive thinking. I know it’s much easier to drown your
                                                           27
sorrows and your frustrations with a big bowl of chips or

simply to eat that tub of ice cream, I’ve done it, I

know...but you need to realize while saying it out loud,

that it doesn’t work for you! You feel miserable after

you’ve done it. You probably were stuck on the toilet for

an hour with diarrhea and on top of everything, you gain

3 pounds. Worth it? No.

Eat a lot of fruits and veggies. Start with your 3 favorite

fruits and eat a lot of them instead of jumping in that

family size bag of chips. Start making yourself

smoothies in the morning. Using bananas and frozen

strawberries, is a good start. You can add natural peanut

butter to have more protein and you won’t get hungry 2

minutes later. Ditch that donut and huge cup of coffee
                                                          28
with a ton of sugary stuff in it. That is not helping at all.

When your blood sugar goes down, it only help getting

you more depressed and cranky.

You need to make a change whether you like it or not.

You can’t expect to have a healthy mind if you don’t

have a healthy body. You don’t expect your car to run

with no gas, so why expect your mind to work well, if

you feed it crap?

Buy yourself a small and simple vegan book or raw food

recipe book and just change 1 meal out of 3 to start with,

and take out off your daily food intake, all those sugary

things. You can keep one a day but not 5 a day! Just go

at the restaurant once a week and cut your caffeine

intake to a minimum.
                                                           29
Those are very easy changes that you can make and they

will affect positively your body and mind. On the plus

side, as a bonus, you will surely lose some weight and

gain clarity of mind. Your face will feel more relaxed

and you will start thinking differently not feeling the

need to stay angry or to become angry at a certain

situation that otherwise would have made you very

frustrated. I am telling you by experience, if you change

your eating habits and throw out the crap, you will thank

me later. Like I said, everything is connected. The mind,

body and spirit.




                                                          30
                         Chapter 6


     Give yourself a chance, you are worth it


Maybe you have spent too much time feeling sorry for

myself and blaming everyone for your misfortune? But,

why not look at you and what you can do instead of what

you can’t?

You surely have lots of qualities and gifts. I am certain

that you have great talents that you forget to use, because

you are too busy complaining about what you don’t

have. Start by listing all that you love about yourself:

Your qualities. You need to see it with your own eyes so

I suggest you write those down. No, you just don’t have

one. Look further into your soul. Get acquainted with

                                                            31
this person, you seemed to have forgotten for way too

long. Everybody has qualities, they just need to be

reminded of them so they can shine and take the first role

of your life. Stop seeing the glass half empty and see it

overflowing. See yourself as a gift from God. You know,

He has great plans for you, you just need to give yourself

a chance. You know the chance, that you wished

someone had given you not too long ago, well, you need

to give it to yourself now. What do you have to lose?

Nothing. You are done with losing. You are now going

to start referring to yourself as a winner, a fighter, a

brave person who as endure a lot but who is presently

transforming herself for the better. Always think of

yourself as an asset. You are worth a lot. You can help

                                                            32
so many people who are lacking love and

companionship. If you are always too busy being angry,

you can’t help anyone with that state of mind but if you

allow yourself to become a success story, you will be

able to help others become who they were met to be.

You could be someone’s mentor. Someone’s friend. This

is priceless!

Start slowly. It will take a while but don’t worry you will

get there. I absolutely need to suggest to you to always

talk to God. This journey of a lifetime, needs the

guidance of the All Mighty. He will tell you when you

are not on the right path and He will guide you back on

your path of truth. This journey will be the most fabulous

trip you have ever taken. Most of all, the most important
                                                           33
one. Don’t take this lightly! By understanding how to

save your life and the importance of it, you will then be

able to save others who are simply waiting without even

knowing it, for you to make the first move.

Get moving my friend, it’s your time to shine and to get

out of the darkness. Forgive yourself and others. Get rid

of all that unnecessary luggage. That bright start you see

from afar: Is you!
