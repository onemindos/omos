their relationship. To be honest, and as far as I could remember, all the
beautiful girls with whom he had been in love with or who had fallen under
his romantic sledgehammer hardly lasted three to four months in the
relationship.

Yesterday as he related the story on how his anger was seriously affecting
his love life with his current girlfriend, I felt so bad. Honestly, I wish I could
help advance his relationship in some way; however, it was evident that I
couldn’t. Finally in the course of our conversation and in words marred with
disappointment, anguish and pain, he intimated me on how their relationship
was gradually hitting the ice berg due to his suspicion that his girlfriend,
Tina, was cheating on him, and how she in turn had accused him of lacking
anger-management skills which was one of the major subject in his field as a
psychologist.

At the initial when I heard the story I laughed with uncontrollable hysteria,
and as you know, that was the first time I heard and added the word, anger-
management skills to my vocabulary. However, few months down the line
after he told me his challenge the relationship went down the drain as they
both left each other in pain, hurt and broken hearted!

The truth is, we are living in a World of them-against-us where every one
have enough reasons to be defensive, be suspicious, angry and to be going
on the offensive! It always seems that something is missing or that we are
facing a war machine that has already set us off-balanced! I might not know
what that war machine is in your own life. But for some, it is the cost of
facing a failed relationship like our psychology student or being hunted down
with a dying health; for others, it is wrestling with the bitter memories of the
past; still for others it could be they are butting their heads and are locked-
in as a target with no job offer!

We all have gone through many phases; phases of negativity and
uncertainty, or where it seems that our lives and destinies, according to Ron
Elsdon, are being controlled by circumstances over which we have no
control. And sometimes when we switch on our TV set in hope of getting
away from “feeling broken and being patched” in order to regain our
wholeness, our emotional release and security, we’re soon greeted with the
story of hostile takeover, lay-off and economic woes, or if worse come to
worse, of terrorist bombs going off without warning; environmental disaster,
chronic inflation and financial meltdown all of which have become a threat to
our lives, safety and existence!
