# Are you bringing a Bengal kitten to your house

Are you bringing a Bengal kitten to your house?
Some people misinterpret that Bengal cats are wild in nature and can be dangerous for children. It is true that they are wild but Asian cat is used for developing Bengal cat and this Asian cat is small, shy and avoid people. The most important part is that Bengals are bred only for domestic purposes. However, if you are bringing a Bengal kitten to your house, it is essential to take special care of giving them a right kind of food. Most veterinarians suggest that Bengals are okay with dry food but giving dry food alone is not enough. Moist and canned food is good for Bengal cats but it is essential to give it in an appropriate quantity. 
Most kittens at the age group of 1 to 4 weeks would like to eat chewy food. They are finicky as well, so they can oppose eating any food which does not suits them. Wet food is also available in foils and you can use it during first 2 to 3 weeks. It is essential to include meat in the diet of Bengals but it is good to introduce meat after 3 weeks of their birth. You can also give corn products during first few weeks but reduce the quantity as they grow. You can also find different kinds of cat foods in the market and that in different flavors. You can use those flavors and your Bengal will certainly like any of those flavors. 
Dry, wet and moist foods are also available in the flavor of lamb, chicken and beef. You can also give the combination of flavors. Bengal kittens have quite sensitive digestion and thus it is essential to give them food with proper care. Bengals keep hungry all time but make sure to feed during intervals of time to prevent health troubles. So, follow the above simple guide for good caring of Bengal kittens.
