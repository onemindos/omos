# When outsourcing goes bad

When outsourcing goes bad: what causes it?

Outsourcing when planned properly will ensure success for you business but when poorly executed will spell losses for your company. So here are a few reasons why outsourcing fails

The process is not clear.
The company fail to define the scope and schedule of the project. From the start you didn’t provide the service provide with your project requirements and your work schedule. You need to map out what you expect from the project and how you want it done. 

No background research on the service provider
You didn’t do a background search on the experience of the service provider and chose them because of the price that they offered. In the end you will get shoddy work and not your money’s worth.

Not phasing
Good outsourcing is done in phases. If you just give one whole chunk of project to a service provider, you might find out that you get shoddy work. Instead give it in phases. You are most likely to find that you retain control over what is happening and you make sure that everything in that phase is perfect before you move on to the next phase.

Outsourcing everything. 
When you want to ensure that everything is run smoothly and allowing you to retain control, don’t outsource everything. Just outsource certain part of the projects. This will offload some of the burden that you have and allow you to concentrate on the core part of your project. If you outsource the whole project, you might find that the end result might not be to your liking.

Effective reporting
Most of the time the service provider will send you detailed information of how the work is progressing. Look at the information, and identify what information that is essential for you to control your process. Get the provider to split the information into 2 or 3 segments or deliver that daily information as a weekly report. When effective reporting takes place, this allows you to be able to study the report more closely instead of being inundated with a lengthy report everyday.


So here are the few things you can learn to avoid poor outsourcing. Remember if outsourcing is done right, it can lead to your business success.
