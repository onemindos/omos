**Discovering the Laws of Attraction**

Thoughts, like everything else around us, have energy. By thinking about
something, you have the power of turning the object of your thought into
a reality. And that, is the most fundamental explanation of the Law of
Attraction, which states that what we consistently think about and have
emotions is what is going to become reality.

Whatever dominant thought is present in our awareness day in and day out
is most likely going to be the actual thing that we are going to
attract. How this happens is from thought to focused thought to
realisation of that thought. How our feeling play a part in all of this
is by telling us what we are attracting. Simply put, if you are thinking
and feeling good, you will be attracting the good stuff; if you are
thinking and feeling bad, you will be attracting the bad stuff.

There are three steps to attracting all your desires:

1.  Ask for it -- Figure out what you want and how you want it.
    Visualise it in its entirety. Focus your thoughts. Then ask for it.

2.  Believe in Success -- You must believe, truly believe without a
    doubt that what you are asking for will be given to you. Even
    thinking of the possibility of failure will mess things up.

3.  Expect and Receive the Result -- Be proactive. Grab onto
    opportunities that come your way. Don't just lay back and wait for
    your goals to manifest magically without actively participating in
    the process.

We must have faith and expect to get what we want in order to get what
we want. This state of expectancy and faith can be learned. When you
have faith and feel good about what you want, you will definitely
attract it to us. If, on the other hand, you feel bad about still not
having what you want, you are pushing your desire away. When you start
to consciously master the laws of attraction, your life will become the
better for it.
