LEGO Star Wars Millennium Falcon -- Lowest Price On The LEGO Star Wars
Millennium Falcon 7965!

In this article we are going to look at the Lego Star Wars Millennium
Falcon 7965 which is the new model to be released from Lego and the most
sought after for Christmas 2011. If you know someone that love LEGO than
you need to check this out as it will be one of the top Christmas gifts
of 2011.

The Lego set gives you a Millennium Falcon that has been taken straight
from the movie Star Wars Episode IV: A New Hope during the escape scene
and you get stunning detail.

The hull plates open up to reveal a stunning interior, you will also
have twin flick missiles and rotating quad laser cannons and a
detachable cockpit cover. As well as that you will also get some figures
to pilot the LEGO Star Wars Millennium Falcon of course!

You will get Lego figures to go with the Falcon and these include Hans
Solo, Luke Skywalker, Chewbacca, Ben Kenobi, Princess Leia Organa and
Darth Vader.

They also come with 3 lightsabers, 3 blasters and 1 crossbow included in
the set.

Here is an overview of the LEGO Star Wars Millennium Falcon:

-   Straight from the Death Star escape scene of Episode IV: A New Hope

-   Includes 6 minifigures: Han Solo, Luke Skywalker, Chewbacca, Ben
    > Kenobi, Princess Leia Organa and Darth Vader

-   Millennium Falcon features opening hull, detailed interior, twin
    > flick missiles, rotating laser cannons and detachable cockpit
    > cover

-   3 lightsabers, 3 blasters and 1 crossbow also included

The Lego Star Wars Millennium Falcon is an outstanding set and should
not be missed!

Make sure that you get the correct model number which is 7965 or you
will be getting an older version and this new one is the most sought
after. Also this is quite a complex LEGO set and we recommend that
children aged 12+ tackle this one as it does require some building but
that is what is the fun of LEGO right?

The actual Millennium Falcon gives you over 1200 pieces so you can see
how big the set is!

You will be able to seat all of the figures in similar places inside the
ship once you have built it that you see in the movies. You will also be
able to open up the roof because it is folding panels so that is cool
when you want to play with the toy and move the figures around.

If you look as well you will also see Luke's training droid true to the
film!

You will also be able to see the table where they played virtual chess
in the film.

This toy is fantastic if you have kids or relations that are into the
Star Wars movies or EVEN for yourself as a collector's item!

When you add all of the above to the fact that you get minifigures as
well this is an awesome LEGO package for the price!
