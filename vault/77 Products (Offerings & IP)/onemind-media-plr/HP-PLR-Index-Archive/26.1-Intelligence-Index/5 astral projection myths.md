5 Astral Projection Myths

**1. You can die physically during astral projection.**

You fear of beings on the astral plane powerful enough to severe your
silver cord (attaches your astral and physical body), thereby causing
the death of your physical body. It is possible but highly unlikely.
However, while you are out of your body, your body is as susceptible to
death as it normally would be.  Someone could come in to your room and
stab or choke you to death or even shoot you.  In fact, if your
consciousness was outside your body you may not even hear them come in. 

**2. The silver cord is easily breakable.**

The silver cord connects your mind to your body and is extremely strong
and durable.  The cord can stretch a really long way and not get
tangled, torn, or cut. Prevention is possible by using white light,
golden light, call upon your guides for protection, keep your vibration
really high (avoid fear if you can help it) and ask Jesus, other
ascended masters, and the angels, and even God to keep you safe while
you travel. 

**3. Demons can possess me while I astral**

You fear if your consciousness is gone, demons can take up residence in
your body. It is highly unlikely because a demon can only possess you if
you let it or is your vibrations are so low that you can easily vacate
your body. Again, for prevention, make use of the white light.

**4. Sex with demons while astral are great!**

Astral sex with other astral beings is possible, and the orgasmic
feeling is something you will likely never experience while you're in
your body. However, having a sexual union with an entity of a low
vibrational frequency (demons) is probably not a good idea since you are
feeding it some really vital energy when you consent to that.  When it
comes to demons, abstinence is your condom.

**5. You can get lost and forget the way back to your body**

No. You can be delayed or sidetracked from getting back into your body
(e.g if you are approached by a demon wanting something from you) but
you will always be able to get back into your body if you want to. Call
upon God or an angel to help you if you run into a demon gang.
