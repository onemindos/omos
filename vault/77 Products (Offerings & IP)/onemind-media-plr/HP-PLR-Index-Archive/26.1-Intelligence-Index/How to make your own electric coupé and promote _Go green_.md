# How to make your own electric coupé and promote _Go green_

How to make your own electric coupé and promote ‘Go green’
Electric vehicles also known as electric cars or electric coupé is not a new concept and has been around since 1850’s; though with the launch of electronic combustion engine, they were nearly forgotten. Nowadays, due to threats of pollution and as the fuel prices are mounting, the benefits of electric cars are clearly visible. 
A bespoke vehicle may not be an alternative to driving. Things have changed and there are numerous petrol and diesel automotives that have been tailored to run on electricity. Vehicle owners can expect to witness huge makeover in the automobile market in the coming years. This is mostly due to the low cost of manufacturing these electric cars. There are other benefits which make the electric coupés the best choice for all vehicle owners. 
The great thing about these electric cars is that they are completely environment friendly as there is no emission. The energy stored inside the batteries is sufficient to run the wheels. This makes the electric coupés more energy and fuel efficient unlike the combustion engines which consume just around 25 percent of the energy in each liter of gasoline.  Electric cars are highly cost effective and offer a better reimbursement for the vehicle owners in terms of savings in the long run. Moreover, with improvements in technology, it is expected to become more durable and more affordable. These days, battery installed inside the electric cars come with longer shelf lives, and offers more energy back up between charging and provides best mileage. 
The electric automotives though are a bit pricey nowadays; they offer better profits to all the owners in the long run. With improvement in technology, the cost of spare parts would become more affordable and more enduring. Due to absence of internal combustion engine, electric cars are more silent and thereby contribute to reducing noise pollution. Being silent is one major feature that makes electric cars quite attractive to most vehicle owners.
