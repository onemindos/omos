# Benefits of using mantras

Benefits of using mantras

Meditation has been used as a relaxation tool worldwide. It cultivates a state of inner peace in the mind and by the end of your meditation, you are sure to feel more relaxed. Mantra meditation which is a form of meditation, cultivates a state of relaxed alertness. Mantra meditation has many benefits to offer. So let’s list down the few benefits of mantra meditation.

When you are feeling stressed the amount of adrenaline in your blood goes increases.  Your body also releases a hormone known as cortisol. Cortisol is also otherwise known as stress hormone. It is present in your blood in small amounts all the time, however, in periods of stress, the amount will suddenly shoot up and it causes physical damages to the body. Furthermore, the high level of cortisol in your blood makes your body store extra fat in your abdomen. It is no wonder that people turn to comfort food when they are feeling frustrated or stressed. During mantra meditation, you are chanting a certain phrase over and over again as your relax your body. The amount of cortisol is lowered and the mantra meditation returns your body to its formerly calm state and repairs all physical damages caused by stress.

Secondly, when you use mantra, not only you are returning your body to its original state, you are also boosting your immune system. Researches have found that non-meditating people are more likely to fall sick or catch a cold as compared to people who meditate. The reason for this is when you are feeling stressed, the stress causes physical damage to the body. You are all tired, fatigued making it easier to fall sick. However if you do mantra meditation, you are giving your body the time and luxury of relaxing. As your body relaxes, this helps repair all physical damage caused by stress, and you are more likely to become energetic and fresher after a short brief period of mantra meditation. 

Thirdly, people who does mantra meditation are less likely to become easily frustrated or stressed when faced with an obstacle or when something unfortunate has happened. This is because their body has been conditioned to accept things as they are and not to sweat the small stuff. As opposed to a non-meditator, who are more likely to react to small little petty issues with anger and frustration. 

Fourthly, mantra meditation not only increases your alertness, it also mellows your mind. When you are doing mantra meditation, what you are actually doing is to allow your mind to clear up and relax. As blood flow to the brain increases, it allows you to view things from a whole different perspective. You also become more creative and are able to solve problems more efficiently after a short period of mantra meditation. It gives you the time to reflect on your problem. 

Most importantly, you need to know that mantra meditation transcends all religion and boundaries. So there is nothing stopping you from trying it. The best part is, it is absolutely free!
