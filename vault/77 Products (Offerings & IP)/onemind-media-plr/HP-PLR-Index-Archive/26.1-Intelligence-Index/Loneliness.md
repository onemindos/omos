Loneliness: 9 helpful tips

Loneliness is a state of mind and is really a form of neediness. Here
are a few ways you can keep your loneliness at bay.

1.  Get a pet. A furry animal pet. Not a tortoise or a fish. But a cat
    or a dog. They will give you unconditional love and they will never
    judge you. They are great for companionship. If you don't have one,
    you don't have to buy one from the pet store, you could just rescue
    a cat or a dog from the animal shelter.

2.  Join social activities. Join groups or activities that require you
    to socialize like kayaking or sailing club. These are great ways to
    learn a new skill and at the same time to make new friends.

3.  Be active. Fill up your day with physical activities. Take up yoga
    lessons, dance classes anything that will get you off your butt.
    It's time to move your body and start getting fit!

4.  Learn to enjoy spending time with yourself. Do not think that you
    have to have someone there to have quality time. Learn to enjoy time
    being by yourself. Go for a walk. Watch a sunset. Read a book while
    snuggled in your bed. Anything to make you feel happy.

5.  Get out of the house. Don't sit indoors and mope around. Work in the
    gardens. Get a bit of sunshine. It will do your body a whole lot of
    good.

6.  Develop your mind. Study a particular subject. You are never too old
    to learn new things. Continue studying. Start reading. Think about
    what you are really passionate in and take it up. Its never too late
    to get into something new.

7.  Join a support group. Make connections with others who have similar
    fears. Together you can help each other through this.

8.  Reconnect. Reach out to your old friends or family members. It's
    time to reconnect with them again. Catch up on old times.

9.  . Don't limit yourself to people your own age. Friendship knows no
    age barriers. You can enjoy the company of people younger or older
    than you.

In conclusion, you are the root of the problem. So let's start from home
to solve this!
