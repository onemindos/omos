# Are you planning a vacation without taking away your Bengal

Are you planning a vacation without taking away your Bengal? 
If you are planning a vacation without taking away your cat, there are some essential things which must be considered seriously by Bengal cat owners. Bengals are affectionate, adorable and playful personalities but it is essential to take good care of cats while you are home and away for long time.
Food of cat is the crucial decision to take as it decides the overall health of your cat. You can easily find different kinds of cat foods available in the market but make sure to choose a good quality food as cat food with cheap quality may harm its digestive system. Make sure to keep enough food while you are away. You can also hire a cat sitter who can keep good care of your cat while you are away. It is good to inform the sitter in case if you are going for long vacation. Ask your sitter to give updates about the health and wellness of your cat. Make sure to leave huge amount of food, toys and several other supplies. 
You can inform anyone who reside close to your home but make sure that he/she is trustable. Neighbors can at least give food and check the cats at least once in a day. Therefore, if you have nice neighbor, do inform them. It is better to place plenty of water so that your cat can access it anytime. Cats are heavy water drinkers and also water is essential to keep them hydrated. 
Do you have radio or any music system? Can you tune it for sometime during particular time in a day? It is good as kitties feel relaxed while listening to music. At least two litter boxes should be there. It is also essential to inform your vet about your home alone kitties. So, by following the above measures you can keep good care of your cats when you are away.
