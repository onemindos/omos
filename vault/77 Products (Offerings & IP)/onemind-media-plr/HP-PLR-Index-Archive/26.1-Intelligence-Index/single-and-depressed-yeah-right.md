# single-and-depressed-yeah-right

                 Single and Depressed: Yeah Right!


       Is it my imagination or is there an epidemic of being labeled as single and

depressed? It’s almost like a longstanding breaking news report that depicts those of us

who are not married as being depressed. Now, I’m not trying to cause a riot or stir up

controversy but I am going to shed some light on those of us who may be single. But

depressed? Yeah, right. Forgive me in advance when I say this because I realize there are

millions of women across the world who will disagree with me but I am going to take a

bold step forward and say these words: You do not have the right to put your life on hold

and render it without purpose because it’s just you. Your new mantra is this: I’m what I

got and I’m working with what I got.

       I have been single since my grand entrance into this world almost (fill in the

blank) years ago. Surely, you don’t think I would give up my age that easily, do you? To

my knowledge, my mother gave birth to me all at once. I do not believe that she gave

birth to me piece by piece. The nurse counted to ten and I came out. Head first. I do not

remember being attached to anyone other than my mother and one snip later, single

became my new normal. I say that because of the misconception that those of us who are

not married, especially women, possess this longstanding emptiness and need to be

completed by our soul mates.

       I don’t mean to make light of those of us who are being chased by the infamous

clock which apparently has told us we only have a set number of years to reproduce.

Trust me, over the years, I have been surrounded by female friends and not-so-friends

who have purses loaded with stop watches, hour glasses, time clocks and sun readers
because they are bound and determined to watch their clocks and hear their ticking. The

sounds of their gripes are almost like nonstop instrumentals and honestly, after a while, it

gets on my nerves. The world will end before they are able to have the one thing they

have been denied. Ladies in particular, I’m talking to you. There is no rule, no law, no

ordinance, no directive that orders us to be single and depressed. There will never be a

math class that will teach you that one plus zero is zero. One plus zero still leaves you

and you have the right to live your life to the fullest, even if you are living it without

another. Why on God’s beautiful earth are you putting your lives on hold until your

knight in shining armor comes in to rescue you from yourself?

       Maybe it’s because I did not grow up playing with dolls and fantasizing about my

wedding that I am not haunted by my singleness. Or maybe it’s because I’ve chosen to

spend my time focusing on those things that are within my control. But for some reason, I

love my life and will continue to love myself fiercely until my beau comes. Even then, I

will love me all the more because I understand that before I love another I must practice

loving myself. If I were given the opportunity to host a boot camp for single ladies, I

would stand before you and command you to get yourselves together and by getting it

together I mean checking that conversation you are having right between your ears.

       Let me throw a hook in the infamous lie that we are incomplete unless we are

married. Please be patient while I check for any pothole size openings in my body. I

declare to you upon everything I believe and all of what I think I know, but you are a

whole person. When you went to work this morning, did you leave part of you at home?

For the life of me, I cannot remember a time when half of my body walked out of my

house and the other half stayed in. I have never taken part of me to the mall in the hopes
of getting a discount. I cannot recall taking half of me and placing me in the passenger

seat so I could ride in the HOV lane. When I’m hungry, I do not feed the part of me that’s

not on a diet. Are you with me ladies? So stop thinking you are incomplete. No one wants

a person who judges herself as being incomplete. You, my dear, have been brainwashed

and it’s time your brain is washed by the truth.

       The truth of the matter is that you were created as an entire being. You were born

with everything in you that you needed to exist and live in joy and peace. Mind you, it

would be nice to have some companionship. It would help out a lot to have someone to

wrestle the lions on your behalf but until that day comes, I need you to grab your bow

and arrow and aim at your target in confidence. I’m not talking to anyone in particular,

but if you feel I am talking to you then I am. There is nothing more annoying than my

married friends who make comments like: You went to the movies by yourself. I could

never do that. You went out to eat alone. Why didn’t you call me? I would have gone

with you. You went to the park. Just you. That’s so sad. I feel so sorry for you.

       Oh, friends. How you annoy me with these words. Let me get this straight. When

my stomach growls, you want me to contact you to find out if your stomach is growling

as well so that I will have permission to feed my own. If a movie comes out, I will have

to consult with you to see if you would assist me in seeing the movie through your eyes.

Commercial Break: Seriously? I know you mean well, but you are really starting to

aggravate me with this pitying me stuff. I have lived with myself for so long and for the

life of me, I cannot remember another person who has been with me longer than I have.

       With that said, I am my own best friend. Yes, I talk to myself. Talking to yourself

is not the problem. The problem is what you choose to talk about. I choose to talk myself
out of believing the thoughts of my mind. I choose to encourage myself to be the best I

can be. I choose to pump myself up to try new things and be comfortable in my own skin.

Just the other day, I did the unthinkable. I hung out with myself. And you know what

happened? I had a great time. In fact, great is an understatement. I had a super-duber-

cali-uper-cotton-pickin wonderful time. I went to the mall and played dress up making a

mental note of all the items I would purchase once they were on sale. Next, I went out to

eat and ate until I heard my stomach cry out in mercy. After my delicious lunch, I went

and caught a chick-flick and I sat in the dark and laughed as loud as I wanted to and cried

shamelessly until the movie was over. Then, I went on my newest hobby. Driving

through neighborhoods and shopping for the multiple homes I planned to own before the

world ends. It was fun until the phone rang and a former friend who has been unfriended

whispered: That’s so sad. I would have met with you had you called me. Me and Junior

could have come. Uh. No thanks. Junior talks back to you and I don’t think that’s cute.

After pretending to lose reception, I erased my mind of her words and went back to my

happy place which is where I choose to live. I am a whole person and so are you. To

believe yourself to be incomplete until you are married is simply not true. Picture me in

full body military gear shouting in your face that this is not true. If that hurts your

feelings, picture me with a king size chocolate bar and a smile whispering that, my friend,

is not true.

        Now mind you, by the time you read this I may be married and chasing Ronnie,

Bobby, Amber, Joshua and Nicole through the house screaming at the top of my lungs,

but for now, my reality is I’m what I got and I’m working with what I got. We were

intentionally created by a Magnificent Maker who loves us with such passion that He
desires for us to live our lives on purpose and with purpose. Ladies, remember our high

school and college days when we had fun hanging with our friends. Remember those

days when we had fun hanging with ourselves. If you don’t recall these days, you have

not lived. You have not lived until you are able to look yourself in the mirror and love

who you are—flaws and all. Let me take you into my world for a brief moment and then

I will step back into yours. I have been an educator for (fill in the blank) years. I

apologize—that seems to happen every time I attempt to admit my true age. Like I was

saying, I have taught school for a long time and one of the things I drill into my students,

particularly my young ladies, is how important it is for us to watch how we view

ourselves.

        A few months ago, one of my young ladies came to me, obviously having had a

bad day. When I asked her what was wrong, she informed me people were saying things

about her. After a series of questions, I finally asked her to really think about why their

words hurt her and you know what she said? “It’s because they said things that I think

about myself anyway.” And that is what I wanted her to see. This is the time we get

ourselves together as women, as the strength of all generations—past and present. This is

the time we address all of the lies of our childhood that threaten to show their ugly heads

in our marriages, careers, and during motherhood.

       Did you know that many of us still have the same insecurities as women that we

had as little girls? You know why? Because we did not deal with them. We waited on the

other part of us to walk us down the aisle and help us to live happily ever after and from

what I understand from my married friends, that does not happen. When you wake up in
the morning, you will be the first person to deal with you. Your thoughts, ideas, desires,

hopes, insecurities must all be dealt with by you.

        So I have an idea and hopefully you will come along with me for a little while.

My idea is this: Instead of being single and depressed, why not be single and free? Free

from your personal insecurities, your lack of self-confidence, your feeling of being

incomplete and most of all, free from the lies between your ears. I’m going to take a

chance and take you to my secret place. Who knows? Maybe you will find refuge with

me.

        It took me a while to be comfortable with myself. During the summer of my

seventh grade year, I grew four inches. Yes, four whole inches. Needless to say, I was

taller than all of the girls in my middle school and most of the boys so I taught myself

how to be small. I refused to wear heels. I stayed seated as much as possible. I walked

two steps ahead or two steps behind my shorter friends and I ducked. Yes, I do mean

ducked when I walked to keep from hovering over my peers. I learned how to cope with

what I judged to be my excess by erasing a very real part of me. I was embarrassed but

on the surface I was confident and strong so those around me did not realize I had

insecurities. In my young mind, something was wrong with me and for the life of me, I

cannot recall where that lie came from. It was the enemy of my mind who tried her very

best to convince me at all times that something is wrong with me. I tell you this because

it is imperative we address the lies being served right between our ears. Anything that

demands your joy and peace is a lie. Far removed from those middle school years, I still

understand the idea of lying to myself. If I admit any regrets, I will admit the times I have

believed a lie.
       Your mind is your greatest weapon but it is also your greatest enemy. If you

believe that you cannot be happy in your singledom, then unhappy is what you will be. A

former colleague of mine almost suffered cardiac arrest when I told her I was dating

myself. She could not fathom going to the movies, dinner, or any other event alone as

she said it. Think it strange but I’ve never known what alone feels like. I’ve known pain,

sadness, frustration, anger and countless others but alone is not a feeling, it is a condition

and as long as I know the joys of loving those around me and being loved by them, I will

not experience the feeling attached to alone. This is the benefit that comes with having a

healthy mind. Remember, the battle is waged inside before it is fought outside.

       I’m speaking as one of the team. We have to encourage each other right? That’s

the only way to be. First of all, and I say this with my hands on my hips, change the way

you think. I’m serious. If I could stand in your face and command you, I would but all

I’ve got right now is a keyboard and my fingers.     Do you realize this is the time of your

life where you have the advantage of getting yourself together? Do you realize that you

are wasting your years wishing away your present? Live your life. Do all the things that

are in your heart to do. Finish school. Get promoted. Learn how to cook. Bungee Jump.

Scuba dive. Do something, anything other than wishing away your lives. We get one life

and we are obligated to live each day as if that day actually matters. I have something

planned that is going to blow your minds. I am going on a vacation. Me, Myself and I are

collectively going on a road trip. We started out pulling weekenders locally just to

develop a level of comfort, but we are planning on going further away for much longer in

a few months. Don’t worry. We play it safe. We don’t hang out too late when we are in

unfamiliar areas. We don’t talk to strangers. We don’t place ourselves in dangerous
situations. In fact, we really just go out and enjoy. We like to keep it simple. We check in

our hotel early in the morning. We make sure we have a full tank of gas and go out and

see something other than our normal. We people watch (doors locked of course) and we

try new things. Eateries that we don’t have in our own areas. Shopping places we don’t

have in our areas. We just have fun. We enjoy ourselves. I’m working with what I got

and that’s me.

       See. This is how I see it. I cannot imagine erasing myself from all things fun and

magically reappearing once I am married. The world would look at me and say, Who are

you and where did you come from? And my response would be I’m sorry. I hid under a

rock until my beau gave me permission to be seen. Thanks but no thanks. My strongest

examples of marriage are the ones I have seen in which each individual retained an

individuality. There is an unwritten and unspoken prenuptial agreement that exists in all

solid marriages and that is permission to retain sole ownership of your thoughts, ideas,

feelings and emotions. I cannot imagine being force fed a food I hate because my

husband loves it. I will learn to cook it for him and even fix his plate. No worries. But

actually place it in my mouth. Uh. No thanks. I cannot imagine sitting down for hours

watching ESPN but I will toss him a few hot wings and yell when he yells as a sign of

support. I’m single. What can I say? I like what I like but I’m willing to learn and revert

back to the first lesson I learned in Preschool: Sharing is Caring.

       Another pointer that bounces off of the previous point is that you have to choose

to live. Life is for the living so we are expected to live. I’m not suggesting that you have

to go out and put yourselves in dangerously compromising positions. I’m saying that in

the beginning, there may be some degree of discomfort as with all things that are new.
Think about it. Don’t we all have a little anxiety when we start a new job or take on a

new project? Weren’t we just a tad bit nervous when we went back to school for the first

time? Usually it is a nervous excitement though. The excitement that comes right before

you buckle in on that roller coaster because you know good things will come if you just

hold on. Start off with something small. If you aren’t comfortable with the night crowds

of restaurants, then walk in when the crowds are a little less threatening. I am a lunch

type of girl if I say so myself. For two primary reasons. It’s cheaper and the waiters tend

to give you more attention so if I’m dressed up, I at least want somebody to see the effort

I put in myself. That’s another thing ladies. What rule book says that we have to look

like American’s Most Unwanted? Always care about yourself. Not in a vain narcissist

sort of way, but in a way that says I’m what I got and I’m working with what I got.



         Let me share a few of the lies we tell ourselves and then take us back to our happy

place:



         Lie #1: If I lose weight someone will love me. Never confuse love and attraction

and never, ever believe the lie that your exterior makes it impossible for you to be loved.

I love you and I’ve never met you. I love you because as women, we will forever share a

certain bond attached to womanhood. There are experiences we will understand as

women that our male others cannot. I’m not bashing the male population at all. That’s

not why I’m here. I’m celebrating womanhood with my sisters of all races, cultures,

shapes and sizes.
        We are bombarded with images of what society has judged to be most beautiful.

The media has “photo-shopped” us into oblivion and those of us who have not been

stroked by a paintbrush do not fit in with the images out there. Little girls as young as

five years of age have been diagnosed with eating disorders because of our own

unhealthy images of ourselves. You must take care of yourselves in order that you

maintain a healthy image of yourself. Not so you can fit into a pair of jeans, but so you

can get into the habit of loving you enough do right by the one body that you have. You

are not inferior because of your size. You are inferior because of your outlook. I’m a

runner. It is a hobby I have developed over the years. I exercise regularly and am mindful

of what I eat. I don’t do this to attract the attention of possible suitors, I do this because I

am fiercely in love with myself—not in a narcissistic sort of way, but in a way that

proves I’m good enough, strong enough, and informed enough to take care of myself.

I’m what I got and I’m working with what I got.



        Lie #2: If I’m not married, I may as well take what I can get until my husband

comes. I disagree. I dis uh agree. This, my friends, is a dangerous lie. All lies are

dangerous but some of them are a tad bit more dangerous than others. This is one of

those more-dangerous-than-others lies. Why? Because this one requires you to relax your

standards, values, morals and expectations. This is like saying Well since I’m hungry

enough to eat a horse, I will settle for eating a mouse. Do you see how ridiculous this is?

We only do this because we are uncomfortable with ourselves.

        I am amazed at my single friends and acquaintances who jump from one

relationship to the next because they don’t want to be alone. Some of them take anything
and when I say anything, I mean anything and they justify it by saying it’s temporary.

Am I the only person who has been damaged by temporary? Do you know how long it

can take us to recover from the aftermath of a temporary situation? We are humans and

no matter how hard we try, we will never be action or superheroes and unless you are

void of a heart, temporary promises to do as much damage as permanent does. You are

wasting precious milliseconds of your lives when you give them to people and places that

they are not to be given. Preserve your strength. Value yourself enough as a woman to

exercise integrity at all times.

        Let me get ready to dodge your bullets after my upcoming statement. Please, for

the love of all that is true, please do not invite yourself or accept an invitation to be the

third party in someone else’s relationship. I am thoroughly convinced a woman who

engages in an affair with a married man is attempting to plug a pothole sized opening

with a temporary fix. If you possess self-love and dignity, you would square your

shoulders, hold your heads up high and measure yourselves to be worthy of more than

scraps. As a rule, married men are off limits. What goes around comes around and when

it comes, it usually brings two left hooks and a black eye with it. To believe that you

have the right to or are only worth left overs, is to believe a lie.



        Lie #3: There are some things I can’t do as a single person. Let’s see. There are

some things you may not want to do as a solo artist and maybe even some things that may

be a bit more challenging but whatever you judge to be impossible for you is not

impossible because of your single-ness, it is impossible because of your decision to make

it so. Years ago, when I purchased my home, many of my single and married friends
informed me that homes are supposed to be purchased after marriage for families, or as

one bluntly argued, “That’s why they are called single family homes.” By the grace of

our Heavenly Father, I have maintained steady employment for years. I have earned an

honest living and take great pride in being able to live a life of purpose and passion. I

have been honored to have people in my life who have helped me regain my footing

when I lost it so to say that I’ve done everything on my own would be a lie. But to say

that I should not purchase a home for myself and my current family of one is an even

bigger lie. When I closed on my house, amid the sneers and frowns, I proved to myself

and them that I was unwilling to allow anyone’s status quo to confine me to a place of

which I wanted to break free. Even now, when I pull into my driveway, I am beyond

thankful to see the fruits of my labor. I cannot imagine longing for something within my

reach but refusing to take ahold of it because of what those around me say or have said.

If you’ve always wanted to buy a house, go for it. If you have your honeymoon planned

before you are even engaged, turn it into a vacation instead. Life is for the living and

when you choose not to live that means you have chosen to die. Why die when you can

live? Single and Depressed? Yeah, right!



       Lie # 4: You cannot really have a fulfilling life as a single woman. After all, you

were created to be wives and mothers. I will not argue with this in the sense of women

being wives and mothers. I do believe, by design of our Father, we are created as

nurturers and caretakers. I believe that we are the soft places where our husbands and

children will rest their heads. I do not, however, believe that my life is unfulfilled while I

am single. To those of you who are truly waiting for your beau to arrive or your children
to come, I want you to remember this: You do not have to be married or give birth to give

love. I say this every Mother’s Day to my friends who do not have children but

desperately want them. While you are waiting on the opportunity to love yours, love

someone else’s.

       Did I mention I was a school teacher? I’m pretty sure I did. Why would I

withhold my love until my own children come when I have hundreds of young people

around me every day who I can give my love to? It’s not like we are given a limited

amount of love. God gives us love in abundance and expects for us to share it. I’m

confident my involvement in the lives of my students has tremendously satisfied my

desire to care for and about someone other than me. Pick someone and invest in that

person. In this vast world of great need, there are millions of people (especially young

people) who need your help. Don’t just do a drive by with them. Share with them the

lessons you’ve learned about yourselves. I am sure this is one of the primary reasons I’ve

personally never felt unfulfilled. I’ve accepted the great commission to love my students

with the same passion I would want someone to love my own. We reap the seeds we sow.

Make them good ones.



       Lie #5: If you are single pass a certain age, something must be wrong with you.

Friend girls, listen. Didn’t I tell you we were the mothers of all things great? There is

nothing wrong with you because you were not married by the time you planned. That’s

life. It doesn’t always go as we planned. If my life had gone as planned, I would have a

mansion, 2.5 children, and ten best sellers by now, but that hasn’t happened yet. I’m what

I got and I’m working with what I got. I heard someone say this phrase and it has carried
me during challenging days. Delay is not denial. Check those lies being tossed in your

mind. Timing is everything. If I’m honest with myself, there are some things I was given

prematurely and because of this, I was not ready for them. I shutter to think of being

given the responsibility of real live people while I was still trying to get myself together.

I’m not an outstanding chess player but I know a bad move when I see it.



       Now back to our happy place. Let’s go on a mental adventure together. Pick a

place, any place and see yourself there. Were you alone or with friends? How did you

feel when you were there? Hopefully, you were happy in your mind because if you

weren’t, you will only go as far as your thinking allows. Single and Depressed? Yeah

Right!!!! We are what we got and we are working with what we got.



       Now it’s time to wash your brains. Here are some words that have helped me

when I am threatened by the thoughts of my mind. I remind myself:



           •   I am fearfully and wonderfully made (Psalms 139:14)

           •   I should not be anxious about anything and must guard my heart and mind

               from everything (Philippians 4: 6-7)

           •   Not to allow my external to be the focus because God looks at my heart (1

               Peter 3: 3-4)

           •   I am beautiful beyond description and flawless to my Heavenly Father

               (Song of Solomon 4:7)

           •   I am a gracious woman who receives honor (Proverbs 11:16)
          •   To open my hands to the poor and receive the needy (Proverbs 31: 20-21)

          •   Should practice wisdom and kindness with my words (Proverbs 31: 26)

          •   To practice wisdom in building my home and not foolishly tear it down

              (Proverbs 14:1)

          •   That I should do good and chase after peace (Psalms 34:14)

          •   I am safe and secure. The Lord will guard me and keep me in peace when

              my mind is stayed on Him (Isaiah 26:3)



Who but God knows what we are capable of doing if we focus on what we have and not

on what we don’t have? Who but Him knows who we could be if we saw ourselves as

meeting the need and not being the needy? Did I tell you how fiercely powerful we are as

women? We have the potential to house nations in our wombs and no matter who’s with

you, believe that even now, you have everything in you to be the best you that you could

be. Single and Depressed? Yeah Right!
