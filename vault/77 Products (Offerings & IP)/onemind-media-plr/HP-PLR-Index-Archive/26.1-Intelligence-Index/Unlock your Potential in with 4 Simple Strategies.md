**Unlock your Potential in with 4 Simple Strategies**

We need to challenge ourselves. We need to push our limits. Discovering
our locked potential requires getting out of our comfort zones. Here are
a few ideas to help you along.

1.  **Challenge Yourself**

> Have you ever wanted to switch jobs, or take up a new skill? If so,
> why not take some concrete steps towards making these thoughts a
> reality. Getting out of your comfort zone and into unfamiliar
> territory will help you uncover hidden talents and strengths. It is
> only when you force yourself to try new things that you are able to
> see all that you are capable of.

2.  **Don't Get Stuck in a Rut**

> When you stay in the same place for too long, you tend to get stuck in
> ruts. Our lives become mundane, dull routines. The key to getting out
> of this is to get out of the house and travel somewhere. Travel is an
> excellent way to open up your mind and creativity. Discovering new
> things about the world we live in will open up our minds to the
> untapped potential within us.

3.  **Create New Connections with People**

> A new acquaintance, friend or romantic interest can open up a whole
> new world of opportunities for us. Seeing ourselves through someone
> else's eyes is a great way to gain valuable insight into how you think
> and perceive the world around you, as well as how the world perceives
> you.

4.  **Go Against the Grain**

> Challenge yourself to do the exact opposite of your first impulse for
> a day. Want to stay in and read? Go out to the park instead. Pack
> lunch? Go out instead. Again we are looking at getting out of your
> comfort zone. Continually push and challenge yourself to try new
> things, and you'll eventually discover new things about yourself.
> Explore these new findings further to seek out the hidden potential in
> yourself.
