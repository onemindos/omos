FAQ
Q: Can the method be used for all three credit bureaus?
A: Yes, you can use this method for Experian, Equifax, And
Transunion


Q: Can I use this method for inquiries older than 1 year?
A: Yes, you can use this process for any closed accounts and
or denied inquires 1 year or older.


Q: What if an inquiry is still on my credit report after 2 years?
A: Call the bureau and let them know they are in violation of
not removing all inquiries that are older than 2 years old and
they need to be removed immediately.
