**Getting What You Want**

Do you want to be richer? Have more friends? Have more good things
happen to you? The Law of Attraction is a universal law that theorises
that we can create our own realities. Through the power of our thoughts
we can attract things that we want, and conversely also attract things
that we don't want. By putting the science of the Law of Attraction into
practice, we can attract the people in our lives, the money in our bank
accounts and the stuff in our homes through our thoughts and feelings.

The wealth and prosperity that we attract is limited only by the limits
of our belief. When we believe the sky is the limit, well, anything
becomes possible. Our positive thoughts can and will break through that
glass ceiling in the sky. When we focus on the negativity and lack of
everything, we are creating a lesser reality. But if we instead focus on
abundance and positivity, we will enjoy a reality beyond our wildest
dreams.

The science and theory behind the Law of Attraction is very simple --
you attract what you want. But putting it into practice on a conscious
level takes hard work. Negative and limiting belief systems are deeply
ingrained in most of us. It is the process of ridding yourself of
negative ideas and self-defeating old habits that is the hard part.

There are three steps to attracting all your desires:

1.  Ask -- Figure out what you want and how you want it. Visualise it in
    its entirety. Then ask for it. Simple, right?

2.  Believe -- You must believe, truly believe without a doubt that what
    you are asking for will be given to you. Even thinking of the
    possibility of failure will mess things up.

3.  Receive -- Be proactive. Grab onto opportunities that come your way.
    Don't just lay back and wait for your goals to manifest magically
    without actively participating in the process.
