**Tips for Finding the Strength in You**

Each and every one of us possesses an inner strength within ourselves
that often lies untapped because we do not look inward, especially
during our everyday busy lives. But when tough times come, that inner
strength can be tapped and is what will sustain us. There a few ways to
access that inner strength and peace when you need it most.

1.  Make a list of factors which are holding you back as a stronger
    person. Create a list of character flaws you observe in yourself
    with input from friends and family who may be able to give a more
    objective view of your flaws. Examples of characteristics which can
    lead to lack of strength are lack of trust, indecisiveness and low
    self-esteem.

2.  Next, create a separate list of your character strengths. By
    acknowledging your positive traits, you can build up your
    self-esteem, a key component to increasing your inner strength.

3.  Affirmations are a great way to increase your self-worth and
    strength within yourself. Repeat phrases like \"I am strong, I can
    succeed\" or \"I am strong and healthy. I believe in myself" to
    yourself everyday to motivate yourself and improve focus.

4.  Make a list of times in your life when you felt or acted strongly
    each night before you go to sleep each night no matter how small or
    insignificant. Remembering the times when you used inner strength
    will help rekindle those feelings in your current self.

5.  Talk to people who inspire you and ask how they channel inner
    strength. Strive to follow their example and use their guidance as a
    model for your own life. Similarly, keep your friends and family
    close. You'll find more inner strength by drawing on the strength of
    those around you.
