                              Prologue


     Here is part 1 of my series of poems that will inspire you as well as
provoke you to think and reflect deeply. There is a message in each one
of my poems and I please ask that you interpret them any way you see
fit. Everybody will have a slightly different interpretation, which is fine
because we are all unique individuals with our own unique perceptions
on this magical journey we call life!
                        Green Grass



The life he wants,
the life he had.
What’s made him bitter,
What’s made him sad?
The riches the spoils,
everything he has.
So why the emptiness,
why the half glass?
If only he knew,
to take it in stride.
For the grass isn’t always greener,
on the other side…
                   The Worst Disease


From famine to disease,
the people sicken.
From the aristocratic,
to the poverty stricken.
What is more sickening?
The disease itself
or the calls for help,
nobody’s listening.
Money can buy happiness,
it will ease the temporary.
But once Greed intervenes,
your soul won’t be necessary.
                    For the love of it


They say you need to be a doctor,
 you need to be a lawyer.
They say you need a steady job,
find you a good employer.
They say you need an education,
you need a degree.
They say they know what you need,
You say, what’s in it for me?
They say your passions are great,
but a career would be greater.
You say you want to be happy,
They say that comes later.
So what are we to do,
at the fork in the road,
follow the money,
or follow the soul?
                        Together


Feeble minds and meager times,
Desperation can often cloud
our eager minds.
Our lives can be defined,
by not the time,
but the bind in which,
our lives are intertwined.
Success through community,
what’s yours is mine,
what’s mine is yours,
harmony and unity.
Such as our Earth,
needs the moon and the sun.
We must work together,
we must work as one.
                             Evil


Darkness is the absence of light,
but what is evil?
Pondering my thoughts
as i seek to unleash these written words,
upon the pad in which my thoughts have blurred.
Through free will we are born sinners,
so is it absurd,
To think we will be brought to the sky,
when judgment occurs?
Fighting mental battles with my psyche,
what if others think like me,
will they suffer the same fate?
The thought alone frightens me.
Is there divine to find in these wicked times,
Or is my faith confined to this wicked mind?
                             Humility


Why must we tell the world,
why must we make it known?
For must we show,
the good we bestow?
Humility is the leaf in the wind,
the log in the river.
the rain in the summer,
the flakes in the winter.
                             Anger


The beast inside is clawing away,
trying to escape my body that lies in wait.
Who can decide what fate to take?
Do I unleash the fire, lead the monster astray,
or pray that my ire starts to decay?
Why can’t I decide what fate to take,
the beast inside me is clawing away.
                            To Exist


L is for Love, from adult to child
I is for Imagination, let it run wild
F is for Fun, from crazy to mild
E is for Experiences, the reasons you smile.
Life is a blessing for which you’ve been granted,
Don’t lose any time, don’t take it for granted.
                              Grim


Skulls and Bones, the cloaked one,
Feared by many, the rite to some.
The unknown black, the end of life
the sharpened fate, the sharper scythe.
The inevitable and holy all in one,
the end of days, the end of one
Whether one with virtue, or one with sin
All must face the day with Grim…
