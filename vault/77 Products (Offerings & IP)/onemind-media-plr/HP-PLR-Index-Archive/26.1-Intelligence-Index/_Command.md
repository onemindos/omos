# Command — Control Center

> *"The bridge of the ship. Not a destination—a navigation system."*

---

## Purpose

Command is the **governance layer** of the Legacy Codex. It orchestrates the four pillars (GE, HP, LE, IT) without being one of them. This is where identity lives, AI collaborates, doctrine is defined, metrics are tracked, protocols are stored, and active work is managed.

## Governance Responsibilities

1. **Vision Alignment** — Ensuring all pillars serve the master mission
2. **Doctrine Enforcement** — First principles applied consistently
3. **Metrics Tracking** — Weekly pillar scores and Legacy Score
4. **Protocol Management** — Maintain and version reusable plays
5. **Resource Allocation** — Time, energy, capital across domains
6. **System Health** — Maintaining the Codex infrastructure
7. **AI Coordination** — Managing Legacy and other AI agents
8. **Capture Processing** — Routing inbox items to proper homes

---

## Daily Cockpit

Your three daily touchpoints in Command:

1. **Inbox** — Process captures, route to pillars
2. **Atlas** — Check active projects, update status
3. **Metrics** — Log signals, check pillar health
