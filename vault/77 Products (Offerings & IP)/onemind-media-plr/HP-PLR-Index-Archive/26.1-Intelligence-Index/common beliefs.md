# common beliefs

What does the world believe?

	Human beings are social creatures. We live, eat, play, and work together. We are pack animals. And because we're wired to live in community, the beliefs of the world around have a big impact on what we believe. To some, this may sound obvious, and others may insist that they are separated, independent of the world in which they live. Because so much of who we are, how we think, and what we do is generated from the beliefs we hold about the world, it's important to look around and see what the world is believing. 
	Although every member of every community believes things unique to themselves, people in the same neighborhood probably believe similar things, people in the same city believe similar things, but maybe not as similar as the same neighborhood. The same goes for people in the same state, the same country, and even the same world. Believe it or not, people in Japan hold beliefs very similar to those in the States. 
	Most people in the world believe in capitalism. Whether they want to or not, they do. They were born into it, they've grown up with it, and they've participated in it. People in India buy and sell goods in very similar ways to those in South Dakota. People in the Phillippines buy and sell goods in ways similar to those in the south of France. Sure, what the people buy and sell is different, the money is different, but the basic system is the same.
	Most people in the world believe in monotheism, that is, they believe there is one God. Even Hinduism, with its 333,333,333 gods, teaches that everything originates from one Uncaused Cause. Just like with capitalism, how people believe in One God is different, but the belief in one, generally benevolent God is common to most people on the planet. 
	In the States,along with capitalism and Christianity, these are some common beliefs. Sure, they are not true for everyone, but they are common enough to say that the majority is in agreement. 

There is an absolute system of right and wrong.
God blesses the United States more than other countries. 
Violence is a pleasurable form of entertainment.
A person's privacy is to be respected at all times.
Hard work is essential for the attainment of wealth. 
Increased security is more important than individual liberty.
Competition brings out the best in everybody. 
The world is a dangerous place. 

	Do you believe any of these ideas? It's not surprising if you do, because much of the world around you believes these or similar ideas. And maintaining different beliefs than the general population requires a diligent effort and a graceful finesse. I don't mean to imply that these beliefs are 'right' or 'wrong', because that's not how it really works. But we can look at them and wonder if they are healthy, if they are useful. And if they're not, maybe it's time to change them for something more beneficial.
