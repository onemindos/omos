# Rules For Dog Walking


Rules For Dog Walking

When we walk our dog, regardless the destination - on the city streets, in parks or in open spaces - it's essential that he respects the disciplinary rules settled before and learned, to avoid getting into issues with other animals or individuals we meet.

So let’s see how our little friend will have to act in a few situations:

Commonly, in crowded places we'll keep him in a leash, to always have him in check. So he will have to know how to walk in a leash, near your foot, without pulling. 

The dog isn't allowed to jump on individuals or animals met or to attack any of those, as long as he isn’t attacked.

The dog should not run after bicycles, motorcycles, automobiles or individuals that are running. Know that some dogs do that because of their hunting instincts.

Once we let the dog free, to relax, it's crucial that when he's called he return on the shortest way and as quick as possible.

Wherever our dog may be, he is not to be allowed to consume food given by somebody else or food found thrown around.

Usually, small dogs are more spoiled than big dogs and they're more aggressive, anti-social and afraid of others or dogs. The ones to blame are the owners that frequently amuse themselves when they see their dogs acting this way. 

The dog must have the initial training learned and practiced, so we'd have no issues when walking him.

All through the walk, the dog isn't to chew on his leash or to pull it with his teeth, as it may happen that he is tied someplace alone and he will chew on it till he becomes free and gets lost.

The dog isn't allowed to jump up and out his front paws on us or other people even if he's simply playing.
