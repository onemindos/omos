Performance
It should be easy to understand that some tasks are best left to
domestic cats. Thus the domestic cat with all its laziness has a role
to play in the scheme of things and is perfectly designed for that
purpose. It can perform in this role better than any super-fast

The Peak is the very height of your Performance. It is
reaching the very best of what is possible with you.
Perhaps from the top of your head you can think of
someone who is or was the best at something. How this
has come about and why is it that others in the same
field are shades behind this person is the chief concern
of this book.

The term Peak refers to the very top of a mountain. As
life involves activity, Performance forms the medium
via which activity can be evaluated and the Peak is the
very height of Performance. It is a question of how
high you can jump and how fast you can you run.
How many words you can type in a minute or how far
can a golf ball go when you strike? Most importantly it
is a question of how effective you can be with what is


                                                                  28
available to you. What is the quality of your output and
what is its effect on others?

Peak Performance for our purposes can be addressed
from two levels: The larger spectrum of one’s life and
their occupation as a microcomponent of the former.
One earns their living with their occupation and
therefore it follows that if the quality of Performance in
their occupation is high, it will directly influence the
quality of their larger life bearing in mind that the
occupation is but one of the many components that
make the larger life. The two must therefore be
seamlessly integrated for the best results. This idea
raises the question of what one’s life is about— what
the occupation must be. If you are going to spend most
of your life doing something, what will it be?

A living is something you earn and you do it by
undertaking an activity that you call work— a harsher
word for occupation. This means that your work is how
you power your life. High Performance in your work
therefore has a way of affecting your standard of life. I
am not suggesting here that your life is only about your
work as we normally understand it to be. In fact your
work is only one of the many micro components that
interact with each other in order to make up your
complete life. Yet your occupation powers all aspects
of your life because it is how you earn your living. In

                                                       29
short, increase the quality of your work and the quality
of your life has a chance to improve.

On another level, your entire life itself and its very
essence is only but a micro component of the
organization or industry you work in and on a much
larger scale, a micro-micro-component of your nation,
continent and the world. The argument therefore is a
very simple one: when individuals perform at their best
in thier occupations, thier lives will improve—nations
and the world will reach its best as a result. What then
is your place in the group that you belong to and how
are you playing your role? What level are you in
trying to reach your ultimate capability knowing that it
is the only way that the group you are a part of can be
the best possible. The answer to these two questions
can have great influence on the image your have of
yourself.


The Bugatti Veyron by VW is at this moment of writing
the fastest production car on the planet. Whiles the
German manufacturer has been building vehicles for a
very long time; this one is like no other. And why have
they decided to outperform those who specialize in
building high performance vehchles? To build an
extraordinary car , VW knew they needed to do
something extra ordinary. So they set up a separate
                                                     30
team in a separate factory to undertake this task. After
years of work the fastest production car was born and
its specifications were extra ordinary indeed.

8.0 liter, quad-turbocharged, W16 cylinder engine
equivalent to two V8 engines. 64 valves,

Your average car will normally have four cylinders,
maximum six. Very rare cars have eight cylinders. The
Buggati Veyron is two high Performance cars in one
with its sixteen cylinders. But there is more to this
extraordinary machine. Whiles there are some high
performing cars that have twenty four valves; this
extraordinary car has sixty four. By now you probably
already understand the point being made here. To make
an extra ordinary car, they needed extraordinary parts.
An extra ordinary car like this must also come with an
extraordinary price as only extra ordinary people can
own one and here is the reason:

A replacement transmission for the Veyron costs just
over US$120,000. It has special Michelin PAX run-flat
tyres that cost US$25,000 per set. Taking the tyres off
costs US$70,000.

With that extraordinary power it will need an
extraordinary control system. The speed of the vehicle
is only as useful as its breaking system. If it cannot
                                                     31
stop when it needs to, all that power then becomes its
bane. Such an extra ordinary machine is sure to get
some attention and there were extraordinary reviews
from extraordinary experts.

It was named Car of the Decade (2000–2009) by the

Clarkson later declared the Veyron "the greatest car
ever made and the greatest car we will ever see in our
lifetime."

James May described the Veyron as "our Concorde
moment."

Clearly the Bugatti Veyron was built for extra ordinary
speed. It became the fastest car in production and cost
almost two million dollars to own. Buyers had to order
it in advance and wait for it to be built and delivered. It
was certainly built for extra ordinary people— a high
performing car for high performing people.

If you have had any experience with cars at all, you
will know that owning a car goes beyond just buying
it. Sometimes the keeping of the car can be more
expensive than the buying. An extraordinary person
who buys the Bugatti Veyron will therefore have to
remain extra ordinary in order to keep it. Consistency
in Performance is required to keep things running at
