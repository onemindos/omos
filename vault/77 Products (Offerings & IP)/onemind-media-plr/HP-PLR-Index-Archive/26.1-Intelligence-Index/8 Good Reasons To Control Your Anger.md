# 8 Good Reasons To Control Your Anger

8 Good Reasons To Control Your Anger
Why should you control your anger? I believe these are 8 good reasons to keep you from blowing your head up.
Anger will caused irrational actions and these actions will be reflected badly on your personality
Unwise words are spoken when in anger. Words spoken are not retractable. So think before you speak and best to control your emotions before speaking in a rash.
Anger will only hurt the person who is angry. The person on the wrong side will escaped unharmed.
Anger will lead to shame.
Consequences caused by anger can be more devastating than the reasons of anger.
Being angry is just falling into a trap that someone sets up so that they can conquer you.
If you are angry at someone’s fault, turn to yourself and study your own faults as well. It might not be the other person’s fault.
