# 9-Feed your dog

Feed your pet’s real need with homemade pet food
Pets become a part of a family as soon as they start pondering their love over the members of the family. Just like the dietary needs of everyone else, their food requirements also need equal consideration. With multiple pet food brands labeled as balanced diet, people make it an alternative to rely on them. These processed foods are mostly recommended by veterinarians since these are assumed to be developed by the experts of the field of veterinary nutrition. Another doctrine talks over the idea of feeding pets with home cooked food only. Although, packed foods are marketed as wholesome diets but they are not nutritious and fresh. These foods have such preservatives and additives that make them harmful for consumption. Many pet owners have affirmed that their pets started losing appetite within a few days of consuming ready to eat pet food which resulted catastrophically in weight loss and bowel malfunction. 
However, when they shifted their pets to human ingredient food, they saw a positive transformation in their pets. It is a proven fact that cats require more nutrition than dogs. Cats are completely carnivorous as compared to dogs which are omnivorous. Cats ought to have meat because they are more vulnerable to nutritional deficiencies in case they don’t get balanced meals. Both of these animals have different nutritional requirements and that’s why their food habits vary as well. If you are entirely focusing on providing nutrition to your pet, then you can always take help of many cook books and recipes from the market or internet. It is a trend these days that pet owners have started to feed their pets with homemade food. This has happened mainly because of the health problems that emerged as a result of lethal commercial pet food intake by many pets. It won’t be a meal if it is from the processed foods. If you want to fulfill your pets’ real nutritious needs, then going the home cooked pet food way is the only alternative to assure a healthy life.
