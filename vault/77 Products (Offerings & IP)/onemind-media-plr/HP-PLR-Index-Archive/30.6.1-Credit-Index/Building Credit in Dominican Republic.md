# Building Credit in the Dominican Republic

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Finance, Credit-Building, International

## Introduction

A guide for foreigners to establish a credit profile in the Dominican Republic.

---

## Steps to Build Credit

### 1. Set Up a Local Bank Account

**Task**: Open a bank account with a local Dominican bank using your USA or Canadian passport.

**Outcome**: A functional bank account ready for regular transactions to build credit history.

### 2. Regular Deposits

**Task**: Make consistent deposits into your new bank account to show activity.

**Outcome**: A bank account with a growing balance, reflecting financial activity.

### 3. Local Phone Service

**Task**: Sign up for a local phone service plan (~$40 USD monthly)

**Providers**:
- Claro
- Altice
- Viva

**Use your bank account for payments to demonstrate regular payment history.**

### 4. Secured Credit Card Application

**Task**: After bank account setup, apply for a secured credit card.

**Outcome**: Use responsibly → eligible for regular credit card release after six months of timely payments.
