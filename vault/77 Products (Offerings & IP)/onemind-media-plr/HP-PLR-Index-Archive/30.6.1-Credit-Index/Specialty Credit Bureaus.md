# Specialty Credit Bureaus

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Finance, Credit-Building

## Beyond the Big Three

Beyond Experian, TransUnion, and Equifax, these specialty bureaus track specific financial behaviors.

---

## ChexSystems

**Purpose**: Tracks banking history (overdrafts, closed accounts, bounced checks)

**Relevance**: Banks use this to determine checking/savings account eligibility

**Access**:
- [ChexSystems Consumer Assistance](https://www.chexsystems.com/)
- Request free annual report under FCRA
- Dispute inaccuracies online or via mail

**Tip**: Review before applying for a new bank account

---

## Innovis

**Purpose**: Alternative credit data (address history, credit behavior not in big three)

**Relevance**: Some lenders use Innovis in addition to main bureaus

**Access**:
- [Innovis Consumer Services](https://www.innovis.com/)
- Call 1-800-540-2505

**Tip**: May list accounts not on traditional credit reports

---

## SageStream (LexisNexis Risk Solutions)

**Purpose**: Alternative credit scores for auto loans and credit cards

**Access**:
- [LexisNexis Consumer Center](https://consumer.risk.lexisnexis.com/)
- Request personal credit report
- Freeze report if necessary

---

## Clarity Services

**Purpose**: Tracks subprime lending (payday loans, installment loans, auto loans)

**Relevance**: If you've used alternative lenders, history appears here

**Access**:
- [Clarity Services](https://www.clarityservices.com/)
- Call 1-866-390-3118

---

## CoreLogic Credco

**Purpose**: Mortgage, rental, and property tax data

**Relevance**: Used by mortgage lenders, landlords, utility companies

**Access**:
- [CoreLogic Consumer Affairs](https://www.corelogic.com/)
- Call 1-877-532-8778

**Tip**: Verify mortgage and rental payment accuracy

---

## TeleCheck

**Purpose**: Check-writing history to prevent fraud

**Relevance**: Retailers use to verify checks

**Access**:
- Call 1-800-366-2425
- [TeleCheck Services](https://www.telecheck.com/)

---

## Early Warning Services (EWS)

**Purpose**: Banking and checking account activity (broader than ChexSystems)

**Access**:
- [Early Warning Services](https://www.earlywarning.com/)
- Call 1-800-325-7775

**Tip**: Use with ChexSystems for complete banking history

---

## FactorTrust

**Purpose**: Alternative financial data for subprime lending decisions

---

## Action Items

- [ ] Request free annual report from each bureau
- [ ] Review for errors or fraudulent activity
- [ ] Dispute inaccuracies promptly
