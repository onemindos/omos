> Social Sharing Website For Viral Traffic
>
> Social media websites have taken the world by storm, with the
> emergence of websites such as Facebook and Twitter, the spread of
> information has become much easier. In this article, I will give a
> brief overview of the various social sharing websites available on the
> internet and how it can help your business to grow.
>
> Facebook is the largest social networking website in the world. With
> over 500 million users, the potential of growing your business on
> Facebook is huge. Facebook has a useful feature called Facebook Fan
> Pages, where local businesses, artists and communities can create fan
> pages to connect with them. You can also advertise to a huge database
> of people through Facebook's advertising system.
>
> Twitter is the largest micro blogging platform in the world. It's user
> base is in the millions and you can easily share content of interest,
> join conversations and connect with your community through short
> sentences known as tweets. The hash tag command allows people to
> search for trending topics to see what's hot and what's not.
>
> Web 2.0 websites such as Squidoo and HubPages allow marketers and
> people to create single page websites that incorporate various social
> sharing tools. It allows people to easily comment on such pages very
> similar to blogs.
>
> Social sharing or social bookmarking websites exist such as Digg.com
> and Reddit.com. This allows people to share sites of interest and
> people who follow you that share the same interest can easily follow
> what you are following.
>
> The power of the internet is endless. The spread of information is
> getting faster and faster. If you truly want to build a profitable
> online business, you'll have to tap into these social media sharing
> tools or you'll be leaving tons of money on the table. Worst still; be
> overshadowed by your competitors.
