An overview of paid traffic Methods

Paid traffic can be a very fast and effective way to build traffic fast
to your online business. The most common types of paid traffics are pay
per click, pay per impression, and fixed advertising rates.

In pay per click, ad networks such as Google and Facebook allow you to
post up text or banner ads which promote your website. You will have to
bid a certain amount to have your ad posted on the network and they will
charge you per click that you get. This is a good way to get traffic but
make sure that you convert your traffic or collect their information
before they leave.

The next type of paid traffic is pay per impression. Similarly, you pay
the advertising network a fixed cost for a certain number of times your
ad is viewed by customers. For example, you might pay 1 dollar for every
1000 eyeballs on your ad. Your ad could be either a text ad or a banner
ad. If you are not good at designing banner ads, you could just take a
screen shot of your text ad.

Finally, we have fixed cost advertising. In this method, people who wish
to advertise typically contacts a website owner or advertiser and
enquires how much it would cost to place a fixed banner ad at a given
position on the website. Normally, ad space with more visibility costs
more so be cautious as to where you wish to place your ad and make sure
it is within your budget.

Some examples of banner and text ad networks include Google Advertising,
Yahoo Advertising Solutions and Facebook. There are also third party
advertising networks which can provide a steady source of traffic at a
price as well. The best way is to test and analyse which network gives
the best quality of traffic for your website.
