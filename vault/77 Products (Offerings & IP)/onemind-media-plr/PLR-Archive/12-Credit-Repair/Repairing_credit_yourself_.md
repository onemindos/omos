<p>&nbsp;I would like to share steps on how to fix
<a href="http://www.amerifico.com">bad credit</a>. These steps may seem easy, 
but they require a lot of dedication, time and patience. I would also forewarn 
you that a single mistake on your part, can further ruin your
<a href="http://www.amerifico.com">credit</a> and you may end up buying another 
7-14 years for that negative item to stay on your report. </p>
<ul>
  <li>First step is to obtain your <a href="http://www.amerifico.com">credit</a> 
  reports from all <a href="http://www.amerifico.com">credit</a> bureaus. </li>
  <li>Review the reports and locate the negative/incorrect items. </li>
  <li>Once you have located these items, start dispute them with the credit 
  bureaus (Be careful here and remember what you tell them; one wrong verifiable 
  statement, and your credit file will be flagged) </li>
  <li>Disputed items are removed or corrected. Items that were not re-verified 
  are removed. <br>
  Helpful Tip: Send disputes during busy times of the year. Delays can be cause 
  in verification process if disputes are sent during the first 2 weeks of 
  November or last 2 weeks of December. </li>
</ul>
