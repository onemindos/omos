# Get to know about some equipment required for making sushi

Get to know about some equipment required for making sushi
There is some special and some common equipment required for making sushi at home. Some of the equipments might be already available at home and some are required to be purchased from the market. Cutting boards, strainer, grater, non stick fry pan, measuring spoons and cups, tweezers, peeler and plastic wraps are some of the essential things required for making sushi.
Peeler – This is required for the peeling the vegetables that are to be used as toppings. 
Tweezers – This is the special equipment which is used for removing the bones that are left-over in fish. It can be very hazardous if you encounter a fish bone in your mouth. There are some tweezers that have a broad tip at front and are ribbed from the inner side; these are preferred to be used to get the best results. 
Plastic wraps – These are used for wrapping the ingredients to keep them fresh. It is recommended to not choose the wraps that are thin in size as they cannot be handled with convenience while making sushi. These can be used for rolling the bamboo mat.
Non stick fry pan – Little bit cooking is required to be done without using oil while making sushi, so it is recommended to make use of non stick fry pan. These are the best for cooking without oil. 
Strainer – This is one of the necessary equipments that are needed for making sushi. For washing the finely cut ingredients, a mesh strainer is required to be used that is used in majority of sushi dishes.
Cutting boards – These are also required for the purpose of cutting the ingredients. A wooden cutting board in lieu of plastic is always preferred that can be used for cutting different items simultaneously without washing again and again.
