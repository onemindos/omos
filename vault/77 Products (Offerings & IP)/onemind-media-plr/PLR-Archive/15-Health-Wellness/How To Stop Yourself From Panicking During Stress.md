# How To Stop Yourself From Panicking During Stress

How To Stop Yourself From Panicking During Stress
Stress could be caused by events that triggered our body’s fight or flight reaction. Stress can be a good motivation factor to get things done or saving yourself from dangerous situations. However, stress could be a bad thing in prolong period. Stress about future is usually caused by fear. Stress about the present could be caused by the feeling of powerless.
Panic attack is similar to anxiety attack. It involves fear and phobia of certain things that trigger the body to experience pounding heart, chest pain, trembling, nausea and many more. One of the contributing factors to panic attack is stress. Therefore, it is important to handle panic attack in times of stress to prevent it from ruining your life.
First of all, it is better to identify the cause of stress. Take a moment to consider what is the think that is causing the stress and making you panic.
Then, think of an appropriate response. Stress is always present but you can choose to handle your panic attack by how you respond to it. You can choose to shake the panic attack off, ignore it, or face it head on. 
Thirdly, let it all go. If there is nothing you could do in certain situation, stop worrying about it. It might seem easier said than done but it is possible. Let yourself relax. Breathe in as slow and deeply as possible through nose and exhale slowly through mouth. Repeat for at least 10 times. It is good to distract yourself with something happy or focus on things that need to be done. If not possible, get some exercise and take a walk out. Let yourself appreciate the nature’s wonders. 
Sometimes, it is also important to remember that you cannot handle everything in one go. Take one step at a time according to plan. For a long period, write down a plan with attainable goals and steps to avoid stressful situations. Being prepared with contingency plans will help to accommodate stress that could be encounter later.
No matter how hard you try but you find yourself struggling, it could be due to unrealistic goals. Try being realistic and learn from experiences. Remember to be true to yourself is what matters most.
