Here are some alternatives to Viome for gut health testing that offer comprehensive insights into your microbiome and personalized recommendations:
1. Tiny Health
• Focus: Gut health test for adults and infants, with an emphasis on diversity of gut bacteria and markers for leaky gut, parasites, or infectious bacteria.
• Pricing: $169 for the Adult Gut Health Test, or $199 for the Gut Baseline Assessment which includes a one-on-one coaching call.
• Pros: Offers dietary, supplement, and lifestyle recommendations. Includes a microbiome coaching call.
• Cons: Takes 3-4 weeks for results (longer than Viome), and does not offer its own supplements.
• Website: Tiny Health
2. Zoe
• Focus: Provides in-depth stool analysis and gut bacteria sequencing, with personalized dietary recommendations to optimize gut health and overall well-being.
• Pricing: Typically priced at $249.
• Pros: Offers advanced sequencing technology and detailed insights, including food and lifestyle recommendations.
• Cons: Not yet available in all countries, and no supplement program.
• Website: Zoe
3. BIOHM
• Focus: Specializes in identifying bacterial and fungal strains in the gut microbiome and provides recommendations for balancing these to improve gut health.
• Pricing: $131.99 for the Gut Test Kit.
• Pros: Affordable pricing and co-founded by renowned scientist Dr. Mahmoud Ghannoum, focusing on the “mycobiome.”
• Cons: No personalized supplements included.
• Website: BIOHM
4. Verisana
• Focus: Comprehensive Gut Biome & Health Test that analyzes bacterial balance, Candida, mold levels, and gut mucosal immunity markers like secretory IgA.
• Pricing: $249.95.
• Pros: In-depth stool analysis with fast turnaround (1 week) and visual reports.
• Cons: No insurance accepted, not available in certain states (NY, NJ, RI, MD).
• Website: Verisana
