                            INTRODUCTION

We’ve all known people who always seem happy, full of energy and ready to take on the
world, no matter what. They’re always smiling and appear to have as much energy at the
end of the day as they do at the beginning, which they always start with a smile. Is
unstoppability some sort of super power? Or do these people know something that we
don’t know? The answer to both questions is yes.
The unstoppable walk among us and you can be one of them. Unstoppability is a super
power, but not a supernatural one, which means that it is something that anyone can
achieve or attain. The power to be unstoppable lies within you. The knowledge to be
unstoppable lies within you as well. Becoming unstoppable is all about developing your
awareness of what is possible and having the determination to get the most out of
everything that you are and that you can be. The purpose of this book is to help you take
action by exploring mindsets and rituals that will help you become unstoppable, every day,
for the rest of your life.
How to Be Unstoppable Every Day of Your Life is a strategic and holistic approach to
every day living.
This book offers several activities that require making lists and thinking out specific
statements. I suggest keeping a notebook or journal for doing this work. This written
documentation in itself can be an excellent reference, refresher and ritual for keeping your
unstoppable power strong.
The author advocates seeking specific expertise and deeper understanding of topics that
we touch on, especially in the areas of food, physical activity, creativity and meditative
techniques. Curiosity and the desire to increase knowledge is one of the keys to living an
unstoppable lifestyle.
                                 CHAPTER 1
                                  Begin the Day


Mindset
Is every day truly a new beginning or merely a continuation of the day before? Can it not
be both? It all is a matter of how you look at it. Perspective is everything. Measurements
of time, whether minutes, hours, days, months, years — or even lifetimes — seem
meaningful only when placed into contexts through which you can understand them. Why
not purposely put each day into a context that is useful as well?
Unstoppable people make their waking hours count by putting them into context before —
not after — the time is spent. Let’s take a deeper look at what that means. Rather than
simply letting your day happen to you, with your mind and mood being controlled by the
events that unfold each hour, why not take the opposite approach? Starting each day with a
statement of intent, with your own purpose-minded attitude as the cast for the coming
hours, gives you the focus, the control, and the power to determine the outcome of the
time you’ve spent. It’s like creating a blueprint that guides you through the day, allowing
you to come out on top — and unstoppable.
How can you create that guiding blueprint? Ask yourself this question: how do I start my
day? Literally, how do you start your day? When you wake up in the morning, what’s the
first thing you do? Some people lie in bed for a moment, just warming up to being awake.
Maybe you yawn and stretch. Perhaps you jump out of bed immediately and make a
beeline for the bathroom or the coffee pot. What about first taking a moment every
morning to get your mind right, to reflect on your purpose and set your intention for the
day?


Ritual
  Take 5-10 minutes when you wake up each day to find focus and set your intention for
 the day.
  Breathe deep – Standing or sitting, in a straight up posture, slowly inhale and exhale to
 clear your mind and your lungs.
  Express gratitude – Make a short mental list of things you are happy to have in your
 life. Be grateful for these things. Say them out loud.
  Set the tone – Become determined to approach your day with positivity, compassion,
 strength, and whatever other moods and feelings you wish to strive for and resolve to do
 so.
  Visualize – Paint a picture in your mind of how you literally wish to look and feel
 throughout your day. If you can see it, you can be it.

Set a specific intention; speak your intention into being. What is it that you will work to
achieve today? This can be tied to the tone and visualization you performed in the
previous step, and can include specific goals or challenges that you expect to tackle during
the day.
Once you’ve taken the time to set your intention and your tone, it’s time to get moving. As
you get into the business of the day, it can be easy to literally lose yourself in that
“business.” In other words: our time becomes so cluttered with activity that our thoughts
become cluttered as well. Do not allow your intention for the day to get lost in that clutter.
Create a reminder for yourself that will pull you back to your intention and purpose as
needed. Some people might choose a talisman or touchstone, like a piece of jewelry or an
image. For others, it might be something even more basic than that. Simply writing the
specific words down on a piece of paper and placing it where you can see it can be just the
thing you need to keep yourself centered in your intention for the day.


Ritual
Movement is a great way to increase your alertness and energy that will last throughout
the day. As a part of your daily routine, I strongly recommend getting at least an hour of
physical exercise— and mornings are an excellent time to do just that. Choose what kind
of movement is right for you. One choice might be gentler exercises, like tai chi or yoga.
A lot of people run in the morning. Some might take part in aerobic or creative dance.
Maybe you’ll decide on a simple stretching routine or perhaps even combine a few
moments of gentle movement and work up to an aerobic exercise.
Mornings are a great time to exercise. You feel great after doing it especially when
interacting with nature. It’s invigorating and a great feeling to know throughout the day
that you’ve already gotten in that needed workout. You will benefit by being noticeably
stronger and more energetic throughout the day when you start your day with exercise. It’s
important to exercise regularly, no matter what; daily, if possible, but consider devoting
time to some kind of physical activity every morning, even for a just a few minutes.
Remember, an object in motion stays in motion. A great definition of unstoppable!
                                  CHAPTER 2
                          Put On The Right Attitude


Mindset
Many people allow their lives to be shaped entirely by external events. It can ruin their
day if something doesn’t go their way. There are many things that you cannot control –
like the weather, the color of the sky or what time your boss decides to leave the office,
right? But there is one thing that you can control, and it’s more powerful and meaningful
to you than any of the things that you can’t control – and that’s your attitude towards
everything else. There are any number of analogies that fit this idea. Two popular ones
immediately come to mind:
 The glass is half full
 and
  “I think I can!” - the timeless mantra of the Little Engine that Could

These little anecdotes and sayings are about mindsets and perspective. The person who
sees the half empty glass is living in a place of lack. Their mind is focused on what they
don’t have rather than what they do have. The “engine” that sees the uphill climb as
impossible is focused on what they think they can’t do. Their negative mindset will keep
them from even trying to reach the top. The great thing about any mindset is that it can be
changed, and you have the power to do that for yourself.
To start taking control of your mindset, take a look at the following areas:
  Inner dialogue
  Word choice
  Tone and attitude

Statistics show that many people are most unhappy in the mornings because once awake,
their minds automatically go to the things they dread doing or feeling. People do this out
of habit, but like all bad habits, this pattern can be broken. Flip the script on negative
thinking by taking action – in ways that are mindful and proactive.
Let’s face it – life happens. We all have things to do that we like less than others, but those
less-liked tasks don’t have to be torture. There’s no reason to let dread stop you from
enjoying yourself, appreciating your worth and getting the most out of every experience.


Ritual
Arrange to do your toughest tasks first. Getting those things out of the way will not only
provide a sense of relief once they’re done – we all have stuff we just want to get over –
but more importantly, it will provide the opportunity for a sense of accomplishment. Don’t
underestimate the power of a task well done – in fact, take advantage of it. Pat yourself on
the back and go on, with your unstoppable self! But, it is while you are doing those
difficult tasks that your unstoppable mindset can be most useful. This is the time to train
and develop your muscles of determination and self-mastery. Instead of focusing on the
things that make a task less than something to look forward to, challenge yourself to focus
on the benefits.


Rewrite your inner dialog:
The conversation that takes place in your head around a task to which you’re not looking
forward might sound something like this:
“I don’t feel like doing that right now. It’s going to be hard to get it done. I don’t want to
do that right now. I’d rather be doing something else.”
When you hear those words come up in your head, first work on amending them by adding
the word “but” and finishing the sentence with a more positive statement. For instance:
“I don’t feel like doing that right now, but I’m smart enough to get it done pretty quickly.”
Or “I don’t want to do that right now, but I’ve got great ideas about how to get it done,
and done right.”
Practice flipping the sunny side up on the negative statements in your head. As you get
good at doing this, begin to eliminate the negative statements altogether, so that you’re left
with a running dialogue of positivity. As you focus on these things, you are developing the
natural habit of seeking out the positive aspects of the tasks and responsibilities that are
naturally a part of your day. You may even find yourself looking forward to doing things
that you disliked before, simply because you have developed a mindset that enables you to
view the benefits and outcomes differently.
As you practice rewriting your inner dialog, you may notice how powerful simple words
can be. Changing a word here or there can completely change the meaning and tone of a
sentence. Try this:
Make a game of eliminating negative language. When you hear yourself saying (or
thinking) in negative terms, challenge yourself to change the sentence by removing words
like “can’t, don’t, won’t.” Removing the “have nots” and “ain’t gots” from your
vocabulary forces you to think and speak in a completely different way, again helping you
to proactively develop the unstoppable mindset that you desire. After all, the person who
says, “I can’t imagine how I’m going to get all of this done in the next hour!” is setting
himself or herself up for failure, as opposed to the person who says, “I’ve got to figure this
out. How can I do it?” That person is gearing themselves up for the challenge, opening
their mind to the possibilities that will ultimately help them solve the task. The
unstoppable mind is open, receptive and ready. The right language is the key.
Mindset - Spread the sunshine
We’ve talked about flipping the sunny side up by eliminating negative language from your
inner dialogue. Doing so in your conversations with others is the next step. Take the
concept deeper by proactively integrating positivity into the mix.
Just as negativity is viral, positivity is even more so. Try this:


Ritual
Look at yourself in a mirror and smile. Do so sincerely – smile like you mean it. Ask
yourself what it is about yourself and your life that deserves a smile. You will be
surprised at how long the list can be. Set an intention to share that smile and sincerity with
other people throughout the day. Then, pay it forward.
Compliment someone today. Again, be sincere about it. Look beyond physical
appearances for the source of compliments. For instance, tell someone else that you think
they have nice handwriting, or a great way with words, or that their positive attitude is a
good influence on the rest of the team. Acknowledging the best in others will make them
feel good – and you will feel good, too. Your ability to do this effectively is another way
to keep developing your unstoppable mindset – it will keep you alert and mindful enough
to notice and appreciate the people and things around you.
                                   CHAPTER 3
                       Reaction: The Choice Is Yours


Mindset
Unstoppable people have mastered the art of self responsibility. This means that they
know their happiness, their motivation, and their determination and ability to get the most
out of life lies within themselves. Instead of blaming other people or external events to
control their moods and responses, they pay attention to what’s going on around them and
carefully choose their responses. Again, there is no magic spell or formula that allows
them to do this – you can do it, too. Another popular saying comes to mind – you’ve
heard it before:
If life hands you lemons, make lemonade!
It’s all about training yourself to think before reacting to situations, giving yourself time to
choose your response, rather than acting reflexively. Author Steven Covey, who wrote the
iconic book, “The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People” wrote extensively about the idea
of operating within your sphere of influence. To sum it up, Covey encouraged people to
work on those things upon which they could immediately affect change, rather than fret
about things that are outside of your control. When you realize that your attitude, your
mindset, and the way that you react to situations, are, in fact, within your control, then it
becomes easy to calibrate your response. It’s all about making a choice to be responsible
for yourself and your actions. Try this:


Ritual
We’ve all been in situations where we wished we had acted differently. Recall one of
them. Perhaps a co-worker made a rude remark, or you became frustrated by someone’s
poor customer service. As you recall the situation, think about how it made you feel and
what your subsequent reaction was. Now think about who, or what, was in control of that
situation? Did you allow someone else’s actions determine your reactions on that day? Or
maybe it was just your emotions, triggered by external forces that created your response.
But, rather than be controlled by the external, can you create your own response?
Yes, you can.
Close your eyes and examine the same situation in a new light. Think critically about how
things might have turned out differently if you had just taken a moment to think about
your response before reacting? What if you had chosen a different reaction – one that was
calmer, more rational, or perhaps one that invited a discussion or cooperative solution
instead of one that left someone feeling angry or even more frustrated?
The ability to choose your response to any situation is within you. Time – a few seconds
worth – is the key to claiming that power. Time to make this a new habit. You can train
yourself to do this with practice. Earlier in the book we discussed the importance of setting
a daily intention – purposely deciding what you want from your day by making that
determination and sticking to it. Try building the time/choice factor into your intention by
making a resolution:
In all situations I encounter today, I will take the time to think before I act. I will use that
time to choose my responses. I will choose to act in ways that are wise, positive and
constructive.
Note that the statement above refers to taking the time to choose an appropriate response
in all situations, not just negative or contentious ones. Practicing the time/choice factor
even in simple situations, like holding the door for someone or striking up a conversation
with a casual acquaintance, is easy to do and taps the power of your imagination. It
requires you to be alert, aware and mindful. More importantly, it taps the power of your
imagination during times when you would otherwise be simply going through the motions.
Instead of living by rote and falling into the same old conversations every day, give
yourself a moment and use it to choose to be spontaneous and make the most of your
opportunities. Choose to be unstoppable.
                                 CHAPTER 4
                                 Sustain Yourself


Think of your unstoppable mind as a garden that needs air, water and light in order to
grow. A garden left untended might not blossom as well as you had hoped. You can leave
your mindset and attitude to chance, dependent on the whims and actions of others, the
position of the moon and stars, or other things that are outside of your control. Or you can
take control and actively nurture your self, choosing to sustain your unstoppable mind
with the things that will promote growth, energy and contentment in your life. How can
you do that?
The unstoppable mind is most energized through freedom of expression. This means
offering yourself the chance to step outside of the box – the confines of necessity that
often define our lives in terms of work, school and day-to-day routines that can become
routine and absent-minded over time, when we allow them to. There are three essential
areas of expression for energizing and replenishing an unstoppable mind and attitude:
  Creative
  Physical
  Elemental

Taking time to explore these areas of expression is a proactive way to nurture and grow
the garden of the unstoppable mind. Let’s take a look at some rituals to connect with the
three expressions.


Mindset
Creative minds enrich the world. We live in a world enhanced by creativity, filled with
shapes, color and sounds, where anyone with the will and opportunity and freedom to do
so can sketch a picture, compose a poem, or dance an impromptu waltz across a crowded
floor. Nature itself is the greatest artist of all, stimulating our lives with starlight and
windsong, and inspiring our own creativity to flourish and grow. Studies have been done
showing that creativity can become stifled, as children become adults, limiting creative
expression in our everyday interactions with each other. But these restraints on our natural
desire to express ourselves creatively are self-imposed. One energizing ritual to promote
an unstoppable mind is to allow regular time for creative expression in your life.
Perhaps you already have a natural talent for art, dance or music. Maybe you would like to
try something you’ve never attempted before, like sculpture or poetry. Whether that’s true
or not, allow yourself the freedom to enjoy a creative activity, free of the pressure for
perfection, free from judgment. Try art for art’s sake. First, simply allow yourself to
appreciate creativity. There are a number of ways to do this:


Ritual
Read a poem – out loud. Poetry written for children is a great place to start. Lose yourself
in the look and sound of the words, the rhymes and the rhythms. Then perhaps attempt
writing your own poetry. If you are into words, explore different forms, like sonnets and
haiku. Language can be a varied and nurturing means of creative expression. It provides a
great way to focus your feelings and connect with your own humanity.
Visual Art – invest a couple of bucks in some crayons or colored pencils, and a blank
notepad. Keep them in your desk or bag. Take some time out to doodle or sketch. Enjoy an
explosion of color and amuse yourself with your depictions of whatever comes to mind.
Music – set aside some time to listen to your favorite music. Close your eyes and enjoy the
sounds. Listen mindfully. Or, get up and dance and sing along. You’ve heard that saying,
“Dance like nobody is watching?” Incredible stress release and a sense of freedom can
come along with letting your body move, uninhibited. Which leads to the next
expression…


Mindset
In order to maintain health, the physical bodies we inhabit need exercise. We often find
ourselves maintaining a minimum state of physical health by doing only that which is
necessary to subsist. But you can increase the value of even the most basic exercises, like
stretching your fingers or taking a daily walk, by not only performing the physical activity
but also doing so with mindfulness. This means actively focusing on the activity, on the
energy, breath and brain power that it takes to execute the motion. Try this out:


Ritual
This is an activity you can do just about anywhere or anytime. I call it the “mindfulness
stretch.” It only takes a few minutes and it is a great way to get in the habit of mindfulness.
Stand as tall and straight as you possibly can, with your arms loose at your sides, and close
your eyes. Inhale and exhale several times. Be mindful of your breathing. Imagine the air
filling and exiting your lungs. Be aware of your shoulders and chest rising and falling with
your breath. Allow your body to relax and slowly bend forward, folding yourself in half.
As you lower your upper body, your fingers will dangle toward the floor. Once you have
bent forward as far as you can do comfortably, without pain or strain, then stop.
Now stretch your fingers downward. Continue to do so mindfully, feeling the energy flow
through your fingers as you extend those muscles in a striving stretch toward the floor.
Slowly return to a standing position. As you do so, focus on how good it feels to be able to
bend and stretch, to relax or stand alert, and to be able to control and enjoy the living,
breathing organism that is your body. This simple ritual allows you to develop the habit of
mindfulness, allowing you to make the most of every moment. Be sure to appreciate and
exercise your body as often as possible. That’s the kind of thing unstoppable people do
every day.


Elemental
Connecting with the universe is an essential way of connecting with your self. We inhabit
a vast universe that we share with other beings like and unlike ourselves, as well as
immeasurable oceans and countless stars. Hearken back to your earliest origins and you
will find evidence that at some point, countless aeons in the past, we are made of the same
stuff as those oceans and stars. To appreciate the elements is to appreciate your world and
your place in it. The same unstoppable force that formed mountains and men lives within
you. Take a moment to respect it.


Ritual
Fire – Light a single candle. Sit or stand before it and practice a simple breathing exercise,
meditate or pray. Doing so in the dark or in an outdoor setting can enhance the experience.
Earth – Go for a walk. Whether hiking in nature or down a city block, allow your feet to
connect with the earth beneath you. Think about what it means to be here today, grounded
in the bounty of our home planet earth. Treat this gift accordingly.
Water – A large percentage of the human body is made up of water. Drink plenty. While
doing so, appreciate the energy and light that water provides to our bodies. It’s amazing
stuff.
A friend of mind had a mantra ritual for staying mindful of the miracle of water.
Whenever he needed to get mentally clear and feel grounded, he’d heed the saying, “Water
in me, water over me.” For this friend, drinking a glass of cold water or even taking a
shower was an opportunity to tap into the healing power of H20. Take every opportunity
even just to touch water at its origins – stand in the rain, dip your toes in a stream, stand at
the shore of a lake or an ocean and run your fingers through the shallows. Water is a part
of you. It’s good for your body, your mind and your overall being. Hydrated bodies and
minds work like well-lubricated machines – fluidly, effectively and efficiently. Calibrate
yourself through nature’s elixir – simple, clear water. It works.
                                  CHAPTER 5
                                    Getting Clear


Your power to be unstoppable has its basis in three things: desire, determination and will.
The desire to live with passion and fulfillment, the determination to pursue that desire, and
the willingness to change your thinking in order to achieve your goals are all you need to
make a life-changing shift. But in order to get there, you need to be clear about what it all
means and what exactly it is that you want. Let’s dive deeper and examine clarity, up
close. We’ll start by attempting to understand your desire.


Mindset
What is it exactly that you want from your life? Not in terms of material things, like a
house on the hill or a boat in the bay – which, ultimately, can often actually be the result of
living in an unstoppable mindset – but what is it that you want to do with your time? What
is it that you love to do that would give you the most satisfaction and make you feel like
your best self? Try this:


Ritual
Close your eyes. Take a few deep breaths to clear your mind. Ask for clarity. Ask
yourself the question, “What is my passion?” Perhaps you’ve always enjoyed art or
writing. Perhaps you love cooking, or helping others do so. The beautiful thing about
passion is that it can be chameleon, changing with the years or the seasons, and it can be
multi-faceted. There may be many things you enjoy doing, but for some reason, you’re not
doing those things. Taking the time to reconnect with your desire is the first step. Once
you’ve figured out what it is, meditate again. This time ask for help in pursuing it.
Think about ways to get involved in the activities that would give you the most pleasure.
Get on the internet or go to the library to research your topic – you will likely find classes,
support groups or clubs, volunteering opportunities, even things like performances and
events – that open a door to engaging with the people and things that you want in your life.
Take it a step further – go beyond thought to action. Join that bicycle club or cooking
class. Audition for that local theater show. Change it up for yourself and try something
new. Doing things you love will enrich your life beyond the day-to-day existence you’ve
become used to.
Too many people get caught up in the routine of their daily lives. Life becomes boring, or
even painful and grueling because we’re focused on mere sustenance and survival. Finding
and pursuing a passion is the way life opens the door to getting the most out of every
moment. It gives you something to look forward to. It gives you purpose and that’s what
will give you the determination and desire to be unstoppable.
                                 CHAPTER 6
      Acknowledge Humanity and Make the Connection


Throughout the book we’ve talked about the idea of being unstoppable as both a state of
mind and a matter of choice. Some would argue that it’s easier to choose that mental state
when things are going the way you want them to. But what about those times when things
aren’t going quite your way? These are the times when you most need your super power of
unstoppability to see you through.


Mindset
Being unstoppable does not mean that challenges won’t happen or that obstacles will
automatically be removed from your path. What it does mean is that you now have the
ability to put those hurdles into perspective, to get over them and come out on the other
side, better for your experience.
When you find yourself faced with a hurdle, whether it’s a problem at work, a health issue,
or just that you are feeling down, power lies in the way you handle the circumstance –
instead of letting it handle you.


Ritual
Acknowledge your humanity – part of the human experience is dealing with adversity.
The amazing thing about being human that we have the ability to face and resolve our
problems. There’s nothing you can’t figure out, but you must be willing to try. Celebrate
your ability to make the choice, get into it and do it.
Acknowledge the issue – literally sit down and face the issue, whatever it is. Write it
down on a piece of paper. A friend of mine likes to draw a picture of a monster that
represents whatever problem she is facing so that she has something tangible to look in the
eye. She goes through the following steps, writing her feelings down as well. Then she
tears up the picture, letting the problem “know” that she intends to destroy it. It’s a
cathartic exercise that may sound silly, but feels quite good. Try it sometime.
Acknowledge your feelings – take a moment to identify how this problem makes you feel.
Scared? Insecure? Angry? That’s okay. It’s normal. You are human, remember? So
identify and experience those emotions, but don’t dwell on them. Allow yourself some
time for those feelings so that you can release them and move on.
Acknowledge your solution - now that you’ve identified and visualized the problem, it is
time to take action. Get another piece of paper and start brainstorming your solutions.
What needs to be done to tackle the problem? Is there someone whose help you can enlist?
(By the way, don’t be afraid or ashamed to ask for help. Unstoppable people know there’s
strength in numbers.) Think it through and come up with a plan. Once you have that plan,
you can take the necessary steps to execute it. You’ve got the courage, creativity and
determination to take on any challenge that comes your way.


Mindset
Many people associate the idea of being successful with the idea of wealth. They think
that money is the key to happiness, but the truth is, they’ve got it exactly wrong. Ever hear
the saying, “Success is nothing without someone to share it with?” The truth lies in that
statement but there’s much more to it than that. First of all, no one achieves “success”
entirely by themselves, without the involvement of others at some point.
The point we’re getting at here is living with impact and meaning is not about the money
you make, but the connections you make with others. Other people are the ones who will
help you get where you are going. Other people are the ones who will support you through
your journey, celebrate your successes with you, and make it all worthwhile. What kind of
connections do you have in your world?
We make a big deal of the concept of networking in contemporary society. The idea that
it’s not what you know, it’s who you know often proves to be true when people are trying
to get ahead in their professional lives. The same is true when it comes to physiological
well being, as well. Think about it. The incredible energy that passes from person to
person in any interaction, a fleeting glance or a touch on the arm can be more powerful
than a spoken conversation. A simple handshake speaks volumes. Thinking about
someone or just saying their name can create a sensation as tangible as a physical
embrace. Our connections to other people matter.


Ritual
Relationships can be a powerful source of the energy that it takes to move forward in your
life with purpose and meaning. Your relationships are worth a second look. Take a
moment to examine them. Make a short list of two or three people in your life with whom
you have regular, frequent close contact with. These may be friends, family members or
co-workers. Then, start writing down the things about those people that you admire or
enjoy. Try to fill up a page with adjectives describing their best characteristics. The list
may look something like this: Friendly. Straightforward. Considerate. Funny. Generous.
Gentle. Bold. Smart. Exciting.
Now expand on the idea by thinking about someone that you look up to or think would be
a good role model. Add to your list by putting down words that describe the qualities in
that person that you think is admirable. You may find yourself duplicating words from the
original list, which is okay. Put checks or stars by those words, or go ahead and write them
again or put checks or stars by them. The idea is to fill the page with words that call out
the attributes that you admire in others.
For the next week or so, make a daily random pick from the list. Close your eyes and put
your finger somewhere on the page. Select the word that your finger lands closest to and
focus on embodying that characteristic for the day. Find ways to incorporate that attribute
into your behavior towards others. Say, for instance, that you pick the word “generous.”
Make a point of expressing generosity to other people today. Be creative and expand your
idea of what it means to be generous. You can be generous with your time, with your
patience and with your love. What about the word “bold?” How can you be bold today?
Maybe you’ll express boldness in your approach to a problem, in your stride, or maybe
even in your choice of the colors you wear.
Get the picture? The point of this exercise is to help you define your own unstoppability
by connecting with the most powerful character attributes that you’ve identified in others.
Like attracts like. Surrounding yourself with admirable people will give you an
inexhaustible source of positive inspiration to shape your best self.
                                  CHAPTER 7
                                   Who Are You?


You are what you eat. You are what you think. You are what you do. It would stand to
reason, then, that eating well, thinking progressively, and doing positive things, all play
important roles in our ability to get the most out of life. Invest in yourself by taking the
time to examine and do the work to improve and make the necessary adjustments in these
areas.


Mindset
You are what you eat. We live in a society where an indescribably vast array of food
choices is made available to us on a daily basis. That is because technological advances
have made it possible to transport, preserve and combine a variety of foods to which our
ancestors would not have had access or even been able to imagine. We are living in an age
of abundance. Certainly, there are a number of benefits as well as disadvantages when it
comes to this idea of “progress” in food technology. Consider what it means to your body
and your health, in terms of your daily diet. What are you consuming?


Ritual
Close your eyes and think of your body as a temple. This is the church of your soul. This
is the vessel through which you live, breathe and express that infinitely higher being which
is your self. Doesn’t that self deserve health? Doesn’t that self deserve the finest treatment
and the highest quality energy that you can provide to it?
As you go through your day, pay attention to the incredible things that this fine body is
able to do. Think about the foods that you are eating. Consider whether you want to make
changes to the quality or content of what you consume in order to give your body the type
of nourishment it deserves. You may already be making lifestyle changes in order to lose
weight or get healthier. If you are looking to do so, consider “clean eating,” which is a
simple way of getting back to basic, wholesome foods, rejecting as many preservatives
and chemical processes as possible, and providing your body with efficient, healthy
energy so that you can do the things you need to do. You’ll get out of your body what you
put in. Is it wholesome? Is it healthy? Is it quality? Those are the questions to ask yourself,
remembering always to be grateful.
Ritual
What qualities can you identify in the foods you eat that you would like also to be
reflected in your character? My list looks something like this:
  wholesome
  pure
  real
  natural
  nutritious
  fun
  good
  conscious

Now, Take two or three of the words from your list and write out a sentence describing
how each word translates to your human self.


Some examples:
Real – To be real means not being fake or phony. It means what you see is what you get
(WYSISWG)!
Nutritious – Being around me is good for other people because I’m positive and fun to be
with, and I also strive to be good for myself.
Conscious – I am conscious and aware of my environment and my choices.
A couple of extra notes about nutrition: Make informed decisions about your food choices
by understanding how different ingredients affect your body. Beware of diets that
eliminate entire food groups – if you choose something like that, make sure you
understand the implications of cutting out certain nutrients and that you do include healthy
replacement foods in your diet. Skipping meals is never a good decision. Your body is an
engine that needs energy – food – to run.
If you’re looking to lose weight, seek specific knowledge about how metabolism works –
you will learn that eating healthier, frequent meals can help you lose weight by providing
the engine with fuel to burn, rather than getting your body in the habit of reserving fuel in
the form of fat. It’s worth it to seek the help of a medical professional or nutritionist, or
even to consult a few books on the subject, to be sure that you are eating efficiently and
effectively. You can improve your intake simply by adding a measure of awareness into
your food choices. Don’t forget to be grateful for the foods that sustain your energy and
keep you alive.


Mindset
You are what you think. What do you think about that? Developing the ability to control
your mindset is the basis of this book. Understanding that your state of mind is the
foundation for anything and everything that you accomplish is the main thing that will
make you unstoppable. When you are able to effortlessly put yourself into the “zone,” that
state of mind, which allows you to act with determination, decisiveness, and a sense of
purpose in everything you do, you will have mastered the super power within you.


Ritual
Visualize the person that you want to be. On the flatscreen of your mind, create your ideal
self. This person is happy, healthy, glowing with confidence and inner peace. Now
imagine that this ideal self is being interviewed on a really great day. Allow your
imagination to run wild. What’s the weather like? Picture what you’d be wearing, the chair
you’d be sitting in, the window you’d be looking out of onto a bright and a beautiful
scene. If you like, write out your answers on a piece of paper or even record a short
interview with yourself on your cell phone (it’s what I do!). Ask your ideal self the
following questions:
  How are you feeling today?
  You’ve really been doing some terrific things lately. Tell us what you’ve been up to
 and how you accomplished these things.
  What would be the best advice you could give to somebody who wants to be like you?

Once you’re done with your “interview,” go through it and review the things you said.
How do you they make you feel?
Putting yourself into a brighter, sharper state of mind is not a difficult thing to do,
although it can take practice. It is a matter of choice, will and determination.


Mindset
You are what you do.
All this talk about determining your mindset and deciding what to do is fine, but these
things exist only in a conceptual sense. In other words, none of it means anything until
you actually do something with the concepts that you’ve created.
Think about the saying, “Actions speak louder than words. In my mind, I could be the
most phenomenal, unstoppable human being on the planet – remember the ideal self you
imagined in the previous section? That person is invisible to everyone else until you start
doing the things that ideal self would do. Walk in those shoes, live that life, and perform
the actions that demonstrate who you want to be, which will show who you really are. Can
you see how the things that you do then become both the inspiration for and the fruit of
your efforts?
What’s so great about this is that you’ve already gotten started. Reading this book is an
action that shows your curiosity and your willingness to invest time and energy to
enhancing your existence. By trying any of the exercises suggested here, you’ve taken
even further action. Your behavior towards others is what people will consider when they
are forming their ideas of who you are. Remember the exercise where you listed different
adjectives to describe the people you most admire? The same things that led you to
describe those admirable people are what others will use to develop their impressions of
you.


Ritual
Revisit that list of descriptive adjectives. These are all desirable traits, right? Select two or
three words from the list and consider them carefully. We’ll use the following three words
for example:
  Friendly
  Loyal
  Strong

Now, ask yourself what things that person has done to make that a good word to describe
them. How does someone who is friendly act? What behavior does a loyal person display?
How does someone demonstrate that they are strong? Your impressions of people are
created by the things they do. Consequently, it is in your power to define yourself by
acting accordingly. Valuable time will be spent doing those things, as well, so make the
most of it by doing things that you enjoy, doing things in service to others, doing things
that set a good example and doing things that allow you to grow. Be unstoppable by
behaving as though you are.
                                  CHAPTER 8
                                Energize the Mind


If the power of unstoppability is within the mind, then it stands to reason that the brain –
that powerful organism that we think of as housing our minds – would require as much
energy and stimulation possible in order to be optimized and fully functional. The adult
brain can either continue to develop and establish new connections, or it can age and
functionality starts to deteriorate. It all depends on what you do with it. Continuous, active
learning is one way to keep the brain stimulated and growing. But sometimes people are
afraid to even try to learn something new. People often suffer from what I call the fear of
failure/fear of success syndrome. Some people fear failure because they don’t want to risk
the possibility of ridicule or feeling disappointed. Others actually, surprisingly, fear the
possibility of success because they wish to avoid the pressure of wondering how to follow
it up. They don’t want to wonder what comes next because they fear the answer.
The unstoppable among us fear only one thing – stagnation. Passionate, engaged
individuals have the determination and will to keep their minds going and growing,
because they know that their brains are like any other muscle. Left unused, it will begin to
atrophy. A life of purpose and meaning is the polar opposite of a stagnant life ruled by
boredom. People who feel they have nothing left to contribute or gain find themselves
caught up in a day-to-day routine that is dull and boring, which in turn feeds on itself.
Folks stuck in this space often become depressed or develop habits that make the situation
worse – such as overeating or dependence on substances like tobacco, alcohol and drugs.
In this way, simple boredom becomes the stimulus for destructive and dependent
behaviors, which subsequently hamper the ability for creativity and learning, and even
stunt the desire to change. Unstoppable people, on the other hand, will not allow their
minds to stagnate. You can proactively do things to stimulate life, growth and a healthy
brain.


Mindset
No one is too old, too good or too smart to learn something new. The process of learning
stimulates the mind, body and spirit. Your active, healthy mind is creative and conscious,
capable of experiencing wonder and creating wondrous things. In this state, you are able to
make the most out of life because it will be interesting, exciting and worth living.


Ritual
Become a lifelong student. Write down two or three fields of study that interest you. Use
your imagination and don’t limit yourself by considering perceived limitations, like time
or money. This is simply an exercise in exploring desire and possibility. For instance, I’ve
always been fascinated by the human brain, and also ballet. It doesn’t mean that I plan to
run away and become a brain surgeon or audition for the New York Ballet Company. But
these are topics that I’d like to know more about. What’s to stop you from learning more
about topics that interest you? To come up with a good list, you can even think back to
your childhood. Close your eyes and pretend you are that child, and ask yourself, “What
do I want to be when I grow up?” Interested in dentistry? Space travel or maybe just travel
to a foreign country? You get the idea. Figure out your interests and write them down.
Next is a challenge. Pick a topic and come up with a reasonable amount of time to expand
your knowledge on the subject. A month is a manageable length of time, during which you
can resolve to set aside one or two times a week when you will engage in learning more
about your topic. Get creative. It can be a lot of fun simply exploring the number of
resources at your disposal for learning more about your subject. You’ll find there are
books, articles or videos online, perhaps lectures to attend or interesting groups to join
(Meetup.com comes to mind). You might find yourself conducting at-home experiments
or taking outings to observe and photograph wildlife. The possibilities are endless.
The important thing here is to stimulate your mind and energize your life by learning
something new. You may decide to delve deeper into a topic, or to move on to a new one.
Keep your mind active and growing. Let learning be a lifelong pursuit. Never let it stop.

                                      Day’s End


Early on in this book we talked about establishing routines and rituals for starting off each
day. I encouraged setting intentions and setting the tone for each day as way to stay
focused, achieve goals and promote positivity. It is just as important to end each day with
routines and rituals that allow for reflection and set the stage for the kind of peaceful,
productive rest essential to good health and unstoppability. Most people already have
some sort of day’s end ritual, whether they call it that or not. Taking a shower, doing sit-
ups, saying prayers, are all rituals that we perform that can be enhanced with an added
degree of mindfulness. Again, mindfulness is that focused sense of awareness of the
action itself, the energy, thoughts and feelings involved in the activity, and the benefits
and purpose to be gained from it. Tonight, when you perform your day’s end routine,
whatever it may be, do so mindfully – and notice the difference.


Mindset
Productive sleep requires inner peacefulness and a quiet state of mind. Busy brains,
preoccupied with endlessly churning thoughts, are one of the reasons people can find it
difficult to fall asleep once they’ve gotten into bed. What can you do to put your mind at
ease and suspend the thought march until tomorrow?


Ritual
Allow extra time to achieve a quiet state of mind once all other activities are completed.
Give yourself a few minutes to think through the following highlights.
Allow yourself to reflect, and celebrate the day’s victories. What actions did you take
today to achieve your goals? Did you commit to and keep your intentions? Let your mind
sink back into the sense of accomplishment this reflection can bring. This feeling of
fulfillment promotes peace.
Close your eyes and drift off to sleep with the knowledge and joy of a day well done and
anticipate the next day.
Sleep with a sense of purpose. Your rest will rejuvenate and re-energize you to wake up to
another amazing day of being unstoppable.

                                    Pay Attention


Throughout this book we’ve talked about several ways to get our minds focused and make
purposeful, positive decisions on how to go about the day. It feels great to be unstoppable.
So how can you put it all together and use your super power to achieve specific goals? It’s
always helpful to take some time to sit down and really think through exactly what you
want and where you’re going. Writing out a personal mission statement is a good way to
stay on task and grounded in your quest for personal success. Try the following exercise as
a way to lead up to devising a clear, concise mission statement.


Ritual – Priorities
Make a list of 3-5 things that are important in your daily life. Most people’s lists will start
out something like this: family, health, job…
Then, take each word, one at a time, and write out as complete and detailed a statement as
possible describing why that person or thing is so important to you.


For instance,
“My family is important to me because they are the ones that make me laugh, take care of
me and support me, no matter what I do.” (By the way, if family is on your list of
priorities, I would challenge you to list each person separately and really get specific on
what that person means in your life. Taking the time to appreciate individuals you care
about can have a great impact, and may bring some surprises as well.) For example, “My
spouse is important to me because she understands what I’m feeling, even when I don’t
talk about it. She does nice things for me because she wants to, without me having to ask. I
could burst into tears in front of her and she wouldn’t ask questions – she would just hold
me. That’s why my spouse is important to me.”
You get the picture. It can be challenging to list out the reasons why the people and things
in your life are important to you, but as we’ve stated all along, part of being unstoppable is
being clear and specific. When you identify not only what is important to you, but why,
you will increase your motivation to maintain and improve the state of those relationships.
Take your time developing these statements. Refer to them often.
After you’ve listed the things that are important to you in your daily life, make another list
of things that are important for you to get done on a daily, weekly, or monthly basis – or
all of the above. Again, think specifics here. The list might look something like this:
  Work out
  Complete deliverable number 3 on the current work project
  Cook dinner – it’s my turn today

Again, come up with statements explaining why these things are important. Take time
developing these statements – the development may occur over weeks as you add to the
list and the descriptions. Get clarity on the things you need to know and the reason why
you are willing to do them.
Finally, look at a bigger picture. If there is some specific long-term goal you want to
achieve, write out what it is. Start out with as elaborate a description as you possibly can
of the ideal outcome to this goal. Write it out as if you had already achieved it and are
describing the final product to someone. Remember the exercise where you pretended to
be the subject of an interview? Write your statement out as if you were answering the
question, “The work you’ve done here is incredible. Tell us exactly what you’ve achieved
here. And how?”
Answering the “and how” part is important. These are the steps you will take to achieve
your goal.
Going through this exercise will help you develop a blueprint. Instead of sitting around
hoping something fantastic will happen to you, you will make it happen yourself by being
unstoppable. Get specific and clear about what you want, apply your will and
determination to the plan, and achieve the results you are looking for. You don’t need
anyone else’s permission, just the desire and determination to get things done.
