# Introduction

As people walk through the supermarket, they cannot help but take a
glimpse at the pre-packaged and ready made meals on the shelf. The
majority of the packaged foods in the grocery are not particularly
healthy or tasty! With a bit of preparation and planning, you can make a
healthier meal with fresh ingredients in no time..

When foods are pre-prepared and pre-packed, chemical flavorings,
coloring, and preservatives are added to the product. The chemical
compounds added to the food can create a seriously unhealthy diet. Most
of the fast foods taste good but aren't actually good for you! Most
people often say that the main reason why they buy pre-packed foods is
that they do not have enough time to cook, however the truth is that
healthy food can be just as quick to cook and prepare.

Healthy recipes contain fresh vegetables, whole grain foods and low fat
meals cooked with love. Right before going on your next shopping
expedition, plan out your meals for the whole week and decide what
ingredients you need to buy.

This book will serve as your ultimate guide when it comes to preparing
tasty recipes for a healthy diet. The simple steps in preparing recipes
will definitely help you a lot, which in turn give you the momentum to
continue preparing healthy meals every day. This will give you a good
reason not to skip time for breakfast, lunch, and dinner.

All the recipes are simply described so you can cook the food that your
body needs without sacrificing your health. Let the recipes in this book
become your healthy and balanced diet. You deserve to have a healthy
lifestyle and you can start this by following the recipes in this book.

# Breakfast Recipes

## Swiss Breakfast

![](media/image41.png){width="2.9723009623797023in"
height="2.3906255468066493in"}

Breakfast is the most important meal of the day. Preparing this Swiss
Breakfast recipe is a tasty and nutritious way to begin your day. This
recipe comes with a combination of tartness and sweetness. This can be
served cold or hot.

**Ingredients**

-   1 TBS dried cranberries

-   1 chopped date

-   2 TBS sliced almonds

-   2 TBS raisins

-   2 TBS sunflower seeds

-   2 TBS chopped dried apricots

-   ¼ tsp cinnamon

-   1 cup rolled oats

**Procedure**

-   Mix all the ingredients together.

-   Bring two cups of water into a boil.

-   Add the mixed ingredients and then turn the heat at a lower
    > pressure. Cook uncovered. Stir it occasionally until the water is
    > absorbed for about seven minutes.

-   Cover and then sit for 2 minutes right before serving.

-   Serve with a dairy-free milk or low-fat milk.

## Poached Eggs with Spinach

![](media/image44.png){width="3.7355424321959756in"
height="2.4843755468066493in"}

Start your day with an extra boost of greens with this easy-to-prepare
meal that you can serve for dinner or lunch.

**Ingredients**

-   1 lb of fresh spinach (or collard greens or kale)

-   1 tsp of light vinegar

-   2 omega-3 enriched eggs

> Mediterranean Dressing

-   1 medium clove garlic

-   1 tsp of fresh lemon juice

-   1 TBS of extra virgin oil

-   Sea salt and pepper to taste

**Directions: The Spinach**

-   Use a large pot and fill it with enough water to let it boil.

-   While the water is nearly boiling, chop or press the garlic and then
    > let it sit for about 15 minutes in order to improve it's healthy
    > properties.

-   Wash the spinach.

-   When the water is at its full boil, put the spinach in the pot,
    > uncovered. This helps the acids to escape in the air. Cook the
    > spinach for a minute and then start the timer as you drop the
    > spinach in the boiling water.

-   Right after the spinach has finally cooked for 1 minute, use a mesh
    > strainer with a handle in order to remove the spinach from the pot
    > and then press out the excess liquid.

-   Toss the spinach with the dressing ingredients while it is hot. The
    > Mediterranean dressing does not have to be created separately

**Directions: The Poached Eggs**

-   Bring 1 quart of water to a high simmer in a 10-inch skillet with 1
    > tsp vinegar.

-   When the water has come to a simmer, poach the eggs for 5 minutes or
    > until the whites are firm.

-   Remove the water with a slotted spoon and then place it over the
    > spinach mixture.

## Basil, Tomato, and Mushroom Frittata

![](media/image56.png){width="2.8645833333333335in" height="1.90625in"}

Frittata recipes are among the greatest additions to a healthy diet at
any time of the day. They do not only have a great flavour but also one
serving contains only 144 calories.

**Ingredients**

-   3 TBS chopped fresh basil, black pepper, and salt to taste

-   3 Large eggs

-   1 TBS + TBS chicken broth

-   ½ medium sliced tomato

-   1 cup of thinly sliced cremini mushrooms

-   3 medium cloves of pressed garlic

-   1/2 medium minced onion

**Procedure**

-   Mince the onions and then press the garlic. Let this sit for about
    > 15 minutes to bring out the flavour

-   Heat 1 TBS of broth into a 10-inch stainless steel skillet and then
    > sauté the onion over a medium low heat for about 3 minutes. Stir
    > frequently.

-   Add mushrooms and garlic and then continue to sauté for 2 minutes.

-   Add 1 TBS of broth, pepper, tomato, and salt and then cook for
    > another minute. Stir it well and then scrape gently using a wooden
    > spoon to reduce any burning.

-   Beat the eggs well and then season with pepper and salt. Mix in the
    > chopped basil and pour the eggs over the vegetables. Turn the heat
    > low. Cook and then cover for 15 minutes or until it becomes firm.
    > Cut into wedges and then serve.

## Five Spice Onion Soup

![](media/image31.png){width="2.34375in" height="2.34375in"}

This Asian-flavored onion soup is a great tasting addition to a healthy
diet. This combines with shiitake mushrooms for a unique and delicious
flavor.

**Ingredients**

-   1 TBS of black strap molasses

-   1 TBS of soy sauce

-   6 medium sized dried shiitake mushrooms

-   3 star anise

-   ½ tsp of dried fennel seeds

-   1 4-inch cinnamon stick

-   6 whole cloves

-   ½ inch of fresh peeled sliced ginger

-   1 TBS and 6 cups of vegetable or chicken broth

> Additional Ingredients in the Soup

-   white pepper and salt to taste

-   6 cloves of sliced garlic

-   2 sliced thin onions cut in half

**Procedure**

-   Chop the onion. Thinly slice the garlic and onion to be be added
    > later.

-   To prepare the onion broth, add 1 TBS of vegetable or chicken broth
    > in a medium soup hot. Sauté the chopped onions in a medium heat
    > for about 5 minutes and then stir frequently until it is
    > translucent. Add 6 cups of vegetable or chicken broth as well as
    > the remaining onion broth ingredients and then stir. Bring it to a
    > boil and then reduce the heat to medium low. Simmer the
    > ingredients together for about 20 minutes, uncovered. This will
    > bring out the flavors from the ingredients.

-   In a separate stainless steel skillet with a moderate heat, add 1
    > TBS onion broth. Sauté the sliced onions, then put on the broth
    > over a medium low heat. Stir often for 5 minutes until it is
    > translucent. Add the garlic and then sauté for 1 minute.

-   After cooking the broth for about 20 minutes, strain while it is hot
    > and then return to the pan.

-   Slice the mushrooms and return to broth.

-   Add the sautéed onions as well as the garlic. Season with salt and
    > pepper to taste.

## Zesty Mexican Soup

![](media/image46.png){width="3.5390627734033244in"
height="2.3593755468066493in"}

This recipe of spicy vegetable soup is packed with flavors. This is easy
to prepare and a perfect addition to your healthy diet. This is also a
great way to include a variety of vegetables into your meal.

**Ingredients**

-   4 medium cloves of chopped garlic

-   1 medium minced onion

-   3 TBS of red chili pepper

-   3 cups + 1 TBS of vegetables or chicken broth

-   1 small up to medium green diced bell pepper into ¼-inch pieces

-   1 small diced zucchini into ¼ inch pieces

-   1 cup of finely chopped collard greens

-   2 cups or 1 15 oz. can of rinsed black beans

-   1 15 oz. can diced tomatoes

-   1 cup of frozen yellow corn

-   1 tsp of dried oregano

-   1 4 oz. can of diced green chili

-   1 tsp of ground cumin

-   Salt and pepper to taste

-   ¼ cup of chopped pumpkin seeds

**Procedure**

-   Heat 1 TBS of broth in medium soup hot. Sauté the green peppers,
    > garlic, and onion in a broth with moderate heat for about 5 minute
    > and stir often.

-   Add the red chili powder and then mix it well. Add tomatoes and
    > broth. Cook for 5 minutes and add the green chili, corn, cumin,
    > oregano, and beans.

-   Bring into boil on a high heat. Once it starts to boil, reduce the
    > heat to medium low. Simmer uncovered for about 10 minutes longer.

-   Add the zucchini as well as the collard green and then cool for
    > about 5 minutes. Add the chopped cilantro, salt and pepper, and
    > pumpkin seeds.

## Seared Tuna

![](media/image47.png){width="3.9023403324584427in"
height="2.5989588801399823in"}

Seared Tuna is a good recipe to do because of the benefits that tuna
have. For just 10 minutes, you can enjoy this food. The way it serves is
very healthy wherein it does not require heated oil.

Cook and Preparation time: 10 minutes

**Ingredients:**

-   12 oz tuna fish cut into two

-   1 tablespoon of mirin rice wine

-   1 tablespoon of lemon juice, squeezed

-   2 tablespoon of soy sauce

-   1 tablespoon of fresh ginger, minced

-   3 tablespoon of scallion, minced

-   White pepper and salt to taste

**Directions:**

1.  Heat a pan for about 2 minutes

2.  While waiting for the pan to be heated, rub the tuna fish with lemon
    > juice and then season it with white pepper and salt and then
    > prepare the scallion and ginger.

3.  Place the tuna in the pan and cook it for about 1 to 2 minutes on
    > both sides. When it is cooked, place it on a plate.

4.  Turn the heat down and add all the ingredients in the pan and cook
    > it for about 1 minute then season with pepper and salt. Pour it
    > over the tuna and it is now ready to serve.

## Asian Tuna

![](media/image39.png){width="3.825in" height="2.3906255468066493in"}

The combination of shiitake mushrooms, seasonings and tuna brings a
great Asian food experience for a healthier eating lifestyle.

Cook and Preparation time: 15 minutes

**Ingredients:**

-   4 oz of tuna steaks

-   1 tablespoon of lemon juice

-   1 cup of scallion, minced

-   3 cloves of garlic, pressed

-   1 tablespoon of ginger, minced

-   2 cups of shiitake mushrooms, thickly sliced

-   1 tablespoon of chicken broth

-   1 cup of orange juice

-   2 tablespoon of soy sauce

-   2 tablespoon of cilantro, chopped

-   White pepper and salt to taste

**Instruction**

1.  Heat the broiler or iron pan for 10 minutes.

2.  Let the pressed garlic sit for 5 minutes to bring out its health
    > benefits.

3.  Rub the tuna with lemon juice and add seasonings. Set it aside

4.  Heat 1 tablespoon of chicken broth. Sauté ginger, scallion, garlic
    > and mushrooms in the broth for 2 minutes. Stir it constantly.

5.  Add the orange juice and let it sit for another 2 minutes. Add
    > cilantro and soy sauce

6.  With the use of a hot pad, hold the broiler pan out and then place
    > the tuna into it and return it back to the boiler. Always keep in
    > mind that it cooks very fast on both sides so it requires fast
    > movement.

7.  Put the tuna on the plate and pour the mushroom mixture to it then
    > serve.

## Spicy Salmon

![](media/image33.png){width="3.842326115485564in"
height="2.5677088801399823in"}

This dish is an Asian favourite with 5 Chinese spices. It is a very
surprising dish since it is flavorful yet light to eat. It's nutritious
and very easy to prepare.

Cook and preparation time: 30 minutes

**Ingredients:**

-   Salmon filter sliced into 4 pieces

-   6 shiitake mushrooms

-   1 onion, chopped

-   2 garlic, chopped

-   1 tablespoon of ginger, chopped

-   1 cup of vegetable stocks

-   1 tablespoon of 5 spice powder

-   ¾ cup of orange juice

-   2 tablespoon of molasses

-   2 tablespoon of arrow root

-   4 cups of Napa cabbage or shredded Chinese

-   6 scallion, chopped

-   Salt and pepper to taste

**Directions:**

1.  Soak the shiitake mushroom in a hot water. Slice it when it is soft
    > enough to cut.

2.  Sauté onions in a saucepan for about 5 minutes and stir it
    > frequently. Add garlic and ginger and continue to sauté. Add 5
    > spice powder, vegetable stock, molasses and orange juice.

3.  Then simmer it for about 15 minutes. Discard and strain all the
    > liquids. Place the liquids on the pan and thicken it with the use
    > of arrowroot.

4.  While waiting for the sauce to be cooked, simmer the mushrooms,
    > cabbage, scallion, salt and pepper in another pan for 5 minutes.

5.  Rub the salmon with pepper and salt.

6.  When the pan is hot, make use of a hot pad to pull the pan out from
    > the heat and then place the salmon back in the boiler.

7.  Drizzle the salmon fillet with the right amount of sauce and then
    > serve it together with your favorite veggies.

## Broiled Chicken with Swiss chard and Pureed Lentils

![](media/image5.png){width="2.5677088801399823in"
height="2.5677088801399823in"}

This recipe is very delicious and healthy. It's a great meal to be
served to a large group of people.

Cook and Preparation time: 35 minutes

**Ingredients:**

-   2 chicken breast, boneless

-   2 cups of lentils, drained

-   1 bunch of Swiss chard

-   1 onion, chopped

-   3 cloves of garlic, chopped

-   1 and ½ cups of mushrooms, sliced

-   3 tablespoon of chicken or vegetable broth

-   ½ tablespoon dried sage

-   ½ tablespoon dried thyme

-   ½ cup of walnuts

-   1 tablespoon of lemon juice

-   2 cloves of garlic, pressed

-   2 tablespoon of olive oil

-   Salt and pepper to taste

**Directions:**

1.  Heat the pan for at least 10 minutes until it gets very hot

2.  While waiting for the pan to heat, rinse the chicken and season it
    > with salt, juice and pepper

3.  Leave its skin on. Put the breast skin side up on the pan and put it
    > in the oven.

4.  While waiting for the chicken to broil, get a pot of water to cook
    > the chard in a boil.

5.  Chop the chard.

6.  Chop the mushrooms, garlic sage and thyme. Sauté for about 5 minutes
    > while stirring it. Add walnuts, lentils and broth and heat.

7.  Pour the mixture in a food processor or a blender while adding salt
    > and pepper to taste.

8.  When the water boils, add the chard and then boil it again for about
    > 3 minutes.

9.  Drain the chard and then toss it with 2 tablespoons of olive oil and
    > 1 tablespoon of lemon juice. Add salt and pepper to taste.

10. Place pressed garlic, lemon juice; salt, chopped rosemary and pepper
    > in a small pan and heat it on the stove for a minute.

11. Remove the chicken skin, slice it and serve with chard and pureed.
    > Lastly, put the rosemary lemon over the lentils and chicken.
    > Serve.

## Roasted Lamb Leg

![](media/image43.png){width="4.085937226596675in"
height="2.7239588801399823in"}

Cook and preparation time: cooking time: 15 minutes, preparation time:
45 minutes

Ingredients:

-   3 to 4 lbs leg of lamb, boneless

-   8 cloves garlic, chopped

-   3 tablespoon of rosemary, chopped

-   2 tablespoon salt

-   1 tablespoon ground pepper

-   3 carrots, peeled and cut

-   2 onions, sliced

-   1 ½ cup of celery, chopped

**Directions:**

1\. Cut the excess fat off the lamb. Lay the lamb in a baking dish. Chop
rosemary and press the garlic. Rub the lamb legs with the two
ingredients. Pour the lemon juice on the lamb and sprinkle it with salt.
Put in the refrigerator overnight.

2\. Heat the pan. Cut the vegetables and place them all over the baking
dish. Sprinkle with salt and pepper. Roast the lamb and the vegetables
