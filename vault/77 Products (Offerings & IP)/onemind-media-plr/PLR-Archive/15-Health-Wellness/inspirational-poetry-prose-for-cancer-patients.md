Preface:

The words are carefully chosen to meet both young and old audiences. The structure is simple
and well drafted making it easy to convey the message across. It is my personal conviction that
this book will provide hope and healing to all fighting all forms of cancers. I am, because we are.
I believe in the courage and strength of anyone battling this dis-ease.
If you can feel your heartbeat, you are a miracle to the world.
Inspirational Poetry & Prose for Cancer Patients is here as a touchstone for all who can read and
relate to the daily wounded emotions presented by the uneasiness of carcinomas, yet giving to
celebrating the heroes in these survivors.
                                                                        ~Wilson the Poet ‘Mandela’

Cure for Cancer

I won’t promise you perfect chemotherapy
without nausea
I owe you a home in my wrinkled palm

I won’t promise radiation
without dizziness
I believe in our mused memories fused with inspiration
to grow our happiness

I can’t afford for you a perfect therapy
without side effects
I can afford for you all my time
to listen to your cherish desires calmly

I am not a fan of promises
I promise to make one journey for you my precious child
‘I will be your cure without side effects’



There’s Something Special About Me

I am weary, but willing
My eyes are watery, but I am still winning
I am dull, but discovering
My purse is not full, but my body is recovering
I am empty, but deep within I feel encouraged
Empathy, medicine to outwit the carcinoma’s sage
Many days, I am not promised
but every day, without compromise
I survive
because I believe
I am here to make a difference
beyond cancer’s sentence



Beauty in Sickness

My ailing best friend,
whenever you cry
broken pieces mend
Little frustrations
churn into unending inspiration
When the sympathetic tongue asks you why,
tell ‘em your tears make you a special creation.



Mending Cancer
I remember the hour that voice broke that strange news
Emotions greeted reality in a way I never knew
My world came to a standstill
Impulses traveled down my spine
so calm and peaceful you appeared in strong will
I guess this insightful spirit made you fine
You didn't let down the water in your eyes
though the carcinoma silently ate your inside
Yet, your generous heart for the world you didn't hide
Your smiles reproduced in us pride
You always placed yourself last and us first
In this good fight
you rightly understood, it is not all about physical might
That's why whenever you prayed
our melodies charm stayed
Angels say only words few
Yet own a million ways to soak us in Heaven’s dew
The diagnose says, ‘cancer’
Together we weep in joy knowing that
what cancer cannot shatter are one's beautiful soul, heart and mind.



Out of Cancer

Out of nowhere
you knocked me down
but you couldn’t take away my crown
I am still here

Out of nowhere
you gave me several shots of pain
I am stronger than that pain
as I sense my spring rising again
I am still here

Out of nowhere
you stole the breath of fresh air
That’s not fair
Why are you now feasting on my cherished hair?
in this unmerited affair

Is this a test?
Because I know this journey is meant for the strong
to come out as the best
I have been through the chills and hills
I am still here
I don’t belong there
No matter the season
I still thrill



HOPE on my Sickbed

You touch my soul
with so much inspiration
Even in so much pain
your heart overflows
like there is no pain to show
Your glow
trades light
for night
I have learned a lifelong lesson;
even in our weakest vulnerability
we have so much to share
The little hope, cares
to help others cope
I am not giving up on my sickbed
If I am making it here
you too can make it in any situation out there!



You Don't Deserve To Be There

Your absence
makes my soul hungrier
Not for food, nor oxygen
but for your charming face
that brings along comfort
In its endearing presence
my heart was pricked sore
when the waves of your failing health news
hit me hard in my heart's core,
I have reserved a special room
In my heart for you
get up, from your sick bed
and take your right place in me.
Get well soon, my dear!
A Letter from a Stranger

Whenever you feel like giving up
close your eyes and imagine my blind friend
playing my birthday piano

Whenever you want to shut all windows
close your eyes and imagine my deaf friend
enjoying my birthday music

Whenever you are in agony
close your eyes and imagine my lame friend
dancing to the rhythm of life

Whenever sorrow draws near
close your eyes and imagine my leper friend
clapping and embracing life to the fullest

I will be sixteen years old this weekend
I am inviting you to my birthday party
my special friend

On my birthday
I want to introduce you to the world
as the most important person

Anyone can get cancer
Not anyone can hold their pain
You give me more than a reason
to live, laugh and love!



One More Year Like Forever

Doctors said, ‘I have one more year’
to live
I say, ‘I have one more heart to touch’
Instead of feeling bad
I smile to make the world glad
every day I survive

I have made it for a decade now
If you ask me how?
All I feel and know:
something inside me
is stronger than cancer!



Solemn Light

Dear Cancer
I say a humble prayer today:
may all that live in your home
thrive in despair’s absence,
survive in your presence
and find daily courage within to overcome
and gain wisdom to know when to thrive, survive and live.



Touching Tribute

You posted no sign to say goodbye
This is not how we expected our relationship to end
Our broken pieces who will mend?
You left with all that inner beauty and trust
into the dust
Your strength admired
even to its last beat
Although the carcinomas were stubborn,
you still won every feat
You are our hero celebrated here
Gone
but still our favourite and number one
Tell Heaven
we need a loan of you
Just a day
for your birthday



Let It Be Full

let it be beautiful

When you frown
let it be peaceful,

Whenever you feel
no longer strong
pour encouragement into your cup
Cancer’s stings are real
but still look up, up
because that is where you belong!

Don’t be afraid of cancer
Cancer is afraid of you
when you are not afraid

Don’t wish pain away
To walk in entangled shoes
and still feel light

Beautiful Blind Eyes

The outside world before my eyes hangs
with daily clouds of illusion
Colours and seasons are one
My sight is sleeping
under a thick blanket
How I wish one of these days
the world won’t treat me as an option
Behind these silent eyes
connection to a deeper meaning
that sees the beyond
Blind eyes see all
and, I won’t stumble or fall
My ‘sixth sense’ will rescue me
No need to call

The worst form of blindness
is not complete darkness
but to have eyes
and not have light in your heart
for others

The beautiful things in the world are abstract
That is why
we close our eyes when we pray
so that our inner strength
will grow and not fade away
The beautiful things in the world can’t be seen
That is why
we close our eyes
when they are raining
to wash away our hurts

Blindness is not a disease
Prejudice is the silent sting
I pray you come to understand
the pictures I see
with my inner gift of sight
My heart, my light
and not concentrate on my plight

You may not understand when I say
my blind eyes have sleepless nights
The window of my heart is my sight
A single heartache could shut that light

I have seen a blind friend cry
a beautiful cry
It was a natural outburst
from the rivers of her heart
flowing gently down the streets of her eyes
It made me weak
I cried too
She told me,
‘I have seen the reflection of a beautiful blue sky
In your eyes for the first time’
My body feels forever free from cancer
I am a miracle



You, You, You

You don’t need someone
to hold you
You need you
to hold yourself
when there is no someone

When we pray
we close our eyes
When we cry
we close our eyes
When our individual heart’s desires stay
within our true feelings
wellness overcomes illness

There is so much
to discover
about ourselves
beyond the daily struggle and fever

When you draw a little closer
to yourself
You’ll find all you were searching for
were You, You, You!



Naked, Ugly, Empty, Unwise, Nothing!

When I am naked
I feel it
not when I don’t have clothes on
but the moments I looked down on others

When I feel ugly
I know it
not when I don’t receive compliments
to complete my being
but when I depend on compliments
completely for my being
When I am empty
I feel it
not when I am broke
but when my words
make hearts broken

When I am unwise
I know it
not when I failed an exam
but when I failed to examine
where I came from

When I am rich
I feel it
not because I have this and that
but because I have you, you and you
who will still stand by my side
if someday I wake up
and I am left with nothing!

Biography of Author:

Wilson Ayinbangya Amooro, known internationally as Wilson the Poet ‘Mandela’ , has won
recognition for creating poetry that speaks deeply of the human experience. Drawing from his
own experiences of life, love, faith, and loss, his work has garnered him praise from awarding
bodies and the appreciation of his fans. He began writing in 2003 at the age of 14, when his first
piece entitled "Mother's Love" won him the second runner up prize in a national competition
organized by Nestle Ghana Limited. He continued to write poetry, and began gaining more
exposure in 2013 when his work received acknowledgment by the United Nations via the
International Organization for Migration (IOM). That year he was featured on Top Radio 103.1
in Accra, Ghana, and in the annual edition of Gong Gong Magazine. His poetry has been
featured in the poetry anthology "Breaking Silence: Poetic Lifeline From Slavery to Love." His
work can also be found in the Amazon-published anthology "Nelson Mandela Tributes," and he
regularly video blogs performances of his works. Wilson is an active member of Poetry
Foundation Ghana, and the Ghana-based People of Equal Thoughts and Spirit (P.O.E.T.S).
He is also a CosmiKids Ghana representative and Hunter School friend in New Hampshire USA.
As a published poet and author, Wilson is launching a permaculture poetry contest with middle
and upper school students. Hunter's School (P4) Pen Pal Project is a global youth collaborative
celebrating children and nature. Bringing together schools and organizations from across the
globe for sharing and learning. He and the Development Director of the Hunter School will be
publishing a book of children’s poetry and photos of the experience.

Aside from writing poetry, Wilson is also passionate about astronomy. He founded Young
Astronomers Ghana in 2010, which is still active today. As a profession, he is a practicing nurse.
He also has a "Naming X" merit from organization Father’s Film U.K.

Wilson's philosophy of life and work can be summed up in a simple quote:
‘’The more I love, the more I grow; the more I grow, the more I love’’---Wilson ‘thePoet’
Mandela.
Wilson's artful way of crafting words from the raw materials of personal experience and emotion
continues to win him fans. His poetry is a testament to his life, and also serves as a touchstone
for all those who read and relate to his words.

A little Child’s letter to God on Christmas Day

Dear God
Many, many Christmases have passed me by,
I sent you several letters,
No sign to show you received any of them.
Please tell me,
If you are taking time to answer me in a special way,
I guess that will be enough to console me with a hug,
and make me feel just alright as your little child.

I starved myself of the soft and yummy ice cream for weeks,
saved my last pennies for my envelope and writing pad.
And the other kids made me into a teasing stock.

Oh, Dear God
Hear me out
Just this one moment
Search through my heart
Please, don’t let this last tear drop,
Walk through my cheeks in vain.
Is it because you know what I am thinking?
I am a sinner
I broke rules too
And I didn’t do my homework the last time

When I want to compare
All the new things the other kids have for Christmas,
It will only increase my pain.
All I have is a piece of my heart I share in this letter.
I have no more tears
I wait for the day when I will also smile
And when they see me from miles afar
The teasing label will become a pleasing star

Dear Lord, I don’t want much this Christmas.
Bless my world to accept me for who I am.

Let Me Lay My Gentle Hands On You

I know just how it feels
When you get that heartache,
To face reality;
Mood so blue and bitter.
Pain won’t last forever.
It will soon be better.

When it all gets too tough
To bid farewell rough thoughts,
Just relax and breathe slooooow.
Close your eyes, feel me close.
I’ll wrap my Earth’s wings ‘bout you,
To keep you always warm.

I want to be the one
You accept in your heart,
Even though you feel pain!

Tattoo now on my heart,
Deep passion not to fade.
I know your emotions
As fervent as they are,
Would surely break me down
If I basked in your shade.

That’s not my illusion.
I am now a part of
Your perfect impression!

Place your fingers near mine.
Fill those empty spaces.
Merge unbreakable bond.
Let me now lay on you,
My willing, gentle hands,
Even for a second!



Something from Football Game versus Life Game Book

Football with God

A little fellow put words together and formed one of the most endearing letters my eyes have
witnessed on earth. With a breath of fresh air, I sighed in delight, soaked in awe for this tender
heart’s tempting invitation to play football with God.

On a seemingly promising day, passion drove ego out of the lonely islets and pushed the
footprints into adventure at a not-so-popular- park.
The gentle efforts exerted on the ball caressed the floor, as eyes gazed in an unblinking direction
like a stubborn stalker on a rich dad’s belle.
Then, that unprepared moment came across a strange appearance of feet. The little boy lifted his
head up, to catch a glimpse of the mighty shadow. He was neither expecting, but was willing to
be filled in by the warm feeling of companionship.

An elderly woman stretched a wrinkled palm for a handshake. ‘This is the best gift I ever
received from a stranger,’ soliloquized into the little figure of the big-heart- boy.
Before, emotions could translate into words, another Hollywood worth capturing ambience
evoked.
‘My little one, please could you teach me how to play football? I have been sitting here and
everyone that passed my way never said hello to me,’ said the old woman. She wore on her face
the most perfect and priceless make-up in the world, her smile.

Throwing, kicking and catching the ball, the process went on and on, as they played joyfully
without any bother of rules of the football game. What mattered to them was finding someone to
bring out the best in them. They surely moved along, as age weighed on the day and dusk traded
place for light. The hugged each other tighter. And they parted ways in opposite directions. As if
they planned it, in between some footsteps away, they turned back to look at each other for the
last, and when their eyes met they even beamed brighter.

When the little boy got home, his face was greeted with a reflection of the pure joy he offered to
his mother.
Mom asked, ‘What is the secret to your unending happiness?’
The reply blew mom away…
‘I played football with God’
Before, his mom could manufacture words for a reply, he added, “You know what? God finally
received my letter and made special time for me, at my special park. She made me the most
special person on earth today.’

Scenes switched to the home of the old woman. The storyline is an irresistible recipe of soft
slices of inspiration as gourmet to nourish the inner being.
Her son was clouded by a piece of heaven from her face and asked, “Mom, you are not only
cheerful looking, you look years younger today. What’s the secret? ‘
She replied, “I played football in the park with God.”

Beyond the Drought

Tears of Hope
Slowly ooze from those sunken eyes
To nurse our arid soils
BEYOND THE DROUGHT
My feeble veins
Calmly await love’s transfusion
From its own home
BEYOND THE DROUGHT

Be witnesses to the raiding torment of history
But our patience will silence the heat
BEYOND THE DROUGHT

That Africa will someday soon flourish
BEYOND THE DROUGHT
