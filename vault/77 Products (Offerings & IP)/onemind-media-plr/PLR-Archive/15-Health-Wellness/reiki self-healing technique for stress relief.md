**Reiki self-healing steps for stress relief**

You can give Reiki to yourself any time of the day. Some people like to
start with it first thing in the morning to prepare for the day ahead.
Others find it helpful to end the day with Reiki, helping the body to
relax and unwind. Ideally, it is best to set aside a full hour for a
Reiki self-treatment, but this is not always possible, so setting aside
15 - 20 minutes on a regular basis will produce good results and real
benefits in your daily life.

Tips to use when conducting this healing is to imagine that golden,
healing energy is flowing from your hands into your body, circulating
along the subtle energy pathways, nourishing every cell and organ.
Besides that, leave your hands at the particular area as long as it is
comfortable. It is normal to notice that the area of that body part
becomes warms as the energy circulates.

**Steps for stress relief with reiki is as follow:**

1.  Either sit in a comfortable upright position or lie down where you
    won\'t be disturbed. Set your alarm clock if you have a time limit,
    and switch off phones.

2.  Place both hands over your eyes and center yourself by breathing
    gently into the abdomen. Take in a deep breath, hold for a few
    moments and exhale. Repeat this a few times. This helps to restore
    strained eyes and clear tension headaches. Move your hands to the
    tempers to clear an overactive or tired mind.

3.  Next, move your hands round to the back of your head and neck area,
    releasing its tension and refreshing the brain.

4.  Then, put your hands on either side of your neck. This helps the
    thyroid gland and the area associated with communication.

5.  Next, place your hands above your chest to help with lymph drainage
    and the clearing of toxins from the body.

6.  Then, move your hands down to the sternum, with fingers meeting at
    the heart chakra. This helps to restore emotional equilibrium.

7.  Finish with your hands below the navel to center yourself.

8.  When you have completed your reiki self healing session, open your
    eyes slowly. Sit quietly for few moments and feel gratitude for all
    that supports you in your life.
