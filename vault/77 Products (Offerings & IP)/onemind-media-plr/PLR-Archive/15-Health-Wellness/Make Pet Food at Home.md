Although, if fed with natural food, a noticeable change

When you shift your cat’s diet from commercial one to
the homemade diet, you will be amazed to see the
differences within a few weeks only. The reason is
simple that a cat’s body processes are designed for
digesting nutrients from fresh and natural foods.
Homemade food is better than the commercially cooked
and   frozen mass   of  diseases  and  dead   animals,
additives, preservatives and other acidic materials.
Cats need more proteins than dogs. Modern cat foods
which are high in carbohydrates may cause your cat to
suffer from digestive problems. So, feeding them
healthy homemade food is necessary. However, before
heading to the kitchen for preparing a meal for your
cat, it is important to have knowledge of some of the
foods that need to be avoided. Some of these include
mushrooms, alcoholic beverages, onions, garlic, baby
food, fat trimmings, caffeine, persimmons, raisins,
grapes, starchy food, macadamia nuts, excessive liver,
raw fish, yeast dough and raw eggs. Besides this, you

and not the hot food directly from the stove or for
that sake, not even cold food from the refrigerator.
Remove bones from poultry, fish and other meats because
they may cause obstruction in the digestive system.
When you prepare homemade food for your cat, don’t add
salt to it as veggies and meats usually contain enough
amount of it meant for a pet. Table scraps shouldn’t be
incorporated for more than 10% in its diet. This way,
you can take care of your cat’s health and keep it away
from health problems.

Possibly, you must have heard regarding the pet food

Mainly, preparation of pet food is just about mixing
the right type of ingredients necessary for a pet’s
diet. Generally, pets require a balanced amount of
vegetables, meat and starches. These are some of the
ways to make homemade dry food for your pet. Mix
vegetables, brown rice, ground meat and water for
cooking them together. Water should be put depending
upon the type of food you want it to be, whether dry or
wet. If you want it dry, then putting only some amount
of water would work well for it. In case, the amount of
food that you cooked for your dog exceeds its usage
limit then keep in the refrigerator but that too for
not more than three days. For dogs, rice is a very rich
source of carbohydrates. Putting teas, coffee and
chocolate in your dog’s food is a strict no- no as it
may cause seizures. You can include flour in the home
cooked food as one of the ingredients. Garlic is a good
source to keep a dog away from infection and stay away
from fleas and worms but only, if taken in normal
quantity. Usage of items like flaxseed oil and cod
liver in the homemade food provides omega 3 which is
extremely healthy for your pet. Always remember to
properly cook the meat. Don’t use onions, grapes,
nutmeg, raisins and macadamia nuts in your recipe.
Making homemade dry food for your pet is a very simple
task, still if you feel the need to clarify certain
things; you can always consult a veterinarian. This way
you will feed the best food to your pet and render a
long healthy life to your four legged furry companion.
