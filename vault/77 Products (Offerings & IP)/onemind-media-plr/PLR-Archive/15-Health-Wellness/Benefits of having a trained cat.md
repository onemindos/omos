**[Benefits of having a trained cat]{.ul}**

Cats claw, scratch, bite and chew for many reasons. Kittens will chew on
things for the same reasons that puppies do. They are either curious or
undergoing teething process. If they are doing these things to food
items or their cat toys, that is great, but many times they will find
something else. Cats that chew on the new carpet or the electrical cords
are not only destroying your possessions, they are also risking their
own lives. Training your cat can ensure that you have a gentle, loving
pet that does not eat your furniture. Training your cat does not only
protect it from potential threats, but keeps your belongings safe from
destruction as well.

A toilet-trained cat will prevent incidents where they use the entire
house as their personal litter box. In addition, it eliminates all
litter box odours and improve the health and hygiene of everyone living
in your household.

A well-trained cat will charm and amuse your guests when they come to
your home. He will make your days seem warmer and brighter. Because he
knows what is expected of him, he will not have as much stress, leading
to a calmer more easily controlled cat. Calmer cats are more
affectionate because they are not nervous or high-strung. Training your
cat allows him to focus on good behaviours and the things that make a
cat's life a good one.

Basically, it makes your life much easier as it is a great way to:

i.  Build a strong rapport between you and your cat

ii. Underline your authority and curb dominant cat behaviours

iii. Keep your cat's mind active and stimulated

iv. Teach good social skills in your cat.
