6 Steps for developing creative visualisation

Creative visualisation is thinking beyond your normal comprehension. It
allows you to solve your problems quickly, brings you the solution you
need, expands your horizon and create great emotional
experience. Besides, it brings excitement in your life as you have
created the interest and passion  in your thinking and that will
initiate and align your physical force to action out  your creative
visualization and bring forth your visualization into reality.

These are the following tips to developing mind power to gain creative
visualisation:

1\. Get detached

Listen to soft meditative music to drop your brain waves to alpha
levels. At alpha level, you will be feeling calm and focus, removed of
any feelings of doubt, fear and anxiety. Optimal mental performance can
then be obtained.

2\. Work with colours in your life

Colours enhance your memory by a sizeable amount and helps you to
recollect memories to understand the situation more accurately and
quickly.

3\. Do things differently, periodically

This will get you out of your comfort zone; which in turns stretches
your emotional experience along with it. This is of great help in
developing your mind power and you will be able to keep stress out in
this positive thinking situation.

4\. Keep it to yourself

Letting the methods of how you are going to do it weakens your
motivation and energy to follow through. It also leaves you open to
negative questions and comments. Get to your destination first then only
tell others about it.

5\. Create an image of your desire and add emotions into it

Create the image and add in details, as specific as possible.This helps
in focusing on a single goal. Emotions added and relieving them
repeatedly will give you positive feeling about what you are going to
achieve; thus help to direct you towards your goal

6\. Movement

Moving your body such as exercising improves flexibility and improve the
body and mind response to change. It is especially important because
physical power is needed to create mind images/creative visualisation
into reality.
