# how-to-be-happy-get-out-of-depression-now

               Introduction

Do you feel overwhelmed? Are you just
trying to cope with the basic challenges
of life? Do you suffer from massive
amounts of anxiety and stress? ...
You don’t want to admit it, but you are
basically feeling like SHIT right now? ...


I am pretty sure you’re like “Who is he to
judge?! He doesn’t know anything about
me or my problems!“

It’s the exact same thing I thought to
myself when I discovered some very
important lessons that literally changed
my life!

Of course, this sounds pretty awesome,
but I‘m not claiming to have discovered
                     2
some alien magic to get rid of everything
unpleasant. Neither am I promoting
some guide on how to become
enlightened or claiming to be
enlightened myself!



The whole purpose of this e-book is to
share the experiences I had that finally
got me out of some of the lowest points in
my life!




                      3
1. You cannot ACHIEVE happiness!


This statement might seem a little
contradicting to you because this e-book
actually is on how to be happy, but let me
explain what I mean.



You can reach a different state of
happiness, which is generated within
yourself and is not attached to anything
except you yourself.

The problem is that everyone thinks
‘‘Once I reach XYZ I will be happy‘‘ –
That’s just wrong!




                       4
Where does this mindset come from?
Why does everyone think their level of
happiness is dependent on certain
circumstances?

Right now imagine five things that you
think would instantly make you happy.



The harsh truth is that even though you
might reach all your goals in life and
these specific things you hold in mind,
you’re not going to be happy because of
it. Think about certain events or
achievements in the past you thought
“That’s everything I need“ and seriously
ask you if it turned out to be true. I doubt
it!

                      5
I had this several times thinking back,
especially wanting to lose weight. As soon
as I did it I didn’t feel that special to be
honest. It changed to be my body again
instead of being my dream body. I bought
my dream guitar and after five minutes I
felt the same as before. After finally
owning the guitar, it changed from being
the dream guitar to being my guitar.

This cycle is endless and you will find
yourself going through this over and over
again.




                       6
Saying things like “XYZ is going to make
me happy“ is basically like saying ‘‘I am
not Happy right now, please fix me!‘‘.

By trying to reach or achieve happiness
you are reinforcing the negative believe
that you are not happy right now.



I even did the same thing on a different
level! I did all the stuff that was supposed
to make me happy like meditation,
reading, working out, playing guitar and
socializing, but couldn’t find it simply
because I was doing this to be happy and
neglected the fact that I truly wanted to
do these things because I love them.


                      7
The problem is that we try to seek so
many ways to be happy, but ultimately
deny the true problem by doing so.

I also caught myself believing that I
needed a big change to mark the
transition from unhappy to happy. So I
was sitting there and waiting for change.

The problem is – if you are waiting for
change, nothing is going to happen and
furthermore every experience that would
mark a transition turned out to be ‘‘not
good enough‘‘.



We now learned why this mindset of
achieving happiness doesn’t work.


                     8
Now let’s work on the more reasonable
mindset you should embrace instead!



2. The right mindset


As I mentioned earlier the right mindset
is not about finding ways to be happy, it’s
about stopping to not be happy.



‘‘How is this possible?‘‘ you may ask
yourself. It’s actually easier than you
might think it is. The mindset that
worked the best for me is “My life is
already amazing and I’m gonna let go of
any kind of negativity surrounding it“.

                       9
This way you will stop denying the true
source of your unhappiness and start
facing the truth!


3. Feeling temporarily happy versus true
happiness



You may experience some sort of
happiness once in a while. Maybe you
bought something you wanted for a long
time or just enjoy watching your favorite
TV Show. This is not the type of
happiness I am talking about. It’s the
long term happiness I want you to
experience!

                    10
Even though it might not be as intense as
those little peaks you can encounter, it is
possible to get rid of all the pain you
experience in your everyday life.

The difference between these two forms
of being happy is extremely important.

One is based on external forces and the
other one on the internal (buying a new
phone versus facing the feelings inside
your body).

If you seek those glimpses externally you
won’t reach a state of true happiness.
Focusing on the outside makes you prone
to develop negative thinking yourself
because you let in any kind of negativity.


                      11
The reason most of your attempts will fail
is that you try to achieve happiness by
gathering those experiences instead of
letting go of negative thoughts and
feelings, because

emotions are addictive – take a minute
and think about it!



Negative thinking has gone so far as to
become a habit for the majority of us and
it’s reinforcing itself over and over again.

The ultimate goal is to let go of negativity
and from there to start embracing the
things you love and truly want to do.

As I said this won’t work if you constantly
look outside yourself. You have to
                      12
embrace all the feelings and emotions
you ignored or suppressed for so long.
They will come up over and over again
and sooner or later you will find yourself
in a downward spiral.

No matter how much money you make,
what car you drive or how successful you
are - you won‘t find it until you focus on
what‘s happening inside of you!


4. How to let go of negative thinking



The main concept I want to share with
you is what I practice on a daily basis –
How to let go of negative thinking!


                     13
What I realized pretty early on is that
people get addicted to negativity! It’s like
a habit which forces you back into it
every single time. The solution is
centered around replacing this habit with
another habit. Instead of practicing
negativity further, you should start
practicing letting go of negativity from
now on!



I am going to show you a step by step
guide on how to deal with negative
emotions the right way instead of
numbing or repressing them.




                     14
This is something most spiritual teachers
talk a lot about by teaching the piece of
mind and feeling the inner body. I am not
claiming to have invented what I am
going to show you. It’s just what worked
for me.

Maybe it doesn’t work for you, but you
have some kind of starting point to go
from there.



Step 1

Sit down in a chair or lay down on your
bed. You may want to close your eyes.




                     15
Step 2

Take a few deep breaths by breathing into
your pelvic floor down your spine. Your
stomach should be fully filled with air
until you exhale. Do this at least 3-5
times. It’s a lot easier to focus on your
inner body this way and you should
notice your mind calming down with
fewer thoughts coming up. You might
also close your eyes.



Step 3

Feel into your body and pay attention to
every emotion or feeling that might come
up. Do not focus on the thoughts
                        16
connected to the feelings and instead, try
to feel them without judgment.



Step 4

Take a deep breath and visualize the air
going into the emotion or feeling (most of
the time you can feel where these
emotions are located) and visualize it
coming out with the exhale. Do this at
least 5 times.



It is also important to consider that
thinking about past events can trigger
certain feelings, intense emotions and
pain you suppressed way too long. You


                     17
can now focus on letting those feelings go
instead of repressing them further.

Also, consider that most feelings need
multiple sessions to fully vanish.



I recommend doing 3-5 sessions per
emotion caused by an event to take full
advantage of this exercise.

If these ideas resonated with you, two
amazing books you can dive into are
Eckhart Tolle’s ‘The Power of Now: A
Guide to Spiritual Enlightenment ‘ and
David R. Hawkin’s ‘Letting Go: The
Pathway of Surrender‘ as they both go
extremely in depth on what we talked
about here.
                     18
Meditating on a regular basis also really
helps, because you practice observing
your mind as well as your emotions. Not
to mention the general benefits of
meditating regularly. This could simply
be listening to a guided meditation or just
closing your eyes and feeling inside your
body for 20 minutes.




                     19
5. What the process of getting out of
Depression could practically look like


First of all, you start to realize that you
need to adopt a different mindset and
that negativity has become an
unconscious habit in your life.

From there you start to process emotions
in the form of letting go instead of
suppressing them further and get back to
feeling neutral after practicing to process
these painful emotions the healthy way.

You can now focus your energy on all the
things you actually want to do, not to
avoid suppressed feelings, but to
embrace life in general because negative


                      20
thoughts consume a lot more energy than
you might think!

Of course, this is oversimplified, but it
represents a basic concept that may or
may not apply to your current life
situation.
