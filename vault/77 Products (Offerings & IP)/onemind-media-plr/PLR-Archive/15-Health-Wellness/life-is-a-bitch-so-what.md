Life is a Bitch… So What?

Life is a bitch indeed. Everything around you sucks. Everyone is stupid and it doesn’t seem like they’re
going to quit getting on your nerves anytime soon either. When it rains it pours. You lost your keys;
left your mp3 player in the apartment; you have a flat tire; your parents call you at the wrong time; your
girlfriend hates your friends and you hate her for hating them. You’ve got one day to kick back and
relax and your husband decides to invite some buddies over. You want a better job you hate your boss
with a vengeance and he knows it but he has no intention of firing you and paying compensation. Your
cat ate your bird and vomited it out along with a bloody fur ball…yada, yada, yada...
Whatcha gonna do? Right?
Funny you should ask…
I have a few ideas.

First of all, life is short and it will be over pretty soon; so don’t think that you’ll suffer forever. That
alone should lift some weight off of your shoulders right off the bat. Yeah, I hate it too when someone
tells me that my problems are some other man’s dream. But hell, that’s kind of true isn’t it? Be honest
now.
Your Problems are Somebody Else’s Wildest Dreams

I’ve got my health. Well at least I’m doing okay. That’s something. I get to live in a country where I’m
free to speak my mind and wouldn’t go to jail for only thinking or questioning something. I feel safe in
general. I have a roof over my head. I’ve got my own bed. I’m not starving. I have water to drink; I
have water to wash and clean up. I get to watch TV whenever I want and have tens of channels to
choose from which automatically means that I have electricity. I go to toilet whenever I want without
having to leave my house. I flush my shit and it’s gone forever.

See my point? There are many people around the world who would love to have all that mentioned
above present in their lives. Some probably don’t even know there is a better life out there. Well…then
again, when one isn’t aware of the things he/she doesn’t have and doesn’t really need, he/she is
probably is happier; although he/she might not really know it. That’s a whole other issue I may get into
in the future.

To cut the long story short, don’t just take what you’ve already got for granted and wait for the day you
have lost it to start appreciating it.
Moderation

Consume everything in moderation. Don’t get crazy blind with whatever it is that you enjoy; be it ice
cream; video games; pizza; drinks; mini golf.

Have your limits man; because it’s way too easy to get carried away with pleasure.

The truth is that there’s no end to it. The more you get the more you’ll want. If you don’t feel happy for
some reason and you think that buying those pair of shoes or that irresistible watch will help you,
you’re absolutely right! YES THEY WILL!; for only an hour or two; definitely. Then what? You will
be feeling depressed again. If you don’t have a constructive outlet; some sort of healthy hobby or an
occupation and you’re looking for the release in consuming, let me tell you, you’re in trouble. Quit it
while you can. Instead, look at yourself. Think about something that could give you pleasure without
having to consume anything; except your time maybe but that’s okay. Sing, draw, play guitar, skip
rope; you’ve got the idea.
Take Charge Dammit!

Don’t blame anyone and point fingers; even when you’re right. It’s just a waste of time. Take
responsibility. I know, probably your parents are the ones who are really responsible (!) for whatever it
is that sucks in your life but to know that or to let THEM know that isn’t going to help anyone. It’s just
going to delay your improvement. Let it go. Say “For whatever reason I am where I am and I am who I
am. Where do I want to go from here?” that is the real question that will get you somewhere not “who
did it? Why did they do it?” It is often very easy to blame everyone and use what they did or did not do
as an excuse for our immobility. Imagine you’re going camping in your car and suddenly, in the middle
of nowhere, you run out of gas because your husband happened to forget to fill the tank. You can
complain all you want but unless you find a way to get your damned car to the nearest station and get
your tank filled you’re in trouble. You can whine about what an idiot your husband is but all that could
possibly achieve in the end is give you an ulcer; and that’s a maybe. Plus, the more you take charge the
more you get used to it and that brings out the strength in you. Then do it, be your own boss.
Be Here, in the “NOW”

This could be the most important factor for a better life. “Being in the now; being present”: I’ve been
hearing about it since I was a teenager. But it was only a few years ago that I realize what that really
meant.

We are all living NOW; literally. There is no past or future; it’s all in one’s mind and mind LOVES to
travel. It loves being anywhere but here. If we followed where mind took us all the time, we’d never be
here, in the real moment.

What’s wrong with that? Let’s see...

First...
Here’s an extremely simple explanation for you:

Mind thinks of something – body reacts accordingly.

Mind = Cause
Body = Effect

Be aware of that; at all times! This fact is the key to real well being.

When you’re taking care of practical matters like

“This magazine costs $ 7, 50. I gave the salesman $10, 00. He gave me $1, 00 back. $1, 50 is missing”

Follow your mind as much as you want; no problem there. But when the issue is an emotional one it
gets tricky. When something happens now, you see it; analyze it; process it and come up with a
solution or a reaction for it; great. But that’s not where your mind wants to reside, remember?

Instead your mind wants to go back and dwell on how badly your sister had treated you at her son’s
birthday party or how your teacher had yelled at you in front of everyone when you were 9 years old,
etc.

Worse yet it fast-forwards to the future and tells how you’re never going to find a partner; how you’re
not good enough for the position you’re applying for, etc.

From emotional stand point, when your mind is stuck in the past or future, there are only two effects:
regret and worry.

Because since they are not present, there is really nothing you CAN do. Sure you can prepare for the
future using your past experiences as a reference, practically speaking of course or apologize for
something you did wrong in the past. But you are still limited with the things you can do NOW. If
you’ve done what you were supposed to do now and yet you’re still dwelling in the past or future
you’ll never have peace; because your hands are tied. There is no time travelling machine to take you
back or to the future to fix things.
It’s exactly like watching a movie; a scary one most of the time, for some reason I don’t know. You are
well aware that you cannot interact with a movie; they are just images being played back to back; YET,
you cannot help feeling scared, happy, and sad when you watch one. You can walk out the theater or
stop your DVD player but your mind is with you everywhere you go. You can’t just shut it down; not
even while you’re at sleep. What you can do however is be aware of its tendencies and tricks and stay
focused in the present moment; not just follow it wherever it wants to go.

Use your mind in a practical manner. Then again, now that you know it wants to ‘travel’, you could
take advantage of that information; lead it to fun places. Remember and focus on good memories from
your past or picture a future you’d like to be in. However, don’t get lost, because in the end nothing
beats being present in the now.
EFT (Emotional Freedom Technique)

This is a technique whereby you tap on certain points on your body to release stress, fear, sadness etc.

There are so many “feel good” methods out there. Everybody claims that their way is the best, yada,
yada...
I’m not selling any EFT products here so take my word for it: IT WORKS!

The problem with EFT is that it looks ridiculous. When you see someone tapping on their forehead
with their index and middle finger you can’t help but smile to say the least; not that you’d see many
people practicing it out there in public. So it’s easy to dismiss it.

When I mention it to others usually the response I get is

“Of course, in the end it’s all in the mind, I already know that...”

Like this is something they have to master; like they have to read tons of pages; analyze themselves and
figure out how it really works and only then they would see the results. And they don’t have the time
for that.

But in reality EFT is pretty simple. It works immediately. You don’t even need to know how it works
exactly. You don’t even have to be so precise on the points that you tap. It doesn’t take all that long
either; just minutes; and it’s FREE!

In an earlier chapter I had mention that

MIND is the CAUSE
BODY is the EFFECT

Your mind has a thought, image, smell, etc. It sends that info to the body and body reacts to it. That
reaction emphasizes how you feel about that cause. You see a huge dog; your brain sends a
neurological signal to the body making it intense. It’s a way of letting you be aware of the dog’s
presence; you may need to run; you may need to walk faster or slower, etc. So it’s not necessarily a bad
thing.

But there are times these signals are absolutely unnecessary like when you’re going to have a job
interview and you’re worried sick about it. It’s actually working against you. You think about how
you’re going to walk into that office, how you’re going to stutter, how you’re going to look nervous
and all that. But this ‘movie’ in your head is going to send signals to your body and make it
unnecessarily intense. What to do?

Here’s when EFT becomes very handy; because it’s not easy to clear those images from your mind. As
I mentioned earlier if you can stay in the NOW, you could avoid picturing irrelevant things but
sometimes trying to forget or avoid things could backfire and make a person think about them even
more.

Imagine your brain as a phone operator.
Imagine your body is a building with many offices; offices being body parts.

When the operator receives an info that it thinks is important for the offices to know, it sends messages
through some wires. A lot of times, instead of deleting an old message, the operator keeps it in a folder
and sends that old and useless info to the offices over and over again.

There are two wires connected to every office: Blue and red. Blue is the info itself and the red is the
one that activates a loud and noisy alarm in every office. Now, if only there was way to prevent that
alarm from going off every time, so that the office personnel could process the info in peace. How
could it be done? By cutting off that damn red wire, right? That is exactly how EFT works. It doesn’t
try to get rid off the information; which is impossible. You can’t just erase your past or future images,
not to mention the ongoing experiences of your everyday life. All you need to do is cancel the extreme
emotional stress they’re causing you; that’s all.

When you see or think of something your body reacts to it. If you tap at the same time, you cancel your
body’s physical reaction to it. As a result you don’t feel overwhelmed by it any more; you process it
more rationally.

For example you had an argument with a colleague and you just can’t stop thinking about it. The more
you think about it the more intense your body gets; your stomach contracts; your head aches. While
staying in that argument moment in your mind, you keep tapping on EFT points on your body. There
are certain EFT sets and after completing only one set, which would take about 1 – 2 minutes, you’ll
already start feeling better. But if you keep doing it, and it won’t take very long unless you’re dealing
with a much more serious and disturbing issue believe me, soon all you’re going to be left with is going
to be the image of you and your colleague arguing; but you will feel numb to it. It won’t disturb you
one bit. It will be like watching a movie without sound and music in a way. Intensity will be gone.
You’ll be easily able to focus on what could be done about it, rather than beat yourself and hate him/her
and increase the negativity of the situation.

There is no one I know that gave EFT a try and regretted it. As I said before it’s simple and it works
immediately; whatever your problem is, however intense it is.

There are two types of EFT as far as I know. The ordinary way which includes points on fingernails
(gamut points); then there is Faster EFT which skips those fingernail points.

I’m not the expert on it but there is a lot of information about EFT on social media. Go check it out. If
you would want me to be more specific about it, personally I find the videos of Robert E. Smith very
useful. But do your own search and you be the judge.
Forgive For Crying Out Loud!

This is a strange one. I don’t exactly remember where I heard about this for the first time but as soon as
I started practicing I felt pretty good about it.

Here’s how it goes:

You forgive; ALL THE TIME!

This way you don’t let negative energy and the toxins it creates dwell in your body. You can try it
anywhere, anytime, in any situation; it doesn’t matter.

You’re walking on the street some idiot bumps into you; maybe on purpose or maybe he/she was just
not looking where she was going. You get angry, understandably by the way, you were in your
thoughts, minding your own business, then boom. It’s not pleasant at all. A sudden interruption like
that makes anybody intense for a few seconds. But some of us react too strongly and still hold on to
that anger way after the incident occurred. Sometimes that person apologizes; we’re still at his/her
throat. During the day we talk about it to our friends “Some idiot bumped into me on the street, people
are unbelievable...”

I know, it’s fun, in a way, to dwell on incidents. I even write about those stupidities sometimes, they
make great comedic material. But in the end no incident is worth keeping on the inside.

Let it go!

Say “No problem” to that person who annoyed you or did you wrong. You didn’t do it and now he/she
is gone? Well, say it later. It was a stranger? Then say it to yourself “I forgive that idiot”. Not only does
it release stress, which is the number one acid producer in the body, but forgiving keeps you in a
position whereby you are aware that YOU are the one who’s in charge of how you feel; not other
people or outside sources.

It doesn’t even have to be always somebody else. We may not realize it but we’re often our own worst
critic. We may be too hard on ourselves as well. That’s why it’s a great idea to forgive ourselves for
whatever it is that we think we’ve done wrong.

It also sounds too silly I know, but it DOES work and it releases a ton of stress.


Perfect? O’ Really?

There is no such thing as “perfect”. Stop chasing it. It’s exactly like a dog chasing its tail. It will never
catch it. A perfectionist, something which I used to claim to be, is a person who has an impressive
capability and a die hard commitment to imagine and create imperfection. Perfection is a subjective
thing that can only be achieved by non- perfectionists.

If you find yourself often thinking
“Could’ve done better?”
No you wouldn’t!!! If you COULD have, you WOULD have!

My advice is: Do the best you can do under the circumstances and let it go. Don’t dwell on what could
have been. Because if you gave your best shot, that’s all that matters. To pretend that we can control
everything we do and everything we achieve is just wishful thinking. There’s always going to be
something that will go wrong. There will be always outer factors that will affect our results. That’s
what Life is all about. I am not so happy about it but that’s the way it is. Why beat yourself about it,
right?
Laugh It Off!

Again: one of those weird things that work. Laughing is a sign of everything going fine that makes our
lives a bit easier. The message is:

There is nothing to fear; we’re safe and in good company; sharing something that makes us feel good
together.

Even when you’re laughing at a joke that you read in a paper, you’re having an intellectual connection
with the creator of that joke. It’s like saying “I get what you’re saying here. We’re part of the same
team, the same community”. Then you share it with your friends the message is similar there as well.
We feel like we belong somewhere.

If we get back to the “cause – effect” subject:

You feel good – you laugh – healthy hormones – feel good.

When we see something that makes us laugh, or at least smile, this creates a certain positive reaction in
our bodies; some healthy hormones are activated; stress is released, that’s always a good thing right?
But what is strange thing is sometimes even when we don’t feel like laughing; faking it can still cause
the healthy effects to come out.
I have never tried laughing therapy but in the light of all that biological information and how our bodies
work I can certainly believe that might work wonderfully.

I don’t feel like laughing much in the company of strangers but I love hanging out with my close
friends and share some funny stories every now and then. It does make life much easier to cope with.

I watch a lot of comedies though. I’m going to talk about it a bit later.
Punch On!

Get a punching bag, if you can afford one and you have a strong enough ceiling. Punch 15 to 30
minutes everyday. Learn the proper technique first, of course. It can be tough on your wrists and
knuckles so use the required glove and the bandages. The store you buy your bag would be kind
enough to assist you on that I believe.

You’re not trying to become the World Heavy Weight Champion here. This is about having fun and
blowing off some steam.

After each session you won’t believe how good it feels and how peaceful you are. Besides all that, it’s
a great cardiovascular exercise, not to mention a perfect arm toner.

Put on a CD you like and punch away with the rhythm. You can even add some kicks here and there if
you wish. Check out the social media for some tips about boxing and kick boxing. Just to make sure
you’re doing the right thing and you won’t hurt yourself.
Remember to F**kin’ Swear Once in a While!

Yes it’s not polite to swear and I for one hate people who swear in public and use the ‘f’ word
unnecessarily only to seem cool and bad ass. However, there is something so relaxing about swearing
which no one can deny.

Just like laughing, swearing can be almost therapeutic at times. When you’re alone, give it a try. You
don’t have to scream your lungs out, although you can do that too if you happen to live in an area
where you’re sure no one would hear you, just let your favorite curse words out; only a few minutes
will do and you’ll feel like free bird afterwards.

Remember to breathe and let negativity go; because you don’t want this to backfire and turn an
otherwise ‘peaceful you’ into an ‘agitated you’ by accident. As I mentioned before sometimes an effect
may trigger a cause factor. This exercise is not about building up anger or frustration and direct it
somewhere, although properly done that can be a useful tool too, it’s about letting the crumbs of anger
that may be still present somewhere in you out; that’s all.

Make sure you’re alone when you swear. It can be a major turn off for your partner regardless of your
reasons.
Don’t Compete with Others

Life is not fair and it doesn’t have to be. But that doesn’t mean you are not allowed to improve and get
better at whatever it is you want to do and have fun doing it.

If you compare yourself to others it’s a losing battle. Because there are many people who are great at
what they’re doing and they may be way ahead of you depending on many factors: Age, where they
live, possibilities, luck, etc.

If you focus on where they are at it will only bring you down and you won’t be able to notice the
progress you’re making. It will never seem enough. If you use the success of others as an inspiration
and not as a torturous envy source, fine; but let others be who they are and concentrate on being YOU.

World is too big and there is a ‘spotlight’ possibility of some sort for everyone. When the time is right
you’ll be there too. But if you focus on getting a little better than you were yesterday; give yourself
time to grow and you do that everyday, you’ll improve much faster.

So you don’t swim as fast as your neighbor. Who cares?
Enjoy the water. Have fun!
Credit Cards (No, No, No…I Said NO!)

I love and hate credit cards. Here’s the deal:

Don’t get more than two credit cards and keep the limits as low as you can. Don’t owe more than you
can pay with your next salary.

In today’s world it’s almost impossible to get anything happening if you don’t have at least one credit
card in your name. If you need to sign up for something on the internet or you want to order something,
you just don’t seem to have any other choice.

Some sites require it only for safety issues even.
That’s all fine.

But if you get into the habit of buying stuff before you’ve actually earned the money for it, you’re
swimming in dangerous water. Because it will start with “I need to have a credit card; what if there’s an
emergency? Where am I going to get all that money that quickly?” I understand that; more or less
everyone starts that way. But what usually happens is as soon as you know that now you have the
access to some quick money, you come up with ‘emergencies’ an excuses; and if you do it a few times
before you know it you’re caught in the current. You get used to using your credit card on every
unnecessary occasion pretty quickly. Since you don’t literally see the actual money disappear like crazy
it’s easy to lie to yourself

“I haven’t spent all that much; it’s fine”

You keep on spending with your head in the sand until one day the bill arrives.

“Oh shit! How is this possible? Have I really spent all money? Where? When?”

Then the bank asks you if you would like to raise your limit.

“Sure why not?”

You say; because let’s face it, you really need the money now, emergency or no emergency. It’s a
vicious circle and swallows you in before you even notice.

If you’re not already there do not get into that water. I’m not against credit cards but one has to be very,
very, very careful and know what he/she is doing with them. One has to have a certain plan and obtain
a credit card only to serve that financial plan.
That Good Ol’ Last Week

Ask yourself
“What would I be doing right now if I only had a week left to live?”
It’s strange but this question really puts things in perspective; at least for me. It’s a bit like the question
I love to ask if someone is arguing with me about something I didn’t do or didn’t know that I had done:
“Are you calling me a liar?”
Only then, they seem to realize that they are offending someone although that wasn’t their intention at
all. They take a step back and think about their approach and change it most of the time.

So when you think about the question “What if you had only one week…” you begin to realize what is
actually important to you in life. It kind of reveals itself to you in a matter of minutes. Of course it is
not always possible to go for what you’d rather be doing than what you have to be doing right this very
second; but I still believe that it is wise to ask this question every once in a while to stay focused on
what our lives are all about; what really matters to us.
Visualization Thing

Visualization has turned into some sort of laughing matter in the recent years due to some gurus
making it sound like it’s a ‘genie in the bottle’ fantasy tool. In reality, focusing on what we really want
just activates our brain to find ways that we didn’t and couldn’t know was possible. You don’t have to
live in a dream world, but I believe if you get out of the way of your brain it can do some great things
for you. As an artist I am well aware of this because sometimes the things I come up with are way
beyond my comprehension.

For example when I improvise on guitar; if I’m relaxed enough and I don’t force my fingers to play
what they already know how to play, they begin to be guided by something else than muscle memory;
something way more original and surprising even to myself.

If we focus on what we really want and not try to keep it within certain limits that our experiences have
created, if we get out of the way of our brains, we’d be seeing much better results I am sure.
Move It, Move It!

Move a lot because you can’t help but feel good after motion; any type of motion.

Anxiety problems? Great!
Start running, fast walking remembering to inhale and exhale properly.
You don’t want to get out?
Fine. Do some star jumps; only for a minute.
Put on one of your favorite dance albums and dance for 5 minutes. Take a break then dance again for
another 5 minutes. Do that every day. See where it takes you.

Don’t start your day without doing some stretches first and doing some jumps to get your blood
flowing. Of course, regular exercise routine would be a much better idea. But just because you don’t
think that you have the time for all that, it doesn’t mean that you can’t do the things I mentioned above.
You’re not running a marathon and it’s only for a few minutes for crying out loud. Get out of that
sleepy mood and shake your ass.

I know what it sounds like. I feel the same way when I read it somewhere else, but dammit, they work!
Horror of It All!!!

Earlier I talked about how beneficial laughing could be for your health and well being. Now I want to
talk about movies. I love comedies. I watch a lot of comedy series, classic comedy movies and the
contemporary ones. I love all that because not only do they entertain you and make you feel good but
they also break your thinking routine whereby you dwell in the past or the future. That allows you to
reset yourself and start focusing on the now again. Because if that routine is not broken consciously
once in a while, you don’t even realize that you’re caught in some stream; unfortunately it starts to feel
too natural. But when you’re out of your own ways and concentrated in something else for a while, it
gets easier to handle the re-focusing process.

Of course, then there is the danger of focusing ONLY in the movies or similar outer sources that might
make you feel good and relaxed but become an escape and put a real distance between you and your
presence. In other words now you’re not lost in the past or the future but you’re lost in a movie’s
dimension. That’s not good either. As I said before moderation is the key. Use your movie time
sparingly; don’t let it be a way to avoid real life.

Some people love comedies but some hate them with a vengeance. What are THEY going to do? I’d
say

“Well… watch thriller, horror and suspense movies then”

If I’m not feeling well and I’m off for some reason and nothing else seems to work-EFT always does
but for the sake of this issue let’s say it didn’t- I go and watch a thriller suspense movie. They are
written and filmed in a way that is supposed to keep you involved at all times; you know: mystery,
‘Who is the killer? Who’s to go next? What’s going to happen next?’ all that stuff; and that’s why they
are perfect to get you out of your routine for a while.

I gave the comedies and thrillers as example because they usually go to extremes a bit and get you out
of your shell easily but if you’re not into that sort of thing, just a drama can create the same effect as
well; chose your own way.
So You’re Single; So What?

Don’t try to be in a relationship just because all your friends happen to be with someone. If you’re not
with the right person a relationship can be nothing but hell. When I say ‘the right person’ I am not
talking about some Hollywood dream guy or a girl. I’m talking about someone who is compatible;
someone you can be friends with; someone you feel comfortable around.

But before that you’ve got to be fine with being single first. If you don’t need anyone else to feel
complete that’s when you’re ready for a relationship. But if you’re expecting someone wonderful to
come along and bring meaning to your life, you’re only going to attract someone who also expects the
same thing from you. I strongly believe that nothing done out of need leads to anywhere good.

Don’t let your happiness depend on anyone but you. When you find someone who can stand on his/her
feet and yet is willing to share go for it.

As I said before life is short. Never waste it on someone who is not worthy of you or simply is not right
for you. Expect respect from your partner at all times; but then again, it has to be natural. Forced
respect doesn’t mean a thing in a relationship. It is much better to be alone than to be with someone
who doesn’t respect you.

Remember to let your partner breathe. Don’t smother him/her. Let him/her have his/her life and
hobbies, etc.; because THOSE are the factors that made him/her attractive in your eyes in the first
place, whether you are aware of it or not. If you want all his/her attention on you and if you do the
same and put your whole individual life aside for the sake of your relationship, all you’d be doing is
putting the future of it at risk.

Be self sufficient.
Let the right person find you.
Live and let live.
Others? What Others? Are You Still There?

Don’t let others make your decisions. Don’t live for the approval of others either. You can be open
minded and hear what your friends, acquaintances and family have to say about your issues. But in the
end, don’t forget, it is YOUR life after all.

You don’t have to do anything that you don’t want to do.
No, really!
If some people are offended by it because you don’t happen to live your life the way they want you to,
so be it.

You cannot make everyone happy. It’s not possible; never. Everyone has a life and opinions of their
own. Whether they admit it or not, they all think about what is in THEIR best interest first and
foremost; then they say or do things in accordance with that. Don’t take it personal. Neither should they
when YOU decide to take steps that are in YOUR best interest.

I’m not saying be rude and alienate people around you. I’m just saying stand your ground; live and
your life the way you want to. Good deeds tend to be forgotten easily so don’t spread yourself thin in
order to make someone who doesn’t give a crap about you happy.
Lists; Charts; Write It Down!

Make a lot of lists and charts. Don’t try to keep everything in your head. That only causes stress. When
you write down stuff, your mind relaxes. It doesn’t have to worry about, say, what you are supposed to
buy from the grocery store; at what time your business meeting was tomorrow; what CDs you were
going to take with you for your holiday, etc.

Have a calendar handy. Write everything down. See it in front of you. Notice how that makes you feel.
If you have a project, take a big white sheet of paper and write everything that needs to be done with
tiny letters and then draw a circle around them. I don’t know how but it makes you focus easier.
Besides when you see all the small details in front of you without having to go through a notebook, or
worse going through them in your mind, you get to prioritize them appropriately and with minimum
effort.

There is something soothing about writing as well. Keep a diary if you think that can be your thing.
Think of all the money you’d save on a therapist.
Reading; Art; Emotional IQ

I talked about movies earlier, but come to think of it, reading can be a great tool to break your routine
and get you out of it for a while too.

The best thing about reading is, unlike watching a movie; you get to interact; because you get to create
your own ‘movie’ in your mind.

I love reading at night or very early in the morning. I always keep a book handy or at least have an e-
book and/or an audiobook in my phone.

What I also believe is that the more a person is involved in art, be it as an observant or be it as an artist,
the better his/her emotional IQ gets. Higher emotional IQ means capability to feel empathy; willingness
to understand others rather than be hostile against them.
