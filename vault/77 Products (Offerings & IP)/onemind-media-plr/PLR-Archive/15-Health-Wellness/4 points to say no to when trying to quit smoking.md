4 points to say no to when trying to quit smoking

Quitting smoking is a long battle uphill. It doesn't happen at the snap
of fingers or overnight. A compulsive smoker will light up a fag at
every chance they get. Years of smoking has made them react to every
emotion that they experienced by lighting up a cigarette.

When they feel happy, they smoke. To celebrate something, a cigarette.
Angry and feeling tired? The answer is obvious now isn't it? The
automatic response will be to reach out for cigarette and take a puff.
It has almost become like a reflex response. All these combined with our
body's addiction to smoking, it makes it even more difficult for us to
quit smoking. So before you have the urge to smoke, memorize this
mantra. H.A.L.T ( Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired ) Most of your cravings
can be checked into these four categories.

When you are feeling hungry, do not reach for the cigarette. Your body
is asking for food but your brain is interpreting it as a sign to smoke.
Eat food instead. Have a healthy snack or a meal. Perhaps a raw carrot
or an apple. Remember to have to have three square meals a day. At every
other time, if your body is still urging you to smoke, drink a glass of
water to silence your cravings. Not only does it have zero calories.
It's good for you.

When you are feeling angry, find an alternative coping mechanism or an
outlet for your anger. Do not replace your smoking addiction with
another addiction like drinking alcohol. Find healthy and positive
alternatives. An example of that would be talking out your frustration
with your spouse or a close friend. You could take up exercise such as
tennis or swimming. Let it all out when you are running and just leave
it there. Don't let it simmer and explode.

If you are lonely or bored, do something to occupy your time. Smoking is
not a way to occupy your time. To waste your money and health maybe.
Take action and redirect your boredom somewhere. You could always join
social activities such as hiking groups or book clubs. This all will
give you the opportunity to make new friends and bring something
positive into your life.

If you are fatigued by a day's work, smoking is not going to give you
the relaxation you need. Take a nap or go to bed early. Book yourself
for a nice relaxing massage. That is what your body needs, some
pampering!

It may seem that getting clean is a never ending battle but be patient
and have some faith in yourself. Soon you will be able to break the
habit and enjoy the long lasting freedom you deserve.
