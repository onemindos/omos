[De-Stressing Tip]
When you next watch a movie at home, lie yourself out on the
floor. Carpet is best, a rug is great, a yoga mat is good. Get comfy,
and let your body chill for a while. Some time later, your body will
want to start stretching and twisting about.
Follow it.
Allow it.
Enjoy it.
Let the movements be organic - Don't worry about yoga postures
and all that. Just let the body stretch out and twist the way it wants.
Be gentle. Let it happen. Take your time... Explore...
When you get up, your body will feel super chilled, the mind will
be soft and the world will no longer appear so troubled.

[Improving Posture Tip]
Always have a yoga mat unrolled in a room you spend a lot of
time. You don't have to do a class to practice yoga, just bust out
some stretches whenever inspired or to target parts of the body.
Also, sit on the floor for at least 30mins a day – while eating,
watching TV or just to chill. The body is well designed to sit on
the floor - it is not well designed to sit in chairs all day long. Sit up
on a firm cushion in the early stages. This will train your body to
support itself, build internal core strength and soften the hips.
Having a body that can support itself with ease also encourages the
mind and spirit to support themselves with ease.
