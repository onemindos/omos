# How To Make Stress Work For You Instead Of Against You

How To Make Stress Work For You Instead Of Against You
There are two types of stress: the good and the bad. The good stress is called eustress that helps to motivate you and get things done. However, our body cannot handle prolong period of stress. We need time to unwind and let our body relax. Without a break from stress, stress can turn into damage to our body system. This is what we called bad stress.
It is hard to differentiate the good from the bad stress but different people handle stress differently. It is all about balancing your work and rest, focusing your mind and be motivated. It also helps to keep in mind steps to help minimize the stress in your life. There is no one who can say they are stress free. It is important to have at least one goal in life while keeping stress levels at optimum level. So it is necessary to learn how make stress work for you instead of against you.
Identify your stress
Is it possible to list out all the things that make you stress in life such as work, relationships, financial problems, family? After listing out, make a column on the right hand column to see if you can drop the stress, change it, embrace it or turn the negative to the positive? Is it possible to eliminate them? Case scenario: If you think that you don’t have enough time to do your work, could you do without your daily television shows? Perhaps you will find more time for yourself to read more, visit friends, relax and finally get down to do things that really matter.
The power is in your hand
Our body are equip with necessary equipments to cope with the challenges life throws at us. Stress is what we defined them to be either good or bad. So we only need to present the correct equipment to counter the stress. It is up to us to turn the stress into a stimulus that motivate us or into distress that we turn away from. Being able to realize that we have the ability to control how we response to stress, we can look at it in a positive way. Instead of being misery and despair when in stress, think of it as an exciting challenge.
Learn when to switch mode
Stress can make you feel high due to the flow of adrenalin but no one can live forever under stress. Stress can age you and make you ill. It will also change your perceptions and thinking without realizing your sense of values are altered into a robot that is irresponsible towards other people as well as yourself. Identify your own stress symptoms that are approaching unhealthy levels so that you can do something to counter it and get your balance back. Example of stress symptoms are feeling of being overwhelmed, headaches, unrealistic goals and fatigue. You have to know when to take it easy and to be able to relax completely. After relaxing, you will be able to renew your energy, clear your thoughts and also able to see things from different perspective.
Following the three rough guides, try to find out ways of stress management that work for you and can be incorporated into your life. After that, you have to practice what you found not just in work but in other parts of life such as relationship.
