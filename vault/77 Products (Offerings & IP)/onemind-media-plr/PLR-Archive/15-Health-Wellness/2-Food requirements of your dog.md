# 2-Food requirements of your dog

Food requirements of your dog
Unless your dog has learnt to order food from the local bistro, it’s your duty to see to its food requirements. While talking about their food requirements, it is important to understand that what a specific dog or breed needs. Just like human beings, there is a difference between dog’s appetites also depending upon their size, age, activity level etc. Though, the basic necessity of every dog is eating a balanced portion to provide them appropriate amount of energy, nutrients, metabolic energy. Again, like humans, dogs also need the basic six elements for gaining optimal health, which are protein, vitamins, minerals, fats, carbohydrates and water. To understand the breakdown and the best sources of each of these elements for what it does to your dog, read the rest of the article carefully.
Protein intake can become its largest source of attainting right ways of calories. It plays a crucial role in a dog’s health as its responsible for its growth and repair of tissues and health. Meat meal is the most favored which a highly concentrated protein form. There are two sorts of vitamins, fat soluble comprising of vitamins A, D, E and K but water soluble comprising of all the complex vitamins B and C must be eaten and replaced regularly. Minerals are extremely important for your dog’s bone and teeth health. This makes it essential to include calcium and phosphorous in its daily diet. Natural fatty acids are also essential as they are the ones that make or break your dog’s skin and hair. They are excellent to add texture and flavor to their meals. Carbohydrates are equally necessary for dogs as they easily consume everything that is inside their prey’s stomach. So, pet dogs too need them through real vegetables, brown rice and fruits for maintaining healthy bowels. Lastly, providing a bowl of clean water is vital for transporting wastes and nutrients all through its body. All this would be sufficient to ensure fulfillment of your dog’s food requirements.
