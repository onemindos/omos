# 10-Treating your dog

Treating your dog’s diarrhea the natural way 
Is your pet suffering from diarrhea? This may be a problem that’s recurring in your dog again and again and since it is so upsetting for him, so you must be looking for ways to treat it. How about going the natural way this time, which is much safer as well. Your dog is not that different from human beings. He is omnivorous like many of you out there. Sometimes, his digestive system also gets out of order and there can be many reasons behind it but the solution to cure it is right there at home. Natural remedies are healthier for a dog and should be the first thing that one should try to normalize him. The main reason that causes digestive problems in your pet’s system is the food that you give him. No pet owners deliberately want to hurt their pet but by feeding them with even few of the left overs from the fridge can cause this problem. 
Feeding him with bland diet is the best way for treating this condition. Unflavored yogurt and cottage cheese is also very effective to stabilize good bacteria in intestines and help relieve diarrhea. Since, your dog can even get dehydrated while suffering from diarrhea, so giving him plenty of fresh and clean water is also necessary. Fasting for a day is also an alternative to get his diarrhea under control. This will be helpful in normalizing his bowel movements. For restoring electrolytes, mixing water with sports drink would also be an idle option. A high fiber diet is also suggested to help treat diarrhea.  Feeding your dog commercial pet food may be the biggest culprit of diarrhea, as these foods have the worst quality. If you really wish a long and healthy life for your dog then make sure to prepare home cooked food for him. You can always take advice from a veterinarian for treating diarrhea in case nothing works at home. Treat him well, as his happiness will make you happy.
