Canada, the U.K. and Australia. Each one sells different items and not all items from the US site
can be shipped outside the U.S.

http://affiliatemarketing.bizcentral.com/affiliateprograms/redirect.php?id=30991

http://www.bizcentral.com/register.php

Body Lastics

http://www.bodylastics.com/affiliate/

This company provides various equipment including low cost options.

Commission is between 20 and 25% per sale with a threshold of $30

You will be paid by Paypal or by check if you prefer. Commissions are paid the first week of
every month as long as your totals are over $30. If not they are rolled into the next month.

They have cookie tracking for 180 days and provide affiliates with banners and custom landing
pages.

All customer purchases come with a one year access to their online site and access to live daily
workout shows.
