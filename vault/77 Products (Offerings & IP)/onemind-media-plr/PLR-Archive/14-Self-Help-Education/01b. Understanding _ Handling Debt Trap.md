# 01b. Understanding _ Handling Debt Trap

Understanding and Handling Debt Trap
Nowadays, many of us actually live in debts. While certain amount of debt is acceptable and not financially unhealthy, a debt trap is the last thing you ever want to get caught in!
A debt trap happens when an individual puts himself under a debt (e.g. credit card) yet unable to earn sufficient money to repay it and resort to borrowing more money, i.e. another debt, to meet the interest payment of the first loan. In many cases, they are likely to borrow again to settle the second debts and the vicious cycle goes on. 
Understanding what contribute to debt trap can help you to handle it better at an individual level:
Ease of spending through credit card
Credit card debt is one of the worst forms of debt due to its high interest. Nevertheless, many people fell into a credit card debt trap because credit card has made it so tempting to delay payment and to spend money more than your monthly income. You must resist the temptation. 
Consumerism
Sadly most advertisements in our media today promote spending by equating materialism to happiness. We need to guard ourselves against being subconsciously influenced by such advertisements. Reduce exposure to TVs is one of the ways.
Impulse buying
Many people buy things on impulse even when there is clearly no need for it. Examples are those technology gadgets which are bought by people who just want to feel cool about having the latest version in the market. To put a halt to this, impose the 24-hour rule on yourself when you are attracted to an item priced over your means. Allow yourself some time to get into the “Do I need it, or do I want it” mindset. Think before you swipe your credit card.
Denial
This is one of the main reasons people fall into a debt trap. They live in a denial mode when they are already in debt, and take no measures to balance up their income and expenditure. In fact, the first step to living a debt-free life is to admit that you are in debt! Then only you would do something about it. 

Some people argue that it is not an individual’s fault when he is caught in a debt trap as the current economy scenario and low wages might also play a role. While this could be true in some cases, everything is beyond our control, except ourselves. Ultimately, understanding how you could fall into a debt trap and take the necessary precautions is key to living debt free happily ever after.
