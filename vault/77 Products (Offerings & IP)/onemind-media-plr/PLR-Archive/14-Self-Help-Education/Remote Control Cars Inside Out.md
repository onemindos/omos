              What You Need To Know About Nitro RC Cars

After "The fast and the furious", it has become a common misconception that you can call any
speed boosting addition to your car a 'Nitro'. As a result many people who come across the term
"Nitro RC car" are often mislead to believe that its been upgraded.

control cars run on actual gasoline. This couldn't be further from the truth. What Nitro car
actually means is gas powered remote controlled car. This is because this kind of RC car
consumes a specialized type Nitromethane and lubricant mixture.


So what should you have to get started on your Nitro RC car?


1.     Experience - it is recommended you have sufficient experience in operating an electric
RC car before you purchase a Nitro RC car. If you're a rookie at RC car usage then you
probably won’t be able to handle a Nitro RC car. This is why beginners are recommended to
first get electric cars before they upgrade to Nitro RC cars. Thus, they can have enough skill to
be comfortable with the Nitro RC cars.




2.     Interest- if you are not interested in Nitro RC cars, then there is absolutely no point in
pursuing the hobby. You have to have a drive behind you to truly get the best from the RC car
hobby. A hobby like Nitro RC car is expensive business and nobody likes spending on
