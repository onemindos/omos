**[Marriage tips : recognising the signs of a marriage breakdown]{.ul}**

Many marriages end up in divorce because they wait too late to get help
that is needed to save their marriage. Ignoring marital problems can
build up resentment, hurt feelings and cause on spouse to emotionally
detach from the other. Seek a professional help if the following signs
are occurring in your marriage:

### 1. [You Often Dream About a Life Without Your Spouse](http://divorcesupport.about.com/od/canorshoulditbesaved/f/thinking_about_divorce.htm)

Thinking about how much better life would be if you were divorced is
common. However, if it occurs ever so frequently, this is a sign of
trouble. It indicates that you are stuck in an unpleasant situation
without any solution.

### 2. [The Bad in The Marriage Outweighs The Good](http://divorcesupport.about.com/od/isdivorcethesolution/qt/bad_marriage.htm)

If the negative outweighs the good in your marriage, your marriage is in
trouble and is in need of help. Not taking pro-active steps to solve
marital problems will lead to other problems that destroys the marriage.

### 3. [You Keep Things to Yourself](http://divorcesupport.about.com/od/signsyourmarriageisover/tp/signsofdivorce.htm)

Communication is an important to relieve stress and build a healthier
bond between couples. Lack of communication indicates the lack of trust
in your spouse. A marriage can\'t survive where there are issues of
trust.

### 4. Arguing over the same subject repeatedly.

If your arguments become routine with all the same issues and no
resolution, then your marriage is either standing still or dying fast.

### 5. [You Feel Like You are The Only One Trying to Solve Problems](http://divorcesupport.about.com/od/canorshoulditbesaved/f/savemarriage.htm)

You feel frustrated because every time you try to discuss[ marital
problems](http://divorcesupport.about.com/od/canorshoulditbesaved/qt/maritalproblems.htm), your
spouse backs up from you. Eventually one or the other spouse will shut
down all together and no longer be interested in solving the marital
problems.

### 6. Intimacy is a thing of the past 

A considerable decline in physical affection is one of the most
recognized symptoms of a failing relationship. Intimacy is the act that
allows us to bond as husband and wife. If your partner is showing no or
very little interest in intimacy, they are less caring about the
emotional bond between the two of you.
