**[Signs That He/She Wants You Back]{.ul}**

You may have recently broken up but that doesn't mean your relationship
is totally and forever over. So how to tell if he/she wants you back?
Here Are 10 signs your ex wants you back:

1- He/She Makes an effort to improve him or herself. To let you know
they are doing their best to make themselves more attractive for you.
This might include working out issues in your relationship, improving
their appearance, getting a better job or working overtime/second job to
become more financially stable, committing to personal/spiritual growth,
becoming more sociable, etc.

2- If He/She wants you back, you can tell because He/She doesn't act the
same with you, He/She is being friendlier. He/She starts to act the same
as when you first started dating.

3- He/She has not looked for or formed any new relationships.

4- He/She has kept the communication lines open and has told you that
they will remain open as long as you want.

5- He/She has extended an invitation to you to situations that bring you
together with no strings attached.

6- He/She has let you know about the efforts they have made or are
making to be a better person. He/She makes nice comments about what you
wear and how you look.

7- He/She frequently makes phone calls, Internet messages or text
messages you.

8- He/She Frequently tries to get your attention or start up a
conversation.

9- He/She follows you around, but not in a stalking kind of way.

10- He/She can't keep their eyes off you!

These are just some of the signs your ex wants you back. Even if you
have not received these signs yet. There is still a chance it might not
be over. It may too early after the breakup to have received any of
these signs.
