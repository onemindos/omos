# 01c. How to Reduce Debts

How to Reduce Debt (when you are already in one)

Are you frowning because your debt has hit another peak this month? Here are some simple yet effective tips to reduce your debts:
Strategize Your Debt
First and foremost, make a list of all your debts, including but not exclusive of credit cards, car loans, student loans, house loans, medical bills, etc. Arrange them so that the one with largest balance appears on top and the smallest at the bottom. Watch out the minimum payment that is due at the current moment and make sure you settle those amount soonest possible.
At the same time, find ways to lower your debts where possible, .e.g. transfer your balance to credit cards with lower interest rate.

Avoid accumulating more debts
Stop using your credit cards. Don’t buy something if you cannot afford to pay in CASH! Do not take up any more loans before you settle your debts.

Reduce Your Expenses
Review your monthly expenses and eliminate any unnecessary ones. Always look for better deals before your make any purchases. Bring a calculator along when you are shopping. Reduce and if possible, eliminate unhealthy habits that cost money, e.g. smoking, alcohol, gambling. 

Live within your means
As your priority now is to balance up your income and expenses, certain sacrifices are necessary. Consider travelling by public transport and cutting down on entertainment and if necessary, moving into a smaller house. Shop for raw materials and prepare your own meals instead of dining in restaurants. 

Save money
Set aside 20 - 30% of your monthly income now into your savings account in bank or even 10% would be good enough if you do it regularly. These money would then become your fund during emergency, say when you get a flat tire and need to spend money to fix it.

Monitor your progress
It is crucial to monitor your debt-busting progress, not just to gauge how well you are doing but also to motivate yourself when you see your debt is reducing! Do it by putting charts of progress on their fridge or in their diary and update them once a month.

With the necessary measures taken, you are one step closer to become step-free!!
