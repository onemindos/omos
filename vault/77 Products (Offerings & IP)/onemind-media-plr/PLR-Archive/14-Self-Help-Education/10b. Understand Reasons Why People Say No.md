# 10b. Understand Reasons Why People Say No

Understand Reasons Why People Say "No"
To be successful in a network marketing business, you need to follow up with your leads and expand your downline. Unfortunately, it is not uncommon to get clients who say “No” to your products regardless of how good you are.  Understanding why this happens would help you to get around this issue in a more effective manner.
1. Employee mindset
Our education system brought us up with an employee mindset which focuses only on our own resources but not the vast opportunities around us. Network marketing business is a great opportunity to run our own business with a low start up cost, yet those who need it and those who can benefit from it decline the idea as they cannot imagine themselves having the extra money to spend on upfront cost. While you have seen the opportunity and already jumped on it (and probably doing well), it takes time for others to change and see from your perspective. Be patient. 

2. Negative experience
Many of us have been ‘harrassed’ to certain extent by people around us to join their network marketing business. People have bugged us at an inappropriate time, some have even begged us. All these indirectly build a negative perception that this is how a networking marketing business should work and deter them from joining. In order to rid this negative perception, you need to show how your approach is different and not just simply begging and bugging. 

3. Confidence on the products
Great leaders and good business plan are insufficient to make the business last. Ultimately, you need good quality products, products that people can see themselves growing up with. Many network marketers get a ‘No’ from clients either because they have no confidence on their products or they do not know enough to feel confident about it. Join an establish network marketing business. Evaluate all your products before you sell them to your clients. Knowing how good they actually are can make you sell better when you talk to your clients about it. Target your products to people who are more likely to be interested.

4. You
Did you speak confident enough? Did you sound reliable enough? Did you dress professionally? Imagine selling the products to yourself like how you sold it to the client. If you are also in doubt whether you would buy the products from yourself, then you need some serious work-out. 
Sometimes, it could also be that you have done something that your clients do not like, or they dislike you for no reason. In this case, there is nothing much that you can do except to be patient.
