                     Lifestyle Vs Liberation

Lifestyle refers to a way of living of families, individuals and societies.
People manifest in coping with their psychological, physical,
economic and social environments on a regular basis. Lifestyle is
articulated in leisure and work behavior patterns. It covers the
aspects of their daily activities, interests, values, opinions and
distribution of income. It also reflects to the self-image of the people,
the way they view themselves and the way they are observed by other
people.


Lifestyle is a combination of motivations, wants and needs. It is also
influenced by multiple factors like family, culture, social class and
reference groups. The breakdown of consumer lifestyles, also known
as psychographics, is an essential factor in identifying how people
make their purchasing decisions.


Liberation, on the other hand, means being set free from oppression,
foreign control and confinement. It is also one way of obtaining equal
rights. It is a state of freedom, where the ego of individual is
eliminated and the real egoless state is cognized.
Absolute or liberation has two major aspects, and these are the
following:
•     Freedom from human’s mental limitations, prejudices and
conceptions
•     Enlightenment as to human’s true or real existence

Distinguishing Positive from Negative Freedom

Political arguments usually result from the conflict between
negative and positive freedoms. Negative freedom is the absence of
obstacles to action. Positive freedom, on the other hand, is the
ability to act and often related with governmental regulations.
However, these types of freedoms are often at odds with each
other. Say for instance, your right to enjoy a cigarette while
watching movie in a theater conflicts with an asthmatic’s right to
breathe fresh and clean air.


To distinguish positive from negative freedom, here are some of
the ways:


1. Determine the Freedom – If a particular structure or rule
enables you to reach your potential, it underlies potential freedom.
Say for instance, rules about ideal diving inhibit your ability to play
in the water. However, it frees you from childishness and lets you
achieve your potential.


2. Consider the Involved Persons – Know if the freedom is
personal or it involves others. Say for instance, rules of negative
freedom say that you are free to drive your car at any speed level.
Just make sure that no one will get injured.


3. Concentrate on Actions and Barriers – Recognize that they are
interconnected. Therefore, a single act can impact freedom in both


                               - 29 -
positive and negative ways for different individuals. Say for
instance, smoking is a positive freedom for all smokers, but
obstruction reducing negative freedom for the asthmatic.


4. Analyze the Role of Society – Proponents of negative freedom
claim that rules restrict freedom for some individuals. However,
supporters of positive freedom feel that mutually agreed rules
permit people to achieve positive freedom. Say for instance,
freedom of religion is negative freedom since you are free from
barriers to your religious expression.
