# **How To Make Money With Bitcoin**

Do you want to make money with Bitcoin?

\...If you haven't heard of the term before you're probably not alone if
you're new to the world of online entrepreneurship.

However, Bitcoin is taking the computing world by an absolute storm.
Bitcoin is the first decentralised digital currency and are, in essence,
a form of money that can be sent through the internet rather than, say,
through the bank.

This is what we mean by decentralised - Bitcoin does not have a central
processing medium required to send and receive money and operates
entirely through the web.

Bitcoin is deemed a currency in itself - just as credible as the pound
or dollar.

## **The Advantages of Bitcoin**

-   They can be transferred straight from one person to another through
    > the internet without going through a bank or other financing
    > agent.

-   Thus, the fees required to use and earn Bitcoins are drastically
    > lower than those needed for a bank.

-   They can also be used in every single country - they're not bound to
    > any geographical location and don't require you to exchange your
    > currency into any other usable format.

-   Your Bitcoin account cannot be frozen like it can with a bank
    > account.

-   There are no arbitrary limits the govern your Bitcoin account.

Each Bitcoin is worth around £800 at the moment. Using a software called
a 'Bitcoin Miner', web-users can earn chunks of Bitcoins online and
store them in an online Bitcoin wallet. Bitcoin mining will require a
certain amount of work depending on the amount earned.

Bitcoins can also be exchanged for physical currencies, such as dollars
and pounds, if required - however they can still be used to purchase
some things online.

## **How To Make Money With Bitcoin**

### **Bitcoin 'Faucets'**

Faucets meaning tap, for you non-Americans.

A commonly overlooked method of earning Bitcoins is by building and
maintaining a 'Bitcoin faucet'.

This method can earn you anywhere from £50-800 per month and involves
sending very small amounts of Bitcoin to site visitors for free. These
small units are called 'satoshis' and are something like a
hundred-millionth of a Bitcoin - very very small. In doing this, you're
giving away a tiny amount of Bitcoin in exchange for ad revenue.

Your Bitcoin-faucet site can be filled with paid advertisements that,
when clicked, generate a decent amount of money that'll far exceed the
amount you pay visitors for in Bitcoins. Your site will attract a lot of
visitors because you're offering something for free, however you'll also
be being paid through advertising by doing so.

## **How To Set Up A Website & Make Money With Bitcoin**

Another simple and effective way to earn Bitcoins is to set up a Bitcoin
information website.

This kind of site will offer information about the basics of Bitcoins to
newbies and will, again, be earning money through advertisements and
affiliate marketing (a form of paid advertising that involves linking
visitors to a product and being paid commission for doing so).

This method takes a little bit of time and minimal investments in
setting up a site, however you'll be earning more than you invest
quickly and won't be out-of-pocket for long. It's also completely
passive and easy to do, even if you're totally inexperienced in the
world of Bitcoin.

## **Writing**

The web is a world filled with infinite amounts forums, message boards
and chat rooms. These are an excellent opportunity to write about
products and services, advertising for other companies and being paid to
do so.

This method can be incredibly simple - in most cases you can write a
general advert and tweak it slightly to suit an ad campaign, rarely
having to do more than, say, a sentence's worth of writing and still
being paid.

Alternatively, some sites will pay you bitcoins for writing articles and
online blog posts. If you already own a blog or are willing to start one
up, simply implement paid ads to the site and start earning bitcoin for
doing nothing more than you already are.

Head on over to Google AdSense and begin dropping a few paid ads to the
site - these generate money simply for being clicked/interacted with and
are totally free and easy to setup.

Additionally, you can be become an affiliate marketer for a business and
start advertising their products to your site's visitors, being paid
each time a visitor either clicks a product or makes a purchase using
your advertising link.

## **Ecommerce**

Additionally, you can begin selling your own products online in exchange
for Bitcoins. By selling products and services and accepting bitcoin as
a payment, you'll be earning bitcoins. Many websites now accept bitcoin
as a payment and you can too.

If you're particularly skilled in, say, marketing, you can sell your
services for bitcoins to those who need them. All you need to do is
accept bitcoin as a form of payment.If you already sell
products/services online, all you need to do is begin accepting bitcoin
as a payment.

## **How To Make Money With Bitcoin Mining**

Bitcoin 'mining' is another way to earn bitcoins online and involves
solving algorithms. In doing this, miners build up the public ledger
and, in effect, allow the bitcoin industry to function.

In doing this, miners are encouraged to join in and help improve the
system and are thus paid bitcoins for doing so.

This form of bitcoin mining isn't exactly beginner-friendly and can be
incredibly difficult for the newbie miner looking to start earning some
extra cash. I wouldn't recommend this method to anybody that doesn't
have sufficient experience in computer science and, in most cases,
you're probably best sticking to the first few methods.

### **How to Make Money Trading Online**

Day traders purchase and sell stocks through the [[stock
market]{.ul}](http://amzn.to/2kEQ3q8) daily as a way of earning money.

### **What actually is the stock market?**

We all hear talk of the stock market and how it's a sure way to make big
money with ease -- buy what even is the [[stock
market]{.ul}](http://amzn.to/2kEQ3q8)? Is it a physical thing? Is it an
actual market or just a metaphor?

The stock market exists simply because companies need money to grow.
When a company becomes public, it may appear in the stock market. On the
stock market, individuals like you and I can pay fees to 'invest' in
companies.

By invest we mean simply paying an amount in order to own a section (or
'share') of a company. Through advertising itself on the stock market,
companies give people the opportunity to invest in their business and
thus begin to accept payments that will help their company grow.

Investors will purchase and sell stock based on their potential future
value -- which is exactly what makes the stock market profitable. An
investor with an eye for company growth may see where a company's stock
value is due to rise or fall and invest/sell shares in it accordingly.

The most profitable trades will be those that accurately foretell a
company's worth and act based upon this. An investor may have heard
rumours of a new iPhone being launched/have been closely following
Apple's campaigns and so predicts that stock prices in Apple will rise,
purchasing more stock in Apple to resell after this inflation of value.

### **Different ways to make money trading online**

'Scalpers' are traders that take advantage of quick changes in the
market, often purchasing low-value stocks and turning them around in
seconds/minutes. These types of traders often earn the most profit as
their sales are very quick and relatively small, decreasing room for
error.

'Intraday traders' may, instead of turning stocks around in minutes,
deal with stocks within a day and buy & sell them within a 24 hour
period. These traders will never hold a stock for longer than a day,
hence the name 'intraday' traders.

'Swing traders', however, will hold stocks for a much longer period of
time, holding stocks for sometimes even weeks.

### **How to make money trading online**

First and foremost, keeping up-to-date with current business trends is
vital to succeeding in the stocks industry. Tracking what's going on in
companies around the world will allow you to get a better idea of how
stock prices may change in the future. Magazines and newsletters such as
The Economist and Bloomberg BusinessWeek are invaluable tools in gauging
stock values and making the right decisions.

Next, you'll need to choose your trading medium. This is simply the site
you'll be using to buy and sell stocks as well as analysing stocks to
see their value changes over time, helping you to make good choices on
reliable stocks. Scottrade, OptionsHouse and TD Ameritrade are all
reputable trading sites to use with excellent reviews. Then, simply
create an account with the site you like the best.

Many sites allow you to run a simulated version of stock trading,
allowing you to get a feel for how the stock market operates and
practice how to trade before using any real money.

Be sure to invest only in reliable stocks that have a good history and
that are predictable and profitable.

From here, the next step to make money online trading is simply to
**buying your first stock.**

As soon as you feel you're ready, make the leap and purchase your first,
reliable stocks. Many people are unsure of exactly how much to spend at
first, however this is largely dependent on how much you actually have
to spend. Try to avoid spending more than 2% of your total investment
funds. Aim to make very small investments to begin with until you find
your feet in the market.

### **Some tips to make money trading online:**

-   Try to invest in companies worth over \$2 billion -- these are known
    > as mid-cap and large-cap companies.

-   Keep an eye on the market to see which stocks are rising and falling
    > in value. Aim to buy stocks whilst they're low and sell them when
    > high.

-   Try to invest in mutual funds -- these are funds managed by a
    > professional with adequate experience in stocks.

-   When you're trying to make money trading online, never panic-sell a
    > stock. While a stock could potentially fall in value and never
    > regain it, you should never sell it in a panic as the likelihood
    > is that this stock will once again rise in value and be sellable
    > for more profit.

### **The basics of how to make money trading online**

The fundamentals of trading involve buying stocks whilst they're lower
in value and selling them once they increase. That way, you maximise
your profits buy paying less than you ultimately sell for. The larger
the share you own in a company, the more money you will eventually make
-- the more you pay for a share then the more it can be sold for.

There are two ways to make money trading online -- fundamental and
technical.

Fundamental analysis involves looking at a company's decisions in
business (like the iPhone launch mentioned earlier) to gauge an idea of
future values of a stock.

Technical analysis, however, looks at statistics and the entire market
as a whole to see why investors buy and sell particular stocks. Much of
this involved analysing data in the form of charts & graphs to unpick a
stock's worth.

Using a combination of these is essential in gaining an accurate insight
into how different companies are faring in the stock market to predict
how their values may change.

Some companies may even pay stock dividends -- these are quarterly
payments from a company to its shareholders for investing in their
company. These act as initial incentive for investors to buy into
companies in order to allow it to grow at a faster rate.

This is just a whistlestop tour of how to make money trading online. But
the fundamentals of stocks, shares & the market is an incredibly
interesting and profitable area to delve into. It's best to conduct
extensive research into the market before making your first investment
so be sure to read up to maximise your potential profits.

# How To Make Money With Dividends

Making with dividends is perhaps the most effective and effortless way
to make a passive income large enough to support your lifestyle. You'll
build a diversified financial portfolio, without having to put in any
work at all. And that's the key part: making money with dividends
requires no active work and can generate more money than a full time
job.

A dividend is a sum of money paid by a company to all of its respective
shareholders (shareholders being all people that have paid for a small
part of that company) from it's profits. Let's say, for example, a
person pays for a share of Apple. Apple would then pay this shareholder
a percentage (this percentage depending on the size of the share bought)
of all of their profits at regular time intervals, perhaps
monthly/yearly, and that shareholder would not need to do a thing after
having paid the initial share costs. It's a simple concept that is often
overcomplicated, but that's the general idea.

It is important to remember, though, that in paying to become part of a
company you are taking on responsibilities. You may need to vote on key
decisions at annual meetings, however as a small shareholder your
responsibilities will likely not be very demanding.

Making money with dividends combined with other passive income sources
can, with time, generate enough money to replace active income sources.
As soon as your passive income (dividend income, interest income etc.)
value exceeds your expenses, active income becomes optional and you
become financially free. If you are generating enough money to pay for
expenses through passive income alone, an active income stream/full time
job becomes an **extra and no longer a necessity.**

### **Making Money With Dividends: Is Dividend Income A Reliable Passive Income Stream?**

While on the surface the idea of paying money to make money may seem
unreliable, dividend income can, in many cases, be more reliable than
employment income.

Employment income and reliable income are often used synonymously -- and
this is a mistake. Covering all of your expenses with one income stream
is not a stable way of living, as if that income stream were to falter
then an individual would have no other way of earning. Many families
struggle financially when a primary earner faces losing there job and
this simply does not need to happen. Establishing multiple passive
revenue streams avoids this problem entirely as if one stream fails then
there are multiple others to fall back on.

When you combine multiple passive and active income streams, you can
really stabilise your financial position. For more information on how to
establish passive streams of income, **click here.**

### **How Is Making Money With Dividends Passive?**

Buying shares may sound all well and good as a way to earn money on the
side, but you're probably wondering **how** exactly can I use them?

The first step to earning money through dividend investments is
searching for companies that are likely to increase their dividend
payouts each year. In searching for companies that increase their
payouts over time you maximise your long-term profit. Over a span of
around 20-40 years, a dividend investor could earn upwards of hundreds
of thousands of dollar per year, easily tripling most salaries and
opening doors to financial freedom.

Buying into some companies may cost as little as 1 cent, even less,
while others could cost upwards of \$100. Buying companies of low price
does not always guarantee better value, though. Once you've found a
handful of companies, calculating their price-to-earnings ratio is the
next step -- this compares the price of the company share to the profits
it makes. Sometimes very low priced companies are involved in unstable
industries so it is important to research carefully before purchasing
cheaper shares.

It is possible to apply for a company's shares during its early days on
the stock market, however shares are typically bought through a
stockbroker. Shares can be bought by filling in online forms, as well as
over the phone. Using a stockbroker/financial firm is the safest option
for an investor as they're easy to use and allow you to pay how you
wish.
