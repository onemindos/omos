10 presupposition for MLP mastery

Neuro linguistic programming is a specially designed programming model
used to enhance the human mind operations. It is a program made to
increase the overall functioning (psychological as well as physical) of
an individual. It is used to get better results, to increase the
performance of the individual, to reach the goal, or to overcome some
psychological disorders such as anxiety, phobia, depression etc. It is a
constantly developing set of model, which involves various techniques
and presuppositions derived from the observations of the behaviour and
communication of the individual. Presuppositions are basically the
assumptions or central principles made by people, which is useful in
implementing the changes, either to themselves or to others. These are
several presuppositions in NLP. A person undergoing NLP has to believe
these assumptions to be true:

1\. **\"We all are in charge of our own minds, and therefore our
results.\"***\
*An individual is responsible for his own actions. Take responsibility
and do not blame others for any situation you are in.\
\
**2. \"You already have all the resources you need.\"**\
Don\'t wait for the opportunities or miracles. Work sincerely, success
will definitely follow you. \
**\
3. \"Mind and body form a linked system.\"\
**Mind and body works together. Think positive as negative thinking can
affect the performance and body functions as well.\
\
**4. \"There is a positive intention behind every individual\'s
behavior.\"\
**Analyze every behavior. Look for the positive intentions in any
behavior. It widens the choices. Everyone is useful in some way, only if
you identify that way.\
**\
5. \"There is no such thing as failure. There is only feedback.\"\
**If at first you do not succeed in your attempt, it is not a failure.
You just have to be persistent and keep trying. Learn from your mistakes
and don\'t repeat them; only then you will succeed. \
\
**6. \"Choice is better than no choice.\"\
**Any option is better than having no options at all.\
\
**7. \"If whatever you are doing isn\'t working, do something else.\"\
**You should always be flexible. Being flexible creates many options for
an individual.\
\
**8. \"If someone can do it, anyone can learn it.\"**\
If something is possible, then there has to be a way to do it.\
\
**9. \"Always respect the other person\'s model of the world.\"\
**Every individual has his own set of assumptions and beliefs. Everyone
creates his own unique world around himself. Do respect other people\'s
world of experiences.\
**\
10. \"You cannot fail to communicate.\"**\
People are always communicating either verbally or non-verbally, using
body language. Body language plays an important role in a person\'s
social and personal life.
