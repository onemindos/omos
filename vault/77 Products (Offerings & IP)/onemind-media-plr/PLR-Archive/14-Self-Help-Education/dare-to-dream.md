                            INTRODUCTION TO
                             DARE TO DREAM



On the cover of this booklet is a picture of the Eiffel tower in Paris, France.
The Eiffel tower was constructed under the direction of Alexandre Gustave
Eiffel. He was a French civil engineer and architect in France, and he had
submitted a design of the tower for the open competition for the 1889
Universal Exposition in Paris, France. Eiffel won the competition, and he
was granted one and a half million francs toward the construction costs,
which was less than a quarter of the estimated cost for the tower.


Because he believed in his dream, Eiffel provided the remainder of the
money himself. In return for his investment, he was to receive all revenue
from the tower for the next 20 years, and eventually his loan was repaid in
full. Work on the tower started in January 1887 and was completed in
March 1889.




Everyone has dreams for their future. Probably none of us will be asked to
finance our dreams to the extent that Eiffel was, but each of us can begin
working on those dreams that matter to us so we can achieve success in
making those dreams come true. This booklet is designed to help you
identify which dream is important to you, and why that dream is important
to you. Once you identify which dream matters most to you, you will then
be better able to begin working on making that dream come true.
                       DECIDING ON YOUR DREAM




To begin working on your dream, you must first decide what that dream is.
Are you looking at going to college to train for a different career? Do you
want to own your own business? Do you want to travel? Are you longing to
write a book or publish a song? Do you want to play in a band or do public
speaking? What longing do you have for your life to be happy?




1. Describe your dream in as few words as possible.



2. What three elements are important to you in this dream? If it involves
making a living, is it the money? Is it the job itself? Is it the location of
the job? Is it the prestige attached to the job? Is it the thrill of the
travel? Is it the people you would meet? Is it inventing something new?
Is it helping others? Why is this dream important to you?
                       GETTING TO KNOW YOURSELF


In order to start working on your dream, you need to get to know yourself
and see if your dream fits with who you are. Go through the following
questions, answering as honestly as you can, to find out more about
yourself.


1. What are three of my strong points? Would my dream utilize these three
strong points?



2. What are three things I need to work on? Would I need to improve these
three things before I begin moving forward with my dream? Would they be a
hindrance to fulfilling my dream?



3. What are three things other people like about me? Would these three things
be an asset to fulfilling my dream?



4. What three things do I like about myself? Would I still like these three things
about myself if I am able to fulfill my dream, or would fulfilling my dream mean
changing any of the three things I like about myself?



5. If I could have any one personality trait in the world for my own, what would it
  be?
6. Why do I want the personality trait I listed in #5? Would fulfilling my dream
help me acquire that personality trait?



7. What personality trait do I like the most in my friends? Would fulfilling my
dream help me acquire the traits I like the most in my friends?



8. What personality trait do I most admire in others?



9. What personality trait do I most dislike in others?



10. What word would I like others to think of when they want to describe me?
                       MY PERSONAL PREFERENCES



When deciding on your dream, you need to take a look at the environment
that would surround having that dream. For Instance:


Am I a touching person or a non-touching person when meeting friends and
acquaintances? Would I rather just shake hands, or do I enjoy a hug from
others? Would fulfilling my dream require physical contact with others I
would be uncomfortable with?


Am I happy to be around a lot of people all the time, or would I rather spend time
with a few people I know well? Would fulfilling my dream require me to be
around groups of people or individuals I would be uncomfortable with on a
regular basis?


Am I a Morning Person or an Afternoon Person or an Evening Person? (is
6 a.m. a good time to start my day, or would I rather start the day around 8 or
9, or would I rather work at night? If my dream involves a normal workday,
would the hours be compatible with my normal body rhythm (starting at a
time and ending at a time I was comfortable with)?


Am I an oral person (like to listen to things) or a visual person (would rather see
things)? Would fulfilling my dream be compatible with my being a visual or
an oral person? For instance, if I am a visual person, would I enjoy spending
my days answering questions on the phone, rather than showing others in
person how something is done?


Am I a "schedule person" or a "whenever" person? Do I do better with a set
schedule, or just take things as they come? If I am more comfortable with a
set schedule, would fulfilling my dream involve a set daily schedule, or
would it involve frequent interruptions?
Do I need quiet times, or am I happy with continual activity? If I need quiet
times, and were to fulfill my dream, would I lose my quiet times? Or if I
enjoy continual activity, would I feel isolated without the continual activity if
fulfilling my dream would require periods of quiet time?
                         JUST FOR FUN: IF I COULD...


1. If I were given a new car, what kind of car would I like to have?



2. If I were given a new car, what color would I choose? Why would I choose
that color?



3. If I were given $50,000.00 with the stipulation that it had to be spent on
something just for me, rather than paying bills or buying things for others, what
would I choose to spend it on?



4. If I were given the opportunity to spend time touring anywhere in the
world at no expense to me, where would I like to go?



5. If I were going to live somewhere other than where I am presently living,
what city or country would I choose to live in? Why?



6. If I were invited to go on a safari in Africa and it would cost me nothing,
would I go? Why or why not?



7. Would I rather go to dinner in a fancy restaurant and to the theater with
someone, or would I rather go to a more casual restaurant and to a movie with
someone? Why?
8. What is my favorite food in the following situations?

    a.at a ball game

    b.for a special dinner with friends at home

    c.for a light lunch

    d.for breakfast (if it is before 7 a.m.)

    e.would I answer "d" differently if it was served at 9 a.m.?



9. Would I rather have a job where:

    a. I sat down most of the time

    b. I got up and down during the day

    c. I was on my feet and moving around most of the time



10. Would I rather listen to a tape about a subject or would I rather read about it?



11. When someone is showing me an object, do I want to hold it and touch it for
myself?



12. Do I gesture with my hands when I am talking?

 1. What three things in my life make me happy? Would fulfilling my dream
 help me achieve these three things?



2. What three things in my life make me sad? Would fulfilling my dream
involve these three things in my life?



3. What three things in my life would I change if I could? Would fulfilling my
dream help me change these three things?
                 MAKING YOUR DREAM COME TRUE



i. Now that you know more about yourself, look again at your dream. If it
needs to be adjusted, describe your dream now in as few words as
possible.



2. What three elements are important to you in this dream?



3. Does the dream you have match who you are?

  If your dream will require a lot of standing and walking, is that who
  you are?


  If your dream will require a lot of interacting with other people, is that
  who you are?


  If your dream will require a lot of reading, is that who you are?


  If your dream will require you being on the computer most of the time,
  is that who you are?


  If your dream will require a lot of public speaking, is that who you
  are?


  If your dream will require face-to-face selling, is that who you are?
If your dream will require getting up early, is that who you are?




If your dream requires that you live in a certain area or a certain city
or rural area, is that who you are?


Look at your answers about what kind of things make you happy. Will
this dream give you those things?
                              TO BEGIN TODAY



1. What is the specific part of the dream that you can begin working on
now? If it requires additional training, how can you arrange for that
training? If it requires a certain skill, how can you start learning that skill?



2. What is the next step that you need to take to begin working on
this dream? If you need schooling, how do you apply for a loan, or
how can you arrange to finance the schooling? Do you need to talk to
someone about the realities of your dream? Do you need to start
saving money to finance your dream?



3. What is the third step that you need to take to begin working on this
dream? Do you need to apply for a different job? Do you need to move
to a different location? Do you need to research what is required for
your dream to be fulfilled?



4. What is the next step that you need to take to begin working on this
dream? Do you need special licenses or permits or travel visas? Can
you start obtaining the needed documents?



5. What is the major thing standing in the way of you achieving your
dream? What are you going to do about it?



6. What are you willing to give up to gain this dream? You need to
decide if you are willing to give up what is required to obtain your
dream. Is it time, or money, or security, or comfort, or approval of
others? You need to plan ahead of time what you’re willing to give up
so it won’t catch you off-guard down the line. Eiffel believed in his
dream, and he was willing to give up his time and his finances to back
that dream. He was able to ignore those who thought he was foolish to
pour so much time and money into his dream, because he believed in
his dream and was willing to do what was needed to fulfill that dream.
             TO BEGIN MAKING MY DREAM COME TRUE



1. When do you plan to start working on this dream?



2. In order to begin working on fulfilling your dream, what three things
are you willing to do to begin doing that?



3. Outlining a timeline and listing the things you are going to do in a
certain period of time will help you keep on track with making your
dream come true. Nothing ever happens unless we keep moving
forward.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR


Annette Oaks Pierce spent 30 years as a Family Consultant, working with troubled children, at-
risk families, and individuals under severe stress from loss of loved ones, divorce, illness, or
other major life-changing events.

Her background is in Education, and she began her consulting career working with emotionally
troubled children, autistic children, and those listed as hyperactive, ADD or ADHD. She
developed the RYES-6™ Program as a result of her work with these children to assist them in
developing solid emotional structures.

It soon became apparent that the principles of the RYES-6™ Program were as valuable to adults
in trauma as it was to children. So she began doing Family Consulting for adults as well as
children, using her revolutionary new program. She has presented seminars, done retreats, and
taught classes in personal growth and understanding of self and others for individuals,
businesses, and organizations throughout the northern United States. In the past she has offered
in-house and on-line classes based on her RYES-6™ Program. Her materials are now available
on-line in e-book format.
