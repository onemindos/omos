Breaking up: Food for thought.

Breaking up is hard to do. Or so the song goes. Yes it is difficult. It
does not mean that you are losing out when you have been broken up. You
can always emerge stronger from it. Take the pains to learn what went
wrong in your relationship and learn how you will grow through this
breaking up period. Here are some foods for thoughts for you to think
over.

Acceptance. You need to learn to accept that your relationship is over.
It's done! Do not go back to your ex asking to rekindle the
relationship. Remember why the both of your broke up and be glad. Say to
yourself, here is your chance to become a better person. So accept that
this relationship is over and let go.

Forgiveness. Don't be rude and petty. There is no need to be badmouthing
your ex. Save face and keep grace. Learn to forgive your ex and
yourself. Relationship fallouts do happen not only to you. Don't hold a
grudge. By holding on to a grudge, you are still holding on to a part of
your relationship. LET IT GO.

Respect. Learn to respect yourself. Do not lose your dignity by being
petty. If you do not respect yourself, how would anybody respect you?

Be positive. Look on the bright side of things. You have so much more
going for you. You are not defined by your relationship with your ex.
You are what you are. Think of it that you will be able to experience
new stuff, meet someone new in your life.

Be surrounded with love. Surround yourself with your friends and family.
It is not the end of the world. You have only broken yourself up with
one person not the whole world. Do not isolate yourself. Meet new people
and make new contacts.

Finally, move on. Breaking up is a fact of life. Some people may be
lucky to have found their love of their life at their first try. But
everybody has a different love story and no love stories are alike. Find
your story. Close this chapter and begin a new one today!
