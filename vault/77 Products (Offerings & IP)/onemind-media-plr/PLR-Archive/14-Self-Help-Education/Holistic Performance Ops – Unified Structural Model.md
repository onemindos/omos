**📁 Holistic Performance Ops – Unified Structural Model**

A complete folder hierarchy model for managing all personal performance, health, skill development, lifestyle, and personal sovereignty data. Designed for use in ClickUp, Legacy AI prompt logic, and cross-platform life operating system alignment.

⸻

📁 **Holistic Performance Ops**

### 🔹 1. Zeus – Performance

_Your body and biology as a measurable engine._

- Fitness tracking, recovery logs, sleep metrics, lab biomarkers
- Daily energy score, movement logs, performance KPIs

### 🔹 2. Zeus – Training

_Turning knowledge into skill through repetition._

- Tactical, creative, martial, and technical skill practice
- Session logs, rep tracking, habit streaks, performance prep

### 🔹 3. Zeus – Intelligence

_The mind’s acquisition system._

- Book vault, course content, summaries, mental models
- Learning dashboards, knowledge libraries, content extraction

### 🔹 4. Zeus – Lifeflow

_Living well by design._

- Travel, shopping, style, entertainment, aesthetics
- Personal rituals, environment tuning, gear optimization

### 🔹 5. Zeus – Finance

_Command over your money, credit, and financial freedom._

- Budgeting systems, credit repair, investments, savings goals
- Emergency funds, wealth strategy, financial dashboard

### 🔹 6. Zeus – Sovereignty

_Total control over your legal, digital, and identity systems._

- Insurance, POA, estate docs, licensing, legal compliance
- Identity protection, digital security, document vault

### 🔹 7. Zeus – Connection

_Your human network operating system._

- Romantic, family, friends, VIPs, community events
- Communication tracking, relationship rituals, outreach logs

⸻

**Intelligence → Training → Performance**
