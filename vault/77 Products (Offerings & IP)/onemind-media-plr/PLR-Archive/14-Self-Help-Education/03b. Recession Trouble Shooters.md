# 03b. Recession Trouble Shooters

Recession Trouble Shooters
The biggest challenge consumers would face during a recession is the rising living cost. If not managed properly, it could easily aggravate one’s financial situation. This article troubleshoots why people suffer financially during a recession.
Maintaining the pre-recession spending habit
Many people maintain their spending habit during a recession, despite substantial increase of food price and that of other necessities.  During a recession, one should always save as much money as he can because things become very uncertain during a recession. In addition there is also a possibility of being made redundant by company. Hence, it is always advisable to save for the rainy days. Work out a budget if you don’t already have one and stick to it! Meanwhile, change your lifestyle to match the current economic situation. 

Not managing current debts
Some people choose to ignore letters from banks on their loans or their current credit card debts. These should be settled on schedule each month or if they can afford it, pay it off! The last thing you want from here is accumulation of interest, which extends your repayment period.

Changing job / Taking job for granted
Some employees took the risk to resign from their jobs during a recession and faced difficulty landing for a new job later. It is important to understand that even big companies would be facing financial pressure in a recession and hence, they are not likely to hire as many staff as they used to. Instead of taking their current jobs for granted, they should work hard to impress their employers in order to keep their jobs.

Adding New Debts
Some people borrow more money during a recession, e.g. to repay their current debt, or to purchase a new car or house, which pushes them deeper into their debt. This makes it more difficult for them to get through a recession without owing thousands, especially if their livelihood is adversely affected later on after taking up the extra debts.

Of course, the list is not exhaustive. 
The bottom line is, pay extra attention to our spending and budgeting during a recession, as well as safeguarding our source of income.
