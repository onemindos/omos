# 03a. Recognising Signs of Recession

Recognising Signs of Recession
Economically speaking, recession is a period of temporary economic decline, characterised by the reduction of a country’s production in terms of gross domestic product (GDP) for two or more quarters. However, this is a lagging indicator, i.e. we would not be certain whether we are in a recession until at least 6 months later. Furthermore, lay people like us would not have the necessary economics knowledge to identify a recession and prepare ourselves for it when it is happening, until perhaps, media announcement.
What are the signs on the ground that a recession is probably hitting our country’s economy?
One of them is rising unemployment, very simply because if more staff are retrenched from companies, it means businesses are slowing or expected to slow and they can no longer afford or need as many workers. On the other hand, if people lose their jobs, they would not be able to spend their money, which leads to a string of other problems to local businesses, e.g. business closure. This would normally be exacerbated by less jobs creation in the market. 
Second telltale sign is the slowing down of the housing industry. Housing industry is a popular market as most people dreams about owning a house in their life time. When such a market is being impacted, it is very likely that the whole economy would be affected.
The next sign is the inflation. When prices of necessities such as food and gas increase faster than the wages, consumer would need to tighten their belt. Lower consumer spending will cause businesses to slow down. On top of that, businesses would also incur higher cost of production and narrower margins, which make them harder to survive.
Being able to recognise signs of recession allows us to prepare ourselves mentally for the possible trickle-down effects in the long term. This is crucial as it is very common for people to fall into depression during the time of economy recession. Always look for family, friends and if necessary, professional support to discuss our problems and solutions. Everyone will be experiencing tough times during a recession and you would definitely want to stay strong mentally to get yourself out of it!
