# 01d. An Easy Action Plan for My Debt

An Easy Action Plan for My Debt

“Debt is the worst poverty.”- Thomas Fuller
Hence, getting out from a debt essentially means getting out of poverty. However, as frustrated as we are with our current situations, many of us often find it difficult to act effectively against debt. Here we offer you an easy Action Plan to eliminate the enemy, once and for all!

Action 1: DO Minimise Your Expenses. Categorise ALL your expenses into either: (a) fixed expenses, e.g. home / car instalments; (b) necessary-but-not-fixed expenses, e.g. petrol, phone bills; (c) variable expenses, e.g. clothes, gadgets, dining out. Cut down or remove as many expenses as possible from Category (c). Save as much as possible from Category (b) e.g. by car-pooling or commuting by public transport and choosing a mobile service package that caters to your need. 
Action 2: DO Develop a lean budget and most importantly, stick to it. Contrary to common notion, it can be pretty simple to start a budget and to maintain it. Just a few clicks and you can download many budget Excel spreadsheets on the internet for free. After that, all you need is 10 minutes a week to update your Budget. They key is to make sure your expenses do not exceed your income. 
Action 3: DO Pay off your Debt. Start paying back either with the smallest debt or the biggest debt. Small debts are fast to be paid off; nevertheless the satisfaction can be overwhelming if you manage to pay off your largest debt first! Consider using your savings and investments for this purpose.
Action 4: DO Increase your income. Find out if you can increase your salary from your current employer, e.g. by working overtime. Alternatively, explore other ways to generate income, e.g. taking an additional job. Do keep your expenses at the minimum even as your income grows.

Action 5: DON’T Add any more debts! It is imperative to be conscious that you should not add any more debts to your shoulders at this juncture.
Action 6: DON’T keep spending in your old way, as it obviously did not work out well for you. If you used to get a venti-sized frappucino daily at Starbucks, mix your own 3-in-1 coffee in your office pantry, which tastes just as good if not better! At the bottom line, it is about engaging alternatives to make ourselves happy in a less expensive way. The amount of money you save from here could be channelled to clear your debt.
Action 7: DON’T Procrastinate. Procrastination is human’s worst habit. The longer you wait the more debts you accumulate and the worse your financial situation will become. In contrast, the sooner you settle your debts, the sooner you can move on with your life and be a happier person.
