What Are Mantras?


Mantras can be defined as words or even syllables that can when chanted
repeatedly cause transformations in a person‟s life. They can have an
impact on various aspects of a person such as the mind and also the body.
There are different types of mantras that are used for specific purposes. For
instance, there are mantras that are used to invoke peace and happiness in
a person. There are also mantras that help in the spiritual growth of a
person.


The whole concept of mantras can be traced back to the power of words.
This can be traced back even in Biblical terms. It is said that God created
the whole universe simply by using words. For instance, he created day and
nights by simply stating “Let there be light!”He even created man and
woman by simply declaring it in a loud clear and firm voice. Human beings
can also order some changed in their lives through the same way.


The Bible talks about God creating the whole universe simply by using
words as commands. This is the same as the use of mantras.


The power that mantras can bestow upon a person is one of the reasons
why this practice has been highly adapted in Hinduism and Buddhism. It is
believed that a person can‟t grow spirituality without the use of specific
mantras.
