Comforts to get you through the day

Breaking up is difficult. You go through a period of depression and
moping. You never want to get out of the house, you lose interest in all
your favorite activities, and you start avoiding your friends. When you
are going through a break up, you will need some comfort. Here are some
basic comfort tips to help you survive the day.

Comfort food. Or what you may know as breakup food. Anything that will
make you feel happy inside. Ice cream, a bottle of wine, chocolate and
many more. Indulge yourself for these few weeks. But do remember don't
overdo it and make it a part of your daily diet. Comfort foods are meant
to just be there for you when you need some comfort!

Comfort clothes. Wear your most comfortable clothes when you are at
home. A large shirt or sweat pants. If you have to go out though, don't
go out looking like a total mess. Have some dignity. For women, wear a
slip on dress and for men, you could just go for the jeans and casual
shirt attire. Put on a pair of sunglasses to cover your swollen eyes if
needed. Prepare an excuse if you want. If people asked why your nose is
so red, just say you are having a cold.

Comfort books. There are plenty of self help books to get you through
this. Go get them and read them. Or you could buy a book that you have
never read before by your favorite author. Buy a book and snuggle in bed
with it. Fill up your time instead of moping around the house
reminiscing about things that have past.

Comfort movies. In your comfort clothes and your comfort food, plop down
on the sofa and treat yourself to comfort movies. A movie from your
childhood perhaps or one of those latest packed action movies would be
good. Anything to get your mind off yourself. Do not watch romantic
comedies if it would make you feel worse.

Most of all, be patient and give yourself time to heal. It takes time to
get through a break up. So sit back and relax and let time do it all.
