# 03d. 7 Powerful Recession Survival Tips

7 Powerful Recession Survival Tips
Stay employed
Maintaining your source of income is mandatory during a recession. Hence, if you are within working age, you must keep your job at all cost as many companies will try to lower their production cost by slimming down their organisation. Make yourself indispensable. Upgrade your knowledge and your skills. Work diligently. Be more involved with your work. Suggest improvements. Meanwhile, explore other career opportunities in case any ‘death sentence’ falls. Start looking for a new job immediately in case it does. 

Budget all your expenditures
This might sound obvious but many people actually fail to keep track of their expenditure even during times of recession. When planning your budget, be comprehensive and take into account any possible ad-hoc expenditures such as year-end vacation, insurance, etc. It is inadequate to estimate one month’s expenditure and multiply it by 12.

Save Money
Identifying how you can save money in all aspects of your expenditures, particularly in groceries, food, entertainment and utilities. Spend less on what you are already buying and stop making any big purchases! Make sure you save enough money to last you up to 6 months without income.

Reduce your debt. 
Consider using your savings to settle your any credit card bills. Otherwise, make sure you are paying as little interest as possible for your bills, e.g. by switching to a card with lower interest rate.
Diversify your investments
This is important to avoid losing all your investments at the same time. 
Get the Best Bargain
Retailers will tend to offer good deals during recessions to stimulate sales. Check out current discounted items or stores whenever you are shopping for groceries or any other daily necessities.

Enjoy life!
Life always has its ups and downs, so does the economy. Don’t be too sad if you are suffering from the recession. Stay optimistic and things will get better!
