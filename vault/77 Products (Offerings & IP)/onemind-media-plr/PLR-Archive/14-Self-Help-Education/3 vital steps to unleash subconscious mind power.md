**3 vital steps to unleash subconscious mind power**

The subconscious mind had unlimited power and we can literally achieve
anything we want with it. The problem is few people have mastered it and
thus are not maximizing its endless potentials. To get started, these
are a few easy steps to use this power to achieve your goals; whether it
is to earn more money to be a more happy, fulfilled and successful
person. The vital steps to take are as follow:

**Step 1: Engulf yourself with pictures, visual representations, or
symbols of the things related to the things you want to accomplish.**

With surrounding positive images or even sounds, your subconscious mind
will remember and you will be reminded of your goals often. This will
result in a focused mind, with the capability of removing all
distractions and ultimately turning your thoughts into reality

**Step 2: Be confident and ask positive questions**

Self esteem is the backbone for any success strategy. Confidence usually
comes before success. The power of the subconscious mind cannot be truly
explored if you are always negative and pessimistic. Avoid negative
questions such as " why all bad things happen to me?", "Why can't I be
smarter, like he is?" or " why do I keep failing to achieve my goals in
life?". This will lead to self-destruction in term of mental and
emotional functioning. Their minds will be conditioned in the way that
gives them an idea that they can do nothing about the situation. It is
then supported with various explanations and justification that persuade
them that they are goal setting failures, are not lucky, creative, rich
and basically a loser in life.

If you feel you are one of those people, change that attitude
immediately. Instead, ask yourself questions that require positive and
self-driven answers. Do not be surprised if you subconscious mind may
come out with solutions that you did not think even exist before the
question. Examples of such questions are "What can I do to get that
promotion?", " How can I improve my memory?", "How can I improve my
relationship with others?" etc.

Step 3: Know your purpose

Going on life without knowing your goals is basically taking a train to
anywhere. Goals need to be precise with more details to work for and not
just " I want to be rich!". A timeline is also needed for that extra
fuel to pull you through. When you have a goal that is strong and
intense enough, your subconscious will always find a way to help you
achieve them. Besides, if you have a highly emotional purpose behind
your actions, you will be more inspired to do whatever it takes to
achieve your dreams.
