Reduce your intake of coffee drinks. Did you know that reducing your

replacement when dirty should be your priority. This is because you
will be using more gas if the filter gets clogged with solid materials.


As much as air conditioners are good, they should only be turned on
when really needed. This is because they contribute to reducing the
number of miles per gallon your car does.


Driving conservatively is a good practice. However, constant
acceleration and brake application leads to more gas usage. In
addition, reduce your speed on highways. Latest autos do have
overdrive and do run at about 65mph. So, ascertain the revolution per
minute of your car ‘safe spot’ and stick to it as much as is possible
while driving.

Although it may seem hard for you to swallow, it is proven that
driving with your windows closed does save you some gas. Driving

Try to understand the road system of your environment so you chose

                        Chapter 8:
     Better Money Management Through Debt Control


                          Synopsis

Having a good method of arranging your bills go a long way to affect
your finances positively. For example, you can easily pay them on
time than defaulting. Also, the ease of finding them out will help you
resolve issues concerning payments too.




                                - 28 -
                              Organize

The first step is to get a monthly planner for each year starting from
the current one. Register the dates of bill payments and place them
side by side with your payday. Keep the bills to be paid in your
planner and ascertain with recurring bill should be shouldered. Make
sure the bill with the closest payment date is keep on top of the
second one. Check the bill again for correctness. Make no room for
too much paperwork that accompanies a bill. Let the thrash can
contain them and not your file.


Set out at least once a month period to organize your bills. Provide a
separate folder for recurring bills and another for other bills each
month. A good practice is to write on the folder the name and due
date of the bill. When the invoice comes, write on it the check
number, amount paid and the date it was paid before committing it to
filing.


After bills have been paid, try to file then immediately into their
various folders. Also, arrange the folders sequentially for ease of
retrieval. All cancelled checks you have must be domiciled in a
particular folder in company of the statements of the bank. Next is to
print the dates onto the folder.


You may also want to make a different folder for bank statements and
“For income taxes. Once things like medical bills and real property
taxes bills are paid [these bills can be deducted on your tax returns],


                                   - 29 -
keep them filed in “For income taxes” instead of paid bills file. Also,
tag one-folder “investments” and use it in housing all paper-works
that emerge because of 401(K) or IRA for each year.


For each month, you must have a folder for all receipts. Put these
folders in a box with the date of the year clearly on the box. For each
year, start a new folder system and a different box for storing them
too.


Doing this regularly has a lot more advantages for you. For example,
it helps you keep track of your spending habits. The evil of over
spending is mainly disastrous when you are not keeping records. In
addition, you will be able to determine your cash inflow and outflow.
This will help you in case you want to undertake a project. You need
to first count the cost to know if your present financial status can
carry the weight or not.
