How to survive a break up.

Breaking up is a hard thing to deal with. What more trying to survive in
a break up. Here are a few ways that will help you survive the day.

1.  Get closure. Closure is much needed especially if you parted in a
    hostile manner. Calm yourself down and call your ex up. Talk things
    out with them to get the closure that you need.

2.  Box up your ex's belonging and store it away. You don't need to be
    reminded of past memories. Learn to let go.

3.  Recognize that feeling sad or depressed after your break up is a
    normal emotion. Do not rush yourself through the healing period.
    Time is a great healer so let time do its work.

4.  Avoid spending time or talking with your ex after the closure.
    Resist the urge to contact your ex.

5.  Fill your time with activities. Once you are broken up, there will
    be more free time. Instead of using that free time to mope, fill it
    up with activities. Go for dance classes, swimming or hiking.

6.  Reconnect. Remember all those times you couldn't go out with your
    friends because you had a date with your partner? Well now is the
    time to mend back old ties and reconnect. Call them up and make
    arrangements to go out with them. Hang out, watch a movie, just a
    friendly night out.

7.  Let it all out. Call up your best friend or a family member. Talk it
    out of your system. You will feel much better after crying it all
    out.

8.  Remember to have a good cry about it. Let it all out of your system.
    Once you are done, you'll feel as though a huge burden has been
    lifted off your shoulders.

9.  Take a trip somewhere. Go for a holiday with a friend to an island.
    Enjoy the sea and the sun and get a nice tan. It will make you feel
    amazing.

Lastly, give yourself time. Coping with a break up is a process not an
event that just happens overnight. Be patient.
