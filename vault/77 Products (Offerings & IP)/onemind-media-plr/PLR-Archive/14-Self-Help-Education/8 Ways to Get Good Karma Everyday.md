**8 Ways to Get Good Karma Everyday**

Many people blame bad luck for everything bad that happens in their
lives. But what many people don't realise is that karma and luck are
closely linked. It only makes sense that if you need good luck, you need
to do things that increase your positive karma. This article will tell
you eight small things you can do to increase your karma and luck over
time. Even if you don't believe in karma or luck, these tips will only
serve to bring some kindness into the world.

1.  Start small. Make a point to smile at a stranger every day. If
    someone is having a bad day, a smile may be just what they need to
    lift up their hearts again.

2.  Fast. Go without one meal (the heaviest meal of the day would be
    good) for one day, and donate the money you would normally spend for
    that meal to those in need.

3.  Donate your things to a charitable cause. Don't just let your old
    clothes, books, toys, etc. clutter up your house -- donate them to a
    thrift store or the Salvation Army.

4.  Pay someone a compliment. This can be done face to face, or by
    leaving positive comments on someone's blog or Facebook wall. It
    might make someone happier.

5.  Send someone cheerful and encouraging e-cards at random, especially
    if you know that they are going through a tough period.

6.  Cook a special meal for someone, maybe the elderly neighbour who
    lives alone, or pay for a stranger's lunch one day just to be nice.

7.  Leave your change behind at coin machines. Someone else may want a
    snack or a drink but may be a few cents short. Your leftover change
    will be appreciated.

8.  While queuing to pay at the supermarket, let someone who has fewer
    items in their cart go ahead first.
