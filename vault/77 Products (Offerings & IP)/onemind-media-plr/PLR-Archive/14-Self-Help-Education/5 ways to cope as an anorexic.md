5 ways to cope as an anorexic.

Anorexia is a serious and life threatening disease. Coping with anorexia
is going to take all your strength, courage and endurance as one's
battle with anorexia often lasts a lifetime. Here are a few steps you
can take to cope as an anorexic.

1.  Focus on a healthy weight. A healthy weight is the one specified by
    your physician. Not the weight that is expected by your family or
    friends.

2.  Avoid reading websites or literature that celebrates anorexia.
    Remember that anorexia is a life threatening disease and not a
    lifestyle choice. It should never be glorified as it has ruins
    plenty of people's lives.

3.  Anorexics often experience a skewed sense of body image. When
    looking at the mirror, ask yourself, what you think of your body.
    List down all the things that you like about your body. List down
    all the things that you don't like about your body. Be kind to
    yourself. If you still obsess about your body image, talk to a
    therapist. They will be able to talk to you and counsel you.

4.  Look out for triggers or situations that may cause you to go back to
    your anorexic habits. These may include stressful situations like
    family gatherings. Your relatives may be always passing off snide
    comments about your body. List them all down. Then think of a game
    plan of what you could do when you are faced with those situations.
    For example, if your relatives commented about your weight, have an
    excuse ready. Say yes I've seen the physician and he said my weight
    was healthy. So I am not too worried. You need to realize that you
    can't please everyone and if you do spend your whole life doing
    that, it is not a life worth living.

5.  Finally reward yourself. Take every day as a new challenge. Reward
    yourself everyday by doing something you enjoy that will make you
    feel good about yourself. Remember nobody will ever love yourself as
    much as you. So start today!
