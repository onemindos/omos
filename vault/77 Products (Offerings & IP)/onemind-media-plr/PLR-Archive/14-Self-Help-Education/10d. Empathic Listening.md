# 10d. Empathic Listening

Empathic Listening: Tricks for Making People Agree To Anything
“The real beginning of influence comes as others sense you are being influenced by them – when they feel understood by you – that you have listened deeply and sincerely, and that you are open.” 
-  Stephen Covey
Empathic listening is a key skill for all network marketers to sell themselves successfully. It is the skill to step into other people’s shoes and see the world from their perspectives, which is crucial to understand when you want to get your message across. 
How to develop emphatic listening skills?
1. Listen with the intention to understand
Not listen with the intention to reply. This means you should never think about what you want to say when your prospect is talking. Instead, focus on every word that comes out from their mouth, notice their intonation and feel what is going on in their minds. In reality, this is easier said than done. It takes numerous practices. 

2. Ask the right questions
You need a greater understanding on your prospect’s situation, how they feel about it and what kind of solutions they are looking for. Many of us have the habit of talking based on our own limited understanding, thinking we know everything and offer what we think is the best. Asking the right questions can get your prospects to open up and share their inner thoughts. Use positive questions.

3. Be patient
In addition of Step 1 and 2, you need to be patient while your prospect is talking. Do not interrupt as you are cutting their thoughts. The idea is to let them express their thoughts in words as soon as the thought flows into their mind. This way, you are more likely to get a complete and honest picture of the whole scenario. 

4. Summarise and Rephrase
If you are able to summarise what you have just heard, it means you have been paying enough attention. It shows that you care. Rephrase your prospect’s ideas in your own words. If they show signs of agreement, you are on the right track!
