# 03e. Common Mistakes Made during Recession

Common Mistakes Made during Recession
Recession brings stress and it is not surprising that many people tend to make the wrong decisions to handle their financial issues under such duress. Let’s learn from mistakes of others to avoid making the same ones if we happen to be in their shoes.
Fail to face reality
There are people who live in denial and who are overly optimistic about the economy, even when the economy is showing a strong sign of recession. Consequently they take no action to prevent themselves from falling into deeper debts, and end up suffering financially.

Fail to plan & save ahead
Those who suffer during recession are typically those who live paycheck to paycheck. Without savings and an emergency fund, they are at high risk of running into financial trouble should they run out of job during recession. It is advisable to have a savings that could last you about 3 – 6 months without income.

Fail to budget
In many times, budgeting is not done properly, e.g. not putting in their Christmas, New Year and summer holidays, or simply multiplying one week expenses by 4, and then by 12 to obtain a one year budget. This is further complicated by the fact that many do not follow their own budgets. They continued their old spending habits and do not restrict themselves from making large purchases.

Fail to ask for help
At a time of recession, rationality is crucial for all financial decisions. Unfortunately, not everyone can handle the stress on their own during recession. Therefore, getting support from family and friends is a positive step which can help you gain control of your situation. You can also discuss with your superiors, trusted co-workers or coach privately and ask for their advices.

Become a co-signer
There are people who run into troubles by becoming co-signer for other people during recession. While this individual might be your good friend, think twice before you agree. Take into account your financial circumstances as you will need to repay the loan if he ever loses his job. What if you are also laid off from your job?

At the bottom line, be cautious and rational during recession.
