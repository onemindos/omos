If you are looking for your should mate the, you must know that there are
certain things which you need to know and certain strategies which you

kept alive and strong. You should never lose your interest in your partner
and that is the key. In other important things following guidelines can help

Find Your True Match

First of all, you should be sure that your soul mate is the only person in the
world with whom you can live your life. If you are confused about your
choice then, things will start to get troubled. Even if you have some
differences but exaggerating them and making them an issue is not the
right way. You should always pay attention to your partner and make them
believe that he or she is special for you. Send an email, text message or do a
phone call when you are at work and tell them that they are important
enough that you can get time from your work to take care of them.

Proper Planning Of Your Time

Planning of your time is important and in fact you can say that planning of
time is most important thing in building good and long term soul mate
relationships. There is an old saying that if you fail to plan then, you are
planning to fail. This is very true in terms of soul mate relationships. In
today’s busy world, this often happens that people with busy schedule and

heavy loaded jobs cannot get time for their soul mate and the more you
ignore each other, more distance will occur and no matter how much love
and affection there was but this less time spending will create
misunderstandings which can lead to total destruction. In order to keep
your relationship healthy, you should find time for your partner, no matter
how busy you are at your work but try and keep your personal life and
career life separate. Never allow your progress at work to disturb your
progress in your relationship.

Be possessive about your relationship

This is very important and you can say that it is for the plant of your
relationship that you need to have some positive jealousy and never ignore
your relationship. Do not compromise the integrity of your soul mate
relationship. Make sure that you are fulfilling the emotional needs of your
partner carefully. Soul mate relationships are forever and you can always
make them flourish with very small but effective deeds.

Never forget special occasions

This is another important aspect of any relationship which makes the other
person realize that you always care for them and you never forget them.
Special occasions like birthday, marriage anniversary, kid’s birthday,
Valentine’s Day and other similar occasions should always be remembered
and wished to each other. These are the occasions which make the other
person realize that you always have their thoughts and you are never
forgetting them. You can give them some kind of gift or just a flower will
also do the work. This is not necessary that you always have to give some

will also do the same job which an expensive gift can.

Add an Element of Surprise to Your Relationship

If your partner like surprises and you have some spare time then, never

favorite thing. Send them a bunch of flowers in office, get an extended
lunch break from their boss and take them to a romantic lunch. The options
are endless and you just have to think about the favorite place or favorite
thing of your partner. You just need to break that chain of routine which
keeps on boring your relationship and add some colors in it through adding
some kind of surprise.

Hang Out With Your Friends

If you both have a very tight work routine and you seldom find time for
each other then, try to hang out with your friends. You can throw a small
party at weekends and invite your friends. This will be great fun to have and
will also make your life more colorful and cheerful. Through these small
gatherings, you both will get to know each other’s friends and this will also
tell you more about the other person’s taste and habits. You will be able to
know more about each other from friends because they will talk of old times
which you may have missed.

Read Your Partner

humble in your approach and try to learn each and everything about your
partner. His likes, dislikes, strengths, weaknesses and in short, each and

These are all the important things which you need to take care for building

                                The Rules

No one can say that dating is easy because it is actually a very difficult thing
and especially in this modern and high tech world, finding a reasonable
date and then convincing him or her to date with you is really a tough task.
In the past, it was relatively easy to find dates because there was lot less
competition and once you ask someone to go out then, he or she will not
consider going out with someone else.


This was the quality of commitment but these days it is not the case as
anyone can leave you even after spending many good and happy dates with
you. Most of these break ups occur due to violation of dating rules and
people are often unaware of those dating rules. There are so many crimes
which you can do and murder you date with your own hands.

Try to Be Punctual and At Your Best

There are people who do not try to look smart on their dates and give an
impression that they are what they are but this is an old technique and you
need to change that. These days everyone wants best for themselves. Even
you can think about your own self that will you date a person with weary
hair style and casual dressing sitting with you in candle light dinner?


Obviously not, so you should try and look your best. Give yourself some
time for the preparation and make yourself totally presentable to your date.


                                      - 12 -
Showing up late on date and looking messy is not the right approach and it
will lead you towards loosing that date in no time.

Try To Be Calm and Happy

You should try and get your dating experience as fun and a very
entertaining experience. This will help you to maintain your posture as a
fun loving person. Sometimes, this thought of presenting yourself to some
stranger for approval can be a dangerous thought and it can make things go
in wrong direction from within but with very little extra attention, you can
get rid of such dangerous thoughts and can keep up the momentum. Try to
take dating as part of your fun and try to enjoy your date no matter what
kind of date it is.



Share Compliments and Try to Be Interested

This is a universal truth that every human being loves to hear his
appreciation and same goes right in dating that you need to share
compliments about dressing and other things of your partner. This gives a
boost to the other person and he or she feels satisfied that finally the efforts
they have made for date have shown a good result.


Similarly, never lose your interest in the conversation and ask questions.
This will keep you both interested in the conversation. If one of the two
starts to show tiredness and starts yawning then, it can send a really wrong
impression and that will be your first and last date.

Be Truthful and Go With Your Own Heart

heart and not with the consent and opinion of your friends. It can often lead
you to a failed dating experience if you ignore your heart’s choice and go
with your friend’s choice. Try and make sure that the person with who you
are going on date suits your habits.


Try to be very honest also and tell the person straight away if you are not
interested in further dates. Hiding and not telling that person in initial time
is not a very good idea. This can hurt the other person’s thoughts if you
gave an impression of acceptance but then you reject. To avoid this kind of
emotional stress, you should tell the person straight away about your
interest.

Be Positive and Put Some Thoughts in Your Dates

This is for sure that you will meet some unpleasant people along your way
to a perfect date but you need to be very positive in that time period and
wait for the right moment to come. It will always take time for you to meet
your right match but along the way, you may find some nice people whom
you can make your friends too.


When you find your right one then, add some thought in your dating and
make the other person believe that you have been thinking about meeting
them. Make some arrangements for your date and try to show your
attraction and level of interest to that person.

You need to meet positive people who have understanding of dating and its

                           What To Avoid

Most of the times, people complain that they have gone out with so many
dates but nothing works for them but they do not realize that there are
some things and gestures which can make your date a failure, no matter
how compatible you are. Most of these things are mistakes which we
commit without even realizing that this can lead to a failure of this whole
date. In rest of this chapter, you will come to know all about such mistakes.


Do Not Be Desperate


It often happens that when you come back from your first date then you
start calling or texting or emailing that particular person. This gestures
sends a very wrong impression and most of the times, the other person gets
the impression of desperation. You should never do that and be stable and
more proper in your approach. When you have started seeing someone
then, give that person some time to settle down. Do not email text or phone
more than once a day unless he or she answers. This is the right approach
which will save you from many failures.


Never repeat a bad experience


Some people have this habit of getting attached with certain people and
although those people hurt their feelings and emotions in many ways once
but they again want to repatch with them. This can never lead you to a


                                     - 17 -
healthy relationship because you need to be away from such people who
can make you feel bad about yourself. You should always find people who
give respect to your thoughts and who accept your personality as it is. You
will meet some people with too many demands and they always want to
change the other person but such people are never trustworthy and they
can leave you in middle of the way at any time. Avoid meeting such people
and even if someone wants to change you then, seek if the change is positive
or negative but if he or she just wants to dictate their terms then, you
should always avoid them.


Do Not Be Late Without Informing the Other Person


Being late is considered just a rude behavior which you should never
commit and if you are late due to some reason then, text the person or call
the other person before time so that he or she can also adjust the schedule.
If you are late without a reasonable excuse then, the other person will take
it just as rudeness and nothing else.


Do Not Lie To Your Date


Lie is the worst thing which you can do in your date and it can destroy the
whole purpose of dating. Lying in very start is just like not trusting the
other person and most of the times it happens that you hide some truth and
she comes to know that truth from some other source and it can make her
believe that you have been lying to her the whole time.




                                        - 18 -
Avoid lying and always tell the truth no matter how bitter and how cold the
truth is. If she is really interested in you then, she will definitely look into
that truth and will believe you.


Do Not Be Over Interested


This is another type desperation that people can show because they become
too available and they start giving first priority to their date. This is not the
way to go because it can decrease your value. You should show some
attitude and make her believe that you have your own life as well which
needs time and you cannot be available 24/7 a day.


Do Not Open Up Too Early


Some people have this habit of trusting their date too easily and they start
revealing their secrets in the second date or even in first. This is not the
right behavior because it can lead you to rejection. You need to be calm and
humble in your approach and talk about more general things in first few
dates and then according to the interest of other person, you can open up as
well. If she is sharing something then, you should share some more but if
she is just going with usual talks then, you do not need to get too involved.


Do Not Distract Your Attention


When you are on date the, try and concentrate only on your own date and
avoid looking around too much. This is very common mistake which most

of the men make. You can always think that your date is not watching you
when you are staring at corner cutie but this is not the case. In most of such
cases, you will see your date walking out from the door after watching your
attitude. You need to make her believe that your whole attention is around
her and you are talking, thinking and listening to only her. This impression
is very solid and it works most of the times.


Do Not Get Drunk And Take Care Of Your Personal Security


This is another mistake which most of the men make that they get drunk in
their first date but this is not the way, you should be in limits and show
some manners towards your date. Another thing is to take care of your
personal security.


If you are on a date without any reference and just from phone or internet
then, you must be very careful because these days this is a common way of
doing robberies. You should always inform some of your friends that you
are going on date and keep your phone charged and on all the time.


You should try and make your first date in public places and well lit places.
Avoid going in the dark and lonely places for your first date. This is
necessary to keep your security intact.


Do Not Get Too Personal On First Date




                                     - 20 -
It is often seen that people give away their home phone number, address
and other similar things in first date. This is not a very good idea because
you merely know the other person and she can use your information in any
way she wants. Another thing is having sex on your first date and this is
another very bad idea.


If you are really interested in someone then, having sex on your first date
will ruin everything and may be that will be your first and last date. So keep
your patience and wait a little for the right moment to come. Sex never
sends a romantic impression instead she will think that you are just
interested in physical relation more than a serious relation.


Never Date Married Or Committed People


If you are single then, you should avoid dating married or committed
people. It is very unlikely that they will leave their ex for you. It can also
involve lot more complications as if her husband came to know that you are
dating with his wife then, he can create some deep problems for you.
Secondly, if you are single then, you do not need to be a shoulder to cry on
and you always deserve better. Go out and explore your possibilities and
find best for yourself.
