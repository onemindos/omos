**The Law of Attraction Explained**

What is the Law of Attraction? The best way to describe it is that your
dominant thoughts and feelings, whether positive or negative, will find
a way to manifest in your life. Quite simply put, your life is a
manifestation of what you think about most or what you predict it would
be. Want to lose weight? Be a millionaire? Be happily married? Then
simply visualise it, and you will get it.

Before the sceptic in you dismisses this as rubbish, take a moment to
think about your life and how it has turned out. Given your beliefs in
yourself and what you think about most of the time, are you really that
surprised as to how your life has turned out? For most people, the
answer is no! Perhaps you have already or are invoking the Law of
Attraction in your lives.

Putting the Law of Attraction into practice requires a leap of faith in
"the universe". People who apply this law to their daily lives believe
that all of us have a connection to the universe, and that using the
power of our dominant thoughts, we can generate tangible results or
manifestations.

The most basic step in invoking the Law of Attraction is by paying close
attention to your thoughts. By clearly focusing your thoughts and goals,
you will be in a better position to start sending out clear intentions
to the universe. The more feeling and emotion you put into your
intentions, the more likely it is to manifest. Do not doom yourself to
failure by feeding your intentions with negative energy, i.e.
visualising failure or fear of failure.

Set clear and compelling goals and visualise them over and over again.
This is the best way to tip the Law of Attraction in your favour and
greatly diminishes that niggling little voice of self-doubt as well.
Take whatever coincidences in your life that are related to your goal as
opportunities the universe has thrown your way and see where the path
leads!
