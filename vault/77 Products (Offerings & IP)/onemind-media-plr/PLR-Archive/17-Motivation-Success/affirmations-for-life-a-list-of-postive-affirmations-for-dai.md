Introduction


Affirmations can be very useful tools that assist in re-contextualising the way we see the world and
essentially the way of being in the world. When affirmations are used effectively (like when we use
acupuncture point tapping), along with other supportive practices like good nutrition, regular
supportive exercise and deepening our awareness through awareness-based exercises like yoga and tai
chi; affirmations can support oneself for rapid and successful change.
One thing I would like to make clear is that the primary aim of using affirmations is not to rid the mind
of negative thoughts or feelings. We are not trying to ‘force’ negativity or unpleasant feelings and
sensations from our lives. The truth is that negative thoughts and unpleasant feelings will present
themselves from time to time and this is nothing to worry about. This is normal and a part of being a
human being. Being human means we will experience a wide range of thoughts, feelings and emotions
from sadness and grief, to worry, fear and anxiety at different times of our lives. Sometimes fear and
anxiety have a role and are sometimes helpful to us; they can get us moving, they warn us of danger
and they are also signs that we are stepping outside our comfort zones. Anytime you step outside your
comfort zone you will have some level of anxiety, so this is OK. It becomes a problem only when it
becomes overwhelming to us and we don’t manage these states well. We may fly off the handle and
abuse someone or we may turn to drugs and alcohol to dampen the intensity. In these cases it is
potentially harmful but it’s not the emotion, thought or feeling that is really doing the harm, it’s the
way we respond or react to it that is the problem.
Over time, when we ‘charge up’ the energy system and the mind with positive affirmations and images
it re-contextualises our reality, it lifts our life force and the negative thoughts and attitudes will simply
start to thin out and dissipate of there of accord. There is no need to “force” anything to happen, let
things happen naturally. The only real effort involved is to make the time to pursue those things that
contribute to uplifting our own life force and also uplift the life force of those around us. Taking time
out to tap in the affirmations listed in this book will contribute greatly to this process.
A Little About E.F.T. and Energy Tapping


Energy tapping is a relatively new discovery that involves tapping various acupuncture points around
the body to reorganise and stimulate the distribution of energy throughout the body. The effects of this
can be profound, from relieving muscular pain to reducing stress and anxiety in a matter of moments.
There have been a few different systems that have been developed over the years and Emotional
Freedom Technique (EFT) seems to be one of the most accessible and easiest to practice for most
people. If you are unfamiliar with EFT or would like to find out more about EFT then please check
www.garythink.com/eft where you can get a lot of free info about it.
I personally find that using techniques like E.F.T. or any acupuncture point tapping to be very useful
when working with affirmations. Tapping acupuncture points allows for the body to really integrate
affirmations into the energy body making it much more effective than simply saying or repeating
affirmations in ones head or writing them down.
This book is not designed to explain the system of E.F.T. but rather to provide you with a set of useful
affirmations that you can ‘tap into’ your body and nervous system. It is not recommended that you try
to tap these affirmations in when you feel wound up, stressed out, fearful or unfocused as its not the
right time as your system is already overloaded with stress. In such a case it is best to use E.F.T. or any
other system that helps you to calm down and acknowledge your feelings, thoughts and emotions.
Therefore use the affirmations listed in this book only when you are feeling calm and focused.
How to Tap the Points?


Firstly, check out the illustrated diagram below to become familiar with the location of the
acupuncture points that are most commonly used in systems like E.F.T. and other tapping style
modalities. These are also the points that I would recommend tapping when using the affirmations
listed in this book.




1st point is the Karate chop point on the outside of the hand.
2nd point is on the very top of the head.
3rd point is on the inside of the eyebrows.
4th point is on the outside of the eyebrows.
5th point is just under the eye on the cheekbone.
6th point is under the nose.
7th point is under the bottom lip.
8th point is in the side of the ribcage when you lift your arm.
To practice:
1. Begin by simply bringing your first and second finger on one hand together and begin to tap the tips
of these fingers on the outside of the opposite hand at the Karate chop point as shown above. Tap 10-
20 times or about 30 seconds or so.
2. Then move the same tapping fingers to the top of the head and tap the head point. Tap 10-20 times
or about 30 seconds or so.
3. Move the tapping fingers down to the point on the inside of the eyebrow. Tap 10-20 times or about
30 seconds or so.
4. Move the tapping fingers to just under the eye, on the cheekbone. Tap 10-20 times or about 30
seconds or so.
5. Move the tapping fingers to just under the nose. Tap 10-20 times or about 30 seconds or so.
6. Move the tapping fingers to just under the bottom lip. Tap 10-20 times or about 30 seconds or so.
7. Move the tapping fingers to just under collarbone on one side. Tap 10-20 times or about 30 seconds
or so.
8. Move the tapping fingers to the side of the rib cage by lifting the other arm to gain access. You can
use all fingers of the hand to tap 10-20 times or about 30 seconds or so.
Ideally, tap through at least 3 rounds of the points described above to gain the best results.
If you have just taped through those points then you may have noticed that you probably feel a lot
more calm and relaxed then before. This is the effect of tapping on these points at any time and
therefore tapping on them is advisable if you are feeling anxious or stressed out in any way. Signs of a
shift in energy and mood include a slight alteration in breath (eg. a sigh), the body and head may feel
buzzy for a bit and the mind will sometime become a lot quieter on its own. After any tapping though,
be sure to drink some clean water to nourish the system.
There is a good video on you tube that shows you how to tap and where to tap, check it out if you get
the chance.
www.youtube.com/watch?v=EKHkT8dHxVY
You can use the simple technique outlined in this chapter at anytime. In E.F.T. they tend to use a “set
up phrase” when they do the initial tapping, but to keep things simple you don’t always need a set up
phrase to gain the benefits. Just know that at any time you can simply tap 3 rounds of these points to
relieve any anxiety and bring about a calmer state within a few minutes.
How to Use the Affirmations in this Book


Traditionally, people would use affirmations by speaking out aloud, internally repeating them within
the mind or writing down the affirmations repeatedly in the hope that they would stick. Sometimes
affirmations do stick with this method but most of the time they don’t. Therefore, I thoroughly
recommend that you add the tapping of the acupuncture points while saying these affirmations to
greatly enhance their potential to stick while gaining much quicker and better results. Tapping on the
acupuncture points when using affirmations works better because it instantly beings to re-organise the
system so that it becomes more comfortable and familiar with the affirmations. The tapping also brings
about a state of relaxation and when then body and mind is in this relaxed state it can absorb and
integrate information a lot more efficiently. Using the tapping method when working with affirmations
increases their power, effectiveness and chances of full body and mind integration by around 80%.
When you are feeling calm, focused and ready to work with these affirmations being by choosing a
topic or subject area that feels most relevant to you. Once you have decided on the subject area, begin
by saying the affirmations to yourself (aloud or internally) one at a time while tapping the acupuncture
points on the hand, head and chest outlined in the previous chapter. I have also added a unique feature,
that is, I have included a picture under each of the topics so that you can also include into looking at
the picture into the tapping process. Images of natural beauty have a positive effect on your nervous
system also, so be sure to also tap the images into your system by simply looking at them and tapping
the acupuncture points. Tap gently but firmly with 2-4 fingers on each point about 10-20 times, or for
30 seconds or so while saying one affirmation at a time. Then move onto the next point and say the
next affirmation and repeat the same amount of tapping. Repeat until you have completed at least 3
full rounds of the tapping. After some time and once you feel more comfortable with the process feel
free to interchange the affirmations if that feels more suitable for you. You can’t go wrong when
working with these affirmations or tapping the acupuncture points so don’t worry if you discover that
you have been doing it in the wrong order or not doing it quiet as described. Just do your best and
simply having the intention to heal yourself or to help another being is the most important and
powerful thing.
I suggest going through and tapping on the affirmations in the subject area you have chosen every day
for about 14 days before moving onto the next subject area. Giving it 14 days will really drive these
affirmations into your system and give them a real chance to fully integrate for lasting change. You
may even want to write in a diary or survey yourself by using a scale from 1-10 (1 being no stress and
10 being full blown stress) before you begin the process so as to track your progress. Take the 1-10
survey at the beginning of your 14 days and then at 7 day mark and then again at the end of the 14
days. You could also take it again 3 months or 6 months down the track to see if things have really
integrated.
Also, be PRESENT with the affirmations as you say or read them. It loses its power and potency if you
are absent minded or thinking of something else as you are practicing the affirmations - this is true for
all activities. Focus on what you are doing, be PRESENT. The more presence you give to a particular
activity or contemplation the more power, awareness and clarity it has within it.
If you find just one affirmation from the group really stands out to you and sums up what you wish to
focus on then it may be enough just to hold that affirmation in mind throughout the day or ongoing
consecutive days until it feels like you have come to fully understand and ‘embody’ it. To hold
something in mind throughout your day is a style of medative contemplation and has been practiced
for thousands of years by many philosophical traditions.
Practice these affirmations in a relaxed and joyful way. Be gentle with yourself, don’t put too much
pressure or any extra strain on yourself to change or improve. Enjoy the process of working with the
affirmations. If working with the affirmations causes you to become more confused or it stresses you
in any way then now is probably not the best time to work with them.
Overall this is resource for you to tap into whenever you feel the need. It’s important to repeat
affirmations on a regular and consistent basis, as the mind and body need repetition for it to really ‘get
inside’. I do not claim that these affirmations will make you rich beyond your wildest dreams, or make
you enjoy the job that you have come to dislike so much, all I do claim is that with the use of these
affirmations and ongoing self analysis, wisdom and greater clarity will arise from within. This wisdom
will lead you to make better choices for yourself and for those around you. Sometimes making these
choices will bring up some pain, as we may need to confront certain things that aren’t honouring our
true path, and in such cases courage and strength will be required. To walk our true path is not easy as
we will be challenged along the way so do not mistake the path to be to be an easy one full of
rainbows and butterfly’s. With patience, persistence and the support of techniques like the one in this
book you will surely succeed in the realisation of a happy and fulfilled existence. There is no time to
waste, so lets get to it.
