                       1. Shift Your Awareness
First, I would like you to find a notebook, journal, or some scratch paper.
There are journaling questions that go along with each step of the process so
you can follow along in your notebook, and create your personal journey.

Next, I want you to think about all of the things you are aware of. Really think
about it. Write down whatever comes to your mind, anything; the day you are
having, your to do list, or even the birds singing. Take 15 minutes or so before
you continue reading and let it flow!

Now, I want you to read what you have written. Circle everything that makes
you feel good, and put a line through everything that makes you feel bad. At
this point, if you have more circles than lines, you have the ship pointed in the
right direction. You just need some fuel to move it forward and allow
momentum to kick in! If you have more lines than circles, then your ship is
bobbing and weaving with no direction. It does not yet realize that you have
the power to take the wheel and point it in the right direction!

"The ultimate value of life depends upon awareness and the power of
contemplation rather than upon mere survival."

-Aristotle

Did you know that we own the power to create our life journey? It does not
happen all by happenstance, which is what most people are led to believe.
We are what we think about. This is a very powerful statement. I know what
some of you are thinking, "That’s ridiculous!" Well, I thought the same thing
until I spent some time really thinking about what I was aware of on a daily
basis, and what my thought responses were to situations that I had no control
over. As I focused more on this, I realized that while we may not have
complete control (I feel we create some control, but I will dig deeper on that
later), what we do control is how we perceive the situations upon us.
Every situation can be looked at through a negative or positive lens, and I
mean every situation! So you OWN the choice! Do you see the GOOD in that
situation, or do you see the bad?

I will give you an example.

Imagine you’re thinking about purchasing a particular car. Up until now you
have not seen many of this car around town.

Now that you’ve been thinking and dreaming about this car, every time you
take a drive you see this car. It almost feels like someone is playing a trick on
you. It sure seems like it, but no one is playing a trick on you. This is simply
called “The Law of Attraction," the idea that like attracts like, or that your
thoughts create energy that acts like a magnet to similar things in the physical
world. This Law of Attraction is the innate power that exists within each one of
us to change our lives, but most are unaware of it. This is why so many people
are unhappy and feel defeated.

You must become AWARE of what you are AWARE of! I know that sounds
silly, but the glass is at the halfway mark no matter which way you look at it.
So are you aware of it’s FULLNESS or aware of it’s EMPTINESS? I know my
choice, what is yours?

Once you have developed a shift in awareness, you will only be seeing and
focusing on the GOOD HABITS in your life! The BAD HABITS will melt away.
The speed of that will be up to you and your “Speed of Implementation.” I go
into this in detail in my 21 D ay Progr am .

Thoughts to Ponder:

- Turn off the TV. Go outside and be with nature. Yo u must stop the
distractions to create self- awareness.

- Choose to take 15 minutes a day for awareness. Take a walk, run, sit
and take in the energ y. Whatever works for you to connect, everyone has
their own way that flows for them.

- Start a journal. This is a great way to see where your awareness is. You
may say to yourself, “I alwa ys see the positive in situations," but as they
sa y, the proof is in the pudding! When yo u keep a journal, you will see loud
and clear how you are responding to situations that appear in your life on a
more sub-conscious level.

- A Positive Role Model. Think about someone that you look up to and
admire. Are your thoughts and awareness congruent with theirs? This is a
great measuring stick of how far or close yo u may be to Ultimate Self
Awareness.

- As k your friends and family if they see you as the glass half full
or half empty kind of person. Yo u can take this a step even further. I
have an incredible questionnaire for yo u to give to them so you can really
find out your best attributes and where your passions lie. You can hone in on
the skills that you are great at, instead of trying to fit a square peg into a
round hole!

                             2. Power of Choice
The Power of Choice is the most important power we control. The choices
that we make today create our future for tomorrow!

When you choose not to choose, you are making a powerful choice! You have
the option of choosing or not choosing, and when you choose not to choose
you are telling the Universe that you do not want control over your life and
future.

Basically you are screaming, “I want you to decide for me!”

“In any situation, the best thing you can do is the right thing; the next best thing you
can do is the wrong thing; the worst thing you can do is nothing.”

- Theodore Roosevelt

You must decide to commit to yourself, and take on responsibility for your own
happiness and future! No more passing the buck onto others. I have heard it
all; “I am from a family of divorce," “My family did not have enough money to
send me to college," “I do not have support," “It’s harder for me because…”
Oh, there is always a reason as to why we cannot do something, how about
creating some reasons why we can? Now that is what I am talking about!

Not too long ago, I was touting the lines above of why I can’t, so I know
intimately where you are and exactly how you can reclaim your power back.
I can show you that your entire past has been a wonderful gift. Stick with me
and I promise the gifts will show themselves. I will go deeper with the gift in
Loving Your Dirty Waters TM later.

There is NO power in passing the buck, folks! This is typical behavior for most
people, and they wonder why they feel powerless. It is time right now to take
back your responsibility, and take back your power!

Personal Freedom comes from awareness and acknowledgement of the
choices that we make. The biggest step toward this freedom is to be aware
that YOU make your choices, not someone else. YOU! Once you begin to
recognize this, you gain freedom and control over your life. This is an
incredible turning point in your journey. Recognition is an amazing tool, and
when ACTIVATED, it changes your world!

The simple step of Recognition is the key to Transformation!

Once recognition is ignited, you now start to think about the infinite
possibilities for your future, just through the power being aware of your
choices.

In your journal here, I would like you to write about the infinite possibilities
that you can see for your future at this time. Just let it flow. Whatever you
come up with, however crazy it may sound!

Your Choices Today bring Transformation Tomorrow!

The Power Of Choice can escalate you to your highest potential, or bring
you to you knees in your lowest depths of desperation! Choose carefully in
full awareness of the consequences of every decision. All the way down to
“should I hit the snooze button and sleep another 15 minutes or get right up
and add 15 more valuable minutes to my day!”

Think about the compound effect of adding 15 minutes a day, per week to
your life. That comes to 1 hour and 45 minutes per week. In one month, that
would be an extra 7 hours. In one year, you would gain 84 extra hours! By
choosing to get up an extra 15 minutes early every day, you could create just
over 2 more whole weeks of quality time in your life! That is just amazing!

Of course, the key to this extra 15 minutes is what you CHOOSE to do with it.
Make it count. Some great ways to utilize the time are meditating, listening to
good mind feed audios, or reading transformational thought provoking
material.
Thoughts to Ponder:

The key here is feed your soul and your mind! Another good option is to split
the 15 minutes up 7 ½ in the AM and 7 ½ in the PM, a great way to start your
day and end your day! The last thoughts we have before sleeping are very
important. I like the split time. I keep the flow of my day and can easily shift
my time when needed.

- Be aware of your choices. Try to recognize this in your journal, planning
how you will respond to a situation that you know you will be confronted with
so you will not react by habit. You will respond by choice! Soon, the carefully
thought and planned response will become a fantastic habit!

Take time to evaluate the choices that you are making. Ar e they moving you
forward in the right direction to succeed? If they are great, put your focus on
these and replicate them in others areas where things are not working. If they
are holding you back, then you need to go back and shift your awareness!

- Act on it. Now that you are aware of the best choices for you, you must act
on them for anything to happen. Decisions without action are worthless, it’s
like a dream that stays in your imagination. Make your choices and create a
plan.

                      3. Small Incremental Steps
It’s time to walk the walk! It’s so easy to talk the talk, is it not? That’s why
I suggest taking it in Small Incremental Steps as you go. A few people are
great at doing things cold turkey, but that would only apply to a small
percentage of us. If you are one of those few, then great, go for it! Otherwise,
think about picking one thing at a time to change, and immediately remove
that thing from your life, then think about a new healthy replacement that can
fill the void and stay with it. I have some great tools that will help you with this
step in my 21 day progr am .

For the majority of you taking small action steps is a no fail way to create
transformation. Results do not happen without action being taken. Keep in
mind that you have a choice to take action or not. Even if you say I am
deciding to do it, I just want to take action tomorrow. This is choosing not to
do it today! That is not taking action. That is called Procrastination. I know
you are familiar with this one, we all are.

Where has it gotten you? Nowhere! When we procrastinate on getting
started, inventing imaginary excuses as to why today does not work for us,
tomorrow is better, we usually stay in this cycle. I want you to shift this cycle
starting today, start right NOW!

TODAY IS THE DAY! I love that statement! I have to admit, I stole it from Mel
Fisher and the Attocha Treasure Hunters based in Key West. He made
a commitment to himself, and everyday said, “TODAY IS THE DAY we are
going to find the treasure.” He went years stating this without finding the
treasure, but he never gave up, and the rest is history! So live and breathe
that statement, TODAY IS THE DAY that you will take action!

You will be amazed how quickly transformation will occur, just by taking
small, simple steps of making GOOD choices. Like choosing to get up
15 minutes early, a simple, easily implemented choice that anyone can do.
No matter how many excuses you have, you can manage 15 minutes and it
can make a profound change in your life.

“Keep on going and the chances are you will stumble on something, perhaps when
you are least expecting it. I have never heard of anyone stumbling on
something sitting down.”

- Charles F. Kettering, Engineer and Inventor

Taking action and implementing your plan comes down to the difference
between being a winner in the race and being the one watching the race.
Do you want to follow or be the leader?

Newton’s Law of Inertia: Objects in motion tend to stay in motion, and objects
at rest tend to stay at rest. Need I say more?

The interesting part with not taking action is that you may think that you are
just staying level, but when you choose not to take action, that is actually not
the case. When you do not take action you are stagnant and you are honestly
dying. When you take action you are growing, moving, and living your life.
You are either living or dying, which do you choose? I know I am not ready for
that dying thing, I have so much more to do, and I’ll bet you do too!

Your Decisions Shape Your Destiny.

Do not allow your life to be created by default! The majority of people do
exactly that. I don’t want you to be the majority! Remember when you
were growing up, and you felt like you wanted to be in the majority and fit in,
be liked etc. I want you to switch this way of thinking right now! I want you
to become the minority, be the black sheep, you will most likely get a lot of
crap for it, but you must trust me. I mean black sheep by standing out in a
good way! This can be interpreted in many ways, so lets be clear, stand out
in an AWESOME WAY!

“Whenever you find yourself on the side of the majority, it's time to pause and
reflect.”

- Mark Twain

Those naysayers, before you know it, will start asking you what your secret
is, and I want you to pat you on the back for embracing your uniqueness! You
have now made it to the other side, and now you can help show others the
keys to reclaiming their power! How cool is that? You just thought you wanted
to transform yourself, I'd bet you did not realize that enables you to help
others! By making these GOOD choices it becomes an incredible domino
effect of fantastic opportunities and synchronistic events in your life. Are you
ready for the ride? Come with me and never look back!

"Great spirits have always encountered violent opposition from mediocre minds."

- Albert Einstein

Change is within you, not outside! You already have what it takes to
succeed, you just need to create a new plan of action. Start creating new
behaviors that are beneficial to the success of your liking. It is very simple,

Thoughts to Ponder:

- Choose one area where you want to change for the better. Write in your
journal about ways to fill in these negative areas with GOOD habits.

Sometimes it’s not so much about what you take out of your life, but more
about what you choose to put in instead. When you focus on the pieces that
you can ADD in, the results are far more powerful. You are thinking about
positive flow and everything you can have. You are not focusing on “I can’t

holding you accountable when you do not follow through. There is too much
closeness and emotional bias. I recommend someone that is where you want
to be. They have been there and done that. They know the trials and
tribulations so they understand intimately what you are going through and
know what is needed to get on the other side.

- Be Patient. It did not take overnight to get here. For some of you it may be
20, 30 or more years that you have been in your pattern of behaviors, so it
will take a little time to get you where you want to be. Don’t get discouraged if
you go off track. I would be shocked if you didn’t, remember you’re human.
Just dust yourself off and claim TODAY IS THE DAY! See it’s that easy!

- Your Highest Point in Power Is This Present Moment. Your
highest point in power is this very moment that you are reading this. What
are you going to do with it? You own it, go watch some TV, go to the bar,
or DECIDE right now to take small incremental steps toward your best
and highest self! Don’t waste the power of this moment; it has the power
to change your life forever! Come with me on this journey, your life will
never be the same, and the world will be a better place for it! I am so
excited for you! I cannot express in words the incredible life and
opportunities that await YOU!

"The man who moves a mountain begins by carrying away small stones."

- Confucius

                         4. Your Surroundings
Ok people, we need to get brutally honest here! Yes, this is what you think
it is… we're talking about your posse, your peeps, your clan! Right now before
you continue, I would like you to look at the journaling piece that goes with
"Your Surroundings". I would like you to answer the questions and fill out
the worksheet honestly. The only way you can figure this out is to honestly
assess who you are spending the majority of your time with.

"You are the average of the five people you spend the most time with."

- Jim Rohn, motivational speaker

I would not put the emphasis on who exactly, but on what are their
personalities, goals, ideals, and values. You know the saying, “You are who
you hang out with.” Well, that is true, but I will take that one step further to say
that you also will only become as good and successful as the best one in the
group. You may not like this, but if you are the best, smartest leader in the
group that you hang around with, then it is time to branch out. Your potential
within that realm is capped, and it's time to grow through that glass ceiling. I
am not saying jump ship altogether, just start spending more time with those
that are where you want and desire to be, people who uplift you, who you look
up to, that have been there and done that.

Types of People

- Negative Nellies- Everything is hard, impossible to do, they have every
excuse in the book. When it’s a beautiful day they say, “Well it won’t be
long before the rain and cold.” They never live in the moment.

- Blood Sucking Vampires- These are the ones that when you see them or
get off the phone with them you feel you need to go take a nap or have some
coffee because they took everything you had and gave nothing back.
- Sleepwalkers- These are the types that lack the “Big Why”. No drive, stuck
in a comfort zone. Life revolves around working, sleeping, partying, eating
and just being able to get by.

- Positive & Upbeat- No matter what is going on they find a way to see the
glass half full. You always feel better when you are around them.

- Driven & Successful- They are very focused, always have something new they’re
involved in. You will not find these types at the bar every night commiserating their life
stories, they may go once in awhile to celebrate their success and move along to the
next story! Normally these types also encompass the Positive and Upbeat quality as
well.

You are one of these, or maybe a combination of some, I am sure. It is time
that you decide who you want to be, and evaluate whether you are
surrounding yourself with those types of people you strive to become.

When I started to take a good look at this and make conscious choices, my
life was changed by leaps and bounds, just by making Small Incremental
Steps. Remember, I never would want you to completely walk away from your
surroundings and sever every relationship you have, or cut away every
person that does not contribute to your goals. This is especially true when it is
a member of your family. This is when using discernment and reducing
exposure is vital, rather than cutting them off completely. It just means to
start turning the ship; it takes some time to change direction. Start focusing
more on spending time with those who are in alignment with your future that
you visualize for yourself!

Now I am sure you are going to ask, "Well, how am I going to spend time
with others that I strive to be like? Why would they want to hang out with me?"
You will be surprised. First, you must be willing to put yourself out there.

You may think, "Yeah, but I am resilient! I am a positive person and that
negativity can’t rub off on me." Well, I am here to tell you that eventually we all
cave, and it happens so subtlety that we get pretty far down the road before
we even realize it. I'm sorry to say this, but it is just not possible to be that
resilient. You must make a conscious decision to choose better surroundings.
It, again, is fairly simple, not brain surgery, just a simple CHOICE we are
talking about here! And even if you feel resilient, do you really want to have
your guard up all the time and live in a sea of negative energy? I know I don't!
Even writing about it makes me squirm!


 Thoughts to Ponder:

- Decide Who You Want to Be. Now make it happen, and change your
environment.

- Shape Your Future By Choosing Who You Spend Time With. You own
the rights to your future, don’t throw those rights away. You will be the only
one to blame. I want to help you see and create a fantastic future! Infinite

- In the beginning of choosing new surroundings… I would like to

absorb their 90%.

                    5. Loving Your Dirty WaterTM
I know what you are thinking, "What in the world is she talking about?" Allow

“… down by the river… I love that dirty water... Oh Boston, you’re my home.” I

past baggage that you have had in your life, your trials, failures, and
difficulties.

Everyone lugs around their own special “Dirty Water," and some of us like to
use it as an excuse for why we are not doing what we really want in life.

It is time to take that dirty water and start transmuting it into clean,
drinkable water! What I mean by that is STOP using it as an excuse for why
you are not where you want to be! Did you know that all of this stuff, is a
GIFT that the Universe has provided to help you grow and move forward?
Yes I said it, it’s a gift! I know that some of us have had things happen that
even some of the most enlightened have a hard time accepting as a gift. Let
me ask you a question, “What is the alternative?” To be angry? Yes that is
an option, and you have every right to be. Has that been helping you? It
might seem like it's helping for a bit, but there comes a time, and I hope it is
NOW, that you start to realize that this immense anger, resentment and
blame are not hurting those who perpetrated you. It is ONLY hurting YOU!

"Resentment is like drinking poison and waiting for it to kill your enemy."

- Nelson Mandela.

It’s time to start filtering that water because soon, and believe me it
can happen, you have no clean water to drink. What happens when you only
drink Dirty Water? Your health fails and you eventually die. I do not mean to
become morbid here, but I am sorry people, THIS IS REALITY!
When you choose to live this way, blaming everything on everyone else, you
are taking no responsibility for why you are in this place. You are not living,
you may think that you are, but you are missing the whole point.

These situations have been placed in your life for you to learn from, not to
churn your anger and hate. You must release these negative feelings and
FORGIVE! Even as hard as it may be, and it could be the hardest thing you
ever do in your life, but I promise you it may be the best thing you ever do!

My 21 Day Prog r a m goes really deep with this part. I know from experience
how important and life changing letting go of baggage is.

What color is your water these days? A little murky? Well, let’s clean it up. It’s
time for some detox! We can take this in more ways than one. Work with me , I
know what it takes to detox our murky water. I have some familiarity there too!

Learning to love the bad that happens to you may be one of the
hardest things you take on in your life. For me it came in all shapes and
sizes. I was never one of those people that could skip all of the emotions
and go right to the forgiving. I had to feel the anger, resentment, and pain.
Please, if this is you, know that you are actually in a good spot, ready for lift
off! If you are actually feeling that negativity is consuming you, on a
conscious level, that’s great. Some people never really feel this consciously, it
exists in their subconscious, this is dangerous! Since they cannot feel this,
they actually convince themselves that they are fine and they have nothing
to release or no one to forgive. Unfortunately, it will rear it’s ugly head at
some point. It can show up in many forms. Sometimes it is the same
situation all over again, so eerily close to the one that hurt you. Or it may be
your health. I pray for you that your latest obstacle will be the last lightning
bolt that you keep getting hit over the head with, and you figure out what the
Universe is trying to tell you before it’s too late!

Denial is not just a river in Egypt, m y friends. It m ay actually be a river in
your own back yard. You have a choice, isn’t that the best part? You can
deal with what life brings you any way yo u want! But, the choices that
you make today either bring transformation or dirty waters for your future.

Remember, YOU choose, and don’t even think about trying to pass the
buck on this one, I think we have done enough of that for a lifetime.

                               6. Perseverance

Ahhh, Perseverance! The name that my husband and I gave our boat! Yes,
there is a story behind this, how did you guess? We really liked what it stood
for; he and I had persevered through quite a few situations together. We would
never give up. Even when we were being held under water, we would keep
coming back for air! So, we thought what an appropriate name for our boat!

I sort of forgot about “The Law of Attraction” on this one! It seemed that for a
long time after we named her, everything, and I mean everything, we did, we
had to persevere! My first advice on this is don’t name your boat Perseverance!
And, if you are wondering, we did keep the name, we just really focused on
shifting our mindset. It is actually a very cool story as to how we found our boat,
but I will tell it at another time. I use it as an example of only searching and
seeking what you want, NOT what you think you can have! This experience has
made me realize that anything is possible; I would have never believed it from
other peoples stories, I could only open that door when I experienced it myself!

Ok, so enough about my crazy boat stories. On to perseverance! It is really
about believing that every obstacle, no matter how big, small, difficult, or
seemingly impossible, CAN BE OVERCOME!

How nice is it that we have choice? You can walk away, give up and never reap
the benefits, or you can choose to persevere and see it through to the end.
Which do you think would be more beneficial to your confidence?

I would like you to do some writing in your journal. Focus on times in your life
that you have stuck to it, even when it was hard. Also touch on the times that
you gave up. Do you see any patterns? Maybe you always gave up when the
going gets tough, or maybe you always stick it out and you already have this
one licked. Let’s figure it out, so we know where we stand.

"Courage and perseverance have a magical talisman, before which difficulties
disappear and obstacles vanish into air."
- John Quincy Adams

There is no substitute for perseverance, every time that you practice this it
becomes a good habit. You make it easier and easier every time you
are faced with adversity and you don’t give up. Without Perseverance, you
will be defeated before you even start. With it, there is no room for failure.
When you decide to persevere, you will always have insurance against
failure.

I will tell you a quick story. I had been doing some thinking about my
patterns of behavior throughout my past. It came to my attention that in classes
that I have taken throughout the years, if they got hard or I lost interest, I
would just walk away! I started to think about how this made me feel. I did not
like the feeling, it felt like I lost more initiative each time, and caused other
parts in my life to feel the effects of that!

Well, I decided that I wanted to change this. Of course, when I decided this
the Universe was up there laughing at me. I decided to take an Audio
Recording class for Sound Healing. This was a great idea! I was planning on
creating healing music specific to each client that I was working with. Yes,
great idea, but I am so far from being a techie that it’s not funny. Up until
this point, it was the hardest class I had taken - nice timing, Marinna, on that
Perseverance thing! With no prior experience in music or recording, I was as
green as you get, and it was so hard!

It was so hard that I was driving my husband crazy. He kept saying, “It’s ok if
you want to quit, you probably won’t use it anyway since it is so hard and you
are not enjoying it! Why would you continue to put yourself through all this
stress?” Ok, here is the perfect example of why a partner or spouse does
not make a good accountability buddy! They mean well, but they want to
see you happy and are quick to give in, they also don’t want the stress on
themselves, so it’s easier all around.

He really was supporting me, telling me it was ok if I wanted to quit. That
was nice, he would not think I was a looser. But, there was something bigger
going on beyond what I was learning in the class. I was on the cusp of
changing my patterns of behavior! I had to follow through because I knew
that the Ripple

Effect of me persevering through this was PRICELESS to my future success,
in all that life would bring me.

Sometimes, it is not about whether you give up when you don’t see it’s
relevance in your future. It’s about showing yourself, and the Universe, that
you have the tenacity to see it through. It shifts your confidence to a new
level. Instead of sub- consciously feeling defeated, you now feel, and know,
that you CAN CONQUER THE WORLD!

I have now changed my pattern of behavior! Mission accomplished! I broke
through that silly invisible wall that I created and I tore it to shreds! Isn’t
it amazing the lies that we create in our minds, that only exist because we
thought them? Ah, the lies and excuses I have been telling myself all these
years when faced with something uncomfortable or hard. We have so much
power, when you think about it. It can become overwhelming! I know, it's
silly. I made myself finish a really hard class, but how powerful! I have now
created an incredible foundation for my future!

One piece of advice for you, be careful when you choose to make a decision
and be ready for it to be harder than all of the times prior. The Universe
knows when to test you and trust me, it will. This is just one example of the
little stuff from my life, that I came to realize was actually not so little. It’s
interesting, I have always prided myself on persevering through the big stuff.
I have become a pro at that, but the little stuff not so much, I guess I never
thought they mattered as much, little did I know...

So what is easy for you? What is the hardest for you? Are you ready to see your
life shift just by making one choice? Then persevere. It is as simple as that!

                     7. Personal Strategic Planning

Everyone needs a plan. No matter what you are doing in life. I know what you
are thinking, that is for a business Marinna, not for me! You know it’s
interesting, how much attention we pay to everything and everyone else. We
seem to allow ourselves to be forgotten in the mix. In our busy lives we lose
sight of the most important piece that makes everything else flow - ourselves!

You are your business. Let's start treating you like one, and use those
strategic tools that make businesses so successful.

Strategic planning is the basic process where companies define a plan as
to where they want to be in a certain period of time, and where best to put
their energy, money, and time to get themselves there. To do this, they must
know honestly where they stand today to get where they want to be tomorrow.

In business, this has become a habit. This process has served them well in
creating excellence and growing their business to be successful. I want you
to do the same with your own personal life, start treating it like a business.
You may be thinking, “That’s so impersonal.” Well, actually, it is the exact
opposite. By planning a strategy for yourself, you will become more intimate
with yourself than you have ever allowed, or thought possible. You don’t get
much more personal than asking yourself what you want in your life!

The other tool that businesses use with strategic planning is called SWOT,
this is an acronym for Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Threats. I
would like you to use these elements when you are creating your plan. Each
element is critical in the evaluation for your plan to succeed.

"Being busy does not always mean real work. The object of all work is production or
accomplishment and to either of these ends there must be forethought, system,
planning, intelligence, and honest purpose, as well as perspiration. Seeming to do is
not doing."
- Thomas A. Edison

This step goes beyond a “To Do List”. Let’s be real here, most of us work better under
pressure. Without dates, deadlines, and goals, it is so easy to keep saying, “Oh, I can
do that tomorrow, no problem.” I am going to challenge you with this, but I do not want
to make it so challenging that you get frustrated and give up, that defeats this whole
program. Start with looking at the big picture, but then drill down, you need to begin with
the steps that you know you can accomplish. By doing this, you allow yourself to create
momentum with the confidence to do the bigger things. This is where the small
incremental steps come into play.

"Reduce your plan to writing. The moment you complete this, you will have definitely
given concrete form to the intangible desire."

-Napoleon Hill

I could write an entire book on just this step, maybe I will one day, but we go further with
tools and processes in my 21 Day Program. You can get started by writing in the journal
piece that goes with this, it contains lots of good nuggets to get you off the ground
running! Part of the strategic planning process is just in the questions themselves and
writing it on paper. It is so important to write it down. When you do this, you take it from
an idea to a physical form, from inertia to action, and the Universe starts to listen!

"All you need is the plan, the road map, and the courage to press on to your
destination."

-Earl Nightingale

Thoughts to Ponder:

- Where Are You Now? Please be honest about where you are right now
and no fluffing. You want this to work right? It is time to fess up.

- Where Do You Want To Be? You cannot get there if you do not know
where the destination is. If you do not choose then remember the Universe
chooses for you. Let’s not live your life by default anymore, let’s start
creating your life together.

- How Will You Get There? What means of transportation will you use, a
car, train, plane, bike- so many choices. Some are fast and you miss the
scenery, some are slower and you can take it all in. The best part is You get
to choose!
