                                           Prologue




    Do you wish you were more self-confident? Gaining confidence is possible! Most experts
agree that self-confidence is a learned skill, a combination of self-esteem and self-efficacy. Why
wait? Start today, have a positive attitude - believe in yourself, your abilities, and your goals.

     Self-confidence is one of the most important life skills we need in all aspects of our daily
lives. Yet, so many people struggle to find it.

    Self-confident people like themselves, and are content with their surroundings.
    They face their fears, are risk takers, and most of the time, achieve the goals they have set.
    They recognize what they are capable of, and know how to use their abilities, to get past the
obstacles in their way.
    They see their lives in a positive light, when things go well, and also when t don’t.

     On the contrary - a person who does not have self-confidence is less likely to feel that he can
achieve his goals, and will feel negative about himself. Sadly, this lack of confidence can make it
extremely difficult for such a person to become successful.

    To increase your self-confidence, you will have to develop a positive attitude about yourself,
while simultaneously learn to deal with any negative emotions that may arise. Anyone can
accomplish this kind of self-confidence, as self-confidence is a learned skill that can be practiced
and eventually mastered. Self-confidence is something you can improve on every day. You
should learn to set goals and take risks, as life's challenges will further help to improve your self-
confidence.

    If you don't believe in yourself, how do you expect anybody else to believe in you?

     Try some of the tips listed in chapter two. Beginning today, practice them daily. With some
time and practice, you will finally begin to feel the foundation of self-confidence growing within
you.
                             What is Self Confidence?

     Self-confidence is to be secure in oneself, and one's abilities. To assign it in another way,
self-confidence is the way we think and feel about ourselves.
     This thought about yourself is changeable as your situation and circumstances are changing,
and it is completely normal to feel confident in one area, and less confident in another.

    Where do you get self-confidence from?
    Self-confidence comes from three powerful abilities that all of us possess:
    - The ability to think positive rather than negative about yourself and your capacities.
    - The ability to be certain that you have powerful capabilities (regardless what other say).
    - The ability to continue thinking positively about yourself no matter what.

    Developing and using these abilities will make you extremely self-confident.

     The difference between self-confidence and self-esteem.
     Self-confidence refers to how we feel about ourselves and if we believe in our own abilities
versus self-esteem, which refers directly to whether we value ourselves, or not. A healthy self-
esteem should lead to being self-confident.

     Where do you get a healthy self-esteem?
     The experiences of early childhood are influential in achieving a good level of self-esteem,
which will lead to ultimate confidence, but, if the experiences we had were mainly negative, we
will think and feel negative about ourselves.

   Your self esteem is formed from early childhood onwards, and could be influenced by:Your
own thoughts,
   The way how other people reacted to you,
   Media messages and experiences you had - to name a few factors.

    Your own thoughts have the biggest impact on your self-esteem, and are completely within
your control.

    Why is it important to have good confidence and self-esteem?
    Low self-confidence can restrict your quality of life, resulting in communication difficulties,
social anxiety and just preventing you from experiencing success in your life.

    There are many benefits of becoming a more self-assured person.
    To mention a few, you will have greater:
    -Self worth and more happiness and enjoyment of life.
    -Freedom from self-doubt, fear and anxiousness.
-Peace of mind and less stress.
-Lots more energy and motivation to act.
                  Ways to increase your self-confidence

    "Confidence comes not from always being right but from not fearing to be wrong."
    Peter T. McIntyre

    In this chapter, we will discuss 17 strategies which will lead to too rapid growth in your self-
confidence levels. They are all easy to implement, and will help you to become more self-
assured:

    Stay away from negativity and focus on the positive. Seriously consider getting away
from the individuals who bring down your confidence. (Remember: You don’t owe anyone an
explanation). Stop focusing on the problems in your life and instead begin to focus on positive
changes. When you remain focused on your purpose, and not your problem, you can be happy
even when life seems to be falling apart. See the positive in everything and focus on it. If you
place more energy into all that is positive around you, your confidence will soon start to shine
through.

     You have been born confident. Confidence was your original nature before time began to
wear it away. Before you started forming doubts and insecurities about how other people saw
you. A kid will keep trying to do the same thing over and over without losing hope, and without
losing confidence. Then why do we lose hope? It’s because we have unlearned how to be
positive! We were taught how to lose hope and how to become afraid before we test something
new. When you start experiencing a lack of confidence again remember that we were all born
with confidence, and we can all get it back if we learn how to do away with all the learned
behavior and thoughts causing them.

     Change your body language and image. Observe your posture, smile and look people in
the eye, speak slowly and clearly. If you look and act confident, you’ll not only feel in control,
people will hold much more confidence in you as well. Draw back your shoulders; this gives the
impression that you are a confident person. Smiling will make others feel more comfortable
around you. Style your hair, and dress nicely. This will make you feel better about yourself, and
others will perceive you as successful and self-assured. (Avoid offending media. What you read
or watch has a huge impact on how you feel about yourself. Be really particular about the
magazines and websites you look at).

     Be kind to yourself - learn to know yourself, and to forgive yourself. Identify your
weaknesses and strengths. It might help to list five things you do well that you enjoy and another
five things you are not good with but you would like to do good. Use your strong points and
improve where there’s room to grow, you’ll develop confidence as time goes on. Do not be too
critical of yourself. Learn how to forgive yourself. (Exactly like you would forgive a friend) The
next time you make a mistake, do not insult yourself. Rather, practice saying something like, “I
made a mistake, but its ok. It does not make me a bad person.

     Never give up, never give in and never accept failure. Low self-confidence is often caused
by the negative thoughts running through our minds. If you are always saying bad things to
yourself, you will become that way. Identify your negative thoughts and stop them right there.
(They will be pessimistic and unhelpful, holding you back from achieving high self-esteem) You
are getting what you are repeating to yourself. Replace these thoughts for positive affirmations.
Pay attention to your negative thoughts and then bend them around to positive thoughts. (Your
positive thoughts should be given more space than your negative thoughts) The more you
counter your negativity with positivity, the more natural this will suit.

     Expect success. Trust in a positive outcome. Don’t wish to attain success, expect success.
Confidence grows out of success… but confidence also combines an attitude shift to an
anticipation of success. It might seem unusual to say expect success as you can’t predict the
future, but pessimism can undermine your performance, preventing you from achieving success.
Instead of concentrating on the negative, focus on what you do want to happen. Think thoughts
such as, "I'm starting to get it right, I will be confident". Attempt to see the successes in every
day and you’ll notice that they will increase over time.

     Become assertive. Take up an assertive way of speaking by avoiding unnecessary nervous
chatter and giggles in your speech. You will feel how your self-confidence begins to rise, and
you will note that people will begin to take you seriously and listen to you more attentively.

    Be prepared. When you do not know what is required from you, it’s easy to get lost in fear.
Find out everything there is to know about the field you are interested in. You will notice that if
you are prepared, you will have self-confidence as well. The more prepared you are, the more
confident you’ll feel.

     Learn how to set practical goals. First, define a small goal and achieve it. Many people
realize the mistake of setting unrealistic high goals, and when they fail, they lose confidence.
Rather, set smaller, more achievable goals. Then achieve it - how will this make you feel? Set
another realistic goal and accomplish that. The more you will achieve regular small goals, the
better you’ll get, and the better your confidence. Before long you’ll be on your way achieving
bigger, but still realistic goals.

     Trust in your capabilities. There is a good deal more to be self-confident than just the way
you look. The psychological effect of trusting yourself has unending positive effects, no matter
how much other people attempt to pull you back. Everyone is good at some things and not so
well at others. If you fall short on something, realize you can get closer next time, just trust in
your own abilities. When you trust them, it will lead to self-confidence. But, you must do your
part. Do some extra effort. Take on a project that you would normally reject. Practice to trust you
and soon it will become second nature

    Show your gratitude. Create a list of all the things in your life that you are thankful for and
another list of all the things you are proud of accomplishing. What are the things that make your
personality great? (Responsibility, sense of humor, compassion, listening skills, and so on)
whenever your self-confidence takes a dip, look at this list and feel inspired all over again.

     Keep your word to yourself and others. Holding on your word is one of the most
overlooked ways to boost your self-confidence. Every arrangement you make with other people
is eventually a commitment to yourself, and if you do not follow through on it, your brain will
learn to not trust yourself.

     Accept and process your emotions. Emotions associated with low self-confidence can
often be feelings like nervousness, anxiety, tension, and fear. Take on these emotions and do not
try to fight them. Acceptance means to be aware of your beliefs and seeing them for what they
currently are, knowing that they won't last. You should be able to change your behavior and
increase your confidence. Say to yourself, “It’s natural and ok to feel stress or anxiety in this
situation" Many people do try to get rid of their emotions by means such as using drugs or
alcohol to feel better, which just makes things worse for them.

     Embracing the unknown. You will never be capable of predicting or controlling
everything that happens in your life. Even confident people lose their jobs, not only the not so
sure-footed. Remember that you are not entirely responsible for what happens in your life. There
are no guarantees, though. We will never for sure know what the future will prevail. Life is
created moment-by-moment, day-by-day. There are external factors too. If you remove this
weight off your shoulders, it’s easier to feel confident in yourself.

     Evacuate all reminders of your negativity. Do not spend time on things that can make you
feel bad about yourself again. Sit down, and think about all of the things that get you down, and
get rid of it. It may be time for a life detox, to declutter what's preventing you from feeling happy
and positive.

    Take risks. The reason that so many people never fulfill their potential is not a lack of
opportunity but too little self-confidence. You will not increase your self-confidence by staying
comfy! Make uncomfortable your friend - take risks. These very risks will build your self-
confidence. Act, despite fear, and don’t over-think a risk. Just do it!

      Identify your talents. Discover the things at which you are good at, which you also enjoy,
and then focus on it. Express yourself, and grow your interest in the subject. Adding a variety of
interests to your life will increase your confidence, and have a therapeutic effect on your life.
(More on how to find your passions in the next chapter) What can you do to be more competent
in these interests? How will you achieve you do that? You have to be committed to doing
research, and practice every day. Just a little bit at a time. The more you practice, the more your
confidence will be increased

     Know what your life values are, and live by them. What are the values that are most
significant to you - on which you live is built? You have to know these things to make certain
there is a direction in your life. A Directionless life is a life without confidence. Practice some
self-reflection and think about whether you actually live these values, or do you perhaps only
believe in them but not live by them?
    Learn to receive praise. Accept compliments from others with grace. Many people with
low self-esteem do not recognize how to accept compliments. (They assume that the person
complimenting them is lying) When you are praised for something you did well, don’t talk
yourself out of conceiving it. Respond with a positive, thank you and smile. Twist it into self-
confidence. You performed a fantastic job and it means you can do it again. It’s ok to admit
yourself to feel proud of something you did well.

     Look at the mirror and practice smiling. According to studies, the expressions on your
face can actually encourage your brain to register or intensify certain emotions. Our
subconscious is really susceptible to things we say out loud, so when we combine saying positive
statements with looking ourselves in the eyes it will have a very big effect in our minds. By
looking in the mirror and smiling every day, you might feel happier with yourself and more
confident in the long run.

    Quit comparing yourself to others. It doesn't matter how hard you try, you cannot be
someone else. Their past is totally different than yours, their everyday life is different, and how
they really are, normally is a lot different than how they are portraying it on social media. Do not
waste your valuable energy on attempting to be something or someone that you are not.
Comparing yourself to others is a direct recipe for failure and feelings of not being good enough
- which in turn will push away your confidence. Rather shift your attention back to yourself, your
goals and your own spirit. Very soon you will experience an increase in self-confidence.

    Identify, write down, and then make a start to move away from your bad habits. Begin
small, like drink more water, quit smoking, eat less sugar, cut out too much coffee or whatever
you have identified on your list. Do Something which is in your reach, that you know you can
do. Do it for a week, and then two. When you’ve accomplished it, stretch it to a month. You will
experience how your confidence grows.

     Practice confidence. Confidence, as most things in life, will improve with practice. As soon
as you discover you have self-doubting thoughts, replace your thoughts with positive ones. Ask
yourself what a confident person would do in the particular situation and just do it. How do you
currently see yourself? How would you like to be in 5 years? Change the picture of who you
expect to be to how you want it to be. Set low, realistic goals that you’ll be able to achieve. Little
by little you’ll feel more hopeful.


     Self Confidence is a state of mind.
     If you have confidence you will have self-worth and you will believe that you are capable of
success. We are totally unique individuals with different goals and dreams that can be reached if
we let ourselves grow and if we are prepared to learn from our mistakes. Everyone owns an
inside voice that leads them through life.
     Do not be prevented by your self-imposed limitations to pursue your inner voice and reach
your true potential.
                 The Last Word on Encouraging Yourself

    It perpetually helps to have a shoulder to lean on, but whether you have support from family
and friends or not, you should have the know-how helping yourself to keep going.

    The change should occur from our inside. Negative thoughts drain us of energy, leading to
an unhappy life. Positive thoughts, on the other hand, lead to magnificent good outcomes and life
success.

     If we are unhappy from the inside it will be reflected on the outside. If we are afraid of life,
if we have guilt, or spirits of low self-confidence, or even if we do not like ourselves on the
inside, then our outside life, is always going to display our unhappiness, every day of our lives.
Always and without exception.

    If we are unhappy and insecure on the inside, then we are broadcasting out negativity on the
outside. To change how the world perceives us, we must change from the inside first
    Don't take on the victim role. You create your own life, take responsibility. Remember, your
thoughts are the foundation where yourself is going to build your future on. It is within your
reach to make a change.

     You must settle your pain, anger, fear and all that is negative from within. You must
understand that you have to take responsibility for your own feelings, actions, and mistakes. You
must learn lessons from where you went wrong, and build the necessary corrections to ensure
that it won't happen again and again.

     We must work on improving ourselves as human beings every day, and very important,
learn to alter our thoughts. Living longer is only one of the advantages of a more positive means
of thinking. A Positive outlook can influence more than just your lifetime and have a great mood.
People who are optimistic are much healthier, have more confidence, are more committed to
their goals, are more successful in achieving their goals. Thus, in a nutshell, think positive and be
more satisfied with your life as a whole.

     All negative thinking is fear-based and can become very dangerous if it goes on day-after-
day, as then stress is produced which eventually damage the body and mind, resulting in disease,
illness, and depression. Depression, in fact, manifests in pessimistic thinking long before it
creates any other of its symptoms. This is why you should realize how critical the quality of your
thoughts are, to prevent low confidence, depression, disease and all the other negative things that
manifest with it. We cannot always control how we feel, but we can always control what we
think.
    Negative thoughts drain you of energy and restrain you from living in the moment. The
more you allow your negative thoughts, the more dangerous they become. One little negative
thought can turn into a gigantic snowball of repulsiveness. Choose your thoughts wisely. A
Slight positive thought can have the same effect - growing into a magnificent good outcome!

    Repeatedly choose your words and thoughts carefully, have good and positive intentions.
Be attentive to what you are stating to yourself and others, and what you are asking about life
every day.

     Condition your mind to take authority over your thought process, by changing a perceived
reality through repeated positive statements. Positive thinking is an exercise of willingly
choosing positive emotions and applying them and beliefs you may possess to create a better
outlook on life. Remember, the brain generates reality as much as it processes incoming
information - thus what you believe in your subconscious mind, will eventually come to the
surface. Good things, or bad, which will change the direction of your life accordingly.

     When you are in what appears to be a negative situation, try to find the positive in those
situations, doesn't matter how small it is. Be on the lookout for what might be good and helpful
in every situation in life. Here are Six ways you may implement to overcome negative thinking:

    1) Hold on to your faith in God. Faith is the only way we can withstand the worst
unexpected events in our lives, even if we don’t understand it ourselves, keeping us optimistic.

    2) Stop thinking in extremes: Do not guess about the future in terms of dramatic disasters
and failures. Except that most of life consists of gray areas as well; it is not always black or
white.

     3) Avoid the snowball effect: If having negative thoughts, don’t nurture it, don’t grow them
bigger and over generalize it, but instead, concentrate on how you should deal with these new
circumstances in your life.

    4) Don’t minimize the positive: Continuous negative thoughts will stop you from
experiencing the positive when it does happen. Do not minimize your successes! Remember that
setbacks are simply temporary.

     5) Focus outwards: By adding positivity to another person’s life, you too can set out to feel
better and more optimistic again. Be kind, give compliments, and simply be there.

    6) Write down your positive affirmations, read it.
    After you begin reading it, you should say it.
    When you articulate it, you hear it.
    When you hear it, you start trusting it.
    When you believe it, circumstances start changing.

    Don't be fixed in negative beliefs about yourself. This will act as chains holding you back
from accomplishing the life you want. We self, are our own greatest enemy. From the time we
were children, we were programmed by words that were spoken to us and experiences that
happened to us. The words people said and experiences we had were stored in our memory and
our mind is making conclusions about what we think we can do and cannot do. This results in a
lot of negative roles that become our life barrier which forms our identity. You may not realize
that you have this self-limiting beliefs, but they are there inside…

    Here are a few examples of negative fixed false beliefs, many people have:
    Money: "you have to work extremely hard towards financial success"
    Time: "It's too late to start" - You never to old to start with something new!
    Mind: "I am not intelligent enough."
    Social: "I cannot make friends."
    Confidence: "I am not good enough for this new task. Will never make it."

     If you suffer from one or more of these false beliefs, it’s time to make a change. You have to
get rid of negativity. If you can become aware of these false beliefs you will be able to modify
them. And by that, change your reality and quality of life. Take back control of your life right
now! Shatter these beliefs and take positive action today.
