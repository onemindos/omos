                                        Chapter One


                                      The Universe


     In the beginning, a mysterious source of energy violently broke free from total
darkness and emptiness. This event was the origin of all life. Although this cosmic
awakening began billions of years ago, it is still active within you at this moment. In this
book, we will explore how we initially live separate from this higher source of life due to
our unremitting stream of thoughts. Your mind is capable of experiencing a pure state of
consciousness, but you must awaken from a deep sleep in order to unleash its power. A
further understanding of your origins will inspire a sense of duty, responsibility, and
purpose. Right now, you can harness this energy field and realize a version of your life
that is truly alive. The source of life is trying to motivate your life into this higher state of
being.
     Many human experiences are influenced by an underlying fear. For the most part,
ignorance causes you to experience fear and anxiety and this stimulates the imagination.
Although you may not understand the source of the fear, it motivates and influences
everything about your thoughts, opinions, and beliefs. This is a living reality called the
human experience. You may formalize the experiences into beliefs and this may cause a
semiconscious state of being. You are formed from a living process; therefore, you are
subject to its intention. It seems that a higher force of will needs you to experience a
reality that is motivated by your own sense of free will. When combined with ignorance,
fear, and the imagination, free will can be either a creative or destructive human ability.
     Your mind is incredibly complex. The brain’s one hundred trillion possible
connections make it the most difficult scientific study. Within this organ, we find a
complex nature that is beyond comprehension. Human thought is supported by a complex
relationship with various mental and physical conditions. For example, as Cognitive
Psychologist Gordon H. Bower discovered, memory retrieval is mood-dependent;
therefore, your perception of reality is even influenced by your mood. As a result, many
people simply do not have the capacity to solve their problems. Unfortunately, your
mood can stimulate your imagination and this could influence your life.
     A powerful force is communicating with you using the power of suffering. A Higher
Will is asking you to find and accept the source of your suffering. Suffering is not caused
by a situation but by your interpretation of it. The extent of the suffering is determined by
your response. An incorrect interpretation will cause you to resist the situation in some
way. On the other hand, acceptance allows the situation to be just as it is. Acceptance is
the most powerful form of nonresistance. Ultimately, you will not let things be until you
experience your life, especially the part of your life that causes suffering for yourself and
others.




The Universe Within


      If you pause for a moment and look out into space on a clear night, you will be
reminded that there is something much greater than you. This sense of something greater
often surfaces inside you although you may not immediately recognize it. While looking
into deep space or deep within yourself, you will not see the essence of your life unless
your mind is free of all agreements, beliefs, and opinions. If you want to understand the
meaning of your life, you must be willing and able to challenge your conditioned mind.
      Despite the compelling ideas, theories, and beliefs about the beginning of life, no one
really knows exactly how we came to be. Many religious and scientific institutions claim
to know for certain, but this often exposes their righteous indignation for the other
theories or beliefs. In its simplest description, something spectacular happened a long
time ago and this allowed us to be. The evidence of a single event or explosion exists in
the astronomical phenomenon known as the Doppler Effect and Redshift. With a
telescope, we can see that light coming from celestial objects is moving away from us
because its light is shifted to red in the color spectrum. The redshift of such objects tells
us that they are moving outward and away from their source. Because objects in the
Universe are moving away from a source, we can reverse the events back to a single
event. This original point of origin is responsible for the form-based reality in which we
live. This orchestrated dispersal of matter created everything, including the amazing
process of life. The energy of life is observable in the galaxies, stars, planets, and all
matter down to the atomic level. This event was responsible for an incomprehensible
process that formed not only a galaxy but also the majestic rhythms of organic life. Since
the moment this event took place, life forms continue to be born, changed, and then
destroyed. The process has never ceased.
      The energy field responsible for life has flowed from a single event as if it is
searching for something. This force is the seed of consciousness trying to flower into
something new. The awakening process is seeking the fertile soil, the hallowed ground,
where all of its intentions can take place. The expansive energy of the Cosmos produced
new creations, including the seemingly irrelevant, vulnerable, and even powerless objects
of life here on Earth. Human life seems so insignificant when contrasted against the
backdrop of the Cosmos. The vigor of life flows into the accumulation of cosmic
expressions such as the galaxies, stars, and planets as if it is certain that it cannot fail at
producing a single collision where all the majesty and beauty of life can take place.
      Because recent technological advancements have provided an opportunity for
civilizations to communicate and share ideas (to express their unconsciousness), there
seems to be a quickening of the awakening process taking place. Although this book is
about the individual human process, we must also consider the possibility of a collective
breakdown caused by the expansion and collapse of many of the unconscious institutions
on the planet. We all live as a member of many unconscious collectives such as
government, politics, and belief or nonbelief systems. Since the evolving process is
motivated by human communications, any advancement in this ability will have an effect
on the collective awakening process. Although this can give rise to a shift in
consciousness within the groups, they may experience not only an awakening, but also
the destruction that precedes it. Fortunately, when a kingdom falls, the surrendered are
able to survive the collapse.
      You may lose your peace if you develop a concern about the world and its
unconscious condition; therefore, you should not concern yourself with the possibility of
the expansion or the collapse of the foundations of the world. Fortunately, a surrendered
state of being insulates you from the imaginations of a seething world. Much like the
Earth, the human being represents the fertile soil that is made ready for life. Although this
is a living foundation for an eternal life, you must first become habitable so a higher force
of will can dwell in your space. Your conditioned thoughts isolate you from this eternal
version of life. Until you give up your independent and separate identity, you will
continue to represent a being that props up a life that can never be made whole; one that
can never become fruitful.
      Incredibly, the event that sparked the formation of the Universe is trying to repeat
itself within all human beings; it is trying to break free within the darkness of the human
mind. Although a Higher Will gave humans an opportunity to awaken, this event is not
assured. The awakening process was placed at the intersection of the independent human
identity and the growing desire to find the meaning of one’s life—they must reconcile in
order to reap life. Your growing desire for consciousness is the beginning of another
episode in the implementation of a Higher Will. With this desire, your primary concern is
to align with something higher than you and allow it to demonstrate its intentions within
your life.
      A spark of energy gave birth to a Universe and its billions of stars and planets.
Within this Universe, natural rhythms pulsate with an ebb and flow of light and darkness.
The particles of matter broke free then reconciled to become the process that unfolded
into new celestial bodies such as the Solar System and the planet Earth, where you exist
right now as a semiconscious living organism. Once this energy expressed itself with
your life, you became part of an entirely new Universe. This mysterious place exists
within your living being. This is the Universe within.




A Grand Design


     From the first spark of the Universe to the first spark of the human essence, there
seems to be an intelligent and Grand Design. Albert Einstein’s dream was to discover the
Grand Design of the Universe; a single theory that explains everything. The Grand
Design is the eternal process of life. Whether you call this an intelligent design or the
evolving processes of nature, it is observable and magnificent. The splendor of the
intertwined light and darkness of the Cosmos is the reason you exist. Limitations within
the human experience are also part of this design. The human experience causes either a
rise in consciousness or more suffering. Although you may think of this as a molecular,
form-based world, the desire of the process is to allow the kingdom of atoms to flourish
into an illusion of reality. The higher force of will that motivates the atoms is the same
force that wants you to learn that this perceived world cannot bring you satisfaction—the
world of form is not a final result.
      By observing life, you probably understand that an awakening is not assured. Some
people yearn for an end result, to be complete, or to arrive at some place. You cannot
seek a final state of being as part of your surrender; furthermore, your awakening may not
be for your own individual transformation. Your life flowed out of a Grand Design. The
Grand Design is the living algorithm of the Cosmos and you are its offspring. You were
born incomplete with an opportunity to become whole, but this requires a transformation
to take place. Some people in the spiritual or self-help community often refer to this as an
“Awakening”. You cannot awaken until you recognize the impulse of a Higher Will—it
is trying to guide you. For a better understanding of this guidance, one only needs to look
to the cosmos.
      Although it is incredibly old, the Universe continues to create, change, and destroy.
The Cosmos is not complete despite the eons of continuous change. As we well know,
the human being is also subject to a process of change, advancement, or destruction.
Your life is encapsulated within the same majestic force that created the Universes in
which you live. You live in an external and an internal world. The Universe is not out
there; you are living inside it. The Universe of your life force is not within; you are living
as part of it. Once you have relinquished control and are free of mental conditioning,
indoctrination, and addictions, you will recognize an eternal version of life. The
accumulated energy that flowed outward from a single event and created every galaxy,
star, planet, and living being also intends to unfold the same creative process within you.
      You are a semiconscious being who can repurpose life energy for your own Grand
Design—a design created in the imagination. Fortunately, the imagination keeps you
from being fully conscious. I say fortunately because, as we will discover in Chapter Four
– The Imagination, this is the most powerful tool of a higher process. The inherited
imaginary world is your semiconscious disposition; it is an inherited conditioning. You
initially exist in a dreamlike state and this causes you to harness the power of the majestic
process for your own intentions. You were set free in a wilderness and allowed to sow the
seed of your own imagined reality—a dreamlike state of being. The seed that you sow
during this process produces a result, or offspring—a new life. Quite often, your creations
(thoughts, ideas, beliefs, opinions, actions, etc.) only cause more problems in your life or
in the lives of others. Only after experiencing the suffering of your own creations will
you learn how to plant a different seed.
      The human process is an exercise of giving your present state of being to the world
and then reaping a consequence. Most often, the result of your choices is more suffering.
With suffering, a commitment to making new choices, and your determination to change
your life, you have the potential to transform your thoughts and align with the source that
allowed you to exist. A seed of intent is already within you; it only needs to be allowed to
spring forth. The most wonderful thing is that you do not need to align with any group or
define a god or gods in order to participate in this process. Every human being is already
in this process and has the full potential to participate.
      When the original force of life broke free in the void and vacuum of space, nothing
could obscure, filter, or harness it. It was the only energy field, and it was good. After the
arrival of human beings, the Higher Will was split into many streams of life, much like
the way a prism splits white light into its components. The conditioned mind is a prism
that separates life into many disconnected and tangled streams of reality. This amazing
process of dismantling a Higher Will to Power is the basis of a process of life. It resulted
in the human struggle to be whole. Until the essence of your life can be reconciled with
an eternal life, the Higher Will lives inside you as a deep sense of incompleteness.
Some people believe that human consciousness makes us powerful beings who can
accomplish anything; however, humans are not always conscious. As it turns out, the
Cosmos has only created semiconscious humans. Humans engage in happiness and
sadness, peace and war, love and hate, joy and despair. The darkness in the human mind
separates you from the light of the Higher Will. The outflow of energy at the beginning
of life was not a final result; it was the beginning of a process. Flowing from this process
is the potential for an eternal version of life—not an everlasting life for your body or
identity, but a relationship with a force that is already eternal. Your light has the potential
to rejoin the light that caused your life to be.
      To find the true you, you must awaken from the deep sleep of your life. This is the
same life that you call “my life”. An awakening shows you that a self-made “my life” is
inferior. Ultimately, you must align with a higher force of will in order to expose the true
essence of who you are. A pure state of being cannot be made into a goal or achievement,
as it requires the same motivation that formed the Universe. You cannot create your life;
you can only create the self. The self is a small, needy, seething, and confused part of a
much more powerful version of you. Eventually, you will recognize the self as a
distraction. This new insight can empower you to let go of the self and surrender to all
that is, allowing all that arises to be exactly as it is.
      Transcendence is not a formal process where one can simply adhere to contrived
laws in order to find the meaning of their life. Absolute laws, such as the laws of physics,
probably do not influence your awakening process—a Higher Will must guide it and it
may do so in extraordinary, dynamic, or mysterious ways. Even the perceived laws of
physics could be altered for the process (by way of the enlightenment of previously
unknown laws). This limitation keeps you from formulating a delusional method by
which to live. This also prevents a self-righteous attitude because you do not formalize
your positions into yet another religion of beliefs. Despite the laws of science and nature,
your humble attitude about the variability of such laws exemplifies your relationship with
a Higher Will—the higher influence is your only law.
      Evidently, the ability of the human mind to discover the greatest mysteries is mostly
restricted. A common belief is that human thinking is the only way to find answers, but
consider many of the unanswered questions: What existed before the formation of the
Universe? Does anything exist beyond the Universe? What was the invisible force living
in a dark void that broke free to form the Universe and all its matter? What was the force
that caused amino acids to change into new structures and begin vibrating with the energy
of life? After millions of years of birth, growth, and permutation, we find ourselves
highly aware of our world. Despite the potential of the human mind, why do people use it
to worry, want, resist, hate, desire, possess, control, and inflict suffering on others? Why
do people also have an urge to love, share, give, nurture, create, empower, worship, care,
and surrender? Why does life often seem to be out of balance for many people? Why are
societies able to indulge themselves while others continue to have famine and struggle to
survive? After thousands of years of human development, why do people still try to
create gods in their own image? What causes each generation to continue this behavior?
Despite years of human thinking, such questions remain unanswered. The human
condition is part of an intelligent process; it is an incomplete part of a Grand Design.




Your Essence


     The force that created the Universe and its light, energy, and life, is trying to flow
through you right now. In fact, this field of life is already part of you. Ultimately, life
does not have to be created; it simply needs to be allowed. Once you are ready, a new
consciousness will be revealed. Your readiness is crucial, as you cannot force life to
happen. If you try to force life, then you do not yet realize that you already are it. An
aggressive attempt to understand will only prevent the understanding. You must align
with a Higher Will to reap your life. This requires your surrender to all strongholds such
as addictions, grievances, and all strong and unshakable beliefs. You must relinquish
your ego, your identity, and all self-seeking behaviors. You must rein in your strong
desire for success, wealth, fame, and any other endeavor that you think will make you
whole. Many ideas about success isolate you from the intentions of a Higher Will. You
must first become nothing before you can experience your essence.
     The values of most societies can be difficult to achieve. Becoming successful in the
eyes of many cultures requires unnecessary education, effort, skills, experience, social
connections, money, and a bit of luck. Since many people are under the delusion of
becoming successful, the road to success is very crowded. Ultimately, those who are
trying to “make it” eventually fail. Those who think they are successful cannot maintain
the success without great frustration. If they seem to be happy, it is only because they
have adapted to the delusion. They think happiness is an act. The only expectation of
your life is that you allow the present moment to be as it is. This state of being is the key
that unlocks your essence. You do not need to be successful to transcend your life.
     A person may seek purpose by strengthening their identity. Many experiences
strengthen a sense of self such as the experience of power, wealth, and fame. The desire
to find purpose emanates from the universal life force that is already part of you.
Although it is trying to break free, this can only happen once you give up the futile
attempt at finding purpose for yourself. Surrender is the true expression of human
purpose; however, the guidance must originate from a higher process. If you attempt this
alone using only your thoughts, willpower, and the imagination, your sense of fulfillment
will not last. Most people have an urge to do something extraordinary with their life. If
they do not fail and end up in a bottomless pit of depression and self-pity, they may learn
how to harness this energy field and accomplish many marvelous things.
     Pain and suffering is a message that something is wrong. The proper response to
pain, one that will make you worthy of the suffering, is your decision to align with a
Higher Will. A common misconception about how you should prevent suffering is that
you should join a religious organization or perform some religious act. Unfortunately,
passionate religious studies will not satisfy the responsibility placed on you. Humans
have had thousands of years and thousands of religious doctrines yet society continues to
unravel. Many people still do not know who they are. Unconscious behaviors continue to
thrive within the human experience. In the final analysis, you cannot find peace, purpose,
or essence on your own—life will find you after an honest commitment to relinquish
control of your idealized version of life.
     If you pay attention, you can feel life emanating from another person’s expressions
even when they are pessimistic, upset, or angry. You were given an innate sense of right
and wrong and it does not require your obedience to facts, laws, beliefs, or opinions. A
sense of moral direction is embedded in the thoughts of all people and it is this sense
alone that started the process of a new human state of being.
     There is an important reason for human suffering. A process was designed as a
loving way to break you and set you free. A Higher Will can guide you to purpose, but
you have to allow yourself to move through its process. This is a real will to power and
this implies a necessary process. The only thing standing in the way of your movement
throughout your awakening is the self. Your self has always been yours. This idealized
identity is your only enemy—you have no other.
     Right now, you have the ability to observe and then separate from your thoughts.
The self was formed from an inherited unconsciousness made up of thoughts. Becoming
aware of the unconscious mind will enable you to abandon the delusion of the self. The
intent is to show you the result of a world that you created in your mind. Despite its
limited sensory abilities, the mind can harness the sense of free will and use this power to
create an image. The information is interpreted in the mind and then presumed to be real.
The consequence of this unremitting mental activity is a tired mind and body. You cannot
think your way through this process. A precise timing exists for your awakening—this
may not be your time. Only you know if you are ready to surrender, give up your
perceived life, and relinquish control. You must first know yourself before you can deny
the self.




Becoming Habitable



The best way to find yourself is to lose yourself in the service of others.

-Mahatma Ghandi




     Of the billions of planets in the Cosmos, most or not habitable. If we locate a
habitable planet, we may find that inhabitants have already destroyed themselves. Maybe
they surrendered, abandoned technology, and no longer emit signals that indicate their
existence. An unconscious civilization may develop advanced technology in an attempt to
define itself. They may end up destroying themselves using their advancements. Maybe
they become frustrated with their inability to find the answers to such questions as “Who
am I?” and “Why do I exist?” The intelligent beings may not have been able to work
together for the greater good. They may have developed weapons to use against their own
species because they refused to accept other cultures just the way they are. Maybe they
could not overcome their differences caused by their religious or political opinions.
      We are quite good at using the imagination and have made huge advancements
utilizing this capability. The failures of the imagination are trying to show you that you
alone cannot find all the answers of your life. Darkness is the result of the absence of
understanding. The imagination is strengthened by ignorance; therefore, the fear it creates
becomes an important instrument of guidance. There are infinite curiosities; however,
most are not helpful and should not be a concern during your journey through life.
Although you may be curious, a search for life in the Cosmos is not necessary for your
search for meaning. A curiosity about alien life or the end of life on Earth only stimulates
the imagination into an even deeper and darker place. A conscious search for meaning
probably does not result in the evidence of life (or death) in the Cosmos—we already
know life exists on a planet.
      Science has partially exposed the instructions for life, known as deoxyribonucleic
acid, or DNA. The instructions written into the seed decide everything about the life
form, such as its size, shape, and color as well as its ability to bear fruit. DNA also
includes flaws, such as the imperfections that make the life form vulnerable to disease.
Consequently, life is required to struggle for existence. When life begins from a seed on
Earth, it is not a random occurrence. A harmony of forces influences the seed to grow. In
other words, the conditions must be suitable for life. The effectiveness of the process that
unfolds is determined by essential conditions such as temperature, humidity, the type of
soil, the variety of seed, and the season in which it is planted. Once the conditions are
suitable, the seed can germinate, grow, and generate new life. Love is integrated into the
process that supports the offspring. The seed just lies there, surrendered, not expecting
anything to happen. This is an example of the powerful state of surrender. Something
within the seed wants to break free. The seed is not pushing out life; it is drawn out by the
conditions of the Universe. In your search for life, you learn that you can only derive life
by giving life to others. Before your seed can generate an offspring, you must allow it to
fall to the ground and die. The original spark of your life came from a seed that was
allowed to spring forth.
      Despite being formed from the essence of an original set of instructions, humans
have contributed to their own organic evolution. Every culture inherits changes from the
success and failures of the preceding generations. Although you may want to be separate,
you remain highly connected to all other life forms. A powerful force is at work; it guides
everything about you especially the choices that you make as part of your free will. As a
result, conflict often arises between the real you and your ego.
      The Higher Will gave you something extraordinary that it did not give other life
forms; it gave you the opportunity for consciousness. Although you could have been
formed like other living creatures, existing in a state of surrender and presence, you were
designed to be independent. In this state, you seek to control life and its situations.
Humans must live this way for a reason. In your early life, you are only slightly aware of
your essence. This loving process of awakening requires your consciousness to be born,
nurtured, and processed into a new cycle of life. Much like a seed, specific conditions
must exist before you can spring forth, grow, and become fruitful. This force of life is
within you; however, layers and layers of inherited, self-created, and collective mind
patterns suppress its faint voice. You already contain the hallowed ground required to
sow a seed, cultivate its growth, and bear fruit. You only need to be processed out of
unconsciousness.
     The noise in your head muffles the subtle guidance that is trying to show you the
next step in your awakening. You cannot determine, create, plan, or execute your own
path since you are not in control of the process. You must be willing to change your
thoughts and begin living in the present moment. Your decisions are not as difficult as
you may think as they can be guided by a Higher Will. The voice of guidance arises
following your surrender. Once you surrender, you will cease the constant enrichment,
glorification, and pampering of the self. The Higher Will is trying to weed out the part of
you that causes your unhappiness and, most importantly, your inability to serve others.
This approach is analogous to the act of Artificial Selection, where humans attempt to
make something better by selecting out desirable or undesirable traits.
     Undoubtedly, the urgency of your awakening is not just for you; it is to allow you to
influence others. Once you begin to serve others instead of the self, you will strengthen
your power to trigger life-giving responses in them! If humanity is at the cusp of a global
awakening, it will arise from the influence of those who have already awakened. Once
you become aware of your inner power to positively influence people, you will realize
that your life never was about you—it was about others. The only way to serve is to
understand who you really are and this begins with learning about your separate self-
identity. Who are you right now?




Who am I?



The characteristics of a happy person include being sociable, creative, flexible, loving,
and forgiving - contrasted by the unhappy person's tendency to be withdrawn, self-
absorbed, moody, and antagonistic. Seeking personal happiness does not have to equal
being self-centered.

-The Dalai Lama (Tenzin Gyatso)




     The semiconscious self does not understand the Higher Will. While living in this
state, you exist predominantly unaware of an underlying process that influences you. This
slowly develops into the human condition, which frets over trivial concerns while
thinking about, defending, and talking to the self. To emphasize, you live in this double-
minded state of recognizing something deeper while wondering aimlessly in a wilderness
of thought. Until you awaken, you can only be guided by the life that you think you are.
Under those circumstances, you live unaware that you are not a static being but a
dynamic living process. With guidance, you can learn about the wondrous forces that
brought you here. A Higher Will is awaiting your decision to find your real life.
     During your brief time on Earth, you have an experience that you may refer to as
“my life.” Your life experience is often influenced by the expectations of your culture.
Although most cultures promote happiness as the fundamental reward of life, many
people find themselves trapped in a life filled with despair where they often worry,
complain, and live with many unconscious behaviors. In reflection, you may realize that
the early part of your life was mostly influenced by an unconscious society.
     Until you awaken, you are predominantly unaware of the unconscious patterns that
cause most of your suffering. A motivating force is trying to provoke your awakening;
however, until you are present, you either insistently control your life or you wander
aimlessly in a state of confusion. While in control you may find happiness, but it will not
provide you with a lasting peace. It is possible to be happy but not peaceful—this is
happiness with a sense of incompleteness. Fortunately, suffering begins to diminish once
you commit to something higher than yourself. Allowing stillness in your life will help
you align with a Higher Will because this force only exists in the present moment.




Free Will


     One of the qualities that makes you human is your sense of free will. This desire is
the basis of the word freedom. By experimenting with free will, you learn about the
things that satisfy you as well as those that cause more suffering. Eventually, you find
yourself as a wanderer in the wilderness of the human experience. Initially, you love the
material world as it gives you an essential playground where you can experiment with
free will. During this phase of your awakening, you drift in and out of a present moment
reality while influenced by your beliefs, ego, and other delusional states. With your free
will decisions and actions, you construct your identity.

source really cannot be given a name. A name or label only conceptualizes this force into
something that it cannot (or will not) be. To understanding this source, you need must
have an open mind that is free of preconceived ideas. This unknowable source cannot
surface in your life if you forge it into something that it isn’t. If you create your own
creator, you will not be able to recognize the real source of your life.
     The story of Christ being raised from the dead is a metaphor. The original Greek
definition of the word Christ means anointed. The word anointed means to be covered in
oil. Since early civilization, oil has been recognized as an essence of life. For instance, if
you press plant seeds, you can extract oil. It was understood that seeds were the container
for life; therefore, the oil they contain was often used to describe the substance of life.
Christ was said to have been anointed because he was covered in the essence of life. The
return of Christ is the moment that you also represent the essence of life. This entire book
is about trying to understand how to do that. Your life is not anointed so long as you
identify with, defend, promote, and expand a fleeting, free-will, independent, and form-
based identity. An anointing requires you to become nothing. Like Christ, once you agree
to die as a self, you also reap everlasting life!




The Higher Will


     In the beginning, in total darkness, a mighty force broke free. A spark of energy
exploded into a majestic process of light, energy, matter, life, and consciousness. Life
began to unfurl from an original source. A precise alignment of forces was the origin of
our biological beginnings as well as the recent potential for a new state of consciousness.
Within recent history, the energy, matter, and will of a grand design have coalesced into
incomplete, semiconscious beings.
     Most people have a strong desire to understand their origins. “Where did I come
from?” “Why am I here?” For the most part, the human experience is a search for
purpose. Most people feel incomplete in some way. This dreadful sense of uncertainty
has caused many people to spend a tremendous amount of energy trying to find the
meaning of their life. A belief system, for example, may attempt to describe how a higher
power thinks, how it works, and the fear and might of its ways. Undoubtedly, a human
being is not capable of completely describing a god, so this attempt usually leads to more
confusion. The narrow band of human perception is restricted and this keeps people from
understanding this majestic force. The determination to find meaning often results in a
struggle to believe. This is especially true when it comes to the conflicting notions of the
many deities created by civilizations. Their gods have been created in many images,
mostly for power and control. The act of creating a god in your own image is part of an
important process. Beware, you must be unbiased about any intuitive evidence that you
have of the existence of God. An intuitive sense is not adequate to determine who God is.
     Civilization is made of many types of collective forces. Many of them are motivated
by their insatiable desire to be right or to gain power over people. Many church leaders
are eager to convince their followers that they know God, have spoken to God, or God
has spoken to them. Throughout history, numerous religious leaders have offered their
special relationship with god for a price. Regrettably, many people aggressively promote
the existence of their version of god. A formidable force that created the Universe does
not need a champion for its existence. Its expressions will flow out of the same current of
energy that formed the Universe and not out of the creative energy of the human
imagination. The Higher Will is a genuine life-giving force because it is flowing out of
consciousness. The message is clear: To align with a higher force of will, you must
practice a present state of being.
     Since the beginning, civilization has tried to advance itself to make life better. For
example, science and technology has improved life with inventions, solutions, tools,
medicine, products, and services. Consequently, you have to maintain the advancement
once it becomes an expected part of the human experience. In recent history, we have
seen a progressive expansion around the world. As a result, modern life is becoming
complicated and even more difficult to maintain. Dissatisfaction eventually replaces
anything that provides satisfaction in the material world. In an attempt to create a better
world, human beings seem to have a very difficult time trying to be.
     The history of civilization is an insane cycle of expansion, followed by destruction,
and then a collapse. Human civilization must endlessly build upon the improvements of
the past. We have thousands of years of improvements, yet unhappiness prevails. If you
examine the history of religion, business, and politics, you find a consistent pattern of
attempting to improve a previous structure. They are never satisfied because their
structure is always changing. Although we stand on the shoulders of geniuses, build on
the lessons from the past, and continue to make incredible advancements, we always find
ourselves wanting more.
     As part of your awakening, you will learn that you should not separate yourself from
the world and its collective patterns. You cannot escape the intent of a Higher Will,
which is to help you surrender. The powerful force of will that binds together the fragility
of the world may be the surrendered beings. The “surrendered” influence others on their
own journey toward consciousness. Indeed, people need each other while they move
through this delicate process. The world and its methods are here for you to rise above
and not resist or run away from.
     A person’s determination to prove something often comes from their own disbelief
in the idea. Ideological contention leads to frustration, which is a form of suffering that is
meant to show you that something is wrong. Initially, misunderstanding this leads to
disillusionment, which may cause you to search for another answer. This partially
explains the thousands of religions around the world, the numerous philosophies, and the
inconceivable number of groups, movements, institutions, and organizations. Every
collective relationship is simply an attempt to improve or define your life by adding
something to it. Eventually, this gives rise to more dissatisfaction. The affiliations are
attractive because they help people develop a sense of self. Eventually, the activity no
longer solves the identity crisis.
     The suffering that you experience throughout life exists primarily in your stream of
thoughts. Thinking is necessary and powerful, but can only be helpful if the thoughts are
rooted in the present moment. Your inner peace can only be found through a state of
surrender. You were not given a mind and imagination to create your answer. The mind
is no more than an instrument that assists you on your journey through a process; it helps
you make a final commitment, which is to surrender the self. You cannot fix the mind
with your mind. The solution to suffering is to surrender and allow your essence to flow
through you.
     The awakening process will show you how to discard your old identity and recognize
the darkness that you represent. You are unconscious when you cannot contrast the light
against the darkness. This is a mind-made identity. To solve this problem, you only need
to accept your internal dialog as your old identity. The ego may puff up at times, but this
is only a reminder of who you were. This contrast is needed before you can understand
the power of surrender. You need to be continually reminded of your old self. The self
tries to keep you helplessly trapped in an incessant identification with form. It uses your
pain system and repetitive mental whispers to remind you of your past. Throughout the
gradually unfolding process, the self can be renounced. Keep in mind that the self may be
the person that you think you are at this moment. If you love yourself and want to keep
this person intact, then you will continue to repeat your mistakes until an event forces this
identity to dissolve.
     Since the self is responsible for your turmoil then how can it be entrusted to show
you life? When you exist as the self, you are part of a group made up of other
unconscious people. As you know, human collectives create most of the pain and misery
that exist in the world. Political, religious, cultural, and intellectual collectives are made
up of predominantly unconscious people; therefore, your first decisive decision is to give
up your associations.
     Permanent happiness cannot be found in any human creation. Many people just do
not know where to look in their search for purpose and meaning. You were given free
will to attempt a state of completeness so you could experience the fleeting results of
your decisions, which is most often more frustration. Suffering is part of a majestic plan
of renewal; it is a necessary tool to help you surrender. Your first experience of the
process comes from the suffering that is created by your attempt to avoid suffering.
Although suffering is often observed as unfavorable or something to avoid; it is trying to
guide you towards an awakening. You need the motivation of suffering to alert you to a
harmful state of being. Pain strengthens your awareness of the collective unconscious
nature and this motivates you to commit to a new decision.
     You must be guided in order to relinquish control. This opportunity is usually
presented to you following the experience of immense suffering. Transformative change
is possible but only after being caught in the vortex of mental and physical pain. Human
suffering is often a result of the desire (or need) to control things. A sense of free will
was given to you as a gift so you can experience the result of being in control of your
own destiny. Despite the fact that this experience is required for most people, it may not
be the only way. Only a Higher Will knows where it will take you on your journey. You
cannot simply imagine a place in the future and then try to get there on your own. The
steps taken on your journey must be motivated from thoughts that are rooted in the
present moment—or the removal of certain thoughts altogether. From within the solitude
of the present moment, the voice of a Higher Will wants to speak its desires and motivate
your thoughts.
     A Higher Will has always known the meaning of your life. Your purpose can be
fulfilled right now! From within a state of presence, your light will shine through. This is
the beginning of your process of aligning with an eternal presence. As a result, your new
life will spring forth as a new relationship with the force that created you. You will
understand the meaning of your life from within this new state of presence. At that
moment, you will recognize that it is not your life. You can transcend the unconscious
stream of thoughts that say “I am my thoughts; therefore, I can think my way into being.”
You must become a new creation that lives as a non-individual; one who lives only in the
present moment, gives to others, and wants only what is best for others. The result of this
new being is a life-giving acceptance of everything that arises.
     You are alive on Earth with an opportunity to reach a peaceful, humble, and present
state of being. The meaning of your life can only be understood once you align your life
with a higher force of will. The Higher Will wants to help you complete your life. You
are not a creation, but a person that is being created right now. This process continues to
produce new life because nothing can prevent the Higher Will from arising within the
human space; the human condition belongs to this will to power. You have been chosen
to relinquish your unconscious identity and find the meaning of your life. A Higher Will
is waiting to guide you, but it wants you to question everything; especially the person you
think you are. Right now, you can allow the Higher Will—you can “Let there be light.”
You really can live a meaningful life regardless of any circumstance. Once you surrender
your life, your heart and mind will unite to create a new life. Right now, you can abandon
your self-seeking identity and spring forth as a selfless spark of the light of
consciousness.
