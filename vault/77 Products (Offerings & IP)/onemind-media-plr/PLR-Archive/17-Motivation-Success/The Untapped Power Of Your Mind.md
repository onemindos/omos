# The Untapped Power Of Your Mind

                      The Untapped Power Of Your Mind


The law of attraction trend has been getting bigger and bigger in recent years and more and
more successful people are emerging and supporting that the law of attraction does indeed
work.

The most powerful component in this whole thing is your mind. That is what in essence controls
your life. You hear a lot of successful people say that you can do anything you set your mind to
and when you think about it, it’s true.

There is no doubt that it is great that this type of thinking is becoming more popular in today’s
society but one problem that has come about is that explanation has been over simplified to
make it more appealing to the main stream audience. The counter problem to that is that
sometimes the law of attraction can be over complicated and become confusing.

This is why so many people try this stuff and ultimately fail. There is just so much information
out there today and only a small portion of it is actually correct. So it’s very easy to get
overwhelmed with the amount of information especially when you don’t know what is accurate
and what isn’t.

If you want to achieve success in any area of your life and use the law of attraction to your
advantage then you first need to have a basic understanding of what it is and how the process
works and you also need to follow a few key principles that will ensure your success.

The purpose of this guide is to clear the confusion and give you a solid understanding of what
the law of attraction is and give you some powerful tools so you can start using the law of
attraction in your life today.

Think of this guide as a quick start guide to the life of your dreams.
                              The Power Of Your Mind


Your mind is such an important component in your success that we need to spend some time
on it. As humans we are gifted with the most advanced brain on the planet. We are capable of
doing amazing things and we have a unique ability to use the resources around us to create
great success.

This is an important area so it’s important to not skip over it. You really need to understand just
how powerful your mind is and just how much CONTROL it has over your life.

Most of us live on autopilot. We go about our daily lives mainly doing the same exact things.
Our mind has been set to autopilot and it controls our lives for us. When this happens we get
stuck in the same pattern, we get stuck in the same loop and it just happens over and over
again.

To stop this from happening you need to take your mind off autopilot and put you back in the
driver’s seat. When you are in command that’s when change can take place and change is good.

Now let’s just think about how powerful the mind actually is and how when you break it down
it literally does control our lives.

Our physical bodies will not act unless it is instructed to by our minds. Really think about that
for a second, we do things based on our decisions. It doesn’t matter if it’s a big decision or a
small decision our physical bodies will only take action if our minds allow it.

A good example of this is the story of the identical twin boys. One of them grew up to be an
extremely successful billionaire while the other was a bum who lived on the street.

Now physically there was virtually no difference. Identical twins are the closest two humans can
be in terms of physical appearance. But yet one of them was wildly successful while the other
was wildly unsuccessful.

They were physically both the same but they had very different minds. Externally they were
identical, stand them next to each other and you couldn’t tell them apart but internally is where
they were radically different.
The billionaire had different beliefs, a different mindset, a different outlook on life and
obviously knew how to use the power of his mind to his advantage. While his twin brother had
a more negative set of beliefs, mindset and outlook on life and obviously didn’t know how to
use the power of his mind to his advantage.

However both men were using the power of their minds. One was doing it in a positive way and
the other was doing it in a negative way.

Here is a great quote from Henry Ford:

“Whether you think that you can, or that you can't, you are usually right.”

That’s how most people live. They have a set of beliefs that tells them what they can or can’t
do. They place limitations on themselves and believe those limitations as if they were absolute
fact.

People do this with money all the time. They might have a belief that says they are only a
$30,000 a year person. That’s their limit, they are comfortable with that amount they believe
that is realistic and achievable for them.

So remember the mind controls the body, so the mind will instruct the body to do the things
required to reach that $30,000 a year belief. That person will continue to make that same
amount of money year after year until they change that belief.

If they believe that $50,000 a year is too much money, they don’t feel comfortable with earning
that much and it’s a firm belief that they can’t earn that amount of money they never will.
Because the mind will never instruct the body to do the things that are required to earn that
amount of money.

This applies to the other end of the financial scale as well. You might have someone who lives in
a million dollar house and is completely satisfied and comfortable with that and has a strong
positive belief about it.

However that same person may have a limiting belief about living in a 5 million dollar house.
They might have the belief that they are only a one million dollar home owner and that 5
million is too much money and out of their league.

It’s those limiting beliefs that control us. It really doesn’t matter externally what the belief is it’s
internally that counts. The person with the million dollar home would look at the 5 million
dollar home the same way as the person making $30,000 a year looks at the person with the
million dollar home.

The numbers aren’t important it’s the beliefs that hold us back.
We are all PHSYCIALLY capable of becoming millionaires. There isn’t a physical requirement that
says you need to be talk, blonde with blue eyes to cash a million dollar check. It doesn’t matter
what you look like or how you are physically built it’s the power of your mind that will get you
that million dollar check.

Our bodies will do whatever our minds tell them. They are not scared, they have no fear.

A lot of people will get drunk so they can do things they are too scared to do sober. They alter
their minds for a moment so they can bypass that negative belief and do something that they
couldn’t do sober.

However your physical body doesn’t care, it can do that exact thing whether you are drunk or
sober.

Public speaking is a great example. A lot of people have the fear of public speaking but when
you really break it down logically everyone is physically capable of talking in front of a large
group of people.

We have the physical tools to do that. We can walk on stage, use our hand to hold the
microphone and use our mouths to say the words. Physically we are capable of doing that task.
It’s how we think about it in our heads that makes the difference.

I personally have the belief that I am a very good public speaker. This is simply because I told
myself that I was. I got asked once to speak at an event which is something that I had never
done before but instead of freaking out about it I just assumed I would be good at it and I was.
The speech went well, I had the audience engaged, I was funny at the right moments and I
presented well.

That didn’t happen because of luck, it happened because I told myself it would happen. I went
in with a strong belief of that I am really good at giving speeches.

I have since done many speeches at various events and I have a strong belief that I am very
good at doing them and since I have developed that mind set every speech I have done has
been a success.

But I know people who totally freak out about giving speeches. They get nervous, they stumble
through it and get off stage as quickly as possible. I could literally write their speech for them
and they still would fail because in their minds they believe they are horrible at giving speeches.

It’s the same with anything.

If you have the belief that your horrible with money and are always broke all the time then that
is exactly what you will be.
I have a female friend who I have known for a few years now and ever since I have known her
she would always tell me that get’s “played” by guys. It’s her belief that for some reason every
guy she ever meets is going to use her or cheat on her or manipulate her in some way and that
is exactly what happens.

The reason that it happens is because it’s her belief. She believes that’s what guys do to her so
she is constantly attracting those types of guys. Her mind instructs her body to do the things
required to attract those types of guys. Whether she wants it or not doesn’t matter. It’s a
strong belief so it becomes a reality.

So it’s really important that you understand the importance of the mind.

I wanted to give you some real world examples instead of some airy fairy hoo ha so you can see
just how powerful the mind really is and how much control it does have over you.

Have a look at your life and ask yourself if you have any limiting beliefs. Do you feel you can
only earn X amount of dollars per year?

Really challenge yourself here and discover what your limiting beliefs are. Then once you have
found them work on changing them to positive beliefs.

I’m going to leave you with a great quote from Earl Nightingale:

"You Become What You Think About"
