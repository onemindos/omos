**The 5 Characteristics of Confident People**

Do you often look at a person that exudes confidence in everything that
they do and think to yourself -- "I wish I could be more like that
person? I hate being constantly afraid of failure and worrying about
what others think of me. How do they do it?"

Well, the good news you are not alone. Many other people in the world
suffer from a lack of confidence, and continue to suffer simply because
they believe there's nothing they can do about it.

But this couldn't be further from the truth. Self-confidence is
something that you can foster and grow within you until one day; you
will feel like a completely different person with a new lease on life.

So if you, like most of the world, can relate to the above, then here
are the 5 characteristics of self-confident people that you should start
to cultivate within you.

**Self-confident people believe in themselves**

Confidence comes from within. If you believe in your ability to make the
right decisions and choose the right path, then you are well on your
way. This does not mean you should stifle others around you with your
beliefs, but instead quietly stay firm on your own ground, trusting your
instincts and knowledge you've gained from living your life.

**Self-confident people learn from their mistakes**

Far from what you may believe, a confident person does make mistakes,
too! The only difference is how they deal with their mistakes. Instead
of dwelling on their inadequacies and how things could have been done
differently, they focus on the future and use the experience as a
reminder not to repeat their blunders.

**Self-confident people don't compare themselves with others**

This is a big problem for many of us. What we don't realize is that if
we continue to compare, there will always be people who are faster than
us, have more money than us, and are better looking than us. The key is
in realizing your own strengths and weaknesses, and knowing that other
people have their own, too. Instead of wishing you had something someone
else does, it's much more productive to focus on your strengths and get
ahead!

**Self-confident people believe they have the right to be happy**

A self-confident person will always go after what he or she wants,
regardless of the obstacles that lay ahead or the critics they may have
to endure. This is because they know that they have the right, just like
everyone else in the world, to fight for their happiness and to follow
whichever path they believe will lead to it.

**Self-confident people are not stubborn**

Although they have great faith in themselves, their ability and their
decisions, they also know when to take advice and when to change course
if necessary. They take the time to see things from the other person's
point of view, instead of simply disregarding it, for they know the
importance of having a balanced perspective.

I hope the above insights will help you move forward and look at life
with more drive, enthusiasm and confidence. The road will not be short
or simple, but it will be worth it.
