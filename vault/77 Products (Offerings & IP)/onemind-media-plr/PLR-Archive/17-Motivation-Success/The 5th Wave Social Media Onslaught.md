                            Chapter 2:
                   Social Media And Network Marketing


                             Synopsis
Social media has a crucial connection with network marketing. Social media
marketing uses the same techniques and strategies of the traditional way to
market a product or service. However, the execution of these two forms of
marketing is being done using different tools. The social network marketing
purpose is more on the establishment of a long-term relationship between
you and your prospective clients by means of non-commercial interactions.
So, instead of paying more attention to the sales output achieved, the goal of
it is to enable a belief or behavior change. To understand the role of social
media in your marketing process, you have to know the benefits that it can
provide to your business.




The Benefits of Social Network Marketing
                                      6
Increased Awareness about Your Brand and Offerings

The first benefit of social marketing is that more people will know your brand
and the products or services offered by your business. Based on statistics,
there are over 85% of internet marketers who said that their effort in social
networking increased the online visibility of their companies. With social
media marketing, more people will be aware about a particular product and
the name of the products you offer will be recalled by your potential
customers easily.

Increased Web Traffic Generated to Your Website

Increased web traffic is the next main benefit that you will get from using
social media in promoting your products or services. With social media, the
ranking of your website will get more chances to increase and it will have a
great effect on the online exposure of your business. As the posts featured in
the networking sites you use like Facebook, Twitter, Tumbler and many more
have a link that will lead the online users to your website. There are
possibilities that your website will get more visitors which means increased
traffic to your site.

Improved Search Engine Optimization

For each discussion about your company, its products or services
accompanied with the right web links, your website will gain a better ranking
in search engines for every search made using the keywords related to your
website, products or services. This means that there are lots of online users
who are viewing the content where your link is added. This may also
encourage them to visit your website if they clicked the provided link.



                                      7
Therefore, other internet marketers value social media marketing as an
important aspect of all Search Engine Optimization strategies.

The links will be assessed by the search engines to find out their connection
and relevance to your business website. The search engines focus on the back
linking process of the users who share the content involved.

Improved Customer Loyalty

The best approach to social media marketing will not only promote your
business but will also contribute to the development of loyalty of your
customers. As shared posts are personal, companies send persistent and
realistic message by using a valuable and useful content. Therefore, your
potential customers will depend on that and they will start to trust your
business.

Instead of selling, other internet marketers prefer to promote themselves as
a reliable resource or an expert. This will be done by providing help and
support with no commercial intentions. Once they are able to do this, the
products or services they want to advertise will be promoted when they
learned that it is the right time for them to do so. This way, you are sure that
the emails sent to your target customers will not be discarded or recognized
as spam messages.

Successful Launches of Products or Services

Social networks offer a more convenient platform where you can monitor the
applicable community for your products’ needs and wants. You can make a
product wish-list without the need to invest more and your product could be
developed with the supervision and participation of the community.


                                       8
Forging New Relationships

The time frame, quality and number of interactions affect the total
partnership inquiries you may get. The businesses which are participants of
B2B selling instead of B2C have more chances to get this benefit.

Reduced Marketing Expenditures

The advantages that internet marketers can get from properly using social
media websites are plenty and these are achieved when there is high ROI.
The online marketers look at it as the crucial initiative even if the efforts take
more time to give successful results. The main advantage here is that the
advantages percolate into the other aspects of internet marketing which will
result to tremendous ROI.

As you can see, social media plays a very important role in product or service
marketing. It makes a faster and deeper effect to your target clients while
maximizing the popularity of your company and the products or services it
offers.

               The Success Measure For Network Marketing


                             Synopsis
Social network marketing success takes time to happen; and to achieve this,
you have to bear clear and measurable goals. The problem is that the
measurement for social media would be the rate where the medium evolves.
There are some new websites that appear continuously and they use well-
established social networking websites. As the new websites are visible, more
of their data will be available to product or service promoters.




                                      20
This massive volume of data will become a problem due to the fact that most
of these are fairly unimportant. The worst here is that the website appears
like something that provides unrealistic information. The most difficult
challenge for online marketers who use social media is that they need to
effectively sort the massive data available.

Usually, the social networking marketers are enticed to use the easy-to-track
tools to measure the level of success of their team. These include the number
of their followers, subscribers, blog posts, fans and many more. You are not
allowed to give more attention to any of these and other absurd metrics. Here
are the 9 metrics that will help you in measuring the level of success you
achieved.

9 Metrics to Measure Success in Network Marketing

    Conversation rate – There are several companies and brand names that
      are proud of the number of their followers on social media websites
      such as Twitter and Facebook. They tend to do it without providing a
      specific and tailored measure that proves that they gained success.
      Social media, compared to television, will provide you with real time
      comments which will let you know if your followers are interested in
      your activities. The simplest way of measuring the degree where the
      post resonates with the target clients is to find out the total count of
      replies and comments provided in each post. This is referred to as
      conversion rate. Yours will be high if you really understand your
      audience and your brand is well-defined. There is no way to manipulate
      this success metric because this will force you to do what is right.
    Referring traffic – Your own website acts as the internet base where
      your accounts on various social media websites direct web traffic as

  well as potential customers. In the same way as traditional marketing,
  you will find a social network marketing mix and it should consider the
  significance of the different satellite accounts such as LinkedIn, Google
  Plus, Facebook and Twitter. Evaluating the web traffic flow that goes
  to your own website coming from your own satellite accounts would
  assist you in understanding what social media points will be the most
  important ones.
 Total reach – Estimating your own total network has a very significant
  role as it will assist you in determining whether you added value within
  the social media world or not. At the most primary level, the bigger
  reach would be important to lift the probable content spread.
 Amplification rate – The main benefit of the social media in promoting
  the products or services of your business is the secondary network
  aside from the network you own. The amplification rate you own would
  be the rate where your followers will share the content you own with
  their own websites. It basic when it comes to measuring the level of

  Facebook and the number of shares will be used on the blog posts you

                 The Dos And Don’ts In Network Marketing


                              Synopsis
Aside from the details you have learned in the previous chapters, you are also
obliged to know the things you need to do and those which you must avoid
as a network marketer. By knowing these things, you will get more chances
to achieve success as you know what to do and what must be prevented.
Likewise, it will be easier to avoid mistakes that will lead you to nothing.




Here are the dos and don’ts in network marketing:

                                       25
Dos

   Make sure that you know the regulations as well as the legislations
      applicable on internet marketing. You need to comply with them.
   Make a comprehensive research about the networks where you would
      like to join. Find out and understand the persons who are members of
      your target networks and the marketing messages that they accept.
   Develop the metrics that will let you track and evaluate the amount of
      gains you get from your marketing campaigns.

Don’ts

   Make assumptions regarding the people or business of your targeted
      networks. Marketing messages which are effective are the ones where
      you should focus your attention more.
   Send your fellow internet users with spam messages. In online
      networks, the quality should be more favorable instead of the quantity.
   Sell or pass on the personal details pertaining to the persons or
      businesses from your target marketing network without asking their
      permission.

The Best Strategies Used in Network Marketing

   Content quality is the ultimate key to achieve success. You have to
     share informative articles, original photos, polls and games. Some
     updates must be something about the products and services you offer.
     On the other hand, all things that you want to share should appear as

     interested in. This may include the blog articles as well as web pages

     sharing the links that are also involved in the competition.
   Update the social network accounts you have regularly. Refrain from
     sharing too many information but you should try to share at least 4
     updates every week. You could share additional shares when you notice
     the most subscribers are fond of using the social media websites for
     longer hours. When possible, you should posts the updates when the
     subscribers are online.
   Look for the best means to encourage your subscribers to join the
     campaign. Try to organize the contents and provide three products as
     gifts to your customers who share their photos when they use your
     products. It is the best way to get in touch with more audience as their
     friends would have the access to the shared photos.
   If you are in need of more persons to subscribe, you may consider
     incentive offerings. For example, you may offer an instant discount to
     a subscriber or you can use the social media websites to disseminate

  the details pertaining to the discounts. There is no reason for you to
  invest more in this campaign but the small incentives like free sample
  and 50% savings would make a bigger difference in almost everything.
  If you do not have enough resources to provide samples or discounts
  to your customers, you may conduct a contest and ask them to connect
  to you using the social media.
 Measure the success you are getting by keeping a track of the total
  number of the customers who follow the links you shared on social
  media websites. Also, make a separate record of the people who
  redeemed the coupons you posted online. The conversion ratio has a
  very important role in the total number of subscribers your website
  has, though the total of the subscribers is important to generate higher
  sales outputs. Quality is the main priority and the volume will become
  a problem when you share the content that is extremely appreciated by
  the audience.
 Make an online search to know more regarding social network

  results and find out the total time frame you need to spend on the social
