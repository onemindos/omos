                               Introduction

Do you still remember the time you discovered something new every day. As a
child everyday was a new adventure for us but when we grew up life became
quite predictable. We know what we can expect when we start reading a book
about personal development for example. We’re ready to read all the so useful
information about what exactly we can do in maybe 10 specific steps to solve
our tiny problem. As far as I’m concerned this whole concept really sucks!
Sometimes the problem we have or the aspect of life we want to change isn’t
really what we think it is. All the books and magazines we can read about topics
like success really do help us, I don’t doubt it at all, because they provide us
solutions for real problems (assumed that they are good magazines/books).
Unluckily these books/magazines can only solve tiny problems and not the
biggest problem of all which is how human beings can be in control of their life
and solve problems independently. Every problem has a solution is the major
principle of success magazines and book. That’s definitely right but it seems for
me like we don’t have to know a solution for any problem and instead should
read more about the basic principles of how you can figure out the solution
yourself. Or how is it possible to solve a problem in your life when you don’t
even know what the exact problem is? These are just a few questions many
people keep asking themselves and I think that it’s time to give them a
guideline. This book is intended to be the guideline I’m talking about. Don’t
read it when you’re just interested in quick fixes or scientific answers for
problems because this book evolves about principles on how you can deal with
aspects of your life. It’s possible to interpret a lot between the lines and I tried to
write many questions in it you can ask yourself to really make you think about
what the book is meant to teach you. You can either read this book the same
way you would read a fantasy thriller and hate it as soon as you’ve finished it, or
you can take some time to think about the content and apply it to your life. One
chapter per day and some extra time to think about what you read and maybe
you will learn something out of this book that will change your entire vision of
life.
Enough talking for now. Just one little annotation before you start: Don’t focus
on the correct use of grammar or punctuation, I’m not a native English speaker
and the main purpose of the book isn’t to teach you grammar but to inspire you
to see your life in a different way than you ever saw it before.
           The Decision – Action relevance


Did you ever hear the idiom „Decisions shape destiny“. Most people are very
familiar with this phrase and heard it so often that they don’t even want to waste
a single second thinking about the actual meaning and importance of it. Even
right now you probably wonder whether you should really continue reading this
chapter or even the whole book because you might expect me to tell you some
information which you’ve already heard a hundred times before.
The point is that I want to present you the conflicts and interdependence
between actions and decisions from a different perspective. I hate it when people
try to waste my time it’s like stealing a part of your life so trust me that I
honestly don’t want to be responsible for any waste of time. You’ve already
decided to start reading this chapter and you did read the first few sentences so I
encourage you to continue reading in case you really want to figure out how you
can control the decision – action relevance (that’s how I call it) to improve your
life dramatically.
Well, before we jump right into the section where I will show you ways to
control the complete process of decision making and action taking it is necessary
to understand the entire concept of what a decision really is so that we are able
to understand the principles outlined in the following text.
From a chronologically perspective decisions represent the beginning point of
something. Did you notice how I wrote down “the beginning process of
something”? Most people immediately think that decisions are the beginning of
an action but this is not true. In case we lack the necessary amount of motivation
or just feel way to comfortable doing whatever we’re doing right now the whole
process starts and ends with a decision. No result, no action, just a decision. In
this very sad case the relationship between our motivation and our outer
conditions stops us from even getting started with at least some kind of an
action. Just imagine you saw a video about someone doing incredibly skydiving
moves. After a few thoughts you decide that you want to learn skydiving
without even knowing what it requires and how it works. Before you even do
something you realize that in order to learn skydiving you need to get up. But
your couch is so comfortable and you’re just tired because you had a huge fatty
meal. At this short moment of your life you feel way to comfortable to perform,
instead you decide to sleep first and give your decision second thoughts. The
moment you wake up your initial motivation caused by a video or any other
media is gone. You don’t even remember why you wanted to learn skydiving.
Probably you were just influenced by the video but now you’re influenced by
some more important events or circumstances of your life. That’s why you
forget about your decision and continue your life like nothing happened.
That’s a sad story, isn’t it? Obviously I chose a very unrealistic or rather
extreme situation to illustrate the problem for you. Whatsoever, the truth is that
in our life tons of decisions just end up the same way like the one in the
example. Can you still remember being a child? Most likely, like every other
child, you dreamed about becoming a lawyer, doctor or policeman. In case you
really have your “dream job” right now that’s rather extraordinary than normal
because so many people just forget or reconsider their decisions.
Now let’s get back to the initial thought that decisions are the beginning of
something. You can consider a decision as the direction you’re heading to, the
action is walking towards the direction and the result can be your chosen
destiny. From an economic perspective it definitely makes sense to spend some
time thinking about your decision otherwise you may walk in the wrong
direction and have to take a lot of detours to arrive where you want to be. If you
think that all the decision planning doesn’t make sense because you can adapt it
anytime on your journey you still need to remember that actions can’t be
controlled as easy as decisions because they depend on circumstances you can’t
always control. If success was an equitation don’t try to make decisions a
variable. You’ll end up solving a problem/equitation with two variables which is
way harder than an equitation with only one variable and a constant, the
decision. Even if you like solving math problems I wouldn’t recommend that
you waste a lot of energy just to do something relatively easy in a difficult way.
So the question should be: How can I determine my decision in a way that I
really stick to it and don’t just forget it whenever it gets difficult to put actions
behind the decision? My favorite strategy to prevent me from wasting my time
with useless entertainment and instead feel the urge to do something, to become
active is based on (how I call them) pre-determined constraints. We all know the
kind of situation when you are feeling really good and at that moment pressure
doesn’t even exist so that there is no real constraint for us to start working on
our dreams which we actually decided to be our priority. This situation can be
seen as a civilization illness. When humans had to fight every day to survive this
problem didn’t exist. We are just feeling too comfortable in many situations
nowadays. Instead of doing something reasonable we just hang around watching
for example another episode of Two and a Half Men and try to calm down by
thinking that tomorrow is another day and we have enough time left. Whenever
we think that actually this is the perfect time to work. I don’t want to disappoint
you right now but there’s never really enough time for anything. The person
who thinks that he or she has enough time for everything just doesn’t have
enough to do. Tons of movie quotes exist about this common misbehavior. My
favorite one is from the movie Fight Club, it’s “This is your life and it ends one
minute at a time”. Just think about this quote. Isn’t it really ironic that most
famous quotes on how to live your life without wasting time are taken from
movies which actually represent in most cases just a method to rather waste your
time than to spend it trying to improve yourself. If you don’t believe me just
answer the question how many movies you’ve ever watched approximately and
how many of them did contribute to any kind of success or improvement in your
life. However, especially for the purpose of improving yourself Fight Club is
one of few movies which really make you think about your life in an
extraordinary way. That’s why I recommend you watching the movie after you
read the chapter three. Let’s continue talking about the “pre-determined
constraints”. The word itself doesn’t seem to make a lot of sense and maybe
doesn’t even make any sense at all. The important information you should know
is how the whole concept works. At first I want you to think about something
that’s really important for you. Something you don’t want to risk and you don’t
want to have less of it. Money is a very good example and fits for most people
as well as in my case. I strongly recommend that you write down what kind of
object you chose. The next step is to figure out what exactly you want to
improve in which period of time. For example you want to lose 10 pounds in 1
month. Do you still remember how I told you that decisions often don’t lead to
any actions as soon as you have to do something unpleasant? The solution is to
simply make a decision unpleasant. In this specific example you give someone
you might even not like that much 500$ the first day you want to start your diet.
Tell the person that he can keep all the money if you don’t lose at least 10
pounds in 4 weeks. What happened now is that you completely changed the
situation. Instead of evolutionary pressure that made us hunt animals in order to
survive during the Stone Age you created your own artificial pressure. Now
every time you see very tempting food you probably would eat without the 500$
insurance you think about how horrible it would be to lose all that money you
had to work for so long. Needless to say, don’t create this artificial situation with
something you don’t care about and don’t make a person responsible for your
success who likes you too much. That’s why I told you in the example to give
the money to someone you don’t even like. This way another source of
motivation for you is that you don’t want the specific person to get your money
and you can’t negotiate with the person to get your money back and stop the
deal. I’m not telling you that there is a magic pill which helps you for instance to
lose 10 pounds in one month. The only way to achieve greatness is to do great
work. That’s it.
If you want to read about another example on how to use this principle you can
continue reading this passage. In case you’re already convinced that this
definitely works just skip the passage. Now what I want to tell you is a story of
my life that really happened. After I finished school I was working at a
supermarket for 2 month about 45 hours per week. Maybe I was just a lazy kid
who didn’t respond very well to monotonous work and was just lazy but the
only fact I’m sure about is that sitting 6 hours on a horrible, uncomfortable chair
moving thousands of objects over the counter whilst pretending to care about the
costumer just made me go crazy after the first few weeks. I used to wake up in
the middle of the night with spasms in my legs although I never had even one
single spasm before this job. Well you might be wondering how spasms are
related to the typical work in a supermarket I can only describe you the situation
which lead to it. Imagine sitting on a chair without backrest in a way to small
niche. Especially during the weekends there are unlimited costumers you have
no chance to just stand up for a few seconds in between the annoying repetitious
process of moving nutrition over the table and counting money. Even from a
medical perspective it’s even dangerous when you don’t stretch, flex or move
your legs for many hours because of our blood regulation system. As you can
see that’s an awesome job. If you are working under similar conditions it’s
really time to change your life, trust me there’s literally only space for
improvement. Whenever someone’s ill you can work for example on Friday
from 12.00 to 21.00 and continue on Saturday from 6.00 to 16.00 which is a
torture. Should you be doing this job for 9 euros per hour? Well it’s not the topic
of this section to answer the question but you should definitely think about it.
Now back to the decision making. After such an experience it becomes quite
easy for you to use money as your own weapon. I always wanted to try out
social media marketing but never even started because I didn’t have the courage
to take risks. But the decision totally deteriorated as a result of my experience in
the supermarket. Suddenly I realized that I don’t have enough time for
everything and that doing nothing can never be accepted. So I just bought an
online gig on fiverr.com about a logo design. I knew that I would need an
awesome logo to start making social media marketing experiences. At this point
I had no idea on which social media platform (twitter, facebook, pinterest…) I
wanted to start, about which topic etc. To sum it up I pretty much knew nothing,
BUT I knew that I would lose some money because I already spend it and
getting nothing for it was no option. So I kicked my ass and came up with the
complete concept of my online presence in a few days. Without the money I
spend on it this process would either have taken weeks or nothing would have
happened.
There are also many other ways that really help you to stop doing something you
actually decided not to do any longer. Can you come up with one? I’m sure
you’ll find many if you just think about it for a few minutes. One strategy I
certainly want to describe in this book could be called the BIG BROTHER
method. It’s based on basic human psychology. This time I want to try to prove
the success of the strategy at least in a few words so that you still consider my
opinion to be relevant. The French author John Paul Sartre who wrote several
books about existentialism and our human nature wrote a book called “Huits
Clos” in which the effects of human observation are described. I had to read this
book at school so I really know that it can be quite confusing at some points but
the hidden truth in this book is certainly true. When we are observed by others
we can’t just continue doing whatever we did before. We don’t want others to
have a negative opinion about ourselves and it can even be a torture to be
evaluated by someone else all the time who doesn’t want you to succeed. Do
you already have an idea in which way this findings can help us to make
decisions more relevant? I hope you do. There are tons of possibilities for you to
use this knowledge. Just consider the difference between telling everyone else
what you just decided to do and only deciding something for yourself. You don’t
want to be known as a person who just talks a lot but doesn’t put actions behind
his words. That’s why you will try whatever has to be done to make sure that
you don’t disappoint all the people you told about your plans. In his book The
Compound Effect Darren Hardy also mentions this strategies. One specific
example he provides is really interesting and in some way I used it in my life.
Darren explains that a woman wanted to make sure that she didn’t spend a lot of
money on useless stuff and therefore created an online blog about all her
expenses. Maybe just a few people saw her posts but I guess it’s pretty obvious
that telling basically the whole word what she did made her feel that it’s really
important to resist any temptations and stick to her decisions.
We are at a point now where I think there were enough information for you to
really overthink some choices and aspects of your life. I don’t want to
overwhelm you with tons of information so that you think you know a lot but
actually you know almost nothing because you probably simply don’t have
enough time to think about more than a few aspects that aren’t part of your
everyday life. If you are interested in more information about this and other
topics feel free to join my e-mail newsletter. You can find more information
about it in the last chapter of this ebook.
Before we continue with an exciting new topic let’s just sum up the results of
this chapter. Basically all we need to say is that we often have the problem that
we simply make decisions but don’t stick to them. Even enormous willpower
may not be enough to resist any influence that makes you dismiss your
decisions. So what we do is we just make decisions which are meant to support
our main decision. Just use whatever is important for you and make sure that
you will feel very uncomfortable when you don’t achieve to turn your decision
into reality. For example make sure that you lose money, social rank or
something similar whenever you are struggling to stick to your decision. The
basic law behind this scenario is that you will not give in any influence unless its
value for you is bigger than the value you lose when you don’t stick to your
decision. That’s a simple math equation right? No excuses any longer. Feel free
to write me about your experience with this strategy via my email address.

asking whether they want stocks in one single question. Like every other

normal? Well, let’s get back to the actual explanation of “normal”. There are
tons of quotes about this word and all its implications. According to Greg Plitt,
one of my personal role models, “Normality is what weak people call living, I
call it death.” So obviously normal isn’t something positive for the number one
fitness model in the world who has been covered on more than 100 fitness
magazines. From another perspective, the etymology of the word normal, it is
derived from the word norm which means that it is basically just something
which society dictates us to do or has been determined to be the best way to do
something. We have to admit that our social structure has many benefits and we
therefore can’t deny positive influences of norms and the responding term
normal. Society developed a script on how we should lead our life which works
out quite good for many people. Nonetheless Greg Plitt’s words are true in many
different ways. At the time of our life when we are babies we are told by our
parents that we have to change a natural way of how we read for example
because of social norms. In this specific example children automatically use
their forefinger pointing at the letters they want to read to increase their eye
focus which has benefits on how fast they can read and how many information
of a text they really remember. Children do this without even thinking about it.
Awesome, isn’t it? When I read Tony Buzan’s book about speed reading from
which I learned this fact it kind of pissed me of that some social norms had
negative effects for me. This isn’t the only example for stupid norms and their
extremely negative influence on our life. If you want to learn more about Speed
Reading you can watch the free online video course I made on Youtube. Just
type in the search box MindDifference and check out the videos on this channel.
Maybe in your opinion this single example can’t persuade you that norms are
sometimes nothing but ridiculous and counterproductive. Just imagine you have
some incredibly skills which could change our entire world or could have
changed your life completely if some norms didn’t exist. I don’t want you to
imagine this scenario because it is not real or I can’t find any evidence for it so
please take in charge that with the help of all requests on you I ultimately only
want to influence you more than you would be influenced if you just read

the power to affect your life, some written text of which you can only remember

and realistic as possible I actually ask you to imagine being Albert Einstein at a
very young age. The way science was taught normally in school didn’t help him
a bit, he only got very frustrated about his bad grades. At this point of his life
nobody would have bet a penny the Einstein could become one of the most
influential scientist ever. The word normal can be closely related to the word
average. As mentioned earlier normal includes both negative and positive
aspects. It’s extremely fascinating how many people justify their actions or even
judge actions of other people with the justification that something is normal. The
truth is that normal should be almost completely meaningless for you. You
really need to figure out what you want to do with your life. Do you want to
spend the rest of your life (a rather predictable life), trying to please everyone,
accepting whatever problems you have and constantly hoping not to have any
challenges or problems you need to master so that you’ll die and be forgotten at
the end of your journey of fear which some people call life? I think nobody
wants to live life that way but nonetheless many people do it. It don’t want to
say that a normal job is something negative, I’m only stating that many people
don’t like having a normal job and life and should change something. Some
people don’t believe in their ability to change themselves and their surroundings,
others are way too comfortable doing what they do and get confused by all the
different information or distractions like television for example which are

why anyone should read an ebook of this kind and still just want to stay the
same way forever. It is possible that you don’t really want to admit that you’re
not satisfied with stagnation in your life or you don’t even know it consciously
but there is no way reading this book without aiming for self-improvement
makes sense. So either stop reading right now and waste your time with other
activities or really start to change at least a bit of your behavior, thoughts or
mindset right now. Change is scary but always remember that even a journey of
1000miles starts with one single step.

Once again I want you to imagine a specific situation. This time imagine that
you want to start your own business to become independent and financially
successful.
What would you do in order to still keep you job which provides you the
necessary financial resources and at the same time start your own business?
The “normal” approach for this problem would be to spend less time with other
activities than working. You stop doing sports, watching tv or you just sleep 2
hours less per day. As a result, it’ll be hard to stay motivated all the time
because your life isn’t really balanced. The lack of sleep increases your craving
for fatty meals and in general has negative effects for your focus and
concentration levels. In addition you won’t have that much confidence any
longer because the lack of physical activity will make you feel being in a bad
physical shape. It would be wrong to state that all these measures you take just
evoke new problems and in the long term don’t help you achieving your goals
because there are elements like watching tv which maybe can be replaced with
more efficient activities. What we can say is that there is a very likely possibility
that cutting out activities of your daily schedule the way most people would
probably do it can have negative result over time.
Here’s another rather “abnormal” way to deal with the time problem. The first
step is that you focus on an aspect of your life which most people don’t consider
to be crucial for your problem. The odds are that you spend a lot of time either
buying food from local supermarkets, preparing your own meals or you buy
your food whenever you’re hungry for example at McDonalds. Buying your
food several times per week and cooking about one hour per day takes a lot of
time. The second option doesn’t necessarily require that much time but is very
unhealthy and expensive. Here’s another way you can handle this problem. Just
buy your food one time per week. Products like oats, fruit bars, dried fruits,
juices, bread, frozen fruits etc. are cheap, relatively healthy, easy to transport &
easy to store. You can save a lot of money shopping inexpensive and healthy
food so that you have more money that you can invest in your business.
Furthermore, you may feel healthier in case you figure out a good meal plan
which works for you. The main effect is that you save many, many hours
because you don’t really have to cook all food and shopping is way faster.
What’s really awesome about this tactic is that most people only focus on
obvious aspects which are related to their goals like working longer but often it
is a lot easier to improve in other aspects of your life. Sometimes side effects are
almost equally important as major effects of your actions. As a side effect of
eating healthier you can definitely for example sleep better.
Maybe some people think that this approach is totally crazy. Probably even most
people who read this book are quite disturbed right now. The initial goal of the
book was to help you improve yourself for example by cutting out time of your
time which doesn’t serve your goals. I don’t want to tell you that you have to
become socially awkward to have personal success. It’s just likely that you have
to be very open-minded and normal behavior should be something you need to
question sometimes. It’s time for a very vivid example which might change your
opinion right now.

excellent work. It’s kind of strange that few of all people who admire him know
about his very abnormal behavior. At first and probably most important is the
story of his company name Apple. Steve Jobs was absolutely convinced that a

fruit for a long time of his life. When he founded Apple with Steve Wozniak he
could only think of apples because there was nothing else he ate at this point of
his life. If you’re a little bit shocked right now I can promise you he did way
crazier things. Even as an entrepreneur with a big business he constantly refused
to wash his body for a long time. He really believed that his special diet would
avoid any bad smell. His ways to deal with work related stress evolved a lot of
laughter at work. After a long exhausting day of work Steve enjoyed putting his
bare feed in a toilet to cool them down. I hope you agree with me that this
practice doesn’t count as body hygiene.
