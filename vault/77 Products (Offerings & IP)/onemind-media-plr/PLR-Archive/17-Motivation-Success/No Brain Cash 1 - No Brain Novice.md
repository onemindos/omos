As the name suggests, you complete surveys online. You sign up to a

What kind of surveys?


Companies are constantly researching markets for new products ideas

This data consists of a broad range of information from income levels to

Probably the most correct view to have is, “this can’t be real…it
seems too easy…I don’t believe that something as easy as
reading emails or clicking links can earn me a LOT of money!”


So simply put, don’t expect any of these “paid to” programs to pay you

How It Works

For this chapter, I’m going to choose the easiest of the “paid to”
methods and explain how to get the most out of it.


Let’s look at “Paid to Read Emails” first.


In a nutshell, you receive emails from advertisers and you get paid a
few cents (generally 1-5 cents) for each email that you open and click
the link to visit the advertiser’s website.

Don’t worry, you don’t have to go out and promote yourself as an “email
ad clicker” or look for advertisers to send you these emails.


There are websites/companies already set up that have masses of
advertisers paying them to send out to their “ad clicker” members.
These advertisers will pay as little as $5 for 1000 clicks up to $50 for
10,000 clicks for example. This money is then shared with the members
of the website.


You simply sign up with one or several of these websites and you’ll start
receiving bulk loads of promotional emails and start earning little by
little for every link inside those emails that you click on.


There are many variations of “paid to click” email reading but I have
outlined the general idea.


“What kind of emails will I be getting?”


The majority of advertisers will send you “business opportunity” type
emails. You are under no obligation to buy or sign up or click on any
links on any of the advertiser’s websites you visit but most programs
will require you to keep the page displayed for anywhere from 10 - 30
seconds.

If you sign up with an established paid to click company, be prepared to

because of claims of scams and other issues brought up by their
members.


The best option then is to not have paypal as your primary form of
payment. Use other reliable methods such as www.Alertpay.com or
www.moneybookers.com if they offer a choice.

How It Works


Basically, advertisers pay through Google’s PPC (pay per click)
advertising program, Adwords – a multi-billion dollar company, to have
their ad displayed on other people’s websites and pay a fee per click of
their ad.


You as the affiliate get paid a portion of the cost of that click paid by the
advertiser. It’s revenue sharing.


For example:

Let’s say the advertiser pays $1 each time someone clicks on their ad.


50c goes to Google and the other 50c goes to you!


50c may not seem like a huge amount but it ads up. Think of it as a tip
jar for your website.


And did you know there are people making an absolute killing with
Adsense everyday? I’m talking $200 to $300 per day from Adsense.

Now don’t get me wrong, those figures take time and a lot of work. And
more often than not, that is earned over a network of dozens of
websites. Everyone starts out small at the start and grows and grows.


Another great thing about Adsense is that it’s passive income. Passive
income means you don’t have to keep working to get paid. Once you
have traffic coming on a regular basis, the clicks will keep earning you
money.

5 Common Adsense Mistakes

1. Don’t click On Your Own Ads!!

This is a big no-no! Google tracks who clicks on which ads and if you
click on your own ads, they will catch you, close your adsense account
and make it very difficult for you to get another one.


2. Using the Wrong Design and Color Scheme in The Ads


You won’t get any clicks on
your ads if they look like
ads. Blend them into the
content so they look like
they are part of your site
and don’t stand out like a
sore thumb as an ad which
people will ignore.


Don’t use a border, use the
same background color as
the rest of your site and
make the links blue and text black or dark grey.

4. Don’t label your ads or provoke clicks


It was very popular and even seems logical to label Adsense ads with a
“Sponsor Ads” or “Recommended Links” title above or near the adsense
ads. This is against the terms of service and will get you in trouble – so
don’t do it.

5. Not Adding Good Content To Your Site


The best kind of traffic is natural search engine traffic and that comes
from lots of good topic related content. If Google sees that you are
adding content which will make the reader happy, then it will list your
website higher. It’s a win-win-win situation!
