# 8 Simple Ways To Coping With Pain

8 Simple Ways To Coping With Pain
It is not impossible to cope with pain. With determination and a set mind, you will be able to overcome anything including pain. Try to take charge of your pain today with these 8 simple ways:
Drink loads of water. By drinking 8 glasses of water a day, you are taking a simple step to keep your body hydrated and your body will thank you for that. Chronic pain tends to be aggravated when the body does not have enough water. It is best to avoid coffee, soda or tea as they have diuretic effects that can cause dehydration instead.
Load your fridge with leafy greens, foods with omega3 fatty acids, asparagus, low-sugar fruits such as cherries, pineapples, plums and cranberries and soy products. They can help alleviate pain and alkalinize your body system to make it less acidic. These foods help to increase chronic pain resistance and fatigue busters.’
Avoid processed foods and foods containing chemicals that can increase inflammation such as dairy, chocolate, eggs, citrus fruits, high fat red meat, coffee, tea, sodas, red wine.
Incorporate turmeric when you are cooking. Originating from India, this brightly coloured spice is used to cook delicious food while known for anti-inflammatory properties. Turmeric contains curcumin and has been used for centuries for treatment of many health ailments such as chronic pain. It is a natural pain reliever without harming liver or kidney.
Try to incorporate a form of exercise into your daily life. Exercise has shown to be beneficial by strengthening the body and increasing blood flow. Start with simple exercises or learn yoga to increase strength and flexibility. Yoga has been shown to be able to calm the mind and reduce stress that was caused by pain.
It is important to have good postures not just to give good impressions to other people but for your health benefits. Bad postures such as bend back with head in front of spine may impair nerve and blood flow causing stress towards your neck muscles and aggravating pain. It is best to keep a straight back with your head resting on top of your spine. Remember you are not a question mark.
Take a rest whenever you feel pain. Pain could be an indication of the body telling you to stop overworking yourself. Sleeping is one of the ways for the body to rest and regenerate itself. By depriving the body of it, your body will have low resistance towards pain.
Distracting yourself with things that make you feel good. Humans are not able to work all the time. It is important to have a hobby that you enjoyed and allow yourself time to engage in the hobby to distract yourself from stress. By doing something that make you feel good, you are indirectly releasing endorphins which is good hormones and reduce pain.
