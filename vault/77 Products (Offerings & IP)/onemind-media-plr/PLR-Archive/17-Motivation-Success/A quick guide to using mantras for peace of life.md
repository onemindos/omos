# A quick guide to using mantras for peace of life

A quick guide to using mantras for peace of life

Daily life can be busy and stressful at times. It may seem impossible for you to be able to find 15minutes a day to relax. So what could you do? You could try chanting mantras. Mantras are a very personalized way of meditating. Mantras can be used as a form of focused meditation to bring about positive changes to all areas of your life.

Firstly, you need to memorize your mantra. You may use traditional or religious mantras. You can even use sounds such as Aum which is commonly used. If you are not comfortable with that, you can use a phrase that is personal to you. Make up your own phrase such as “I will be happy today”.

Secondly, you need to find a place for you to use your mantra. Choose a place that is quiet, away from noise and distraction. It can be a bedroom, park or even a bathroom stall at work. You need to allocate time for that. It doesn’t have to be long. It could take 5-10 minutes or even 30 minutes. To use your mantra effectively, the most important part is to be undisturbed.

Next, sit in a comfortable position. Close your eyes and start chanting your mantra. Whilst you are chanting your mantra, relax every part of your body and start taking deep breaths. As you chant your mantra, you will feel your body start to relax and every part of your frustration start seeping away out of your mind. 

After 10 minutes of chanting, you can use another 5-10 minutes to help you absorb the mantra you have been meditating. This can help you alleviate your stress as well as clear your mind of the stress and frustration that has been clouding your mind throughout the day. So try our beginner’s guide to using mantras and you will be surprised at what a huge difference it makes.
