SELF-HELP FOR SUPER VILLAINS
       Christopher Holmes Nixon
    “Deep within man dwell those slumbering powers, powers that would astonish him, that he never
     dreamed of possessing, forces that would revolutionize his life if aroused and put into action.”

                                           – Orison Swett Martin



Hello. My name is Bill Masterson.

I want to begin by taking a moment to thank you. I know we’ve never met, but I already know you. I
know who you really are because you’re like me. We’re the same.

I respect you. I really admire and appreciate you because you’re different from the rest. You’ve already
set yourself apart from the thousands of people who’ve been overcome by the frustration of daily life,
allowed their dreams to dissipate, and lost the sense of certainty that creates the winning edge. I
recognize you as the type of person who wants to make a change, and more importantly, you’re willing to
do something about it.

For over a quarter of a century, I’ve helped millions from around the world achieve the life advantage and
not settle for anything less than they know they can be.

I’m the guy who gets the call. I get the call when the problems start to burn out of control. I get the call
when the octopus man is in a jewellery store surrounded by the police, and his robotic arms have started
to malfunction. I’m the guy who gets the call when the plan has failed, and the evil genius is being beaten
senseless live on national television. I get the call when the fifth robot has been captured, and now the
ultra-bot is fighting the climactic final battle with only one leg. Or sometimes I get the call just to say
thank you. I recently received a call from a client who said, “Bill, I’ve attended all your seminars and
read all your books, now I’ve taken the president hostage on my zeppelin, and how would you like a state
named after you as a token of my appreciation?”

From all the people that I’ve helped, the biggest lesson I’ve learned is that we all behave in the same way.
We may have different backgrounds, different standards, different super powers, but we all encounter
similar obstacles. Our challenges are all alike, and the science for overcoming limiting beliefs in order to
achieve peak performance apply whether you’re a criminal mastermind, a psychotic dictator, a systematic
killer, a rogue computer program, or just the by-product of a horrible lab accident.

I recognize the power that individuals possess to change virtually anything in their lives in an instant.
I’ve learned that the resources to turn our dreams into reality are already within us, merely waiting for the
moment that we accept the challenge to claim what is rightfully ours. Let my words be your impetus to
accept the call to greatness, the catalyst to tap into the powers that you already possess and to live your
life to its fullest.

Let me extend my sincere gratitude for deciding to make a change in the quality of your life, and thank
you for joining me. It’s my privilege to know you.

Every great journey starts with a first step, so let’s begin.
                   “Whether you think you can or you can’t, either way, you are right.”
                                                 – Henry Ford



Creating a meaningful life is simple, so simple in fact that many of us overlook the first step. The secret
to realizing your true potential is to know what you want.

Is it that simple? Yes, it’s that simple.

Successful people ask better questions, they get better answers, which leads to better results. Ask
yourself, what do you want?

Do you need to feel the reverberations of your nemesis’ vertebrae crushing together as you play their
spine like an accordion? Are you dissatisfied by the fact that no one wants to rescue the damsel that
you’ve kidnapped? Have your death traps become tired and predictable, and you’re looking to try
something new? Do you want to steal more money, build a larger underwater lair, or form the league of
evil that you’ve always dreamed of?

Here’s what I need you to do: sit down and write out your goals. I’m serious! Sit down right now and list
all the things that you truly want.         If you’re writing out this assignment with the blood of your
vanquished, then I salute you, but I can’t do this for you. Take a moment to yourself and write down your
goals, and when you’re finished, come back to me.

Once you’ve completed your assignment, place your goals where you can always see them. Keep your
goals inside your exoskeleton, use your orbital laser to etch your ambitions into the surface of the
rainforest, or even have your minions chant your goals as a mantra to wake you up in the morning.
Always keep your goals right in front of you to fuel your journey along the road to success.

Now that you’ve laid out your purpose for the future, you should be thinking, “Well, what’s next?”
Exactly! What’s next?

I need to bring an important point to your attention so that you can avoid one of the major pitfalls that
traps many of us. While you’re in the process of achieving one goal, one scheme, one master plan,
always consider the next achievement. You don’t want to find yourself sitting on a pile of money
wondering what to do, or stand triumphantly over the broken corpse of your arch-nemesis thinking, “Is
this all there is?” Be sure to think ahead, validating your current exploits with the absolute certainty that
you will reach the next level.
When you’re planning your next accomplishment, always push yourself to think big. Why destroy one
city, when you can destroy them all? You owe it to yourself to dispel your limiting beliefs, to lay the
mental framework for turning your dreams into reality. Remember, there is no such thing as failure, only
results.

If you can imagine it, you can achieve it.
                             “Action is the foundational key to all success.”
                                             – Pablo Picasso



We’ve talked about why you’re here. We’ve talked about the importance of knowing what you want.
Now we’re going to discuss how to expedite your ascension to excellence through peak performance.

Once you’ve selected your goals for the future, the next step is to take massive action. Never incite the
ambition without taking immediate steps to pursue its attainment. Is your goal to make more money?
Then go out and rob a bank, or better yet, rob two! Are heroes constantly escaping from your dungeon
lair? Then start designing that force field that you’ve always been talking about. Are you serving
quadruple sequential life sentences in a maximum security prison?          Then maybe it’s time to start
smashing through some walls, both metaphorical and real. Change can happen in an instant, and taking
decisive action builds personal momentum. If you’re unsure when to do this, then ask yourself, “Would
now be a good time?”

The next empowering strategy that I’d like to share is the power of positive self-indoctrination. The
language we use is very important, and there are specific phrases, if repeated with enough emotional
conviction, that will galvanize your mind into a state of unquestionable certainty. These are called
incantations. For example, try something as simple as “fight, win, prevail.” Or use a generic phrase such
as “bad guys have all the fun, God damn us, everyone.” Or maybe something more complex like “the
blood of my enemies will line my path to victory.” Create your own incantations, and incorporate them
into your daily habits to transform yourself into an unstoppable juggernaut of success.

The next step is simple, yet highly effective. See your life as a game, a challenge, or a contest. Score
your life! Give yourself specific quantitative measures of effectiveness to chart your accomplishments.
Track the size of your money pillar. Count the number of terawatt hours that you’ve consumed with your
death ray. Keep track of how many of your followers have sacrificed themselves in order to gain your
appeasement. You’ll be excited about the results as you advance towards your next triumph.

Out of all the peak performance sciences that I’ve studied, the practice which consistently produces
extraordinary results is the power of beliefs. A belief is the psychological state in which an individual
holds a concept or principle as true. Beliefs control our behaviour, either consciously or unconsciously,
and subsequently dictates our reality. Do you believe that you’re not evil enough? Do you believe that
you’re too busy conquering the netherworld to start going to the gym? Do you believe that your cape
makes you look old fashioned? None of these are true! When you endorse limiting beliefs, you create
limitations, and its time to start substituting your mental barriers for empowering convictions and unlock
your full potential.

If anyone can do something, then so can you.
      “No man chooses evil because it is evil, he only mistakes it for happiness, the good he seeks.”
                                           – Mary Wollstonecraft



My final piece of advice is absolutely critical. Everything I’ve taught you up until this point has been
regarding the science of achievement, or the process required to go from where you are now to where you
want to be. To have an extraordinary life you need to master the science of achievement, but you also
need to fully appreciate the art of gratitude. Success is focussed on the future, fulfillment on the present,
and to be truly successful you need to balance the imminent with the immediate. Commit yourself to
taking the most scare resource that you have, time, and give it back to yourself. Take the time to truly
enjoy the screams of despair radiating up from your torture chamber.            Stop to thank yourself for
possessing the mental fortitude, fiscal capacity, and personal determination to unleash a super-virus on
the unsuspecting local population. Give time to yourself to respect your own super powers, and truly
appreciate the fear that you inspire in the hearts of the common man. Fulfillment means experiencing
tremendous joy and excitement in the process of pursuing your dreams while retaining the enthusiasm for
everything that you already possess.

Success without fulfillment is ultimately failure.

Now that we’ve reached the end of our time together, I already know what you’re thinking. I know this,
because I’m constantly asked the same question. How can I act as a mentor, a guide, and a coach for
super villains, if I don’t even have a super power of my own?

Over the years I’ve helped millions of people change the quality of their lives, and what we’ve discussed
here today is the best that I have to impart. I’ve shared my secrets with you, and for that I’m truly excited
and grateful. I sincerely believe that each of us has our own purpose, one outstanding quality, one
characteristic that sets us apart from everyone else. What is my super power you ask? I’ve already given
you my power to take with you on your journey, your quest, and your life: the power of motivation.

I’m Bill Masterson, and thank you for joining me. I hope you’ve enjoyed our time together.

Know who you are, know what you want, and know how to get it. Starting now!
