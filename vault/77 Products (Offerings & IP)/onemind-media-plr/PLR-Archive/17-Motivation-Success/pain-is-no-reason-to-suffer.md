                        PAIN IS NO REASON TO SUFFER

PAIN AND SUFFERING

You might not be able to avoid all pain but you can avoid suffering.

Pain is an unavoidable part of life.
Suffering is a dysfunctional and avoidable consequence of pain.


PAIN

Pain is a warning of injury or disease.

And, while one should try to fix the cause, in the long-term, distraction or turning off the pain can
be worthwhile, in the short-term.

Pain can be dull and bearable or sharp and unpleasant.

While the following methods can switch off pain, distract you from feeling pain or remove the hurt
from pain, most do not remove the cause.

If you cannot turn off the pain then relax so the pain does not hurt so much.

Short fasts of up to 2 to 3 days, attitude, diet and exercise, can, in the long-term, remove causes.


COMMON PAINS

Muscle soreness and spasm, headaches, stomach upsets, backaches, toothaches, cramps and other
pains, are a common part of life for most people.

So is emotional or psychological pain.

But, you do not have to suffer.
                                 UNDERSTANDING PAIN

PAIN IS IN YOUR BRAIN

The amount of pain you feel, is not due to how much damage is happening to you, but how much
damage you think is happening.

If you understand pain, and so are not afraid of pain, then it is less painful.
Indeed, it is possible for you to feel pain, but for pain not to hurt you at all.

Pain is in your brain – not your body.
Your body sends your brain signals by nerves and chemicals.
And, pain is your brain interpreting those signals.

This is why people can feel pain in a different place to the injury.

People can feel a toothache in their cheek or a stomach-ache in their back.
And, even feel pain in a wooden leg.

Your body also signals your brain by nerves and chemicals to turn off pain.


PAIN AS A WARNING SIGN

Regard pain as a warning alarm of over exertion, injury or disease.
A warning of something to be fixed or managed – not something to be feared.
Fear of pain increases the pain.


                                     TOLERANCE OF PAIN

Your attitude to pain, and building up your internal resources and self-esteem, can increase your
tolerance for pain.
But, as much as it is possible, fix or remove the causes of pain.

Rather than trying to stop the pain, just note the warning the pain is giving you, and try to remove
or fix the cause. This can increase your tolerance.

But, if you are really suffering then switching off pain by distraction, herbs or other methods can be
worth doing. While you think about the cause and what to do about it.


                                   INTERNAL RESOURCES

Build yourself up, physically and mentally.
And, have a positive, useful, beneficial purpose in your life.

Self-esteem, self-worth, self-knowledge and self-reliance, can reduce, or eliminate, depression,
anxiety, panic attacks, self-harming.
                                OVERRIDING NERVES

You can consciously concentrate on only a few things at a time.
By concentrating on other things you can distract yourself and not feel pain consciously.

While you are talking to someone, or playing a game, you might notice that you are not feeling
pain.
And, that this effect can continue after you stop talking or playing.

And, nerves that transmit feelings of pain can also be overridden by heat, cold and touch.
Heat pads and cold compresses.

And, affection, self-massage or just holding or stroking yourself.


TOUCH

Touch and hold the painful part of you or lightly stroke yourself.

By stroking yourself, you can stimulate other nerves to send signals to your brain, so that the pain
signals can be crowded out.


SELF-MASSAGE

Self-massage alone can fix pain and sometimes the cause of the pain.

For some people self-massage is a substitute for affection.


MUSCLE CRAMPS

Sitting with your legs up, like on a couch, can prevent cramps in your legs.

If you already have a cramp then gently and slowly stretch the muscle.

For example, if you have a cramp, in the back of your leg, move your toes and foot slowly up
towards your body. This can stretch the back of your leg.


KEEP WARM AND RELAXED

Keeping warm and relaxed can reduce a lot of aches and pains.


WRINGING HANDS

While clasping your hands, like a Christian praying, press the sides of the base of your fingers
against each other hard enough to cause mild pain.

This can distract you from feeling other more painful pain.
                                      DISTRACTION

Distract yourself by thinking of something else or doing something else.
Especially, something you really enjoy and get really involved in.

While looking for some cloves in my cupboard I noticed that the pain, in my teeth and bleeding
gums, had stopped.

Sometimes the pain comes back.
Other times the pain comes back but not as painfully.

A sharp, painful pain can become a dull, bearable pain.

But, I should fix the cause of my tooth and bleeding gum pain.

[NOTE: This happened when I was a Vegan. As a Vegan, I found that using sublingual vitamin B12
supplements, stopped this bleeding and pain permanently, by fixing the cause.]


PLAY A COMPUTER GAME

Anything that you concentrate on can distract you from pain.
Even typing this pamphlet.

One time I was in pain, I played a rubber of Bridge, on my computer.
The pain soon disappeared and did not come back after I stopped playing.

With pains, like headaches, you might be able to distract yourself long enough for your headache to
pass.


COMEDY

Comedy is another good distraction that can make you feel good and painless.
Indeed, daydreaming about anything pleasant can stop you from feeling pain.

EXCRETING PAIN CHEMICALS

PRODUCING HAPPINESS CHEMICALS

Exercises, including walking, running and dancing, can produce happiness chemicals.

But, you should still try to remove the causes.

enjoy eating and can digest, can reduce disease, and so reduce pain.

Including psychological pain due to nutritional deficiencies.

Some foods, such as dairy products, have natural happiness constituents.


SHORT FASTS

Fasting can fix the pain, of most diseases and allergies, by eliminating the toxins that cause most
diseases.

Fasting can fix painful conditions such as headaches, backaches, arthritis, angina, muscle spasm
and cancer pain.

SUNSHINE

Walking in the sunshine can make you feel good and happy.


EXERCISE

Exercise, like walking up hills, walking along ocean beaches, running and dancing can make you
feel good.

Exercise can also strengthen your body, and improve your posture, so you have less pain like lower
back pain.


SORENESS AFTER EXERCISE

If soreness after exercise is not due to injury then do some light exercise.

For example, if you are sore from too much running go for a gentle walk.

Back pain, one of the most common chronic pains, can often be fixed by removing the causes.

And, by appropriate exercises, including light weights, to strengthen your bones, posture,
abdominal and back muscles.

Be careful not to make it worse.

Some backaches are really stomach aches that you feel in your back.
So, improve your digestion, through food-combining and order-of-eating.



                                     STOMACH-ACHES

Stomach-aches can be caused by fear, tension, anxiety.
Or, by poor food-combining and incorrect order-of-eating.
                                   ORDER OF EATING

Different foods and drinks are digested at different rates.

So, one should have quickly, digested drinks and foods at the beginning of a meal.
And, the more slowly digested at the end of a meal.

So, fruits should be the entrée and not the dessert.
And, one should drink before eating – not after.

Unless, it is alcohol, which one should drink while or after eating.
And, milk, which acts as a neutral food, and so can be drunk after a meal.


ORDER OF EATING IS MORE IMPORTANT THAN FOOD COMBINING

According to food combining fruit and nuts are a poor combination.
But, according to order of eating it is good to eat fruit, followed by nuts.


ORDER OF EATING AND DRINKING

One need not eat all of the following at one meal.
But, in any one meal, one should consume those higher on the list before those lower on the list.

1: WATER;
2: FRUIT JUICE;
3: VEGETABLE JUICE;
4: FRUIT;
5: VEGETABLES;
6: STARCHES;
7: OILS;
8: PROTEINS.


SOME EXCEPTIONS

Some things, such as tomatoes, carrots, carrot juice, green leafy vegetables and their juices, alcohol
and milk can also be eaten, or drunk, at the end of a meal.
Indeed, the eating of celery stems at the end of a meal, can clean your teeth.
While drinking milk at the end of a meal can re-mineralise your teeth.


IF YOU ARE THIRSTY AND NEED TO DRINK AFTER EATING

Plan by drinking first.
But, if you are thirsty at the end of a meal then drink milk, carrot juice or green leafy vegetable
juice.
Or, eat a neutral food, with a high water, content like tomatoes, lettuce or celery.
Or, drink a little water.
                                    FOOD COMBINING

Food combining is the eating of compatible combinations of food.

Poor combinations of food can lead to poor digestion, indigestion gas, acute pain in the intestines
and, eventually, more chronic conditions.

The larger the meal the more noticeable the effects of poor food combining.


In food combining, foods can be divided into proteins, starches, oils, green vegetables, root
vegetables, fruits and melons.

Any food can be eaten alone and melons should be eaten alone.


PROTEINS

Nuts, sunflower and pumpkin seeds, sesame seeds and legumes.

Legumes include peanuts, peas, beans and lentils.


STARCHES

Starches, of all foods, need to be well chewed and well in-salivated, as starch is digested in your
mouth.
So, starches are best eaten dry.

Starches include cereals, grass seeds, such as oats, wheat, rye, rice and maize.

Legumes. [Peanuts, mung beans and lentils, are the most digestible legumes.]

Some vegetables, such as potatoes, sweet potatoes and Jerusalem artichokes.

Starches also include chestnuts, bananas and pumpkins.


OILS

Avocados, most nuts, peanuts, coconut, vegetable oils and copha.

It is healthier to eat nuts, or avocados, than processed oil.


GREEN LEAFY VEGETABLES

Green leafy vegetables combine well with everything.
Green leafy vegetables include broccoli, lettuce, celery, cabbage and sprouts.
HERBS

Basil, borage, fennel, oregano, sage, thyme, mint, parsley, ... are also green leafy vegetables.


SPICES

Most spices, like turmeric and ginger, are vegetables.
Others, like cayenne pepper and star anise, are fruits, but act like vegetables.


ROOT VEGETABLES

Plants often store starch in their roots.
So roots, such as beetroot, carrots and parsnips, tend to be starches.
Potatoes and sweet potatoes are also starches.


FRUITS
THAT ARE CALLED VEGETABLES

People often call fruits, such as tomatoes and cucurbits, vegetables.

Cucurbits include pumpkins, zucchinis and cucumbers.
Most act like green leafy vegetables.
But, pumpkin is a starch.


MELONS

Melons, including watermelons and cantaloupes, do not combine well with anything.

Melons should be eaten alone.
Or, at the beginning of a meal.


FRUITS

Most fruits can be divided into acid, sub-acid and sweet fruits.

Except avocados which are oils.
And, bananas and pumpkins which are starches.

Acid fruits, include citrus fruits, pineapples and sour plums.

Sub-acid fruits, include apples and pears; most stone fruits; and, many berries, like blackberries and
blueberries.

Sweet fruits include mangoes, papaya and dried fruit.

Remember to clean your teeth after eating dried fruit.
The fruit sugar has become concentrated and sticky.

AVERAGE COMBINATIONS

Sub-acid and sweet fruits.


POOR COMBINATIONS
