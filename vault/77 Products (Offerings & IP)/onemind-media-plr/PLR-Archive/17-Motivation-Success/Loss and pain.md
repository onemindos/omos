Loss and pain: How to survive

Everybody's journey through grief is unique and no two people will
grieve in the same way. There isn't a standard amount of time also that
people will usually get through grief. When you lose someone that is
very precious to you, the grief is intense. You may feel as you might
never recover, never really laugh or never be whole again. However, it
is possible to heal yourself emotionally and mentally. Don't allow the
pain to pull you down.

1.  Face the issue. Although the people around you may help you forget
    your pain temporarily, you will never be ale to truly heal yourself
    until you have confronted yourself. Ignoring the pains by the means
    of alcohol, medications or drugs will bring serious repercussions
    later.

2.  Confide in others. You need to share your feelings with others. Talk
    to your family or friends about your loss and have a good cry. A
    good cry will help you feel much, much better. Let the tears flow.
    You will be crying all your pain out.

3.  Remember the good times. Don't focus on the negative things. Think
    back to all the good times that you have had with your loved ones.
    Let those memories carry you through this difficult period.

4.  Occupy your time. Do something that will give you peace such as
    gardening, cooking, fishing or listening to your favorite music. You
    need to fill your time instead of moping around in the house.

5.  Volunteer! You could involve yourself in social work. When you
    involve yourself with other people's life, you may gain a different
    perspective on how to cope better.

6.  Don't regret anything. Don't keep depressing yourself and think that
    you didn't have the chance to say goodbye or to say I love you. You
    can still say it.

7.  Lastly remember that time heals. Give yourself time. You are
    suffering and it is okay to take for yourself. If it necessary, you
    could always seek professional help.
