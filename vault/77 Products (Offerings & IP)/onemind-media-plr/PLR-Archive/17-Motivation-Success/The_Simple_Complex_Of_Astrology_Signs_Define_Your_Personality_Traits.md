Astrology is an age old science that is still flourishing, probably at it's best. Although many people question astrology's recognition as a science, though a greater chunk of folks consider it a science. Astrology gives incredible insight into one's personality. Predictions and suggestions accorded by astrologer often turn out relevant to your life. Crucial determinant of your astrological fate happens to be your astrology sign. Your astrology sign is the sign accorded to the period of a year in which you were born. These astrological signs also give behavioral insights into your persona along with fortune telling.

Most of the people have this wrong perception that astrology is all about making predictions about your fate. It is in fact a totally wrong connotation. Astrology signs are meant to offer a detail on each and every aspect of your life in consonance with your birth chart detail.

In western astrology there are twelve astrology signs. These signs have been clubbed under four different elements such as earth, water, fire and air. Each of these elements is assigned with three zodiac signs. These elements possess their distinct traits and zodiac signs under the common element share common traits.

These signs are Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius and Pisces. Aries, Leo and Sagittarius are grouped under Fire. Earth consists of Taurus, Virgo and Capricorn. Gemini, Libra and Aquarius come under Air element. Cancer, Scorpio and Pisces are signs assigned to Water group.

Modes or quadruplicities are divided into three groups of four planets each. Each group has one sign from each of the four elements. The three groups are the cardinal signs, the fixed signs, and the mutable signs.

The cardinal group includes These signs are more inclined to embrace activity and have a quick grasp of situations and opportunities. The fixed group consists of This is a goal oriented group that succeed by unwavering resoluteness and even stubbornness. The mutable signs are These signs rely on flexibility, experiences, and memories.

There could be another clubbing up of zodiac signs. There are three modes in which four signs each have been categorized. The three modes are: cardinal, fixed and mutable signs. Aries, Cancer, Libra, and Capricorn come under the Cardinal mode. Folks of these signs happen to be trend setters. Taurus, Leo, Scorpio, and Aquarius are a part of fixed group and are known for stability and endurance. The remaining four such as Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, and Pisces are put under mutable mode.

Each astrology sign has its own characteristics and features. If you happen to be an Aries, you are bound to be very confident, aggressive and arrogant. And you love to be the best in whatever you do. An Aries aggression is because of its association with fire element. Capricorn is from Earth element and Folks born with this sign happen to be very practical and responsible.

A Gemini person of Air element happens to be versatile but possess a dual nature. Cancerians come under water element. They are very sensitive and emotional people. Similarly all other signs have their inherent traits.

If you want to know about yourself through astrology, keep track of your daily sun sign horoscope. This is easiest way of knowing your astrological details. Birth chart prepared with the help of your birth details are rather based on your sun sign in conjunction with other planetary movements.
