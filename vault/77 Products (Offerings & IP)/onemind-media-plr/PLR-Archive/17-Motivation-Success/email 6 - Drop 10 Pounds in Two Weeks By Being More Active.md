Drop 10 Pounds in Two Weeks By Being More Active

Hi Firstname!

Hopefully you've made some dietary changes based on what you learned in the last e-mail. 

It doesn’t stop there, however -- you also need to make sure you’re exercising the right way. 

That doesn’t mean it has to be hard! All you need to do is follow these simple tips, as well as your weight loss program, and you’ll drop the weight in no time.

You don’t have to devote your entire life to exercise. All you need to do is plan to exercise about three times per week. 

You can get some workout DVDs for in-home use, join a gym, or even get a personal trainer. It’s all up to you. 

If you find that one method is not working for you, you’ll want to find a different method.

If you get an exercise DVD that does not inspire you, you’re probably not going to work out as much as you should. 

On the other hand, if you find one that really motivates you and gets you going, you're to see the success you want to see. 

Setting yourself up right for exercise is incredibly important. The important thing is that you’re motivated to exercise and to lose the weight. 

While planned exercise sessions are great, they are not the only way to exercise. You can even get some exercise during everyday activities. 

For example, instead of taking the elevator at work or when you’re shopping at the mall, you can just take the stairs! 

Another idea is to park far away from where you’re going. That way you’ll be forced to walk farther than if you were parked right by the door. 

You can also do some squats as you’re walking around the house, work out your arms by lifting your body when you’re sitting on a chair, and lots more! 

These are just a few little things, but making these small choices really makes a huge difference because it all adds up.

If you find that these things are motivating there is still more you can do. 

You can start taking a dancing class, for example. This might be a fun thing for you to do if you have a significant other (and even if you don't!). 

Dancing is a great fitness exercise and it’s very motivating because it’s so invigorating. 

Remember -- exercising should not seem like a chore, it’s something you should want to do. If you can find an exercise routine that suites you, you’ll definitely be on your way to dropping 10 pounds in two weeks.
