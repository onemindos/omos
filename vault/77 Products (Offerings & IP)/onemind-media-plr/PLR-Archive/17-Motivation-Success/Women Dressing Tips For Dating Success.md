**[Women Dressing Tips For Dating Success]{.ul}**

What to wear is often a stressful point for both men and women. These
are some tips for what to wear as a woman on a first date:

**i) Dress appropriately for the venue**

Use your common sense. If you're heading to a football match, throw on
something comfortable to wear outdoors such as a cute top or tee and a
pair of jeans. On the other hand, if you're going out for drinks, pick
something less casual, yet appropriate for the bar you're heading to.

### ii) Be comfortable

It's said that beauty is pain, but forget about it! There's enough to be
stressed about on a first date. Make sure that you're comfortable in
whatever outfit you choose, whether it's jeans or a skirt and heels.

### iii) The LBD

Ah, the Little Black Dress. If you're stuck and need something more than
jeans, the LBD is a fabulous, classy option for a first date. Channel
your inner Audrey Hepburn with a sleek silhouette, comfortable heels and
simple accessories.

### iv) Don't wear too much makeup

There's a time and a place for tons of makeup, and it's rare that it
doesn't involve a tent, ringmaster, and a trapeze act. In all
seriousness, a study by [St.
Ives](http://www.dailymail.co.uk/femail/article-1240767/Bad-news-Katie-Price-Millions-men-think-women-wear-make-prefer-natural-look.html) revealed
that one in five men wish their partner would tone down the makeup,
while one in ten said they liked women who wear no makeup whatsoever.
So, don't suffocate your natural beauty with too much makeup. It's one
thing to cover up a zit, but it's another to make yourself look like an
entirely different person.

### v) Trends

Don't overdo it. You might dig harem pants in your off time, but a first
date is not a time to wear these babies. You can incorporate tamer
trends in moderation. For some current yet tasteful trends, a great
cross-body satchel or a high waist flare jean will do wonders.
