                             The Basics


Recently I heard somebody say, “You can’t get wealth. It’s the other
way around. Wealth is forever reaching to get you.” Uh-huh! This is
what I’ve experienced as well. I may feel the vibration of The Law of
Abundance all around and inside me.

And as well, I'm becoming mindful of places where I've backed away
from it, really resisting it simply by fretting about it! For many of us,
this is caused unconsciously or semi-consciously. To gain abundance,
we have to be willing to become mindful of where we center our
attention and what we trust to be true.

In our physical domain, the sun is a great symbol of this law. It's a
light that's constantly shining, in all ways at all times. It lights up
everything, bestowing clarity. Its unlimited light gives life. There's
nothing that any of us have to do to “earn” this light. It just shines on
us.

So why isn’t everybody consciously receiving abundance and
prosperity all the time? Continue on.

As we live our life story we encounter conditions that cause us to
envisage or dream of something that lies past our present experience.
Frequently this comes from going through the opposite of what we
wish. If there's a contrast between what we wish and what appears to
be available, out of that contrast a desire is born. This desire rallies
creative power.


                                  -6-
This automatically- produced desire is how we get channels for the
infinite creative power to flow through us, and is a part of the
procedure of how we line up with the Law of Abundance.

This has occurred since the birth of our kind and is how inventions
are brought about, symphonies are made, evolutionary discoveries
occur, and how our bodies have evolved. This summoning procedure
happens on each level of life, including the cellular level of our bodies.

There are no bounds to this summoning procedure or what may be
produced with it. As a desire summons forth originative power and
the desire is given more attention by picturing the desire fulfilled, we
start go through how it feels to have the desire satisfied.

That shifts our vibrations to be in sync with the vibration of the state
we wish to attain and by the laws of the universe that state brings
about. This is The Law of Abundance working.

The way that we block off ourselves from drawing in abundance is by
dedicating more attention to the deficiency of what we wish instead of
the fulfillment of what we wish. Then, by the like-attracts-like
precept, we recreate the deficiency of what we wish.

This procedure of contrast, want, imagining the want fulfilled, and
then manifestation, is the energy of creation that's within all life. This
creative energy is the thriving pulse of all creation. I've felt this pulse,
deep inside my being.

With this procedure, the universe is forever expanding. Ever consider
how different our world is from the days of our ancestors? Everything
that every generation thinks up summons that thing into our world
for the following generation to capitalize on.

This law guarantees that everything that we're thinking up today is
making the world that the following generation will be born into, for
better or worse. We may thank all of the human souls who have gone
ahead us, for dreaming into reality a lot of the things that we love
now.

Consequently, there isn’t a finite measure of love, well-being,
prosperity or fulfillment that we all have to compete for. That sort of
thinking may only come from one not being conscious of our link with
the source.

Anything that anybody may dream of they may bring about. Anything
is possible! With the Law of Abundance working, the universe simply
continues expanding to include whatever we think up.

What decides our experience of abundance and prosperity or the
seeming deficiency of it is:

    Where we center our attention, either on the fulfillment of
       abundance or on the deficiency of it (on positive thinking or
       negative thinking)

    What we accept and trust we merit

    Experience or inexperience in opening and receiving


                                  -8-
Collectively, these 3 things determine how much abundance we let
into our lives.

What you deserve is a deeply revealing question to ask yourself. The
surface answer might be, “Well, I deserve to be pleased, prosperous
and to have all great things.” Some interpretation of that will likely be
the conscious response for many of us.

All the same, on the deeper levels, there are frequently subconscious
or semi-conscious contradictory beliefs from the past implanted in
the cells and in the psyche, which drag us out of alignment with The
Law of Abundance.

A way to open up your awareness to these notions is to look at your
life and note what occurs if you contemplate a greater level of success
in your life story, whether it's in the arena of vocation, financial
success, love relationships, and so forth. Does fear come up? If so,
there's a belief that's about a limit to your abundance. This link may
help you release it.

You’ll find it valuable to take a little quiet time and consciously ask for
the fear to come up. Feel it in your body. Don’t judge it or do anything
with it. Simply let it be there, as though it was a terrified youngster
and you're flooded with understanding and love for them.

Don’t try to fix or alter the fear. Simply give it room to be as it is. This
will take a little practice. As you work with that, you’ll find that the
energy will start to unwind and spill. It might likewise alter and shift


                                    -9-
to a different part of your body/being. That's all right; it means it's
releasing layer by layer.

You may likewise do an energy healing session for yourself.

I've discovered it enormously valuable to search out my limiting
feelings, bring them out into the forefront, then consciously dismiss
them. This will free you from the self-sabotage that a lot of us deal
with, so that The Law of Abundance may help you in your life story.

It's a useful exercise to take time to make a list of the matters your
parents or basic caregivers taught you (either verbally or by
illustration and/or by producing severe consequences if you didn’t
abide by their rules) about who you have to be to be “alright.” Then, I
invite you to think about everything on the list. For every item ask, “Is
this actually true?”

You will be surprised to find a few odd things on the list! Commonly
our parents were well meant but, all the same, they passed on their
own concerns and limits to us, consciously or unconsciously.

From these acquired beliefs you build self-judgments about how you
look, what becoming “successful” ought to look like, how you ought to
act, feel and think, and so forth.

A lot of us were really taught to be leery and critical of anybody who
was rich. What sort of beliefs do you believe that would create inside
you about cash? You'd unconsciously block abundance, because you
wouldn’t wish to be one of “them” - individuals you were instructed to
judge.


                                     - 10 -
A lot of us likewise were disciplined to think that self-care was
“selfish” and that we ought to constantly give to other people before
ourselves. This was a huge one for me. I came to see that the sole way
I may give to other people is if I’m tapped into my own well-being
first.

Occasionally that calls for me taking care of my own needs before
others, saying no to requests from other people if I recognize I need to
recharge first, or if I understand that doing what they wish of me
would produce a draining condition for myself.

I’ve discovered that the best way I may serve other people is to follow
what makes me feel better. That’s if I know whatever I do from that
place will be of the greatest benefit for all affected.

Many of us were never schooled how to receive. The ironic thing is
that receiving is really an inborn “skill” that we all have, however it’s
been disciplined out of us. Woefully, we simply haven’t had adequate
role models to show us how to open up and receive.

But! It's never to late to learn - or, I ought to say, to unlearn the
disciplining that veils our power to cease striving, to open up and
experience the love, joyfulness, and abundant aliveness that's our
birthright.

To discover how to receive, self-investigation is crucial initially, to get
to be more aware of your personal dynamic for getting (or not
getting). This is a crucial initial step in getting to be aware of your
special relationship with abundance. A few of us are open and free


                                    - 11 -
with getting cash but block love and connection. For other people it
might be the opposite.

Or you might have dreams to be a musician but can’t really seem to
get there. Other stuff seems to get in the way. So where are you stuck?
Is it in the area of relationships, cash, self-empowerment, or
discovering and exercising the career that arouses and inspires you?

Remember, we’re not simply discussing getting objects here. This is
about our power to open up and be cognizant of the flow of love, light,
joyfulness, and well-being that's constantly flowing and available to
us. From there, things we want could appear, but that's a spin-off.

The abundance we’re talking about here is the experience of getting
tapped into what decides the quality of our lives. Out of that, all great
things flow to us, both objects and experiences that nurture and
affirm our hearts.

There's an amazing “Catch 22” that I should mention. As we
relinquish resistance and accept the flow of love and abundance,
while initially we might feel relief and even blissfulness, that
procedure will expose thicker levels of resistance, which we might
experience negatively as the resistance arises for release. Then if we
open up more and bring out the resistance, we feel relief, then the
following layer opens for release. On and on…

The amazing thing about that is that as we go forward with this
procedure and continue opening our connection with abundance,
then abundance happens faster. Then there's a bigger collective

channel for love, grace, and prosperity in this world. This has an
enormously favorable affect on all of us!

                              Conclusion




We draw in a reflection of what’s going on within us into our life
story. So if you are feeling pitiful, mad or restricted because of your
weight, wellness, job, or past relationships, then you'll draw in
somebody into your life who's likewise sad or mad.

If you had an issue with one of your parents, you'll likewise draw in
an individual with the same issue into your life story so you are able
to continue to heal that relationship.

Let’s say, for instance, that your father abandoned you. This means
that you might draw in a partner who's afraid of commitment. If your
mom was mad and always yelling, you could draw in a mate with an
atrocious temper.

So before you center on attracting new love into your life, it’s crucial
to do a little good work on yourself first. Once you’ve achieved these
tasks, you’re ready to draw in the right love into your life!
