Now I am showing may age a bit here. I remember the reruns of I love Lucy show. Now when we reach this new consciousness are you going to be saying hello God I am home? You look around and there is no one left but a few stragglers here and there. Now was this the second coming of Christ, or are you one of the few unlucky ones that did not evolve?
Chances are we have not evolved yet as the rest of the human race. Is this hell? I mean if we did not make to the next level are not we out of lives like a video game. Yes and no, what do I mean? It is sort of like Mario brothers game you hit the magic mushroom and you get a new life!
Now this means that if you missed the boat on the first trip. If you are left out in the cold. Do you remember in the bible where it says the dead will arise and be judged, its my belief it is just another chance for those who missed out on the moving up, with God because they were on a different level.
That does not mean we cannot still win the game. There are always more power ups and extra lives. Just when you think you have lost, you hear Hello Lucy I am home! Yes now you are on the right level and you are moving up to the next consciousness. I do not pretend to know all the answers. I am writing this as the chapters are coming to me!
I do not really think life is like a video game, but I do believe that we can never be separate from God. Jesus did say The Kingdom of Heaven Is Within. Which is what he really meant is it is within you. That part of you that is the I AM. The part of you that was once in the no thing and went to the thing. Therefore, God could feel life on both levels at same time. Sort of like two players on a video game and they can be playing the same game but be on different levels.
It does not mean that they can never power up, they can and they will as we just got through discussing the fact that we never, separated from God. This is where we hear, Hello God I am home where are you? I am here my son or daughter with you. Why did it take you so long to get here.
Well remember the fact that, we never strayed far from the sidewalk? That is what I am saying here, God says to us hello I am home where are you? If you do not hear him, it could just be the fact that you are on a different level. You need an extra life or a power up. If you will but just listen, we can move from the thing we call life rather quickly and get home, with God, in heaven and you will know you are home. That is when Lucy comes to great us at the door!
We are now on the same level as God, we have finally heard him as he was talking to us all along; remember the foot prints in the sand? When we thought we where alone, thats when God was carrying us and that is why there is only one set of foot prints in the sand. So Lucy dont stop telling us you are home until all the sheep come into the fold.
There are no loser in this thing we call life. Only different relations of consciousness and we will not even know it. We will just be glad that Lucy was indeed there all the time, so when her husband got home she would be there to greet him and the truth shall set you free, and it is because of you Lord GOD Almighty!
