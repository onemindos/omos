**Gaining Commitment from the Customer**

The art of selling is a process. It's not just talking stiffly and
hoping your customer already wants to buy it. You need to make the
customer want to buy, and to commit himself to your product. Without
commitment, there will be no sale. Thus this is the golden rule - the
best way to ensure a sale is to gain commitment as soon as possible.

A definite way to do this is to make the customer verbally indicate that
if "X" happens they will purchase your product. Once you have gained
commitment it is your job as a good salesman to hold up your end of the
bargain, so you also have your work cut out for you!

If they say they will buy your product if you can get a stain out of
their carpet, then get the stain out. Most people won't go back on their
word. Often times you gain commitment when overcoming an objection. For
example if the customer says "I would, but I can't afford it". If you
said "So if I made it 100% affordable you would definitely invest in
this product". Once they commit, it is up to you to work with the price
to fit it into their budget.

Locking a customer into commitment is not an easy task. People have a
natural fear of commitment. What you have to do is put them into a
corner. Wait for them to ask you if your product can "do this". That is
the ideal moment to get commitment. Simply state to your customer, "if
it can do this, I'm sure you will invest in my product."

Gaining commitment early gives you an edge in your sales pitch. Find out
what their main problem is right away, and if possible solve it
immediately. This will not only create an immediate want in the
customer, but it will help you gain commitment early. After you have
commitment, constantly remind them of the problem you solved.

Gaining commitment is a great asset to a sale, but is not necessary. It
will however make the sale that much easier. Look for certain keys that
may help you lock them into a commitment. Many times it's as simple as
them asking you answering a question they have about your product.
