# 2 - LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet

LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet - Have Fun And Learn This Xmas 2011!

If you are looking for the kids to get a bit more than just fun this Xmas and you are looking for something that will also educate them then maybe the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet is worth having a look at!

This is an awesome Christmas gift and is tipped as one of the top sellers for 2011!

The best thing is that it mixes fun and education so your kids will actually have fun and not even know that they are getting more brainy!

Here are some features and details of the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet:

The screen is large and gives you a big 5" screen so you will be able to see clearly. Another thing to note is that the screen is larger than the Leapster Explorer Screen which was only 3.2".
The LeapPad also gives you a 480x272 resolution. 

The other thing to note is that it is a touch screen and you will be able to control the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet with your finger or it comes supplied with a stylus as well that is magnetic and you can also attach it to the device with the cord. If you DO end up losing the stylus there is an extra one supplied with the package so keep this one safe.

You obviously get some games and activities with the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet and these include:

PetPad: This will allow you to learn how to draw letters and all the while it will teach you custom pet tricks. You need to keep your pet happy and clean and fed while playing the game and you do this by drawing letters.

Another interesting feature of this game is that you can use the microphone to "blow dry" your pet after a wash and if you shake the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet it will make your pet jump. If you want more food and accessories for your pet then you can connect the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet up to your computer and you will be able to purchase more.

Story Studio: In the Story Studio kids can create their own book that is a story about them that you can upload to Facebook or share via email.

Art Studio: This is an awesome feature of the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet and it allows you to use brushes, pencils, erasers, shapes and stamps to make their own art and express their creativity.

Cameara/ Video Recorder: As the name suggests with the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet you will also get a Camera and Video Recording and the kids love this. The only thing thing that you must bear in mind is while the camera is very good it is low res.

You also get a whopping 100 games and apps that come with the LeapFrog LeapPad Explorer Learning Tablet so it really is an awesome product this Xmas to help your kids learn and have fun at the same time!
