# **How To Make Money With Amazon FBA**

## Do you want to make money with Amazon FBA?

## FBA, Fulfilment By Amazon, is a system through which you can sell products on Amazon and have them processed, packages and sent to buyers. Retail arbitrage is the process of buying products at low prices and selling them higher to make a profit.

## **Here's our complete step-by-step guide on how to make money with Amazon FBA quickly & effortlessly...**

##  

## **How Does FBA Work?**

## Using FBA, you may buy a product from a clearance sale a department store for cheaper than its usual retail price.

## Then you would list the product on Amazon, have it shipped to their warehouse and they'll send it off for you once it's been bought.

## ...It really is that simple!

##  

## **How Much Should You Charge With Amazon FBA?**

## It can be difficult to know how much to charge for products on FBA when you're buying them for such a low value.

## Many retail arbitrage-ers aim to sell products at triple their initial value. If you were to buy a product at £4, you should aim to sell it for £12. This will earn you back 100% of your expenses and accounts for product fees as well as shipping & storage fees paid to Amazon. £4 is paid for the product, £4 is paid to Amazon and £12 is earned back for each product sold.

## ...Therefore, for each product sold you'll make 100% return and suffer no losses.

##  

## **How To Get Started Making Money With Amazon FBA:**

## Before you get started selling through FBA you'll need to set up an account first. Amazon give you two options when you set up your account, personal and professional.

## **Personal account:** this is a free account that'll allow you to sell anywhere up to 40 items a month. You will need to pay for all standard Amazon fees though, such as postage and packaging. I'd recommend this for beginners as it'll give you some wiggle room -- if you don't make any sales then you won't be losing any money.

## **Professional account:** this account will require a \$39.99 monthly fee as well as shipping fees, however you'll be able to sell more than 40 items a month. I wouldn't recommend this if you're just starting out as you probably won't be selling enough products to earn your money back.

## To begin with, you're much better off going with a personal account as you likely won't be exceeding 40 items in your first month anyway.

## ...Sign up through [[Amazon Seller Central]{.ul}](http://sellercentral.amazon.com/) to start. Then, add your credit/debit card to your account to control where your money will go.

# Retail Arbitrage With Amazon 101:

So, you're feeling motivated to make some extra dough this year. New
year, new me, right? One way of doing this is by setting up your own
business. If you are interested in starting a profitable business where
you can service customers with quality products, then you have found the
right blog. Today, we will be discussing retail arbitrage using Amazon.
How does someone without a speck of experience begin?

Fear not, as many novices had to start somewhere before becoming highly
successful. Before we look at every nook and cranny of beginning this
fine business on Amazon, the largest internet retailer in the world,
let's discuss the definition of retail arbitrage. It's important to be
on the same page with clear understanding. So, get ready to take some
notes. Class is in session.

### **Defining Retail Arbitrage**

So, what is retail arbitrage? Let's say you have a strong interest in
card games. Imagine you want to make money from this niche by selling
fantasy card games to customers. Instead of making your own game (which
you could), you decide to buy certain brands of card game products from
retail stores and sell them online for a higher price. Retail arbitrage
is simply defined as purchasing physical products from retail stores and
selling these items for profit.

You have the option to sell these products online or from a
brick-and-mortar store. In this case, we will be discussing online
retail arbitrage using Amazon.

### **What Does Amazon FBA Mean?**

FBA is an initialism which stands for "Fulfilled by Amazon". In other
words, when you want to sell a product, you ship the product to the
Amazon Fulfillment Center. The center will store and ship the product
for you whenever a customer makes a purchase. You do not have to store
and ship your product this way, but it is very convenient.

### **Advantages of Amazon Retail Arbitrage**

Amazon retail arbitrage gives many advantages to the seller. Efficient
delivery of your products is made possible thanks to Amazon. The
customer can receive free, quick delivery using Amazon Prime. This is
good because more customers are using Amazon Prime. Because of this, you
are able to reach many customers due to Amazon's popularity and service.

You will save money when it comes to shipping products. Instead of
shipping the products directly to the customer, Amazon FBA will ship the
products to the customers for you. Also, you can sell more without
making your home look as if you're hoarding. No products will take up
your home's space.

Another huge advantage is no customer service. Well, at least done by
you. Customer service is handled by Amazon, so you don't have to worry
about delivery schedules, tracking numbers, and so on. Very convenient.

### **Starting Out With An Amazon Account**

As said earlier, Amazon is the largest online retailer in the world.
Because of this, the advantage is possible attracting a decent number of
customers. First, you need an account. When you visit Amazon's website,
you must create a seller account. As you are feeling up to starting your
business, upgrade your seller account to the Pro Seller version. You
will end up paying \$40 per month, but it's worth the investment (the
first month is free).

As a pro seller, you will receive more tools to use to help your
business run smoothly. Tools include inventory management and analytics.
Also, you will save \$1 for each transaction. If you sell more than 40
products in a single month, you will save yourself at least \$40. Nice,
right?

A word of caution: If you do not plan on selling this number of
products, then you may need to stick with the individual account. The
downside to using the individual account will be ending up having an
extra \$0.99 added to each product sold. You'll have to figure out what
works for you.

### **Choosing Your Product**

Now, you may be wondering how to decide which products are good to sell.
You need to know what would sell successfully and which items (or
categories) should be avoided.

There are many product categories to choose from. Toys, Decor, Pet
Supplies, and Games (like the fantasy card game example from earlier)
are perfect categories to start looking for items. You can also sell
products that would be categorized under Beauty, Entertainment, or
Clothing & Accessories. The problem is competition. Another problem is
whether or not Amazon will approve the product. Once Amazon approves,
you can sell the item(s).

Certain product categories must be avoided per Amazon's rules on FBA
prohibited products. Tires, alcohol, and products weighing more than 150
pounds are examples of products that cannot be sold under Amazon. Check
Amazon's website for more information.

### **Figuring Out Pricing**

When you are trying to figure out the pricing of the product, one way to
do this is by taking the price of the item you paid for and multiplying
by 3. For example, if you purchased headphones that were \$5, you can
try to sell them for \$15. Other factors are involved with pricing such
as the size of the item because of the price of shipping. Luckily, there
are apps available to calculate projections for each item you are
selling.

### **Download the Amazon Seller App**

If you own a smartphone, you should download the Amazon Seller app. This
piece of software is necessary to scan various products before buying
and selling. You will be able to determine if the item will sell or not.
It will help you make the right decision.

### **Time Is Money: Selling Your Product (Finally!)**

So, you have picked the products you are going to sell and your Amazon
account is active. On your account go to Seller Central. Next, go to
Inventory. Select "Add a Product". List your items; make sure they are
priced competitively to increase the chances of selling. Make sure each
product is clear and organized so the customer will understand what the
product consists.

The products that you have listed will have labels that you can print
out at home. Place the corresponding label to the product. After you
have completed the labeling, pack the items up and ship them to Amazon.
Since Amazon has several fulfillment centers, you may need to send each
product separately (Amazon will tell you where to send the items). Once
Amazon receives the packages, they will unpack the products to add to
your inventory. Now, your product is available to the public to be sold!

### **Continuing to Grow as a Seller**

As a newbie, you will not know everything there is about Amazon FBA. You
need to continue to grow as a seller through educating yourself and
experience. Amazon has an FBA forum section for sellers to read and
absorb new information. You can ask questions if you are stuck. Video
tutorials are available on Amazon and will answer common questions FBA
sellers have.

Hopefully, you have a better idea of the basics of retail arbitrage on
Amazon. Having a business takes time to develop. In time, you will have
the income you have dreamed of earning.
