# 4 - FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup

FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup - The Ultimate Xmas Gift For 2011!

If you are looking for an awesome gift for someone special this Xmas then you need to check out the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup! This is tipped to be one of the best selling toys for Christmas 2011 and it comes in at number 4 on our list!

If you have the kids or close family member's or even friend's children asking for a Dog this Christmas then remember the saying "A dog is for life not just for Christmas" - well this is where the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup comes in and you can indeed have a dog for Christmas and as long as you want it without having to worry about high vet's bills!

The features of the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup are impressive as well you will see it looks, barks and pants just like the real thing! The actual dog is modeledon a Bichon Frise dog so it looks really cute. She is soft to the touch and the size is roughly the same as a real dog of this breed as well.

If you pat her on the head she will bark with enthusiasm and if you put her on her side she will turn and meet you. She also tail wags and has a cute high pitched bark!

The other cool thing is that the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup is a remote control pup and you can control her with the leash that you get. All you need to do is pick up the leash and push the forward arrow and she will start walking at a kid's pace. If you press the left or right arrow on the control she will be able to turn corners.

Be careful though once you set GoGo off she might not want to come back!

Just a word of warning the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup is meant to be used inside or anywhere with a smooth, dry surface. If you are using GoGo outside make sure that the ground is dry and it is best to wipe off the rubber paws after use if you are going to be venturing outside with GoGo.

The other cool thing is that you get batteries included and the FurReal GoGo My Walkin Pup will take 4 C batteries so you know if you want to stock up as you suspect heavy use.

The other thing parents will want to know that are worried about the barking all of the time thankfully there is an off switch so you can get some peace after your big Xmas dinner.
