# A beginner

A beginner’s guide to manifestation

You have many goals and desires but it may be difficult to reach them. Now there is a way for you to achieve them. You need to manifest your desires! Most people find it hard to believe that they can manifest what they want. So how exactly do you manifest your desires? Here’s a few simple steps on how to.

Firstly, think about something that you would like to have. You should start with small things. The one you think is easily attainable. An example of this can be a small amount of money. Once you manifested that small amount, you can start moving on to larger and larger things. The reason, you start small is because once you attain the small goals, it will give you the confidence to manifest bigger stuff. The first step is where most people make their mistake. They always start with manifesting big items such as cars, houses with their first intention. But of course that wouldn’t work. This is because you still doubt yourself while trying to manifest those items.

Once you thought of what you want, start visualizing it. The more vivid your visualizations, the more real it becomes. For example, you want to manifest $50, visualize that image in your mind.  Now imagine holding that piece of money. Think about how you are feeling and what you will do with the money.

After you have done visualizing, you need to believe in what you have just visualized in. Be positive that you will get it. Don’t think about how the money is going to reach you, just let everything run its course. Very soon you will soon receive that money!

Lastly, don’t let any negative emotions get in the way. Negative emotions can be like anxiety, stress, impatience or doubtfulness. Remember to stay positive! You need to believe that it will happen and it will!
