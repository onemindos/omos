# 47-Gratis Website Promotion

Gratis Website Promotion


Can you ever get free site promotion? Is that even workable? 
Naturally yes! Today, your site can collect huge traffic in no time thanks to free site promotion.

How does this free site marketing go anyhow? What are the things to be done?

1. List your site.
Seek the hottest Net directories and list your site there. This is the easiest and best free site promotion maneuver. Begin with this step and the rest of the great things will come after.

Simply don't forget to prep your site and make it all smartened up for a higher opportunity to get accepted in your directory of choice.

2. Understand  forums.
One reason why discussion boards are created is for free site promotion for everybody. Log-in, post actively, let them know about your web site in each post and you draw in instant visitors right there.

3. Author a press release.
Free your writing prowess and begin a press release that promotes your site! This is a free site promotion maneuver that you are able to do anytime. Type a brief paragraph or 2 and e-mail it to your friends, co-workers, internet e-zines, papers and other media and massive traffic will come to you promptly!

4. Be friendly in cyberspace.
Free site promotion means you need to be friendly to other webmasters. How come, you ask? So they may link you at once! Make contacts and never tire of link petitions and exchanges. 

5. Author an article.
Say, your site is about your travel agency. Author an article about the fringe benefits of traveling or the most red-hot travel spots in the world. On the final paragraph, refer to your site in passing. This article works as an advertorial and doubles as a free site promotion plan of attack.

6. Simply let the whole world know about your web site.
What is free site promotion without the buzz? Insert your site, its URL and features in daily conversations and let the great news spread from one mouth to some other!

7. Create a banner ad.
Create a banner ad for your web site and ask another webmaster to do the same for his web site. Then trade!

8. Take a free site promotion class online.
Yes, there are free site promotion tutorials. You can actually take a free site promotion course that may help you out further. 

Once you make a site, you need not pay anything to market it. You just read it -- there's such a thing as free site promotion!
