B. Price Your Item Competitively

By “competitively” we do not mean to price the item with only the
profit in sight. What it entails is to price it in such a way as to include
other factors such as marketability of the product, the demand for it,
the prices of similar items, and the innate value of the item.


To get help on pricing the item, you can check eBay’s “Advanced
Search” option and select among the completed listings to find the
price range of similar products.


Some items have seasonal demand, so if you want to get the highest
price for the item, wait until the demand for it picks up and withdraw
once demand starts to slacken.


In case the price you see is lower than what you want to set, it may be
because there are plenty of similar other products on sale.
