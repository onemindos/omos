Zeus Delacruz Personal Brand Audit and Strategy
1. Trademark Availability
Before fully adopting "Zeus Delacruz" as a personal brand, it’s prudent to ensure the name isn’t legally
protected by someone else. A comprehensive trademark search across major databases (USPTO in the US,
EUIPO in Europe, WIPO’s Global Brand Database, etc.) reveals no existing trademarks identical to "Zeus
Delacruz" in relevant classes. In other words, no conflicting registrations were found, suggesting a clear
path to use this name commercially 1 . (Note: The word “Zeus” is used in some trademarks by companies
like Zeus Inc. and others 2 , but the full combination "Zeus Delacruz" appears unique.) It is still wise to
conduct an official search and perhaps file a trademark application to secure the name: - USPTO Search:
Use the USPTO’s TESS system to confirm no identical mark in the same industry 3 . A thorough search
helps avoid future legal issues, as the USPTO will reject applications that conflict with existing marks 4 . -
Global Check: Utilize WIPO’s Global Brand Database to search internationally protected trademarks 5 .
This ensures the name is clear worldwide, not just in one country. - Register if Clear: If no conflicts arise,
consider registering "Zeus Delacruz" as a trademark in key regions for your services (e.g. classes related to
coaching, education, software, or consulting). This provides legal protection and exclusive rights to the
name in those domains. - If Conflict Found: In the unlikely case a similar name does appear, you might
need to tweak the brand name or obtain legal advice. Fortunately, no exact matches were found in
preliminary searches, which means you likely have a unique brand name.


Why this matters: Securing a trademark is important to prevent others from using or infringing on your
brand name. It also adds credibility (you can use the ® symbol once registered). Skipping this step could
risk having to rebrand later if someone else holds rights to the name 6 . Given the current checks show a
clear path, moving forward with "Zeus Delacruz" seems legally safe.

To dominate search engines for "Zeus Delacruz", we need a multi-pronged SEO strategy focusing on
personal branding. The aim is that whenever someone searches your name, they find relevant, positive, and
authoritative content about you. Here’s a step-by-step plan:


4.1 Personal Brand Website & SEO Foundations

     • Launch ZeusDelacruz.com: Develop a personal website as the central hub of your online presence.
       This site should ideally be ZeusDelacruz.com (as discussed, likely available). Include a strong
       homepage or bio page optimized for "Zeus Delacruz" (in the title tag, headings, etc.). This site will
       likely become the top result for your name over time. Remember, your personal website is your digital
       home base 7 .
     • On-Page SEO: Optimize the website’s content for your personal brand:
     • Write an About/Biography page highlighting you as a tech entrepreneur, AI innovator, speaker, coach,
       and founder of Legacy AI. Use those keywords naturally in the text, so search engines associate "Zeus
       Delacruz" with those roles.
     • Include a blog or articles section where you write about your expertise (e.g., posts on AI
       innovations, tech entrepreneurship, coaching tips). Regular content updates can help with SEO and
       demonstrate thought leadership.
     • Implement schema markup (Person) on your site. Use JSON-LD schema to mark up your name,
       title, photo, birth details (if you wish), and importantly, your sameAs social profile links (LinkedIn,
       Instagram, etc.). This structured data helps feed Google’s knowledge graph. Google pulls info from
       trusted sources like Wikipedia, but you can help it along by providing structured data on your site 26 . This
       can enhance your chances of getting a knowledge panel (that info box for your name).
     • Optimize meta tags: Ensure the meta description for your site includes "Zeus Delacruz – Tech
       Entrepreneur, AI Innovator, Speaker, Coach – Founder of Legacy AI" (or similar). This will likely appear
       as the snippet on Google.
     • Schema for Legacy AI connection: If Legacy AI has a website, make sure it mentions you as
       founder (with a bio) and perhaps link back to your personal site. On your own site, mention Legacy
       AI and link to it. Interlinking your name with "Legacy AI" on authoritative pages helps associate the
       two entities. (For instance, the Legacy Platform LinkedIn page already links your name 27 . Do
       similar cross-linking on all platforms.)

5. Credibility & Authority Plan
Building a strong personal brand isn’t just about SEO – it’s about credibility. You want to be seen as a high-
authority individual in your space. This will naturally boost your SEO as well (Google favors entities that
are mentioned in authoritative contexts). Here’s a roadmap to elevate your credibility:


5.1 Wikipedia Page

Getting a Wikipedia page is a hallmark of notability and often triggers a Google knowledge panel. However,
Wikipedia has strict guidelines: - Notability: You (or your brand) must have significant coverage in
independent, reliable sources (news articles, reputable publications). So before attempting Wikipedia,
accumulate press. Aim for several articles on known websites (tech blogs, news sites, industry magazines)
that specifically profile you or at least give substantial mention. - Creating the Page: Once you have
enough sources: 1. Make sure no page already exists (it doesn’t, in this case) 33 . 2. Create a Wikipedia
account and build edit history by contributing to other topics first (this builds credibility as a non-
promotional user). 3. Draft the page in neutral, encyclopedic tone using the third person. Include facts
about your career (founder of Legacy AI, any notable achievements, speaking engagements, awards, etc.)
and cite those reliable sources for each claim. 4. Submit the draft through Wikipedia’s Articles for Creation
process. - Outcome: A successful page will list your background and link to your website and possibly
Legacy AI. This will likely become one of the top Google results. More importantly, Wikipedia is a “trusted
resource” that Google pulls into its knowledge graph 26 , which can generate that side Knowledge Panel
with your photo, bio snippet, and social links. - Patience: It might take a few attempts as Wikipedia can be
fussy. If this is challenging, there are PR professionals or Wikipedia consultants (ensure they follow
Wikipedia’s ethics) who can help. Having a Wikipedia page greatly boosts credibility, as many people Google a
name and click the Wikipedia result first 34 .


5.2 Knowledge Panel and Google Authority

With Wikipedia and schema markup in place, monitor if Google creates a knowledge panel for you. To
increase chances: - Verify your identity on Google (you can search “claim knowledge panel” which usually
involves verifying via an official site or social media). - Also, create a Google People Card (available in certain
regions) by searching “add me to search”. This is like a virtual business card Google sometimes shows. It’s
not as powerful as a knowledge panel, but it’s something to consider if available. - Continue to add
structured data on your site with all new info (if you publish articles, add them in a “sameAs” or
“knowsAbout” list in schema if relevant).


Aside from Google, also try to get an entry on other reference sites: - WikiAlpha or Everipedia: These are
alternative wikis with less strict rules. They don’t carry the weight of Wikipedia, but often rank on name
searches and can be created easily. - IMDb: If you have any on-camera appearances or speaking roles (even
in documentaries, interviews), an IMDb page can be another credible result. - Google Scholar Profile: If you
have any published research or even articles, a Scholar profile (if applicable) could be useful for credibility in
the AI domain.

release for you for relatively low cost. These services often syndicate the release to dozens of news sites

news sites 35 . - Guest Posting Services: Some services or Fiverr sellers can help place an article (authored
by or about you) on mid-tier news sites or blogs. Be cautious to choose reputable ones so the content

credibility piece and a source for Wikipedia. - HARO (Help A Reporter Out): This is free and just costs time.
Sign up as a source on HARO and respond to queries in your field (e.g., reporters asking for quotes about AI
trends, or entrepreneurs’ advice). If your quote gets selected, you’ll be mentioned in their article as “Zeus
Delacruz, founder of Legacy AI, said …”. This is an excellent way to get organic mentions in the press and
build a portfolio of citations across many sites. - LinkedIn PR: Make announcements on LinkedIn for any
notable achievement (new product launch, reaching a milestone with Legacy AI, etc.). Sometimes these can
be picked up or at least spread widely. Additionally, engage with journalists or contributors on Twitter/
LinkedIn who cover AI or entrepreneurship – building those relationships can lead to opportunities to be

SEO but also gives you content to share on your site’s “Media” or “Press” section, further boosting credibility.
According to PR experts, press releases lend authenticity: when people see news outlets publishing your
story, it signals that your brand is noteworthy 36 .
