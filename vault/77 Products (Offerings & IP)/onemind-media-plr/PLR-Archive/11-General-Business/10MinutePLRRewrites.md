Why You Should Rewrite Your PLR

The ultimate goal for rewriting your PLR content is to turn the article or report into
something which you had written yourself. The article should reflect your passion
and interests. It is possible to make this happen even if you are developing content
on which you know nothing about.

The reason which you purchased PLR was to save you time. All of the writing and
the research has been done for you. If you have ever attempted to research and then
write a good article from scratch, you know it takes about an hour and a half to
accomplish this.

Take a look at the top website in your niche. Their website is going to be filled
with content with hundreds of pages, if not more. If you want any chance of
beating out your competition you have to write more content than they do. This can
all be achieved by using quality PLR.

Remember that when you buy any type of PLR, even limited quantities you are
competing with others who purchased the same pack.

If you don’t rewrite or change your PLR and try to submit it to a top article
directory you are asking for trouble. If they discover that your article is indeed
PLR material they will very likely ban you for life.

Your easiest option is to learn how to quickly rewrite your PLR material. Once you
have the skills down pat you can rewrite an article in 10 minutes.

Before rewriting your PLR content you want to find good keywords and a product
to promote your article. I will outline how to do this below.

Steps to Rewriting PLR
To rewrite your article quickly you need to start with quality PLR. The articles
should be at least 400 words in length and written in good English.

A good PLR article will talk about one thing throughout the entire article. Articles
which begin on one subject and end on another should be avoided. Don’t use
articles which are nothing but a checklist of points. These are an absolute waste of
your money.

I will take you through the process of rewriting an article step by step. If you
follow this guide you will see just how easy it is to rewrite your PLR yourself.



Create Appealing Titles

The first thing you want to do with your PLR articles is to change up the titles. The
best titles should have your keyword in it. You want your title to be appealing to
the reader and create a desire for them to read your article.

How to articles are very popular and an easy way to create an appealing title.
Your title is asking a question and your article MUST provide an explanation and
of course a link to the solution.

Top # articles will offer your reader reasons, methods and benefits in an outlined
form. Such as: Top 3 Methods to Make Money Online

Secret titles, articles with this type of title offer the reader the chance to discover
something new and previously unknown. Your title could include words like
Hidden Secrets, Undiscovered facts, Hidden Reasons and Little Known.

Warning Titles these titles simply have the word warning placed somewhere in
them. You are telling your reader that you could have information that could be
harmful or dangerous to them.
Comparisons with this type of title you are comparing two products or niches.
This can be extremely effective for affiliate marketers, you can compare the
benefits of two similar products or items with your final recommendation at the
end of the article.

Reasons & Benefits titles with reasons and benefits will grab your reader’s
attention. A good example would be: Benefits of Using Green Tea Daily or 3
Reasons to Switch to Natural Dog Food.

You want your title to tell the reader that they have a solution to their problem.
Then you don’t want to forget the simple formula of constructing titles around the
following words:

        Who
        What
        Why
        When
        How to
        Where

Using the above examples let’s see just how to change up some titles.

The Article Body

Once you have a title and a summary that you are happy with you want to start
rewriting the body of the article.

Here is the first paragraph of the article:


The formula for losing weight is simple: eat less and exercise more. But, it's not
really all that simple, is it? There are just so many more factors that come into
play. Motivation, time and just life in general can all get in the way when you are
trying to lose weight.


You want to take one sentence at a time and rewrite it.

The formula for losing weight is simple: eat less and exercise more.
Rewrite: There are two simple rules to remember when losing weight: eat less and
exercise more often.

But, it's not really all that simple, is it?
Rewrite: Unfortunately nothing is as easy as it sounds!

There are just so many more factors that come into play.
Rewrite: It is just too easy to run into everyday problems.

Motivation, time and just life in general can all get in the way when you are trying
to lose weight.
Rewrite: It can be so difficult to find the time to exercise and it is easy to blame
life in general for our dwindling motivation when attempting to lose weight.

Let’s continue with some more of the article.


You really do need to be committed to doing whatever it takes to lose the weight.
Here are ten items that should go into your weight loss plan.
1. Eat breakfast. This keeps you from getting too hungry later and then losing
control over what you choose to eat later in the day. Try eating a high fiber cereal
as this fills you up and lasts longer, allowing you to stay out of the fridge for

2. Stock your refrigerator and pantry with healthy foods and snacks and limit high-
fat, high-salt snacks such as potato chips and cookies. Being particularly careful

To lose weight effectively you must be disciplined and be prepared to make new

   1. Eating a good breakfast each morning is critical in succeeding at weight loss.
      By getting into this habit you will find you won’t suffer from those mid

   2. Make sure your kitchen cupboards and your fridge are filled with healthy

Continue doing this throughout the article and it will be rewritten easily. The more

Tips on Creating Your Resource Box
When creating your article you want to remember that your article title is what will
entice your reader to read your article. Your resource box has to get them TO GO
TO YOUR WEBPAGE. It MUST be a strong call to action.

If you don’t give them a resource or bio box that blows them away they won’t visit
your site.

Here is an example of a BAD resource box:

John Smith is a writer of natural health products. You can find John’s webpage at:
http://johnsmith.com

Are you feeling compelled to click on that link? NO!

GOOD

John Smith has been a health nut for as long as he can remember. Visit his site
today at http://johnsmith.com and get your free report on how the drug companies
are killing us and get a 52 week series on various natural treatments for a number
of ailments.

Think people are going to flock to John’s site to get their free report and learn how
to cure one of their problems?

You bet your last dollar they will.

TIPS

Try to make your resource box about 2 – 3 sentences long.

Part 1 – Remind the prospect of their problem or give a benefit.
Part 2 – Give them the URL where their problem will be solved.
Part 3 – Tell them what they’ll find at the site to solve problem.

Hyperlink your keyword in your resource box and use it as your anchor text.

<a href=http://yourdomain.com>Keywordgoeshere. Make this a benefit or feature
to the reader.</a>
By using your PLR report as a giveaway to your list you are making full use of
your resource box.

      Don’t use the word free in your article unless you are giving away a free

Reusing Your New Article

Now that you have your new article ready to submit to online directories I want to
show you how to make further use of it.

You can easily create one or more blog posts for your site by taking the main idea
of the article and summarizing it. Ideally you want to end up with an article of
around 150 – 200 words.

Another idea is to take your article and compare it to something similar. Then post
your comparison on your website.

By taking time to do some additional research you can create an additional article
or blog post on the same topic. You can easily add images, links to a video or
additional references and resources for your reader. Again you are providing your
reader with useful and relevant information.

If you have a fairly long article or short report you can split up the content and post
it to your blog. If you have a wordpress site then it is easy to schedule your posts.
Just set and forget it!

If you have an article that lists the top 10 benefits of a product then shorten into
two articles with 7 and then 3 Top Benefits.

Remember the more you add content to your site the more often the search engine
spiders will come to visit.

Are you beginning to see just how easy it is to use PLR to creative massive
amounts of relevant content for your site?

Another way you can use your new article is to add your own personal story to it.
So if your article is about weight loss and you have successfully lost 10, 20 or
more pounds write a story about how you accomplished this. By making your story
personal you will add value to your article and build a connection with your reader.
Creative Ways to Use Your PLR
There are some easy ways to use your PLR content besides rewriting them for
article directories.

Reports: You can easily package five or ten articles into a report to sell or give
away to your list. Simply add in some graphics to make it different and you are
ready to go.

Blog Post: Break down each article into 250 words and post on your blog. You can
schedule these posts to go out over a certain time period and create your own
unique auto blog.

      Tip: When I do this method I use my Google calendar and make a reminder
      to myself of the last scheduled date. This then arrives in my inbox and I
      know it is time to get some new PLR content.

EBooks: You can purchase PLR eBooks and then lightly edit them, or you can use
several PLR article packs and create your own ebook. Adding graphics and
additional content is a great way to come up with your own product. If you don’t
like to write get a freelance writer to add additional content for you.

You can also turn your eBook into a physical book and sell it at Amazon and other
off line places.

      Tip: Your eBooks and reports can be turned into PDF’s by using a program
      such as http://www.openoffice.org

Autoresponder Messages: Break down your articles to use as content for your
mailing list. Additionally you can use an entire article and make an informational
course for your subscribers.

Web 2.0 Sites: Use your PLR material for content for various Web 2.0 sites. This
includes Wordpress.com, Blogspot.com, Weebly.com, Zimbio.com, Quizilla.com,
Webs.com, Hubpages.com, Squidoo.com and Tumblr.com.
Presentation Content: If you need content for your online presentation then use
PLR articles as the base for your content. This can be condensed into short form
along with bullet points.

Using Power Point to create an online presentation is easy. It is just a matter of
copying each paragraph of your article onto one slide at a time. Then use a
program such as Audacity to record yourself narrating the slides and export as a
wav. File and import it into your presentation.

You can download Audacity for free here.

Offline Marketing: Marketing offline is becoming extremely popular. Use PLR
and turn it into flyers, pamphlets and informational brochures. Additionally use
PLR content as messages targeted to your mailing list.

Social Networking Posts: Break down your PLR content into small posts and use
to promote your products or services to social networking sites. Take the main
benefits of your article and break them down into tweets and post at your twitter
account.

Audio & Video: Both these types of marketing are huge right now. PLR provides
you with the content for creating your own videos, audio recordings and pod casts.

Create New Articles: Using PLR is a great jumping point to writing your own
unique articles. I find that after reading several articles my head is full of ideas and
I can easily write an additional 400 word article quickly.

Yahoo Answers: Use your rewritten PLR article to offer solutions to questions
being asked at Yahoo answers.

Many times PLR content will spur your creative ideas and thoughts and can be a
