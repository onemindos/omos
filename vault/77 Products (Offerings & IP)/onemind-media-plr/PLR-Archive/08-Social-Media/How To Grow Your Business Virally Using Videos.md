How To Grow Your Business Virally Using Videos

We all have 24 hours in a day. The amount of leads we can generate in
our business is limited to how much time we have, or is it? There's one
way to attract tons of leads and grow your business faster than ever --
Through the power of viral marketing.

In this article, I'll show you how you can grow your business virally
using videos. Viral marketing is the marketing of word of mouth (or
online sharing) which helps you grow your online business by leaps and
bounds in a short amount of time.

With the emergence of social sharing video sites such as YouTube, people
can easily upload videos for free and reach a wide audience in a short
amount of time. The key is in creating good viral content which makes
people want to share it.

Make some funny videos related to your content, hire a professional to
make a cool video or if you have some skills in video editing, try
making something cool yourself. Then, post up the video on YouTube and
get people to view it.

Blast your video to your email list, ask your friends to watch it and
encourage your viewers to share it with their friends. The more people
that share or view it, the higher ranking it will get in the video
search engine and hence, more people will see it in return.

Keyword research is also very important. In your video description, make
sure you include keyword rich descriptions inside. This is because
YouTube ranks very high in the search engines, so the more targeted your
keywords are, the easier people can find your content and help your
videos get higher viewership.

Most importantly, don't forget to include a link in your description to
a website of your online business or include a call to action at the end
of your video.
