Viral Marketing Basics For Beginners

Do you know what is Viral Marketing? Viral marketing is the spread of
information of interest via word of mouth or any other sharing medium.
It is the fastest way to get news of interesting content out to a huge
market in the least amount of time.

This was never been able to be done efficiently until today, with the
massive growth of internet users and the emergence of social media
websites such as Facebook, Twitter and YouTube (video sharing site).

Facebook is the largest social media website in the world and has about
500 million users (making it the 3^rd^ largest country in the world if
it were one). People can easily share things they like with their
friends on Facebook because of the news feed feature there.

Twitter is a micro blogging platform that allows people to "tweet" or
share a short sentence of characters to post their thoughts, share
content and share a conversation with other "tweeps" (twitter people).
Twitter is also the largest micro blogging platform in the world.

YouTube is the largest video search engine in the world and also
incorporates social sharing tools and medium, making viral video content
easily shared to friends of people around the world. Uploading and
sharing is totally free and easy.

At the pace the internet and the world is growing, more and more people
are going online. There is a huge growing trend of the spread of
information online, and businesses and individuals can benefit alike
from the speed of how information can be shared and obtained.

From a business standpoint, you can now grow your business virally
online in niche communities if you tap into the power of viral
marketing. The world is getting more and more competitive, and if you
fail to tap into this great marketing resource, you'll be losing out to
your competitors.
