How To Tap Into The Power Of Viral Videos

> Online and offline businesses have much to gain from viral videos. The
> potential of viral videos in reaching a wide audience is endless. In
> this article, I'm going to share with you a powerful strategy of using
> viral marketing of videos to grow your business fast.
>
> Have you heard of YouTube? YouTube is the largest video sharing site
> in the world. It incorporates social sharing tools and rewards popular
> videos with more views. You can post up videos for free and share them
> with ease.
>
> The key here is to create a "Viral Video". A viral video is a cool and
> interesting video which makes people want to share it with their
> friends or people in the same community. If you are in a specific
> niche, you can create a niche-specific viral video and post it on
> YouTube.
>
> From there, you can do a few things. You can promote your video to
> your subscribers and followers and ask them to share it with their
> friends if they like it. You can draw traffic to your video to boost
> it's view count to gain more visibility on the search engines and
> YouTube channels.
>
> You can include incentives to share the video through Facebook or
> Tweeting it to further expand your reach. You can include a
> description at the bottom of your video promoting your business or
> other bits of information which you might deem valuable to your
> viewers.
>
> You can post multiple videos and ask viewers to subscribe to your
> YouTube channel to get more and more followers.
>
> Viral videos have the potential to grow very fast because of the vast
> amount of people congregating on the internet. YouTube has over a
> million viewers daily and if you don't tap into this massive source of
> traffic, you'll be leaving tons of money on the table.
