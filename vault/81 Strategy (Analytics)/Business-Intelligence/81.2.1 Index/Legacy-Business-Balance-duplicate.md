# Legacy & Business Balance

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Sovereignty

### **Legal Considerations**

1. **Child Support and Visitation**: Make sure you have a legal agreement in place for child support and visitation rights for all your children. Consult a family lawyer to ensure you're meeting all legal obligations and protecting your rights as a father.
2. **Tax Implications**: Living part-time in Mexico could have tax implications both in the U.S. and Mexico. Consult a tax advisor to understand your obligations.
3. **Immigration Rules**: Make sure you understand the visa requirements for living part-time in Mexico.

### **Work Arrangements**

1. **Flexible Schedule**: Since you work virtually, try to maintain a flexible schedule that allows you to spend time with your kids in both New York and Florida.
2. **Time Zone Management**: Working part-time in Mexico and part-time in New York means dealing with different time zones. Make sure your work schedule is adaptable.

### **Family Time**

1. **Scheduled Visits**: Plan your visits to New York and Florida well in advance so that you can coordinate with the mothers of your children and any other involved parties.
2. **Quality Time**: Make the most of your time with your kids by planning activities that allow you to bond.
3. **Virtual Presence**: When you're not physically present, make use of video calls to maintain a relationship with your children.

### **Travel and Logistics**

1. **Frequent Flyer Programs**: Take advantage of frequent flyer programs and travel rewards to minimize travel costs.
2. **Accommodation**: Consider maintaining residences in New York and Mexico, and perhaps a more temporary arrangement in Florida for when you visit.
3. **Emergency Plans**: Have a plan in place in case you need to be with your children on short notice.

### **Financial Planning**

1. **Budget**: Plan your finances to accommodate travel, accommodation, and any other expenses related to maintaining multiple households.
2. **Trust Funds for Kids**: Since you mentioned you are wealthy, consider setting up trust funds for your children's future.
3. **Professional Help**: Hire a financial advisor to help you manage your assets and plan for the future needs of your children.

### **Communication**

1. **Open Dialogue**: Keep an open line of communication with the mothers of your children to coordinate schedules, discuss your children's needs, etc.
2. **Transparency with Kids**: As your kids grow older, be transparent with them about your lifestyle and why you have to travel so much. Make sure they know that they are a priority.

By carefully planning and maintaining open lines of communication with all involved parties, you can make this complex arrangement work for everyone.
