CLIENT NURTURE SOP FROM BEGINING TO END OF EVERY INTERACTION 

The first 90 days are the MOST critical, we need to make sure clients STAY EXCITED AND STAY ENGAGED!
HANDLING APPOINTMENTS:
Clients are unable to book appointments for times less than 48 hours. This gives you time to prepare for appointments.
Clients automatically get an appointment link from Active camp requesting they order their reports within the first 24 hours of making their appointment. However, we need to send them the reminder “Let’s order your report” email 24 hours prior to appointment start time.
Set a reminder to call all clients 24 hours in advance to confirm appointment time still works for them and to ensure report was ordered and they have submitted their credentials.

Appointment Prep: These calls just talk about pricing and what we offer. You are securing the client’s interest. This is NOT the audit call (Please reference the client sales script) 
Before the consultation we must review the report, as well as the intake notes from acuity so we know EXACTLY what we are going to talk about prior to the appointment. This allows us to be more efficient and reduces call times.
Consultations should take 30 minutes at the most if reports were ordered in advance. Remember is this a “brief” consultation about what we do and what we offer.
Clients are only scheduled for 30-minute appointments; however, you are INTERNALLY GIVEN AN HOUR in case they have not ordered their report or need additional assistance. 
Within this hour you are expected to complete you after call tasks. (Please see below)
In the event the consultation call goes beyond 30 minutes, you should use this to you advantage to make the client feel valued. For Example: “I see we have gone over our scheduled time, but I am going to ensure you have everything you need before we end our call today.” 😊
When onboarding, clients are most receptive to the information being given. Expectations need to be clear of what we are going to do for them as well as what we need them to do. Example: Letting them know an action plan will be in place for the best results and they are expected to send us all mail correspondences by email or via secure portal. 
A successful call leaves the client feeling confident in the service they’re about to receive. There should be no sense of uncertainty or the consultation was not executed properly.
