Upcoming Formula 1 Events (Apr 2025 Onward) –
Business Networking & Hospitality
Formula 1 Grand Prix events are not only world-class sporting spectacles but also premium business
networking environments. From April 2025 onward, there are numerous F1 races around the globe offering
a range of ticket packages – from basic grandstand access to ultra-exclusive VIP hospitality. High-level
executives, investors, and VIP guests often attend these events, making them ideal for corporate
networking and client entertainment 1 2 . The F1 Paddock Club, present at virtually every Grand Prix,
represents the pinnacle of trackside luxury and access, featuring gourmet catering, open bars, pit lane
walks, and opportunities to meet F1 insiders 3 4 . Many races also offer unique local hospitality (such as
trackside yachts or executive club suites) and host business forums, conferences, and VIP parties in
conjunction with the race weekend, enhancing the networking potential. Below is a summary table of
upcoming events, followed by detailed sections for each Grand Prix.

Saudi Arabian Grand Prix (Jeddah) – April 18–20, 2025
The Saudi Arabian GP in Jeddah is a night race on a fast street circuit along the Red Sea coast 40 . This
event, scheduled for April 18–20, 2025 41 6 , offers a vibrant festival atmosphere and is backed by
significant corporate investment. It attracts many regional business VIPs and government dignitaries.


Packages & Access: For individuals, 3-Day grandstand tickets provide reserved seats with views of the
high-speed Jeddah Corniche Circuit 6 . For example, F1’s official ticket packages for Jeddah bundle
grandstand seating with insider activities 13 . Starter access typically includes general admission to F1
Fanzones and concerts. On the premium end, the Premium Hospitality Lounge in Jeddah offers a climate-
controlled indoor suite with an expansive outdoor terrace overlooking the track 7 . A Premium pass
includes gourmet dining and beverages in a luxurious setting 42 – a popular choice for corporate groups.
The top-tier Formula 1 Paddock Club is also available, granting access to an exclusive VIP suite above the
pits. Paddock Club guests enjoy pit lane walks, meet-and-greets with F1 drivers, and deluxe hospitality 4 .
Companies can book entire tables or private sections in Paddock Club for client entertainment. Official
Links: The F1 Experiences portal provides official packages for Jeddah (from grandstands to Paddock Club)
  43 , and the race’s official site details corporate hospitality offerings 44 .




Networking Highlights: The Saudi Arabian GP’s VIP areas are frequented by business leaders from the
Middle East and beyond. The Paddock Club and premium lounges facilitate networking over gourmet
meals. While not tied to a specific conference, the event’s exclusive hospitality scene (supported by title
sponsor STC and Saudi enterprises) fosters high-level mingling. Many executives use this Grand Prix as an
opportunity to network in a more informal, glamorous setting. For instance, the Premium Lounge at Jeddah
provides a “luxurious atmosphere” for guests to form connections while enjoying the race 7 . With Saudi

the race weekend (often organized by sponsors or local business groups).


Official Hospitality Info: Interested attendees can explore packages via the official F1 Experiences page for
Saudi Arabia 43 or the Saudi Arabian GP’s hospitality page. These sources provide pricing and booking for
options ranging from grandstand seats to private suites.


Formula 1 Crypto.com Miami Grand Prix (Miami, USA) – May 2–4,
2025
The Miami Grand Prix is hosted at the Miami International Autodrome (around Hard Rock Stadium) and is
set for May 2–4, 2025 8 . As one of F1’s newer races, Miami has quickly become a glamorous stop known
for its blend of racing and luxury lifestyle. The event is heavily attended by celebrities, tech entrepreneurs,
and international business figures, making it “a high-speed networking playground”.


Packages & Access: Starter options include 1-day or 3-day Campus Passes and grandstand seats, which
give access to spectator areas and fan zones 45 46 . Miami offers various grandstands (e.g. along the main
straight or turns) for individuals or companies buying blocks of seats. For those seeking an upgraded
experience without going full VIP, Miami has unique mid-tier offerings like the Vista Club and Marina/
Boathouse sections, which provide upscale trackside environments (some with a “nautical oasis” theme by
the fake marina) to enjoy the race while mingling 11 . On the premium end, the Miami GP features an
elaborate array of corporate hospitality: the official F1 Paddock Club (located above the team garages)
offers climate-controlled lounges, rooftop viewing decks, gourmet cuisine, open bars, and pit access 47
  48 . Additionally, Miami has private suites (such as luxury hospitality suites in the stadium and Turn 18

Suites) and branded club spaces. Companies can rent private suites for 50–100 guests (e.g. Paddock Club
rooftop suites) or smaller reserved tables in shared clubs. Miami’s organizers explicitly market the event as a
business networking hub: “Elevate your brand’s visibility, network with industry leaders, and entertain
clients in a private space at the Miami Grand Prix” 46 . This reflects offerings like the “Strictly Business”
package that helps companies design custom hospitality experiences (even build new trackside structures if
desired) 49 .


Networking Highlights: The Miami GP week is filled with networking opportunities. There are often VIP
parties, sponsor events, and industry meet-ups at Miami’s hotels and nightlife venues around race
weekend. The trackside hospitality itself is a meeting point for executives from finance, tech, sports and
media. The presence of many Fortune 500 companies (some bringing clients to Paddock Club) means
informal networking is abundant. One unique aspect in Miami is the “Boathouse” club – a luxe hospitality
area by Turns 4-5 with a yacht-themed vibe – explicitly created to “redefine networking in a trackside nautical
oasis” 11 . Miami’s combination of racing and South Beach style nightlife (including F1-themed parties and
concerts) ensures that beyond the on-track action, there are multiple high-end social events for business
networking 50 .


Official Links: The official Miami GP site has a Business Hospitality section outlining corporate packages
 51  52 . F1 Experiences also lists official Miami GP ticket packages, including Paddock Club and Champions

Club options 53 .

Emilia Romagna Grand Prix (Imola, Italy) – May 16–18, 2025
The Emilia Romagna GP at Imola (Autodromo Enzo e Dino Ferrari) will take place May 16–18, 2025 12 .
Imola is a historic circuit in Italy’s “Motor Valley,” and while the vibe is more traditional racing, the event still
offers modern hospitality and networking opportunities, especially for automotive industry figures and
European business guests.


Packages & Access: General attendees can opt for 3-day grandstand tickets at various points around
Imola (such as the Main Straight, Rivazza, etc.). These provide standard access to watch all sessions. Official
ticket packages from F1 Experiences combine grandstand seating with extras like pit lane walks or a guided
track tour 13 . For example, a “Starter” package might include a seat and a paddock tour on Thursday.
Hospitality packages at Imola include the F1 Experiences Champions Club – an upscale hospitality venue
with gourmet food, an open bar, and visits from an F1 insider. Champions Club at Imola offers a 3-day
experience with luxury seating, pit lane walks, trophy photo ops, and guest appearances 14 . At the top
tier, the Formula 1 Paddock Club is available in Imola as well. Located above the pits, the Paddock Club
provides VIP hospitality (5-star cuisine, premium open bar) and exclusive pit access on all three days 4 .
Team-hosted Paddock Club suites are also an option (for instance, Ferrari or AlphaTauri – being the home
GP for Ferrari – often have dedicated areas for their partners). Companies can book tables in Paddock Club
or even a private space (“F1 Garage” package) for a tailored experience.


Networking Highlights: The Imola race is a bit more low-key on glitz compared to Monaco or Miami, but it
draws a strong contingent of European automotive executives, Italian business figures, and sponsors
(given the region’s automotive industry heritage). Ferrari often hosts many of its corporate partners at this
event, so the Paddock Club and Ferrari’s suite become prime networking spots for those in automotive and
luxury sectors. Additionally, there are usually factory or museum tours (Ferrari, Ducati, etc.) and
business gatherings in nearby Bologna during race week, which many corporate guests attend. While
there isn’t a specific conference tied to the race, the paddock hospitality areas at Imola are where
connections happen – over authentic Italian food and wine. Official F1 packages note that Imola’s ticket
packages come with “expert hospitality & insider access” for an exclusive experience 13 , underlining the
opportunity for personal interactions with F1 insiders that can spark business conversations.


Official Links: The F1 Experiences page for the Emilia Romagna GP offers an overview of packages from
basic to VIP 54 . The Imola circuit’s official ticket portal (imolaf1.com) also lists “F1 Experiences Suite –
Legend” options 55 for VIP seating, which is essentially the Paddock Club offering.


Monaco Grand Prix (Monte Carlo, Monaco) – May 23–25, 2025
The Monaco Grand Prix is the crown jewel of Formula 1 and is scheduled for the weekend of May 23–25,
2025 15 . Taking place on the streets of Monte Carlo, this event is synonymous with glamour, luxury, and
high society. For business networking, Monaco is unparalleled – it’s known as a place where billionaires,
royals, and business moguls mingle amid the F1 action.


Packages & Access: Standard spectator access in Monaco is mainly via grandstand tickets, as there is no
general admission zone due to the street circuit layout. Grandstands like K Grandstand (with views of the
harbor) or those at Casino Square and the Swimming Pool are popular. The Automobile Club of Monaco
sells grandstand seats (e.g., a 3-day package in Stand K) 56 . These starter packages give individuals entry




                                                         5
to the event, but note that even “standard” Monaco tickets are pricey and in high demand. On the premium
side, Monaco arguably has the most variety: the official F1 Paddock Club Monaco is located in a purpose-
built structure near the pit lane/start-finish. Paddock Club guests get terrace views of the pits, fine dining,
champagne, pit walks, and VIP access to the paddock. However, what truly sets Monaco apart are the Yacht
Hospitality packages. Companies and wealthy individuals rent trackside superyachts in Port Hercule to
host clients and friends. These yachts offer an exclusive vantage point of the race with luxury hospitality on
board – “gourmet canapés, champagne, and networking with UHNW (ultra-high-net-worth) guests,” as one
luxury yacht package describes 17 . For example, bespoke event companies offer single-day yacht
hospitality passes or full-weekend yacht charters, including catering and open bars, right next to the circuit
  17 . This has become a quintessential Monaco GP experience for corporate hospitality. Additionally, there

are balcony hospitality packages (private terraces on buildings overlooking the track) and the famous
Amber Lounge after-party events (though not official F1 products, they are elite nightlife parties attended
by drivers and celebrities).


Networking Highlights: The Monaco GP is arguably the best F1 event for high-value networking. The
entire week turns Monte Carlo into a networking hotspot. The race coincides with countless invite-only
events: galas, charity auctions, fashion shows, and yacht parties. Many deals in finance, real estate, and
sports sponsorship are discussed in the Monaco harbor during this weekend. Yacht hospitality in particular
provides a “prestigious vantage point” for both race viewing and connecting with other guests 17 . It’s
common to find CEOs, venture capitalists, and celebrities socializing together. Even F1’s own Paddock Club
in Monaco is frequented by dignitaries and stars – one might rub shoulders with the likes of tech billionaires
or even royalty (Prince Albert II of Monaco is usually present) in these VIP enclosures. In short, Monaco
combines sport and an exclusive social scene, making it a prime environment to cultivate business
relationships in a relaxed, ultra-luxurious setting. (As one hospitality organizer put it: “This is more than a race
weekend. It’s an unforgettable lifestyle experience,” blending F1 adrenaline with Monaco’s social scene 57 .)


Official Links: Official ticket booking for grandstands is via the Monaco Grand Prix organizers (Monaco
Grand Prix official ticketing 56 ). For hospitality, F1 Experiences offers Monaco packages including VIP
access 16 . Specialist agencies (e.g., Senate Grand Prix, Monaco Star Events) offer yacht hospitality –
prospective corporate guests can book through those channels, often linked from F1 Experiences or via
referrals.


Spanish Grand Prix (Barcelona, Spain) – May 30–June 1, 2025
The Spanish Grand Prix at Circuit de Barcelona-Catalunya is slated for May 30 to June 1, 2025 18 .
Barcelona is a well-established race venue with a mix of passionate fans and a strong corporate presence
(many Spanish and European companies entertain guests here due to the circuit’s proximity to Barcelona
city).


Packages & Access: For individuals or smaller groups, General Admission (weekend “Pelouse” passes)
allows roaming access to grassy spectator areas around the track. This is a budget-friendly starter option,
though limited for structured networking. More popular are Grandstand tickets – e.g., Main Grandstand,
Grandstand J (overlooking Turn 1), etc. These can be bought as 2- or 3-day tickets. For corporate hospitality,
the circuit offers official packages such as the Circuit de Catalunya VIP Gold and Silver suites. These
hospitality suites (often located above the pits or at key turns) come with catering, an open bar, and pit lane
visits. The Formula 1 Paddock Club is also present at Barcelona, typically housed above the pit garages/
start line, giving VIPs a prime view of the pit stops and podium ceremony. Paddock Club in Spain includes

gourmet Spanish cuisine, fine wines, pit lane walks each day, and F1 driver appearances, as per F1’s
standard offering 48 4 . For an intermediate tier, F1 Experiences sometimes runs a Champions Club at
Barcelona – a hospitality venue with a view (for example, overlooking the chicane or main straight), offering
premium food/drinks and an exclusive Q&A with an F1 personality, at a cost lower than Paddock Club.


Networking Highlights: The Barcelona race is known for its relaxed yet professional hospitality
atmosphere. Many Spanish corporations (banks, telecoms, etc.) host clients in the VIP suites, which means
plenty of regional business networking. Additionally, because Barcelona is a hub for tech startups and
innovation (e.g., it hosts Mobile World Congress), you’ll find tech executives and sponsors at the Grand Prix
as guests of big tech partners (like Telefónica/Movistar, a past F1 sponsor). The Paddock Club and suite
guests often network over tapas and cava, combining business talk with the excitement of the race. While
not as glitzy as Monaco, Barcelona’s advantage is that the city’s nightlife is easily accessible – companies
often organize evening networking dinners or parties in Barcelona after each day at the circuit. The
circuit’s official hospitality slogan emphasizes unique experiences, and indeed guests often get behind-the-
scenes tours (paddock visits) where serendipitous meetings with team executives or drivers can occur.
Overall, the Spanish GP provides a balanced environment for both avid racing fans and corporate
networking, given its mix of passionate local ambiance and structured VIP services.


Official Links: The official F1 ticket site and the circuit’s site ( circuitcat.com ) list “Hospitality VIP”
packages for the Spanish GP. F1 Experiences also details Spain packages, including Paddock Club bookings
 3 and Champions Club availability.




Canadian Grand Prix (Montreal) – June 13–15, 2025
The Canadian Grand Prix at Circuit Gilles Villeneuve in Montreal is set for June 13–15, 2025 20 . Montreal’s
race is a favorite on the calendar, known for its festive atmosphere. It also has a significant corporate
turnout, with many North American companies using the event for client entertainment, given Montreal’s
ease of access from the US.


Packages & Access: General Admission in Montreal (commonly called “Bronze” areas) allows fans to watch
from the grass or standing areas on the island – an affordable starter option but limited in comfort.
Grandstand seats are the most common purchase: Grandstand 11/12 (Senna corner), Grandstand 31
(Hairpin), and the Main Grandstand are coveted spots. These can be purchased as 3-day packages for
individuals or groups. For hospitality, the Canadian GP offers the Elite Lounge and Loges Suites. The Loges
are private suites (for 40-50 people) overlooking the track (usually near the start/finish or hairpin), often
rented by corporations. They include catered meals, bar service, and VIP parking. There’s also a Club
Canada package (an upscale shared hospitality tent with dining and an appearance by a racing celebrity).
As usual, the F1 Paddock Club is available – in Montreal it’s typically located across from the pit lane (on the
outside of the track near the start line) due to space constraints. Paddock Club guests in Montreal get
terrace viewing, gourmet catering (often with local flavor, like Quebec specialties), unlimited champagne,
and pit lane access each day 4 . F1 Experiences might also run a Champions Club at Montreal, such as a
hospitality suite at the hairpin where guests get an F1 executive Q&A in addition to premium food and open
bar.


Networking Highlights: Montreal is famous for turning the city into a giant party during GP week – “Grand
Prix Week” in downtown features festivals and car shows. This translates into plenty of informal networking
opportunities at restaurants and events in the city. On the circuit premises, the VIP areas are buzzing with a

Street (Toronto) finance companies reward clients or brokers with Paddock Club passes in Montreal. The
atmosphere is convivial – think less formal than Middle East races, but very high energy. Often, networking
is facilitated by the communal nature of suites on Ile Notre-Dame; for example, a hospitality tent might
host multiple companies, creating a shared space to meet new people. Additionally, the Senna Club (if
running) or similar top-tier lounges gather prominent figures (the Montreal race has seen attendances from
figures like business magnate Lawrence Stroll, who often hosts guests when present). Overall, the Canadian
GP offers a lively networking scene with a backdrop of great racing and citywide celebrations, ideal for
mixing business with pleasure.


Official Links: The official Canadian GP website ( gpcanada.ca ) provides info on suites and VIP packages.
F1 Experiences also lists Canada VIP Packages, including Paddock Club tickets and possibly local “F1

Austrian Grand Prix (Spielberg) – June 27–29, 2025
The Austrian Grand Prix at the Red Bull Ring in Spielberg is scheduled for June 27–29, 2025 22 . Set in the
Styrian hills, this race has a relaxed, scenic atmosphere. While it’s heavily attended by passionate fans (lots
of Max Verstappen/Red Bull supporters), it also features Red Bull’s signature hospitality for corporate
guests and high-profile attendees.


Packages & Access: General admission (Steiermark region grassy hills) is a popular starter option for fans
to camp and watch the race, though not a typical corporate choice. Grandstand seats (such as the Start-
Finish grandstand or the “Nord” and “Süd” stands) can be purchased for the weekend; companies
sometimes buy blocks of these for employee incentives. For upscale experiences, the Red Bull Ring has the
“Steiermark” and “Legends” Club hospitality areas. These offer covered dining, drinks, and views of key
sections. The F1 Paddock Club at Austria is usually located above the pits/main straight, offering the
standard top-tier hospitality – gourmet Austrian-influenced cuisine, open bar (yes, Red Bull cocktails
abound), pit lane walks and possibly a visit from Red Bull Racing team members given their home race.
Moreover, Red Bull Racing (as the circuit owner) often creates special experiences: for instance, there might
be a Red Bull Racing Team Garage Tour for select VIPs or a chance to visit the Red Bull motorhome for
partners 58 . F1 Experiences also might provide a “Hall of Fame” or Champions Club at the Red Bull Ring
with celebrity appearances (given Red Bull’s ties to sports, sometimes famous athletes attend and mingle).


Networking Highlights: The Austrian GP has a unique flavor because Red Bull’s corporate presence
looms large. This race is a prime destination for Red Bull GmbH’s partners, distributors, and sponsored
athletes. The energy drink giant usually hosts numerous VIP guests (from extreme sports athletes to
business partners) in hospitality – which means if you’re in those circles, the networking is top-notch.
Outside of Red Bull, many European industrial companies (Austria and Germany-based) use the event for
client entertainment. The vibe in the Paddock Club is a bit more casual (Austrian countryside setting) but
can quickly turn into after-hours networking at the exclusive “Hangar-7” in Salzburg or at Red Bull’s owned
hotel properties, where invite-only parties happen. While Spielberg is a small town, the concentration of
VIPs at the circuit’s lounges (and perhaps in the drivers’ hotel in Graz) ensures that those with access will
have ample opportunity to meet industry leaders in an informal environment. The Legends Club at Red Bull
Ring, for instance, often hosts former drivers or team principals as guest speakers, adding value for guests
who might be looking to connect with motorsport insiders.

British Grand Prix (Silverstone, UK) – July 4–6, 2025
The British Grand Prix at Silverstone (July 4–6, 2025) 24 is one of the most historic and well-attended
races. It’s also a major corporate hospitality event, given many F1 teams and sponsors are UK-based.
Silverstone’s expansive hospitality offerings and huge crowds make it a key networking occasion in the UK’s
sporting calendar.


Packages & Access: Silverstone offers a wide range of options. General admission (access to spectator
areas like Luffield or Stowe banks) is the budget starter option for fans – not especially suited for business,
as it’s first-come lawn seating. More appropriate for business use are Grandstand tickets (e.g.,
International Pit Straight, Becketts, Woodcote grandstands). Companies sometimes purchase grandstand
blocks or reserve the Silverstone Six grandstand which has a nearby hospitality tent. Silverstone is famous
for its official hospitality: the circuit has multiple club facilities, such as Ignition Club, Fusion Lounge,
Legends Club, Trackside Terrace, and the Heritage Club. For example, Ignition Club offers views of six
corners, a gourmet buffet, an open bar, and even guest speakers like F1 personalities for Q&A sessions 25
 26 . The Fusion Lounge is a trendy hospitality suite with garden areas, a rooftop deck, and live

entertainment – great for mingling. At the very top, the Formula 1 Paddock Club at Silverstone sits above
the pit garages on the start/finish straight, delivering the premier VIP experience: exquisite dining,
champagne, pit access, and terrace viewing of the pit lane and podium. Given Silverstone’s layout, Paddock
Club and many hospitality lounges have excellent sightlines. There are also Team Paddock Club suites –
e.g., Mercedes or Red Bull may have their own section for team sponsors, which sometimes can be booked
through F1 Experiences if space allows. Additionally, some companies opt for private suites in the
Silverstone Wing (the main pit building) or at Stowe for a more secluded corporate gathering.


Networking Highlights: The British GP is often sold out in hospitality because nearly every major
sponsor and partner in F1 has a presence. You’ll find a who’s who of the motorsport industry – team
executives, engineers off-duty, VIP guests of drivers, etc. – circulating in Silverstone’s VIP enclosures. This
event is especially valuable for B2B networking in automotive, engineering, and finance sectors (lots of City
of London firms treat it as an outing). The atmosphere in Silverstone’s corporate areas is lively; for instance,
the Ignition Club includes live entertainment and guest talks, which can spark conversations among guests.
Silverstone’s official materials call it a “classic must-see race” with unique VIP packages 60 , emphasizing both
the heritage and the high-end experience. Many UK-based corporations host hospitality days here – it’s
common to see big consulting or banking firms having reserved tables. Because of the large number of VIP

that many areas are packaged with hospitality or perks. On the premium side, the Las Vegas Paddock Club
is unlike any other: it’s hosted on a newly built permanent Paddock building. Notably, Vegas has a Paddock
Club™ Rooftop that gives 360° views of the Strip circuit 68 . Paddock Club in Vegas includes ultra-premium
amenities – think celebrity-chef dining, top-shelf open bars, nightclub-style entertainment within the club,
and the usual pit access and driver appearances. There were also Skybox Suites and exclusive viewing
terraces atop casino properties (for instance, some hotel partners offered VIP packages with balconies
overlooking the track). Additionally, teams and sponsors created their own venues: the Mercedes-AMG
Petronas “Las Vegas Club” was a three-floor hospitality space blending racing and Vegas showtime,
complete with live entertainment from a Cirque-style troupe 37 69 . Other teams, like Red Bull and Ferrari,
had high-end club spaces for their partners. For mid-tier options, Vegas had things like the “Legacy”
grandstand packages with food included, and experiences in the Fan Zone with VIP areas. Essentially,
Vegas provided a tiered system: Starter grandstand, then club-level seats with catering, then ultra VIP in
Paddock Club or hotel-suite packages.


Networking Highlights: The Las Vegas GP is networking gold due to the novelty and exclusivity. The 2024
race saw a large influx of corporate groups treating it as a luxury retreat. The city’s entertainment focus
meant many events around the race: for example, there were networking mixers at winemaker lounges,
casino-hosted high-roller events with F1 themes, and even a dedicated Local Business Network that F1
launched to connect teams with local Vegas businesses 70 . In hospitality, you’re rubbing shoulders with
top executives who splurged on this event – tech billionaires, Hollywood celebrities, sports team owners,
etc. The team hospitality clubs like Mercedes’ All-In Club (sold out quickly) were essentially private clubs
where VIPs could interact with team personnel in a casual environment, all while enjoying Vegas-caliber
shows 37 . Because the race is at night on Saturday, the whole week in Vegas becomes an F1 festival,
culminating in a weekend of high-end parties. Expect invitation-only nightclub events (with superstar
DJs), gala dinners at resorts (some tied to F1’s charity efforts), and casino parties where business tycoons
network over gambling tables. Even the practice sessions and qualifiers saw VIP suites buzzing since many
guests arrive by Thursday and make it a multi-day affair. An observation: Las Vegas naturally encourages
deal-making and networking; people are in a celebratory, opulent mood, which can ease business
discussions. In summary, the Vegas GP offers perhaps the most extravagant networking setting in F1,
where business meetings might happen over champagne at 1am while the city’s neon lights blaze – truly a
merger of business and pleasure Vegas-style.

Qatar Grand Prix (Lusail) – November 28–30, 2025
The Qatar Grand Prix at Lusail International Circuit is scheduled for Nov 28–30, 2025 38 . Qatar, having
debuted in 2021 and become a regular, is a night race at a circuit outside Doha. It’s heavily supported by the
state and offers high-end hospitality, aligning with Qatar’s luxury event strategy.


Packages & Access: General admission at Lusail (if available) would allow access to certain grassy areas,
but much of Lusail’s viewing is via grandstands. Grandstands include the Main Grandstand and others
around a few corners. The local organizer often includes extras even for standard tickets (like fan zone
access, etc.). For hospitality, the Formula 1 Paddock Club at Qatar is typically atop the pit building, offering
VIP guests a plush indoor setting (vital in desert climates for comfort) and a rooftop or terrace view.
Paddock Club in Qatar features world-class catering, open bars with champagne, and pit lane walks daily.
Qatar, being a wealthy nation, also offers VIP Tower and Club suites. Lusail has a distinctive Media/Club
building at Turn 10 often repurposed for hospitality: from there guests can view a large portion of the track
and enjoy fine dining. There’s also the possibility to watch from luxury VIP boxes in the pit building aside
from Paddock Club – these might be reserved for sponsors and dignitaries (the emir and royal family often
attend, and they host VVIP guests in exclusive boxes). F1 Experiences likely has offerings in Qatar too,
possibly including guided paddock tours or a Champions Club if demand calls for it.


Networking Highlights: Qatar’s GP is a prime networking event in the Middle East, second only to Abu
Dhabi. It attracts a similar profile of attendees: royals, ministers, CEOs from the Gulf region, and
international executives interested in the Middle East market. Given Qatar’s investment in sports (they
hosted FIFA World Cup 2022), they utilize F1 to strengthen business relationships. You might find that
official government delegations (from finance, tourism boards, etc.) are present in hospitality to court
investors. The environment in Paddock Club is luxurious and somewhat formal; dress code and etiquette
might be slightly more conservative given local customs (though international norms largely apply).
Nonetheless, once the champagne flows, the networking is lively. Often there are adjacent events like a
motorsport business forum or a summit on sports technology in Doha during F1 week, where one could
network in a conference setting before meeting the same people informally at the track. Many corporate
guests also enjoy Qatar’s attractions – e.g., a group might be invited to a desert safari or a dinner at the
Museum of Islamic Art as part of the GP hospitality program – which further facilitates relationship-building
in unique settings. In sum, the Qatar GP provides a high-caliber networking arena with an exclusive guest
list. Because it’s a relatively new event, the numbers are smaller than Abu Dhabi, potentially allowing more
face-time with key individuals. It’s a great place to connect with stakeholders in infrastructure, energy, and
tourism sectors, all while enjoying state-of-the-art hospitality.

Abu Dhabi Grand Prix (Yas Marina) – December 5–7, 2025
The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix at Yas Marina Circuit is the final race of 2025, set for Dec 5–7, 2025 39 . As the
season finale and an established luxury event, Abu Dhabi is renowned for its corporate hospitality and
star-studded attendance. It’s a destination where business, sport, and entertainment converge.


Packages & Access: While general admission exists (Yas Marina has Abu Dhabi Hill, a GA viewing mound),
the emphasis is on grandstands and hospitality. Grandstands like North (views of the hairpin and longest
straight), West (Marina view), South (near the hotel section), and Main Grandstand can be bought as 2- or 3-
day tickets. Many fans opt for these, and companies may purchase blocks especially in Main or North
grandstands to give guests a taste of the action. The real highlight is the array of hospitality: the Formula 1
Paddock Club at Abu Dhabi sits atop the pit garages, providing an opulent experience – gourmet
international cuisine, premium beverages (often including bespoke cocktails), pit lane walks, and wonderful
rooftop viewing as day turns to night during the race. In addition, Yas Marina Circuit has Yas Suites
(trackside suites in various locations, like near Turn 1 or the chicane) for corporate hire. The Marsa Suites
and Shams Suites are examples of hospitality packages that include fine dining, balcony seating, and visits
from F1 personalities. The circuit’s iconic W Hotel (formerly Yas Hotel, now W Abu Dhabi – Yas Island)
straddling the track also offers hospitality: many rooms and rooftop venues become VIP viewing areas that
can be reserved. For example, the hotel’s Skylounge becomes a premium viewing party. There’s also the
Yas Marina Yacht Club where superyachts line the marina – similar to Monaco, companies or wealthy
individuals rent yachts and host private hospitality parties on-board with direct track view. F1 Experiences
sells Paddock Club and sometimes “Legend” packages (like meet-and-greet with an F1 legend at Abu Dhabi).


Networking Highlights: Abu Dhabi’s finale status makes it a magnet for VIPs. It’s common for business
councils and international trade delegations to coordinate their meetings around this event 72 . For
instance, the Australia-UAE Business Council led a delegation for high-level meetings and networking over
the GP weekend 72 , including an exclusive F1 dinner for business leaders 73 . The Paddock Club and
surrounding VIP areas bustle with global CEOs, Middle Eastern royalty, celebrities, and even government
leaders, all in a celebratory mood to cap off the season. Abu Dhabi also hosts the Yasalam After-Race
Concerts (world-famous artists perform each night), and VIP guests get premium access. These concerts
and the Amber Lounge after-party (hosted in Abu Dhabi as well) are prime social mixers. Additionally,
because it’s the last race, many F1 teams and sponsors throw end-of-season parties on Sunday night –
often at lavish locations like rooftop clubs or beach clubs – where those with the right connections can
attend and network with the F1 fraternity and business partners. The U.S.-U.A.E. Business Council noted that
their board members “enjoyed the many festivities surrounding Abu Dhabi’s... Grand Prix” as far back as 2010 74 ,
illustrating the long tradition of corporate festivity here. The Yas Marina setting, with yachts and the
illuminated hotel, gives a classy backdrop where introductions are easily made (people hop between yacht
parties and club suites). High-profile individuals from Richard Branson to Prince Albert of Monaco have
been spotted in the Abu Dhabi Paddock Club in years past 75 , underlining the level of networking possible
(where else could you casually chat with a prince or a billionaire investor?). In essence, the Abu Dhabi GP is
often the climax of F1’s corporate networking year – a place to solidify relationships formed over the
season and engage in new opportunities amid gala events and racing action.


Official Links: Yas Marina Circuit’s official site provides details on hospitality packages (like “Club Yas”,
“Lounge @ Turn 1”, etc.). F1 Experiences offers official Abu Dhabi packages (Paddock Club, maybe team
hospitality options like Ferrari F1 Club). Prospective yacht hospitality can be arranged via the Yas Marina
Yacht Club or specialized agencies.
