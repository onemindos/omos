## **Streamlined Master Plan to Get All Reports Quickly and Accurately**

### **Step 1: Create a Report Request Packet**

Prepare **one universal packet** that you’ll mail or fax to each agency. This avoids online rejections due to name mismatch and gives you a paper trail.

### **Include the following in every packet**:

1. **Cover Letter** (customizable template below)
2. **Proof of Identity (2+ forms recommended)**:
    - Copy of your driver’s license or state ID (with new name)
    - Social Security card (or W-2/1099 with full SSN)
    - Recent utility bill, bank statement, or lease (showing current address)
    - Legal **Name Change Document** (court order, marriage cert, etc.)
