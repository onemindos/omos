INTRODUCTION

WHEREAS, Founders Marketing possess certain confidential proprietary
information, and

WHEREAS, in connection with the pursuit, evaluation and/or feasibility
of a business relationship, and/or consummation of a transa ction
between Contractor or Employee, The Executive Credit Repair,
confidential proprietary information of The Credit Executive may become
available to Contractor or Employee; and

WHEREAS, The Credit Executive desire to prevent the unauthorized use and
disclosure of it confidential proprietary information;

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of this premises and other good and
valuable consideration, Receipt of which is hereby acknowledged, the
parties agree as follows:

I.  "Confidential Information." For purposes of this Agreement,
    > Confidential Information shall mean all product and service
    > development plans, product and services internal marketing
    > processes, strategic and development plans, financial condition,
    > business plans, co-developer identities, data, business records,
    > client/customer list, project records, market reports,employee
    > list and business manuals, policies and procedures, information
    > relating to processes, technologies or theory and all other
    > information which may be disclosed by The Credit Executive or to
    > which Contractor/Employee may be provided access by The Credit
    > Executive or other in accordance with this Agreement, or which is
    > generated as a result of or in connection with the Business
    > Purposes, which is not generally available to the public.

II. Non Disclosure Obligations. Contractor or Employee promises and
    > agrees to receive and hold the Confidential Information in
    > confidence. Without limiting the generality of the foregoing,
    > Contractor or Employee further agrees:

    A.  To protect and safeguard the Confidential Information against
        > unauthorized use, publication or disclosure;

    B.  Not to use any of the Confidential Information except for the
        > Business Purpose;

    C.  Not to, directly or indirectly, in any way, reveal, report,
        > publish, disclose, transfer or otherwise use any of the
        > Confidential Information except as specifically authorized by
        > the The Credit Executive in accordance with this Confidential
        > Agreement;

    D.  Not use any Confidential Information to unfairly compete or
        > obtain unfair advantage of The Credit Executive in any
        > commercial activity which may be comparable to the commercial
        > activity contemplated by the parties in connection with the
        > Business Purposes;

    E.  To restrict access to the Confidential Information to those who
        > clearly need such access to carry out of the Business
        > Purposes;

    F.  To advise each of the persons to whom it provides access to any
        > of the Confidential Information, that such persons are
        > strictly prohibited from making any use, publishing or
        > otherwise disclosing to others, or permitting others to use
        > for their benefit or to the detriment of The Credit Executive,
        > any of the Confidential Information, and,upon request of the
        > Credit Executive, to provide The Credit Executive with a copy
        > of a written agreement to that effect signed by such person:

    G.  To comply with any other reasonable security measures requested
        > in writing by The Credit Executive.

III. Exceptions. The confidentiality obligations hereunder shall not
     > apply to Confidential Information which:

     A.  Is, or later becomes, public knowledge other than by breach of
         > the provisions of this Agreement; or

     B.  is in the possession of a Contractor or Employee with the full
         > right to disclose prior to its receipt from The Credit
         > Executive, as evidenced by written records.

IV. Return of Confidential Information. Contractor or Employee agrees,
    > upon termination of the Business Purpose or upon the written
    > request of The Credit Executive., whichever is earlier, to
    > promptly deliver to The Credit Executive all records, notes, and
    > other written, printed, or tangible materials in the possession of
    > Contractor or Employee, embodying or pertaining to the
    > Confidential Information.

V.  No Right to Confidential Information

    A.  Contractor or Employee hereby agrees and acknowledges that no
        > license, either express or implied, is hereby granted to
        > Contractor or Employees by The Credit Executive to use any of
        > the Confidential Information.

    B.  Contractor or Employee further agrees that all improvements,
        > copyrightable works, methods, compositions, or product of The
        > Credit Executive directly resulting from or relating to the
        > Confidential Information and the right to market, use, license
        > and franchise the Confidential Information or the ideas,
        > concepts, methods or practices embodied therein shall be the
        > exclusive property of The Credit Executive, and Contractor or
        > Employee has no right or title thereto.

VI. Non-solicitation of The Credit Executive Customer or Employees.
    > Contractor or Employee hereby agrees that for a period of two
    > years following termination of this Agreement for any reason,
    > Contractor or Employee will not interfere with The Credit
    > Executive, either produced directly and solely by the Contractor
    > or Employee by The Credit Executive, or by any customer of The
    > Credit Executive acquired by The Credit Executive, Contractor or
    > Employee also agrees for a period of two years following
    > termination of this Agreement for any reason not to directly or
    > indirectly solicit any employee of The Credit Executive's to leave
    > that The Credit Executive employs.

    i.  Term and Termination.This Agreement shall commence on the data
        > first written above. Contractor or Employee's right to use the
        > Confidential Information in connection with the Business
        > Purposes shall continue in effect until The Credit Executive
        > provides the Contractor or Employee with written notice of
        > termination of such right. Notwithstanding the foregoing
        > Contractor or Employee's obligations with respect to the
        > Confidential Information hereunder shall continue in full
        > force and effect until further notice from The Credit
        > Executive.

VII. Remedies. Contractor or Employee understands and acknowledges that
     > any disclosure or misappropriation of any of the Confidential
     > Information in violation of this Agreement may cause The Credit
     > Executive irreparable harm, the amount of which may be difficult
     > to ascertain, and, therefore agrees that The Credit Executive
     > shall have the right to apply a court of competent jurisdiction
     > for an order restraining any such further disclosure or
     > misappropriation and for such other relief as The Credit
     > Executive shall deem appropriate. Such right of The Credit
     > Executive shall be in addition to Remedies otherwise available
     > for The Credit Executive at law or in equity.

VIII. Successors and Assigns. Contractor or Employee shall have no
      > rights under this Agreement, whether expressly or by operation
      > of law, without the written consent of The Credit Executive.
      > This Agreement and Contractor's or Employee's obligations
      > hereunder shall be binding on Representatives, permitted
      > assigns, and successor of Contractor or Employee and shall
      > insure to the benefit of Representative, assigns and successors
      > of The Credit Executive.

IX. Governing Laws. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in
    > accordance with the laws on the Republic of the Philippines under
    > the Department of Trade and Industry and Department of Labor and
    > Employment.

X.  Attorney's Fees. If any action at law or in equity is brought to
    > enforce or interpret the provisions of this Agreement, the
    > prevailing party in such action shall be entitled to reasonable
    > attorney's fees.

XI. Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the sole understanding
    > of the parties about this subject matter and may not be amended or
    > modified except in writing signed by each of the parties to the
    > Agreement.

On termination of these Terms:

The Credit Executive shall immediately pay to the Contractor or Employee
of the outstanding unpaid time. The Contractor or Employee, from one day
from the date of such termination, cease to have access to any
particular Brief:

> Termination of expiry of this Agreement shall not affect by any
> rights, remedies, obligations or liabilities of the parties, that have
> accrued up to date or termination or expiry, including the right to
> claim damages in respect of any breach of the agreement which existed
> at or before the date of termination or expiry.
