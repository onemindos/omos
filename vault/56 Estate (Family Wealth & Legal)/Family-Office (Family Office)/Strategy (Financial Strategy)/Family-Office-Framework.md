# Delacruz Family Office — Framework

> *A family office is not just for billionaires. It is a mindset — a system for managing, growing, and protecting family wealth with intention across generations.*

---

## What Is the Delacruz Family Office

The Delacruz Family Office (DFO) is the internal structure that governs how the family:
- Makes investment decisions
- Manages cash flow across entities
- Plans for taxes
- Prepares the next generation to steward wealth
- Protects assets through trusts, insurance, and legal structures

It is not a registered entity — it is a *function*. The family office lives in how Zeus operates.

---

## Governance Philosophy

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Stewardship over ownership** | We are custodians of wealth for the family line, not consumers of it |
| **Structure before spending** | Every dollar gets a category before it moves |
| **Diversification by design** | No single point of failure in assets, income, or entities |
| **Education is inheritance** | Teaching children about money is as important as leaving them money |
| **Patience compounds** | Long time horizons beat short-term gains |
