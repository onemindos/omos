## Purpose

Generational-Wealth is the **generational wealth center**. This area covers estate planning, wealth transfer, family office functions, and building financial legacy that transcends a single lifetime.

## What Lives Here

- **Estate Planning** — Wills, trusts, succession plans
- **Wealth Transfer** — Inheritance strategy, gifting, education
- **Family Governance** — Family council, decision structures
- **Asset Protection** — Legal structures, risk mitigation
- **Philanthropy** — Charitable giving, family foundation
