# Delacruz Estate Plan

> *The legal backbone of the Delacruz legacy — how everything we build transfers to the next generation intact.*

---

## Purpose

This is the master estate planning document. It governs how the Delacruz family's assets — real estate, business interests, investments, and personal property — are protected during life and transferred at death.

The goal is not just to pass down money. It is to pass down **structure** — a functioning family office, a working trust, and a set of values that governs how wealth is held and grown.

---

| Instrument | Purpose | Status |

## Key Principles

- **Avoid probate** — everything of significance should be titled in the trust or have named beneficiaries
- **Protect minors** — trust holds assets for children until defined ages (suggest: 25% at 25, 25% at 30, 50% at 35)
- **Business continuity** — business interests need a succession plan separate from personal estate
- **Keep it updated** — review annually and after every major life event (new child, new property, marriage)
