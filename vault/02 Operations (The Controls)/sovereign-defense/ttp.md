# TTP — Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures

## Definition
The structured breakdown of how military and security operations are executed. Standard doctrine across DoD, intelligence community, and law enforcement.

| Layer | Definition | Example |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| **Tactic** | The high-level goal of an action | Suppress enemy air defense |
| **Technique** | The method used to achieve it | SEAD — Suppression of Enemy Air Defenses |
| **Procedure** | Exact step-by-step execution | Specific sortie profile, loadout, ingress/egress routes |

## Military Context
- TTPs are codified in field manuals (FMs), technical manuals (TMs), and SOPs
- Units train to TTPs so execution is consistent regardless of who's in the seat
- Classified TTPs = operational security — not written down in open channels
- TTPs evolve after AARs (After Action Reviews) — what worked, what didn't

## Cybersecurity Adoption
MITRE ATT&CK framework catalogs adversary TTPs:
- **Tactics** = attacker goals (Initial Access, Persistence, Exfiltration)
- **Techniques** = how they achieve them (Phishing, Credential Dumping)
- **Procedures** = specific observed implementations by threat actors
