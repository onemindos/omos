## Synopsis

An ex-con and master thief assumes the identity of the sheriff of Banshee, a small town in Pennsylvania Amish country. As the new sheriff, "Lucas Hood" must navigate local criminal elements, his own violent past, and his complicated relationship with his former lover, now married to the town's most dangerous crime boss.
