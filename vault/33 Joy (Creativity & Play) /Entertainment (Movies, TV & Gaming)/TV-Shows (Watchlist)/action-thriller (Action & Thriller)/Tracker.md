## Synopsis

Colter Shaw is a lone-wolf survivalist who roams the country as a reward seeker, using his expert tracking skills to help private citizens and law enforcement solve mysteries while collecting the bounties. His skills were honed by his survivalist father, and he applies his unique expertise to find missing persons and solve crimes that others can't.
