## Synopsis

LAPD Detective Harry Bosch works in the Hollywood Division's homicide unit. A man who lives by his own moral code, Bosch navigates the complexities of L.A.'s criminal underworld while dealing with his own traumatic past—including his mother's unsolved murder.
