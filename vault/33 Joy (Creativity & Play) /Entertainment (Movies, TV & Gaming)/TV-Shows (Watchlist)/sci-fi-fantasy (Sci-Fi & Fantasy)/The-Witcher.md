## Synopsis

Geralt of Rivia, a mutated monster-hunter known as a Witcher, struggles to find his place in a world where people often prove more wicked than beasts. His path becomes intertwined with a powerful sorceress named Yennefer and a young princess named Ciri, who holds a dangerous secret.
