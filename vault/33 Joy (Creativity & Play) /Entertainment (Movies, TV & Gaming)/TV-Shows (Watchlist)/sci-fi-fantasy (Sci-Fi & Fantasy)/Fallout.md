## Synopsis

Set in a post-apocalyptic Los Angeles, 200 years after a nuclear war, the series follows Lucy, a vault dweller who ventures out into the wasteland for the first time searching for her father. She encounters the brutal surface world filled with mutants, raiders, and the mysterious power struggle between factions fighting for control of the irradiated landscape.
