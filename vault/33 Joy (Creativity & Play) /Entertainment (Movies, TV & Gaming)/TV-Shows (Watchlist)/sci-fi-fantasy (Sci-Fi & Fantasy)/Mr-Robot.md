## Synopsis

Elliot Alderson, a cybersecurity engineer and vigilante hacker with social anxiety disorder and dissociative identity disorder, is recruited by Mr. Robot, the mysterious leader of an anarchist hacktivist group called fsociety. They aim to take down E Corp, a massive conglomerate, and erase all consumer debt by encrypting their financial data.
