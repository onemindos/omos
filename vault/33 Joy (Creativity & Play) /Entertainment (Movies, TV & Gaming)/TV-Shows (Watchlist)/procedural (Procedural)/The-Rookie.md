## Synopsis

John Nolan, a 45-year-old man from Pennsylvania, moves to Los Angeles and becomes the oldest rookie in the LAPD. Despite facing skepticism and physical challenges compared to younger officers, Nolan is determined to prove himself and pursue his dream of being a police officer.
