## Synopsis

After being shipwrecked and stranded on a hostile island for five years, billionaire playboy Oliver Queen returns home to Star City determined to fight crime and corruption as a hooded vigilante. Armed with his bow and a list of corrupt individuals, he becomes the Green Arrow.
