## Synopsis

More than a decade after the events of the first film, Jake Sully and Neytiri have formed a family and are doing everything to stay together. When a familiar threat returns, Jake must work with the Metkayina, a reef-dwelling clan led by Tonowari and Roneai, to protect his family and their new home.
