## Synopsis

In 2029, machines rule the Earth after the AI Skynet triggered nuclear war. A cyborg assassin—the Terminator—is sent back to 1984 Los Angeles to kill Sarah Connor, whose unborn son John will lead the human resistance. The resistance sends soldier Kyle Reese to protect her. A deadly chase ensues as the unstoppable machine hunts its target.
