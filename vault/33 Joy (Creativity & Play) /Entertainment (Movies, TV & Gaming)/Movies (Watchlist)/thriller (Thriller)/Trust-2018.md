## Synopsis

Brooke and Owen are a successful New York couple living in a high-rise apartment. When their art dealer neighbor is murdered, Brooke becomes entangled with a handsome stranger who claims to be investigating the crime. As secrets unravel, the couple's trust in each other—and everyone around them—begins to crumble.
