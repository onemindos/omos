## Synopsis

Mike Blueberry, a U.S. Marshal in the late 1800s American West, must confront his traumatic past when a ruthless criminal named Blount returns seeking an ancient Native American treasure with mystical powers. Blueberry's journey leads him through shamanic visions and ayahuasca-induced spiritual experiences as he battles both external enemies and inner demons.
