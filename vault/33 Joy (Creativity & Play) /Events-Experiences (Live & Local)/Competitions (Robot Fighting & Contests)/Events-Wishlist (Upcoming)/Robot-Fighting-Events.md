# Robot Fighting Events

> Combat robotics competitions. Robots are built to fight in an arena — weight classes from 1lb (antweights) up to full combat (250lb +). Blends engineering, strategy, and spectacle.

---

## Major Events / Organizations

| Event | Scale | Location | Notes |
|-------|-------|----------|-------|
| **BattleBots** | TV / National | US | ABC/Discovery, top-tier builders |
| **RoboGames** | International | San Mateo, CA | Multiple weight classes, largest open tournament |
| **Robotic Death Match** | Regional | Various | Entry-level-friendly |
| **Antweight World Series** | UK-based | UK + online | 150g class, very active |
| **SoCal Maker Combat** | Regional | Southern CA | |
| **Robot Battles** | Regional | Dragon Con, Atlanta | Annual at Dragon Con |
