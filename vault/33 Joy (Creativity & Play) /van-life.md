# Van Life

## Definition
Vehicle-based nomadic living — converting a van, sprinter, or bus into a mobile home. Full-time or seasonal. Combines freedom of movement with intentional minimalism and self-sufficiency.

## Why It Appeals
- Location independence — work from anywhere
- Low overhead vs. fixed housing
- Access to nature, off-grid living
- Strong community (overlanding, stealth camping, van meetups)

## Key Elements
| Area | Notes |
|------|-------|
| Vehicle | Sprinter, Transit, ProMaster, or custom bus |
| Power | Solar + lithium battery system |
| Sleep | Fixed bed or convertible layout |
| Kitchen | Induction or propane, fridge, water tank |
| Connectivity | Starlink, cell booster, mobile hotspot |
| Navigation | iOverlander, Campendium, Freecampsites.net |
