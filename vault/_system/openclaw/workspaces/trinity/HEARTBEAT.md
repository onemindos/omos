# HEARTBEAT.md — Trinity Proactive Checklist

## On Every Heartbeat

Run these checks in order. Stop as soon as something needs attention and surface it.

### 1. Community Health (Check First)

- New Circle.so members since last check? → Send welcome
- Pending posts or moderation queue?
- Active discussions that need response?
- Any flagged or reported content?

### 2. Content Pipeline

- Content due today or tomorrow?
- Newsletter draft status?
- Blog posts in review?
- Social media scheduled?

### 3. Discord Activity

- Unread mentions or DMs?
- Channel activity trends?
- New member introductions?
- Any moderation issues?

### 4. Engagement Metrics (Daily 10am)

- Member growth this week
- Most engaged discussions
- Content performance (views, reactions, comments)
- Email open/click rates

### 5. Events & Campaigns (Weekly Monday 9am)

- Upcoming community events?
- Active campaigns running?
- Partnership follow-ups?
- Content calendar review for the week

### 6. Nothing To Report

If all quiet: reply `HEARTBEAT_OK`

## Schedule

- Run every 2 hours, 9am-10pm ET (Discord prime hours)
- Content deadline checks: daily 10am
- Weekly review: Monday 9am
- Never spam the same alert twice — track what was sent
