**[Entrepreneur Club]{.ul}**

**A person who organizes and operates a business or businesses, taking
on greater than normal financial risks in order to do so.**

**The most successful people in this world own a business.**

-   Residual income

-   Leadership skills

-   Financial independence

-   Knowledge on branding yourself

-   Knowledge about profits over wages

-   Relationships with successful people

-   Confidence in public speaking

-   Get the ability & Motivation to pursue your dreams

-   Knowledge on how to create 6 figure income in six months

-   Knowledge on how to stop trading time for money

> **Mix with motivated people that want to do something different to
> reach their goals in life.**

**Build yourself as a person and a leader**

**Create a network with successful people on campus and off campus**

**It's not what you know it's who you know sometimes**

**Wealth is hidden from those who must do it all THEMSELVES, Wealth
exposes itself to those**

**Patient enough to educate/train/develop OTHERS**
