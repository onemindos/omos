The world is a nasty place, I don't care how tough beat to knees and
keep you their no one gonna hit as hard as life; it's not about how you
hit it's about how hard you can get hit and keep going

You got a dream you got to protect it, you want something go get period

He who says he can and he who says he can't are both right

Who do you want to be to be figure out what makes you happy no matter
how crazy it sounds it's possible

What's it going to be, who you're going to be, how you're going to do it

Why can't you be? Why? I can't see why not.

We can sit there and get the shit kicked out of us or fight back into
the light find our way out of hell on inch at a time,

Watch a lot of young people miss around With no idea Why their doing
what they're doing, To want, to be ambitious and want to be successful
is not enough that's just desire To know what you want, to understand
why you want it, to dedicated every breath in your body to achieve, if
you feel you have something to give and your talent is worth developing
worth caring for than their nothing you can't achieve

When you grow up you tend to get told that the world is the way that it
is & your life is to live your life in world that don't get in trouble,
and maybe get an education and get a job and make some money and have a
family but life can be a lot boarder than that when you realize one
simple thing, that everything around you that you call life was made up
by people that were not smarter than you

Habits are the building blocks of our lives, not the goal, the vision,
the big picture or the big idea; it's what you do every day that matter
more than everything; build good habits that will pull you through to
have a very successful life

Human being / being = existence but who do we existence too? Friends
family why not the whole world but local and not global;

Robbed story -- knowledge is the new money
