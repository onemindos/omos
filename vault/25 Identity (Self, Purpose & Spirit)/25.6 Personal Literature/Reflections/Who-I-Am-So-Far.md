Who I am so far

Opening statement: By the show of hands how who here has ever
experienced something so horrible that they ask themselves why me? And
didn't understand why it was happening to them.

I believe that being born on this earth without a manual of who were
meant to be gives us the opportunity to go into the world and find out
ourselves and I believe that experiences bad or good we go through
create the person who were meant to be

1\) 22 years old 5 years ago I graduated high school was always a d
student because I always was rebellious of school because I felt were
teaching me what to think rather what to think for myself

2\) I tried out college and when I did I went for business and the first
day in class my professor told me that 70% of the businesses I try will
fail. So I asked why I am here. I dropped out and decided to go back to
work and I had no idea what I wanted to do or where I wanted to go

3\) I got a retail job and made money of course but I realized that I
didn't know being told what to do so I saved my money

4\) Went to school and became an emergency medical tech I've seen a lot
of things that not only scared me but changed my perspective on how
valuable life is. I saved lives and also seen what it was to doing
everything that you can possibly do and it still not be enough for
someone to live.

6\) after two years I got into some legal trouble that stopped me from
progressing in my career and hitting a dead end. But after hitting rock
bottom I was forced to reset myself and ask myself what is it that I
truly want to do.

7\) I came to this conclusion that I wanted to help by sharing my story
my perspective because my story can help someone out there being a
thought leader.

The reason for my mini stories is to show you that the experiences I
went through didn't happen to me by coincidence or by accident I believe
they happened to me to create me and to create a message that everyone
can take something away from and learn from it. For years I thought life
was happening to me and I was like why me but then I changed my
perspective and understood that it was happening for me because if
wasn't for those experiences I wouldn't be standing in front you
speaking today.

But this is my message to you, you will go through things you won't
understand and it may feel like it's the end of the world but I promise
you it's not Trust me you're not in a storm you're in transition and
There is no growth without pain

So when you experience something horrible instead of asking why me? Ask
yourself what can I learn and how can this lesson benefit me and
everyone around me and understand that what you went through has made
you who you am so far.
