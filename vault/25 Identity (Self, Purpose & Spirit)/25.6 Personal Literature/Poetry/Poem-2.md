That special someone ;)

I seen u walking one day and there was a halo around your head.

Just like god told me in my dreams that I'll meet an angel one day and
so i did.

Your beauty is like looking at the ocean as the sunset falls.

I\'m only afraid that we\'ll turn out to be nothing after all.

All I can think of is me gazing into your eyes all night

With the thought in your mind that i loves you to the day that I die.

I can watch you softly sound asleep

As I watch the sun rise, rise above my feet.

I would love for you to be mines.

So i can hold you tell you it's alright when you cry.

I'll be with you through bad or good.

Stay by your side just the way I should. But most of all you can hold my
hand because i love you and I'll be with you to the very end.
