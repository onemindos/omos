Love\
\
love a four letter word that could either make or break your life. Sadly
but truly love can control your life if you let it. Happiness comes from
within but we live in a dream thinking happiness comes from things we
either receive or think we need. Sometimes things are not always what
they seem to be. Sometimes im the only one in this world that thinks the
way I think I don't ever find someone to be on my mental status. Its
like everything I talk about people think im crazy but honestly I just
think im different. I honestly wish everyone wasn't so brainwashed and
consumed by technology and learned to spend time with there loved ones
because time is the only think we have in this life and we waste it
looking and watching other people lives instead of enjoying our own. We
feed into the bullshit and negativity of other people that think they
cant make it in life but that's not the case its just that people become
lazy. Im tired of world and way it goes round it doesn't make sense to
me at all. I never thought that emotions could control the best part of
us which is our hearts. We say were human being but humanity isn't human
anymore.
