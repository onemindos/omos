Worth My Heart \<3

When you left; i didn't know what to do.

Because all the time i had to myself; i gave to you.

I always held steady when you was ready to fall.

I treated you like a queen most of all.

When you cried so did i because urn feelings depended on mine.

But i miss you as the days go by.

Worst part is; i didn't even get to say goodbye.

I loved you so much that your problems were mines

And if you were to hurt at any point of the day so would i.

But my love for you couldn't get any deeper.

But the hill we were going down kept getting steeper.

I always fight for watt i love.

But kill for watt i live for; which is you.

But you're the other part of me & without u i can't see myself succeed.

But baby you were the bandage to my wound and without you; it\'ll will
continue to bleed.

But every wound leaves a scar; but at least yours was worth my heart.
\<3
