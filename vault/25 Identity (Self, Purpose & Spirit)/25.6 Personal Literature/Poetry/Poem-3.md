SOMETIMES

Something takes a part of me.

Something lust can never see.

Every time i start to believe.

Something's ripped and taken from me.

Life is always going to be messing with me.

Can't picture myself being left alone and being free.

Cant i take away others pain?

I try to forget all in vain.

Sometimes i cannot take this place.

Sometimes it's my life i just hate.

Sometimes i cannot feel my face.

You'll see that imp not far from brave.
