## USA Street Legality

**Short answer: NO — not legally drivable on public roads in the USA.**

The GTX is classified as a race vehicle / track-only by KTM.

Why it fails U.S. street legality:
- No airbags
- No crash testing certification
- No DOT approval for the canopy
- No emissions certification
- Track-only classification by manufacturer

| Import Path | Street Use Allowed? | Notes |
|-------------|---------------------|-------|
| Track-Only Import | No | Most common — cannot be registered |
| Show & Display | No (for driving) | Can own it; road driving still forbidden |
| State "kit car" loopholes | Very rare | GTX canopy + crash compliance likely fails |
| 25-year rule | Not applicable yet | Car is far too new |
