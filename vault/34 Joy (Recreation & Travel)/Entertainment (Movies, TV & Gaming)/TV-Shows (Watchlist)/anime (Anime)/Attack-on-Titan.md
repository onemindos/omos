## Synopsis

In a world where humanity lives within enormous walled cities to protect themselves from Titans—gigantic humanoid creatures who devour humans—young Eren Yeager vows to exterminate all Titans after witnessing his mother being eaten. As he and his friends join the military, they discover dark secrets about the Titans and their own world.
