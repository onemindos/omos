## Synopsis

An epic romantic adventure based on the life of Billy the Kid—one of America's most infamous outlaws. The series follows him from his Irish roots in New York City slums, to his first murder at age 12 in Kansas, to his years as a cowboy and killer in the American frontier during the Lincoln County War.
