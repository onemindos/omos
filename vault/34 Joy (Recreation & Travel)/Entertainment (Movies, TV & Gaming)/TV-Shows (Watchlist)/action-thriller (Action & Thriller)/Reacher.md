## Synopsis

Jack Reacher, a veteran military police investigator who has just entered civilian life, is a drifter with no phone, no connections, no ties. He's a giant of a man who lives off the grid, traveling the country with just the clothes on his back. When he arrives in a small Georgia town, he's arrested for a murder he didn't commit, uncovering a deadly conspiracy.
