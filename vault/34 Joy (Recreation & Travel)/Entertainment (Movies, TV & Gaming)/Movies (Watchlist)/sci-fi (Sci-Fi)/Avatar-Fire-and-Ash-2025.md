## Synopsis

The third installment continues the saga of the Sully family and their allies as they face new threats on Pandora. The film is expected to introduce the "Ash People" - a fire-based Na'vi clan that represents a darker side of the Na'vi species, challenging Jake's belief that all Na'vi are inherently good.
