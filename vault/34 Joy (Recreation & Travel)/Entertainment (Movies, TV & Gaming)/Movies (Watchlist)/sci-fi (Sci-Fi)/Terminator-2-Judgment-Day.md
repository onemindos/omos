## Synopsis

Ten years after the first film, Skynet sends a new, more advanced Terminator—the liquid metal T-1000—to kill young John Connor. The resistance reprograms a captured T-800 and sends it back to protect John. Now the Terminator is the protector, and Sarah Connor must work alongside the machine she once feared to prevent Judgment Day.
