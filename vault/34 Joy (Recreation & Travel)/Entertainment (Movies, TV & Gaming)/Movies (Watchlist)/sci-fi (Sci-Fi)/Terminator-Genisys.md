## Synopsis

Kyle Reese is sent back to 1984 to protect Sarah Connor, but discovers the timeline has been altered. Sarah was raised by a reprogrammed T-800 she calls "Pops" since she was 9. Now the trio must travel to 2017 to prevent Skynet—now called "Genisys"—from launching as a global operating system that will enslave humanity.
