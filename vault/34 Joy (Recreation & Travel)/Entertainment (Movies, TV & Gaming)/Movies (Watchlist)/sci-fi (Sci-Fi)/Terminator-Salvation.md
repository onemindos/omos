## Synopsis

Set in 2018, Judgment Day has happened and the machines rule. John Connor has risen in the Resistance but isn't yet its leader. Marcus Wright, a death row inmate who donated his body to science, awakens as a human-machine hybrid with no memory of how he became part Terminator. Their paths cross as each questions what it means to be human.
