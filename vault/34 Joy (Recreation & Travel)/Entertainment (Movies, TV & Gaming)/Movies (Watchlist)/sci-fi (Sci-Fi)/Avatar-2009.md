## Synopsis

In 2154, paraplegic Marine Jake Sully is recruited to join the Avatar Program on the distant moon Pandora. Using a genetically engineered Na'vi body, Jake explores the lush alien world and falls in love with Neytiri, a Na'vi princess. When corporate interests threaten to destroy the Na'vi homeland, Jake must choose between following orders and fighting for the world he's come to love.
