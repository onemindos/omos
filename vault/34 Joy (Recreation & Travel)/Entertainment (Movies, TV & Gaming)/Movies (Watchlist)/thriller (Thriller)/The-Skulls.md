## Synopsis

Luke McNamara, a working-class student at an elite Ivy League university, is invited to join the Skulls—a powerful secret society that promises wealth, power, and connections. But when his journalist friend discovers the society's dark secrets and is murdered, Luke must expose the truth while fighting the powerful alumni who will stop at nothing to protect their organization.
