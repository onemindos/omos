# Help Kids Concentrate

> *"Children who have breakfast and enough to eat during the rest of the day will be better able to concentrate in school."* — Head Start (Dept. of Health and Human Services)

---

## Quick-Reference Tips

**1. Take time for breakfast.**
Children who eat breakfast consistently concentrate better. This is non-negotiable — not optional.

**2. Introduce them to music.**
Playing instruments has been shown to increase focus and concentration in other areas. Even casual music exposure helps.

**3. Get enough sleep.**
Children and most teenagers concentrate best after about **9 hours of sleep**. Late nights destroy the next day's learning.

**4. Give them clear space.**
Children concentrate better on homework when they have a clean, uncluttered workspace. Turn off the TV completely — no "studying during commercials."

**5. Help them get organized.**
For older kids: use calendars or planners to record assignments, due dates, and completion dates. The National PTA recommends this explicitly.

**6. Discourage cramming.**
Cramming increases anxiety and interferes with clear thinking. Kids do better when they spread studying over several days and can relate new information to what they already know.
