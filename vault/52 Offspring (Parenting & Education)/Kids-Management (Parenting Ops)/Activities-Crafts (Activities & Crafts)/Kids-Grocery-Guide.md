# Kids Grocery Guide

---

## Whole Grains

- Whole wheat bread
- Whole wheat tortillas — wraps, quesadillas, burritos, PB+banana roll-ups
- Whole-grain pasta
- Whole grain rice

---

## Refrigerated

- **Eggs** — hard-boiled for salads, breakfast sandwiches, "breakfast for dinner"
- **Cheese** — sticks for snacks, sliced for lunchboxes, fresh mozzarella, cheddar
- **Yogurt** — Greek, squeeze tubes, or single-serving vanilla (pairs with granola)
- **Hummus**
- **Orange juice**
- **Milk**

---

## Frozen

- **Frozen fruits** — for smoothies
- **Whole wheat waffles or pancakes** — easy breakfast or lunch
- **Sweet potato fries** — frozen is fine
- **Veggie burgers**
- **Ice cream** — for when the kids have been extra good

---

## Vegetables

- **Carrots** — snack with hummus, or roasted with maple syrup
- **Red pepper** — dinner staple; kids eat it raw
- **Broccoli** — stir-fry, mac and cheese add-in, dipped in dressing
- **Cauliflower**
- **Tomatoes**
- **Cucumbers**
- **Avocados** — Taco Tuesday
- **Winter squash** — roasted or mashed with maple syrup
- **Sweet potatoes** — mashed or baked into fries
- **Fresh greens** — spinach, kale, arugula, lettuce

---

## Fruits

- **Apples** — snacks, salads, sides
- **Bananas** — cereal, smoothies, pancakes
- **Grapes** — grab-and-go, hiking, picnics
- **Berries** — pairs with yogurt or homemade whipped cream
- **Melon** — buy pre-cut for easy snack
- **Clementines** — easy for young kids to peel
- **Mandarins**

---

## Healthy Snacks

- Peanut butter & jelly
- Cereal — avoid rainbow-colored sugar varieties
- Granola (loose and bars)
- Pretzels
- Popcorn
- Wheat crackers
- Tortilla chips
- Salsa
- Nuts
- Raisins
- Apple sauce

---

## Delacruz Family Extras

- Honey
- Maple syrup
- Waffles & pancakes
- Oatmeal
- **Sweet plantains** ← family staple
- Yogurt
- Smoothies
- Cookies & donuts — occasional treats
