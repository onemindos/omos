# Your Child Is Already Your Teacher

> *"I have nothing to teach him on how to live life. He's doing it all by himself. He's my teacher, and a good one at that."*

---

## The Anxiety of the Conscious Parent

Many parents who have done deep inner work — overcome fears, explored their own identity, developed spiritually — arrive at parenthood with a new anxiety: *How do I give my child what I struggled so long to find?*

I've spent years trying to develop my inner self. Overcoming fears, inhibitions, inertia — it was difficult, and worth it. But when our son was born, I became more and more worried: *What's the right life coaching plan? How old should he be before I start teaching him? What if the techniques I've learned don't work for him?*

It was my wife who ended it. During one of my fretting sessions, she said: *"It's you who wants help — our son needs no help at all."*

She was right.

---

## What I Saw When I Actually Looked

When I stopped projecting my own journey onto him and started really observing my child, I realized: he has no issues to sort out. No inhibitions. No inertia. No self-doubt. He is a dynamo — constantly exploring the world, always himself, fully in touch with his inner being.

I can teach him to cross the road, hold a handrail, ride a bike.

I cannot teach him how to live. He's already doing it.

---

## Key Takeaways for Zeus

- The anxiety of "am I teaching my kids right?" often comes from *your own* unresolved questions — not theirs
- Watch **Cielo** especially — she is living fully right now, without a single filter. That's not a phase. That's truth.
- Your job isn't to hand them a life philosophy. It's to give them the space to discover their own.
- Sometimes the best parenting is getting out of the way and following their lead
- When you feel the urge to teach, ask: *Am I doing this for them, or for my own peace of mind?*
