# 3D Printed FRT (Forced Reset Trigger) — Hoffman Tactical

**Author:** Hoffman Tactical
**Source:** https://hoffmantactical.com/frt_intro/

---

## What Is a Forced Reset Trigger (FRT)?

A Forced Reset style trigger allows the operator to pull the trigger faster than a standard mil-spec fire control group. Completely legal.

When the bolt cycles to the rear, it cams the hammer down. The tail of the hammer pushes down on the back of the trigger — forcing the trigger AND the shooter's finger forward. A Safety lever then engages and prevents the trigger from firing until the bolt is almost fully closed (preventing hammer fall on an open chamber). Once the bolt locks, the trigger is released and can be pulled again.

Result: finger is forcefully reset after every shot, enabling faster follow-up pulls.

---

## The 3D Printed Design

Hoffman designed a 3-part 3D printed conversion kit that converts a standard mil-spec AR-15 trigger group to forced reset.

**Parts required:**
- 3 3D printed parts (Plate, Safety lever, Block)
- 1 pin — cut down 3/32" AR-9 roll pin (3/8" long)
- 1 spring — AR-15 bolt hold open spring

**Print materials used:**
- Super Lower: Push Plastic Carbon Fiber Nylon (V2.8 / V3.0 Super Lower)
- FRT parts: Keene Village Plastic Performance PLA

**Status:** Experimental at time of writing — a few days of design/testing. Functional but more work needed.

---

## Key Parts

### The Plate
- Replaces the factory disconnector in the trigger assembly
- Cannot pivot when installed — becomes part of the trigger
- Acts as a spacer for the hammer tail to push against
- Has a curved tail that wraps back over the selector switch drum
- That tail interacts with the Safety lever

### The Safety Lever
- Mounted inside the Block
- Locked position: trigger is held open, bolt carrier is free to move
- Trip point on bottom of bolt carrier contacts top of Safety lever as bolt closes
- When tripped: Safety moves forward, trigger unlocks, can be pulled
- After shot: bolt cycles, trigger forcefully resets, Safety re-engages

### The Block
- Stationary housing for the Safety lever
- Slides into the rear of the fire control group well on the Super Lower
- Engages with slots in the lower receiver
- Noted as the area with most room for improvement — better mount = easier assembly + wider upper compatibility

## Assembly Notes

1. Remove all FCG parts — hammer, trigger, disconnector, selector, pins, springs
2. Set disconnector and its spring aside (needed to revert to standard)
3. Reinstall trigger with spring — use Plate in place of factory disconnector
4. Reinstall hammer and selector as normal — ensure pins fully seated
5. Assemble Safety lever into Block with spring and roll pin
6. Sand/file Safety lever sides if needed for friction-free fit
7. Press pin through Block to complete Safety assembly

**Critical install trick:**
- Move hammer to fallen position before closing upper (Block will pop back — this is correct)
- Front pivot pin must be attached
- **Pull trigger and hold it down firmly** before closing upper
- Nudge Block forward to clear rear take down lug
- Holding trigger prevents Safety from snapping to locked position which would block bolt carrier

## Compatibility

- Block designed specifically for **V2.8 and V3.0 AR-15 Super Lowers** (3D printed)
- Future versions planned to support wider range of uppers
