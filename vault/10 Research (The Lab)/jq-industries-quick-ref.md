## Technology Overview

**Core Innovation:** Fabric-based tactile sensing

**Advantages:**
- Flexible, conformable to complex surfaces
- Large-area coverage (full robot body)
- Potentially lower cost than rigid sensor arrays
- Textile manufacturing scalability

**Sensing Capabilities:**
- Multi-point pressure detection
- Real-time data acquisition
- Integration with robot control systems
