# SaaS Tool Survey — May 26 2026

Quick reconnaissance pass on 5 tools dropped for review.

## quso.ai
**Category:** AI Video Content + Social Scheduling
**URL:** https://quso.ai
**Formerly:** vidyo.ai

All-in-one content creation and social scheduling platform. Core capabilities:
- Long-to-short video clipping (AI Clips Generator)
- AI Subtitle Generator
- AI Content Repurposing
- AI Influencer tool
- Social Media Scheduler (7 platforms: X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram)
- AI Content Planner + Analytics
- AI Video Editor with filler word removal

Target users: coaches, consultants, podcasters, agencies, content creators.
Relevant to: Zeus brand content ops, Legacy Platform Holdings content scaling.

## MindsHub (minds.db / mindsdb.com)
**Category:** AI Agent Infrastructure Platform
**URL:** https://mindshub.ai (mindsdb.com → 301 → mindshub.ai)
**Note:** minds.db domain does not resolve; mindsdb.com redirects to mindshub.ai

Open platform for running AI agents with managed infrastructure. Supports:
- OpenClaw, NanoClaw, Anton, Hermes (yes — lists Hermes as a supported agent)
- Model Router — routes each step to right LLM by cost/latency/capability
- Scratchpads (protected execution environments)
- Long-term agent memory (cross-run)
- Credentials Vault (encrypted, agents never see raw creds)
- Tool access: databases, warehouses, docs, SaaS apps, email, chat, calendars, CRM, APIs
- Multi-provider: Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi

**Intel:** Direct competitive reference for OneMind OS agent infrastructure. They're building managed hosting for the same open-source agents Zeus runs (Hermes, OpenClaw). Watch for partnership or differentiation opportunities.

## GeeLark
**Category:** Cloud Phone / Social Media Scaling Platform
**URL:** https://geelark.com

AI copilot for social media marketing at scale. Core capabilities:
- Cloud phones (ARM-based, native Android, not emulators)
- Each cloud phone has unique device fingerprint (IMEI, OS, MAC randomized)
- GPS + SIM simulation across 150+ countries
- Runs mobile apps natively: TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter/X, Pinterest, Vinted, Gmail, Telegram, Tinder, Venmo
- Phone farm management (unified dashboard for multi-account)
- Proxy browser + Android emulator + multi-account browser
- Automation: posts, engagements
- Use cases: social media marketing, affiliate marketing, ecommerce/dropshipping, airdrop farming, cloud gaming, ad verification

Relevant to: Legacy Platform Holdings social media scaling ops. Replaces physical phone farms.

## Dovly AI
**Category:** AI Credit Platform
**URL:** https://dovly.ai (also dovly.com)

All-in-one AI credit engine. Features:
- Fix, build, monitor, and protect credit score — 100% free
- AI-powered disputes with TransUnion
- Monthly TransUnion credit score + report access
- Personalized tips and guides
- Claimed avg improvement: +93 points
- Available on iOS and Android

Competitor set: Credit Karma, Kikoff credit builder.
Relevant to: Legacy Capital (financial wellness products), personal finance tooling (HP/30 Finance).
