# JQ Industries — Fiber Sensors & Robotic Skin

**Published:** 23 May 2025 | Source: KrASIA / 36Kr
**Company:** JQ Industries — Weihai, China | Founded: Early 2024
**Founder:** Zhang Jingqi (EE degree, University of York; ex-CTO at Xiaomi ecosystem company)
**Funding:** Series A — eight-figure RMB. Backers: JHF, New Oriental, Freyr Graphite

---

## What They Build

Flexible fiber sensors made from plant-derived materials and textiles. Fourth-generation resistive flexible fiber sensor architecture — not thin-film like most competitors.

**Sensor architecture:**
- Fiber nylon core
- Piezoresistive outer layer
- Electrode core
- All woven into a flexible yarn

**Specs:**
- Fold radius: < 0.1mm
- Minimum detectable force: 0.01 newtons
- Pressure sensitivity range: up to 3,500 kPa
- Passed automotive-grade impact tests
- High elasticity, no recalibration needed
- Breathable and skin-comfortable when woven into textiles

## Products

- **Robotic skins** — large-area electronic skin for humanoid robots
- **Fingertip sensors** — multi-point tactile feedback, motion capture, remote manipulation
- **G1 Skin Suite (Unitree Robotics)** — glove, torso, and sole components
- **Eldercare mattress** — monitors pressure distribution, posture, sleep quality, breathing (bedsore/fall detection)
- **Automotive cockpit sensors** — comfort tuning, gamified seat experiences (entering mass production)
- **Software platforms** — algorithmic processing, smart eldercare, in-car comfort monitoring

## Robotics Focus

- Primarily targeting **humanoid robotics** — supplying upper-body and limb skin systems
- Negotiating with 10+ humanoid robotics companies, some already shipping in volume
- Integrated with **NVIDIA Isaac Sim** for tactile data modeling and closed-loop deployment
- Foot-embedded sensors detect surface hardness and balance for stable walking
