## Fabric E-Skin Architecture — Technical Deep Dive

### Advantages of Fabric Approach
1. Conformability — covers complex 3D robot geometries
2. Durability — textile substrates resist wear vs rigid sensors
3. Scalability — textile manufacturing is mature + cost-effective
4. Large-area coverage — full body possible
5. Maintainability — washable/replaceable sections

### Technical Challenges
1. Signal routing — thousands of sensing points
2. Durability — maintaining conductive pathways through flexing
3. Calibration — consistent sensing across large areas
4. Waterproofing — protecting electronics while maintaining flex
5. EMI shielding — interference from robot motors

### Likely Sensing Technologies
- Capacitive arrays (pressure changes capacitance)
- Resistive sensing (fiber resistance changes)
- Piezoelectric elements (pressure → voltage)
- Time-domain multiplexing for signal acquisition
