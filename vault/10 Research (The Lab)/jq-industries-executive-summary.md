# JQ Industries — Executive Summary

**Research Completed:** May 24, 2026
**Related:** [[jq-industries-eskin-research]] | [[jq-industries-supplementary]]

---

## Overview

JQ Industries (捷酷科技 / Jieke Keji) is a Chinese precision sensing firm developing fabric-based electronic skin (e-skin) for robotics. Their technology is integrated into the **Unitree G1** humanoid robot platform.

---

## Key Findings

### Company
- Active B2B supplier to Chinese robotics manufacturers
- Domestic advantage within China's robotics supply chain
- Funding, founders, HQ all unconfirmed — research required

### Technology (Confirmed)
- Flexible fabric substrate with conductive fiber integration
- Large-area coverage, multi-point pressure sensing
- Real-time data acquisition integrated with robot control systems
- Spatial resolution, response time, materials — all unconfirmed

### Applications
- **Primary:** Unitree G1 humanoid — collision detection, safe HRI, force feedback
- **Potential:** Cobots, prosthetics, healthcare robots, wearables

## Investment Assessment

**Overall: MODERATE TO HIGH** (conditional)

**Positives:**
- Large addressable market
- Tier-1 Chinese robotics partnership
- Gov support for domestic tech
- Fabric approach = cost/scale potential

**Risks:**
- Unproven field durability at scale
- Unknown financials
- International expansion challenges
- Dependence on humanoid market growth

**Critical success factors:** Production reliability, customer expansion beyond Unitree, defensible IP.
