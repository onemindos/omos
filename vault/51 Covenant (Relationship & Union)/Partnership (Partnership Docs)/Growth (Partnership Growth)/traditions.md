# Traditions

> *What you build together becomes the culture of your household.*

These are not inherited traditions — those live in domain 54 Heritage. These are the ones **you and your partner create from scratch** — the rituals that will define your marriage and eventually your family.

---

## Anniversary Rituals

The annual marker of the covenant. Design it intentionally — it shouldn't just be dinner.

**Examples to build from:**
- **Return to the proposal spot** — every year on the anniversary, go back to where it started
- **Letter exchange** — each write a letter the night before, exchange in the morning, read aloud
- **Anniversary jar** — throughout the year, drop notes of meaningful moments into a jar; read them together on the anniversary
- **The 24-hour reset** — no phones, no work, full presence with each other for 24 hours on that day
- **New experience rule** — every anniversary must include something neither of you has done before

---

## Weekly Rhythms

The operational cadence that keeps connection alive between the big moments.

**Examples to build from:**
- **Friday evening debrief** — 30 min, no phones, review the week: what was hard, what was good, what's coming
- **Sunday morning ritual** — coffee + conversation before anything else starts (no news, no scrolling)
- **Date night lock** — one protected night per week, non-negotiable, no exceptions except emergencies
- **Cooking together** — one meal per week cooked as a team, not assigned to one person
- **Weekly check-in question** — one rotating question you ask each other every week (pull from a shared list)

---

## How You Celebrate

Most couples default to restaurants. Design how YOU celebrate — it becomes your fingerprint.

**Examples to build from:**
- **The Delacruz Birthday Rule** — the birthday person chooses the entire day's agenda, no input from anyone else
- **Wins get marked** — every major win (deal closed, goal hit, breakthrough) gets a specific celebration — define what "major" means before you need it
- **Seasonal rituals** — something you do every fall, every December, every spring that's yours
- **Morning of milestones** — on big days (promotion, new home, new child), start with prayer + intentional conversation before the chaos begins
- **The quiet celebration** — not every win needs a party; some get a bottle of good wine and a private dinner at home

---

## Family Culture Markers

The things that, in 20 years, your children will describe as "how our family does things."

**Examples to build from:**
- **No phones at the table** — ever, for anyone, including guests
- **Sunday is family day** — protected, not negotiable for outside commitments
- **We read** — books in the house, reading as a shared activity, library trips
- **We pray before meals** — or whatever your spiritual practice is — but it's consistent
- **We do hard things** — a shared ethos that discomfort is not a reason to stop

---

## How to Use This File

1. Start with **one thing per category** — don't design 40 traditions you'll never execute
2. Try it for 90 days before calling it a tradition — if it sticks, it's real
3. Add a `status` next to each: `active`, `trying`, `retired`
4. Review annually — some traditions will evolve, some will be retired, new ones will emerge
