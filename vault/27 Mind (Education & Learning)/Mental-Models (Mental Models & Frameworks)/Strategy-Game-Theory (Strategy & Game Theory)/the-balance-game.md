Our thoughts and feelings determine our behavior. Everything we do is a direct result
to what goes on inside us. Hence, the change in our life occurs. Everyone should try
to keep himself balanced by reforming himself and keeping it as flawless as possible.
An off-balance psychology is an easy prey for mishaps and causes chaos in the least
situations. On the other hand, a firm, sound psychology can easily overcome any
problem that hauls its owner and trespass any dilemma.
What really controls our behavior and feelings are inner elements like instincts, ideas,
knowledge, and sensations, all of which are reflected in the form of behavior and
expression.
It’s a fact you should know that ever since our universe has been created till it
perishes, no other person is similar to you. You might have common features with
others, yet your psychology and character are always unique. The character of each
person determines his path in this life and the concepts he abides by. We are often
overwhelmed and over-concerned with our outer appearance and materialistic issue
like buying a new house or changing our cars. However, in the middle of all this, we
overlook and ignore a very essential issue: our souls. As you need to clean up your
desk every now and then, so does your soul that often needs attention and meditation
to be reshaped, trimmed, and developed.
Everything we do in our life bears both possibilities: winning and losing. Every thing
can be anticipated except the human life. You live in this world without actually
realizing what your future will be: winning or losing. Have you ever thought about
sitting down and writing the good and bad aspects of your life? To convince you, I
will list three bad habits that we all do: wasting our time, making ourselves busy with
trivial issues, and fruitlessly debating with some people.
Late marriage has become a growing trend in our societies, and if you ask about the
reason, they often say that they want to enjoy their lives before indulging in the
responsibility of marriage. Don’t you think that such a trend is the outcome of
previous social pressures? Basically, getting married and having kids are the most
important reasons for us to rejoice. Why are they considered imprisonment
nowadays? Many reasons are unfolded behind such a phenomenon. Maybe it’s the
selfishness that is engrossed inside us, or it’s because of the social demands, or maybe
it’s the wrong upbringing. Why are people constantly over-loaded and burdened
nowadays? The technological advancement we are witnessing nowadays has
facilitated our lives to a high degree. Human beings, unlike animals, constantly try to
elevate their status. However, while over-focusing on technologically developing our
life, we forgot about our psychologies and inner selves. It all starts when we lose the
balance between our minds and feelings. The more balanced we are, the more
balanced our actions become, and the vice versa. For example, an imbalanced courage
becomes recklessness, and over-generosity becomes waste. Here comes the role of
mind in controlling and orienting our inner instincts, desires, and feelings. Ignorance
envisages wrong deeds as good ones. Lack of balance triggers people to misbehave
and mistreat their families, friends, and even themselves to the extent of committing
suicide. In such situations, people descend to the level of animals. Even when you
pass by a beggar without sympathizing with him, this is just selfishness and lack of
sensation, which is also a form of lack of balance. Originally, all sensations we feel
are for our welfare; for example, courage is for our protection. Yet, when such
feelings go off-balance, they thwart our lives. That’s why we should always employ
our feelings for our benefit. Even why you have the “goose-bumps” and your heart
pounds repeatedly before exams or meeting your manager, these are good signs for us
to be always ready in such situations. Knowledge leads us to realize that merely
following our desire and instincts is more of hindrance to us than asset. Every
achievement is accompanied with a victorious and balanced psychology, and the
opposite is true of course.

1. People between the sorrows of past and the fear of future:

a. The complex of the past:

Considering our past is advantageous once it’s done to learn from it. However,
referring to the past to remorse and regret is wrong. We shouldn’t spend all our life
according to our past failure or experiences; we should always look forward to the
future. Sorrows and pains of the past just chain the person. Why instigate a need to
suffer? What’s gone is done and nothing can be done about it. That’s why you should
learn from your past to build your future. When we remember the past to benefit from
it, we should ask ourselves: why did this thing happen? And how could I have
overcome that problem? When you think like that, you should always focus on how to
avoid such situations in the future, and not to indulge yourself in the past again. The
insistence on knowing the reason for a problem is always desirable once it is still at
the beginning. However, if the problem had already happened, then it’s wrong and
futile to keep asking about the reason because it will get you nowhere except further
depression. For example, when a married couple goes through a certain
misunderstanding, they should focus on how to solve it rather than why it happened
so that they reach a resolution and avoid constant headaches or accusations that only
complicate the situation rather than ease the tension. Always be ready to accept what
happened; to admit the presence of a problem is the first step to solving it. Peace of
mind can never be attained unless we take it for granted that what happened had just
happened and that we should accept it and try to adapt ourselves to it. We all make
mistakes and that’s normal because we are not impeccable; but what’s completely
rejected is to exaggerate our sorrows and never bounce back again. Whenever
possible, try to avoid falling into the same mistakes, and learn from your past as much
as possible.

b. The fear of future:

Another common mistake most of us commit is the deep and prolonged thinking
about our future. It’s always advisable that we plan; yet planning too much gives rise
to illusions and obsessions about the future. This makes us see our future in the dark
and negative sense, and we become worried and pessimistic about it just because of
what we’re imagining. Even if our expectations happened later, it’s wrong to ruin our
present with future concerns. Never fear the future! Whoever worries about blushing
in front of others will actually blush! Don’t be a prey to illusions and hallucinations or
else you will actually suffer from them. Even in psychology, it’s been proven that
being afraid of something actually makes it happen.
Some people may anticipate future disasters, so they suffer worrisome and concern
and ruin their present happiness. As I stated above, to be afraid of something makes it
happen even if it weren’t possible before, the one who is afraid of being bad in sex
will actually do bad in sex.
Don’t start your day with illusions about the future. What’s ironic here is that some
insurance companies abuse such fear of the future to make massive amounts of money
by intimidating people about possible future disasters.

c. Liberate yourself from the pains of the past and the fear of future:

Living your present life doesn’t mean not to look forward or plan for the future. It
neither means dropping your past as if it never happened. Theirs is difference between
planning for the future and be worried about it, and between learning from the past
and indulging in it. A wise man once said,” There’s one day between me and the
kings. Yesterday is gone with all its leisure, tomorrow is yet to arrive, and today is
just here”. The moral behind this is that a king is the person who has achieved all his
goals in the day he’s living. Peace of mind for just a single day is enough to give our
minds recreation and some space for self-exploration. This enables us to change our
lives for the best. Live your life as it is; life is too short to be wasted between our past
sorrows and future concerns. You should always have a positive approach to all
situations. As l said, it’s all a game of balance between learning from past
experiences, and planning for the future. To balance your life means to learn from the
past for the sake of the future. It also means planning for the future without
exaggeration and illusion. Finally, it means not to be over occupied with your daily
tasks so that you have enough space to dream and plan ahead. It’s all about balancing
our life. Don’t make your aspirations too high so that you aren’t hurt if they failed,
nor be pessimistic so that you give yourself a chance to try and make the effort.

2. Balancing your feelings:

Wise people are moderate concerning both their happiness and remorse. This doesn’t
mean that you become void of feelings, yet it does mean that you don’t delve too
much in either case. Some people are extremists with their feelings: they either love
till worship or despise till complete hatred. Balanced people love and hate moderately.
Exaggerating our feelings, whether those of rejoice or sorrow, without constraints on
them makes perplexed between what’s right and wrong. Rarely are those people who
are fair, and rarely are those who admit the truth and live according to it. We have to
separate our minds from our feelings because certain cases are just too evident. For
example, one plus one equals two, and no matter how much you wish them to be five,
they won’t, we always accept the things that suit us easily yet it is so hard for us to
accept things that we don’t like. Moreover, try to be fair and objective while judging
others. Don’t judge a person on something bad he did and forget all his good deeds all
of a sudden. Don’t base your relationship with people on single actions whether good
or bad. Don’t be extreme with your feelings so that if you misjudge someone, you
always have a way back to reconcile him.
3. The cognitive-knowledge balance:

The reasons why people sometimes refuse justice are:

-Blindly following your ancestors and getting too much attached to their customs and
habits.
-Haughtiness and over-confidence that blinds some people and make then resent
accepting the truth, especially from others who might be viewed as inferior to them.
-Following the majority even when mistaken.
-Following men of power.
-Being unaware about the truth itself.
-Not fully investigating some news and taking them directly for granted.
-Over-trusting some parties because you consider them sacred and credible like what
happens in case of some religions.

4. Balancing your thoughts and actions:

Man has a double power: cognitive and practical. The first one aims to know facts;
thus it originates from knowledge. The other one means putting your ideas into
actions; thus, it originates from will and determination. All information and advice
should be transformed into actions. Many people have sound ideas, yet their lack of
behavior prevents them from accomplishing or doing them. Here, they just remain
perplexed and baffled. Don’t think too much or else you will hesitate; once you’re
done thinking, then it’s time for action. Once you take your decision, don’t hesitate
and give space for illusions and concerns; just do it. Glory and success will remain
abstract as long as they are trapped within your mind and not executed. Don’t allow
yourself to fall prey for pessimism; act and work on your ideas once you shape them.

5. Balancing your love for yourself and selfishness:

Loving ourselves is not wrong or undesirable; on the contrary, it’s the reason why we
are constantly developing. However, some people become extreme about this and turn
into selfish, egoistic individuals consequently, this love mischief others instead of
improving our universe. A self-centered person views himself to be unique and
superior to others. What’s really dangerous about this is that anything done for such a
person is considered mandatory and not a favor or for the sake of love or care. This
person considers anything made for him as sacred duty. Moreover, such people
become blinded and don’t notice their own flaws anymore. Furthermore, such people
see that they don’t owe others anything, and only care about their own welfare even if
it were on the expense of others. Any advice given to such people is considered as
insult and demeaning. Admitting our mistakes is not something shameful; it makes
you earn others’ respect. Yet, this is not the case for such selfish people. It’s normal to
love ourselves and do whatever it takes to be always satisfied; however, this shouldn’t
become haughtiness so that we develop our universe and continue living on this
planet. Many mischief actions as the problems between married people and family
problem are just outcome of selfishness although most people don’t admit that.
Although our dignities are important, yet we should offer sacrifices whenever
necessary to keep things going. How to overcome haughtiness? Only by gaining
knowledge and considering others and the importance of living among people.
6. Balancing between personal satisfaction and people satisfaction:

Satisfying others’ needs can be troublesome as some people sacrifice their own
happiness and ease for the sake of others. On the other hand, we find people who only
care about their own happiness even if they had to hurt others. Moreover, this other
kind of people may ignore their duties if they become burden. Both kinds are
extremists: the first type melts their own characters for others’ sake, and the second
becomes self centered. A sound character is the one which is the middle. Don’t live a
way that satisfies others or is imposed on you unless you’re satisfied and convinced of
it. Even when you’re taking decisions, think of others yet don’t do that on your own
personal expense or comfort. In the same way, don’t imitate those who only act well
once they are treated well and vice versa. Don’t be mindless imitator for those who
worship appearances even if they had to commit wrong deeds.
Every person has his own separate personality and his own conventions which he
lives accordingly. Once you’re satisfied and self content, nothing else will matter
anymore. Don’t imitate others in everything, and don’t wait any piece of advice from
anyone all the time. Take your own decisions and don’t feel angry if you’re
condemned, nor happy once you’re praised. Remember: everyone who seeks to
satisfy all people is doomed. In the same wave, to respect others doesn’t mean
satisfying all their demands blindly. Respecting others means foremost respecting
ourselves. If you want to get your rights, make sure to fulfill all your duties first.

7. Balancing our reactions:

Many people exaggerate their reactions and take them to the extreme like those who
take revenge from someone who lied to them by killing him! if your reaction is bigger
than the action itself and more intense, then you will be a tyrant instead of being a
victim. Once you think about your nemeses and foes, take their circumstances into
consideration. If you were in their shoes, wouldn’t you act like them? So, instead of
despising those who once hurt you, thank God for not being like them. It’s become so
easy to forget our own mistakes and constantly highlight the mistakes of others
unfortunately. Tolerance is the key to building successful and strong relationships
with others. We all make mistakes, so if we don’t tolerate and forgive each other, then
we will all become isolated, social binds will become obsolete, and life will end
eventually. There are many things that people do and hurt others unintentionally;
that’s why we should think well before doing anything that we might regret later.
People have to control their reactions towards other people’s words or deeds. Those
sensitive people should overcome this and gain more confidence. You should care
about what others think about you, yet you shouldn’t take their words as bases for
your life. Always elevate the way you think away from trivial things; if someone
curses you for example or criticize you, just keep going. An eye for an eye makes the
whole world blind.
Knowledge enables you to control your reactions. Let’s take gossiping for instance.
How can we deal with it? Most people care so much about what others say about them
to the extent that if their words were negative, they would get nervous. Let’s look at
this issue from a different angel; I believe that we should be happy when others talk
about us because their worlds show our flaws, which we have to reform. Remember
that people are usually reoccupied with their lives, so if they gossip about you a little,
don’t exaggerate what they’re saying and make it all your concern. Remember that
once you die for example, none of those gossipers will even care. It’s true that you
can’t cease gossiping, yet you can ignore it. Don’t think too much about those who
envy you because this inly shows how successful you are. Also, delete the idea of
revenge from your brain and only think about improving your life. Don’t forget that
the more you get criticized, the more successful you are. A fruitful tree is always
bombarded with pebbles. Care about what others say and take it into consideration,
yet don’t make it an illusion on which you base your whole life. No matter what
others say about you, don’t do what satisfies them unless you’re convinced about it.
Don’t waste your time replying on those critics and cursers; shut them up with your
deeds not words. At the same time, if what’s said about you were true, take advantage
of it and change it for the better.
The reasons why people gossip are:
-Envying others which means having a certain blessing similar to what they have, yet
we wish that they lose it. It’s an ancient concept in our life to the extent that those
who envy others despise whatever they see. Everything those people lack becomes
their enemy.
-Jealousy and underestimating certain issue by saying that anyone can do what you
did or it’s not a big deal for example. In addition, such people go into the tiniest
details just to criticize you or highlight a flaw in your character. Of course, they don’t
admit their own mistakes.
-Having a weak personality; that’s why such gossipers butter up others and try to
steam them against those whom they already hate. Such people just follow the current
in their behavior: if others act wrongly, they act the same and vice versa.
-Some just gossip out of hatred.
-Jealousy, especially among women.

8. Balancing relationships:

Why are some people popular and trustworthy? How to build successful
relationships? To be a unique, attractive person is something that springs from your
own pure personality, which triggers you to act in a way that’s appealing for others.
Although the atmosphere a person is raised in shapes his personality to some extent,
you can still reshape yourself as well as your behavior. An example about that is a
true story about a lady who was married from a miser. She couldn’t take him
anymore, and she once took a vow on herself not to complain about her husband’s
miser anymore and to thanks and appreciate him for anything he makes for six
months. And guess what? The husband became more generous on his house and
started spending more and more. This wife gave her husband a chance to change, and
he did. We always hold others responsible and blame them for any mischief we face
in our life. The truth is that such bad luck is the result of the chaos and havoc in the
way we live. Many husbands complain that their wives are over-demanding and don’t
appreciate them no matter what. Such husbands could have avoided this situation if
they had already been honest with their wives about their financial status and what
bothers them. Such frankness from the beginning would have been the remedy for
such nagging and complaints. Moreover, married couple should take into
consideration that seeking advice from others doesn’t necessarily mean applying it. It
just means that it remains advice they do follow or not.
Some people are so calm and nice towards other people, yet so strict and mean
towards their families. Here you should know that your reality is shown in the way
you treat your family. Most problems are a result of accumulations for minor events.
It’s a shame that a break-up or divorce is caused by such trivial things. Everything
starts as nothing as increases to become gigantic with the passage of time. That’s why
try to tackle every obstacle no matter how small it is.

9. Don’t be fake:

As I stated earlier, our actions are determined by both our thoughts and feelings.
Some people try to conceal their feelings and act in a fake way. No matter how hard
you try to act in a fake way other than your true self, there comes a time people
discover this. Acting honestly and according to your true personality is critically
essential especially when it comes to choosing a spouse.
Some characteristics that you should notice in your fiance:
-Honesty: it’s the corner stone for a sound relation between married people. An honest
person is the one who always says the truth even in those situations when he can
easily lie. Don’t forget that no matter how much you lie, you’ll be caught eventually.
How can we know that a person is not honest? If he fulfills his promises, if he’s
punctual, and if he completely fulfills his duties and commitments, then he’s honest.
-Self-content: this means elevating yourself from trivial things, and being satisfied
with what you have. This person is the one who talks about what he has without
regretting the things he misses or lacks, he doesn’t borrow things from others
randomly, and he doesn’t complain about his status.
-Mercy: this means to feel with others, appreciate their feelings, and try to ease their
pains and soothe them.
Who can be considered merciful? Here are some examples: A person who steps on the
brakes while driving in order to allow others to cross, a person who walks in the parks
without stepping on grass, a person who walks without cutting the tree leaves, how he
treat children: nicely or ironically, how he behaves once he hears about an accident:
feeling sorrow or not, and whether he is loved by the elders of his family or not.
-Controlling your rage and anger: this is very difficult and requires a will of steel and
a strong personality which is capable of controlling itself. Many people fail to do that
especially if their rights were violated, so they act violently whether in words or
actions. Some might get so angry to the extent of committing a murder. How can we
distinguish such high-tempered people? Here are some questions whose answers
prove this type of people. How many times does this person raise his pitch while
talking? When he gets angry, how do his facial expressions become? How does he
behave when he encounters beggars? How does he treat his younger siblings? Does
everyone call his crazy?!
-The ability to forgive: it means sparing others and not punishing them when they
commit mistakes. We forgive because we can, not because we are weak. The
following cases show whether a person is forgiving or not: in case there’s a
misunderstanding, does he refer to previous events? Does he keep on reminding
others with how they behaved earlier towards him? Does he have more enemies than
friends? The answers for such questions show the truth about forgiveness, which is an
essential trait in our life especially towards those whom we care about.
-Stop mischief others: this means not to hurt others, verbally or physically, and
perform good deeds whenever possible. If you can’t do a good deed, at least don’t do
a wrong one. Who is the person who doesn’t mischief others? He’s the one who talks
the least about others and doesn’t gossip behind their backs, he always tries to
reconcile others, he’s the one who doesn’t abuse certain situations for his own benefit,
and his opinion usually agrees with most people.
All the above points are indispensable when it comes to choosing a spouse.
Unfortunately, most people chose their spouses on the bases of their financial status
only, and often overlook the above traits that are keystones for the success and
continuity of any relation, especially marriage.

10. General rules:

a. Don’t judge others subjectively and whether you love them or not; don’t judge
them according to how they treat you only because you are not the center of the
universe. Be fair and objective in your judgment.
b. Control yourself; it you did a good deed, reward it, and if you committed a mistake,
punish yourself. Try to have a personal supervision on your acts.
c. Don’t relay your personal experiences with everyone since you might slip your
tongue with something you might regret later on.
d. Ask your sons what they think about you; they will always say the truth.
e. Once you demand for a good deed, be the first to do it. Don’t just preach.
f. Any sound relationship starts from a balanced psychology.
g. Stop scrutinizing other people’s mistakes and focus on your own.
h. To know whether or not you’re a good person, you should always refer to your
conscience. How you feel while doing good or bad deeds determine whether you have
conscience and humanity or not.

11. A final word:

Life is what we make it with our own will, ideas, feelings, and inner psychology and
determination. As long as the combination of our feelings and ideas is a positive one,
then our actions are surely positive as well. The opposite is also true. Our instincts can
sometimes disorient and mislead us in life. That’s why we should always balance such
instincts and desires with our own mental abilities. Self-reform is not easily achieved,
yet it’s very essential to improve our life and truly change some of our twisted morals
and concepts. That’s why we have to take advantage of our past and avoid its
drawbacks in the future. Changing your life for the better is not temporal or easily
attained by doing some good deeds every now and then; it should be radical and
overwhelming. Nothing happens suddenly; everything in this life is gradual. Try to
train yourself on performing good deeds and it will happen eventually. Self-reform’s
never easy, yet with proper supervision and self-assessment, it would be attainable
hopefully.
