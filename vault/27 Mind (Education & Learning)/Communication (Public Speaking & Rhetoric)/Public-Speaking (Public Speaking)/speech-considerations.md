# Speech Considerations

## Recommendation Tiers

### ⭐ Highly Recommended
- Evidence-backed, widely accepted best practice
- Consistently produces positive outcomes
- Safe with minimal risk or downside
- Should be the default starting point

### 🔶 Recommended (Context-Dependent)
- Works well in specific situations or age groups
- May require professional guidance to apply correctly
- Benefits outweigh risks for most cases

### 🔵 Neutral / Exploratory
- Emerging or situational approaches
- Limited evidence but promising early signals
- Worth monitoring, not yet actionable

---

## Statement Verification Framework

### ✅ Verified Statement
- Backed by research, clinical study, or professional assessment
- Source is identifiable and credible
- Can be acted on with confidence
- *Example: "Children with late language emergence catch up by age 5 in most cases (ASHA, 2023)"*

### ⚠️ Unverified Statement
- Anecdotal, heard from a single source, or not yet cross-referenced
- Could be accurate — needs further validation before acting on
- Flag for follow-up with a specialist or second opinion
- *Example: "Someone told me screen time causes speech delays"*

### ❌ Contradicted Statement
- Directly conflicts with verified evidence
- Do not act on without professional review
- Document who said it and when for context

## Key Speech Consideration Areas

### Development Milestones (General)
- 12 months: 1–2 words (mama, dada)
- 18 months: 10–20 words minimum
- 24 months: 50+ words, starting 2-word phrases
- 36 months: 3-word sentences, 75%+ intelligible to strangers
- 48 months: Full sentences, storytelling, 90%+ intelligible

### Red Flags (Seek Evaluation)
- No babbling by 12 months
- No single words by 16 months
- No two-word phrases by 24 months
- Loss of previously acquired language at any age
- Difficulty understanding simple instructions

### Evaluation Options
- [ ] Pediatrician referral (first step)
- [ ] Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP) assessment
- [ ] Early Intervention program (free, under age 3 in most states)
- [ ] Audiologist (rule out hearing issues first)

### Environmental Factors
- Talk-time ratio: Child should hear 4:1 adult-to-child talk ratio
- Reduce background TV/noise during key interaction windows
- Parallel talk: narrate your own actions ("I'm washing the dishes")
- Expand: repeat what child says + add one word ("ball" → "big ball")
- Avoid correcting directly — model the correct form instead

### Therapy Approaches
| Approach | Verified? | Best For |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| DIR/Floortime | ✅ Verified | Social-communication, engagement |
| ABA (discrete trial) | ✅ Verified | Structured skill building |
| PROMPT | ✅ Verified | Motor speech disorders |
| Hanen (It Takes Two) | ✅ Verified | Parent-implemented, early language |
| AAC (Augmentative Comm.) | ✅ Verified | Non-verbal / limited verbal |
| Screen-based apps alone | ⚠️ Unverified | Not a replacement for human interaction |
