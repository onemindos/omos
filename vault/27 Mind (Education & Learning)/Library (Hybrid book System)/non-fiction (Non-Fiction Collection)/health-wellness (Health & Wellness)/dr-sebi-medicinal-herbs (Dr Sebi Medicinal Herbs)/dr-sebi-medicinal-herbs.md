## Description

A guide to Dr. Sebi's approach to medicinal herbs, drawing from ancient holistic healing traditions including Ayurveda, Traditional Chinese Medicine, and Shamanic healing. Covers the alkaline diet — what made Dr. Sebi's methodology stand out — combining both alkaline foods and medicinal herbs for disease prevention and recovery.
