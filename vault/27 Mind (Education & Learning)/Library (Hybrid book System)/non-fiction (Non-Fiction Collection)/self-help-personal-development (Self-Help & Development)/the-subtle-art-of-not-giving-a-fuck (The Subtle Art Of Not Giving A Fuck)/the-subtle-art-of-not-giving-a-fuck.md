## Description

A counterintuitive self-help book that argues for embracing limitations, choosing what to care about, and finding meaning through struggle rather than avoiding pain. Draws on stoic philosophy and modern psychology.

## Key Topics

- Choosing your values and what to care about
- The feedback loop from hell (caring about not caring)
- Responsibility vs. fault
- The importance of saying no
- Embracing uncertainty and being wrong
- Death as the ultimate motivator
