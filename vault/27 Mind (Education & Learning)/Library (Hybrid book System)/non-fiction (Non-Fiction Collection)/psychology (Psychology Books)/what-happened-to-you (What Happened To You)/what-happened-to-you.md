# What Happened to You?

## Conversations on Trauma, Resilience, and Healing

By Bruce D. Perry, M.D., Ph.D. and Oprah Winfrey

---

## Overview

A #1 New York Times bestseller that offers a groundbreaking and profound shift in how we think about and treat trauma. Through deeply personal conversations, Dr. Bruce Perry and Oprah Winfrey explore how trauma impacts the brain, shapes behavior, and what we can do to heal.

## Key Topics

- How trauma shapes the developing brain
- The biology of stress and resilience
- Shifting from "What's wrong with you?" to "What happened to you?"
- Personal stories of healing and recovery
- The power of connection in overcoming adversity

## Notes

- Co-authored by Oprah Winfrey and child psychiatrist Dr. Bruce Perry
- Based on their years of conversations about trauma and healing
- Integrates neuroscience with personal narratives
