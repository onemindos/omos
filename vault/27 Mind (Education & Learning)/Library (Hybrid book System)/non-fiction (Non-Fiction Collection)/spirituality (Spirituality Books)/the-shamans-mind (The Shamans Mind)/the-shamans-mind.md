## Description

Rooted in ancient Hawaiian teachings, this book teaches readers how to align and transform their mind into one that sees the world through the lens of the indigenous healers of old. Based on the Omega workshop by the same name. Author Jonathan Hammond is a teacher, energy healer, shamanic practitioner, and spiritual counselor — a graduate of Harvard and the University of Michigan, certified master teacher in Shamanic, Usui, and Karuna Reiki. Endorsed by Brett Bevell, Llyn Roberts, Rev. Diane Berke, and Serge Kahili King.
