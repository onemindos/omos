## Description

The ultimate guide to overcoming fear of intimacy and the havoc it can wreak on any relationship. Dr. Ralph Earle, a noted psychologist and family therapist (president of Psychological Counseling Services, past president of the American Association for Marriage and Family Therapy), reveals the many ways we hide from intimacy and how this common conflict can be resolved. Covers the eight factors that are prerequisites for true intimacy, recognizing intimacy "substitutes," and breaking the chains of the past. Coauthor of "Lonely All the Time."

## Key Topics

- Fear of intimacy and the "intimacy paradox"
- Eight prerequisites for true intimacy
- Recognizing unconscious intimacy avoidance
