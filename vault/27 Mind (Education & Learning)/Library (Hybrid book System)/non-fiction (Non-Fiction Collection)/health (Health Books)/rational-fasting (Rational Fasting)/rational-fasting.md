## Description

Professor Arnold Ehret's classic guide to fasting for physical, mental, and spiritual rejuvenation. Includes Ehret's "Your Road to Regeneration" and "The Definite Cure of Chronic Constipation," plus illuminating writings by Ehret disciples Fred S. Hirsch and Teresa Mitchell. New introduction by Robert A. Miller. This is the ORIGINAL, AUTHENTIC, copyrighted edition. "Get Fit! Look Younger! Keep Fit!" Ehret teaches how to successfully conduct and complete a fast, when to break the fast, and how to live in harmony with Nature's laws.
