## Description

A sweeping history of Paris from Roman times to the present day by acclaimed historian Colin Jones. Covers the city's transformation through Roman Lutetia, medieval splendor, the Revolution, Haussmann's redesign, and into modernity. Praised by *The Daily Telegraph* as "splendid... innovative... a fascinating and cogent account of the development of Paris from the Romans to the era of Mitterrand."

## Key Topics

- Paris from Roman Lutetia to modern era
- French Revolution and its urban impact
- Haussmann's transformation
- Cultural and social history
