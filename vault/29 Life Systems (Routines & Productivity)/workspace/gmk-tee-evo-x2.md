# GMK Tee Evo X2

## Definition
High-end mechanical keyboard by GMKtee — part of the Evo line. The X2 is a premium 65% or TKL form factor board targeting enthusiasts who want gasket-mount, premium materials (aluminum top/bottom), and sound signature tuning. Designed for desk setups where tactile feel and aesthetics matter.

| Spec | Detail |
|------|--------|

| Mount | Gasket mount — softer, bouncier feel |
| Material | Aluminum case |
| Sound Profile | Thocky / muted depending on foam config |
| Target User | Enthusiast / creator / daily driver |
