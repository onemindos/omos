# Revenue Analysis: Second Brain vs OneMind

> **"They built a $2M/year course business on organizing files. We're building a multi-vertical human enhancement ecosystem."**

---

## Second Brain (Tiago Forte / Forte Labs)

### Timeline
- **2017** — Launched "Building a Second Brain" cohort course
- **2022** — Published book (*Building a Second Brain*)
- **2026** — ~9 years in, mature business

### Revenue Breakdown

| Stream | Revenue |
|--------|---------|
| Course (per cohort) | $115,000 - $455,000 |
| Annual course revenue | ~$1M - $1.5M |
| Book sales | 320,000+ copies, ~10,000/month |
| Book advance | $478,000 |
| Newsletter sponsors | ~$16,000/year |
| Summits & events | ~$21,000/event |
| **Total Annual Revenue** | **~$1M - $2M** |

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Newsletter subscribers | 125,000+ |
| Revenue concentration | 90% from ONE course |
| Product types | Digital only (course + book) |
| Markets served | Productivity / knowledge management |
| Total addressable market | ~$325B (e-learning) |
| **Revenue ceiling** | **~$2-3M/year** |

### Limitations
- Single product (BASB course) drives 90% of revenue
- Digital only — no physical products, hardware, or locations
- One category (productivity/organization)
- Time-limited (cohort model requires Tiago's involvement)
- Transitioning to self-paced to reduce burnout

**OneMind's white space: The only brand unifying AI + wellness + agriculture + robotics + personal sovereignty under one human enhancement framework.**

## Why OneMind Surpasses Second Brain

1. **Multiple revenue streams** — Tiago has ONE course. OneMind has 7+ divisions that cross-sell.
2. **Physical + digital** — Second Brain is PDF and video. OneMind includes gyms, farms, robots, and hardware.
3. **Recurring revenue** — Gym memberships, community subscriptions, licensing fees create predictable cash flow.
4. **Franchise potential** — "OneMind Certified Coach" and "OneMind Wellness" locations scale without Zeus's time.
5. **Hardware moat** — Growing systems and robotics products create defensible IP that courses can't.
6. **Generational vision** — Second Brain is about YOU organizing YOUR files. OneMind is about enhancing human potential across generations.

> **Second Brain is a book. OneMind is a company.**
