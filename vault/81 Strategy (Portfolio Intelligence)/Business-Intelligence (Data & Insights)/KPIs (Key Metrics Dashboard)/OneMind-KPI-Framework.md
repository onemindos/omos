# OneMind KPI Framework

> **Purpose:** Reference document for all business metrics tracked across OneMind OS. Use this to design dashboards, set up reporting, and run reviews. Not a task list — each section has a corresponding action task in Todoist (81 Strategy).

---

## 1. Revenue & Profitability

**Primary tool:** Zoho Books + Zoho Analytics

| Metric | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) | Track $97 + $79 plan split |
| Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) | Derived from MRR × 12 |
| Gross vs. Net Profit Margins | Revenue minus COGS and operating costs |
| Net Profit Margins | After all expenses |
| Operating Cash Flow | Monthly inflow vs. outflow |
| Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | Per-service cost (DisputeFox, postage, printing) |
| Revenue per Client vs. Cost per Client | LTV efficiency ratio |
| Billed vs. Collected Amount Trends | Collections lag |
| Credit Repair Profitability Metrics | Cost per dispute case vs. case revenue |
| Cost per Dispute Case | Postage, printing, labor per case |
| Subscription Plan Revenue Breakdown | $97 plan vs. $79 plan performance |
| Customer Upgrade/Downgrade Trends | Plan migration patterns |
| Average Subscription Length per Customer | Retention indicator |

## Review Cadence

| Frequency | What to Review |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Weekly** | Revenue (MRR), active tickets, system uptime, automation failures |
| **Monthly** | Full revenue & costs, CAC, NPS, employee metrics, feature adoption |
| **Quarterly** | Compliance audits, full HR review, KPI targets vs. actuals, strategic adjustments |

## Dashboard Setup Priority

1. **Revenue & Profitability** — highest priority, Zoho Books + Analytics
2. **Sales & Lead Funnel** — Zoho CRM dashboards
3. **Customer Experience** — Zoho Desk + NPS
4. **HR & Workforce** — Zoho People
5. **Operations Health** — Automation monitoring
6. **Security & Compliance** — Zoho + DisputeFox
