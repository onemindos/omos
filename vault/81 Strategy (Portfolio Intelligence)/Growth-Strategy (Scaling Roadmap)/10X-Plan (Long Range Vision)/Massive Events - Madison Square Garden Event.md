Estimating the total cost of such a large event at a high-profile venue like Madison Square Garden (MSG) is complex and depends on several variables. Here are some of the major costs you'll need to consider:

1. **Venue rental:** This could range significantly based on the specifics of your event. As of my knowledge cutoff in 2021, renting out Madison Square Garden can cost between $200,000 - $300,000 per night depending on the specific room or arena you're booking and the date of the event. However, these prices can vary, and it's recommended to reach out to MSG directly for an accurate quote.
2. **Event production and equipment rental:** You'll need to factor in the costs of staging, lighting, sound, and video production, which could range anywhere from $10,000 to several hundreds of thousands, depending on the complexity of your setup and the quality of equipment you require.
3. **Speaker fees:** These can also vary greatly. High-profile speakers can command anywhere from $10,000 to $100,000 or more per event. If you're bringing in several top-tier speakers, this could easily become one of your largest expenses.
4. **Catering:** If you plan to offer food and drinks at your conference, this is another significant cost. Prices will vary based on the quality and quantity of food and beverage provided, but could easily range from $30 to $150 per person, or more.
5. **Marketing and promotion:** Depending on how you plan to promote your event, you might need a significant marketing budget. This might include advertising costs, graphic design for promotional materials, social media campaign costs, and more.
6. **Staffing:** You'll need staff to handle setup and teardown, manage the event, provide security, and potentially more. This could also run into the tens of thousands of dollars.
7. **Insurance:** For an event of this scale, insurance could easily run into the thousands or tens of thousands of dollars.
8. **Miscellaneous costs:** Don't forget about smaller costs that can add up, like printing event programs, providing amenities for your speakers, decorating the venue, and more.

Given these factors, you're likely looking at a minimum cost in the low millions to host a conference at Madison Square Garden. Please note that these figures are estimates and can vary widely based on your specific needs. Always seek out quotes from vendors and service providers for the most accurate budgeting.

Organizing an event at a high-profile venue like Madison Square Garden without having to personally finance the venue costs could be challenging, but it's not impossible. Here are a few strategies you could employ:

1. **Sponsorships:** This is the most common method used to offset costs. Look for companies that might benefit from exposure to your audience. They might be willing to sponsor the event in exchange for their branding being prominently displayed and included in marketing materials. It's essential to create a compelling sponsorship package that outlines the benefits a sponsor will receive.
2. **Ticket sales:** You could try to cover the costs through the sale of tickets. To do this, you'll need to accurately estimate the number of attendees and set a ticket price that would cover your costs.
3. **Partnerships:** Partnering with a larger organization or company that aligns with your cause could help offset the costs. They could co-host the event, which would split the cost and also potentially increase the event's reach.
4. **Crowdfunding:** Depending on the nature of your event and your community, you might be able to crowdfund some or all of the costs. Websites like Kickstarter, GoFundMe, or Indiegogo can be useful platforms for this.
5. **In-kind contributions:** Some businesses may not be able to offer monetary support but can provide in-kind contributions, such as event equipment, technology, or other services necessary for the event. This can help reduce your overall costs.
6. **Grants:** Some organizations offer grants for educational and community-focused events. Research grant opportunities that align with the focus of your conference.

Remember, while these methods can help to offset costs, they also require a significant amount of planning and effort to secure. Also, there's always the risk that you may not be able to raise the full amount needed, so it's important to have contingency plans.

Great! It sounds like you're considering some of these strategies to offset your costs. Here are a few additional tips:

1. **Start early:** Sponsorships, partnerships, crowdfunding, and grants can take a lot of time to secure, so start as early as possible.
2. **Network:** Utilize your connections to find potential sponsors or partners. If you don't ask, you won't know who might be willing to help.
3. **Create a compelling pitch:** Whether you're approaching sponsors, potential partners, or crowdfunding supporters, you'll need a compelling pitch that communicates the value of your event.
4. **Offer tiered sponsorship:** This will allow smaller companies to contribute at a lower level, while still offering substantial benefits to higher-level sponsors.
5. **Focus on value for sponsors/partners:** Make sure any companies or organizations you approach can see clear value in sponsoring your event. This might be marketing exposure, the opportunity to sell their product or service, or alignment with their corporate social responsibility goals.
6. **Promote your event heavily:** The more successful your event looks, the more likely sponsors and partners are to get on board. Utilize all available channels to promote your event, including social media, email marketing, press releases, and more.

Planning and hosting an event can be a daunting task, but with the right strategies and a lot of hard work, it's definitely possible to create a successful event without having to personally finance all the costs. Best of luck with your event planning!
