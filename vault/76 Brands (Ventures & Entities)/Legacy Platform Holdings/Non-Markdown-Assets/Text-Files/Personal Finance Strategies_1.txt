Here are some further notes from the Tony Robbins Video:

1) You don’t lose money unless you sell in bear market

—> A correction (decrease in value of financial market) happens every year (10-20% drop from peak)

—> Bear markets (decrease in value of financial market) happen every 5 years (20% or more drop from the peak)

You need realize that economic and financial markets are always up trending longterm. Once you realize that and adopt the longterm mindset you will not lose if you consistently invest and diversify your investments. In the hundreds of years the financial markets have been open, no one that consistently invested over a 20 year period lost money. Why are we so open to buying clothes that are on sale, but not stocks? There is too much fear associated with investing. This cannot be the case with you if you want to reach financial independence.

 

2) The power of compound interest over time

An 18 year old boy:

Saves $300/month for 8 years ($4,000 a year) and consistently invests that money over those 8 years into the S&P 500 (an index that combines the top 500 companies in the United States).

—> At 65 that boy (now a man) has 1.8 million dollars (10% return per year)

Also, a blue collared worker that never made more than $14,000 per year retired with $70,000,000 because he consistently invested over the span of his life.

Keep reinvesting your money! It grows geometrically!

Warren Buffett calls compound interest the 8th wonder of the world

 

3) Most financial advisors do not have your best interest in mind.

(I have seen this first hand working in the wealth management industry at multiple firms).

In my opinion, for the average person, it is not necessary to pay for an investment advisor until you have over $2,000,000 in assets and/or have a complex financial situation.

If you feel you need an advisor, make sure to find a fiduciary (RIA). They are obligated to put the clients best interest before their own.

—> Only 10% of 310,000 advisors are fiduciaries but 26,000 are still brokers (which means they make money from selling financial products). 90% of brokers work for the house (make commission), which doesn't mean they are bad people. It means that they have incentives to sell you something that may not be the best for you.

 

4) Why your 401k is not a good idea (fees)

401k is an actively managed mutual fund retirement account (average has 17 hidden fees).

These mutual funds pay to be the vehicle for the investments for these large companies, which means there are high fees associated with them.

How bad do fees hurt your wealth?

If you have $100,000 at 35 years old and invest for 30 years, while getting an 8% return but pay 3% in fees, you will have a return of $442,000. This person would run out of money at 74-75 years old if they retire at age 65.

Let's look at the same situation as above but they only pay 1% in fees, this person would have $762,000 and would run out of money at the age of 93 years old.

***Every 1% in fees will cost you 10 years of income!! Do not get waxed by fees from advisors and retirement plans that do not have you're best interest in mind.

 

5) It is not about timing the market, it is about the time in the market

On average we break a record once a month. Do not get worried about the news are emotional investors over hyping market cycles. We think long-term, not short-term.

Here are some studies that back up why you need to stay in the market:

Jp Morgan

If an investor is out of the market for 10 best days over last 20 years (average 8.2% return per year) that investor makes almost half of the money that someone that just stayed in the market (return of 4.5%)

If an investor misses the top 20 days in the market over the last 20 years, he/she will make a 2% return (as opposed to a 8.2% return).

If an investor misses the top 30 days, he/she actually loses money!

Very, very few people can actually time the market. Get in the market and stay there.
