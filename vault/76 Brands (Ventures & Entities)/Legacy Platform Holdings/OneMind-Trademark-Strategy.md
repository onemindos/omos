# OneMind Trademark Registration Strategy

> **Goal:** Dominate trademark positioning across all current and planned OneMind business verticals — framework, AI, wellness, agriculture, robotics, education, and speaking.

---

```
ONE MIND (Parent Brand)
├── OneMind Codex         ← framework/method

├── OneMind Wellness      ← gym, health, biometrics
├── OneMind Earth         ← agriculture, homesteading, food systems
├── OneMind Robotics      ← hardware, automation, machines
└── OneMind Institute     ← speaking, education, certification
```

## Competitive Trademark Landscape

### Existing "OneMind" Entities (Different Classes)

| Entity | Their Class | Their Market | Conflict Risk |
|--------|------------|-------------|---------------|
| OneMind Technologies (Affluence Corp) | 9, 42 — IoT SaaS platform | Smart cities, enterprise IoT | **Medium** — Class 9/42 overlap possible, but different goods/services |
| OneMind Services | 42 — Cloud consulting | Enterprise cloud, cybersecurity | **Low** — consulting vs. framework |
| OneMind Technologies (Brussels) | 42 — IT consulting | Hardware relocation | **Low** — different geography and service |
| One Mind (nonprofit) | 44 — Mental health | Mental health advocacy | **Low** — nonprofit vs. commercial, different sector |

### Why We Can Win

1. **"OneMind Codex" and "OneMind Framework"** are compound marks — much more defensible than bare "OneMind"
2. **Different classes** — our Phase 1 classes (16, 41) have NO overlap with existing entities
3. **Delta precedent** — Delta Airlines, Delta Faucets, and Delta Dental all coexist under the same name in different classes
4. **First to file in our classes wins** — register before anyone else claims these classes
