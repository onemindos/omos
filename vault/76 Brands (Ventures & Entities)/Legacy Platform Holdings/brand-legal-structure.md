# OneMind — Brand, Legal & Business Structure

---

## One Mind OS (The Platform)

> *"The operating system for sovereign living"*

**Features:**
- Home integration (lights, climate, security)
- Workflow automation (tasks, routines, habits)
- Knowledge management (your personal Codex)
- Robotics control (garden bots, maintenance bots)
- Offline mode (local AI when off-grid)
- Cloud sync (full power when online)

**The AI: Legacy AI (or just "Legacy")**

> "Hey Legacy, what's the status of the garden?"
> "Legacy, run my morning routine."
> "Legacy, prepare for off-grid mode."

**Platform tagline:**
> *"One Mind OS is the operating system for regenerative living. Legacy AI is the intelligence that runs it — your AI companion that manages your home, automates your workflows, and helps you build a multi-generational legacy."*

**Product slogan:**
> *"Legacy AI — Your companion for sovereign living. Powered by the One Mind Codex."*
