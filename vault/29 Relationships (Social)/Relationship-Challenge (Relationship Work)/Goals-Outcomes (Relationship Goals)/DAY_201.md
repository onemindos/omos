***DAY 1: RELATIONSHIPS ARE ASSIGNMENTS***

Welcome to Day 1! I am so thrilled that you\'re here! I\'m psyched to
take you through this challenge. For the next 14 days, I'll guide you to
release any blocks that are holding you back in relationships and help
you open up to more love and connection.

And we're going to start big! Get ready---I have a HUGE lesson in store
for you today.

Here\'s the story: I believe that the world is our classroom and that
people are our assignments. Take that in.

**The world is our classroom and people are our assignments.**

If you choose to show up for these spiritual assignments with a
willingness to heal yourself and a willingness to heal how you relate to
others, then you can expect a lot of miracles.

By signing up for this challenge, you\'ve already sent out a message to
the Universe that says, \"I\'m willing! I\'m willing to show up for
these assignments.\" When you have this willingness, you can release the
stories you\'re stuck in and the patterns that don\'t serve you in your
relationships. And today\'s practice is designed to elevate all your
relationships, whether they\'re with a romantic partner or not.

Keep that in mind throughout the challenge. You can apply this challenge
to one specific relationship, or to all your relationships. Often the
issues that show up in our romantic relationships also show up in our
work relationships, in our friendships, and in our family relationships.
The people in our life who challenge us most, trigger those core wounds
from our childhood.

Today, we\'re going to begin by taking a good look at the opportunities
and lessons your relationships offer you. Let\'s shift your perception
right now so you can see your relationships as assignments. This is big
work, but I've broken it down into simple steps.

**LET'S GET STARTED!**

Download your Relationship Challenge Journal on the Welcome page. (Be
sure to save the journal to your device so you can return to your saved
copy whenever you want to review it.) Then press play for today's
lesson. If you prefer to read the lesson, you can follow along below.

00:00/00:00

1.  **Recognize the assignment and call it by its name.**\
    Choose one relationship in your life that you'd like to work on.
    Reflect on the ways this relationship is causing discomfort in your
    life. Instead of blaming or shaming, for today I just want you to
    view this relationship as an assignment.\
    \
    This might feel really uncomfortable, but that's okay. The
    relationships that cause the most discomfort are the ones that we
    can learn from the most.\
    \
    So witness the relationship that's causing you discomfort, and call
    it out as the perfect opportunity for you to heal and grow. When we
    do that, we actually start to see the relationship not as an
    annoyance, but as a chance to grow.\
    \
    Honor yourself for having the strength to just simply witness this
    assignment instead of running away from it. Be proud of yourself for
    seeing this difficult relationship as a spiritual assignment.

2.  **Accept that you cannot avoid this assignment.**\
    The assignment can't be avoided. You can only postpone showing up
    for it.\
    \
    You have two choices whenever you encounter a difficult
    relationship. The first choice is that you run away in fear. (I love
    the acronym FEAR: F--- everything and run!) That might feel like the
    safest option, but trust me, it\'s not. Running from fear is like
    running around a track. You will inevitably wind up back in the same
    place, and it\'s just going to keep happening again and again until
    you truly show up for the assignment and get off the track.\
    \
    Instead, I recommend the second option, which is to show up for the
    assignment with grace. Become willing to show up for this assignment
    once and for all, and trust that the Universe has your back
    throughout this process. If you\'re willing to follow step 1 and see
    your relationship as an assignment, then you\'re ready to show up
    for it.

3.  **Ask the Universe for help.**\
    You\'ve already recognized the assignment and become willing to show
    up for it. Now you can just sit back, relax and allow the Universe
    to support you through the next 14 days.\
    \
    Allow this prayer to take over your conscious awareness:

    -   ***Thank you, Universe, for presenting me with this
        relationship. I choose to see it as a divine assignment for
        spiritual growth and healing. I\'m ready and I\'m willing to see
        my relationships as assignments and show up with love and grace.
        I welcome support. Show me where to go, show me what to do, show
        me what to say. I trust I\'m being guided.***

> I couldn\'t begin this challenge without this practice, because I want
> you to shift your perception around your relationships from the
> get-go. I want you to see your relationships as spiritual assignments
> that you can continue to show up for each day of this challenge. When
> you do, expect a lot of miracles.
