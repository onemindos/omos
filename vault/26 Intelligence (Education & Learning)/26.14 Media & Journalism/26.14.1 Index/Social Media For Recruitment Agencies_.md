# 1. WHY SHOULD RECRUITMENT AGENCIES USE SOCIAL MEDIA?

Social media is quickly becoming an indispensable tool in the
recruitment world. Not only does social media offer agencies new
opportunities to build relationships with candidates and clients but it
also builds your brand's reputation.

Below are the 4 key reasons why recruitment agencies should be using
social media:

## 1. ATTRACT NEW CANDIDATES

Huge numbers of potential candidates will be using social media and the
amount of people joining social media is growing rapidly, making it the
perfect platform to advertise current vacancies. Potential candidates
are able to see and respond to your posts meaning your message is spread
further.

## 2. BUILD YOUR REPUTATION

You can build your reputation and highlight how credible your
recruitment agency is by using social media. For example, by being quick
to respond to candidates and clients, sharing content from trusted news
sources and interacting with followers you can build your reputation as
a knowledge source in the industry using social media.

## 3. DIFFERENTIATE YOUR BRAND

Social media allows you to create a voice for your agency, which can be
developed to reflect your brand's positioning and convey how unique your
agency is in comparison to the rest of the industry. Whether it's
through quirky customer interactions or highlighting your company's
USPs, social media can be a very effective tool for differentiating your
brand.

Many companies spend large amounts of money trying to build their PR
strategy. Yet social media is a free channel that allows you to lay the
foundations of your PR campaigns. You can give opinions on current
industry topics and share company news to a large audience, whilst
encouraging interaction with this content.

# 2. BEFORE YOU BEGIN

So you have decided to venture into the world of social media, great!
However, before you begin here are some key considerations for you to
think about before diving head first into creating your social media
profiles.

## DECIDE WHICH SOCIAL MEDIA ACCOUNTS WILL WORK BEST FOR YOU

Unless you are a large organisation with the time and resources to plow
full speed ahead into every social media site, the trick is to
prioritise the platforms that will work for your agency and post
frequently. When you have selected the social media accounts for your
agency you will need to ensure you have an up-to-date profile, otherwise
it could look like an abandoned account which will counteract all of
your social media efforts.

In this guide we will be looking at the three biggest social networking
sites for recruitment agencies: Twitter, LinkedIn and Facebook.

## DEFINE YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA GOALS

Before you jump straight in, you will need to define what you want to
get out of social media. Is it brand awareness? Increasing candidate
retention? Reaching out to new candidates? Or becoming a thought leader
in your industry?

Add a focus to your social media marketing and stick to an overall goal
e.g. I want to increase traffic to our website by 10% over the next 3
months.

## 6 SOCIAL MEDIA TOP TIPS

1\. Humanise your brand and be emotive -- People use social media to
connect with real people; show your candidates and clients the real you
behind your company logo.

2\. Don't over promote your agency - Build relationships and provide
value to your followers through interesting and informative content.

3\. Adopt the 80/20 rule - 80% of the time you should post valuable,
helpful or personal content and reserve the last 20% for
promotional/sales activities.

4\. Provide great customer service - Use your social media platforms to
interact with candidate and client queries, comments and complaints.

5\. Be consistent - Use a social media content calendar to provide
consistent up to date content.

6\. Slow and steady wins the race - Remember just like in real life,
friendships and bonds over social media won't happen overnight, it will
take time to grow your followers and get them to interact with your
agency.

# 3. WHICH SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORM TO USE: TWITTER

If you only have time for a couple of social media networks you should
have a serious look at Twitter. Unlike other networks that require you
to connect with an audience first, Twitter is public facing allowing
your messages to be seen on a larger scale. Twitter is sometimes called
a 'micro blogging' platform, as it consists of short updates. You can
add photos, videos and website links to tweets to maximise your word
count.

## AUDIENCE:

Twitter has a large audience base; in fact there are over 645,750,000
active users on Twitter with 135,000 signing up each day (Twitter,
2014), which means that your target market are more than likely already
there.

## BEST TIMES TO POST:

There is a lot of conflicting advice when it comes to when the best time
to tweet is, however we have found that late morning /early afternoon is
best. If your posts are targeting candidates', tweets that are posted on
Friday, Saturday and Sunday have better engagement than those posted
during the week. Whereas if your communications are targeting clients
there is higher engagement on weekdays during work hours.

## TONE OF VOICE:

Topical and immediate - Twitter is a place to mix professional and
personal by collaborating personal observations and ideas as well as
industry news and updates.

## TWITTER TOP TIPS:

1\. Twitter name - Your Twitter name is extremely important- it will be
part of your Twitter URL which is what you will be publicising to the
world. Try to keep it short, simple and memorable e.g.
www.twitter/companyname

2\. Language - Avoid text speak on your tweets, you only have 140
characters but make sure your spelling, punctuation and grammar are spot
on.

3\. Aim for quality not quantity - Too many tweets in one day has seen
to decrease engagement.

4\. Interactions - Interact with any new followers and follow them back
to start building relationships.

# 4. WHICH SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORM TO USE: LINKEDIN

LinkedIn has always been said to be the most effective social media
platform for recruitment agencies sourcing candidates and clients.
Candidates that are looking for new job opportunities can post their
CVs, experience and skills on their profiles. This allows you to
efficiently use the search criteria to find people with the talent,
skills and work history needed for a client and allows you to connect
with them directly.

## AUDIENCE:

LinkedIn is used as a professional networking site and has over 15
million members in the UK alone with 2 new members per second (LinkedIn,
2014).

## BEST TIMES TO POST:

LinkedIn, unlike other social networking sites, is busiest Monday to
Friday in the mornings and midday, due to the audience of LinkedIn
business hours work best to interact with professionals.

## TONE OF VOICE:

Friendly and Professional LinkedIn allows users to post status updates
and to join groups, this is a great way of attracting attention
especially if you have a job you wish to advertise, it also allows you
to post updates to your connections which will be displayed on their
news feed.

## LINKEDIN TOP TIPS:

1\. Relevant key words - Ensure you complete your personal and company
profile with relevant keywords and links on both. Using keywords will
allow others to search for your profiles, not only on LinkedIn but also
on other search engines.

2\. Create different accounts - There are 2 different accounts on
LinkedIn: Personal accounts for individuals and a company page dedicated
to your agency. LinkedIn best practice is to ensure all staff have
personal profiles that portray your agency in a good light, which then
link to the company page of your agency.

3\. LinkedIn personal account - Personal accounts are used to connect
with people on LinkedIn, direct message connections and post updates.

4\. LinkedIn company pages - Company pages allow you to generate
followers and strengthen customer relationships by sharing valuable and
rich content via company updates.

5\. Company email address - Bear in mind that you will need a company
email address (name\@companyname.co.uk), in order to create a LinkedIn
company page. You cannot use an address with a domain such as Outlook.

# 5. WHICH SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORM TO USE: FACEBOOK

Many businesses use Facebook pages to support their social media
activities. By having a company page on Facebook you can put your
updates in front of a ready made audience. With 84% of job seekers
accessing Facebook daily, using Facebook to recruit is becoming an
increasingly effective way of interacting with candidates.

## AUDIENCE:

Over time Facebook has changed from an online place where students hung
out to a resource for friends and families of all ages to share
pictures, stay in contact and interact with each other. Facebook is
brimming with opportunities for you as recruiters, as there is a large
network of potential candidates already active on the site.

## BEST TIMES TO POST:

The best times to post on Facebook are early morning, lunchtime and
evenings on Monday through to Friday. In most companies workers are
unable to access Facebook during working hours. However using Facebook
analytics will show you the best time to post specifically for your
page.

## TONE OF VOICE:

Lively and friendly Professional and personal social circles may
overlap, so think carefully about what you post especially the language
you use and the pictures you upload.

## FACEBOOK TOP TIPS:

**Facebook business page** - Make sure you are using a business page as
opposed to personal pages there is a possibility that Facebook could
find your page and shut you down if you are using the wrong one. This is
the last thing you want to happen after all of your hard work.

**Facebook name** - Keep your Facebook page name short and get it right
first time! -- You can only change your Facebook page if it has less
than 200 likes.

**Vanity URL** - Create a vanity URL for your Facebook page, which is
available after you have 25 likes. Try and keep your URL simple so
people can remember it. e.g. [www.facebook.com/yourcompanyname]{.ul}

**Create loyalty** - Don't get hung up on how many likes you have, focus
on creating loyalty and interaction amongst your followers.

# 6. BUILDING AN AUDIENCE

So you have chosen and set up your social media accounts but how do you
tell everyone you're there? Follow these 7 simple points to build your
audience and grow your presence on social media.

**1. Identify your target audience** - Separate them into groups for
each social media account.

**2. Promote your social media profiles** - Once you have created social
media profiles add them to your website and email signatures and ask
your staff members to do the same.

**3. Use online and offline advertising** - Promote your social media
profiles by combining online and offline advertising i.e. include your
accounts on any printed collateral.

**4. Join groups** - Use LinkedIn and Facebook to find groups that your
ideal audience already belongs to.

**5. Find members of groups** - Find members of these groups and follow
or add them to find out what they are talking about.

**6. Participate in groups** - Actively participating in groups by
posting comments and answering questions, will make people want to learn
more about what you do.

**7. Build relationships** - Build relationships with other thought
leaders in your industry, you could forge a partnership with them to
share ideas and content.

# 7. MANAGING YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA ACTIVITIES

By now you will be wondering how you are going to manage your social
media platforms, post updates, comments and interactions as well as your
normal day-to-day workload.

Don't worry, help is at hand! With easy to use dashboards to help you
monitor your social accounts, you are able to keep track of mentions,
comments, messages and trends easily and efficiently.

Two of the most popular social media dashboards are TweetDeck and
Hootsuite and the best news is they are both free to use.

## TWEETDECK:

TweetDeck is a fantastic platform where you can schedule your tweets as
well as organise and keep track of updates from those you follow. You
are also able to search for keywords allowing you to interact with users
that are tweeting relevant information.

Using TweetDeck allows you to keep everything in one place, meaning you
don't have to check individual sites. There are many other features of
TweetDeck, including keyword searches, tracking mentions of your name
and following threaded conversations. These features allow you to
interact with relevant Twitter users and grow your followers.

To download Tweetdeck for free visit their website:
[http://www.tweetdeck.com]{.ul}

## HOOTSUITE:

Hootsuite is a similar platform to TweetDeck which again is a web-based
platform that allows multiple users on one account. The Hootsuite
dashboard allows you to schedule posts in advance and cross post them to
all of your social media platforms, however be sensitive to the fact
that your Twitter followers may not be the same audience as your
Facebook and LinkedIn accounts. Our recommendation is to change the way
you communicate on different platforms by posting content tailored to
each.

To download Hootsuite for free visit their website:
[http://www.hootsuite.com]{.ul}

# 8. MANAGING THE RISKS OF SOCIAL MEDIA

Inevitably, there are risks to using social media, which can be seen in
the form of staffing, training, productivity and content strategy. These
risks cannot be completely eliminated but can be minimised. It's
essential that any recruitment agency capitalises on the benefits and
opportunities of social media whilst also managing the associated risks
effectively, without endangering their organisation's reputation.

**Your staff** -- You should have a set team in place that are
responsible for managing all social media activity. You need to be able
to trust this team with the responsibility of posting suitable,
appropriate content that reflects the ideal positioning of your brand.
You may also want to consider training your staff externally so that
they understand the risks of social media and know how to deal with
them.

**Handle complaints** -- Responding to complaints quickly and
appropriately is essential. There is the risk that if you don't respond
to a complaint in the right timely manner, the complaint may spiral out
of control. Other social media users may pick up on the complaint that's
been poorly dealt with, potentially commenting on it and sharing it with
others. Therefore always try to deal with complaints effectively. Any
social media complaint is an opportunity to turn it around and present
how good your company is at customer service.

**Increase your productivity** -- If you have no plan in place, social
media can be a time consuming activity. This can lead to a lack of
productivity, caused by searching for new articles to share, thinking of
new posts or being indecisive on how to respond to certain comments.
It's therefore important to have a set strategy about how social media
will be managed on a daily basis, to be efficient with time. For
example, it's a good idea to schedule your social media posts at the
start of the week, rather than posting on an ad hoc basis. This ensures
you're dedicating a set amount of time to drafting your social media
content, focusing your attention on that task rather than dipping in and
out of the project.

**Avoid controversial topics** -- Generally, it's recommended that you
shouldn't comment on certain topics that could be seen as controversial.
This is because they can attract negative publicity and some of your
followers may have opposing views to yours, which creates conflict.
Always try to steer your social media conversations away from
controversial topics, to deflect the potential risks.

**Check your language** -- It's vital to ensure the language you've used
in your social media posts is suitable for your brand. For example, if
you're trying to convey a professional image, potentially the language
you use on social media may be quite formal. However, if you're trying
to build a brand that's approachable and friendly, the language can be
slightly more colloquial. Either way, the language you use should always
reflect the positioning of your brand, so spelling and grammar are
important.

# 9. MEASURING YOUR SOCIAL MEDIA ROI

Measuring the return on investment from social media should be one of
the most important parts of your strategy. Once you have defined your
goals you need to ensure you have the ability to target, track and
measure them. It is unlikely that you will have the perfect social media
strategy from the start, so evaluate your progress often and don't be
afraid to test out new ideas.

Here is a list of useful tools that you can use to measure the
performance of your social media activity.

## HOOTSUITE:

This is an excellent all round tool which can also be used for metrics
tracking as well as scheduling your posts. Using Hootsuite allows you to
generate reports of all of your social media platforms however these
features are limited with the free version.

[http://www.hootsuite.com]{.ul}

## GOOGLE ANALYTICS:

Apply campaign tracking to your social media updates to monitor
conversions to your website. When using Google Analytics you can set a
unique parameter to track when a user clicks from your social media
platform to your website. You are then able to track the user journey
through your site and assess if your social media traffic converts to
opportunities.

[http://www.google.com/analytics/]{.ul}

## FACEBOOK INSIGHTS:

Find out how many people have liked your page, seen your posts and
engaged with your content compared to previous weeks. Add pages to your
newsfeed and analytics to keep up to date with industry news.

[https://facebook.com/yourfacebookpage/insights/]{.ul}

## BIT.LY:

A bit.ly link shortens a URL to prevent it from taking up all of the
characters in your posts. When you add a bit.ly link to your posts it
also measures the click-thorough rate on these links.

[https://bitly.com]{.ul}

## SOCIAL MENTION:

This clever website allows you to track mentions of your business name
over the whole social media spectrum. Here you will see the strength of
your social activity, the passion and reach. This will not only give you
an insight into what is being said but who is saying it and where it has
been mentioned.

[[http://www.socialmention.com]{.ul}](http://www.socialmention.com/)
