# Health Optimization Path

Structured learning sequence from basic health literacy to complete biological optimization.

## Overview

This path takes you from health fundamentals to advanced biohacking and longevity optimization.

**Estimated Timeline:** 18-36 months (lifelong practice)
**Prerequisites:** Willingness to experiment and track data

---

## Stage 1: Health Foundations (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** Understand basic health principles and establish baseline.

### Core Topics
- [ ] Human physiology basics
- [ ] Macronutrients and micronutrients
- [ ] Hydration and water quality
- [ ] Sleep hygiene fundamentals
- [ ] Basic exercise principles
- [ ] Stress and its health impacts
- [ ] Preventive health screening

### Key Resources
- **Folder:** [Health Sciences/](../../Health%20Sciences/)
- **Folder:** [Health Sciences/Preventive Medicine/](../../Health%20Sciences/Preventive%20Medicine/)
- **Books:** "How Not to Die", "The 4-Hour Body", "Why We Sleep"
- **Apps:** MyFitnessPal, Sleep Cycle

### Milestones
- [ ] Completed full health screening (bloodwork, vitals)
- [ ] Established health baseline metrics
- [ ] Understanding of basic nutrition
- [ ] Sleep tracking for 30+ days
- [ ] Basic fitness assessment completed
- [ ] Stress level awareness
