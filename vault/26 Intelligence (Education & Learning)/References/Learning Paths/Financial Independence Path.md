# Financial Independence Path

Structured learning sequence from basic personal finance to global financial mastery.

## Overview

This path takes you from financial literacy to financial sovereignty, covering personal finance, investing, business finance, and international wealth strategies.

**Estimated Timeline:** 12-24 months (depending on depth and pace)
**Prerequisites:** Basic math, willingness to track spending

---

## Stage 1: Personal Finance Foundation (Months 1-3)

**Goal:** Master personal financial management and establish solid habits.

### Core Topics
- [ ] Budgeting and cash flow management
- [ ] Emergency fund building (3-6 months expenses)
- [ ] Debt management and payoff strategies
- [ ] Banking fundamentals and account optimization
- [ ] Basic personal taxes

### Key Resources
- **Folder:** [Finance & Investing/Personal Finance/](../../Finance%20%26%20Investing/Personal%20Finance/)
- **Books:** "Rich Dad Poor Dad", "The Total Money Makeover", "Your Money or Your Life"
- **Tools:** Mint, YNAB, Personal Capital for tracking

### Milestones
- [ ] Created detailed monthly budget
- [ ] Opened high-yield savings account
- [ ] Started emergency fund
- [ ] Tracked spending for 90 days
- [ ] Created debt payoff plan (if applicable)

---

## Stage 2: Credit Mastery (Months 2-4)

**Goal:** Build and optimize personal credit for financial leverage.

### Core Topics
- [ ] Credit score fundamentals (FICO, VantageScore)
- [ ] Credit report reading and monitoring
- [ ] Credit building strategies
- [ ] Credit card optimization and rewards
- [ ] Credit repair techniques

### Key Resources
- **Folder:** [Finance & Investing/Personal Finance/Personal Credit/](../../Finance%20%26%20Investing/Personal%20Finance/Personal%20Credit/)
- **Services:** Credit Karma, Experian, myFICO
- **Books:** Credit repair guides in folder

### Milestones
- [ ] Obtained all 3 credit reports (Experian, Equifax, TransUnion)
- [ ] Disputed any errors on credit reports
- [ ] Opened credit building accounts (secured card, credit builder loan)
- [ ] Credit score improved by 50+ points (if starting below 700)
- [ ] Optimized credit utilization below 30%

---

## Stage 3: Investing Fundamentals (Months 4-8)

**Goal:** Understand investment principles and start building wealth.

### Core Topics
- [ ] Investment account types (401k, IRA, Roth IRA, brokerage)
- [ ] Asset classes (stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities)
- [ ] Index funds and ETFs
- [ ] Diversification and asset allocation
- [ ] Risk tolerance and time horizon
- [ ] Dollar-cost averaging

### Key Resources
- **Folder:** [Finance & Investing/Investing/](../../Finance%20%26%20Investing/Investing/)
- **Folder:** [Finance & Investing/Investing/ETF/](../../Finance%20%26%20Investing/Investing/ETF/)
- **Books:** "The Simple Path to Wealth", "A Random Walk Down Wall Street", "The Intelligent Investor"
- **Courses:** Khan Academy Investing, Coursera Financial Markets

### Milestones
- [ ] Opened retirement account (IRA or 401k)
- [ ] Opened taxable brokerage account
- [ ] Made first investment in index fund
- [ ] Set up automatic monthly contributions
- [ ] Created target asset allocation plan
- [ ] Invested consistently for 3+ months

## Stage 5: Active Trading (Optional - Months 9-18)

**Goal:** Learn active trading strategies for those interested in hands-on investing.

### Core Topics
- [ ] Technical analysis and chart reading
- [ ] Day trading strategies and risk management
- [ ] Options trading
- [ ] Swing trading
- [ ] Trading psychology
- [ ] Risk/reward ratios and position sizing

### Key Resources
- **Folder:** [Finance & Investing/Investing/Day Trading/](../../Finance%20%26%20Investing/Investing/Day%20Trading/)
- **Books:** "Trading in the Zone", "Technical Analysis of the Financial Markets"
- **Platforms:** TradingView, Thinkorswim paper trading

### Milestones
- [ ] Completed 3+ months paper trading (simulated)
- [ ] Developed and backtested trading strategy
- [ ] Profitable on paper trading for 2+ months
- [ ] Started live trading with small capital ($500-1000)
- [ ] Maintained trading journal for 100+ trades
- [ ] Achieved consistent risk management (never risk >2% per trade)

**WARNING:** Skip this stage if not interested in active trading. Passive index investing outperforms most active traders.
