Tapping Into Social Media Traffic Strategies

Social media is the next big thing in the internet when it comes to
drawing massive traffic. Did you know that Facebook has over 500 Million
users and is now the number one internet activity of all time? (It used
to be email)

Twitter and Facebook has been estimated to be worth billions and is here
to stay. If you want to draw truckloads of traffic into your website,
you'll be shooting yourself in the foot if you fail to tap into this
huge traffic source.

That being said, how does one tap into social media traffic? Well, for
example you can start a Facebook page for your online business and ask
your fans and customers to follow your updates there. The more buzz you
create on your Facebook fan page, the more it will appear on the feeds
of the fans and other friends of the fans would be able to see it and
join in.

Twitter is also a great source of social media traffic. By using the
"\#" tag, you can generate hype or organise tweeting competitions to get
your customers involved in your community and generate buzz as well. You
can also ask your fans to "Follow you" on twitter and get the latest
updates about your business.

Also, there are many social sharing sites which allow people stumble
upon or "like" websites of interest that other social media fans can
follow such as reddit.com and digg.com. You can also create web 2.0 mini
websites with free services such as Squidoo or Hubpages which integrate
social sharing tools.

In short, the secret to high traffic loads is integrate a social media
targeting strategy into your traffic generation plan in conjunction with
conventional traffic generation methods such as article submission.
