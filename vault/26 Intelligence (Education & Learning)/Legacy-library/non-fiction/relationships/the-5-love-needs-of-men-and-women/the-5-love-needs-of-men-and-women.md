## Description

America's Family Coaches, Dr. Gary and Barbara Rosberg, share new research that may surprise you and change the way you experience your marriage. Part of the "Divorce-Proofing America's Marriages" campaign. Dr. Gary talks to wives about a husband's top five needs, while Barbara talks to husbands about how wives spell intimacy, how to safeguard your marriage, and what it means when a woman withdraws. The Rosbergs host "America's Family Coaches... LIVE," a nationally syndicated daily radio program. Endorsed by Max Lucado, Dr. Tony Evans, and Patsy Clairmont.
