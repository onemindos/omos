## Description

Build Multiple Companies With High Dollar Credit Lines. A workbook on developing aged corporations with business credit, covering the process of acquiring aged/shelf companies, building corporate credit profiles through Dunn & Bradstreet and Paydex scoring, and scaling credit building plans to $1,000,000. Includes chapters on company structures across multiple states (Nevada, Wyoming, New Mexico, Colorado, Montana, Ohio), bank loans, business plans, and advanced options like leased bank instruments and B2B trade lines.

## Key Topics

- Aged corporation acquisition and development
- Business credit building (Dunn & Bradstreet, Paydex)
- Credit building plans to $1,000,000
- Company structures across multiple states
- Bank loans and business plan templates
- B2B trade lines and corporate guarantees
- Mergers and acquisitions for faster credit
