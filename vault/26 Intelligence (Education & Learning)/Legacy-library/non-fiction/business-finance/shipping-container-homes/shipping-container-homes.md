## Description

A complete guide to building shipping container homes — covering everything from site preparation and container conversion to fitting the roof, installing utilities (water, electricity, sewage), flooring, framing, insulation, drywall, and exterior finishes. Designed for those in the tiny homes movement or interested in off-grid, durable, low-cost alternative housing.

## Key Topics

- Site preparation and container delivery
- Container conversion and placement
- Fitting the roof
- First fix services: water, electricity, sewage, phone
- Flooring, framing, and ceiling installation
- Insulation and second fix services
- Drywall, interior walls, and exterior finishes
- Real estate strategies and tips
