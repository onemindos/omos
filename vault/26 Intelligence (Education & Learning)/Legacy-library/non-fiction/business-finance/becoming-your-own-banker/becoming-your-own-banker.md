## Description

The seminal work on the Infinite Banking Concept (IBC) by bestselling author R. Nelson Nash. Teaches how to use dividend-paying whole life insurance as a personal banking system, allowing individuals to recapture the interest they would normally pay to banks and finance companies. A blueprint for taking charge of personal financial well-being through self-financing.
