## Description

Your guide for increasing flexibility, improving range of motion, toning muscles, and relieving pain and discomfort. Features over 550 full-color anatomical illustrations and more than 130 exercises showing how muscles interact with surrounding joints and skeletal structures. Includes 13 proven programs for optimizing training and performance in sports including running, cycling, basketball, soccer, golf, and track and field.

By Frédéric Delavier — former editor in chief of *PowerMag* in France, journalist for *Le Monde du Muscle*, and author of *Strength Training Anatomy* (2M+ copies sold).
