## Description

Little Cub wonders aloud, "What is heaven like?" With tender words, Papa polar bear describes a wonderful place, free of sadness and tears, where God warmly welcomes his loved ones. This gentle story provides satisfying answers for a young child's most difficult questions about what happens after this life. Part of the *God Gave Us* series (also: *God Gave Us You*, *God Gave Us Two*, *God Gave Us Christmas*).
