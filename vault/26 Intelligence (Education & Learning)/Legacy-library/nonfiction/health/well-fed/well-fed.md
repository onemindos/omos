# Well Fed

## Paleo Recipes for People Who Love to Eat

By Melissa Joulwan

---

## Overview

A Paleo cookbook designed for people who genuinely love food. All recipes are grain-free, sugar-free, legume-free, and dairy-free while remaining flavorful and satisfying.

## Features

- Grain, sugar, legume, and dairy free recipes
- Photography by David Humphreys
- Foreword by Melissa & Dallas Hartwig (Whole9 / Whole30)
- Practical approach to Paleo cooking

## Notes

- Self-published via Smudge Publishing
- Companion to the Whole30 / Paleo lifestyle
