# The Complete Guide to Fasting

## Heal Your Body Through Intermittent, Alternate-Day, and Extended Fasting

By Jason Fung, MD with Jimmy Moore

---

## Overview

A comprehensive guide explaining why fasting is actually good for health, how it helps you lose weight without cutting calories, and how it works as a treatment for type 2 diabetes. Covers all fasting protocols from intermittent to extended fasting.

## Key Topics

- Why fasting is beneficial for health
- How fasting helps with weight loss and metabolism
- Fasting for type 2 diabetes treatment
- Various fasting protocols (intermittent, alternate-day, extended)
- Managing common concerns during a fast
- What liquids to consume during fasting
- 20 grain-free recipes for healthy eating around fasts

## Notes

- Dr. Jason Fung is a nephrologist with extensive clinical experience
- Co-authored with Jimmy Moore, veteran health podcaster
- Published by Victory Belt Publishing
