# The 21-Day Sugar Detox Cookbook

## Over 100 Recipes for Any Program Level

By Diane Sanfilippo, BS, NC

---

## Overview

A companion cookbook to the 21-Day Sugar Detox program featuring more than 100 guilt-free recipes that are 100% gluten-free, grain-free, dairy-free, and Paleo-friendly. Includes complete 21-day meal plans and special recipes for various dietary needs.

## Features

- 100+ recipes for breakfasts, lunches, dinners, snacks, and treats
- 21-day meal plans (simple to follow, whole-food plans)
- Special recipes for athletes, pregnant & nursing moms, pescatarians, and autoimmunity
- All recipes are grain-free, gluten-free, dairy-free, and Paleo-friendly
