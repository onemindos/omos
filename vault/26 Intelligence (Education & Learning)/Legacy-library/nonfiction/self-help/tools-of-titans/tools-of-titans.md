# Tools of Titans

## The Tactics, Routines and Habits of Billionaires, Icons and World-Class Performers

By Tim Ferriss

## Overview

The distilled tips, tools, and tactics from over 200 world-class performers interviewed on The Tim Ferriss Show podcast. Organized into three sections: Healthy, Wealthy, and Wise — covering fitness, money, and wisdom.

## Featured Insights From

- Tony Robbins — morning 'priming' routine
- Chris Sacca — going on the offense
- Arnold Schwarzenegger — psychological warfare
- Robert Rodriguez — turning weaknesses into strengths
- Jocko Willink — overcoming setbacks
- Jamie Foxx — pushing through fear
- Marc Andreessen — stress-testing opportunities
- Malcolm Gladwell — asking better questions
- Rick Rubin — saunas and tiny homework assignments
- Paulo Coelho — getting unstuck creatively
