# The Slight Edge

## Turning Simple Disciplines into Massive Success & Happiness

By Jeff Olson

---

## Overview

The Slight Edge is a way of thinking that enables you to make the daily choices that lead to the success and happiness you desire. The core philosophy: simple, positive actions repeated consistently over time create radical results through the power of compounding.

## Core Concept

Small daily disciplines — things that are easy to do but also easy not to do — compound over time into massive success or failure. The slight edge is understanding this principle and choosing the positive path consistently.
