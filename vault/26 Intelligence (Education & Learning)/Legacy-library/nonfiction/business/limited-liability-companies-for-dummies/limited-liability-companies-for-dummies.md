# Limited Liability Companies for Dummies

## 3rd Edition

By Jennifer Reuting — Founder of InCorp Services

---

## Overview

A comprehensive guide to starting and effectively managing a limited liability company. Covers everything from understanding the LLC structure to creating articles of organization, managing day-to-day operations, and navigating federal regulations and fees.

## Key Topics

- The ABCs of LLCs — benefits and structure
- Creating articles of organization
- Putting together an operating agreement
- Record-keeping and tax filing
- Managing day-to-day operations
- Attracting investors
- Advanced topics: real estate, estate planning, asset protection
