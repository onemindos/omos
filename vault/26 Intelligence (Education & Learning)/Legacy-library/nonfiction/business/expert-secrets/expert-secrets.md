# Expert Secrets

## The Underground Playbook to Find Your Message, Build a Tribe, and Change the World

By Russell Brunson — Foreword by Robert Kiyosaki

---

## Overview

The second book in Russell Brunson's marketing trilogy. Expert Secrets teaches how to turn specialized knowledge, talents, and abilities into a business by finding your voice, building a mass movement, and making your calling a career.

## Key Themes

- Finding your voice and becoming a leader
- Building a mass movement of people you can affect
- Making your calling a career where people pay for your advice
- The expert's journey from personal development to contribution
