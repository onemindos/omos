# Twelve and a Half

## Leveraging the Emotional Ingredients Necessary for Business Success

By Gary Vaynerchuk

---

## Overview

Gary Vaynerchuk's exploration of the emotional skills that are essential for business success. He identifies twelve and a half key emotional ingredients and demonstrates how developing these traits can transform both business outcomes and personal fulfillment.

## The 12.5 Ingredients

1. Gratitude
2. Self-Awareness
3. Accountability
4. Optimism
5. Empathy
6. Kindness
7. Tenacity
8. Curiosity
9. Patience
10. Conviction
11. Humility
12. Ambition
- My Half: Kind Candor
