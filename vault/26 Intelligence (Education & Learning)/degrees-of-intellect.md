## 🏛️ The Hierarchy of Mind

Intellect is not a binary ("Smart vs Dumb"). It is a ladder of increasing complexity and agency. One Mind OS is designed to move you up this ladder.

### Level 1: Signal (The Sensor)

- **Definition:** Raw, uninterpreted input.
- **Example:** "The thermometer reads 30 degrees."
- **Role:** **Collection.**
- **OneMind Tool:** Oura Ring sensors, Server Logs.

### Level 2: Information (The Clerk)

- **Definition:** Signal + Context. We know _what_ it means.
- **Example:** "30 degrees is freezing. Water will turn to ice."
- **Role:** **Organization.**
- **OneMind Tool:** Obsidian Folders, NocoDB tables.

### Level 3: Knowledge (The Technician)

- **Definition:** Information + Utility (How-to).
- **Example:** "To prevent pipes freezing, we must drip the faucets."
- **Role:** **Application.**
- **OneMind Tool:** Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), "How-to" Docs.

### Level 4: Intelligence (The Engineer)

- **Definition:** Dynamic problem solving in novel situations.
- **Example:** "The faucet barely drips. I need to design a recirculation pump system to solve this permanently."
- **Role:** **Optimization.**
- **OneMind Tool:** Activepieces Automation, Python Scripts, Agentic Workflow.

### Level 5: Wisdom (The Sage)

- **Definition:** Intelligence + Values (The "Why"). Knowing what is _worth_ solving.
- **Example:** "Is it worth staying in this climate? Maybe we should move the family to a warmer zone for better long-term health."
- **Role:** **Selection.**
- **OneMind Tool:** The Codex (Journaling, Reflection), The "Legacy" Pillar.

### Level 6: Prescience (The Oracle)

- **Definition:** Pattern recognition so deep it looks like predicting the future.
- **Example:** "Based on the last 10 years of weather patterns and current energy prices, a crisis is coming in 3 weeks. We will stock up now."
- **Role:** **Anticipation.**
- **OneMind Tool:** Quantimo.do (Predictive Analytics), Mega-Studies.

---

## 🧠 The "AI Flip"

In the Hybrid Age (`reference-hybrid-civilization.md`), the hierarchy flips.

- **Humans** used to do Levels 1-3 manually.
- **Now, AI** dominates Levels 1-4 (Collection -> Intelligence).
- **The Human Advantage** moves strictly to **Levels 5 & 6 (Wisdom & Prescience).**

> **"The AI solves the puzzle. The Human chooses which puzzle to solve."**

---

## 🛠️ Practical Application

When entering a new domain (e.g., `Soil Biology`), assess your degree of intellect:

1.  **Am I just collecting facts?** (Level 2).
2.  **Can I fix a problem?** (Level 4).
3.  **Do I understand the deep principles?** (Level 5).

**Goal:** Automate Levels 1-3 via One Mind OS so you can live in Levels 4-6.
