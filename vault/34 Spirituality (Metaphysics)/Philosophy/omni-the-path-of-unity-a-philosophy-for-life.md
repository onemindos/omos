      Omni - The Path of Unity - A Philosophy for Life was received as channellings from The
Master, by The Abbotts, in the autumn of 2001. This book is a Divine response, which fulfills a
need for a spiritual philosophy that modern men and women can incorporate into their lives.
      The Master favors no one particular denomination and is never identified by name. His kind
and compassionate discourses and practical answers, to both spiritual and every day earthly
questions, are recorded, as He narrated them, in both modern and archaic language.
      Omni - The Path of Unity has 10 major Principles and 114 parables or short stories.
      The first parable of each Principle is always given by the Master, as His explanation of that
Principle. Each parable is listed under a major Principle heading, but it may also refer to any of
the remaining Principles and many different life situations.
      Omni - The Path of Unity can be read in the usual manner or used as a quick reference
guide for Divine guidance and inspiration on any specific problem that you are currently
experiencing. By using the Table of Contents below you will be able to quickly find a list of
parables that include your key word of interest.

                                       Principle One

   “All embrace the Divine Spirit that is Omnipresent, Omnipotent
                            and Omniscient”
     The Master’s explanation of Principle 1

                                  Principle 1 : Parable 1
       “I would speak of the Divine Spirit, that is Omnipresent, Omnipotent and Omniscient that is
everywhere, all powerful and all knowing.
       The Divine Spirit is neither feminine nor masculine. It is a culmination of all human life, all
animal life, mineral kingdoms and the many layers and levels between that and the Divine Spirit.
       The Divine Spirit is compassionate love, is respect and caring and is all-powerful. The
Divine Spirit has sent out It’s sparks of life to create life forms throughout the Universe, and on
Earth has created humanity. It sees all these Divine sparks as equal in Its sight. No one is greater
or lesser than another.
       All are experiencing life, in it’s own particular aspect, in order to grow, to learn lessons to
improve oneself, to perfect oneself, that that spark of Light one day, will rejoin with the Divine
Spirit, on the very highest levels of the heavenly realms and once more, be the Divine Spirit, in
all It’s Omnipresence, in all It’s Omnipotence, in all It’s Omniscience.”

     The Master, a wise and divine being, journeyed through distant lands, talking to the people,
discussing daily issues and solving spiritual problems for them, in a wise and loving manner.
One warm summer morning He came to the outskirts of a large village and the people there,
hearing of His fame and drawn by His radiant nature, came to Him with their queries and doubts.


                                  Principle 1 : Parable 2
     And they came to Him and said, “Is not the Divine Spirit our father and not our mother?”
     And He said to them, “The Divine Spirit is mother and father, is brother and sister, it is
male and female and it is neither. For the Divine Power is a culmination of all living forces
throughout the universe.
     But look upon it as a mother or father if you wish, to give you comfort, for this is the way
of humanity. But those who are of a higher mind, realise that it is an Omnipotent force that loves
them from the profound depth of It’s being. And you yourselves are sparks of that mighty force.”


                                 Principle 1 : Parable 3
      Then an arrogant man came to the Master and said, “I am an atheist. I do not believe there
is a Divine Power. For if there is a Divine Power, why would It allow suffering upon the Earth?”
      And the Master said to him, “Take my hand, do you feel it?
      And he said, “Yes”
      “This proves therefore, that I have a hand, does it not?”
      And he said, “Yes, I believe that you have a hand, Master.”
      Now the Master said, “I send you my love.” And He enveloped the man in a warmth and
said, “Do you feel my love?”
      And the man said that he did feel the Master’s love.
      “But where is the physical evidence of it?” said the Master.
      There is no physical evidence of love, thought the man and he expressed it so, to the
Master.
      Then the Master said, “Then do you see, that for some things, you can find no evidence.
You cannot find evidence, for love, nor hate, nor fear, nor joy, nor harmony, nor peace. These
are expressions of humankind. And in that same way, the Divine Source is an expression of all
the living forces that are within and without our cosmos. It cannot come under physical scrutiny;
there is no physical evidence for it. But it is still there, whether you believe in it or not.”
      And the man thought on this and said, “Then why does this Divine Power, allow us to
suffer?”
      “You suffer because you have asked to suffer,” said the Master. “You need the lesson of
suffering. You need it to wake you up, to the blocks, to the problems that you have created in
your soul, through karmic ties and many past lives. You need to perfect yourself. Think of a
diamond. Must it not be honed, must it not be shaved, and must it not be shaped? It does this
under pressure; it does this from a harsh outside element. In the same way, your soul needs to be
made into a diamond. It can only do so at this time, through hardship and suffering, because
humankind does not seem to learn easily, by gentleness and love. You could learn these things
by gentleness and love, but indeed, it is through despair that you learn quickly and then you do
remember the lesson.”
      Then the atheist thought on this and said, “Perhaps I am wrong, perhaps there is a Divine
Power.”
      “Go seek evidence of a Divine Spirit.” said the Master, “Go seek evidence not in a physical
manner, but by opening up your heart and mind and come back to me in a time and tell me, if
you have discovered the Divine Spirit.”
      And so, the atheist wandered for many months and in a year’s time, he found the Master
and said, “I have seen, I have opened up my heart and mind and now I believe there is a Divine
Spirit, there is a Divine Power and I am part of that Divine Being.”
      And the Master embraced him and said, “Indeed you are, indeed you are loved.”

      “But what does the Divine Source do for us, that we should love It, we should worship It?”
They asked.

appreciate It for the many gifts that It does give to you?”
      And the villagers then understood and were grateful to the Divine Spirit.

still preferring the things of the physical nature. Do not despair at them, do not condemn them, if
they do not understand about spiritual things in this lifetime, for surely in a lifetime still to come,
they will one day understand and open up their hearts and minds.”

      I would say to you, one world religion that is based upon the precepts of equality, respect,

guidance and encourages you to build up your Divine powers; a religion built from unconditional

mind, at this present time on Earth. But it is hoped, that one day in the future, all humankind
shall rise as one, forgetting the limitations of their own religions, forgetting the limitations of
their own choices and truly become One, One again with the Divine Spirit.”
