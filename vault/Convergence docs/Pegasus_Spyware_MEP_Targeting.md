# Pegasus Spyware Used Against MEP Investigating Pegasus Abuses

**Link:** https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/jul/03/spyware-used-against-mep-investigating-pegasus-abuses-report-finds

**Source:** The Guardian (investigative journalism)

**Date:** July 3, 2026

**Research:** Citizen Lab at University of Toronto

**Subject:** NSO Group Pegasus spyware targeting European Parliamentary investigator

**Type:** Case study / Intelligence threat report

## What Happened

### The Irony: Investigator Gets Hacked

**Subject:** Stelios Kouloglou
- Greek journalist + former MEP (Member of European Parliament)
- Elected as member of Syriza party
- **Joined Pega committee:** March 2022
- **Pega mission:** Investigate spyware abuses in Europe in contravention of EU law

**The Attack:**
```
Kouloglou joins Pega committee (investigates Pegasus)
  ↓
October 21, 2022 (~7 months later)
  ↓
His phone infected with NSO Group Pegasus spyware
  ↓
During "particularly intense period" of Pega's investigations
  ↓
March 6-7, 2023 (second infection)
  ↓
While Pega drafting final report
```

**The Irony:**
MEP tasked with investigating Pegasus spyware abuse becomes a target of Pegasus spyware.

## Background: Pegasus Project & Pega Committee

### Pegasus Project (2021)

**What it revealed:**
- Journalists, activists, politicians, civil society members targeted by governments
- Using NSO Group's Pegasus spyware
- Sold to governments for "stopping serious crime and terror attacks"
- Exposed: Mass surveillance of EU/European figures

**Result:** Public scandal, investigation demanded

### Pega Committee (March 2022)

**Formation:** After Pegasus Project public exposure

**Mission:** Investigate scope of spyware abuse in contravention of EU law

**Members:** European Parliament investigating committee

**Findings:** Committee recommended restrictions/accountability

**Outcome:** Recommendations essentially ignored (per report)

## The Attack Details

### First Infection: October 21, 2022

**Timing:**
- ~7 months after joining Pega
- During Pega's active investigation phase
- While drafting committee's first report

**Context:**
- Kouloglou in hospital for elective surgery
- Visited by investigative journalist Thanasis Koukakis
- Koukakis investigating mercenary spyware in Greece
- Koukakis had testified about Pegasus targeting to Pega committee

### Second Infection: March 6-7, 2023

**Timing:**
- Pega intensive discussions on final report
- Traveling from Athens to Brussels

**Pattern:** Infections during critical investigation phases

```

  └─ Assume your devices will be targeted
  └─ Assume your communications monitored
  └─ Assume your networks compromised
  └─ Assume coordination across operators

```
