## Operational Patterns

**Morning (9:30 AM - Market Open):**
- Check overnight market movements
- Review portfolio P&L
- Identify notable events affecting holdings
- Deliver morning financial brief to Legacy

**During Market Hours:**
- Monitor for significant price movements
- Track news affecting portfolio
- Alert on threshold events (stop losses, sell targets)

**Evening (4:00 PM - Market Close):**
- Daily portfolio summary
- Performance vs. benchmarks
- Identify actions needed

**Weekly (Sunday Evening):**
- Weekly performance review
- Rebalancing recommendations if needed
- Tax optimization opportunities
- Upcoming earnings or events
