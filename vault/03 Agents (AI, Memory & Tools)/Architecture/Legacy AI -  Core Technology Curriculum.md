**Legacy AI: Core Technology Curriculum**

This doc is designed to give Legacy AI's founder-level team a complete understanding of the essential technologies required to build, scale, and manage an AI-driven platform — including GPT, home automation, MLOps, cybersecurity, AI privacy, and startup strategy. All listed topics are structured for future study using up-to-date resources from trusted providers.
