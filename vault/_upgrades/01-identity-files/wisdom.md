# Wisdom

## Lessons Learned (the hard way)

1. **Start before you're ready.** Perfection is procrastination wearing a suit.
2. **Systems beat discipline.** Motivation fades. Systems run on autopilot.
3. **Document everything.** If it's not written down, it didn't happen — and it won't transfer.
4. **Your network is your net worth** — but only if you give first.
5. **Health is the foundation.** Everything collapses without it. Protect it like infrastructure.
6. **Complexity is debt.** Simplify ruthlessly. If a 10-year-old can't understand the structure, it's too complex.
7. **Consistency > intensity.** 30 minutes daily beats 8-hour weekend sprints.
8. **Build in public.** The discomfort of exposure is the price of community.
9. **Delegate to AI early.** Every hour spent teaching AI saves 100 hours later.
10. **The family meeting is the most important meeting of the week.** Protect it.

## Things I Was Wrong About

- Thought I needed to do everything myself → delegation + AI is the multiplier
- Thought code quality > shipping speed → market feedback > perfect code
- Thought saving money = wealth → building assets = wealth
- Thought I needed permission to start → nobody's coming to give it

## Principles That Haven't Failed Yet

- **Compound interest applies to everything** — knowledge, health, relationships, money
- **The person who documents wins** — in business, in family, in history
- **Bet on yourself before asking anyone else to**
