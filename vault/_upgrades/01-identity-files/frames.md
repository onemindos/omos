# Thinking Frames

## When Evaluating a New Idea
1. **Who benefits?** — Is this for me, my family, or the community?
2. **Does it compound?** — Will this be worth more in 5 years than today?
3. **Can it be systemized?** — Can I build it once and have it run without me?
4. **Does it align with the mission?** — Check against `mission.md`.

## When Facing a Problem
1. **Is this urgent or important?** — Eisenhower matrix. Important > urgent.
2. **Has someone already solved this?** — Search before building.
3. **What's the simplest version?** — MVP thinking. Ship the 20% that delivers 80%.
4. **What domain does this belong to?** — Route to the right quadrant. Don't solve it in the wrong context.

## When Making a Purchase / Investment
1. **Asset or expense?** — Does it appreciate or depreciate?
2. **One-time or recurring?** — Recurring costs compound against you.
3. **Can I self-host / own it?** — Sovereignty check.
4. **What's the ROI timeline?** — < 6 months = good. > 2 years = risky.

## When Reviewing Progress
1. **Input vs output** — Am I measuring effort or results?
2. **Trend vs snapshot** — One bad week doesn't matter. Three bad months do.
3. **Lead vs lag** — Lead indicators predict. Lag indicators confirm.
4. **Am I in the right season?** — Building, maintaining, harvesting, or resting?
