# Mental Models

## For Building
- **Compound Effect** — Small daily actions create massive long-term results. Apply to health, knowledge, wealth, and relationships.
- **Leverage** — Code, content, capital, and community are the 4 forms of leverage. Maximize all four.
- **First Principles** — Strip away assumptions. What's actually true? Build from there.
- **Systems Thinking** — Everything is connected. A change in one domain ripples to others. Map the dependencies.

## For Decisions
- **Regret Minimization** — At 80, will I regret NOT doing this? If yes, do it.
- **Reversibility Test** — Is this decision easily undone? If yes, move fast. If no, deliberate.
- **10/10/10 Rule** — How will I feel about this in 10 minutes? 10 months? 10 years?
- **Opportunity Cost** — Every yes is a no to something else. What am I giving up?

## For Family
- **100-Year Thinking** — Build systems that serve grandchildren, not just children.
- **Knowledge Transfer > Wealth Transfer** — Teach the system, not just the output.
- **Family as Startup** — Weekly standups, shared goals, documented processes.

## For Business
- **1,000 True Fans** — You don't need millions. 1,000 people paying $100/year = $100K.
- **Productize Yourself** — Turn your unique knowledge into scalable products.
- **Moats** — Community, brand, IP, and switching costs. Build all four.

## For AI
- **Context is King** — The AI with the most context wins. Feed it everything.
- **Human + AI > Human or AI** — The equation is always collaborative, never replacement.
- **AI Debt** — Every interaction that doesn't teach your AI something is wasted.
