# Predictions

## High Confidence (>80%)
- **Personal AI becomes a basic need by 2030** — Like smartphones in 2015. Everyone will have one. The question is who owns it.
- **"Second Brain" becomes a commodity** — Note-taking is table stakes. The market will demand action, not just capture.
- **AI agents replace SaaS subscriptions** — Why pay for 10 apps when one agent can do it all? The SaaS unbundling.
- **Generational knowledge transfer becomes a market** — Families will pay to preserve and transmit knowledge systematically.

## Medium Confidence (50-80%)
- **Obsidian (or similar) becomes the OS layer** — Plain text + plugins + AI = the new operating system for knowledge workers.
- **OneMind-style frameworks become a category** — Like GTD was a category. "Human Enhancement Framework" becomes a recognized market.
- **Home robotics goes mainstream by 2032** — And the families with documented SOPs (like OneMind) will integrate them fastest.
- **Community-owned AI > corporate AI** — Open-source, self-hosted, community-governed AI wins long term.

## Bets I'm Making
- Building OneMind as a **100-domain system** (not just productivity) because life is bigger than work
- Investing in **self-hosted infrastructure** because platform risk is real
- Starting the **community now** (before the product is polished) because early believers become evangelists
- Documenting **everything in public** because transparency is the ultimate moat
