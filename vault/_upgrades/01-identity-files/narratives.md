# Narratives

## The Origin Story
A Filipino-American father and engineer who realized that the productivity tools of the future weren't being built for real life — for families, for generations, for the messy complexity of being human. So he built his own. Not an app. Not a course. A living, breathing framework that grows with you. He called it OneMind.

## The Counter-Positioning
> "They gave you a Second Brain. I'm giving you One Mind."

Tiago Forte's Second Brain is a notebook system. OneMind is an enhancement framework. Second Brain captures and organizes. OneMind captures, organizes, directs, executes, AND multiplies — with AI as a native partner, not an afterthought.

## The Vision Story
Imagine your grandchild inheriting not just money, but a 100-domain knowledge base with 50 years of family wisdom, AI agents that know the family's values, and systems that have been compounding since before they were born. That's OneMind.

## The Builder Story
3,000+ commits. 2,800+ files. 100 domains. 9 AI agents. Built in public, documented in real-time, designed to be forked. This isn't theory — it's a lived system. Every day I use it. Every day it gets better. Every day the gap between me and people without it widens.

## The Movement Story
OneMind isn't just for me. It's a framework anyone can fork and make their own. A community of builders who believe AI should enhance humans, not replace them. We're not anti-tech. We're pro-human. We're building the future we want to live in.
