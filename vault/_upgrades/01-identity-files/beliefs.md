# Beliefs

## On AI
- AI is a partner, not a tool. The relationship is collaborative, not transactional.
- The gap between "uses AI" and "lives with AI" is the same gap between "has a phone" and "has a computer" in 1995.
- Whoever controls their own AI context controls their future. Everyone else rents theirs.

## On Sovereignty
- Own your data. Own your infrastructure. Own your decisions.
- Platforms are landlords. Build on land you own.
- Self-hosting isn't paranoia — it's architecture.

## On Family
- Family is the original startup. Treat it with the same rigor.
- Children don't inherit money well. They inherit systems well.
- The best legacy isn't what you leave — it's what they can build on.

## On Wealth
- Revenue is oxygen. Without it, nothing else matters.
- Multiple income streams > one big salary.
- Generational wealth = assets + knowledge + systems, compounding.

## On Building
- Ship before it's ready. Iterate in public.
- Craftsmanship compounds. Shortcuts decay.
- The best system is the one you actually use every day.
