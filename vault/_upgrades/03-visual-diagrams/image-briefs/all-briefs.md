## Style Guide for All Images

- **Background**: Dark charcoal/blackboard (#1a1a2e or similar)
- **Lines**: White chalk-style, hand-drawn feel
- **Accents**: Blue (#4285f4), Green (#34a853), Gold (#fbbc04), Red (#ea4335), Purple (#9c27b0)
- **Typography**: Clean sans-serif for labels, elegant serif for headlines
- **Mood**: Professional but approachable. Technical but human.
- **Resolution**: 2x for retina (3200×1800 for 16:9, 2400×2400 for 1:1)
