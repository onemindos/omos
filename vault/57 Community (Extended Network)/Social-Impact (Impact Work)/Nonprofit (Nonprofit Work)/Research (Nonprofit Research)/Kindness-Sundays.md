# Kindness Sundays — Homeless Outreach Nonprofit

> *A recurring act of service — meeting unhoused people where they are every Sunday with food, supplies, and presence.*

---

## Vision

Kindness Sundays is a nonprofit initiative built on a simple premise: **show up every week, no matter what.** Not a one-time charity drive — a sustained, relationship-based model of community care.

The goal is to build an organization that:
- Provides consistent, dignified service to unhoused individuals and families
- Involves the Delacruz children from an early age — service as a family value
- Grows into a 501(c)(3) with sustainable funding and community partnerships
- Models what it looks like for a legacy family to reinvest in their city

---

## Core Model

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Frequency** | Every Sunday |
| **Format** | Mobile outreach — go to where people are |
| **Services** | Hot meals, hygiene kits, clothing, water, human presence |
| **Target** | Unhoused individuals and families — local area |
| **Family involvement** | Zeus + children participate as able by age |

---

## Why Sundays

Sunday is chosen intentionally:
- Most shelters and services are reduced on weekends
- It creates a sacred rhythm — the family gives on the day of rest
- Consistent day builds trust with unhoused community members over time
