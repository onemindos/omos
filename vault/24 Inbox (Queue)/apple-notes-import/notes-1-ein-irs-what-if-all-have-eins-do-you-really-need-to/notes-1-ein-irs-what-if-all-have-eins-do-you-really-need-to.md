# 1\. EIN / IRS — What If All Have EINs? Do You Really Need to Send Anything?

**

Which entities have EINs based on your screenshots:

**Entity** | **Has EIN?**  
---|---  
Legacy Platform Holdings | Yes (EIN doc on file)  
Legacy Restore | Yes (EIN doc on file)  
Legacy Acadacom | No (&quotNo; EIN available")  
Legacy Capitalist | No (&quotNo; EIN available")  
Legacy Partner Network | No (&quotNo; EIN available")  
  

  

So only Holdings and Restore have EINs. The other three are clean — no federal footprint.

What the IRS technically expects:

  * Since these are single-member LLCs (disregarded entities), the IRS treats them as extensions of you. Your income/expenses flow through your personal Schedule C on Form 1040.
  * Technically you should: (1) file a final Schedule C marked &quotFinal; return" showing zero activity, (2) send a letter to the IRS to close the EIN.

What happens if you do NOTHING:

  * The IRS may eventually send you a notice asking &quotwhere;'s the return for EIN xxx?" This is usually a CP259 notice (request to file) or a CP518 (second notice).
  * If you ignore it, the IRS could assess a substitute return — essentially they guess your income and send you a bill for tax they think you owe. For a zero-activity LLC, this is usually $0 in tax, but the failure-to-file penalty can be $250/month per partner/member for partnerships (Form 1065). For disregarded entities (Schedule C), the penalty is lower since it's just part of your personal return.
  * The real risk: If these were taxed as partnerships (multi-member), the penalties are harsh. If they're single-member (disregarded), the risk is much lower — it just blends into your 1040.

My honest take: For zero-activity single-member LLCs, the practical risk of doing nothing is low. The IRS is unlikely to pursue you aggressively for a dormant LLC with $0 revenue. But the cleanest move is to spend 30 minutes writing two letters:

Internal Revenue Service

Stop 343G

Cincinnati, OH 45999

  

RE: Closure of EIN [your EIN]

  

Dear Sir/Madam,

  

I am requesting the closure of the above EIN for [Legal Entity Name]. 

The LLC has been administratively dissolved by the State of Wyoming 

and has ceased all business activity. No income was earned and no 

employees were hired. Please close this account.

  

Legal Name: [Entity Name]

EIN: [Number]

Member: Zeus Delacruz

Address: [Your address]

  

Sincerely,

Zeus Delacruz

[Date] Send one per entity. That's it. No CPA needed. Free. This eliminates any future IRS surprise letters.

For the three without EINs — do nothing. They don't exist federally.
