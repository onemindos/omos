# Delacruz Family Traditions

> *Traditions are not what your family accidentally does. They are what you decide to do on purpose, again and again, until it becomes who you are.*

---

## The Philosophy

The Delacruz family does not inherit traditions passively. We choose them. Every tradition listed here was either carried forward from Dominican heritage, built deliberately, or is being intentionally seeded now.

A tradition that is named becomes real. A tradition that is unnamed just disappears.

---

## Established Traditions

### Sunday — Family Day

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| What | Full family day. No work, no screens for Zeus, full presence |
| When | Every Sunday |
| Why | The family needs a day that belongs to it. Sunday is that day. |
| What it looks like | Time together — food, activities, conversation. Whatever the family needs that week. |

This is the anchor. Everything else builds around the fact that Sunday is ours.

## Traditions to Consider Adopting

*Not yet adopted — but worth discussing.*

| Tradition | Source | Why It Fits |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| **Annual family photo — same location each year** | Common in intentional families | Visual record of the family growing over decades |
| **"When I was your age" dinners** | Oral tradition | Zeus or grandparents share one story from their youth at a family meal |
| **The family word of the year** | Modern intentional families | Each January, the family picks one word to anchor the year (e.g., "build", "rest", "connect") |
| **Rite of passage at 13** | Many cultures | When a child turns 13, something deliberate marks it — a trip, a conversation, a responsibility given |
| **Dominican Republic trip** | Heritage | At some point, take the kids to the DR — to see where the family came from |

## How to Add a Tradition

A tradition becomes real when:
1. It has a name
2. It has a "why"
3. It has been done at least twice
4. Someone wrote it down here

When a tradition moves from "in progress" → "established," update this document.
