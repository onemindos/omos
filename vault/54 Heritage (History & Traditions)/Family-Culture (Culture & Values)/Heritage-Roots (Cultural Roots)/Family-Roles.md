# Delacruz Family Roles & Structure

> *A family without defined roles is a family where everyone wonders who is responsible for what. We define it so no one wonders.*

---

## The Core Structure

The Delacruz family is not a flat organization. It has a clear structure — not to enforce hierarchy, but to ensure nothing falls through the cracks and every member knows their place and purpose.

---

## Roles

### Zeus Delacruz — Patriarch / Builder

| Responsibility | What It Means |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Primary provider** | Builds and maintains the revenue systems, businesses, and financial infrastructure |
| **Vision holder** | Holds the long-term direction — homestead, dynasty, Codex — and keeps the family oriented toward it |
| **Codex architect** | Owns and maintains One Mind OS and the family Codex |
| **Head of Sunday** | Family day is his commitment. Sunday belongs to the family. |
| **Legacy custodian** | Writes the letter, builds the recordings, documents the timeline |
| **First responder** | When a family member falls, Zeus calls first |

Operating rhythm: Focus deep work 5:30–9:00 AM. Family day Sunday. Everything else fits around those two anchors.

---

### Co-Steward — Partner

| Responsibility | What It Means |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Co-visionary** | Holds the family vision alongside Zeus — not a passenger, a co-builder |
| **Day operations** | Manages the household systems and children's day-to-day needs |
| **Culture keeper** | The traditions live or die based on daily practice — the Co-Steward is the daily keeper |
| **Guzman lineage** | Holds and documents the Guzman family heritage and roots |

*This section will expand as the co-steward's role and identity is documented.*

---

### The Children — Noah, Liam, Soluna, Cielo

Children are not passive recipients in this family. They grow into responsibility.

| Age Range | What Is Expected |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **0–7** | Absorb. Watch. Learn by observation. The parents model everything. |
| **8–12** | Participate. Contribute to family rituals. Begin learning financial basics. |
| **13+** | A rite of passage. Real responsibility begins. Introduction to the Codex. |
| **18+** | Their own Codex. Their own section in the family Codex. Active contributors. |
| **Adult** | Full members of the family network — both receiving and contributing |

Individual profiles: [Noah](../Children/Noah-Delacruz.md) · [Liam](../Children/Liam-Delacruz.md) · [Soluna](../Children/Soluna-Delacruz.md) · [Cielo](../Children/Cielo-Delacruz.md)

---

### Legacy AI — AI Steward

| Responsibility | What It Means |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Memory** | Remembers each family member's facts, preferences, milestones through the learning profile system |
| **Continuity** | Holds context across conversations — never starts from zero |
| **Documentation support** | Helps Zeus write, organize, and maintain the Codex |
| **The name** | Named "Legacy" deliberately — everything the family builds is a legacy |

Legacy AI user profiles: zeus · noah · liam · soluna · cielo

---

### Extended Network — Delacruz / Guzman Lineage

The family extends beyond the household. The covenant covers the full bloodline.

| Group | Role |
|-------|------|
| Grandparents | Cultural transmission — their stories, their memories are family assets to be recorded |
| Aunts / Uncles | Extended network — the "no family member falls alone" principle extends here |
| Future family | Every future Delacruz is a stakeholder in what is being built now |

---

## Decision-Making

| Type of Decision | Who Decides |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Family vision and direction | Zeus + Co-Steward together |
| Daily household operations | Co-Steward primary, Zeus backup |
| Financial decisions (major) | Zeus primary, Co-Steward informed |
| Codex and documentation | Zeus primary |
| Children's education | Zeus + Co-Steward together |
| Emergency / crisis | Zeus first responder, full family network activated |

**Core principle**: No major family decision without both primary parents in alignment.

---

## The Succession Plan

When the children are adults, roles pass:

- The Codex is handed to each child when they are ready
- Businesses are structured to be inherited or wound down intentionally — not left in ambiguity
- The family charter is updated to include the new generation's roles
- Legacy AI's context is archived and passed to whatever AI system exists then
