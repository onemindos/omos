## Who We Are

**The Delacruz / Guzman family.**

Name: **Delacruz** — *of the cross.* Carried with weight. Carried forward with intention.

Heritage: **Dominican Republic** — Taíno indigenous, Spanish colonial, and African ancestral roots. The tri-racial blend that built a nation.

Base: United States. Building toward a sovereign, off-grid, multi-generational life.

Children: **Noah, Liam, Soluna, Cielo,** and future siblings. The goal is 10–12. Not a burden — a civilization.

AI Steward: **Legacy** — named deliberately. Everything we build is a legacy.

---

## The Five Family Laws

These are not suggestions. They are the operating system of this bloodline.

1. **We take care of our own** — No family member falls without the network catching them
2. **We invest in each other** — Time, attention, capital, skills — shared freely within the bloodline
3. **We build, we don't consume** — Every member leaves things better than they found them
4. **We tell the truth** — Especially the hard truths, especially to ourselves
5. **We choose regeneration** — In our bodies, our land, our relationships, our finances
