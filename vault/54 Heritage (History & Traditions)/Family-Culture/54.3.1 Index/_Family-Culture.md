# Family-Culture — Family Culture

> *"Culture eats strategy for breakfast."*

---

## Purpose

Family-Culture is the **family identity center**. This area houses family values, traditions, stories, and the cultural DNA that defines who you are as a family.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Core Values** — What the family stands for
- **Traditions** — Recurring rituals, celebrations, practices
- **Family Stories** — History, narratives, legacy tales
- **Family Motto/Crest** — Identity symbols, declarations
- **Heritage** — Cultural roots, ancestry, origins

---

## Values Framework

| Level | Examples |
|-------|----------|
| Non-Negotiables | Integrity, respect, honesty |
| Priorities | Family first, education, faith |
| Aspirations | Excellence, adventure, service |

---

## Tradition Categories

| Type | Examples |
|------|----------|
| Daily | Family dinner, bedtime routines |
| Weekly | Family night, worship |
| Monthly | Special activity, celebration |
| Annual | Holidays, vacations, reunions |
| Life Events | Birthdays, milestones, rites |

---

## Cultural Transmission

1. **Tell the Stories** — Share family history
2. **Live the Values** — Model what matters
3. **Create Rituals** — Build regular practices
4. **Document** — Write it down
5. **Celebrate** — Mark the moments

---

## Key Questions

- What do we stand for as a family?
- What traditions define us?
- What stories do we tell?
- What will the next generation remember?
