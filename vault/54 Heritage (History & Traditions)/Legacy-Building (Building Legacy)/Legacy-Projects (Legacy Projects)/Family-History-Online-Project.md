# Delacruz Family History — Online Archive Project

> *Every family has a story. The question is whether it gets written down.*

---

## The Goal

Build a permanent, accessible digital archive of Delacruz / Guzman family history — so future generations know where they came from, what was built, and why.

---

## Platform Options

| Platform | Best For | Cost | Notes |
|----------|----------|------|-------|
| **Obsidian Publish** | Codex-native, markdown, already in your workflow | $8/mo | Best fit |
| **Notion (public page)** | Living doc, easy to share | Free | Password-protect sensitive content |
| **FamilySearch** | Genealogy tree + DNA context | Free | LDS-backed, permanent |
| **Ancestry.com** | DNA results + family tree builder | $25/mo | Best for lineage depth |
| **Forever.com** | Long-term digital media archive | Paid | 100-year storage guarantee |
| **GitHub Pages** | Full custom control | Free | Good for technical builders |

**Recommended path**: Obsidian Publish for the living family codex + FamilySearch for the genealogy tree.

---

## Content to Build

### Tier 1 — Foundation (do first)
- [ ] Dominican heritage overview *(exists — see `Heritage-Roots/Dominican Heritage.md`)*
- [ ] Delacruz / Guzman family tree (start with what you know)
- [ ] Family timeline — key birth dates, migrations, milestones
- [ ] The Family Charter *(exists — see `Legacy-Building/Legacy-Vision/family-lineage-charter.md`)* — publish a clean version

### Tier 2 — Stories to Write
- [ ] How the Delacruz family came to the United States
- [ ] Founding of The Credit Executive — Anthony Jeremiah Delacruz
- [ ] Origin of One Mind OS and Legacy AI — the decision to build sovereign infrastructure
- [ ] Birth story of Soluna
- [ ] Birth story of Cielo
- [ ] The homestead vision — where it came from, what it will be

### Tier 3 — Living Archive (ongoing)
- [ ] Annual family recap (start 2026 — document this year)
- [ ] Photo archive with captions and context
- [ ] Audio or video recordings of family elders **(priority — do before it's too late)**
- [ ] DNA results linked to Dominican heritage context

---

## Action Steps

1. **Choose platform** — Obsidian Publish or FamilySearch + Obsidian combo
2. **Start the family tree** — FamilySearch is free; start with names and dates you know
3. **Write one story this month** — pick the easiest (founding of The Credit Executive or the homestead vision)
4. **Set an annual ritual** — every January, add the previous year's recap to the archive
