# DNA & Family Tree Project

> *The Delacruz / Guzman lineage is documented forward by this Codex. This project documents it backward.*

---

## Why This Matters

The Dominican Republic was built from three bloodlines — Taíno, Spanish, African. Every Dominican family carries that mix in different ratios. DNA testing makes the abstract real: it tells you exactly *which* roots, *how much*, and sometimes *where specifically* in West Africa or Spain or the Caribbean your people came from.

For the kids — for Soluna and Cielo — this is identity fuel. It's one thing to say "we're Dominican." It's another to show them the map, the percentages, the migration routes. That's something they can hold.

---

## DNA Kit Comparison

| Service | Best For | Cost | Notes |
|---------|----------|------|-------|
| **AncestryDNA** | Largest genealogy database, best for family tree matching | ~$99 | Best cousin-matching network |
| **23andMe** | Health reports + ancestry | ~$99–$229 | Detailed health variants if wanted |
| **MyHeritage DNA** | European + Caribbean coverage | ~$79 | Strong DR community matches |
| **FamilyTreeDNA** | Paternal (Y-DNA) + maternal (mtDNA) lines | ~$79–$179 | Best for deep lineage tracking |

**Recommended**: Start with **AncestryDNA** (biggest matching network). Add **FamilyTreeDNA** later for Y-DNA (the Delacruz paternal line specifically).
