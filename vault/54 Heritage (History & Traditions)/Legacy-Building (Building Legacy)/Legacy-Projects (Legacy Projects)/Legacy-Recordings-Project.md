# Legacy Recordings Project

> *Write down what you can. Record what you can't. The voice of someone you love is irreplaceable.*

---

## Why This Is Urgent

Documents preserve facts. Recordings preserve people.

The things that will matter most in 30 years are not the dates and names in the family tree — they are *how your grandmother laughed*, *what your grandfather's voice sounded like when he gave advice*, *the way your father explained why he built what he built*.

These recordings cannot be created after people are gone. **This project has a deadline that is not announced.**

## Interview Question Guide

Use these for elder/family interviews. Don't read them mechanically — let the conversation breathe.

**Origin & Identity**
- Where were you born? What was that place like?
- How did your family end up in the United States?
- What do you remember about the Dominican Republic?

**Family Memory**
- What is your favorite memory of your own parents or grandparents?
- What do you wish you had asked them while they were alive?
- What stories do you want to make sure get told?

**Values & Lessons**
- What is the most important thing life has taught you?
- What do you want your grandchildren to know about you?
- What do you wish someone had told you when you were young?

**The Future**
- What do you hope for the family — for Soluna, Cielo, and the children to come?
- What should we never forget?

## Equipment & Setup

**Minimum viable setup (start now):**
- iPhone, good lighting (face a window), external Rode Wireless microphone (~$99)
- Record in a quiet room, no echo

**Preferred setup:**
- Sony ZV-1 or similar camera ($400–600)
- Rode VideoMicro or DJI Mic ($80–150)
- Ring light or natural window light
- 2TB external drive for raw footage

**Storage plan:**
- Primary: External drive + cloud backup (Google Drive or iCloud)
- Archive copy: `Forever.com` (100-year storage guarantee) for the most important recordings
- Codex link: Metadata + transcript notes stored here in Obsidian
