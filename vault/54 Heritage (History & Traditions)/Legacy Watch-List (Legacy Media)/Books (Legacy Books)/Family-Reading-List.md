# Delacruz Family Reading List

> *Show your children what a reader looks like. They will become one.*

---

## How This Works

The documentary and movie watch-list shows your kids what to see. This list shows them what to read. Books are organized by:
- **Who they're for** — Zeus, the kids now, the kids when older
- **What they teach** — the values in book form

Add books as you discover them. Mark them read when done. Assign them to children when the time is right.

---

## For Zeus — The Patriarch's Shelf

Books that support the mission: sovereignty, dynasty-building, systems, leadership.

| Title | Author | Why It's Here | Status |
|-------|--------|--------------|--------|
| *Rich Dad Poor Dad* | Robert Kiyosaki | Financial mindset foundation — assets vs. liabilities. Essential for kids to read at 13+ | Add |
| *The Millionaire Next Door* | Stanley & Danko | Wealth building through boring discipline — counters the flashy consumption trap | Add |
| *Atomic Habits* | James Clear | Systems over goals — the operating system for building any tradition or behavior | Add |
| *The 48 Laws of Power* | Robert Greene | Strategic thinking, power dynamics — know the rules before you break them | Add |
| *The Way of the Superior Man* | David Deida | Masculine purpose and presence — relevant to the patriarch role | Add |
| *Mastery* | Robert Greene | The long path to world-class at anything. Build this into how you train the kids. | Add |
| *Deep Work* | Cal Newport | Protect the 5:30–9 AM block. This book explains why. | Add |

## How to Add a Book

When you find a book worth adding:
1. Add it to the right section
2. Note why it fits the family — what does it teach, who is it for
3. When read, mark status "Read — [Date]"
4. When you give it to a child, note it in their profile in `Family-Culture/Children/`
