## Synopsis
Years after a virus destroys humanity, Dr. Robert Neville appears to be the last immune man in NYC. By day he hunts for a cure. By night he fights to survive against mutated Darkseekers.

## Why Watch
Will Smith at his absolute best. The Sam the dog scenes are among cinema's most emotionally devastating. Tense, atmospheric, and gut-wrenching.

## Notes
- ⭐ Watch the **Alternate Ending** — completely changes the meaning of the title 'I Am Legend'
- Based on Richard Matheson's 1954 novel (influenced Resident Evil, 28 Days Later, The Walking Dead)
