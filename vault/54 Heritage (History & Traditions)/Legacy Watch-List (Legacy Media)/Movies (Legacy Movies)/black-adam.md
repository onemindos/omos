## Synopsis
Nearly 5,000 years after being given the powers of Egyptian gods and imprisoned, Black Adam is freed and must decide between hero and villain in the modern world.

## Why Watch
Dwayne Johnson embodying a role he's been working toward for years. Fast, fun, action-packed. The Dr. Fate subplot adds surprising emotional depth.

## Notes
- Contains a major cameo that rocked DC fans
- Watch the mid-credits scene
