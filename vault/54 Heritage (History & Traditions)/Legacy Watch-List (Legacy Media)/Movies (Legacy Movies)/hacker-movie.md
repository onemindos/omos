## Synopsis
Shy Benjamin is recruited into hacker collective CLAY (Clowns Laughing At You). What starts as pranks escalates into targeting Europol, making them the world's most wanted. But who is really who?

## Why Watch
More realistic social engineering than Mr. Robot or Hackers. One of the greatest twist endings in thriller history. A must for tech/security enthusiasts.
