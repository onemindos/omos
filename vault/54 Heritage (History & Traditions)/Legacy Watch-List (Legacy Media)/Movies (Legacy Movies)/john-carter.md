## Synopsis
Confederate Army Captain John Carter is teleported to Mars (Barsoom). Lower gravity gives him superhuman strength and leaping ability. He's thrust into a civil war between alien nations.

## Why Watch
Criminally underrated epic. The source novels (1912) literally inspired Star Wars, Avatar, Flash Gordon, and virtually every modern sci-fi franchise. Disney's biggest flop but a genuinely great adventure film.

## Notes
- Edgar Rice Burroughs' 1912 novels pre-dated and inspired: Star Wars, Avatar, Flash Gordon, Buck Rogers, He-Man
- Disney lost ~$200M on release but it has a passionate cult following
