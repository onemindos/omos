## Synopsis
US Army Sergeant Kinley is saved by Afghan interpreter Ahmed, who carries him miles through enemy territory. Months later, back home, Kinley learns Ahmed was left behind in Taliban Afghanistan — and makes it his mission to bring him out.

## Why Watch
Guy Ritchie's most emotionally powerful film. The bond between Gyllenhaal and Dar Salim is exceptional. A tribute to real interpreters abandoned after US withdrawal.

## Notes
- Dar Salim (Ahmed) gives an Oscar-caliber performance — the standout of the film
- Inspired by the real abandonment of Afghan interpreters post-US withdrawal
