## Synopsis
Dr. Bill Harford becomes obsessed with jealousy after his wife admits she fantasized about an affair. An all-night odyssey into New York's secret world, including an eerie elite masked society.

## Why Watch
Kubrick's masterpiece finale. Hypnotic and unsettling. One of cinema's most discussed explorations of marriage, desire, and the subconscious. A film you must discuss together after.

## Notes
- Based on Arthur Schnitzler's 1926 novella 'Traumnovelle'
- Infamous orgy scene was digitally altered for US theatrical release
