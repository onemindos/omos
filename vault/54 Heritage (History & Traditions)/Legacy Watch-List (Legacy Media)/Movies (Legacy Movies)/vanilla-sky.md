## Synopsis
Publishing magnate David Aames has a perfect life, meets the woman of his dreams, and has it all shattered when a jealous lover drives them off a bridge. Reality begins to blur in disturbing ways.

## Why Watch
Visually stunning mind-bender. The third act recontextualizes everything. Penélope Cruz is magnetic. A must-discuss-afterward film.

## Notes
- American remake of 'Abre los Ojos' (Open Your Eyes, Spain 1997)
- Penélope Cruz played the same character in both the original AND this remake
- Title references Claude Monet's sky paintings — impossibly perfect
