## Synopsis
An art thief breaks into a luxury Manhattan penthouse to steal priceless paintings. The heist goes wrong and he's trapped — weeks pass, sanity frays.

## Why Watch
Nearly a one-man show by Willem Dafoe. Visually gorgeous, meditative, strange. A film about survival, madness, and the irony of being surrounded by priceless art while starving.
