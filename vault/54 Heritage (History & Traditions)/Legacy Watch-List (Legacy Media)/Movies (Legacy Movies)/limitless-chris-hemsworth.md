## Synopsis
Thor submits to 6 radical experiments probing the outer limits of human endurance and health — stress, memory, strength, cold shock, fasting, and courage — with leading scientists.

## Why Watch
One of the best health documentaries made. Spectacular production, genuine science. Hemsworth's discovery of his APOE4 Alzheimer's gene risk mid-filming adds unexpected depth.

## Notes
- Chris discovered he carries 2 copies of APOE4 gene (elevated Alzheimer's risk)
- Each episode standalone — watch in any order
- Also highly relevant to HP domain
