## Synopsis
Boba Fett and Fennec Shand claim Jabba's old crime empire on Tatooine while flashbacks reveal his post-Sarlacc survival and time with Tusken Raiders.

## Why Watch
Essential Star Wars. Watch after Mandalorian S1 & S2. Episodes 5 & 6 (Din Djarin crossover) are some of the best Star Wars TV ever made.

## Notes
- ⭐ Episodes 5 & 6 are effectively Mandalorian S2.5 — DO NOT skip them
