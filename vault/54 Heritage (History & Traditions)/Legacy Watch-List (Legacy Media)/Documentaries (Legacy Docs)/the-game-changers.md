## Synopsis
UFC fighter James Wilks investigates the link between meat and optimal performance — and discovers the world's strongest, fastest, most enduring athletes are powered by plants.

## Why Watch
The most persuasive athletic performance argument for plant-based nutrition. The celebrity athlete roster (Arnold, Hamilton, Djokovic, Paul) gives it instant credibility. Changed how thousands of athletes eat.
