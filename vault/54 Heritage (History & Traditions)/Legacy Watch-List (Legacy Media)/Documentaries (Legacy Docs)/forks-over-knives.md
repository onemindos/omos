## Synopsis
Two pioneering researchers — Cornell's T. Colin Campbell and Dr. Caldwell Esselstyn — independently discovered that most degenerative diseases can be controlled and even reversed by adopting a whole-food plant-based diet.

## Why Watch
The research behind the China Study. Even if you don't go plant-based, the mechanistic science is compelling. One of the most cited nutritional documentaries ever made.

## Notes
- Based on T. Colin Campbell's 'The China Study' — the most comprehensive nutritional study ever conducted
- Dr. Esselstyn reversed Bill Clinton's heart disease with diet alone
- Watch before 'What the Health' for the foundational research first
