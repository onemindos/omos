## Synopsis
Canadian crypto exchange QuadrigaCX's CEO Gerald Cotten dies suddenly in India with $250M of customers' funds seemingly locked in cold wallets he alone could access. Was it a scam? Is he even dead?

## Why Watch
One of the most gripping cryptocurrency true-crime stories ever told. Blends financial education with a genuine mystery thriller. Essential viewing before any crypto investment.

## Notes
- The Quadriga CX scandal took $250M from 115,000+ users
- Similar fraud to FTX (Sam Bankman-Fried) in structure
- Ending is deliberately unresolved — you decide
