## Synopsis
Undercover investigators go deep into the illegal ivory trade — from elephant poachers in Africa to black market ivory dealers in China. A race-against-time wildlife crime exposé.

## Why Watch
Visually stunning and heartbreaking. Reveals the full chain of elephant killing for profit. Leonardo DiCaprio executive produced it to maximize impact. One of Netflix's best wildlife docs.

## Notes
- Executive produced by Leonardo DiCaprio and Alix Paultre
- Sparked policy changes in China's ivory ban (2017)
- Follow up: 'Kifaru' (2020) for a softer conservation story
