## Synopsis
Each episode investigates a different massive financial scandal: Volkswagen's diesel fraud, HSBC money laundering, Scott Tucker's predatory payday loans, Jared Kushner's housing empire, and more.

## Why Watch
Best financial crime documentary series ever made. Alex Gibney's team dissects exactly how powerful people steal billions while facing minimal consequences. Eye-opening and infuriating.

## Notes
- S1 Ep1 (VW emissions scandal) is the perfect entry point
- S2 Ep4 (Sackler family/OxyContin) is devastating
