## Synopsis
Former Google, Twitter, Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest employees blow the whistle on the deliberate, addictive design of social media platforms and the manipulation of human psychology for profit.

## Why Watch
Required viewing for anyone using social media. The whistleblowers built these systems and are terrified of what they created. Directly relevant to SO domain digital sovereignty.

## Notes
- Tristan Harris coined the term 'humane technology'
- Center for Humane Technology (humanetech.com) grew from this film
- Pairs perfectly with Shoshana Zuboff's 'The Age of Surveillance Capitalism'
