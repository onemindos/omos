## Synopsis
Filmmaker Kip Andersen investigates why major environmental organizations avoid discussing animal agriculture — despite it generating more greenhouse gases than all transportation combined.

## Why Watch
One of the most impactful environmental documentaries ever. Forces you to reconsider the relationship between diet, environment, and climate. Leonardo DiCaprio backed the Netflix version.

## Notes
- Kip Andersen also made 'Seaspiracy' and 'What the Health'
- Animal agriculture: 18% of greenhouse gases vs. transport: 13%
- Major environmental orgs initially refused to respond on camera
