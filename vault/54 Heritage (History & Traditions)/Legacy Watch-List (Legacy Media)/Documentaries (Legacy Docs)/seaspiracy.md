## Synopsis
Filmmaker Ali Tabrizi sets out to document ocean plastic — and discovers the fishing industry is the #1 cause of ocean destruction. Investigates sea slavery, dolphin slaughter, and the 'sustainable seafood' fraud.

## Why Watch
Infuriating and eye-opening. Follows the same formula as Cowspiracy but for oceans. The 'sustainable seafood' label investigation is its most damning segment.

## Notes
- Made by same team as Cowspiracy and What the Health
- Critics questioned some statistics; core thesis remains impactful
- Plastic straws: 0.03% of ocean plastic; fishing nets: 46%
