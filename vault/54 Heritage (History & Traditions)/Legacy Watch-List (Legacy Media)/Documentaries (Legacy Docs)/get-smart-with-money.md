## Synopsis
Four real people with different financial struggles (debt, spending, no savings, irregular income) are paired with financial coaches. Practical, actionable, and unpretentious.

## Why Watch
Highly watchable financial literacy film. Gets past abstract advice to actual implementation. The irregular-income gig economy case study is particularly relevant for entrepreneurs.

## Notes
- More practical than 'The Minimalists' — focused on doing, not philosophy
- Paula Pant ('Afford Anything') and Tiffany Aliche ('The Budgetnista') are exceptional coaches
- Watch after 'Dirty Money' for the contrast between system-scale theft and individual financial mastery
