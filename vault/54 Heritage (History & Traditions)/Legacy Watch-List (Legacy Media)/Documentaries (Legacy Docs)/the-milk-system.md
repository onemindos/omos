## Synopsis
A deep investigation into the global dairy industry — from European milk mountains to African market dumping to the relationship between Big Dairy, government subsidies, and public health recommendations.

## Why Watch
Exposes how dairy marketing and government subsidies shape what we're told is healthy. Connects health, economics, and geopolitics in ways that reshape how you think about food systems.

## Notes
- Rare documentary examining dairy specifically (most focus on meat)
- European perspective — different policy angle than US docs
- May be harder to find — check Kanopy (free with library card)
