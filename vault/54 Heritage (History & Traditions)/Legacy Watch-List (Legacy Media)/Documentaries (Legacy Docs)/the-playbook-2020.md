## Synopsis
Five legendary coaches from football, basketball, soccer, and swimming reveal their personal rules for life — their playbooks. Each episode dives deep into failure, discipline, and what it really takes to lead.

## Why Watch
Elite coaching wisdom that applies directly to life and business. Dawn Staley episode (women's basketball) is particularly powerful. Think of it as a leadership masterclass.

## Notes
- Each episode is self-contained — any order works
- Sports managers without athletic background episode is gold for leadership outside sport
