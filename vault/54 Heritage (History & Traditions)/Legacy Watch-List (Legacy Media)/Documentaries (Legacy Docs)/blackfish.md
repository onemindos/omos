## Synopsis
The story of Tilikum, a performing killer whale at SeaWorld who killed three people including trainer Dawn Brancheau. Former SeaWorld trainers and researchers expose the psychological damage of orca captivity.

## Why Watch
One of the most impactful documentaries ever made. Directly caused substantial decline in SeaWorld's attendance and led to California legislation banning orca captivity. Impossible to unsee.

## Notes
- Led to SeaWorld ending theatrical orca performances by 2019
- California Orca Protection Act passed after film's release
- Dawn Brancheau's death is handled with extraordinary care and respect
- The film Tilikum died in 2017 after years of deteriorating health
