## Synopsis
Filmmaker Kip Andersen investigates why leading health organizations fund animal agriculture while recommending products linked to cancer, diabetes, and heart disease — and follows people reversing chronic illness through diet.

## Why Watch
The most controversial of the Kip Andersen trilogy but also the most personal. The case studies of people reversing diabetes and heart disease through whole food plant-based diet in 2-3 weeks are remarkable.

## Notes
- Part of the Kip Andersen trilogy: Cowspiracy → What the Health → Seaspiracy
- Most contentious — rebutted by nutrition scientists for cherry-picking data
- Core claim (diet reverses chronic disease) supported by peer review even if specific stats disputed
- Watch with critical thinking intact
