## Synopsis
Joshua and Ryan explore how minimalism — choosing to own less — can create more freedom, more space, and more meaningful life. Covers consumerism, debt culture, and the psychology of stuff.

## Why Watch
Companion to their first film 'Minimalism: A Documentary' (2015). Perfect for examining what you own vs. what owns you. Directly relevant to HP + GE philosophy.

## Notes
- Matt D'Avella also directed 'The Social Dilemma'
- The Minimalists have a podcast + multiple books
- Their Netflix special 'The Minimalists: Less Is Now' (2021) is the sequel/update
