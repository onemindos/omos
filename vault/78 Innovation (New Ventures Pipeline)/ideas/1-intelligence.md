# 1 Intelligence

## Concept
One source of intelligence. Not fragmented across tools, apps, agents, and notes — unified into a single coherent operating system. The "1" is the unification, not the limitation.

## Name Angles
- **1 Intelligence** — singular, unified, the one source of truth for your mind
- **One Intelligence** — human + AI merged into a single operating unit
- **The Number 1** — first, best, primary — the intelligence layer everything else runs on
- Echoes OneMind OS: "One Mind" = "1 Intelligence" stated differently

## Distinction from Related Concepts

| Concept | What It Is |
|---------|-----------|
| **1 Intelligence** | The unified source — the single operating intelligence |
| **Supermind** | The system that produces amplified cognition |
| **Super Intelligence** | The capability level a person achieves |
| **OM Cortex** | The routing/decision layer inside the system |

## OneMind OS Connection
Could be the brand name for the OS itself — cleaner, more direct than "OneMind":
- "OneMind OS" → built on 1 Intelligence
- Or: 1 Intelligence IS OneMind OS, just named for the market
