# Super Intelligence

## Concept
Intelligence that surpasses the best human minds across all domains. As a brand/product concept — not the AGI definition, but the aspiration: a person operating at a level that feels superhuman because of how they've integrated AI.

## Two Readings

| Reading | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Technical (AGI)** | An AI system that exceeds human intelligence across all tasks — Bostrom's definition, the existential risk framing |
| **Personal / Brand** | A human who has achieved super-level intelligence through systems, AI, and compounding — the OneMind OS end state |

## OneMind OS Angle
Not about building AGI. About becoming:
- A person whose decision-making, recall, synthesis, and execution runs at a level others can't match
- The product of Human + AI done right
- Where Supermind is the system, Super Intelligence is the individual capability level achieved
