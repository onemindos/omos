# Real Time Story

## Concept
An in-person community event where students collaboratively build a story in real time. The session is recorded, then AI tools transform the audio + student likenesses into a produced video — with each student as an actual character in the story they created together.

## The Experience

### Live Session
- Students gather in person
- Together they build a story in real time — characters, plot, climax, resolution
- Session is recorded (audio primary)

### Story Structure Required
- **Characters** — one per student or assigned roles
- **Setting** — where the story takes place
- **Plot** — the sequence of events
- **Conflict** — the central tension
- **Climax** — the peak moment
- **Resolution** — how it ends
- **Theme** — the underlying message (optional, adds depth)

### Post-Session Production Pipeline
1. **Audio capture** — clean recording of the live session
2. **Face sourcing** — pull student photos from social media (with consent)
3. **Video generation** — use video AI models to animate the story with student faces as characters
4. **Delivery** — students receive a produced video of the story THEY made, starring THEM

## Key Differentiator
Students don't just hear a story — they ARE the story. Their face, their voice, their narrative. Deeply personal artifact they keep.
