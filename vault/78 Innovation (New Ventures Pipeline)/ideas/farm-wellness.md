# Farm Wellness

## Definition
Wellness experiences hosted on working or lifestyle farms — combining nature immersion, agricultural rhythms, and health-focused programming. Guests come not just to stay but to heal, reset, and reconnect through farm-anchored activities.

## What It Looks Like
| Element | Examples |
|---------|---------|
| Setting | Working farm, homestead, permaculture land |
| Programs | Yoga, meditation, breathwork, cold plunge, sound healing |
| Agriculture tie-in | Harvesting, animal care, soil therapy, foraging |
| Food | Farm-to-table, fasting, functional nutrition |
| Stay format | Glamping, cabins, yurts, converted barns |

## Market Position
Sits at the intersection of agritourism + the $5.6T global wellness economy. Differentiates from standard wellness retreats by grounding the experience in real land, real food, and real work.

## OneMind OS Angle
Potential venture tied to Zeus's land/property roadmap. Could layer onto greenhouse infrastructure, robotics (FarmBot), and the farm hotel concept. Wellness programming = recurring revenue on top of a productive land asset.
