# Supermind

## Concept
A mind that operates beyond individual human capacity — through AI augmentation, collective intelligence, or systemic amplification. Not just a smarter person; a fundamentally different class of cognition.

## Name Angles
- **Super + Mind** — elevated cognition, beyond baseline human
- **Collective Supermind** — group intelligence that exceeds the sum of its parts
- **OneMind OS angle** — the end state of the Human + AI equation: Zeus + Legacy = Supermind

## Tech / Research Context
- Thomas Malone (MIT) coined "Supermind" for collective intelligence systems (humans + AI acting as one)
- Distinct from superintelligence — Supermind is augmented, not replaced
- The human is still the center; the system amplifies

## OneMind OS Connection
Could be the aspirational brand name for what OneMind OS produces:
- OneMind OS is the system
- Supermind is the outcome
- "You don't just use AI — you become a Supermind"
