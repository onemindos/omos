# Family-Sovereignty — Family Independence

> *"A resilient family is an independent family."*

---

## Purpose

Family-Sovereignty is the **family resilience center**. This area covers self-reliance, preparedness, independence, and building a family that can thrive regardless of external circumstances.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Self-Reliance** — Skills, capabilities, independence
- **Preparedness** — Emergency planning, supplies, contingencies
- **Food Security** — Growing, storing, sourcing
- **Energy Independence** — Alternative power, efficiency
- **Privacy** — Digital, physical, information security

---

## Sovereignty Domains

| Domain | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| Food | Growing, storing, sourcing independently |
| Water | Clean water access, storage |
| Energy | Alternative sources, efficiency |

| Health | First aid, basic medical |
| Financial | Multiple income streams, reserves |

## Preparedness Tiers

| Tier | Scenario | Prep |
|------|----------|------|
| 1 | 72 hours | Bug-out bag, basic supplies |
| 2 | 2 weeks | Extended supplies, backup power |
| 3 | 3 months | Significant reserves, skills |
| 4 | Long-term | Full self-reliance capability |

---

## Key Principles

1. Reduce single points of failure
2. Build redundancy in critical systems
3. Develop practical skills
4. Maintain reserves (cash, supplies)
5. Know your neighbors
