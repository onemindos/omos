# Sleep Environment Checklist

> *Optimize your bedroom for maximum sleep quality and recovery.*

---

## Overview

Your sleep environment is one of the most controllable factors affecting sleep quality. This checklist provides a systematic approach to creating the ideal sleep sanctuary.

**Expected Impact:**
- Faster sleep onset (10-15 min reduction)
- Fewer nighttime awakenings
- Better deep sleep percentage (+5-10%)
- Improved sleep score (+10-15 points)
- Enhanced morning alertness

---

## Temperature Optimization

### Bedroom Temperature: 65-68°F (18-20°C)

- [ ] **Room temperature control**
  - Thermostat set to 65-68°F
  - Or personal preference within 60-70°F range
  - Some people sleep better at 60-65°F

- [ ] **Active cooling system**
  - AC or fan for air circulation
  - Window open (if outside temp appropriate)
  - Ceiling fan on low

- [ ] **Cooling mattress technology** (optional but powerful)
  - [ ] **Eight Sleep Pod:** Temperature-controlled mattress cover
    - Can program temp changes throughout night
    - Wake with gradual warming
    - Cost: ~$2,000-3,000
  - [ ] **ChiliPad / Ooler:** Water-based cooling pad
    - Adjustable temp (55-115°F)
    - Cost: ~$500-1,000
  - [ ] **BedJet:** Climate control for bed
    - Air-based cooling/heating
    - Cost: ~$500-700

- [ ] **Cooling pillows**
  - Gel-infused memory foam
  - Water-based cooling pillows
  - Phase-change material (PCM) pillows
  - Bamboo or cooling covers

### Personal Temperature Regulation

- [ ] **Keep extremities warm** (paradoxically helps cooling)
  - Wear socks if feet are cold
  - Cold hands/feet = blood vessel constriction = harder to cool core

- [ ] **Breathable sleepwear**
  - Natural fibers (cotton, bamboo, linen)
  - Moisture-wicking athletic wear
  - Or minimal/no clothing

- [ ] **Breathable bedding**
  - Cotton sheets (avoid polyester)
  - Bamboo sheets (naturally cooling)
  - Linen (best for hot sleepers)
  - Lightweight blanket or duvet
  - Remove heavy comforters

### Pre-Sleep Temperature Protocol

- [ ] **Warm bath/shower 90-120 min before bed**
  - Temperature: 104-109°F (40-43°C)
  - Duration: 10-15 minutes
  - Effect: Body temp drops after, triggering sleepiness

- [ ] **Cool bedroom in advance**
  - Start cooling 60-90 min before bed
  - Pre-cool to target temp or slightly below

## Darkness & Light Control

### Complete Darkness (Critical for Melatonin Production)

- [ ] **Blackout curtains or shades**
  - Block 100% of outside light
  - No light leaks around edges
  - Double-layer curtains if needed
  - Cost: $30-200 depending on quality

- [ ] **Sleep mask** (backup or travel)
  - Contoured to avoid eye pressure
  - Adjustable, comfortable strap
  - Blocks 100% of light
  - Recommended: Manta Sleep Mask, Sleep Master
  - Cost: $10-35

- [ ] **Remove or cover light sources**
  - [ ] Alarm clock (dim or turn display off)
  - [ ] Power strips with LED lights (cover with tape)
  - [ ] Smoke detectors (if blinking LED)
  - [ ] Phone notification lights
  - [ ] TV standby lights
  - [ ] Cable box/modem lights in bedroom

- [ ] **No electronics with lights**
  - Remove or unplug devices with LEDs
  - Use blackout tape for unavoidable lights
  - Charge devices outside bedroom

- [ ] **Door light seal**
  - Light under door from hallway
  - Door sweep or towel to block

### Morning Light Exposure

- [ ] **Allow morning light in** (after wake time)
  - Set curtains to automatically open (if smart curtains)
  - Or manually open immediately upon waking
  - 10-30 min of bright light essential for circadian rhythm

- [ ] **Light therapy box** (if no natural light)
  - 10,000 lux
  - Use 20-30 min upon waking
  - Especially important in winter
  - Cost: $30-200

## Sound & Noise Management

### Quiet Environment

- [ ] **Identify noise sources**
  - Traffic noise
  - Neighbors
  - Household sounds (HVAC, appliances)
  - Partner snoring
  - Pets

- [ ] **Soundproofing** (if feasible)
  - Weather stripping on doors
  - Heavy curtains (also block sound)
  - Rugs on floor (reduce sound transmission)
  - Acoustic panels (for serious noise issues)

### White Noise & Sound Masking

- [ ] **White noise machine**
  - Consistent, non-varying sound
  - Masks disruptive noises
  - Recommended: LectroFan, Dohm
  - Cost: $30-70

- [ ] **Fan** (serves dual purpose: cooling + white noise)
  - Box fan or oscillating fan
  - Consistent hum masks sounds
  - Cost: $15-50

- [ ] **White noise apps** (if no machine)
  - myNoise, White Noise, Noisli
  - Play through phone or speaker
  - Set sleep timer

### Earplugs (When Needed)

- [ ] **High-quality earplugs**
  - Foam: Mack's, Flents (disposable)
  - Silicone: Moldable, reusable
  - Custom-molded: Best fit, expensive
  - Loop Quiet: Comfortable, reusable
  - NRR (Noise Reduction Rating): 30+ dB ideal

## Bed & Mattress Quality

### Mattress Assessment

- [ ] **Age of mattress:** ___ years
  - Replace every 7-10 years (lose support over time)
  - Sagging or visible wear = replace sooner

- [ ] **Mattress type suited to preference**
  - [ ] Memory foam (contouring, pressure relief)
  - [ ] Hybrid (coils + foam, balanced support)
  - [ ] Innerspring (traditional, firm support)
  - [ ] Latex (responsive, cooling)

- [ ] **Firmness appropriate for sleep position**
  - Side sleepers: Medium to medium-soft (pressure relief)
  - Back sleepers: Medium to medium-firm (support)
  - Stomach sleepers: Firm (prevent lower back sag)

- [ ] **No pain or discomfort**
  - Wake without back/neck pain
  - No pressure points (hips, shoulders)
  - If pain: Reassess mattress, pillow, or position

### Pillow Optimization

- [ ] **Pillow supports neck alignment**
  - Neck should be neutral (not angled up/down)
  - Head supported without tilting

- [ ] **Pillow suited to sleep position**
  - Side sleepers: Thick, firm pillow (fill gap neck to shoulder)
  - Back sleepers: Medium thickness
  - Stomach sleepers: Thin or no pillow

- [ ] **Pillow freshness**
  - Replace every 1-2 years
  - Washable covers or cases
  - No lumps or flat spots

- [ ] **Body pillow** (optional, for side sleepers)
  - Support between knees
  - Reduces hip/lower back strain
  - Can hug for comfort

### Bedding Quality

- [ ] **Sheets are clean, comfortable, and breathable**
  - Washed weekly (minimum every 2 weeks)
  - Natural fibers (cotton, bamboo, linen)
  - Thread count: 300-500 (higher ≠ always better)
  - Avoid polyester (traps heat, less breathable)

- [ ] **Blanket/duvet appropriate for season**
  - Lightweight for warm months
  - Heavier for cold months (but not too heavy = overheating)
  - Down alternative (if allergies)

## Air Quality

### Ventilation

- [ ] **Fresh air circulation**
  - Crack window (if outside air quality good)
  - Fan for air movement
  - Avoid stagnant, stuffy air

- [ ] **HVAC filter changed regularly**
  - Every 1-3 months
  - High-quality filter (MERV 11-13)

### Air Purifier (Optional but Beneficial)

- [ ] **HEPA air purifier** (especially if allergies or poor air quality)
  - Removes dust, pollen, allergens
  - Can improve breathing during sleep
  - Run on low (quiet) setting
  - Recommended: Coway, Levoit, Dyson
  - Cost: $100-500

### Humidity Control

- [ ] **Humidity level: 30-50%** (optimal)
  - Too dry: Irritated sinuses, dry throat
  - Too humid: Mold, discomfort

- [ ] **Humidifier** (if too dry, especially winter)
  - Cool mist preferred
  - Clean regularly (prevent mold/bacteria)
  - Cost: $30-150

- [ ] **Dehumidifier** (if too humid)
  - Especially in basements or humid climates
  - Cost: $150-300

## Bedroom Setup & Organization

### Bed is for Sleep (& Intimacy) Only

- [ ] **No TV in bedroom**
  - Remove TV or move to other room
  - Or cover when not in use
  - Screen time interferes with sleep association

- [ ] **No work in bed**
  - No laptop, paperwork, or work activities
  - Bed = sleep signal only

- [ ] **No reading in bed** (if sleep association issue)
  - If reading helps you wind down: okay
  - If you read for hours in bed: read elsewhere, then get in bed

- [ ] **Phone charging outside bedroom** (ideal)
  - Removes temptation to check
  - No notifications disrupting sleep
  - Use traditional alarm clock instead

### Clutter & Environment

- [ ] **Minimal clutter**
  - Clean, organized space
  - Calm, peaceful environment
  - Not a storage room

- [ ] **Calming colors**
  - Neutral tones (white, beige, soft blues/greens)
  - Avoid stimulating colors (bright red, orange)

- [ ] **Plants** (optional, can improve air quality)
  - Snake plant, peace lily, pothos
  - Low light tolerant
  - Proven to filter air

### Aromatherapy (Optional)

- [ ] **Calming scents**
  - [ ] **Lavender:** Most studied, promotes relaxation
  - [ ] **Chamomile:** Gentle, soothing
  - [ ] **Valerian:** Sedative properties
  - [ ] **Bergamot:** Reduces anxiety

- [ ] **Delivery method**
  - Essential oil diffuser (run 30-60 min before bed)
  - Pillow spray
  - Sachets (dried lavender)
  - Avoid strong scents (can be stimulating)

## Electronics & EMF Reduction

### Remove Electronics from Bedroom

- [ ] **No phone in bedroom** (or airplane mode + do not disturb)
  - Charge in another room
  - Use traditional alarm clock
  - Reduces EMF exposure (debated, but can't hurt)

- [ ] **No TV, computer, or tablets in bedroom**
  - Remove or keep powered off/unplugged

- [ ] **Wi-Fi router not in bedroom**
  - Place in central area of home, not near bed

### Blue Light Elimination

- [ ] **No screens 1-2 hours before bed**
  - TV, phone, tablet, computer

- [ ] **If screens unavoidable:**
  - [ ] Blue light blocking glasses (amber/orange lenses)
  - [ ] Screen filter apps (f.lux, Night Shift)
  - [ ] Screen brightness < 30%
  - [ ] Dark mode / night mode on all devices

- [ ] **Bedroom lights: Red or amber bulbs** (2-3 hours before bed)
  - Minimal blue light spectrum
  - Doesn't suppress melatonin like white/blue light
  - Cost: $10-30 for bulbs

## Pre-Sleep Environment Preparation

### 60-90 Minutes Before Bed

- [ ] Start cooling bedroom (if needed)
- [ ] Dim lights throughout home
- [ ] Turn on white noise machine
- [ ] Close blackout curtains
- [ ] Set temperature to 65-68°F
- [ ] Remove clutter from bed (clothes, items)
- [ ] Set out comfortable sleepwear
- [ ] Prepare sleep environment so it's welcoming

## Maintenance Schedule

### Daily

- [ ] Make bed (clean space = better sleep association)
- [ ] Remove clutter from bedroom
- [ ] Open curtains in morning (light exposure)
- [ ] Close curtains in evening (darkness)

### Weekly

- [ ] Wash sheets and pillowcases
- [ ] Vacuum/clean bedroom
- [ ] Empty any water in humidifier, refill
- [ ] Check white noise machine battery (if applicable)

### Monthly

- [ ] Wash pillows (if washable)
- [ ] Dust bedroom, including behind bed
- [ ] Check air purifier filter (if applicable)
- [ ] Review and adjust any environment factors

### Annually

- [ ] Replace pillows (every 1-2 years)
- [ ] Deep clean mattress (vacuum, spot clean)
- [ ] Replace air filters (HVAC, purifier)
- [ ] Assess mattress condition (replace every 7-10 years)
- [ ] Audit sleep environment, make improvements
