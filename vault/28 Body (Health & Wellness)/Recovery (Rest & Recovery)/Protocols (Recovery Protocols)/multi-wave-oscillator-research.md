# Multi Wave Oscillator (MWO) — Research Overview

## What It Is

The Multi Wave Oscillator (MWO) is a broad-spectrum electromagnetic frequency generator originally invented by **Georges Lakhovsky** in the 1920s–1930s. It produces a wide range of radio frequencies simultaneously, based on the theory that living cells are microscopic oscillating circuits that resonate at specific frequencies — and that disease occurs when those natural frequencies are disrupted.

The MWO is designed to bathe the body in a rich field of harmonics so that each cell can "find" and lock onto its own healthy resonant frequency, restoring normal oscillation.

---

## History

### Georges Lakhovsky (1869–1942)

- Russian-born French engineer, biologist, and author
- Published *The Secret of Life* (1935) — the foundational text on cellular oscillation theory
- Proposed that the nucleus of every living cell acts as an electrical oscillating circuit (the "radiating cell" hypothesis)
- Built the first MWO in Paris in the late 1920s
- Conducted experiments at Hôpital de la Salpêtrière in Paris — used the MWO on geranium plants inoculated with cancer-causing bacteria; treated plants reportedly recovered while controls died
- Collaborated with **Nikola Tesla** — the MWO uses Tesla coil principles for high-voltage, high-frequency generation
- Fled to New York in 1941 during WWII; died in 1942 under unclear circumstances
- His work was largely suppressed or ignored by mainstream medicine after his death

### Key Lineage

| Figure | Contribution |

## Core Theory: Cellular Oscillation

Lakhovsky's hypothesis:

1. **Every living cell is a resonator** — the nucleus contains filaments (chromosomes) that act as oscillating circuits with specific capacitance and inductance
2. **Health = proper oscillation** — when cells vibrate at their natural frequency, the organism is healthy
3. **Disease = disrupted oscillation** — pathogens, toxins, or environmental stressors can shift cellular frequency, causing dysfunction
4. **External fields can restore oscillation** — if you expose cells to a broad spectrum of frequencies, each cell will resonate sympathetically with the frequency that matches its healthy state (selective resonance / entrainment)

The MWO doesn't target a single frequency (like a Rife machine). It generates a **simultaneous broadband spectrum** — the idea being that among thousands of harmonics, every cell will find its match.

## How the MWO Works

### Hardware Components

| Component | Function |
|-----------|----------|
| **Tesla coil** | Steps up voltage to very high levels (50–100 kV+), generates high-frequency oscillations |
| **Spark gap** | Creates pulsed discharge, generating rich harmonic content across a wide frequency range |
| **Two concentric ring antennas** | Typically copper or aluminum rings of varying diameters, acting as the transmitting and receiving elements |
| **Resonant antenna array** | Multiple nested rings (open at different points) — each ring resonates at a different frequency based on its diameter |

### Operating Principle

1. Tesla coil generates high-voltage, high-frequency pulses
2. Spark gap creates broadband electromagnetic noise rich in harmonics
3. The nested ring antennas radiate these frequencies into the surrounding space
4. The subject sits or stands between the two antenna arrays (one transmitter, one receiver)
5. The body is immersed in a field containing frequencies from low kHz to hundreds of MHz
6. Cells theoretically "pick up" their resonant frequency and re-establish healthy oscillation

### Frequency Range

- Original Lakhovsky MWO: estimated 750 kHz to 3 GHz (some sources claim wider)
- Modern reproductions vary widely depending on Tesla coil design, spark gap, and antenna geometry
- The key is **broadband** — the goal is maximum harmonic richness, not precision tuning

## Modern Research & Status

### Scientific Evidence

The MWO remains **outside mainstream medical acceptance**. Current status:

- **No peer-reviewed clinical trials** in major medical journals
- **Anecdotal reports** are extensive — users report improvements in pain, inflammation, energy, wound healing, and chronic conditions
- **Lakhovsky's plant experiments** are the most cited evidence — replicated informally by hobbyists but never in a controlled modern study
- **Priore's work in Bordeaux** (related but not identical technology) did produce peer-reviewed animal studies showing tumor regression, but funding was cut and work was never replicated
- **Bioelectromagnetic research** is a legitimate field — PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) therapy is FDA-cleared for bone healing and has growing evidence for pain management

### Modern Builders & Sources

- DIY community builds MWOs from Tesla coil kits — plans widely available online
- Commercial units range from $500 to $5,000+ (varying quality and authenticity)
- Key consideration: **no standardized design** — modern MWOs vary enormously in output power, frequency range, and safety features

## Safety Considerations

| Risk | Details |
|------|---------|
| **High voltage** | Tesla coils produce 50–100 kV+ — lethal if contacted directly. Proper insulation and spark gap design are critical. |
| **RF exposure** | Broadband RF at close range. No long-term exposure studies for MWO-specific configurations. |
| **Pacemakers** | Absolute contraindication — strong EM fields can interfere with cardiac devices |
| **Pregnancy** | Not studied — generally advised against |
| **Metal implants** | EM fields may cause heating in metal implants — use with caution |
| **Seizure disorders** | Theoretical risk from strong EM stimulation — no data either way |
| **Electrical safety** | DIY builds carry significant electrocution risk if not properly constructed |

**Bottom line:** The MWO is an experimental device. Treat it with the same respect as any high-voltage equipment. Never use as a replacement for medical treatment.

## Practical Considerations for Exploration

### If Evaluating an MWO

1. **Source quality matters** — commercial units from established builders with safety features vs. DIY from internet plans
2. **No medical claims** — any builder claiming to cure specific diseases is a red flag
3. **Start low, go slow** — short sessions (5–15 min), observe response
4. **Document everything** — if experimenting, track sessions, duration, subjective effects, any measurable biomarkers
5. **Complement, don't replace** — any frequency device should complement, not replace, conventional medical care
