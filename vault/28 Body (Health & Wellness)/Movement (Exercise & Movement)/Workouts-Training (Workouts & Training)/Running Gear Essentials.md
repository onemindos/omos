# Running Gear Essentials

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Performance, Fitness

## Essential Running Gear

### Footwear

| Item | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Running Shoes** | Get gait analyzed at a running store for proper support, cushioning, and fit |
| **Running Socks** | Moisture-wicking to keep feet dry and prevent blisters |

### Clothing

| Item | When to Use |
|------|-------------|
| **Moisture-Wicking Tops/Bottoms** | Always - keeps you dry and comfortable |
| **Running Tights or Shorts** | Based on weather/preference |
| **Running Jacket** | Cold/wet weather |
| **Gloves & Hat** | Cold weather |
| **Cap & Sunglasses** | Sunny weather |

### Safety Gear

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Reflective Gear** | Visibility for morning/evening runs |
| **Headlamp or Flashlight** | Low light conditions |

### Hydration & Nutrition

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Water Bottle / Hydration Pack** | Essential for longer runs |
| **Gels or Chews** | Nutrition for long runs |

### Technology

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Running Watch / Fitness Tracker** | Track distance, pace, heart rate |
| **Music Player & Headphones** | Motivation |

### Accessories

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Running Belt or Armband** | Carry keys, phone, nutrition |
| **Sunscreen** | Skin protection |

### Recovery

| Item | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Foam Roller** | Work out muscle knots and tightness |
| **Compression Gear** | Aid recovery after runs |

---

*Every runner is different - consider your own needs, preferences, and running conditions.*
