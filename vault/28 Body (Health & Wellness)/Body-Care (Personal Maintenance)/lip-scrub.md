# Lip Scrub

Two ingredients cover everything: something gritty to exfoliate, something moisturizing to hydrate.

---

## Best Recipe (Recommended)

| Ingredient | Amount | Role |
| ---------- | ------ | ---- |
| Sugar | 1 tsp | Exfoliates dead skin |
| Coconut oil | ½ tsp | Smooths, moisturizes |
| Olive oil | ½ tsp | Deep hydration |

**Steps:**
1. Mix into a paste
2. Warm damp cloth on lips for 30 seconds first (loosens dead skin — makes scrub work better)
3. Rub gently on lips for 20–30 seconds
4. Wipe with warm cloth
5. Apply lip balm

---

## Base Options

| Base | Best For |
| ---- | -------- |
| Coconut oil | Softer lips, smoother scrub — thick, melts into lips |
| Olive oil | Very dry or cracked lips — deeper hydration, more nutrients |
| Both mixed | Best overall — balanced exfoliation + hydration |
| Honey | Moisturizes + helps heal cracked lips |
| Vaseline | Backup if no oil available |

**Default:** coconut oil for most uses. Olive oil when lips are very dry or cracked.

---

## Optional Add-Ins

| Add-In | Effect |
| ------ | ------ |
| Coffee grounds | Boosts circulation |
| Cinnamon (tiny pinch) | May make lips look slightly plumper |
| Strawberry mash | Adds vitamin C and flavor |

---

## Quick Hack (No Ingredients)

Rub lips with a warm damp towel for gentle exfoliation → apply lip balm. Done.

---

## Rules

- Scrub **1–2x per week max** — lip skin is thin and delicate
- Always apply lip balm after scrubbing
- Warm cloth before scrubbing for better results
