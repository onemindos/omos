# Military Glossary

> Military terms, acronyms, and concepts — with One Mind applications.
> Used by Zeus and Legacy agents across all domains.

---

## A

**AUDACITY**
Bold, decisive action executed with confidence. Seizing initiative rather than waiting for perfect conditions.
*One Mind:* Bias toward action. Move before you're fully ready. The × compounds faster when you act.

**AAR — After Action Review**
A structured review conducted after an operation or event. Covers what was planned, what happened, why it differed, and what to do differently.
*One Mind:* The Review stage of CODE×. Every Sprint close-out, decision post-mortem, or project wrap.

**AO — Area of Operations**
The geographic or functional zone assigned to a unit for executing its mission.
*One Mind:* A domain (e.g., HP 28 Body is the AO for fitness ops).

**AOR — Area of Responsibility**
Broader than AO — the persistent zone an agent/commander owns and is accountable for.
*One Mind:* Each agent's assigned pillar. Empire's AOR = GE 75–99.

**Assets**
Resources available for mission execution — personnel, equipment, funds, intelligence.
*One Mind:* Tools, agents, money, skills, relationships available in a domain.

---

## B

**Battle Rhythm**
The recurring schedule of meetings, briefings, and reporting that keeps an operation synchronized.
*One Mind:* The review cadence — daily standups, weekly reviews, quarterly strategy sessions.

**BLUF — Bottom Line Up Front**
Communication format: state the conclusion first, then supporting detail.
*One Mind:* Default communication standard for Legacy reports and agent outputs.

**Branch**
A contingency plan built into an existing operation — triggered when conditions change during execution (as opposed to a Sequel, which comes after).
*One Mind:* The "if X then Y" logic embedded in a project plan. Built before execution, not improvised mid-op.

**Breach**
A forced entry through an obstacle or defended position.
*One Mind:* Breaking through a resistance point — financial, mental, or structural.

---

## C

**C2 — Command and Control**
The authority and capability to direct forces and coordinate operations.
*One Mind:* The SO pillar — the operating layer that commands and coordinates HP, LE, GE.

**C4ISR — Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance**
The integrated system enabling commanders to gather, process, and act on information.
*One Mind:* The full SO intelligence stack — agents, memory, tools, monitoring, channels.

**CAP — Combat Air Patrol / Course of Action Plan**
A sustained patrol or a planned sequence of actions to achieve an objective.
*One Mind:* A structured plan for addressing a domain challenge.

**CASEVAC — Casualty Evacuation**
Emergency extraction of casualties from the field.
*One Mind:* Crisis response protocol — removing yourself from a situation that's causing harm.

**COG — Center of Gravity**
The source of power, strength, or will that everything else depends on. Attacking or protecting the COG is the decisive action.
*One Mind:* The single most critical asset in a domain. In GE it's cash flow. In HP it's energy. Identify and protect yours; find and attack competitors'.

**COA — Course of Action**
A specific plan or option being considered for execution.
*One Mind:* One of multiple strategic paths being evaluated before committing.

**CONOPS — Concept of Operations**
A narrative document describing how an operation will unfold from start to finish.
*One Mind:* A project brief or campaign plan. The "how we're doing this" document.

**CONTINGENCY**
A planned response to a situation that may or may not occur.
*One Mind:* A backup plan or fallback protocol built into a project or system.

**CULMINATING POINT**
The moment when a force can no longer sustain its current form of attack or advance. Pushing past it leads to collapse.
*One Mind:* Burnout threshold. Recognizing when to consolidate gains instead of pressing forward. Knowing when to rest is a tactical decision.

**COP — Common Operating Picture**
A shared, real-time view of the battlefield that all commanders and units operate from.
*One Mind:* The shared dashboard or context that Zeus and all agents operate from — the Command domain (SO 03).

---

## D

**D-Day / H-Hour**
The designated day and hour for the start of an operation.
*One Mind:* Launch date and launch time for a campaign, project, or release.

**DEAD — Destruction of Enemy Air Defenses**
Eliminating threats that block your operational space.
*One Mind:* Removing blockers — clearing obstacles before executing a mission.

**DECISIVE POINT**
A specific condition, event, or location where a commander can act to achieve a significant advantage or accomplish the mission. Not the objective itself — the condition that *enables* the objective.
*One Mind:* The leverage point. The one action or decision that unlocks everything else in a domain. Find it before committing resources.

**DECISION POINT**
A moment in an operation where a commander must make a go/no-go or branch decision. Planned in advance.
*One Mind:* Pre-defined checkpoints in a project where Zeus decides whether to continue, branch, or abort. Built into every OPORD.

**DEFCON — Defense Readiness Condition**
A five-level alert system (DEFCON 5 = normal, DEFCON 1 = maximum readiness).
*One Mind:* System health/urgency levels. DEFCON 1 = full crisis mode.

**DEBRIEF**
A post-mission structured interview to extract lessons and intelligence.
*One Mind:* Post-project review, experience capture, memory write-back.

**DENY**
To prevent the enemy from using a resource, terrain, or capability.
*One Mind:* Cutting off a bad habit, distraction, or threat access to your time/attention.

---

## E

**ECONOMY OF FORCE**
Using the minimum necessary resources on secondary efforts to mass strength where it matters most. A core principle of war.
*One Mind:* Don't over-allocate agent time or Zeus energy to low-priority domains. Keep secondary domains on maintenance mode while the main effort gets 80% of resources.

**EMCON — Emission Control**
Restricting communications and electronic emissions to avoid detection.
*One Mind:* Strategic silence — going dark on social, comms, or signals when needed.

**ENDEX — End Exercise**
The formal end of a training exercise.
*One Mind:* Project close-out, sprint end, or campaign completion.

**ENDSTATE**
The desired conditions that must exist after an operation is complete.
*One Mind:* The vision — what success looks like when the mission is done.

**EXFIL — Exfiltration**
Covert extraction from a hostile or denied area.
*One Mind:* Graceful exit from a bad situation — leaving a market, relationship, or role.

---

## F

**FRAGO — Fragmentary Order**
A brief, partial order that modifies an existing OPORD. Issued when plans change mid-operation.
*One Mind:* A mid-sprint course correction or updated directive from Zeus.

**FRATRICIDE**
Accidental damage to friendly forces.
*One Mind:* Self-sabotage or friendly fire between your own systems/agents.

**FININT — Financial Intelligence**
Intelligence derived from analyzing financial data, transactions, and money flows to identify threats, opportunities, or adversary behavior.
*One Mind:* GE domain surveillance — tracking market signals, competitor funding, cash flow patterns, and financial red flags in investments or ventures.

**FLANK**
An attack from the side rather than the front, bypassing main defenses.
*One Mind:* An asymmetric approach — entering a market or solving a problem from an unexpected angle.

---

## G

**G2 — Intelligence Officer (S2 at lower levels)**
The staff officer responsible for intelligence.
*One Mind:* The Oracle agent or any agent running in intelligence/research mode.

**GRID**
A coordinate system for precise location on a map.
*One Mind:* Precise identification of where something is in the domain structure (e.g., HP 28.3).

**GROUNDWORK**
Preparatory actions that enable future operations.
*One Mind:* Infrastructure, systems, and foundation work before execution.

---

## H

**HUMINT — Human Intelligence**
Intelligence gathered from human sources.
*One Mind:* Insights from conversations, relationships, advisors, and real-world interactions.

**HOT WASH**
An immediate, informal debrief conducted right after an operation — faster and rawer than an AAR. Captures first impressions before they fade.
*One Mind:* The 5-minute capture right after a meeting, call, or event. What just happened, what worked, what didn't. Goes into inbox immediately.

---

## I

**INFIL — Infiltration**
Covert entry into a denied or hostile area.
*One Mind:* Entering a new market, space, or relationship without revealing full intent.

**IPB — Intelligence Preparation of the Battlefield**
Systematic analysis of the environment, threats, and opportunities before a mission.
*One Mind:* Research phase before a major decision or campaign launch.

**ISR — Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance**
Persistent collection and analysis of information about the operational environment.
*One Mind:* Ongoing monitoring — agent memory search, news feeds, metrics dashboards.

---

## L

**LOC — Lines of Communication**
The routes and channels through which supplies, information, and orders flow.
*One Mind:* The communication channels between Zeus, agents, and domains (Telegram, OpenClaw, etc.).

**LOE — Lines of Effort**
Logical lines of operations that link tasks to end state conditions.
*One Mind:* The parallel tracks of a campaign — each domain running simultaneously toward a shared objective.

**LOGISTICS**
The planning and execution of the movement and maintenance of forces.
*One Mind:* The behind-the-scenes systems that keep operations running — infrastructure, finance, routines.

---

## M

**METT-TC — Mission, Enemy, Terrain, Troops, Time, Civilians**
The six factors analyzed before planning any military operation.
*One Mind:* Pre-decision framework. What's the mission? What are the obstacles? What resources? What's the timeline? Who's affected?

**MISSION**
The task and purpose assigned to a unit or individual.
*One Mind:* The specific objective for an agent, sprint, or project.

**MOE — Measure of Effectiveness**
A metric that tells you if the mission is achieving its intended purpose.
*One Mind:* Outcome metrics — did the action move the needle on the target goal?

**MOP — Measure of Performance**
A metric that tells you if the task was executed correctly.
*One Mind:* Process metrics — was the action executed as planned?

---

## O

**OBJECTIVE**
A specific, tangible goal to be seized, secured, or attained.
*One Mind:* An OKR, project milestone, or domain target.

**OPSEC — Operations Security**
Protecting sensitive information from being used by adversaries.
*One Mind:* Information hygiene — what not to post publicly, what not to share prematurely.

**OPORD — Operations Order**
The complete, formal plan for executing a military operation (5 paragraphs: Situation, Mission, Execution, Service Support, Command).
*One Mind:* A full project brief. Any major initiative should have an OPORD-equivalent.

**OODA Loop — Observe, Orient, Decide, Act**
John Boyd's decision-making cycle. Faster loops beat slower ones.
*One Mind:* The core execution rhythm. CODE× is One Mind's OODA loop.

**OSCAR / OSINT — Open Source Intelligence**
Intelligence derived from publicly available sources.
*One Mind:* Research from web, social, news, and public data — foundational agent skill.

**OVERWATCH**
A tactical position where one element provides covering observation and fire while another element moves or operates. The overwatch element monitors, detects threats, and responds instantly to protect the moving element.
*One Mind:* The Overwatch operation — Legacy and designated agents maintaining persistent, active surveillance across all domains. While Zeus executes, Overwatch watches. Detects anomalies, flags threats, monitors metrics, and can intervene before problems escalate. See `overwatch (Overwatch)/` for full protocol.

---

## P

**PACE — Primary, Alternate, Contingency, Emergency**
A communications plan with four escalating fallback methods.
*One Mind:* System redundancy design — primary channel, backup, fallback, emergency protocol.

**PERSISTENCE**
Maintaining continuous presence in an area or operation over time.
*One Mind:* Consistency — the × (Multiply) in CODE×. Compounding through sustained action.

**PIR — Priority Intelligence Requirements**
The intelligence questions a commander *must* have answered to make critical decisions. Not everything — just what matters most right now.
*One Mind:* The top 3-5 things Legacy needs to monitor at any given time. Defined per sprint or campaign. Overwatch tracks PIRs continuously.

---

## R

**RECON — Reconnaissance**
Gathering information about an area, enemy, or situation before committing forces.
*One Mind:* Research, discovery, and due diligence before making a decision.

**RED TEAM**
A dedicated group that adversarially stress-tests your own plans, assumptions, and systems — thinking like the enemy to find your blind spots.
*One Mind:* Pre-mortem thinking. Before launching a venture or major decision, run a Red Team session: "How does this fail? What are we missing? What would an adversary exploit?"

**RETROGRADE**
A deliberate, planned withdrawal to a more defensible position. Not a retreat — a calculated redeployment.
*One Mind:* Strategic pullback. Exiting a market, ending a partnership, or deprioritizing a domain — done deliberately, not reactively.

**ROE — Rules of Engagement**
The directives that define when, where, and how force may be used.
*One Mind:* Agent operating guidelines — what actions agents are and aren't authorized to take autonomously.

---

## S

**SALUTE Report — Size, Activity, Location, Unit, Time, Equipment**
A standardized format for reporting enemy contact.
*One Mind:* Standard format for reporting a problem, threat, or anomaly to Zeus.

**SEQUEL**
The operation that follows the current one — planned in advance so transition is immediate when the current op ends.
*One Mind:* The next sprint or campaign, planned before the current one closes. No gap between execution cycles.

**SHAPING OPERATIONS**
Actions taken before the decisive operation to set favorable conditions — degrade threats, position assets, isolate the objective.
*One Mind:* The prep work that makes the main move possible. Building an audience before launching a product. Getting fit before starting a new training program.

**SIGINT — Signals Intelligence**
Intelligence gathered by intercepting signals and communications.
*One Mind:* Monitoring patterns in data streams — metrics, logs, behavioral signals.

**SITREP — Situation Report**
A concise update on the current state of an operation.
*One Mind:* Daily/weekly agent status reports. Format: what happened, what's next, any blockers.

**SOF — Special Operations Forces**
Small, elite units for unconventional, high-stakes missions.
*One Mind:* The agents operating in high-autonomy mode on complex, non-routine tasks.

**SOP — Standard Operating Procedure**
A set of step-by-step instructions for routine operations.
*One Mind:* A skill, workflow, or protocol in `_codex/skills/` or `SO 06 Protocols`.

**STANDBY**
A state of readiness — not yet executing but prepared to move.
*One Mind:* Queued tasks or agents on hold awaiting a trigger.

**STAND-TO**
A period of heightened readiness at a specific, scheduled time — typically dawn and dusk when attacks are most likely.
*One Mind:* Scheduled review moments when Overwatch is at full attention. Morning and evening check-ins. The transition windows where most things go wrong.

**SYNCHRONIZATION**
Aligning all actions — forces, fires, effects — in time, space, and purpose to maximize combined impact at the decisive point.
*One Mind:* All agents and domains working toward the same objective simultaneously. Not coordination — synchronization. The difference between a team and a system.

---

## T

**TASK & PURPOSE**
The two required elements of any mission statement. Task = what to do. Purpose = why.
*One Mind:* Every agent instruction should have both. "Search X *in order to* Y."

**TECHINT — Technical Intelligence**
Intelligence derived from analyzing enemy equipment, technology, and technical capabilities.
*One Mind:* Competitive technical analysis — understanding how a competitor's product works, what stack they're running, what their tech enables or limits.

**TEMPO**
The rate of activity. Controlling tempo = dictating the pace of operations.
*One Mind:* Sprint velocity. Fast tempo = rapid iteration. Controlling tempo = operational advantage.

**TRIGGER**
A pre-defined condition that automatically initiates a planned action or branch. Set before the operation begins.
*One Mind:* Automation logic and decision rules. "If X metric crosses Y threshold, Legacy fires Z action." Built into Overwatch monitoring protocols.

**TTP — Tactics, Techniques, and Procedures**
The specific methods used to accomplish tasks at the unit level.
*One Mind:* The how — detailed execution steps for recurring operations.

---

## V

**VECTOR**
A direction or heading toward an objective.
*One Mind:* The strategic direction being pursued in a domain.

**VULNERABILITY**
A weakness that can be exploited by an adversary.
*One Mind:* A gap, risk, or exposure in a domain that needs addressing.

---

## W

**WATCHKEEPER**
The individual or system designated to maintain continuous watch over an operation, situation, or area. Responsible for monitoring, logging, and alerting — not executing.
*One Mind:* The Overwatch role. Legacy operating in watchkeeper mode — persistent monitoring of all domains, flagging anomalies, and escalating to Zeus when triggers are hit.

**WTF (WHISKEY TANGO FOXTROT)**
NATO phonetic alphabet spelling of "WTF" — military radio-friendly expression of disbelief, confusion, or frustration at an unexpected situation.
*One Mind:* What Zeus says when an agent does something unexpected, a trigger fires at 3am, or a SITREP contains information that makes no sense. Also acceptable as a standalone response to any Overwatch alert.

---

## Z

**ZERO DEFECT**
A standard that tolerates no mistakes — often counterproductive in real operations.
*One Mind:* Perfectionism. Recognize when "good enough to execute" beats waiting for perfect.

**ZERO DARK THIRTY**
Midnight / the hours before dawn — time of maximum operational advantage.
*One Mind:* Deep work hours. The time when others are inactive and you compound.
