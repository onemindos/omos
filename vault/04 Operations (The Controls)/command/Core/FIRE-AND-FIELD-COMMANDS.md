# Fire & Field Commands — One Mind Tactical Protocol

Adapted from SEAL Team doctrine. Cleaned up, improved, and customized for the One Mind team and agent fleet. Legacy uses these. Team members use these. Teach these before any coordinated operation.

Two categories: **Fire Commands** (engagement control) and **Field Commands** (position and movement). 24 commands total.

---

## Fire Commands — Engagement Control

These govern when and how you engage a target. Discipline here is the difference between a precision strike and wasted ammunition.

---

### ENGAGE
**Definition:** Weapons free. You are authorized to fire. Return fire or initiate contact.

**SEAL origin:** "Weapons free" — cleared to engage any target that presents itself as a threat.

**Usage:**
```
"ENGAGE HVT-1"
"ENGAGE — weapons free, return fire only"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Agent or team member is authorized to take full action on an objective. Stop waiting, move.

**Rules:** Selective and disciplined. ENGAGE is not SUPPRESS — you're shooting smart, not spraying.

---

### HOLD
**Definition:** Cease all fire immediately. Weapons safe. Freeze in place.

**SEAL origin:** Universal stop command. Non-negotiable. Everything stops.

**Usage:**
```
"HOLD" — all elements stop
"HOLD FIRE" — weapons only, maintain position
```

**One Mind meaning:** Pause all activity on an objective. Do not proceed until EXECUTE or ENGAGE is called again.

**Note:** HOLD also applies to position (see Field Commands). Context tells you which.

---

### RHYTHM
**Definition:** Maintain your fire cadence. Sustain the tempo. Don't let suppression lapse.

**SEAL origin:** Fire discipline — keeping a consistent rate of fire to deny the enemy movement without burning through ammo.

**Usage:**
```
"RHYTHM" — keep the pace
"RHYTHM on Sector 2" — maintain suppression on that sector specifically
```

**One Mind meaning:** Keep the work output consistent. Don't sprint-then-stop. Maintain operational tempo — agents, tasks, outputs all need cadence to compound.

**Key distinction:** RHYTHM ≠ SUPPRESS. Rhythm is sustainable. Suppress is maximum burst.

---

### SHIFT
**Definition:** Redirect fire to a new target or sector. Awareness call — something better has been identified.

**SEAL origin:** "Shift fire" — move your point of aim to a new priority target, usually called by a spotter or team lead who sees a better angle or more critical threat.

**Usage:**
```
"SHIFT LEFT"
"SHIFT to HVT-2"
"SHIFT — new sector, 3 o'clock"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Redirect effort. What you're working on is no longer the priority — shift to what I'm pointing at.

**The awareness component:** SHIFT implies the caller has better visibility than the shooter. Trust the call.

---

### COVER
**Definition:** Break contact. Take a defensive position. Minimize exposure.

**SEAL origin:** "Take cover" — find hard cover, reduce your silhouette, stop being a target.

**Usage:**
```
"COVER" — all elements find cover now
"COVER — fall back to Alpha position"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Pull back from an active engagement. Reduce exposure on a sensitive topic, deal, or situation. Defensive posture until further orders.

**Different from HOLD:** HOLD stops action. COVER changes position.

---

### SUPPRESS
**Definition:** Maximum rate of fire on target. Keep their heads down. Enable friendly movement.

**SEAL origin:** "Suppressing fire" — you are not trying to kill the target, you are pinning them so someone else can move, flank, or execute. Full capacity, no hesitation.

**Usage:**
```
"SUPPRESS Sector 1"
"SUPPRESS — keep them pinned while Bravo moves"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Go full capacity on a problem. Throw everything at it. This is not precision — this is overwhelming force to create space for something else to happen.

**The cost:** Suppression burns resources fast. It's a short burst, not a sustained state.

---

### COVER FIRE
**Definition:** Provide fire to support a team member's movement. You're not shooting to hit — you're shooting so they can move.

**SEAL origin:** "Cover fire" — coordinated with movement. The shooter calls "moving" and the cover element immediately suppresses to protect them.

**Usage:**
```
"COVER FIRE — Bravo moving to Checkpoint 2"
"COVER FIRE on my move"
```

**One Mind meaning:** One agent or team member takes action. Another provides support, interference, or parallel work to make that action safer or faster.

**Pair command:** Always paired with movement. No cover fire without a mover.

---

### TANGO DOWN
**Definition:** Target is down. Objective neutralized. Threat eliminated.

**SEAL origin:** Radio call confirming a kill or a successful takedown. The op isn't over — but that specific target is.

**Usage:**
```
"TANGO DOWN — HVT-1 neutralized"
"TANGO DOWN — deal closed"
"TANGO DOWN on task 47"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Task completed. Objective achieved. The confirmation call after EXECUTE lands. Use it to close the loop — tells the team something is done and off the board.

**Pair command:** Usually follows EXECUTE. EXECUTE → work happens → TANGO DOWN.

---

### BINGO
**Definition:** Resources at critical minimum. Still operational, but the warning light is on.

**Aviation/SEAL origin:** Pilot call for fuel at minimum safe return level — must RTB now or risk running dry. Not empty, but critical.

**Usage:**
```
"BINGO on budget — $2K left"
"BINGO — I'm at capacity, need to wrap"
"BINGO on time — 30 minutes left"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Resources (budget, energy, time, capacity) are critically low. Not dead yet — but if you don't acknowledge this now, WINCHESTER follows. The warning before the hard stop.

**Rule:** BINGO triggers a decision point. Either RTB, redistribute resources, or consciously accept the risk of continuing.

---

### WINCHESTER
**Definition:** Out of ammunition. Cannot engage. All resources expended.

**Aviation/SEAL origin:** Pilot call for complete weapons depletion — no more ordnance, must RTB immediately.

**Usage:**
```
"WINCHESTER — budget gone, operation paused"
"WINCHESTER on this sprint — need resupply before continuing"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Full stop — resources (money, time, energy, capacity) are completely exhausted. Cannot continue the operation. RTB and resupply before re-engaging.

**The chain:** BINGO → warning → WINCHESTER → full stop → RTB.

---

### EXECUTE / XXX
**Definition:** Unified action trigger. All elements initiate simultaneously on this signal.

**SEAL origin:** The green light. Breaching teams, snipers, assault element — everyone moves at the same moment. No hesitation after EXECUTE.

**Usage:**
```
"EXECUTE"
"XXX"
"On my mark — three, two, one, EXECUTE"
```

**One Mind meaning:** All agents, all team members, all systems — go simultaneously. Used for coordinated launches, deployments, campaigns, or any operation that requires synchronized action.

**Rule:** Once EXECUTE is called, there is no pause, no clarification, no hesitation. If you're not ready, you should have said so before the count.

## Field Commands — Position & Movement

These govern where you are and where you're going.

---

### POS — Position
**Definition:** Report your current position. Where are you?

**Usage:**
```
"POS?" — requesting position report
"POS — Checkpoint Alpha, holding" — reporting position
```

**One Mind meaning:** Where are you on the objective? Status check mid-operation.

---

### HVT — High Value Target
**Definition:** High Value Target identified. Priority redirect — this person or objective overrides current tasking.

**Usage:**
```
"HVT spotted — investor meeting in 20 minutes"
"HVT on the board — SHIFT to HVT"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Something high-priority just appeared. Drop what you're doing or adjust resources toward it. Applies to people, deals, opportunities, threats.

---

### RTB — Return to Base
**Definition:** Mission complete or aborted. Return to base.

**Usage:**
```
"RTB — objective complete"
"RTB — mission abort, extraction in progress"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Stand down. Return to default state. The operation is over — successful or not.

---

### AVALANCHE
**Definition:** Explosive clear — get out of the kill zone immediately. Full extraction, no hesitation.

**SEAL origin:** Inspired by demo/breach protocols — when explosives are in play, everyone clears the zone on a single command. No questions.

**Usage:**
```
"AVALANCHE" — all elements extract immediately
"AVALANCHE — clear the building"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Emergency exit from a situation, deal, relationship, or environment. Drop everything and get clear. Used when a situation turns toxic, dangerous, or irretrievably bad. This is the nuclear option for extraction.

**Rule:** AVALANCHE is not called lightly. When it's called, you move.

---

### RALLY
**Definition:** Consolidate at a pre-designated rally point. Regroup.

**Usage:**
```
"RALLY at Point Bravo"
"RALLY — regroup before next push"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Pull all agents and team members together for a sync. Debrief, reassess, redistribute before the next operation.

---

### SITREP — Situation Report
**Definition:** Give me a status update. What is happening right now?

**Usage:**
```
"SITREP" — all elements report status
"SITREP on the GE operation"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Standard status request to agents or team. Expected response: current status, blockers, ETA.

---

### RECON
**Definition:** Intelligence-gather phase. Observe, assess, and report before committing.

**SEAL origin:** Reconnaissance — go in soft, no weapons hot, no engagement. The objective is information only.

**Usage:**
```
"RECON the market before we ENGAGE"
"RECON on that partner — who are they?"
"RECON phase — 48 hours, then SITREP"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Research before action. Do not commit resources, make offers, or engage until RECON is complete. This is the intelligence-gathering mode before an operation.

**Rule:** RECON ends with a SITREP. No RECON without a report.

---

### OSCAR MIKE
**Definition:** On the Move. Currently executing. In transit to next objective.

**SEAL origin:** Phonetic alphabet — O.M. = "On the Move." Radio call confirming movement is active.

**Usage:**
```
"OSCAR MIKE to Checkpoint Bravo"
"Legacy, OSCAR MIKE on the GE sprint"
"OSCAR MIKE — ETA 20 minutes"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Actively executing and moving toward the objective. Not stuck, not waiting — in motion. The status call between EXECUTE and TANGO DOWN.

---

### BREACH
**Definition:** Force entry. Initiate access. Going in now.

**SEAL origin:** The breach command — door goes down, team moves through. Committed entry, no hesitation.

**Usage:**
```
"BREACH — initiate outreach to the VC"
"BREACH on the market — launch now"
"BREACH the account — get inside"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Initiate access or entry into something new — a market, a relationship, a system, a negotiation. The moment you go from outside to inside. Committed. No pulling back after BREACH.

---

### GHOST
**Definition:** Go dark. Radio silence. No outbound communication, no visibility.

**SEAL origin:** GHOST protocol — operator goes dark in hostile territory. No transmissions, no signals, no trace.

**Usage:**
```
"GHOST on this deal — no communication until I say"
"GHOST — I'm going offline for 72 hours"
"GHOST mode on the launch — no leaks"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Intentional silence on a topic, deal, or operation. Can mean: protecting information, strategic silence during a negotiation, or pulling back from visibility on purpose. Not the same as RTB — you're still active, just invisible.

**Rule:** GHOST is deliberate. Always have a pre-agreed trigger to break ghost (time-based or event-based).

---

### STANDBY
**Definition:** Loaded, ready, not executing yet. Awaiting the go signal.

**SEAL origin:** Pre-breach position — stack is formed, gear is set, everyone is in position. The trigger hasn't been pulled.

**Usage:**
```
"STANDBY for EXECUTE"
"All elements STANDBY — waiting on Zeus"
"Legacy, STANDBY on the report until I confirm"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Prepared and ready but holding. Different from HOLD (which stops action already in progress) — STANDBY means you never started. You're in position, awaiting the signal.

**The distinction:** HOLD pauses. STANDBY waits. HOLD comes after action starts. STANDBY comes before.

---

### REDLINE
**Definition:** Critical threshold crossed. Escalate immediately.

**SEAL origin:** Instrument redline — the gauge has hit the danger zone. Immediate action required or the system fails.

**Usage:**
```
"REDLINE on cash — escalate now"
"REDLINE — this partnership is compromised"
"REDLINE alert: server is at capacity"
```

**One Mind meaning:** Something has hit a critical threshold that requires immediate escalation to Zeus or a senior decision-maker. Not a normal problem — a danger-zone problem. The system cannot self-correct without intervention.

**Rule:** REDLINE always goes to Zeus. It doesn't get resolved at the agent level.

---

### SECURED
**Definition:** Objective confirmed locked down. Area is clear and controlled.

**SEAL origin:** Post-breach confirmation — room is cleared, threat is neutralized, the space is ours. Safe to bring in the next element.

**Usage:**
```
"Area SECURED — advance element can move"
"Deal SECURED — contracts signed"
"System SECURED — vulnerabilities patched"
```

**One Mind meaning:** The objective is confirmed complete, locked down, and safe. Different from TANGO DOWN (which is about a specific target) — SECURED is about a whole zone or operation being under control.

**The distinction:** TANGO DOWN = that target is gone. SECURED = this area is ours.

## Usage Rules

1. **Commands are short.** One word when possible. In fast-moving situations, clarity beats completeness.
2. **Acknowledge receipt.** When you receive a command, echo it back: *"HOLD — confirmed"* or *"EXECUTING"*.
3. **EXECUTE is final.** No pause after XXX. If you're not ready, speak up before the count.
4. **AVALANCHE is nuclear.** Don't call it unless you mean full extraction.
5. **SUPPRESS is a burst, not a state.** Call RHYTHM if you need sustained output.
6. **SHIFT requires a target.** "SHIFT" alone is incomplete — always call what you're shifting to.
7. **BINGO → WINCHESTER is a sequence.** Don't skip to WINCHESTER — call BINGO first so the team has a chance to respond.
8. **RECON ends with a SITREP.** No reconnaissance without a report.
9. **GHOST needs an exit trigger.** Always define how and when you come out of GHOST before going in.
10. **REDLINE always escalates.** It doesn't resolve at the agent level — goes to Zeus.
11. **STANDBY ≠ HOLD.** STANDBY = ready, never started. HOLD = action already in progress, now stopped.
12. **TANGO DOWN closes the loop.** Call it after EXECUTE lands so the team knows the board has updated.

## For Legacy

Legacy recognizes all commands above. In Telegram or Discord:

- Single-word commands trigger immediate routing and action
- Commands can be chained: `"ENGAGE HVT — SUPPRESS until EXECUTE — TANGO DOWN when done"`
- Legacy will echo the command back with a brief status: *"SUPPRESS confirmed — running at full on [objective]"*
- Use `SITREP` at any time for a fleet status summary
