# Mental-Wellness

> *Subcategory of Zeus - Performance (Body & Biology)*

---

## Purpose

Mental health is as vital as physical health. This area manages **psychological wellbeing, emotional regulation, and mental healthcare**.

---

## What Goes Here

- Therapy notes & sessions
- Mental health tracking (mood, anxiety, stress)
- Psychological assessments
- Emotional regulation techniques
- Coping strategies & tools
- Mental health resources
- Medication tracking (if applicable)
- Crisis plans & support contacts

---

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Frequency | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Mood Score | Daily | 7+/10 |
| Anxiety Level | Daily | Low-Moderate |
| Therapy Sessions | Monthly | 2-4x |
| Stress Events | Weekly | Track triggers |
