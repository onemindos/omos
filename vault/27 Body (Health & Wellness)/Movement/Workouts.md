- Monday: Chest
- Warmup:
- Pull-ups 3x15 reps
- Warm up all rotator cuff muscles
- Arm circles (small, medium, and large) 2x10 each direction
- Stretch as needed
- Start of workout!

1. Incline dumbbell press: *2 warmup sets of 15-20 reps

*4 working sets of 15,12,10,15 reps

1. Incline hammer strength press: (drop set training)

*2 warmup sets 20 reps

*3 working set going up in weight each set

- - Last set is a drop set, drop the weight by 10-20 lbs and perform reps until failure

1. Incline dumbbell flys: (tempo training: 3 second negative ➡️ 1 second squeeze at the top)

  *2 warmup sets 10 reps

  *4 working sets of 12,10,15,15 reps

- **BONUS. Flat dumbbell crush press 4x12

         Or incline pinch press 4x10

1. Decline barbell press: (tempo training: 3 second negative ➡️ no pause at the bottom ➡️ explode up)
    - 3 working sets of 10 reps
2. Body weight dips (use assisted dip machine if needed)

*3x10 reps

- Tuesday: Legs:
- Warmup:
- Body weight squats: 3x20 reps
- Bodyweight walking lunges: 2x10 reps each leg
- Stretch as necessary
- Start of workout!

1. Barbell back squats or hack squats (progressive overload training) *2 warm up sets of 10 reps *3 working sets 12-15 reps
2. Leg press: (feet shoulder width apart) (triple drop set training)
    - 4x10 reps

*Last set triple drop set

*Perform 10+ reps each drop set

1. Walking barbell lunges: *3x10 steps each leg
2. Leg curls: (superset training and tempo training: 3 second negative ➡️ 1 second pause at the bottom ➡️ 2 second squeeze at the top) *4x10 reps

Right into ⬇️

1. Leg extensions (tempo: 3 negative negative ➡️ 1 second pause at the bottom ➡️ explode up) *4x12 reps

- Wednesday: Shoulders
- Warmup:
- Push ups
- Shoulder rotator cuff exercises
- Arm circles (small, medium and big) 10 reps going forward and back wards. 3 total sets
- Start of workout!

1. Seated shoulder press with barbell: (tempo training: 3 second negative ➡️ no pause at the bottom ➡️ explode up)
    - 2 warmup sets 15 reps
    - 4 working sets of 12 reps
2. Lateral raises: (drop set training) (Make sure you are driving elbows up as if you were pouring a glass of water) *2 warm up sets 15 reps
    - 3 working sets of 20 reps
    - On the last set, drop the weight by 15 lbs and perform until failure)
3. Bent over dumbbell rear deltoid flys *5x15 reps
4. Single arm lateral raises with dumbbells
    - 3x15 reps each arm
5. Barbell Upright rows (progressive overload training) *4x12 reps
6. Rope face pulls (superset training) 4x10 reps

Right into ⬇️

1. Dumbbell frontal raises

*4x20 reps
