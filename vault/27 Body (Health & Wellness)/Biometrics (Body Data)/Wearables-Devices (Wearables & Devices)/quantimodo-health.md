## 🌎 Overview: What is it?

**Quantimo.do** (which powers the "Decentralized FDA" or **dFDA**) is a website and ecosystem dedicated to automating the discovery of cures for chronic illnesses.

It is based on a simple premise: **Millions of people are already tracking their health every day (FitBit, Apple Health, Diet logs).** If we aggregate and analyze that data, we can find out what _actually_ works, faster and cheaper than any pharmaceutical clinical trial.

> "A clinical trial of 1 billion people, running 24/7."

---

## 🛠️ The Three Core Pillars of the Platform

### 1. The Global "Studies" Database

([studies.dfda.earth](https://studies.dfda.earth))

This is the public-facing library of human knowledge generated by the system. You can go to this website right now and look up any condition or treatment.

- **Example:** Search for "Migraines".
- **Result:** The site shows a ranked list of "Treatments" sorted by their **Statistical Effectiveness** based on real user data.
  - _Top Result:_ Magnesium (Strong positive correlation).
  - _Bottom Result:_ Red Wine (Strong negative correlation).
  - _Hidden Insight:_ It might show that "Weather Changes" are a stronger predictor than diet.

### 2. The Personal Analytics Engine (Your "Digital Twin")

This is the tool for the individual user. When you connect your data to the platform, it builds a **Personal Model** of your biology.

- **The Problem:** Most doctors guess. "Try this pill, see if it works."
- **The Solution:** Quantimo.do analyzes your history. "When you take Vitamin D, your sleep improves by 14% after 2 days."
- **Onset Delays:** The system is smart enough to know that some things take time to work. It doesn't just look at _instant_ reactions; it looks for delayed effects (e.g., food sensitivities taking 24h to show up).

### 3. Outcome Labels

We have "Nutrition Labels" for food that tell us calories and fat.
Quantimo.do proposes **"Outcome Labels"** for everything else.

- Imagine skimming a bottle of supplements or a specific diet plan and seeing a label:
  - _"In 40,000 users, this treatment improved Energy by 22% but reduced Sleep Quality by 5%."_
- This moves medicine from "Anecdote" ("My friend said it worked") to "Data" ("It works for 82% of people like me").

## 🏥 The "dFDA" (Decentralized FDA) Vision

The creators argue that the current FDA model is broken:

- **Too Slow:** Takes 12 years to approve a drug.
- **Too Expensive:** Costs $2 Billion per drug (so only profitable drugs get made).
- **Too Exclusive:** Only tests "Patentable Molecules." No one spends billions to test if "Broccoli" cures cancer because you can't patent broccoli.

**The dFDA Alternative:**

- **Crowdsourced:** Users donate their data anonymously.
- **Universal:** Tests _everything_ (Foods, Supplements, Meditations, Weather).
- **Fast:** Insights appear in real-time as data flows in.
