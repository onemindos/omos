# Sleep Optimization Protocol

> *A comprehensive, evidence-based protocol for maximizing sleep quality and recovery.*

---

## Overview

This protocol is designed to systematically improve sleep quality through layered interventions. Follow the phases in order, allowing 2 weeks per phase before adding the next layer.

**Expected Results:**
- Increase sleep score from ~70 to 85+
- Improve HRV by 10-20%
- Reduce resting heart rate by 3-5 bpm
- Enhance next-day energy and performance

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Weeks 1-2)

### Consistency Protocol

**Goal:** Establish consistent sleep-wake rhythm

**Actions:**
- [ ] Set consistent bedtime (±15 minutes)
  - Target: 10:00 PM - 10:30 PM
- [ ] Set consistent wake time (±15 minutes)
  - Target: 6:00 AM - 6:30 AM
- [ ] No snooze button (wake immediately)
- [ ] Track actual sleep/wake times daily

**Why it matters:** Circadian rhythm thrives on consistency. Variable sleep times disrupt your body's natural clock.

### Sleep Hygiene Basics

- [ ] Bedroom temperature: 65-68°F
- [ ] Complete darkness (blackout curtains or sleep mask)
- [ ] Quiet environment (white noise if needed)
- [ ] Comfortable mattress and pillow
- [ ] Clean, breathable bedding

### Last Caffeine Rule

- [ ] No caffeine after 2:00 PM (10+ hours before bed)
- [ ] If sensitive: No caffeine after 12:00 PM

**Baseline tracking:** Use [Sleep Log template](../Templates/Sleep Log.md) to establish baseline metrics before making changes.

---

## Phase 2: Pre-Sleep Routine (Weeks 3-4)

### 90-Minute Wind-Down Protocol

**Goal:** Signal to body that it's time to prepare for sleep

#### 90 Minutes Before Bed (8:30 PM)
- [ ] Dim all lights in home (or use red/amber bulbs)
- [ ] Activate blue light filters on all screens
- [ ] Finish last meal (3+ hours before bed ideal)
- [ ] Hydration cutoff (minimize fluids to reduce waking)

#### 60 Minutes Before Bed (9:00 PM)
- [ ] No screens (TV, phone, computer)
- [ ] Take evening supplements (magnesium glycinate)
- [ ] Lower room temperature (start cooling bedroom)
- [ ] Begin quiet, relaxing activity:
  - Reading (physical book)
  - Gentle stretching
  - Meditation or breathwork
  - Journaling or brain dump

#### 30 Minutes Before Bed (9:30 PM)
- [ ] Complete evening hygiene routine
- [ ] Set room to optimal sleep temperature
- [ ] Ensure complete darkness
- [ ] Do 5-10 minute relaxation practice:
  - **Box breathing:** 4-4-4-4 (5-10 rounds)
  - **4-7-8 breathing:** Inhale 4, hold 7, exhale 8 (4-8 rounds)
  - **Body scan meditation:** Progressive relaxation

#### Bedtime (10:00 PM)
- [ ] Get in bed only when sleepy
- [ ] If not asleep in 15-20 minutes, get up and do relaxing activity
- [ ] Return to bed when sleepy

**Sleep restriction rule:** Bed is for sleep and intimacy only (no reading, TV, phone in bed)

## Phase 3: Light Management (Weeks 5-6)

### Morning Light Exposure

**Goal:** Set circadian rhythm with morning bright light

**Protocol:**
- [ ] Get 10-30 minutes of direct sunlight within 30 min of waking
- [ ] No sunglasses during morning light
- [ ] Outdoors ideal (even cloudy day = better than indoor)
- [ ] If no sun: Use 10,000 lux light therapy box (30 min)

**Why it matters:** Morning bright light is the strongest circadian cue. It sets your body clock for the day and improves evening sleepiness.

### Evening Light Reduction

- [ ] After sunset: Dim all lights to <50 lux
- [ ] Use red or amber bulbs in living spaces (2-3 hours before bed)
- [ ] Blue light blocking glasses (if using screens)
- [ ] Candlelight or salt lamps for ambiance
- [ ] Blackout curtains or sleep mask for complete darkness

**Screen protocol (if unavoidable):**
- All devices on night mode (minimal blue light)
- Screen brightness <30%
- Blue light filter apps (f.lux, Night Shift)
- Stop 1-2 hours before bed (minimum 1 hour)

## Phase 4: Supplement Stack (Weeks 7-8)

### Core Evening Stack (Daily)

**60-90 minutes before bed:**

1. **Magnesium Glycinate: 300-400mg**
   - Promotes relaxation and deep sleep
   - Supports muscle recovery
   - Minimal morning grogginess
   - Best form: Bisglycinate

2. **Optional: L-Theanine: 200-400mg**
   - For racing thoughts or difficulty winding down
   - Promotes relaxation without sedation
   - Synergistic with magnesium

### Advanced Stack (Cycle as Needed)

**For enhanced deep sleep:**

3. **Glycine: 3g**
   - Lowers core body temperature
   - Improves sleep quality
   - May reduce time to fall asleep
   - Take 60 minutes before bed

4. **Apigenin: 50mg**
   - Chamomile extract
   - Mild anxiolytic (anxiety-reducing)
   - Gentle sedative effect
   - Cycle: 4 weeks on, 1 week off

### Melatonin (Use Sparingly)

**Dosage: 0.3-1mg (NOT 3-10mg)**
- Only for circadian rhythm disruption (travel, shift work)
- Take 30-60 minutes before target sleep time
- Cycle: Use only when needed, not nightly
- Lower doses more effective than high doses

**When NOT to use:**
- If sleeping well without it
- Long-term nightly use (can suppress natural production)
- As first intervention (fix habits first)

### Supplements to AVOID Before Bed

- ❌ Caffeine (obvious)
- ❌ Vitamin B12 (can be stimulating)
- ❌ High-dose vitamin C (can be stimulating)
- ❌ Pre-workout ingredients (beta-alanine, L-carnitine)
- ❌ Stimulating adaptogens (rhodiola, cordyceps)

## Phase 5: Temperature Optimization (Weeks 9-10)

### Core Body Temperature Regulation

**Goal:** Lower core body temperature to trigger sleepiness

**Evening temperature drop protocol:**

1. **Warm bath or shower (90-120 min before bed):**
   - Temperature: 104-109°F (40-43°C)
   - Duration: 10-15 minutes
   - Why: Vasodilation after bath drops core temperature

2. **Cool bedroom (target: 65-68°F):**
   - Some people prefer cooler (60-65°F)
   - Use fan or AC to actively cool
   - Keep head cool (important for melatonin release)

3. **Warm extremities:**
   - Wear socks if feet are cold
   - Cold hands/feet constrict blood vessels, preventing cooling
   - Paradoxically, warm extremities = cooler core

### Advanced Temperature Tactics

- **Cooling mattress pad:** Eight Sleep, ChiliPad, BedJet
  - Can drop temp throughout night
  - Wake with gradual warming
- **Cooling pillow:** Gel or water-based cooling pillows
- **Bedroom fan:** Air circulation + white noise

## Phase 6: Advanced Optimization (Weeks 11+)

### HRV-Guided Sleep Adjustments

**High HRV (Green):**
- Maintain current protocol
- Body is recovering well
- Training intensity can be normal or high

**Normal HRV (Yellow):**
- Stick to protocol
- Monitor trends
- Adjust if declining

**Low HRV (Red):**
- ⚠️ Prioritize extra sleep
- Earlier bedtime (+30-60 min)
- Extra recovery day
- Reduce training intensity
- Review stress and nutrition

### Sleep Cycle Timing

**Understanding sleep cycles:**
- Complete cycle: ~90 minutes
- Optimal sleep: Multiples of 90 min (6h, 7.5h, 9h)
- Waking between cycles = easier wake, better energy

**Sleep cycle calculator:**
- If wake at 6:00 AM:
  - 9h sleep: Bed by 9:00 PM (6 cycles)
  - 7.5h sleep: Bed by 10:30 PM (5 cycles)
  - 6h sleep: Bed by 12:00 AM (4 cycles - minimum)

### Power Nap Protocol (Optional)

**When to nap:**
- Energy dip (typically 2-4 PM)
- Sleep debt from previous night
- Before late-night event

**Optimal nap duration:**
- **20 minutes:** Alertness boost, no grogginess
- **90 minutes:** Full sleep cycle, deeper restoration

**Avoid:** 30-60 min naps (wake during deep sleep = groggy)

**Nap timing:**
- Not after 4:00 PM (can interfere with nighttime sleep)
- Ideal: 1:00-3:00 PM window

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Difficulty Falling Asleep

**Likely causes:**
- Late caffeine
- Insufficient wind-down
- Screen time before bed
- Racing thoughts
- Room too warm

**Solutions:**
1. Earlier last caffeine (12 PM cutoff)
2. Extend wind-down to 120 minutes
3. Add magnesium glycinate + L-theanine
4. Brain dump journaling (get thoughts out)
5. 4-7-8 breathing (10 rounds)
6. Lower room temp to 65°F

### Issue: Waking During Night

**Likely causes:**
- Alcohol consumption
- Late/heavy meal
- Stress/anxiety
- Sleep apnea
- Bladder (too much fluid)

**Solutions:**
1. No alcohol within 3 hours of bed (disrupts REM)
2. Finish dinner by 6-7 PM
3. Add glycine (3g) to evening stack
4. Reduce evening fluid intake
5. Consider sleep apnea evaluation if frequent
6. White noise machine (mask disruptions)

### Issue: Early Morning Waking

**Likely causes:**
- Cortisol spike (stress)
- Light exposure (room not dark enough)
- Blood sugar crash
- Insufficient sleep pressure

**Solutions:**
1. Complete darkness (blackout curtains + mask)
2. Small protein snack before bed (stabilize blood sugar)
3. Stress management practices (meditation, therapy)
4. Later bedtime (if waking too early consistently)

### Issue: Low HRV / Poor Readiness

**Likely causes:**
- Overtraining
- Insufficient recovery
- Poor nutrition
- High stress
- Inflammation

**Solutions:**
1. Add recovery day
2. Reduce training volume 20-30%
3. Earlier bedtime (+30-60 min sleep)
4. Review nutrition (anti-inflammatory diet)
5. Extra recovery modalities (cold plunge, sauna)
6. Stress management focus

### Issue: Restless Sleep / Low Deep Sleep %

**Likely causes:**
- Room too warm
- Stress/anxiety
- Alcohol or drugs
- Magnesium deficiency
- Overtraining

**Solutions:**
1. Lower room temp to 65°F or below
2. Add magnesium glycinate (300-400mg)
3. Add glycine (3g)
4. Reduce training volume
5. Meditation before bed (body scan)
6. No alcohol (even 2-3 drinks affects deep sleep)

## Sleep Tracking & Metrics

### Essential Metrics (Daily)

**Track with wearable (Oura, Whoop, Garmin):**
- [ ] Total sleep time (target: 7-9 hours)
- [ ] Sleep score (target: 85+)
- [ ] HRV (target: baseline + 10-20%)
- [ ] Resting heart rate (target: 3-5 bpm below baseline)
- [ ] Deep sleep % (target: 15-25%)
- [ ] REM sleep % (target: 20-25%)
- [ ] Sleep efficiency (target: 85%+)

**Track manually (if no wearable):**
- [ ] Bedtime and wake time
- [ ] Estimated sleep duration
- [ ] Sleep quality (1-5 rating)
- [ ] Energy upon waking (1-5 rating)
- [ ] Next-day performance (1-5 rating)

### Weekly Review

Analyze trends:
- Average sleep score
- HRV trend (improving/declining/stable)
- Deep sleep average
- Factors affecting sleep (late caffeine, stress, alcohol, training)
- Protocol adherence

### Monthly Assessment

- Compare to baseline (before optimization)
- Celebrate wins and improvements
- Identify remaining issues
- Adjust protocol as needed
- Set next month's focus area
