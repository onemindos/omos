# Sleep Supplements Guide

> *Evidence-based supplement protocols for optimizing sleep quality and recovery.*

---

## Philosophy

**Supplements are not a substitute for good sleep hygiene.** They enhance an already solid foundation of:
- Consistent sleep schedule
- Optimized sleep environment
- Pre-sleep routine
- Light management

**Approach:** Start with lifestyle interventions first, then add supplements strategically.

---

## Core Sleep Stack (Daily Use)

### 1. Magnesium Glycinate: 300-400mg

**Timing:** 60-90 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Promotes muscle relaxation
- ✅ Supports parasympathetic nervous system (rest & digest)
- ✅ Increases GABA activity (calming neurotransmitter)
- ✅ Improves deep sleep percentage
- ✅ Reduces muscle cramps and restless legs
- ✅ Minimal morning grogginess

**Why glycinate form:**
- Best absorption
- Least likely to cause digestive upset
- Glycine itself has sleep-enhancing properties
- Avoid magnesium oxide (poor absorption, laxative effect)

**Dosage guidelines:**
- Start: 200mg
- Standard: 300-400mg
- Maximum: 600mg (split morning + evening)
- Titrate up if no effect after 1 week

**Safety:**
- Very safe, wide therapeutic range
- Side effects rare (diarrhea if too high dose)
- Avoid if kidney disease (consult doctor)

**Brands:**
- Thorne Magnesium Bisglycinate
- Pure Encapsulations Magnesium Glycinate
- Doctor's Best High Absorption Magnesium
- Life Extension Magnesium

**Cost:** $15-30 / month

---

### 2. L-Theanine: 200-400mg (Optional)

**Timing:** 60-90 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Promotes relaxation without sedation
- ✅ Reduces racing thoughts and mental chatter
- ✅ Increases alpha brain waves (calm, focused state)
- ✅ Reduces stress and anxiety
- ✅ Improves sleep quality
- ✅ No morning grogginess

**When to use:**
- High stress or anxiety
- Racing thoughts preventing sleep
- Difficulty winding down mentally

**Dosage:**
- Start: 100-200mg
- Standard: 200mg
- Higher anxiety: 400mg
- Can also take during day (reduces stress without sedation)

**Synergies:**
- Pairs excellently with magnesium
- Can combine with glycine
- Safe with other sleep supplements

**Safety:**
- Extremely safe, minimal side effects
- Non-habit forming
- Can use daily or as-needed

**Brands:**
- Suntheanine (patented, high-quality form)
- Thorne L-Theanine
- NOW L-Theanine

**Cost:** $10-20 / month

## Advanced Sleep Stack (Cycle as Needed)

### 3. Glycine: 3g

**Timing:** 60 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Lowers core body temperature (sleep-inducing)
- ✅ Improves subjective sleep quality
- ✅ Reduces time to fall asleep
- ✅ Enhances memory consolidation
- ✅ Next-day alertness and energy (better sleep quality)
- ✅ Safe, minimal side effects

**Mechanism:**
- Activates NMDA receptors
- Reduces core body temperature
- Increases serotonin (precursor to melatonin)

**Dosage:**
- Standard: 3g (3,000mg)
- Can take up to 5g if no effect
- Powder form recommended (capsules require many pills)

**Taste:**
- Mildly sweet (easy to mix in water)

**Cycling:**
- Can use daily
- Or cycle: 4 weeks on, 1 week off

**Synergies:**
- Excellent with magnesium
- Often combined in sleep formulas
- Magnesium glycinate = magnesium + glycine (bonus!)

**Safety:**
- Very safe, it's an amino acid
- Side effects extremely rare
- Large doses needed = powder recommended

**Brands:**
- BulkSupplements Glycine Powder
- NOW Glycine Powder
- Thorne Glycine

**Cost:** $10-15 / month (powder is cheap)

---

### 4. Apigenin: 50mg

**Timing:** 60 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Mild anxiolytic (reduces anxiety)
- ✅ Promotes relaxation
- ✅ Binds to benzodiazepine receptors (like Valium, but mild)
- ✅ Antioxidant properties
- ✅ May reduce cancer risk (research ongoing)

**Source:**
- Chamomile extract (primary source)
- Also in parsley, celery, but much lower amounts

**Dosage:**
- Standard: 50mg apigenin
- Equivalent to ~2-3 cups strong chamomile tea

**When to use:**
- Anxiety preventing sleep
- Need mild sedative effect
- Prefer natural alternatives

**Cycling:**
- Use as needed, or
- 4 weeks on, 1 week off

**Safety:**
- Generally safe
- Possible drug interactions (metabolized by CYP450 enzymes)
- May interact with blood thinners (consult doctor)

**Brands:**
- Swanson Apigenin
- Most chamomile extracts standardized to apigenin content

**Cost:** $10-15 / month

---

### 5. Tart Cherry Extract: 500-1000mg

**Timing:** 60-90 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Natural source of melatonin
- ✅ Reduces inflammation (helps recovery)
- ✅ Decreases muscle soreness
- ✅ Antioxidant-rich
- ✅ May improve sleep duration and quality

**Source:**
- Montmorency tart cherries (highest melatonin content)

**Dosage:**
- Capsules: 500-1000mg extract
- Juice: 8-12 oz (not concentrate)
- Powder: 1-2 scoops

**When to use:**
- Hard training (dual benefit: sleep + recovery)
- Natural melatonin alternative
- High oxidative stress

**Side effects:**
- Minimal, generally well-tolerated
- Juice form = significant calories/sugar (be aware)

**Brands:**
- Dynamic Health Tart Cherry Juice Concentrate
- Sports Research Tart Cherry Extract
- Jarrow Tart Cherry Concentrate

**Cost:** $15-25 / month

## Targeted Sleep Supplements

### 6. CBD: 15-50mg (For Anxiety or Pain)

**Timing:** 60-90 minutes before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Reduces anxiety (main benefit for sleep)
- ✅ Pain relief (if pain preventing sleep)
- ✅ May improve REM sleep
- ✅ Non-intoxicating (no "high")

**Dosage:**
- Start low: 10-15mg
- Standard: 20-30mg
- Higher anxiety/pain: 40-50mg
- Full-spectrum or broad-spectrum preferred (entourage effect)

**Legal status:**
- Federally legal in US (hemp-derived, <0.3% THC)
- Check local laws

**Quality matters:**
- Third-party tested (heavy metals, pesticides, potency)
- Reputable brands only
- COA (Certificate of Analysis) available

**Side effects:**
- Generally minimal
- Possible: drowsiness, dry mouth, diarrhea (rare)
- Drug interactions possible (CYP450 enzymes)

**Brands:**
- Charlotte's Web
- Lazarus Naturals
- NuLeaf Naturals
- CBDistillery

**Cost:** $30-60 / month

---

### 7. Ashwagandha (KSM-66): 300-600mg (For Stress)

**Timing:** With dinner or 2-3 hours before bed

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Reduces cortisol (stress hormone)
- ✅ Improves stress resilience
- ✅ Enhances sleep quality (indirect, via stress reduction)
- ✅ May increase testosterone
- ✅ Adaptogenic properties

**Dosage:**
- Standard: 300mg KSM-66 extract (2x daily)
- Or 600mg once daily (with dinner)
- Takes 2-4 weeks for full effects

**Not a direct sleep aid:**
- Works by reducing daytime stress
- Better sleep as a result of lower stress
- Take earlier in day or with dinner (not right before bed)

**Side effects:**
- Generally well-tolerated
- Possible: GI upset, sedation (if too high dose)
- Nightshade family (avoid if sensitive)

**Cycling:**
- 8-12 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off

**Brands:**
- KSM-66 is the patented, well-researched form
- Examine.com recommends KSM-66 specifically

**Cost:** $20-30 / month

## Melatonin: The Misunderstood Hormone

### When to Use Melatonin

**Appropriate uses:**
- Jet lag / travel across time zones
- Shift work (adjusting circadian rhythm)
- Delayed sleep phase disorder
- Short-term circadian disruption

**NOT appropriate for:**
- Daily, long-term use (suppresses natural production)
- As first intervention (fix sleep hygiene first)
- High doses (3-10mg is too much)

### Correct Dosage: 0.3-1mg (NOT 3-10mg!)

**Why low dose:**
- 0.3-1mg matches natural physiological levels
- Higher doses don't work better (may work worse)
- Most supplements are 3-10mg (way too high)

**Timing:**
- 30-60 minutes before desired sleep time
- For jet lag: Take at target bedtime in new time zone

**How it works:**
- Signals to body that it's nighttime
- Shifts circadian rhythm
- NOT a sedative (common misconception)

**Side effects (especially at high doses):**
- Next-day grogginess
- Vivid dreams / nightmares
- Suppressed natural melatonin production
- Hormone disruption (it is a hormone)

**Brands with low-dose options:**
- Life Extension 0.3mg
- Natrol 1mg
- Pure Encapsulations 0.5mg or 3mg (break in half)

**Cost:** $5-10 / month

### Cycling Melatonin

- Use only when needed (travel, schedule shift)
- Not for daily use
- If using nightly, taper off and address root causes

## Supplements to AVOID Before Bed

❌ **Vitamin B12** (especially methylcobalamin)
- Increases energy, alertness
- Take in morning

❌ **High-dose Vitamin C** (>500mg)
- Can be stimulating for some people
- Take earlier in day

❌ **Stimulating Adaptogens**
- Rhodiola, Cordyceps, Ginseng
- These increase energy
- Take in morning/afternoon

❌ **Pre-Workout Ingredients**
- Caffeine (obviously)
- Beta-alanine
- L-carnitine
- Take early in day

❌ **Tyrosine**
- Dopamine precursor, can be stimulating
- Take in morning

❌ **CoQ10** (for some people)
- Can increase energy
- If it affects your sleep, take in morning

## Monitoring Effectiveness

### Track These Metrics

**Subjective (daily):**
- Sleep quality rating (1-5)
- Time to fall asleep (estimated)
- Number of wake-ups
- Morning grogginess (1-5)
- Next-day energy (1-5)

**Objective (if wearing device):**
- Sleep score
- Deep sleep %
- REM sleep %
- HRV
- Resting heart rate

### Evaluation Timeline

**Week 1-2:**
- Baseline + initial effects
- Magnesium alone should show some improvement

**Week 3-4:**
- Full stack effects becoming apparent
- Sleep score should increase 5-15 points

**Week 5-8:**
- Consistent high-quality sleep
- HRV improvement (10-20%)
- Energy and recovery enhanced

**If no improvement by week 4:**
- Reassess sleep hygiene (environment, routine, consistency)
- Evaluate stress levels (may need stress management first)
- Consider underlying sleep disorder (sleep apnea, etc.)

## Safety & Interactions

### General Safety

✅ **Safe for most people:**
- Magnesium glycinate
- L-Theanine
- Glycine
- Tart cherry
- Apigenin (low dose)

⚠️ **Consult doctor if:**
- Pregnant or breastfeeding
- Taking medications (especially psychiatric, blood pressure, blood thinners)
- Kidney or liver disease
- Existing sleep disorders

### Drug Interactions

**Magnesium:**
- May reduce absorption of certain antibiotics (take 2-3 hours apart)
- May interact with bisphosphonates (osteoporosis drugs)

**CBD:**
- Metabolized by CYP450 enzymes (many drug interactions possible)
- Consult doctor if on medications

**Apigenin:**
- May interact with blood thinners
- Metabolized by CYP450 enzymes

**Ashwagandha:**
- May interact with thyroid medications
- May interact with immunosuppressants

## FAQs

**Q: Can I take all these supplements together?**
A: Yes, the core + advanced stack supplements are safe to combine. Start with one at a time to assess individual effects.

**Q: Will I become dependent on sleep supplements?**
A: No, these are not habit-forming (except possibly melatonin suppressing natural production if used long-term). They support natural sleep processes.

**Q: How long until I see results?**
A: Magnesium and L-theanine: 1-2 weeks. Ashwagandha: 2-4 weeks. Glycine/Apigenin: Immediate to 1 week.

**Q: Do I need to cycle these supplements?**
A: Magnesium, glycine, L-theanine: Daily use okay. Apigenin, CBD: Consider cycling. Ashwagandha: Cycle 8-12 weeks on, 2-4 weeks off. Melatonin: As-needed only.

**Q: What if supplements don't help?**
A: First ensure sleep hygiene is dialed in (environment, routine, consistency). If still no improvement, consider underlying sleep disorder (sleep study may be needed).

**Q: Can I take these with prescription sleep medication?**
A: Consult your doctor. Generally, these supplements are mild and unlikely to interact, but professional guidance recommended.
