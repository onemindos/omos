## 🧠 The Science: Why It Works

Aromatherapy is often dismissed as pseudoscience, but the **Olfactory System** provides the most direct pathway to the brain.

1.  **Direct Connection:** The olfactory bulb has direct neural links to the **Amygdala** (emotion) and **Hippocampus** (memory).
2.  **Bypass:** Unlike sight or sound, smell does _not_ pass through the Thalamus (the relay station). It hits the emotional center immediately.
3.  **Pavlovian Anchoring:** Because of this connection, you can "program" your brain to enter a specific state (e.g., Focus, Sleep) instantly by anchoring it to a scent.

## 🌿 Core Compounds & Protocols

We don't just "smell things." We use specific compounds for specific desired states.

### 1. The "Deep Work" Protocol (Focus)

- **Compound:** **Peppermint** (_Mentha piperita_) or **Rosemary** (_Rosmarinus officinalis_).
- **Mechanism:** Increases beta-wave activity in the brain. Rosemary contains _1,8-cineole_, which is proven to increase acetylcholine (neurotransmitter for memory).
- **Usage:** Diffuse 30 mins before a work block.
- **OneMind Trigger:** When `Focus Mode` activates, the diffuser turns on via smart plug.

### 2. The "Decompression" Protocol (Sleep/Calm)

- **Compound:** **Lavender** (_Lavandula_) or **Bergamot**.
- **Mechanism:** Linalool (a terpene in lavender) acts on GABA receptors (same as Valium), actively lowering heart rate and blood pressure.
- **Usage:** Shower Steamers or Pillow Spray.

### 3. The "Morning Activation" Protocol (Energy)

- **Compound:** **Eucalyptus** or **Lemon**.
- **Mechanism:** Bronchodilator. Opens the airways for oxygen intake (Eucalyptus). Terpenes in citrus are stimulating.
- **Usage:** **Shower Steamers.** The hot steam vaporizes the oil instantly, creating a "gas chamber" of activation.

## 🚿 Shower Aromatherapy (The Steam Method)

This is the most efficient delivery system for physical recovery (opening sinuses, relaxing muscles).

**The Tool:** **Shower Steamers / Pads**.
These are tablets (baking soda + essential oils) you place on the floor of the shower (not in the direct stream).

- **How it works:** Hot water hits the tablet -> Dissolves -> Oils vaporize -> You inhale deep concentrations.

**Recommendation:** Look for "Menthol + Eucalyptus" for recovery (Tactical Games prep) or "Lavender" for evening wind-down.
