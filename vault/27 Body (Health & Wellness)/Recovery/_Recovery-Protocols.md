Optimize recovery and regeneration. This area manages **active recovery modalities, restoration practices, and performance recovery protocols**.

## What Goes Here

- Heat therapy (sauna, steam room, hot tub)
- Massage & bodywork (deep tissue, sports massage, foam rolling)
- Compression therapy (Normatec, compression gear)
- Stretching & mobility routines
- Red light therapy
- Breathwork for recovery
- Floatation / sensory deprivation
- Recovery tracking & logs
- Deload protocols
- Injury rehabilitation
