# Different Main Types of Juicers

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Performance, Nutrition, Equipment

## Overview

Guide to choosing the right juicer for your needs.

---

## Juicer Types

### 1. Centrifugal Juicers

**How it works:** Uses a fast-spinning blade to chop produce and spin the juice out through a mesh filter.

**Pros:**
- Fast juicing process
- Usually more affordable
- Easy to use

**Cons:**
- Produces more foam/oxidation
- Louder operation
- Less efficient with leafy greens
- Shorter shelf life for juice

**Best for:** Beginners, quick morning juices

---

### 2. Masticating (Cold Press) Juicers

**How it works:** Uses a single auger to slowly crush and press produce.

**Pros:**
- Higher juice yield
- Less oxidation = longer shelf life
- Handles leafy greens well
- Quieter operation
- Can make nut milks, baby food

**Cons:**
- Slower juicing process
- Higher price point
- Takes more prep time (smaller feed chute)

**Best for:** Serious juicers, maximizing nutrition

---

### 3. Twin Gear (Triturating) Juicers

**How it works:** Uses two interlocking gears to crush produce.

**Pros:**
- Highest juice yield
- Maximum nutrient retention
- Longest juice shelf life
- Extremely versatile

**Cons:**
- Most expensive option
- Slower process
- More complex cleaning
- Heavy/bulky

**Best for:** Professional use, maximum nutrition extraction

---

### 4. Citrus Juicers

**How it works:** Specifically designed for citrus fruits using a reamer.

**Pros:**
- Simple and effective
- Easy to clean
- Affordable

**Cons:**
- Only works for citrus

**Best for:** Orange juice, lemonade
