# Tech-Lab — Experiments & R&D

> *"Play is the highest form of research."*

---

## Purpose

Tech-Lab is the **experimentation center**. This area houses R&D, experiments, proofs of concept, and the sandbox for testing new ideas before committing.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Experiments** — Active tests, trials, explorations
- **POCs** — Proof of concept projects
- **Research** — Technical research, evaluations
- **Prototypes** — Early versions, mockups
- **Learnings** — Results, insights, conclusions

---

## Experiment Framework

| Phase | Activity |
|-------|----------|
| Hypothesis | What are we testing? |
| Design | How will we test it? |
| Execute | Run the experiment |
| Measure | Collect data |
| Analyze | What did we learn? |
| Decide | Continue, pivot, or kill? |

---

## Lab Rules

1. **Fail fast** — Quick experiments, quick answers
2. **Document everything** — Learnings are valuable
3. **Time-box** — Set clear limits
4. **Low stakes** — Sandbox environment
5. **Share learnings** — Even failures teach
