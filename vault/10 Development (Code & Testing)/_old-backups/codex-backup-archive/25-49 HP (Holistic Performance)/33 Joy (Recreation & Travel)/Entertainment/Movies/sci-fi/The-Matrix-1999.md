## Synopsis

Thomas Anderson lives a double life: by day a computer programmer, by night the hacker Neo. Contacted by legendary hacker Morpheus, Neo discovers the shocking truth—the world he knows is an elaborate simulation called the Matrix, created by machines to subjugate humanity. Neo must embrace his role as "The One" prophesied to end the war between humans and machines.
