## Synopsis

Six months after the events of The Matrix, Neo has embraced his role as The One. But the machines have launched an assault on Zion—the last human city. Neo must journey deeper into the Matrix, confronting upgraded Agents and discovering the truth about the prophecy, the Architect, and the true nature of the Matrix itself.
