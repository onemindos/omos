## Synopsis

The story follows Forrest Gump, a slow-witted but kind-hearted man from Alabama, who witnesses and unwittingly influences several major historical events in 20th century America. From teaching Elvis to dance, to fighting in Vietnam, to meeting three presidents, Forrest's simple approach to life takes him on an extraordinary journey—all while pining for his childhood love, Jenny.
