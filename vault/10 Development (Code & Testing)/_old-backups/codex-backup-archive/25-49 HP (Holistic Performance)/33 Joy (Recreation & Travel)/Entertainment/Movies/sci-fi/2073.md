## Synopsis

Set in a dystopian future of 2073, a narrator looks back at our current moment in history—the 2020s—warning us about the path humanity is taking. The documentary blends real archival footage with fictional future framing to examine how current political, technological, and social trends could lead to catastrophic outcomes.
