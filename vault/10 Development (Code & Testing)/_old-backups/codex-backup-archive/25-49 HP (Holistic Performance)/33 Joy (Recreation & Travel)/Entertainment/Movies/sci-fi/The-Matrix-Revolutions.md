## Synopsis

With Neo trapped between the Matrix and the real world, the Machines launch their final assault on Zion. Trinity and Morpheus must rescue Neo while the remaining humans make their last stand against the machine army. Neo realizes the only way to end the war is to confront the rogue Agent Smith, whose power now threatens both humans and machines.
