## Synopsis

27 years after the events of T2, a new future sends back warriors. The advanced Rev-9 Terminator targets Dani Ramos, a young Mexican woman destined to lead the future resistance. Grace, an augmented human soldier, is sent to protect her. Together with an aged Sarah Connor and a T-800 living as "Carl," they must stop the relentless machine.
