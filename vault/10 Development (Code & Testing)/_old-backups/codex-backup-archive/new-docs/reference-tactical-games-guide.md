## Overview

**The Tactical Games (TTG)** is a hybrid sport often described as "CrossFit meets Combat Live Fire." It combines high-intensity functional fitness with precision marksmanship under stress.

**Core Philosophy:**

> "To test the skills and readiness of tactical athletes from all backgrounds."
> It validates the "Part 2" of fitness—not just _being_ fit, but _using_ that fitness to perform a lethal or precision skill while heart rate is maximum.

---

## 🏋️‍♂️ Fitness Requirements

Stages (called "Battles") are designed to redline your heart rate immediately before or during shooting.

**Key Movements to Master:**

- **Stress Carries:**
  - Plate Carrier Runs (Wait: 15lbs Men / 12lbs Women)
  - Farmer's Carries (Heavier than you think: 150lbs+)
  - Sandbag Drags & Cleans
- **Grip Strength:**
  - Rope Climbs (Often 20ft+)
  - Static Holds
- **Obstacles:**
  - 6-8ft Wall Climbs (in full gear)
  - Barbed wire crawls
  - "Bag Over Bar" tosses (Sandbags: 100-150lbs)

**Standards:**
To be competitive, you should be comfortable with:

- Running 5 miles in kit (sub-50 min)
- Deadlifting 2x bodyweight
- Shooting accurately with HR > 160bpm

## 🔫 Gear List (The Loadout)

TTG has strict gear rules. You must finish with what you started with.

### Required Gear (The "Must Haves")

1.  **Plate Carrier:**
    - **Men:** 15 lbs (slick weight, no pouches included)
    - **Women:** 12 lbs
    - _Note:_ Weight is strict. Lightweight foam plates don't count unless you add weights.
2.  **Rifle (Primary):**
    - Caliber: .223 / 5.56 recommended
    - Optic: LPVO (1-6x or 1-8x) is the meta. Red dots work but struggle at distance (300-400y).
    - Sling: **Mandatory** 2-point sling (must retain weapon while climbing).
3.  **Pistol (Secondary):**
    - 9mm Striker Fired (Glock/Sig/Walther)
    - Holster: **Level 2 Retention Minimum** (Must not fall out while hanging upside down/running).
4.  **Magazines:**
    - 5 Rifle Mags (30rd)
    - 5 Pistol Mags (17-21rd)
5.  **Belt Setup:**
    - Sturdy Battle Belt (Inner/Outer system recommended)
    - Mag pouches (Esstac Kywi or similar for fast reloads)
    - Dump pouch (Vital so you don't lose mags on a run)

### Suggested Gear (The "Nice to Haves")

- **Boots/Shoes:** Salomon Quest 4D or Speedcross (Train in them!).
- **Apparel:** Durable pants (Crye/5.11) that won't rip on barbed wire. Knee pads are highly recommended.
- **Gloves:** Thin shooting gloves (PIG/Mechanix) to protect hands on rope climbs/walls.
- **Hydration:** Camelback (attach to plate carrier) - dehydration is the #1 killer.

## 🏆 Divisions

There is a division for every skill level.

| Division              | Description                           | Movement Standards                    |
| :-------------------- | :------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------ |
| **Intermediate**      | Entry level. Weights are lighter.     | Subs available for hard obstacles.    |
| **Tactical**          | Military/LE/First Responders.         | Moderate weights. No subs.            |
| **Masters (40+/50+)** | Age groups. Highly competitive.       | Adjusted weights for age.             |
| **Elite**             | Professional level. Heaviest weights. | No subs. Highest shooting difficulty. |
| **Sniper**            | Focus on long-range precision.        | Less cardio, more math/ballistics.    |
| **Team**              | 2-Person Teams.                       | Shared workload strategies.           |

## 🎯 Training Protocol (How to Prep)

**The "OneMind" Approach to TTG:**

### 1. The Gassing Session (Cardio + Shoot)

_Simulate stage stress._

- 10 Burpees
- Sprint 50y
- Pick up Rifle
- 5 Shots at 50y (Target: A-Zone)
- _Repeat 5 times._ Trigger control under fatigue is the skill.

### 2. The Heavy Carry (Grip + Core)

_Simulate movement._

- Wear Plate Carrier.
- Find a sandbag (80-100lbs).
- Carry 100m.
- 10 Box Step-ups.
- Do not drop the bag.

### 3. Dry Fire (daily)

- Practice mag exchanges.
- Practice finding the dot/reticle from high port.
- Practice transitions (Rifle to Pistol) smoothly.

## 🧠 Mental Game

TTG is 80% mental.

- **Chaos Management:** Something will go wrong (gun jam, dropped mag, missed shot). The winner is who recovers fastest.
- **Pacing:** Don't sprint the first 100 yards and then fail the shot. "Smooth is fast."
