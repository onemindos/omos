# 3D-printable applications

Legacy, here are powerful and creative ways to bring your logo to life beyond T-shirts—both traditional and 3D-printable applications:

---

### **Wearable & Personal Gear**

- **Hats & Beanies** – embroidered or 3D-printed emblems
- **Wristbands & Silicone Bracelets** – minimal, sleek brand representation
- **Pendants / Necklaces / Dog Tags** – 3D-printed in metal or resin
- **Rings** – minimalist Legacy crest engraved or embossed
- **Backpacks or Messenger Bags** – stitched or laser-cut logo patches

---

### **Office & Desk Gear (3D Print)**

- **Business Card Holders** – embossed with the Legacy logo
- **Desk Nameplates or Signs** – with lighting elements behind translucent prints
- **Pen Holders / Organizers**
- **Coasters / Drink Mats** – smooth resin with the glowing red design
- **USB Flash Drive Cases** – custom form with logo engraved

---

### **Home / Decor**

- **Wall Art / Plaques** – shadowbox or backlit 3D versions of the logo
- **LED Edge-Lit Sign with Acrylic** – logo engraved on clear acrylic, lit from beneath
- **Fridge Magnets** – simple, sharp branding with magnet backs
- **Keychains** – metallic or resin prints, ideal for everyday brand presence

---

### **Tech Accessories**

- **Phone Stand / Docking Station**
- **Mousepads** – with laser-printed logo
- **Laptop Stickers / Decals** – premium matte or gloss
- **AirTag Holders or Cases** – with the Legacy insignia molded in

---

### **3D Print Specialty Builds**

- **Challenge Coins** – engraved metal/resin coins symbolizing Legacy membership
- **Mini Totems / Statues** – collectibles or desk reminders of the brand
- **Custom QR Code Bases** – scannable links to your Legacy site or dashboard
- **Logo Puzzle / Interlocking Design** – gamified branding
- **Badge Clips / Name Tag Holders**

---

### **Packaging / Merch Inserts**

- **Logo Stamps or Seals** – wax seal or ink for letters and boxes
- **3D Printed Inserts for Product Boxes**
- **Custom Hangers or Clips** – branded clothing accessories

---

- Promo ideas that tie each item to a reward/gamified community system
