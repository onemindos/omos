## LLC Protection - How It Works

### What's Protected (Personal Assets)

From research (Nolo, SBA, Cornell Law):

> "All LLC owners are protected from personal liability for business debts and claims."
> "Creditors can't legally come after an LLC member's house, car, or other personal possessions."

**Protected personal assets:**
- Personal home
- Personal vehicles
- Personal bank accounts
- Personal investments
- Other separate LLCs

### What's At Risk (Business Assets)

If Acadacom is sued, these are exposed:
- Acadacom bank accounts
- Acadacom equipment/assets
- Revenue in Acadacom accounts

**But NOT:**
- Personal assets
- Legacy Restore LLC assets
- Legacy Platform Holdings LLC assets

### Piercing the Corporate Veil (When Protection Fails)

From Cornell Law School:

> "Courts maintain a strong presumption against piercing the corporate veil and only act when serious misconduct exists."

**What triggers piercing:**
- Mixing personal and business assets (commingling)
- Undercapitalization at incorporation
- Fraudulent creation to evade liability
- Using company as alter ego of shareholder

### How to Maintain Protection

| Do This | Why |
|---------|-----|
| Separate bank accounts | Business money ≠ personal money |
| Proper records | Meeting minutes, decisions documented |
| Adequate funding | Don't undercapitalize the LLC |
| No commingling | Don't pay personal bills from business account |
| Formal contracts | Written agreements with clients |
| Follow LLC formalities | Annual reports, maintain good standing |

## DBA Liability Note

**IMPORTANT:** DBAs do NOT provide separate liability protection.

A DBA is just a trade name, not a separate legal entity. All DBAs under Acadacom share the same liability pool.

```
LEGACY ACADACOM LLC
├── DBA: Zeus Delacruz      ← Same liability pool
├── DBA: One Mind Codex     ← Same liability pool
└── Affiliate Income        ← Same liability pool

If ANY is sued → ALL Acadacom assets at risk
(But personal assets and other LLCs still protected)
```

This is acceptable because all businesses under Acadacom are LOW liability.
