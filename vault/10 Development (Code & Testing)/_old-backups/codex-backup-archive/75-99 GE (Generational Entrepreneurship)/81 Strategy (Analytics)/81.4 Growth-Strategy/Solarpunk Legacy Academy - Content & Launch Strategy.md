## Project Management Framework

### Managing Multiple Work Streams

You have **7 major work streams** to coordinate:

1. **Platform Setup** (Circle.so, Stripe, domains)
2. **Content Creation** (courses, protocols, templates)
3. **YouTube Production** (filming, editing, publishing)
4. **Social Media** (LinkedIn, X/Twitter, Instagram, TikTok)
5. **Email Marketing** (newsletter, sequences, broadcasts)
6. **Community Building** (moderation, engagement, events)
7. **Business Operations** (LLC setup, banking, legal)

## Content Strategy Overview

### Core Principle: The Content Pyramid

```
                    🏔️ PINNACLE
                 Premium Products
              (Homestead Kit, Franchises)
                       ↑
                  EMPIRE TIER
              (1-on-1 coaching content)
                       ↑
                 BUILDER TIER
           (Masterclasses, deep-dives)
                       ↑
              FOUNDATION TIER
           (50+ courses, protocols)
                       ↑
                  FREE TIER
        (5 courses, community access)
                       ↑
              LEAD MAGNETS
      (YouTube, Social, Email freebies)
                       ↑
         AWARENESS CONTENT
    (YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X)
```

**Strategy**: Create awareness content (free, viral, shareable) that drives people into lead magnets, which convert to free tier, which upgrades to paid tiers.

### Content Pillars for Marketing

Map your **Four Pillars framework** to **content themes**:

1. **Generational Entrepreneurship (GE)** 🟡
   - Topics: Building LLCs, multi-generational business, wealth creation
   - Platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube long-form
   - Content type: Case studies, tutorials, frameworks

2. **Holistic Performance (HP)** 🔵
   - Topics: Life optimization, 9 domains, personal development
   - Platforms: Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts
   - Content type: Quick tips, transformations, daily practices

3. **Legacy Evolution (LE)** 🟢
   - Topics: Homesteading, family systems, generational wealth transfer
   - Platforms: YouTube, Instagram (visual homestead content)
   - Content type: Vlogs, tutorials, behind-the-scenes

4. **Innovative Technology (IT)** 🔷
   - Topics: Self-hosted AI, automation, sovereignty stack
   - Platforms: X/Twitter, YouTube, GitHub
   - Content type: Technical tutorials, demos, open-source

**Embrace**:

**Example Messaging**:

- ✅ "Build regenerative systems that serve your great-grandchildren"

### Content Strategy: 3 Video Types

#### 1. **Pillar Content** (Weekly long-form, 15-30 min)

**Purpose**: Deep education, SEO, authority building

**Examples**:
- "The Four Pillars Framework Explained (Complete Guide)"
- "How to Build a Self-Hosted AI Stack for Your Family"
- "Creating Multi-Generational Wealth: The Protocol Approach"
- "My Homestead Tech Setup: IoT, Solar, and Automation"
- "Why I Run 6 LLCs (And How They Work Together)"

**Formula**:
1. Hook (0-15 sec): Bold claim or question
2. Context (15 sec - 2 min): Why this matters
3. Framework (2-10 min): The system/protocol
4. Example (10-20 min): Real implementation (your life)
5. Action (20-30 min): What viewer should do next
6. CTA: "Join free tier at [link]"

**Production**:
- Film in batches (4 videos in 1 day = 1 month content)
- Use b-roll from homestead, office, family life
- Screen recordings for tech tutorials
- Simple editing (jump cuts, b-roll, text overlays)

#### 2. **Shorts/Clips** (Daily, 30-90 sec)

**Purpose**: Virality, algorithm, top-of-funnel awareness

**Examples**:
- "The 3 types of wealth you need to pass down"
- "Why self-hosted AI matters for your family"
- "How I organize 4.2GB of knowledge"
- "The protocol that changed my business"
- "Homestead tech hack #37"

**Formula**:
1. Hook (0-3 sec): Scroll-stopping statement
2. Value (3-60 sec): One clear takeaway
3. CTA (60-90 sec): "Follow for more" or "Link in bio"

**Production**:
- Extract from long-form videos (repurpose!)
- Film spontaneously (phone, 1-take)
- Trending sounds/formats when relevant
- Post 1-2 per day

#### 3. **Behind-the-Scenes** (Monthly, 10-20 min)

**Purpose**: Community building, authenticity, brand affinity

**Examples**:
- "Month in the Life: Building Solarpunk Legacy Academy"
- "Homestead Tour: Where the Four Pillars Meet Real Life"
- "How I Actually Use My Obsidian Vault Every Day"
- "Q&A: Your Questions About the Academy"
- "Family Business: Teaching My Kids the Protocols"

**Formula**:
- Vlog style (handheld, authentic)
- Show your process (not just outcomes)
- Vulnerability and lessons learned
- Invite community into your world

## Metrics & Success Tracking

### North Star Metrics

1. **MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue)**: Ultimate success measure
2. **Email List Size**: Leading indicator of MRR growth
3. **Free Tier → Paid Conversion Rate**: Efficiency metric (target: 5-10%)
4. **Content → Click Rate**: Traffic quality (target: 3-5%)

### Content Performance Dashboard

**Track Weekly** (in ClickUp or Obsidian):

```markdown
## Week of [Date]

### Traffic
- YouTube views: X (+Y% vs last week)
- LinkedIn impressions: X
- X/Twitter impressions: X
- Website visitors: X

### Engagement
- YouTube watch time: X hours (avg X min/view)
- LinkedIn engagement rate: X%
- X/Twitter engagement rate: X%
- Comments/replies: X

### Conversions
- Email signups: X (from Y channel)
- Free tier signups: X
- Paid conversions: X
- MRR: $X (+$Y vs last week)

### Top Performers
1. [Best YouTube video] - X views
2. [Best LinkedIn post] - X impressions
3. [Best Tweet] - X likes

### Learnings
- What worked: [insight]
- What flopped: [insight]
- Next week's test: [hypothesis]
```

### Pivot Indicators

**When to adjust strategy**:
- YouTube videos under 100 views after 7 days → improve SEO/thumbnails
- LinkedIn posts under 500 impressions → change hook style
- Email open rate under 20% → rewrite subject lines
- Free → Paid conversion under 3% → improve onboarding/messaging
- MRR growth stalled 2 weeks → launch promotion/campaign
