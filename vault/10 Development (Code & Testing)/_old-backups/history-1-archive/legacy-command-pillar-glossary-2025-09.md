📒** Legacy Command – Pillar Glossary (one-pager)**

**How to read it:**

**Pillar ▸ List** — *what lives here / primary purpose*

(⚡ = list you’ll touch most days, 🗄️ = mainly reference/archive)

⸻

🟢** Generational Entrepreneurship Ops**

**ListWhat it’s for**

⚡ **Founder Ops & Venture Execution**Your personal CEO command board: daily/weekly actions, design sprints, hiring, analytics pulls, etc.

**Marketing & Content Systems**Funnels, content calendars, campaign tasks, asset library.

**Finance & Capital Systems**Budgets, cash-flow models, credit lines, funding apps.

**Legal & Entity Management**Contracts, IP filings, state filings, compliance dates.

**Data & Analytics Management**Dashboards to track KPIs / BI reports (view inside Founder list if you prefer).

**Product Development & Launches**Idea → roadmap → launch checklists for every offer.

**Collaborative Entrepreneurship**JV deals, affiliate programmes, partner negotiations.

**Research & Market Intelligence**Competitive audits, blue-ocean notes, SWOT docs.

**Investments & Asset Management**Business-side investments (PE, VC notes, SAFE docs).

**Global Business Infrastructure**Intl. banking, cross-border licences, ops playbooks.

⸻

🔵** Holistic Performance Ops**

**ListWhat it’s for**

⚡ **Zeus – Lifeflow**Travel, hobbies, recreation, personal logistics.

**Zeus – Sovereignty**Personal legal, identity, insurance, risk protection.

**Zeus – Finance**Personal budgeting, debt, credit, investments.

**Zeus – Intelligence / Education**Courses, books, research pipelines.

**Zeus – Training**Skill practice (martial, creative, tactical).

**Zeus – Performance**Health metrics, supplements, lab work, recovery.

⸻

🟣** Legacy Evolution Ops**

**ListWhat it’s for**

⚡ **Delacruz – Homestead**Household projects, permaculture, renovations.

**Delacruz – Family**Parenting tasks, events, relationship rituals.

**Delacruz – Wealth**Family finance, estate transfer, generational plans.

**Delacruz – Sovereignty**Wills, trusts, custody, medical proxies.

**Delacruz – Legacy**Heirloom docs, journals, family media archive.

**Delacruz – Intelligence / Education**Kids’ schooling, tutoring, family-wide learning.

**Delacruz – Training**Family skills (swimming, firearm safety, languages).

**Delacruz – Performance**Family health routines, meal planning, activities.

⸻

🟠** Innovative Technology Ops**

**ListWhat it’s for**

⚡ **Launch Pad**One-click links & status pings for every app/service.

**Capture Hub**Inbox for ideas/links automatically piped in; triage centre.

**Automation Ops**Scripts, Zapier/Make flows, GPT build-outs (view = GPT Dir).

**Data & Security Ops**Storage map, backups (3-2-1), encryption keys, audits.

**Stack Ops**Master software directory, licences, renewal dates.

**Calendar Sync**Rules & webhooks bridging ClickUp ↔ Zoho calendars.

**Hybrid Email Directory**All domain inboxes + management tips.

**Studio Ops**Video gear inventory, OBS templates, scene checklists.

**Home Lab Ops**NAS builds, network diagrams, homelab experiments.

⸻

🟥** Legacy AI (Sub-workspace – team space)**

*Lives outside personal pillars; contains team-level service desks (Support Ops, Tech Ops, etc.).*

⸻

🔑** Cheat sheet**
- ⚡ Daily cockpit = Launch Pad (IT) + Founder Ops (GE) + Zeus Finance (HP)
- Weekly review = view “Next 7 days” across all Status ≠ Done lists
- Quarterly strategy = Founder Ops “CEO Directives” + Research & Market Intel
