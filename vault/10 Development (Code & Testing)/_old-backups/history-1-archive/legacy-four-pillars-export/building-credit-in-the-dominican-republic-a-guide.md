# Steps to Build Credit

### Set Up a Local Bank Account

- Task
: Open a bank account with a local Dominican bank using your USA or Canadian passport.

- Outcome
: A functional bank account ready for regular transactions to build credit history.

### Regular Deposits

- Task
: Make consistent deposits into your new bank account to show activity.

- Outcome
: A bank account with a growing balance, reflecting financial activity.

### Local Phone Service

- Task
: Sign up for a local phone service plan, estimated at $40 USD monthly, with providers like Claro, Altice, or Viva, and use your bank account for payments.

- Outcome
: Demonstrated regular payments contributing to credit activity.

### Secured Credit Card Application

- Task
: After bank account setup, apply for a secured credit card. Use it responsibly to be eligible for a regular credit card release after six months of timely payments.

- Outcome
: A credit card that evolves from secured to regular, building your credit rating.
