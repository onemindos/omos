When this is running well:
	1.	You live your life.
		You experience things, learn, decide, reflect.
	2.	Those experiences get distilled into the Codex.
		Notes, protocols, beliefs, systems.
	3.	The Meta-Cog layer reviews and shapes how its organized.
		Does this support HP/LE/GE/IT?
		Does this reflect our values?
		Does this help or clutter?
	4.	Legacy AI reads from that Codex to make decisions and suggestions.
		It can say: Based on your Codex, this option is more aligned.
	5.	Meta-Cog evaluates the AIs behavior.
		Was that a good recommendation?
		Should our rules change?
		How do we upgrade the system from this lesson?

This is literally a loop of meta-cognition:

learn  encode  organize  act  observe  refine the way you think  repeat. How to Explain This to Your Students (Simple Story)

Use this story:

Think of our lives and systems as a country.
		The One Mind is like the government and constitution of that country. It defines the rules of how decisions are made, how power is used, and what we value.
		The Legacy Codex is the national library  all our knowledge, laws, playbooks, and history.
		Legacy AI is the civil service + AI co-pilot that reads the library, follows the constitution, and helps us get things done.

The whole point is to build a civilization where humans and AI think together, under shared rules, using a shared knowledge base.

Even simpler version for slides:
	1.	One Mind  the operating system.
	2.	Meta-Cog  the rules of thinking layer inside One Mind.
	3.	Legacy Codex  the organized brain we all share.
	4.	Legacy AI  the AI team that reads the Codex and acts under the rules.

# One Mind OS  Stack Overview

## Layers

1. **Reality Layer**
   - Zeus, family, environment, business, community, world

2. **Knowledge Layer  Legacy Codex**
   - Organized truths, experiences, frameworks, protocols
   - This vault = the Codex

3. **Meta-Cognitive Layer  Meta-Cog (One Mind)**
   - One Mind Constitution (laws)
   - Cognitive Architecture (how we think)
   - Attention & Decision Rules

4. **Agent Layer  Legacy AI**
   - Mother AI (orchestrator)
   - Path Agents (HP/LE/GE/IT)
   - Automations & tools (n8n, Home Assistant, finance APIs, etc.)

## Simple Story
Humans and AI share:
- One knowledge base (Codex)
- One rule set (Constitution / Meta-Cog)
- One operating system (One Mind)
So we can think together as a single civilization.
