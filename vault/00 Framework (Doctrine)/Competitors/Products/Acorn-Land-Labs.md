## What It Does

Educational platform teaching self-sufficiency in the 5 basic physiological needs every human has:

1. 🥕 **Healthy Food** — organic produce, free-range chickens
2. 💧 **Fresh Water** — rainwater collection and storage
3. ⚡ **Clean Energy** — solar power, methane generation
4. 🏕 **Creative Shelter** — sustainable building methods
5. 🪴 **Green Sanitation** — composting, waste processing

**Mission:** "By connecting more directly to our basic needs, we build valuable skills, learn to appreciate agriculture, build healthy independence, increase our community resilience."

## Relationship to OneMind

**Acorn Land Labs is a philosophical sibling, not a software competitor.**

**Shared Values:**

- Solar-punk philosophy
- Technology serving human flourishing
- Self-sufficiency and sovereignty
- Generational thinking
- Decentralized, local approaches

**Different Domains:**

- Acorn = physical skills (farming, building, energy)
- OneMind = digital coordination (AI, automation, knowledge)

**Potential Integration:**

- OneMind could use Acorn principles for LE (Legacy Evolution) domain
- Acorn's 5 needs map to OneMind's HP (Holistic Performance) metrics
- FarmBot integration brings Acorn principles into OneMind IoT

**The Full Picture:**

```
Acorn Land Labs (Philosophy + Physical Skills)
              ↓
OneMind OS (Digital Coordination Layer)
              ↓
Physical Implementation (IoT, Robotics, Land)
```
