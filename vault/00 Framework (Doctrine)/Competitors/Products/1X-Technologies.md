## Weaknesses ❌

- **Hardware company** — builds robots, not software platform
- **No life OS** — no PKM, tasks, or knowledge system
- **Not available yet** — NEO still in development
- **Will be expensive** — consumer robots likely $10,000+
- **Proprietary AI** — closed system
- **Single focus** — robotics only
- **No integration platform** — robots are standalone

## Relationship to OneMind

**1X and OneMind are complementary, not competitive:**

1. **1X builds the hardware** (NEO, EVE robots)
2. **OneMind could orchestrate the software** (agent coordination layer)

**Potential Integration:**

- OneMind's Legacy AI could direct NEO robots
- NEO handles physical tasks; OneMind handles cognitive coordination
- Similar to how OneMind integrates with Home Assistant for IoT

**The Vision:**

```
OneMind OS (Brain)
     ↓
1X NEO Robot (Body)
```
