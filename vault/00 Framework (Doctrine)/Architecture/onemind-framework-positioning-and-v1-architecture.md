## 3. One Mind Framework v1.0 — Structure

### Layer 0 — Identity & Principles ("Why")

- **One Human, One Mind.** Everything — humans, AI, robots — plugs into a single cognitive layer instead of 100 siloed tools.
- **Solar-punk first.** Tech and automation exist to grow health, family, sovereignty, and community, not just productivity.
- **AI as companion, not overlord.** Agents assist, propose, and sometimes act — but always within human-defined guardrails.

*This becomes the opening chapter of your book.*

### Layer 1 — Realms & Pillars ("What life is made of")

**Four Pillars × Seven Kits:**

- **Pillars (columns):** GE, LE, HP, IT
- **Kits (rows):** INTEL, TRAIN, SYS, OPS, SEC, FIN, COMM

This grid defines your life's topology. Every project, task, note, agent, or metric belongs somewhere in this matrix.

**Examples:**

- "Homestead solar build" → HP / IT × SYS / OPS
- "Credit optimization" → GE × FIN / SEC

*This grid is your equivalent of PARA's P/A categories, but richer and more future-proof.*

### Layer 2 — Entities & Data Model ("The nouns")

Universal objects One Mind works with — across any software:

| # | Entity | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Areas** | Ongoing responsibilities (similar to PARA Areas) | Family, Health, Homestead, Legacy AI, Finance |
| 2 | **Projects** | Time-bound outcomes inside an Area | Launch community beta, Set up robotic greenhouse |
| 3 | **Workflows** | Reusable sequences for how work flows | Content pipeline, Weekly review, Agent-assisted research loop |
| 4 | **Tasks / Actions** | Atomic actions; touch your calendar or today list (GTD-compatible) | — |
| 5 | **Knowledge Nodes** | Notes, docs, Zettels, SOPs, patterns | How to dispute credit errors, Greenhouse layout v2 |
| 6 | **Interfaces** | Surfaces where humans interact | Apps (Obsidian, Circle, HA), dashboards, voice, robots |
| 7 | **Agents** | Human, AI, or robotic actors | Role / Scope / Autonomy level |
| 8 | **Metrics & Signals** | Numbers that define "better" | Net worth, bodyweight, sleep score, credit score, soil quality |

**Agent Properties:**

- **Role:** Researcher, Planner, Executor, Monitor
- **Scope:** Which Areas/Projects they can touch
- **Autonomy level:** Observe → Suggest → Act with confirmation → Act within budget

*These 8 entity types are your "API surface" for humans and robots.*

### Layer 3 — Flows ("The verbs")

Knowledge ↔ Workflows ↔ Actions ↔ Metrics ↔ Agents

#### 3.1 Knowledge Flow (PKM)

Inspired by LYT + Zettelkasten, but operational:

1. **Capture** — Raw inputs into an "Inbox" (ideas, links, scans, sensor data)
2. **Distill** — Convert into Knowledge Nodes (atomic notes, patterns, SOPs)
3. **Connect** — Link nodes to Areas, Projects, Workflows, Metrics
4. **Codify** — Turn mature knowledge into Codex pages (canonical references)

> Result: your Legacy Codex is just the stable, battle-tested subset of your Knowledge Nodes.

#### 3.2 Action Flow (Tasks & Projects)

Loosely GTD but AI-native:

1. **Intake** — A Project or Area receives a new intent ("Fix credit score", "Automate irrigation")
2. **Design** — Human and agents co-design a Workflow for it
3. **Decompose** — Workflow generates Tasks/Actions with owners and deadlines
4. **Execute** — Humans and robots complete actions; agents update status and metrics
5. **Review** — Weekly/monthly views bridge Metrics ↔ Projects ↔ Codex updates

#### 3.3 Intelligence Flow (Agents & Metrics)

*This is the unfair advantage vs old frameworks:*

**Sensors / Inputs:**

- Human logs, devices, apps (Apple Health, banking, CRM), IoT, robots

**Agents:**

- **INTEL agents** — Observe & summarize (trends, anomalies)
- **OPS agents** — Suggest next actions, re-prioritize workflows
- **SYS/SEC agents** — Guardrails: budget caps, time caps, security rules

**Outputs:**

- Updated Tasks, Workflows, Codex pages, and Alerts

> **For robotics:** a robot is just an Agent with extra actuators (can move stuff, not just click APIs) and harder safety rules inside the SEC kit.

### Layer 4 — Implementation Map (Tool-Agnostic)

One Mind always answers 3 questions separately from tools:

1. **Model** — How do you define Areas, Projects, Tasks, Knowledge, Agents, Metrics?
2. **Mapping** — In *this* stack, where does each entity "live"?
3. **Protocol** — How do they sync and talk?

**Zeus's setup:**

| Entity | Tool |
|---|---|
| Areas / Projects / Tasks / Knowledge | Obsidian + TaskNotes |
| Agents / Workflows | Letta + n8n |
| Metrics | Supabase + dashboards |
| Interfaces | LiveKit voice, Circle community, solar-punk dashboards |

**Someone else's setup:**

- Could be Notion + Google Calendar + Alexa + a spreadsheet
- **Same framework, different tools**

> Your book and public content always teach the framework, then show mapping examples for popular stacks.

## 4. Canonical Vocabulary

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Workflow** | A repeatable path that moves Inputs → Desired Outcomes across humans, agents, and robots |
| **Project** | A finite, outcome-driven effort inside an Area, powered by one or more workflows |
| **Area** | A long-term responsibility that must never drop (health, family, finance, craft) |
| **Task / Action** | The smallest unit of effort; can be done by a human, an AI agent (software), or a robot (hardware) |
| **Agent** | Any actor (human/AI/robot) with a defined role, scope, and autonomy level |
| **Knowledge** | Connected notes, references, and SOPs that inform actions |
| **PKM** | The discipline of turning raw information into Knowledge Nodes and updating the Codex |
| **Intelligence** | When Agents + Knowledge + Metrics combine to propose better actions |
| **Interface** | Any way you see/touch the Mind (apps, dashboards, voice, robots) |
| **Metrics** | The numbers that define "winning" for each Pillar/Area/Project |
| **Solar-punk** | The design principle that all tech + automation must increase life, community, and ecology — not just profit |

## The Meta-Cognitive Loop

When the system is running well:

1. **You live your life** — experience things, learn, decide, reflect
2. **Experiences get distilled into the Codex** — notes, protocols, beliefs, systems
3. **The Meta-Cog layer reviews and shapes how it's organized:**
   - "Does this support HP/LE/GE/IT?"
   - "Does this reflect our values?"
   - "Does this help or clutter?"
4. **Legacy AI reads from that Codex** to make decisions and suggestions:
   - "Based on your Codex, this option is more aligned."
5. **Meta-Cog evaluates the AI's behavior:**
   - "Was that a good recommendation?"
   - "Should our rules change?"
   - "How do we upgrade the system from this lesson?"

**The loop:**

```
learn → encode → organize → act → observe → refine the way you think → repeat
```
