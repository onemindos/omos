## Naming Conventions
### Prefix: "One"
*   Represents unity and integration
*   Single source of truth
*   Unified platform approach
*   One-stop solution
### Suffix Patterns
*   **Intel/Insights**: Intelligence and data
*   **Health/Guardian**: Wellness and security
*   **Finance**: Financial management
*   **Sync/Cortex**: Integration and coordination
*   **Optimize/Growth**: Improvement and development
*   **Evolution/Execution**: Transformation and action
