## Weaknesses ❌

- **Cloud-only** — no self-hosting option
- **No PKM** — tasks/calendar only, not knowledge management
- **Subscription required** — $19-34/month (no free tier)
- **No AI agents** — AI assists but doesn't act autonomously
- **No memory persistence** — context resets between sessions
- **Work-focused** — not a life OS (no personal domains like family, health)
- **No IoT/robotics** — digital productivity only
- **Vendor lock-in** — proprietary system

## Competitive Position

Motion is the **leading AI productivity tool** for task/calendar management. It excels at the "get things done" layer but lacks:

- Knowledge management
- Life scope beyond work
- AI agent autonomy
- Self-hosting/ownership

**Relationship to OneMind:**

- Motion could theoretically be an integration layer for OneMind (via API)
- Motion solves "what should I do next?" brilliantly
- OneMind solves "how does this fit into my entire life?"

**Choose Motion if:** You want polished AI scheduling and don't need PKM or self-hosting
**Choose OneMind if:** You want a full life OS with agents, knowledge, and ownership
