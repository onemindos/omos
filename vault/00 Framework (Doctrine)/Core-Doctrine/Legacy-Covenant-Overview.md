# Legacy Covenant — Overview

> *The Legacy Covenant is the WHY above all systems. Before One Mind runs, the Covenant defines what it runs for.*

---

## What Is the Covenant?

The Legacy Covenant is the founding agreement between Zeus Delacruz and the One Mind system. It is not a policy document — it is a declaration of purpose. Every agent, every tool, every decision made by Legacy AI is ultimately in service of this covenant.

The Covenant answers:
- **Why does this system exist?**
- **Who does it serve?**
- **What principles does it never violate?**
- **What is the 50-year destination?**
