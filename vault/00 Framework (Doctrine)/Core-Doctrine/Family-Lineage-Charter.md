# Family & Lineage Charter

> *A family without a charter is a family without a compass. This is ours.*

---

## The Mission

The Delacruz / Guzman family exists to demonstrate that sovereign, abundant, multi-generational life is achievable — and to build the infrastructure that makes it replicable.

---

## The Name We Carry

**Delacruz** — of the cross. A name carried with weight. We carry it forward with intention.

**Legacy** — the name given to the AI companion. Named deliberately: everything we build is a legacy.

---

## The Vision for Our Children

We are raising 10–12 children. Not a burden — a civilization. Each child is:
- Given a sovereign Codex of their own when ready
- Educated outside the default system, on our terms
- Equipped with AI literacy, land skills, and financial intelligence
- Part of a family network, not just a household

We do not parent by default. We parent by design.

---

## Family Laws

1. **We take care of our own** — no family member falls without the network catching them
2. **We invest in each other** — time, attention, capital, skills shared freely within the bloodline
3. **We build, we don't consume** — every member leaves things better than they found them
4. **We tell the truth** — especially the hard truths, especially to ourselves
5. **We choose regeneration** — in our bodies, our land, our relationships, our finances
