## What "Civilization" Means Here

Not a state. Not a corporation. A **network of sovereign families** who share:
- A common methodology (the Codex)
- A common AI infrastructure (Legacy / One Mind OS)
- Common laws and principles (the Covenant)
- Physical land nodes (homesteads, farms, community spaces)
- A shared commitment to regenerative, abundant living

Each family is sovereign. The network is voluntary. Legacy AI coordinates, never controls.
