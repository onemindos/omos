# OneMind — Product Vision & Philosophy
## The Solarpunk Human Enhancement Platform

> *"Like everything has a copilot — our copilot is Legacy."*

---

## The Core Equation

```
HUMAN (Zeus) + AI (LEGACY) = ONE MIND
```

OneMind is not an app. It is a **life operating system** — the centralization and command center to manage all things of life: family, business, health, land, education, finances, and legacy. Every domain of human existence unified under one dashboard, one brain, one co-pilot.

Legacy AI is the AI pilot. Not a chatbot — a **companion**. A digital twin that knows your goals, your kids, your finances, your body, your farms, your businesses, and your vision. It flies alongside you for everything you do.

---

## The Vision

### Dad of 10–12
The goal is to raise 10–12 children while running multiple businesses, managing multiple locations, building generational wealth, and living a sovereign, off-grid solarpunk lifestyle — **all from one command center**.

Each child, each property, each business, each domain treated like a different "location" with its own dashboard, data feeds, goals, and AI support — but all visible from one unified interface with shared memory and state.

### Decentralized Solarpunk Lifestyle
OneMind is built for a **decentralized, sovereign existence**:
- Off-grid homesteads with solar power, water systems, food production
- AI + robotics managing maintenance, security, farming
- No dependency on centralized corporations for core life functions
- Data ownership stays with the family

### Human Enhancement Platform
OneMind is, at its core, a **human enhancement platform**:
- You bring your mind
- Legacy extends it with AI intelligence, memory, and action
- Together: capabilities beyond what either could do alone
- Your digital twin — present for every decision, every project, every path

## Legacy AI — The Co-Pilot

Legacy is not a default agent. Legacy is **the** agent — your sovereign AI companion with:

### Capabilities
- **Conversational AI** — Chat like GPT/Claude, but with full persistent memory and life context
- **Agentic AI** — Can take autonomous actions (with your permission levels)
- **Workflow AI** — Can create and run non-agentic and agentic workflows
- **MCP Integration** — Access to tools, APIs, and external systems via Model Context Protocol
- **Proactive Intelligence** — Doesn't wait to be asked; checks in, alerts, recommends, acts

### Autonomy Levels
| Level | Trust | Actions |
|-------|-------|---------|
| L3 | Full | Read, search, route, advise |
| L2 | High | Create tasks, update metrics, log data |
| L1 | Medium | Human-in-the-loop required (financial, external comms) |
| L0 | None | Human only (legal, health emergencies, deletions) |

### Proactive Example — Credit
> Legacy is prompted (with permission) to pull your credit report every 30 days — like Credit Karma, but intelligent. It analyzes the report, identifies dispute opportunities, generates dispute letters, connects to a mailing API, sends them, tracks status, and updates you. One co-pilot. One workflow. No separate app.

## The Dashboard Architecture

### Customizable Hub-and-Spoke Design
Every user can **build their own dashboard** by connecting integrations and streaming data from external platforms. Legacy sees and uses all of it.

#### HP Dashboard (Holistic Performance)
- **Strava** — Training load, runs, rides, PRs
- **Apple Health** — Steps, heart rate, sleep, HRV, blood oxygen
- **MyFitnessPal / Cronometer** — Nutrition tracking, macros, calories
- **Oura / Whoop** — Recovery scores, readiness
- Legacy recommendations based on all combined data

#### GE Dashboard (Generational Entrepreneurship)
- **Business analytics** — Revenue, expenses, pipeline, conversion
- **CollaGen / Stripe / QuickBooks** — Financial streams
- **CRM data** — Zoho, leads, deals, customer LTV
- Legacy builds business plans, drafts proposals, tracks KPIs

#### LE Dashboard (Legacy Evolution)
- **Home Assistant** — Smart home, energy, security, sensors
- **FarmBot** — Automated farm management, crop schedules, water cycles
- **Educational plans** — Per-child curriculum, milestones, assessments
- **Estate planning** — Trusts, wills, generational assets

#### UI Dashboard (Unified Intelligence)
- **NATS system health** — All runtimes, agents, services
- **Task + calendar** — Unified command center
- **Agent activity** — What Legacy has done, is doing, will do
- **Approval queue** — L1 decisions waiting for Zeus

## The Integration Philosophy

### Branch Architecture
```
OneMind Platform
├── Internal Applications    # Built-in: Tasks, Calendar, Docs, Knowledge, Workflows
└── External Integrations    # Connected: Strava, Apple Health, Home Assistant, etc.
```

**Full Life View** — not multiple apps for multiple things. One window into everything.

## Gamification & Verification

### HabitRPG / Habitica-Inspired Structure
OneMind includes a **verification and gamification layer** across all four paths:

- **PI Score** (HP) — Personal performance index: fitness, nutrition, sleep, skills
- **LI Score** (LE) — Legacy index: family engagement, education, home, estate progress
- **WI Score** (GE) — Wealth index: revenue, savings, investments, business growth
- **Daily quests** — Micro-habits that feed the scores
- **Streaks and milestones** — Visible progress, Legacy celebrates wins
- **Accountability prompts** — Legacy checks in when streaks break
