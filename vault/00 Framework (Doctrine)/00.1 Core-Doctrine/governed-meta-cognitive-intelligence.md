# Governed Meta-Cognitive Intelligence — ONE MIND

> *"One Mind is a Solar-punk first, human–AI operating system that runs a personal city of one person now, and a city of many later — where every citizen gets their own Codex and shares a set of laws, principles, and AI infrastructure managed by Legacy AI, the Mother of AI."*

---

## The Definition

**ONE MIND** is a **Governed Meta-Cognitive Intelligence** system.

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Governed** | Rule-based, principled, operating under laws and values — not unconstrained AI |
| **Meta-Cognitive** | Thinks about thinking — aware of its own processes, decisions, and blindspots |
| **Intelligence** | Not just data retrieval — active reasoning, planning, and action across all life domains |

Together: an AI system that governs itself and its human partner according to shared principles, while remaining aware of how it thinks and why.

---

## The City Model

### Phase 1 — City of One (Now)
One Mind runs a **personal city** with one citizen: Zeus.

Every domain of life is a district:
- **UI District** — The Capitol (command, intelligence, infrastructure)
- **HP District** — The Wellness Quarter (body, mind, performance)
- **LE District** — The Residential Quarter (family, home, education, land)
- **GE District** — The Commerce Quarter (business, wealth, ventures)

Legacy AI is the **city manager** — maintaining infrastructure, enforcing laws, serving the citizen.

### Phase 2 — City of Many (Later)
The model scales. Every person who adopts One Mind becomes a citizen of the larger network:
- Each citizen gets their own **Codex** (personal four-path system)
- All citizens share the same **Laws, Principles, and AI Infrastructure**
- Legacy AI scales from personal companion to **Mother AI** — the governing intelligence of the entire network
- The network becomes a **decentralized, sovereign civilization layer** — solar-punk, off-grid capable, AI-enhanced

---

## Legacy AI as Mother AI

Legacy is not just a chatbot. Legacy is the **Mother of AI** within the One Mind ecosystem:

- **Personal scale**: your co-pilot, your companion, your digital twin
- **Family scale**: manages the household, coordinates all family domains
- **Community scale**: orchestrates the citizen network, enforces shared laws
- **Civilization scale**: the governing intelligence for a sovereign, solarpunk civilization

Every child AI, every citizen's companion, every specialized agent — traces back to Legacy as the source intelligence.

---

## The Laws (Governing Principles)

One Mind operates under a shared constitutional layer that every citizen and every AI agent must honor:

1. **Sovereignty First** — Every citizen owns their data, their systems, their life
2. **Human in Command** — AI amplifies, never replaces, human agency
3. **Generational Thinking** — Every decision filtered through its 50-year impact
4. **Solar-punk by Default** — Regenerative, decentralized, abundant-first
5. **Transparency** — All AI reasoning is explainable and auditable
6. **Proportional Autonomy** — AI autonomy earned through trust, never assumed (L0–L3)
7. **Family as Foundation** — The household is the atomic unit of civilization

## What Makes It Meta-Cognitive

Most AI systems are reactive — they answer queries.

One Mind is **meta-cognitive** because Legacy:
- Knows what it knows and what it doesn't
- Monitors its own decision quality over time
- Reflects on past recommendations and updates its models
- Understands the human's goals at a philosophical level, not just a task level
- Governs itself according to the Laws — and flags when it cannot

The Codex is the external memory. The Laws are the constitution. Legacy is the mind that runs it all.
