# Operating Rhythm — Execution Cadence

> *"A → Q → W → D: From vision to daily action."*

---

## Purpose

The Operating Rhythm defines **how work flows through time**. It's the temporal architecture that connects annual vision to daily execution.

---

## The Rhythm Stack

```
ANNUAL THESIS (Direction)
     ↓
12-WEEK ROADMAP (Themes & OKRs)
     ↓
WEEKLY PLAN (Scorecards & Priorities)
     ↓
DAILY EXECUTION (Focus & Ship)
     ↓
REVIEW/REFACTOR (Learn & Update)
```

---

## Annual Thesis (Yearly)

**Purpose:** Set direction and non-negotiables for the year.

**Contains:**
- Vision statement for the year
- 3-5 major themes/initiatives
- Non-negotiable commitments
- Pillar-level annual goals
- Legacy Score target

**Timing:** January (with Q4 prep in December)

---

## 12-Week Roadmap (Quarterly)

**Purpose:** Break annual vision into executable 12-week sprints.

**Contains:**
- 3-4 OKRs per pillar
- Key missions (projects) for the quarter
- Resource allocation decisions
- Milestone checkpoints (Week 4, 8, 12)

**Timing:** Week before each quarter starts

**Format:**
```
Q1 2025 ROADMAP
├── GE: [OKR 1] [OKR 2]
├── HP: [OKR 1] [OKR 2]
├── LE: [OKR 1] [OKR 2]
└── IT: [OKR 1] [OKR 2]
```

---

## Weekly Plan (Every Week)

**Purpose:** Translate quarterly goals into weekly priorities.

**Contains:**
- Top 3 priorities for the week
- Pillar scorecards (0-100)
- Scheduled deep work blocks
- Key meetings/commitments
- Balance check (Wheel of Life)

**Timing:** Sunday evening or Monday morning

**Template:**
```
WEEK OF [DATE]
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TOP 3 PRIORITIES:
1. _______________________
2. _______________________
3. _______________________

PILLAR SCORES (last week):
GE: ___/100  HP: ___/100
LE: ___/100  IT: ___/100
LEGACY SCORE: ___/100

DEEP WORK BLOCKS:
□ Mon: _______________
□ Tue: _______________
□ Wed: _______________
□ Thu: _______________
□ Fri: _______________
```

---

## Daily Execution (Every Day)

**Purpose:** Single focus, ship, log.

**Contains:**
- MIT (Most Important Task)
- Time blocks (minimum 90 min deep work)
- End-of-day log
- Next-day prep

**Timing:** Morning kickoff, evening closeout

**Daily Cycle:**
```
MORNING (15 min)
├── Review today's calendar
├── Identify MIT
└── Set 90-min deep work block

EXECUTION
├── Deep work first
├── Process inbox second
└── Meetings third

EVENING (10 min)
├── Log what shipped
├── Update task statuses
└── Prep tomorrow's MIT
```

---

## Review & Refactor

**Purpose:** Metrics, lessons, standard updates.

| Cadence | Focus | Duration |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Daily | What shipped? What blocked? | 10 min |
| Weekly | Pillar scorecards, priority check | 30 min |
| Monthly | Trend analysis, course correction | 1 hour |
| Quarterly | OKR review, next quarter planning | Half day |
| Annual | Year in review, thesis update | Full day |

---

## Balance Checks

At Plan/Review cycles, use these lenses:

- **Wheel of Life:** 8 life domains balanced scoring
- **Integral (I/We/It/Its):** All quadrants covered?
- **Maslow/PERMA-V:** Needs hierarchy satisfied?
- **Energy (Chakra lens):** Where is energy blocked?
