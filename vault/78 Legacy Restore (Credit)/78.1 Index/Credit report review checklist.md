**CREDIT REPORT REVIEW CHECKLIST**

1\. Is your name correct?

2\. Is your current address, correct? Is your current phone number,
correct?

3\. Are the previous addresses they have listed for you correct?

4\. Is your marital status listed correctly?

5 Is the employment history they have listed for you accurate?

6\. Is everything listed in the personal information section correct?

7\. Is there anything listed in the public record information? Is it
correct? Highlight the information you think may not be correct

8\. Are the accounts on the list still open? Review each item under the
credit account (trade account) section.

9\. Are all of the current balances, correct?

10\. Are accounts where you are an authorized user or joint owner
listed?

11\. Are zero balances recorded for debts discharged in bankruptcy? For
debts paid in full?

12\. Are you listed as a co-signer on a loan? Is this correct?

13\. Are accounts that you closed listed as "closed by the consumer"?

14\. Is negative information reported on each credit account correct?
Look for late payments and missed payments. Highlight those items you
think are not correct.

15\. Are any accounts listed more than once? Check to make sure the same
account is not listed multiple times in the collections section.

16\. Is old negative information still being reported? If yes, highlight
the information that has exceeded the negative information reporting
limit, which is usually seven years.

17\. Do you suspect that you have been the victim of identity theft
after reviewing your credit reports?
