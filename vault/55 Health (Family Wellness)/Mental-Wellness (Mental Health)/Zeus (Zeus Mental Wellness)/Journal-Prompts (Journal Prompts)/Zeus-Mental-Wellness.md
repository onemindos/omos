## Mental Sovereignty Principles

These are not rules — they are what I've learned about protecting my mind:

1. **Guard the morning.** The first 3–4 hours of the day determine the mental quality of everything after.
2. **Information diet matters.** What I consume mentally shapes my inner state. Not all inputs are equal.
3. **Identity before achievement.** I am not my output. Who I am to God and to my family comes before what I build.
4. **Process emotions, don't suppress them.** Name them, understand them, release them. Suppression is debt.
5. **Rest is infrastructure, not weakness.** Sunday is not slack — it's maintenance.
