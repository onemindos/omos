# Children — Emotional Development

> *Emotionally intelligent children become emotionally intelligent adults. We teach this on purpose.*

---

## What This Family Teaches

Every Delacruz child learns:

- **Naming emotions** — You can't manage what you can't name
- **Conflict is normal, not catastrophic** — Siblings fight; the skill is repair
- **Asking for help is strength** — No child carries it alone in this house
- **Identity comes from God and family** — Not from performance or approval from outsiders
- **Hard things are survivable** — Resilience is practiced, not inherited

## Age-Appropriate Emotional Milestones

| Phase | What We're Building Toward |
|-------|--------------------------|
| 0–3 | Secure attachment — child knows they are safe and seen |
| 3–6 | Vocabulary for emotions — mad, sad, scared, happy, embarrassed |
| 6–10 | Self-regulation basics — pause before reacting, ask for help |
| 10–13 | Identity formation — who am I outside of my performance? |
| 13+ | Emotional autonomy — they manage their inner world, we guide and witness |

---

## Family Conflict Protocol

When conflict happens between kids (or between Zeus and a child):

1. Pause — no one talks while activated
2. Name what happened without blame
3. Repair — what does each person need?
4. Return to connection

*This is not a lecture framework — it's a muscle. We practice it.*
