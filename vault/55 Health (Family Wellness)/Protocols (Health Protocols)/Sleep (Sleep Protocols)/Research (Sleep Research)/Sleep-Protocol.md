# Family Sleep Protocol

> *Sleep is not rest. Sleep is construction. Everything gets built or broken here.*

---

## Why This Document Exists

Zeus's 5:30–9 AM focus block only works if sleep is protected. A 5:30 AM start with 7–8 hours of sleep means **in bed by 9:30–10 PM**. This isn't aspirational — it's math. This document makes the math intentional.

For the kids: sleep is the single biggest driver of mood, learning, growth, and immune function in childhood. Protecting kids' sleep is as important as feeding them well.

### Zeus Sleep Hygiene Rules

- No screens after 9 PM — blue light suppresses melatonin
- Room dark and cool (65–68°F is optimal for sleep quality)
- No heavy food within 2 hours of bed
- No caffeine after 2 PM
- Wind-down routine: 30 min of low-stimulation (reading, prayer, light stretch)
- If sleep is disrupted — do not scroll. Read or breathe until sleep returns.

### Kids Sleep Hygiene Rules

- Consistent bedtime every night — weekends included (within 30 min)
- Wind-down routine: bath, book, prayer/gratitude, lights out
- No screens within 1 hour of bed
- Room dark and cool
- White noise machine for infants/toddlers
- Kids do not come out of their room after bedtime (except emergency) — this is a hard rule once they're old enough to understand it

## When Sleep Is Disrupted

### For Zeus

- One bad night: don't compensate with excessive sleep — maintain the schedule
- Chronic disruption (new baby): accept the season, adjust expectations, protect the structure as much as possible, use the 5:30 AM block for lower-intensity work
- Sleep debt: nap before 2 PM if needed, max 20 min

### For Kids

- Nightmares: calm reassurance, no prolonged engagement — they should return to their room
- Sleep regression (toddlers): normal at 18 months, 2 years — maintain routine, don't revert to co-sleeping as a permanent fix
- Illness: sleep rules are suspended during sickness — let them rest as needed, return to schedule when well
