# Dominican & Traditional Remedies

> *This knowledge came before the clinic. It came through the family. Keep it.*

## About This Document

These are remedies rooted in Dominican, Caribbean, and broader Latin folk medicine tradition. Some came from family directly. Some are well-documented in herbal medicine. All are worth preserving — both as health tools and as cultural heritage.

## Teas (Agüitas)

The Dominican kitchen runs on *agüitas* — medicinal teas made from plants. These are first-line responses to most common ailments.

| Tea / Plant | Spanish Name | Used For | How to Prepare | Safe for Kids |
|-------------|-------------|---------|----------------|--------------|
| Ginger | Jengibre | Nausea, digestion, cold/flu, inflammation | Slice fresh root, steep 10 min in hot water, add honey | Yes (mild dose) |
| Chamomile | Manzanilla | Stomach upset, anxiety, sleep, colic in babies | Steep dried flowers 5–7 min, strain | Yes |
| Peppermint | Yerba buena | Headaches, indigestion, nausea | Fresh or dried leaves, steep 5 min | Yes (age 6+) |
| Lemongrass | Hierba limón | Fever reduction, anxiety, digestion | Fresh stalks boiled 10 min | Yes |
| Soursop leaf | Hoja de guanábana | Immune support, sleep, inflammation | Dry leaves boiled 10 min | Adults only |
| Cinnamon | Canela | Blood sugar regulation, cold/flu, digestion | 1 stick steeped or boiled in water | Yes (small amounts) |
| Star anise | Anís estrella | Colic, gas, respiratory | 2–3 pods steeped 10 min | Yes (small dose) |
| Oregano | Orégano | Respiratory, antibacterial, cold/flu | Fresh or dried steeped | Yes |
| Eucalyptus | Eucalipto | Congestion, respiratory | Steam inhalation or dried in hot water | Adults / steam only for kids |
| Lime/Lemon | Limón | Immune, vitamin C, sore throat | Fresh squeezed in warm water with honey | Yes |

> **Histamine note**: Most of these teas are histamine-safe. Avoid adding lemon/citrus in large amounts if histamine is a concern.

## Fever Practices

Traditional Dominican approach to fever (before medication):

1. **Luke-warm cloth** on forehead and neck — not cold (cold causes shivering, raises internal temp)
2. **Sponge bath** with room-temperature water
3. **Hierba limón tea** — gentle fever-reducing properties
4. **Rest and fluids** — agua de coco (coconut water) is ideal hydration
5. **Monitor** — below 102°F in kids is manageable at home; above escalate
