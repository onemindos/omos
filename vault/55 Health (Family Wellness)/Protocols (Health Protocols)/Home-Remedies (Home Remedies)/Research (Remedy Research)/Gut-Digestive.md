# Gut & Digestive Remedies

> *The gut is the foundation. Fix the gut, fix everything downstream.*

---

## Why This Matters Extra for This Family

The household operates on a low-histamine protocol because histamine sensitivity is rooted in gut function — specifically the DAO (diamine oxidase) enzyme, which is produced in the gut lining. A compromised gut = less DAO = more histamine reactions.

Full gut health research: [Gut-Health.md](../../Medical-Research/Diet-Nutrition/Gut-Health.md)

This file is the *practical remedies* layer — what to actually do when gut issues arise.

---

## Common Situations & What We Do

### Bloating / Gas

| Remedy | How | Histamine-Safe |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| Ginger tea | Fresh ginger steeped 10 min, sip after meals | Yes |
| Fennel tea | 1 tsp dried fennel seeds steeped 10 min | Yes |
| Warm water + lemon | Small amount of lemon in warm water, morning | Caution (small amount) |
| Walk after meals | 10–15 min light walk stimulates motility | Yes |
| Abdominal massage | Clockwise circles on belly, gentle pressure | Yes |
| Reduce gas-forming foods temporarily | Beans, broccoli, cruciferous — reduce during flare | Yes |

### Constipation

| Remedy | How | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Hydration increase | Add 2–3 glasses water to daily intake | First move |
| Magnesium glycinate | 200–400mg at night | Gentle, effective, safe for adults |
| Prune juice or prunes | 4–8 oz or 4–6 prunes | Check histamine — some are fine |
| Ground flaxseed | 1 tbsp in water or food daily | Soluble fiber support |
| Movement | Any physical activity stimulates gut motility | |
| Castor oil | Traditional — 1 tsp in adults (rare use) | Old-school Dominican |

### Nausea

| Remedy | How | Histamine-Safe |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| Ginger tea | Fresh, not bottled | Yes |
| Chamomile tea | Mild and soothing | Yes |
| BRAT diet | Bananas, Rice, Applesauce, Toast — easy on stomach | Yes |
| Cold cloth on neck | Reduces nausea signal | Yes |
| Acupressure P6 point | Inner wrist, 3 fingers from crease, press firmly | Yes |

### Diarrhea

| Remedy | How | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|
| Hydration + electrolytes | Water + pinch sea salt + small amount honey | Critical — dehydration risk |
| BRAT diet | Bananas, rice, applesauce, toast | Binding foods |
| Chamomile tea | Anti-inflammatory, gut-soothing | |
| Avoid dairy, fatty foods, raw vegetables | Until resolved | |
| Escalate | If blood present, fever above 102, lasts more than 48 hours in kids | Doctor |

### Heartburn / Acid Reflux

| Remedy | How | Histamine-Safe |
|--------|-----|---------------|
| Baking soda | 1/4 tsp in 8 oz water — occasional only | Yes |
| Aloe vera juice | 1–2 oz inner gel or pure aloe juice | Yes |
| Ginger tea | Sip slowly | Yes |
| Elevate head of bed | Sleep with head raised if chronic | Yes |
| Avoid: tomatoes, citrus, spicy food, large meals before bed | | |

## Gut Healing Support (Ongoing)

For general gut maintenance and DAO enzyme support:

- **Bone broth** — homemade, cooked same day (not slow-cooker overnight which raises histamine). Glutamine-rich, gut lining repair.
- **Collagen peptides** — neutral flavor, dissolves in any liquid, supports gut lining
- **Probiotic foods (histamine-safe strains only)** — not all probiotics are safe for histamine sensitivity. See [Gut-Health.md](../../Medical-Research/Diet-Nutrition/Gut-Health.md) for strain guidance.
- **Cooked vegetables over raw** — easier on an inflamed gut
- **Chew thoroughly** — digestion starts in the mouth; rushed eating = poor breakdown = more gut distress

|-------|----------------|-------------------|
| Gas/colic (infants) | Chamomile tea (small amount), bicycle legs, abdominal massage | Inconsolable crying >3 hrs |

| Diarrhea (any age) | Electrolytes, BRAT, chamomile | Blood, fever, >48 hours |
