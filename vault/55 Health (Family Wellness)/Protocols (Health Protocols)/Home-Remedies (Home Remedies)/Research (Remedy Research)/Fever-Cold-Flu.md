# Fever, Cold & Flu Protocol

> *Know what to do before the panic. This is the plan.*

---

## Fever Management

### Thresholds

| Person | Low-Grade | Watch | Act | Emergency |
|--------|----------|-------|-----|-----------|
| Infants under 3 months | Any fever | — | Call doctor immediately | ER if 100.4°F+ |
| Kids 3 months–3 years | Under 101°F | 101–103°F | 103°F+ call doctor | 104°F+ or seizure → ER |
| Kids 3 years+ / Adults | Under 101°F | 101–103°F | 103°F+ | 104°F+ or not responding |

### What We Do First (before medication)

1. **Luke-warm cloth** on forehead, neck, armpits — not ice cold
2. **Room-temperature sponge bath** if fever above 102°F
3. **Hydration** — coconut water, water, herbal tea (chamomile, hierba limón)
4. **Rest** — no screens, quiet environment
5. **Light clothing** — don't bundle up, let the body breathe

### If Medication Is Used

- Children: weight-based dosing per label — never aspirin for kids under 18
- Adults: ibuprofen (reduces inflammation) or acetaminophen (reduces fever/pain)
- Document time and dose in the child's health profile

## Cold Protocol

### First 24–48 hours (onset)

- **Zinc lozenges** — start within first 24 hours of symptoms to reduce duration
- **Vitamin C** — 500–1000mg (histamine note: avoid high-dose liposomal C with citrus base)
- **Raw honey** — 1 tsp straight or in warm tea, natural antibacterial
- **Garlic** — raw crushed clove in food or with honey
- **Steam** — boil water with eucalyptus leaves or drops, lean over with towel for 10 min
- **Salt water gargle** — 1/4 tsp sea salt in 8 oz warm water, 3x/day for sore throat
- **Nasal rinse** — saline rinse or neti pot to clear congestion

### Histamine-Safe Cold Remedies

When someone is histamine-sensitive, these are the safe options:

- Fresh ginger tea (not store-bought, no additives)
- Raw honey (small amounts)
- Chamomile tea
- Fresh garlic
- Salt water
- Steam inhalation
- Avoid: citrus in large amounts, fermented elderberry syrup, vinegar-based remedies

---

## Flu Protocol

Flu is more aggressive than a cold. Signs: sudden onset, body aches, high fever, fatigue, chills.

### Immediate

1. Rest — completely. No pushing through.
2. Hydration — minimum 8–10 glasses of water or electrolyte fluid daily
3. Bone broth (homemade, histamine-safe: made same day, not slow-cooked overnight)
4. Fever management per above thresholds
5. Isolate from newborns/infants in the home

### Escalate to Doctor If

- Fever above 104°F or fever that breaks and returns
- Difficulty breathing or shortness of breath
- Confusion, severe headache, stiff neck
- Symptoms improve then suddenly worsen
- Child under 2 with flu symptoms
- Any infant under 3 months

## Kids — Special Notes

| Age | Safe Remedies | Cautions |
|-----|--------------|---------|
| 0–6 months | Breast milk, saline drops, nasal bulb, fever thresholds (see above) | No honey under 12 months |
| 6–12 months | Chamomile tea (small amount), saline rinse, honey (after 12 months) | No OTC cold meds |
| 1–3 years | Honey, ginger tea (mild), chamomile, steam | No aspirin, caution with mint for under 2 |
| 3–6 years | Most teas (mild), zinc drops, vitamin C | Dose carefully per weight |
| 6+ years | Full tea protocol, zinc lozenges, vitamin C | Full salt water gargle |
