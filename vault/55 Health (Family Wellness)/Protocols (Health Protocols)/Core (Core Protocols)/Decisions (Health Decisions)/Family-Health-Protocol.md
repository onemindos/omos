# Delacruz Family Health Protocol

> *Health is not a product. It's a system. This is ours.*

---

## Overview

The Delacruz family follows a **health-conscious, low-histamine household protocol**. This was established due to histamine sensitivity in the household (see `Zeus-Health.md`). Over time this expands to cover nutrition, fitness, sleep, and mental wellness for all five family members.

This document is the master reference. Individual profiles are in `Family-Health-Profiles/`.

---

## Household Rule #1 — Low-Histamine Kitchen

The household kitchen operates as a **low-histamine environment** by default.

Key rules:
- Fresh proteins cooked same day (no aged, fermented, or leftover proteins)
- No aged cheeses
- No alcohol in cooking or consumption
- No vinegar-based condiments (ketchup, mustard, pickles)
- No processed/cured meats (salami, hot dogs, deli meats)
- Tomatoes, spinach, and eggplant limited or avoided
- Citrus minimized (lemon sparingly, oranges avoided)

Full food reference: [Low-Histamine Foods Guide](../Medical-Research/Diet-Nutrition/low-histamine-foods-guide.md)

## Family Nutrition Framework

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|--------------|
| Whole foods first | Real ingredients, minimal processing |
| Plant-forward | Heavy vegetables, varied starches, beans and legumes |
| Quality proteins | Fresh chicken, turkey, eggs, lamb, beef — cooked and eaten same day |
| Safe fats | Olive oil, avocado, coconut oil |
| Minimize sugar | No sodas, minimal added sugar, natural sweeteners only |
| Hydration | Water as the default drink — not juice, not soda |
| Dominican staples | Mangú, arroz con habichuelas, root vegetables, yuca — these are heritage and health |

## Children's Health Approach

| Phase | Approach |
|-------|---------|
| Infants/toddlers | Breastfeeding as default when possible; whole food introduction early |
| Ages 3–8 | Involve in food prep; explain why the family eats the way it does |
| Ages 8–12 | Health literacy begins — teach them to read labels, understand nutrition |
| Ages 13+ | Own their health; understand the histamine protocol; know their own body |

**The goal**: Children who understand food as information for the body, not just entertainment. This is taught, not assumed.

## What Gets Passed to Each Child

Every child when they leave the household receives:
- Their personal health profile (`Family-Health-Profiles/[Name]-Health.md`)
- A copy of this protocol
- Knowledge of the histamine sensitivity and what to watch for in themselves
- Basic nutritional intelligence — whole foods, quality fats, hydration

## Vaccine & Medical Decision Framework

The family takes an **informed and research-based** approach to medical decisions. Research is documented in `Medical-Research/Vaccine-Research/`.

- Medical decisions are made with full information review, not default compliance
- Exemption documentation is on file where applicable
- Each child's vaccination record is tracked in their health profile
