### Dental Protocol (Daily)

**Kids:**
- Begin brushing at first tooth — water only until age 2, tiny smear of fluoride-free toothpaste 2–3
- Brush twice daily — morning and before bed
- Floss when teeth touch (usually age 2–3)
- No juice or milk in bottles at bedtime — water only after brushing
- Mouthwash: age 6+ (must be able to spit reliably)

**Zeus:**
- Brush twice daily, 2 minutes minimum
- Floss daily — before bed
- Oil pulling (optional, traditional practice) — 1 tsp coconut oil swished 5–10 min in morning
- No eating after brushing at night

### Signs to Watch For (Kids)

- Squinting at the TV or books
- Sitting very close to screens
- Rubbing eyes frequently outside of tiredness
- Head tilting to see better
- Complaints of headaches after reading or screen time
- One eye turning in or out

### Screen Time & Eye Health

- **20-20-20 rule**: every 20 minutes of screen time, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds
- Natural light exposure daily — outdoor time has protective effect against myopia progression in children
- No screens in the dark — always ambient light when using a screen
- Enforce screen-free periods — especially before bed (covered in [Sleep-Protocol.md](../Sleep/Sleep-Protocol.md))
