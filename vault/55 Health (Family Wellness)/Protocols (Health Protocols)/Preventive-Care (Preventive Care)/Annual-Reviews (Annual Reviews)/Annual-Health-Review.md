# Annual Family Health Review

> *Once a year, stop and look at the whole picture. This is the audit.*

---

## When to Run This

**Each January** — or at the start of any new year. Budget roughly one month to complete all appointments and reviews. Update each family member's health profile after.

## Protocol Review (Annual)

At the end of each year's audit, review and update:

- [ ] Low-histamine protocol — anything to tighten or relax?
- [ ] Supplement stack — what's working, what to stop, what to add?
- [ ] Sleep protocol — is the family actually sleeping well?
- [ ] Meal planning — is the weekly structure working?
- [ ] Mental wellness — any concerns from the year with any child?
- [ ] Emergency contacts — all current, no outdated providers?
- [ ] Insurance — still covered, any gaps?
- [ ] Home environment — any new concerns (mold, air, water)?
