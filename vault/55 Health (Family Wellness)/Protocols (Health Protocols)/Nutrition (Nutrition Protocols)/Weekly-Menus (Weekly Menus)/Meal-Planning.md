# Family Meal Planning Framework

> *Rules without meals are just restrictions. This is what we actually eat.*

---

## The Framework

The family protocol has rules (see [Family-Health-Protocol.md](../Core/Family-Health-Protocol.md)). This document makes those rules into actual food — real meals, a weekly rhythm, and a batch cooking approach that works for a busy household with young kids.

**Core constraint**: Low-histamine household. Fresh proteins same day. No aged/fermented/processed meats.

---

## Weekly Meal Structure

| Day | Protein Focus | Starch | Notes |
|-----|--------------|--------|-------|
| Monday | Chicken (roasted or stewed) | Rice or yuca | Batch cook rice for 2 days |
| Tuesday | Eggs (frittata, scrambled) | Plantains (tostones or maduros) | Light protein day |
| Wednesday | Ground turkey (picadillo) | Arroz con habichuelas | Dominican staple day |
| Thursday | Lamb chops or beef | Root vegetables (batata, yautía) | Higher protein day |
| Friday | Fish (fresh only) | White rice or corn | Keep it simple end of week |
| Saturday | Flexible / leftovers repurposed | Whatever was bought fresh | Market day — shop for the week |
| Sunday | Family meal — roast chicken or pernil (pork) | Full spread | Sunday Family Day meal — this one matters |

> Adjust as needed — this is a rhythm, not a prison. The constraint is: **protein must be fresh that day**.

## Dominican Staples That Fit the Protocol

These are heritage foods that are also protocol-compliant — eat them proudly:

| Dish | Notes | Histamine Status |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Arroz blanco (white rice) | Safe, filling, base for everything | Safe |
| Habichuelas guisadas (stewed beans) | Fresh-cooked, not canned | Safe (cook fresh) |
| Mangú (mashed plantains) | Boiled green plantains with olive oil and onion | Safe |
| Tostones (fried green plantains) | Fried in avocado or coconut oil | Safe |
| Maduros (sweet ripe plantains) | Pan-fried in coconut oil | Safe |
| Pollo guisado (stewed chicken) | Fresh chicken, sofrito-based (no vinegar in sofrito) | Safe (check sofrito) |
| Sancocho | Fresh proteins, root vegetables, herbs | Safe (fresh only — no leftovers next day) |
| Yuca con mojo | Boiled yuca with olive oil and garlic | Safe |
| Batata (sweet potato) | Roasted, boiled, mashed | Safe |
| Ensalada verde (green salad) | Romaine, cucumber, olive oil — keep simple | Safe |

**Sofrito note**: Traditional Dominican sofrito can include vinegar (recao, ají). Make family sofrito without vinegar — use olive oil, garlic, onion, recao (culantro), fresh ajíes, salt.

## Breakfast Ideas (Low-Histamine Safe)

| Meal | Components | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Mangú con huevo | Mashed plantains + fried eggs + sautéed onion | Classic Dominican breakfast |
| Avocado toast | Fresh bread + avocado + salt + olive oil | Simple and safe |
| Oatmeal | Rolled oats + honey + banana | No dried fruit, no citrus zest |
| Egg scramble | Eggs + sautéed vegetables (onion, peppers, zucchini) | Keep veggie-forward |
| Fresh fruit bowl | Mango, watermelon, pear, apple | Avoid citrus and strawberries |
| Rice porridge (congee-style) | Leftover rice + coconut milk + honey | Gentle, filling |

## Lunch Ideas

| Meal | Components | Notes |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Rice bowl | White rice + beans + fresh protein + avocado | Keep it simple |
| Chicken soup | Fresh chicken, vegetables, herbs — same-day broth | Not slow-cooker — histamine builds |
| Yuca bowl | Boiled yuca + olive oil + garlic + protein | Filling, nutrient-dense |
| Salad with fresh protein | Romaine + cucumber + grilled chicken + olive oil dressing | No vinegar in dressing |
| Plantain with eggs | Tostones or maduros + fried or scrambled eggs | Quick, satisfying |

## Batch Cooking Approach

What to prep on **Saturday or Sunday** for the week:

- **Dry beans** — soak overnight Saturday, cook Sunday, store in fridge (use within 3–4 days)
- **Rice** — cook large batch, refrigerate, reheat as needed
- **Root vegetables** — boil batata, yuca, yautía and refrigerate (use within 2 days)
- **Plantains** — slice and freeze for tostones; keep ripe ones for maduros
- **Sofrito** — make a batch without vinegar, freeze in ice cube trays for easy use
- **Proteins** — do NOT batch cook proteins ahead (histamine risk). Buy fresh and cook same day.

## Seasonal Rotation Principle

The family will rotate emphasis seasonally — winter = root vegetables and warming stews, summer = fresh produce and lighter proteins. Full seasonal guide: [Seasonal-Food-Rotation.md](../../Medical-Research/Diet-Nutrition/Seasonal-Food-Rotation.md)

## Grocery Framework

**Weekly staples always in the house:**
- Fresh proteins (chicken, turkey, eggs, lamb, beef)
- White rice (bulk)
- Dried beans (black, red, pinto)
- Green and ripe plantains
- Avocados
- Garlic, onion, fresh herbs (recao, cilantro, parsley)
- Root vegetables (yuca, batata, yautía)
- Olive oil, coconut oil
- Honey (raw, local preferred)
- Fresh vegetables (zucchini, peppers, cucumber, romaine, carrots)
- Fresh fruit (mango, watermelon, apples, pears, bananas)
