# Toxin Reduction

> *Most "clean" homes are full of chemicals. Most people don't know it.*

---

## Non-Toxic Cleaning Products

### The Problem With Conventional Products

Standard cleaning products — bleach, Lysol, Febreze, scented sprays, fabric softener — contain:
- Quaternary ammonium compounds ("quats") — linked to respiratory irritation and microbiome disruption
- Synthetic fragrances — contain dozens of undisclosed chemicals, often VOCs and endocrine disruptors
- Chlorine bleach — produces chloroform and other DBPs when used in enclosed spaces

Children are more vulnerable: smaller body mass + still-developing lungs, liver, and hormonal systems.

### What to Use Instead

| Product Need | Safe Alternative | Notes |
|-------------|-----------------|-------|
| All-purpose cleaner | Branch Basics concentrate + water | Enzyme-based, fragrance-free, EPA Safer Choice |
| Disinfecting | 3% hydrogen peroxide in spray bottle | Natural, kills most pathogens, no toxic residue |
| Glass / mirrors | Distilled white vinegar + water (50/50) | Streak-free, no fragrance |
| Floors (mopping) | Castile soap + hot water | Dr. Bronner's unscented |
| Toilet | Baking soda + vinegar or Branch Basics | Avoid blue-dye tabs in tank |
| Dish soap | Branch Basics or Seventh Generation Free & Clear | Fragrance-free only |
| Laundry detergent | Branch Basics or Molly's Suds | No synthetic fragrance, no optical brighteners |
| Fabric softener | **None** — or wool dryer balls | Fabric softeners coat fabric in chemicals |
| Air fresheners | **None** — or beeswax candles | Synthetic air fresheners are concentrated VOC sources |

> **Rule**: If it has "fragrance" in the ingredient list and is not essential-oil-based, it doesn't come in the home.

## Personal Care for Kids

### Standards for Children's Products

Kids absorb more through skin per body weight than adults. Anything going on skin enters the body.

**What to avoid:**
- Synthetic fragrance in any form
- Parabens (preservatives — endocrine disruptors)
- Phthalates (fragrance carriers — endocrine disruptors)
- SLS/SLES (sodium lauryl/laureth sulfate — skin irritant, gut disruption when ingested)
- Talc (in baby powder — lung irritant, possible carcinogen)
- Dimethicone and heavy silicones in baby products

## EMF Reduction

*Also referenced in [Sleep-Protocol.md](../Sleep-Protocol.md)*

EMF (electromagnetic fields) from wireless devices and routers — the science is ongoing. The precautionary approach is low-cost.

| Source | Reduction Strategy |
|--------|-------------------|
| Wi-Fi router | Place away from bedrooms; turn off or enable timer to power down at night |
| Phones in bedroom | Charge outside the bedroom or in airplane mode |
| Phones near young children | No devices near babies/toddlers; limit direct contact time |
| Baby monitors (wireless) | Position at maximum distance from baby's head; wired preferred if possible |
| Laptop on lap | Use on desk — not on body |
| Smart devices near sleep areas | Keep away from beds |

> **Philosophy**: Not fear-based — simply: reduce unnecessary proximity to high-output devices during sleep and near children.

## Mold Response Protocol

If mold appears:

1. **Find the source** — mold is always caused by moisture. Find the leak or humidity problem first.
2. **Do not use bleach** — bleach kills surface mold but does not penetrate porous materials and is a lung irritant
3. **Hydrogen peroxide (3%)** — spray on mold, leave 10 min, scrub, dry thoroughly
4. **White vinegar** — for porous surfaces (grout, caulk area). Spray, leave 1 hour, scrub.
5. **Baking soda paste** — for stubborn spots
6. **Replace if saturated** — drywall or insulation with significant mold growth must be replaced, not cleaned

> If mold area is larger than 10 square feet, or in HVAC system, call a professional remediation company.
