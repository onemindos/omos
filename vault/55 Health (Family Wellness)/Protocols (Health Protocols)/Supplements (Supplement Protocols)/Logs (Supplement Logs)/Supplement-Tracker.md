## Kids — Standard Pediatric Considerations

*These are commonly recommended for children — confirm with pediatrician before starting:*

| Supplement | Purpose | Age | Notes |
|-----------|---------|-----|-------|
| Vitamin D | Bone health, immune, mood — most kids are deficient | All ages | 400–1000 IU depending on age and sun exposure |
| Omega-3 | Brain development, focus, anti-inflammatory | 6 months+ | Liquid fish oil or algae for young kids |
| Magnesium glycinate | Sleep, stress, constipation | 2 years+ | Low dose — 50–100mg for small children |
| Probiotics | Gut health, immune support | Birth+ | Especially after antibiotics |
| Iron | Only if bloodwork shows deficiency | As needed | Don't supplement without confirmed deficiency |

> **Rule**: No supplement starts for any child without pediatrician awareness. Log all supplements in that child's health profile too.

## Sourcing Principles

- **Third-party tested** — NSF, USP, Informed Sport certified where possible
- **Avoid proprietary blends** — can't verify actual doses
- **Histamine note** — some supplement fillers and capsule materials can trigger histamine (dyes, certain binders). Opt for clean/minimal ingredient supplements.
- **Preferred forms**: glycinate (magnesium), methylcobalamin (B12), D3 not D2, triglyceride form (omega-3)
