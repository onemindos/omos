# Luma — NYC Startup Events

> Luma (lu.ma) is a modern event platform popular in tech/startup communities. The NYC Startup Events calendar aggregates founder meetups, investor nights, product demos, and networking events across NYC.

## What Luma Is

Modern alternative to Eventbrite — cleaner UX, popular with YC founders, VCs, and tech communities. Events tend to be:
- Startup networking nights
- Investor + founder meetups
- Product launches and demos
- AI/tech community events
- Side project showcases

---

## How to Use

- **Discover:** Browse https://lu.ma/nycstartupevents for upcoming events
- **RSVP:** Create a Luma account, RSVP directly — confirmation goes to email
- **Host:** Create your own events for free (unlimited attendees on free tier)
- **Follow:** Follow calendars (like nycstartupevents) to get notified of new events
