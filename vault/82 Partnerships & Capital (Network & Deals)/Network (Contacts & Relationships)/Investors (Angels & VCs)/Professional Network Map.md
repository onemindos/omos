## Networking Protocol

### First Contact Template
**When reaching out cold:**
1. Personalized opening (common ground)
2. Specific reason for reaching out
3. Value proposition (what's in it for them)
4. Clear ask (meeting, intro, advice)
5. Easy out (no pressure)

### Maintenance Rhythm
- **Tier 1:** Monthly touchpoint
- **Tier 2:** Quarterly check-in
- **Tier 3:** Annual reconnection

### Value-First Approach
**Ways to provide value:**
- Relevant introductions
- Share useful articles/opportunities
- Offer expertise/help
- Congratulate on wins
- Engage with their content
