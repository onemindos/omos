- Sleep Target: 7.5 hours per night
- Optimal Sleep Window: 10pm - 5:30am
- Body Composition: Pending initial measurement

**Cognitive Performance:**
- Peak Focus Hours: 6am-11am (protect ruthlessly)
- Decision Quality Pattern: Morning decisions superior to afternoon
- Context Switching Cost: HIGH - minimize interruptions
- Optimal Work Block: 2-4 hours of uninterrupted deep work

**Energy Patterns Throughout Day:**
- 6am-12pm: HIGH energy (peak productivity window)
- 12pm-3pm: MODERATE energy (good for meetings, lighter work)
- 3pm-6pm: LOW energy (worst time for complex decisions)
- 7pm-9pm: SECOND WIND (good for creative work, planning)

## Training Philosophy & Protocols

**Approach:** Functional fitness for longevity, not aesthetics or competition  
**Modalities:** Strength training, cardio, mobility work, recovery protocols  
**Frequency Target:** 5-6 days per week  
**Periodization:** Undulating - adjust intensity based on recovery state

**Training Principles:**
- Recovery is as important as training
- Data-driven adjustments based on HRV and sleep quality
- Never sacrifice sleep for training
- Adapt workouts to energy state (Performance Agent monitors this)

## Recovery Protocols

**Sleep (Non-Negotiable Priority):**
- 7+ hours minimum, 7.5 hours target
- Optimization: Dark room, cool temperature, no screens 1 hour before bed
- Tracking: Wearable device integration (pending setup)
- Performance Agent monitors sleep quality and alerts on declining patterns

**Nutrition Philosophy:**
- Whole foods emphasis, high protein, moderate carbohydrate
- Dietary restrictions: To be documented
- Meal timing: Intermittent fasting window to be established
- Hydration: Track and optimize

**Stress Management:**
- Techniques: Breathwork, meditation, nature time on farm
- Monitoring: HRV trends, subjective feeling tracking
- Work-life boundaries: Protect family time, farm work as active recovery
