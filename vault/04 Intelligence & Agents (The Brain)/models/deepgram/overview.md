# Deepgram

**Type:** Speech-to-Text (STT) + TTS
**API base:** `https://api.deepgram.com/v1`
**Key var:** `DEEPGRAM_API_KEY`
**Website:** deepgram.com
**Free tier:** $200 credit on signup

## Why Deepgram

Industry-leading real-time ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) — Nova-3 is the most accurate and fastest STT model available. Powers most production voice agents including LiveKit's recommended STT layer.
