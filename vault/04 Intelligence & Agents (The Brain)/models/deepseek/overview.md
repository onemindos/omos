# DeepSeek

**Type:** Cloud inference API + open-weight models
**API base:** `https://api.deepseek.com/v1` (OpenAI-compatible)
**Key var:** `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY`
**Open weights:** Yes — R1 and V3 weights publicly available

## Why DeepSeek

Chinese lab (DeepSeek) that shook the industry with V3 and R1 — trained at a fraction of Western model costs, fully open-weight. R1 reasoning matches o1 at a fraction of the price.

- Servers in China — 300–700ms from US/EU, use async/batch
- Self-host R1 or V3 locally via Ollama for zero-latency, zero-cost

- Budget reasoning alternative to o3/claude extended thinking (R1)
