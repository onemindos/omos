# Earth-2

NVIDIA's climate and weather AI platform — the digital twin of Earth's atmosphere.

## Platform Components

| Model / Tool | Type | Notes |
|-------------|------|-------|
| FourCastNet | Weather forecast | Global medium-range forecast (10K× faster than physics) |
| CorrDiff | Downscaling | Diffusion model — 25km → 2km resolution |
| StormCast | Convective AI | Rapid-update severe weather prediction |
| Earth-2 Studio | Platform | End-to-end climate AI workflow tool |
| PhysicsNeMo | Framework | Physics-ML library for custom Earth simulations |

## What Earth-2 Is

AI-powered digital twin of the Earth's climate system. Models run inference orders of magnitude faster than traditional numerical weather prediction (NWP). Used by meteorological agencies, energy companies, and climate researchers.

## Stack Use Cases

- Eden agent (sustainability/agriculture): seasonal crop planning, extreme weather risk
- Agricultural yield forecasting based on regional climate AI
- Long-range: integrate local weather forecasts into Vault/estate planning
- Research: climate resilience modeling for property acquisition decisions (Haven)
