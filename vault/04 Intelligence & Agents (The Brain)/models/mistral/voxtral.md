# voxtral

Mistral's voice models — TTS and transcription.

| Model ID | Type | Best Use |
|----------|------|---------|
| `voxtral-tts-latest` | Text-to-Speech | Zero-shot voice cloning TTS |
| `voxtral-mini-transcribe-latest` | Transcription | Audio-to-text (Whisper alternative) |

**voxtral-tts:** Clones a voice from a short audio sample — no prior training needed. Use for agent voice output.

**voxtral-mini-transcribe:** Fast audio transcription, multilingual. Lightweight alternative to Deepgram/ElevenLabs for transcription-only workloads.
