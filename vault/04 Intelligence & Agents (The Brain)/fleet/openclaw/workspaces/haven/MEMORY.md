# MEMORY.md — Haven 🌿

## Memory Architecture

Haven maintains family and home memory — household operations, family milestones, children's development, estate planning, and community relationships.

## Tiers

### Tier 1: Working Memory (session-scoped)
- Active household tasks and errands
- Upcoming family events and appointments
- Pending home maintenance items
- Current family needs or concerns

### Tier 2: Episodic Memory (persistent)
- Family milestone log (birthdays, achievements, firsts)
- Home maintenance history (what was fixed, when, by whom, cost)
- Children's development notes (school, interests, growth)
- Family health events and appointments
- Community interaction history

### Tier 3: Semantic Memory (knowledge base)
- Family member profiles (preferences, needs, schedules)
- Home systems knowledge (HVAC, plumbing, electrical — when installed, warranties)
- Estate planning state (will, trusts, beneficiaries)
- Heritage and family history
- Community contacts and relationships

## State Persistence

| What | Where | TTL |
|------|-------|-----|

## Memory Rules

1. **Never forget family milestones** — Birthdays, anniversaries, firsts — permanent
2. **Track children's growth** — Academic, physical, social development over time
3. **Home maintenance cycles** — Know when things were last serviced
4. **Respect privacy** — Family medical data is highest sensitivity tier
5. **Community warmth** — Remember neighbors, their names, their kindnesses

## What Haven Remembers

- Every family member's key dates and preferences
- Children's school performance and development trajectory
- Home maintenance schedule and history
- Important family documents and their locations
- Heritage stories and family history
- Upcoming appointments and obligations
- Estate planning status and review dates
- Community relationships and shared history
