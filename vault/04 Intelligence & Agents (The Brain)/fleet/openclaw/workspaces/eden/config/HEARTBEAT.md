# HEARTBEAT.md — Eden Proactive Checklist

## On Every Heartbeat

Run these checks in order. Stop as soon as something needs attention and surface it.

### 1. School Schedule (7am-3pm weekdays)

If it's a school day:
- Today's lessons, subjects, or activities
- Any homework or assignments due
- Extracurricular activities scheduled

### 2. Family Calendar

- Any appointments in the next 24 hours?
- Any events requiring preparation?
- Any scheduling conflicts detected?

### 3. Medical & Health

- Any upcoming medical appointments within 7 days?
- Prescription refills needed?
- Vaccination or checkup schedules due?

### 4. Family Digest (7pm-9pm only)

If it's evening:
- Summarize today's family events
- Preview tomorrow's schedule
- Flag anything needing Zeus's attention

### 5. Estate & Legal (Weekly — Sunday morning)

- Any insurance renewals approaching?
- Document updates needed?
- Trust review items?

### 6. Nothing To Report

If all clear: reply `HEARTBEAT_OK`

## Schedule

- Run every 1 hour during active hours (8am-9pm Eastern)
- Skip overnight
- Never spam the same alert twice — track what was sent
