**CC 10 Card Sequence Hack Example**

Do your research on Creditboards.com & Uscreditcardguide.com

Depending on the state you're in, use this link to see which bureaus
does each bank pulls from in what states.

<https://www.doctorofcredit.com/knowledge-base/which-credit-bureau-does-chase-pull/>

Application Requirement

**Amex (Experian)**

-   Doesn't typically care about hard pulls

-   Credit History \<6 Months

-   Only 2 Amex credit cards can be approved within 90 days. Multiple
    cards approved in the same day will have one hard pull.

-   You can have at most 5 Amex CCs, not applicable to charge cards

**Barclays (Transunion)**

-   Value hard pull a lot, recommend when you have less than 6 hard
    pulls in 6 months.

**BOA (Equifax & Transunion)**

-   Bank of America is one of the easier banks to get approved for
    multiple credit cards

-   Can have multiple cards from BOA. Must wait at least 90 days to
    apply for the same credit card, otherwise the application will
    cancel

-   2/3/4 rule (2 every 2 months,3 every year, 4 every 2 years)

**Capital One (Pull All 3)**

-   Recommend to apply \[1/6 rule\] (2 per period)

    -   Can only apply for 1 capital card for every 6 months, including
        both personal and business credit cards.

**Chase (Experian/Equifax)**

-   5/24 Rule: If you have 5 new accounts opened in the past 24 months,
    Chase will not approve your application.

-   Don't apply for more than 2 chase cards within 30 days

**Citi (Equifax)**

-   \[1/24\] BONUS TY Points are not available if you received a new
    card member

-   \[8/65 rule\] You can apply for at most 1 Citi Cards Every 8 Days,
    and at most 2 citi cards every 65 days, NO MATTER APPROVED OR NOT

-   Citi Values the number of recent hard pulls a lot, we recommend you
    apply when you have less than 6 hard inquiries in the last 6 months.

-   Credit history of at least 1 year

**Discover (Equifax & Experian)**

-   Get approved w/ no credit history

-   Must wait a year before apply for a second discover

-   Most 2 cards
