## Purchase Decision Framework

**Before making major purchase:**
- [ ] Do I really need this?
- [ ] Have I researched alternatives?
- [ ] Can I afford the full cost?
- [ ] What's the opportunity cost?
- [ ] Sleep on it for 30 days (for purchases >$1,000)
