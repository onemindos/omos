# fal.ai Video + Other Models

## Video Generation

| Model ID | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| `fal-ai/kling-video/v2/standard/text-to-video` | Kling 2 — top video quality, 5–10s clips |
| `fal-ai/kling-video/v2/pro/text-to-video` | Kling 2 Pro — highest quality |
| `fal-ai/minimax-video` | MiniMax Hailuo — fast video gen |
