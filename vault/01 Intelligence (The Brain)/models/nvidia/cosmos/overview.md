# Cosmos

NVIDIA's world foundation models — physical simulation and video world generation.

| Model | Type | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| `nvidia/cosmos-1.0-diffusion-7b-text2world` | Text→World | 7B diffusion, generate 3D world video from text |
| `nvidia/cosmos-1.0-diffusion-14b-video2world` | Video→World | 14B video continuation, predict next world frames |
| `nvidia/cosmos-nemotron-34b` | LLM | Cosmos-specific language model |
| `nvidia/cosmos-guardrail` | Safety | Filters unsafe physical simulation requests |
| `nvidia/cosmos-tokenizer-cv8x8x8` | Tokenizer | Continuous video tokenizer for Cosmos pipeline |

## What Cosmos Is

Physical world models — trained on video of real physical environments. Outputs are photorealistic video simulations. Primary use case is synthetic data for robotics training (Isaac Lab) and AV simulation (DRIVE Sim).

## Stack Use Cases

- GE/75 content: Photorealistic product visualization videos
- Eden/LE: Agricultural environment simulation
- R&D: Synthetic training data for embodied AI projects
- Physical simulation for business planning (visualize locations, spaces)
