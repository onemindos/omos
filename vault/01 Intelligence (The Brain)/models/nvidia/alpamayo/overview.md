# Alpamayo

NVIDIA's autonomous vehicle (AV) platform — full-stack AI for self-driving.

## Platform Components

| Component | Type | Notes |
|-----------|------|-------|
| Alpamayo | Full AV stack | End-to-end autonomous driving AI |
| Hydra-MDP | Planning model | Multi-future trajectory prediction |
| cosmos-dataset-search | Data tool | Search Cosmos synthetic AV datasets |

## What Alpamayo Is

NVIDIA's next-generation AV AI stack — successor to DRIVE Thor. Combines perception, prediction, and planning into a unified transformer model trained on real + Cosmos-synthetic driving data.

## Stack Use Cases

- Research track only at this stage
- Potential: AV fleet management, logistics optimization
- Integration with Cosmos for synthetic driving data generation
- Context: NVIDIA positions this as the "AI driver" layer for robotaxis and autonomous trucks
