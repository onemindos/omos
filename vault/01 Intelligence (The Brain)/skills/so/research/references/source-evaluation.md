# Source Evaluation Guide

## CRAAP Test

Use this framework to evaluate sources:

### Currency
- When was it published/updated?
- Is the information current for your topic?
- Are links functional?

### Relevance
- Does it relate to your topic?
- Who is the intended audience?
- Is it at an appropriate level?

### Authority
- Who is the author/publisher?
- What are their credentials?
- Is there contact information?
- What is the domain (.edu, .gov, .com)?

### Accuracy
- Where does the information come from?
- Is it supported by evidence?
- Can it be verified elsewhere?
- Is it peer-reviewed?

### Purpose
- Why does this information exist?
- Is it fact, opinion, or propaganda?
- Is there obvious bias?
- Are there ads that might influence content?

## Source Hierarchy

| Tier | Source Type | Reliability |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Peer-reviewed journals, official docs | Highest |
| 2 | Books, reputable news outlets | High |
| 3 | Industry reports, expert blogs | Medium-High |
| 4 | General websites, Wikipedia | Medium |
| 5 | Social media, forums | Low - verify |

## Red Flags

- No author or date
- Emotional language
- No citations
- Too good to be true
- Domain doesn't match content
