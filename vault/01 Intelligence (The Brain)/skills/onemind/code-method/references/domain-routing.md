**GE is a portfolio, not a single business.** Route by which part of the portfolio it belongs to:

| Content Type | Domain |
|-------------|--------|

| Real estate deals, properties, management | `76 Real Estate (Landlord & Property)` |
| Business acquisitions — deal flow, due diligence | `77 Acquisitions (Bought Businesses)` |
| Legal entities, contracts, trademarks, compliance | `78 Holdings (Legal & Entity Structure)` |
| Stocks, angel investing, funds, crypto, real assets | `79 Investments (Wealth & Assets)` |
| New venture ideas, validation, launch pipeline | `80 Innovation (New Ventures Pipeline)` |
| Portfolio KPIs, growth strategy, market research | `81 Strategy (Portfolio Intelligence)` |
| Affiliates, JVs, network, fundraising, events | `82 Partnerships & Capital (Network & Deals)` |
| Giving, causes, foundation, impact | `83 Philanthropy (Giving)` |
| Acquired businesses (fully operating) | `84`, `85`, `86`… |

**GE routing rules:**
- `75` = Zeus Delacruz brand — brand, content, products, ops, team, finance
- `78` = Legal structure ONLY — entity docs, contracts, trademarks
- `79` = Investment assets — not business operations
- `81` and `82` = Portfolio-level, serve ALL of GE
- Fully acquired + operating business → graduates to its own domain number (84+), not `77`
