# Three Paths Framework

Use this skill to categorize and prioritize work across the Three Paths of life management.

Philosophy: "Optimize self (HP) to serve family (LE) and create value (GE)"

## When to Use

- Categorizing new tasks or projects
- Prioritizing work across domains
- Balancing effort across life areas
- Planning sprints or time blocks

## The Three Paths

### HP (10-19) - Holistic Performance
Self-optimization, health, and personal development.

**Includes:**
- Physical fitness (workouts, nutrition, sleep)
- Mental health (meditation, therapy, stress)
- Personal development (learning, skills, habits)
- Energy management
- Focus and productivity

**Priority Prefix:** 10-19

### LE (20-29) - Legacy Evolution
Family, home, heritage, and relationships.

**Includes:**
- Family relationships and quality time
- Home maintenance and improvement
- Property management
- Generational planning
- Relationship nurturing

**Priority Prefix:** 20-29

### GE (30-39) - Generational Empire
Business, wealth, and value creation.

**Includes:**
- Business development
- Revenue generation
- Investment management
- Strategic partnerships
- Financial independence

**Priority Prefix:** 30-39

## Prioritization Process

1. **Identify Path** - Which path does this task belong to?
2. **Assess Impact** - How significant is the outcome?
3. **Check Balance** - Are we neglecting any paths?
4. **Assign Priority** - Use prefix (10-39) + urgency (1-9)

## Example Priority Codes

| Code | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| 11 | HP - Urgent (workout, health issue) |
| 25 | LE - Medium (home project) |
| 31 | GE - Urgent (client deadline) |

## Balance Guidelines

- **Weekly:** Touch all 3 paths
- **Daily:** Focus on 1-2 paths max
- **Crisis:** One path can dominate temporarily
- **Review:** Monthly path balance check

## Best Practices

- HP is foundational (enables others)
- LE compounds over generations
- GE funds everything else
- Balance creates sustainable progress
