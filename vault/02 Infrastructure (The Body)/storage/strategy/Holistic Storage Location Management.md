# Holistic Storage Location Management

Owner: Zeus

# **Overview**

You have a robust data storage system designed to manage different aspects of your life and business effectively. Here's how it's structured:

- 
    - **Proton Drive (500 GB)Purpose**: For ultra-sensitive files requiring high security and privacy.
    - **Use Cases**: Legal documents, private contracts, highly sensitive financial data.
    - **iCloud Drive (6 TB)Purpose**: Centralized storage for business operations and personal life.
    - **Use Cases**: Documents, business assets, personal files, creative projects.
    - **Folders**:
    - 
        - **Generational Empire**: For legacy planning and entrepreneurial projects.
        - **Personal Content (Review and Organize)**: For personal files and creative content.
        - **Legacy Evolution**: For legacy-building resources and personal development.
        - **Holistic Performance**: For health, wellness, and productivity resources.
        - **Innovative Technology**: For technology-related assets and tools.
    - 
    - 
    - 
    - 
    - 
    - **Temporary NAS (Local Drives)Setup**:
    - 
        - Two 5 TB Drives
        - Two 4 TB Seagate Drives
    - 
    - 
    - **Purpose**: For local backups and temporary file storage before migrating to a permanent NAS system.
    - **Use Cases**: Redundancy for business files, local access to media, large project storage.
    - **Future Plan**: Upgrade to a dedicated NAS solution.

### **Goals for Storage Management**

- **Centralized Access**: Maintain accessibility through iCloud for everyday work.
- **Redundancy**: Ensure local backups on temporary NAS drives for critical files.
- **Organization**: Keep folders clearly categorized by purpose and usage.
- **Future-Proofing**: Plan for migrating to a scalable NAS with RAID configuration.
