# Trainerize

> **Fitness coaching and workout program delivery platform**

## Overview

Trainerize is a platform for delivering structured workout programs, tracking client progress, and managing fitness coaching. Used for structured training plan delivery and logging within the HP/27 Physics domain.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Workout Programs** — Structured training plans in the HP domain
2. **Progress Tracking** — Training volume, PRs, and progress photos
3. **Coaching Interface** — If working with a trainer, shared access
4. **Habit Integration** — Workout completion feeds into HP metrics

## Key Features

- Workout program builder
- Exercise video library
- Progress photos and measurements
- Nutrition and habit tracking
- Client-coach messaging
- Calendar and scheduling
