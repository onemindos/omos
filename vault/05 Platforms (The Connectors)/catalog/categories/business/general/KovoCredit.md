# KovoCredit

> **Credit-Builder Card & Financial Health Tool**

## Overview

KovoCredit (kovo.com) is a fintech platform that offers a credit-builder installment loan and credit card designed to build or repair credit history. Works by reporting on-time payments to all 3 credit bureaus.

## Use Cases

1. **Credit Building** — Establish or improve credit score via on-time payment reporting
2. **Thin File Fix** — Useful if credit file needs more positive tradelines
3. **HP Domain** — Belongs under HP 30 Finance tracking

## Key Features

- Reports to Equifax, Experian, TransUnion
- No hard inquiry to apply
- Credit-builder card with real-world purchases
- App for tracking progress
