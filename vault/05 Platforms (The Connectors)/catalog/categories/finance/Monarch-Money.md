# Monarch Money

> **Modern personal finance and net worth tracking platform**

## Overview

Monarch Money is a comprehensive personal finance platform for tracking income, expenses, investments, and net worth. Clean UI, bank-level data aggregation, and collaborative finance for couples. Primary HP/30 Finance tracking tool.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Budget Tracking** — Income and expense categorization in HP/30 Finance
2. **Net Worth** — Real-time asset and liability tracking
3. **Investment Monitoring** — Brokerage, crypto, and retirement accounts
4. **Cash Flow** — Monthly income vs. expense analysis
5. **Financial Goals** — Track savings targets and debt payoff

## Key Features

- Automatic bank transaction sync
- Custom budget categories
- Net worth tracking with all accounts
- Investment portfolio view
- Spending trends and reports
- Collaborative (partner access)
- Goals and projections
