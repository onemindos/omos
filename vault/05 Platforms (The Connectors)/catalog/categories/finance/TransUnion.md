# TransUnion

> **Credit bureau — credit reports and identity protection**

## Overview

TransUnion is one of the three major U.S. credit bureaus. Direct access via TransUnion.com provides credit monitoring, dispute management, and identity protection. Part of the HP/30 Finance credit monitoring stack.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Credit Report** — Full TransUnion credit report access
2. **Score Monitoring** — VantageScore 3.0 tracking
3. **Freeze / Lock** — Instant credit freeze to prevent fraud
4. **Dispute** — Contest inaccurate items on TransUnion report
5. **Identity Alerts** — New account and inquiry alerts

## Key Features

- Free TransUnion credit report
- Credit lock (instant freeze)
- Identity monitoring
- SMS and email alerts
- Dispute filing
- TrueIdentity (free monitoring tier)
