# Experian

> **Credit bureau — credit reports, FICO scores, and identity protection**

## Overview

Experian is one of the three major U.S. credit bureaus. Direct access via Experian.com provides the actual FICO score (not VantageScore), full credit report, and identity theft protection. Part of the HP/30 Finance credit monitoring stack alongside TransUnion and Equifax.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **FICO Score** — Official FICO 8 score used by most lenders
2. **Credit Report** — Full Experian credit report access
3. **Boost** — Add utility/phone payments to improve score
4. **Identity Protection** — Dark web monitoring and fraud alerts
5. **Dispute Management** — Contest inaccurate items on Experian report

## Key Features

- Free Experian FICO Score 8
- Experian Boost (add extra payment history)
- Full credit report
- Social Security number monitoring
- Credit lock (instant credit freeze)
- Experian Go (no credit history starter)
