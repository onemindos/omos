# Credit Karma

> **Free credit score monitoring and financial product recommendations**

## Overview

Credit Karma provides free credit score and report access from TransUnion and Equifax, updated weekly. Includes credit monitoring alerts, identity monitoring, and financial product recommendations. Passive HP/30 Finance credit health tracker.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Credit Score Tracking** — Weekly score updates from TransUnion + Equifax
2. **Credit Monitoring** — Alerts for new accounts, hard inquiries, changes
3. **Identity Monitoring** — Dark web and data breach monitoring
4. **HP Finance Score** — Credit score data feeds into HP/30 financial health

## Key Features

- Free TransUnion + Equifax scores (VantageScore 3.0)
- Credit report cards (utilization, payment history, etc.)
- Identity monitoring and alerts
- Tax filing (Credit Karma Tax)
- Savings account and financial products
- No credit card required

## Note

Credit Karma shows VantageScore — lenders typically use FICO. Track both for complete picture. Use Experian app for FICO score access.
