# Origin

> **Financial wellness platform combining personal finance with tax optimization**

## Overview

Origin (UseOrigin) is a financial wellness platform that connects all financial accounts and provides tax optimization guidance, equity management, and financial planning in one place. Used for HP/30 Finance domain holistic financial health tracking and tax strategy.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Tax Optimization** — Tax-loss harvesting and annual tax planning
2. **Equity Management** — Track stock options, RSUs, and vesting schedules
3. **Financial Planning** — Long-term wealth projections
4. **Benefits Optimization** — 401k, HSA, and employer benefit analysis

## Key Features

- All-in-one financial dashboard
- Tax optimization guidance
- Equity and stock option tracking
- Net worth monitoring
- Benefits breakdown
- Financial advisor access (premium)
- Estate planning tools
