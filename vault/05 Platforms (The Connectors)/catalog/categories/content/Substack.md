# Substack

> **Newsletter and publication platform for independent creators**

## Overview

Substack is a platform for writers and creators to publish newsletters, podcasts, and posts directly to subscribers. Handles payments, email delivery, and community. A natural distribution channel for One Mind content — essays, framework updates, and community building funnel.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **One Mind Newsletter** — Regular essays on the framework, AI, and life design
2. **Launch Funnel** — Build audience before manifesto/ebook launch (Layer 1-2)
3. **Community Building** — Free tier to paid community conversion
4. **Content Distribution** — Syndicate codex insights to broader audience
5. **Paid Writing** — Paid tiers for premium content (Layer 2-4 bridge)

## Key Features

- Free to start (Substack takes 10% of paid revenue)
- Email + web + app distribution
- Paid subscriptions (monthly/annual)
- Comments and community
- Podcast hosting
- Chat (Substack Notes)
- Custom domain

## One Mind Fit

Substack sits between Layer 1 (free ideas) and Layer 2 (paid manifesto). A free newsletter builds the email list; a paid tier tests willingness to pay before launching the full product suite.
