# Tech-Stack — Tool Inventory

> *"Know your tools. Master your tools."*

---

## Purpose

Tech-Stack is the **tool management center**. This area houses the complete technology inventory—subscriptions, configurations, evaluations, and the overall tech landscape.

---

## What Lives Here

- **Tool Inventory** — Complete list of tools in use
- **Subscriptions** — SaaS, recurring services, costs
- **Configurations** — Settings, preferences, customizations
- **Evaluations** — Tool reviews, comparison analyses
- **Migrations** — Switching tools, data movement

---

## Tool Categories

| Category | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| Productivity | Task/project management, notes |
| Communication | Email, messaging, video |
| Creation | Writing, design, media |
| Development | Code, hosting, deployment |
| Data | Storage, backup, analytics |
| AI | Assistants, automation, ML |
| Security | Password, VPN, encryption |

---

## Stack Audit Questions

1. What does this tool do?
2. Is there overlap with another tool?
3. What does it cost (time + money)?
4. What would I lose without it?
5. Is there a better alternative?

---

## Review Cadence

- **Monthly**: Subscription review
- **Quarterly**: Stack audit
- **Annually**: Full rationalization
