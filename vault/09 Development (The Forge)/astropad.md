# Astropad

**Site:** https://astropad.com
**Tagline:** Pro tech for creatives and makers

## Products

- **Astropad Studio** — Turn iPad into a professional drawing tablet. Magic gestures, Stroke Lab, unlimited per-app shortcuts. Free trial available.
- **Luna Display** — Turn any iPad or Mac into a wireless second display.
- **Workbench** — Modern remote desktop for Mac, iPad, and iPhone.
- **Rock Paper Pencil** — iPad screen protector + precision Apple Pencil tips.
- **Fresh Coat** — Anti-reflective glass screen protector for iPhone.
- **Astropad Slate** — Control your Mac using iPad and Apple Pencil.
- **Rock Daily Planner** — Digital planner and notebook for iPad.
