## Loan Product 1: FlexTerm Loan

**Purpose:** Long-term fixed-rate investment property financing with no balloon payment.

### Rate Matrix

| Tier | FICO | Investor 1-4 (SFR, Condo, 2-4 Units) | Multi-Family & Mixed-Use (5+) | Commercial (Office, Retail, Warehouse, Self-Storage, Auto) |
|------|------|---------------------------------------|-------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | ≥ 750 | 9.74% | 10.74% | 11.24% |
| 2 | 700–749 | 10.24% | 11.24% | 11.74% |
| 3 | ≥ 650 | 10.99% | 11.99% | 12.49% |

### Terms
- **Term:** 30-year fixed, fully amortized (shorter terms available)
- **Interest-Only Option:** Add .25% to above rates, 5-year I/O then 25-year amortization
- **Loan Amounts:** $75K–$5MM (Multi-Family/Mixed-Use/Commercial min $100K)
- **Loans > $1MM:** Max 70% LTV
- **Investor 1-4 max:** $2MM
- **LTV Max:** 75% (Investor 1-4 and Multi-Family), 70% (Commercial)
- **Credit Score:** 650 minimum (mid FICO)
- **Prepayment Penalty:** 5-year declining: 5%, 4%, 3%, 2%, 1%
- **Prepay Buydown:** Reduce 1 year by adding .375% fee (min 1-year penalty)
- **Rate Buydown:** 1-point fee = .375% rate reduction (minimum rate 8.49%)
- **Rebates:** 1-point rebate = .50% add-on to rate (max 1%)
- **Broker Compensation:** Max 5% of loan amount including fees
- **Lending:** Nationwide
- **Income Verification:** Simple documentation
- **Impounds:** Required (property taxes and insurance)
- **Foreign Investors:** 65% maximum LTV
- **CLTV:** 90% maximum
- **First-Time Investor:** 5% LTV reduction for all property types
- **First-Time Buyers:** Ineligible for investment 1-unit properties
- **Refinance:** Add .50% to rates shown
- **LTV Rate Adjuster (1-4 only):** Reduce rate by -.125 for every 5% below LTV max (floor at 50%)
- **No Prepay States:** NJ (1-6 units, individual title) and NM (1-4 units) = no prepay, add 1% to rate or 1% in fee

## Loan Product 2: ARV Pro Loan

**Purpose:** Short-term rehab/improvement loan to maximize property value and rental appeal.

### Rate Matrix

| Tier | FICO | Rate (I/O) | LTC / LTARV |
|------|------|------------|-------------|
| 1 | ≥ 700 | 10.24% | 80% / 75% |
| 2 | 650–699 | 10.74% | 80% / 75% |

### Terms
- **Purpose:** Improve or rehab properties (purchase and refinance OK)
- **Loan Amounts:** $75K–$2MM, up to 75% ARV and 80% as-is value
- **Initial Loan Amount:** 80% (lower of purchase price or current value)
- **Term:** 1-year interest only, 6-month extension at 1% fee
- **Credit Score:** 650 minimum
- **Prepayment:** None
- **Rate Buydown:** 1-point fee = .375% reduction (max .75% reduction, min rate 8.49%)
- **Broker Fees:** Max 5% of loan amount
- **Lender Fees:** 2% + $999 underwriting fee
- **Experience:** Must show proof of prior RE investment/ownership
- **First-Time Investors:** 70% max LTV
- **Income Verification:** Simple documentation
- **Down Payment:** Must be sourced
- **Heavy Rebuild:** Rehab >50% of loan = case-by-case, no ground-up construction
- **Impounds:** None
- **Foreign Investors:** 65% max LTV
- **Improvements:** Schedule of improvements required, inspection fees per draw apply

## Originator Compensation Plan

### Company
- **Entity:** Triumph Solutions LLC (Florida corporation)
- **Role:** Processor — negotiates commission percentage from lenders on closed deals

### Commission Structure
- Commissions vary **1%–10%** of funded amount (paid by lender)
- Starting originators earn **30%** of the company's commission for first 2 deals
- Advancement to higher commissions + ability to manage a team after qualifying

### Example Payout
| Funded Amount | Lender Payout (10%) | Originator Share (30%) |
|---------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| $100,000 | $10,000 | **$3,000** |
| 3 deals/month | $30,000 | **$9,000/month** |

### Contractor Terms
- **Type:** Independent contractor agreement
- **Payment:** Within 30 business days after customer gets funded
- **Drawback Clause:** If merchant defaults within 30 days and commission was paid early, clawback applies
- **No outside compensation** without Triumph Solutions consent
- **Client ownership:** Deal originator retains client for renewals and future funding while active

### Job Description
- Lead generation for:
  - Merchant Cash Advances
  - Small Business Loans
  - Commercial Real Estate Financing
  - Credit Card Processing
  - Marketing and other business services

### Required Documents (from clients)
1. One-page Triumph Solutions Application
2. 4 months business bank statements
3. Picture ID for owner(s)

## Strategic Notes

- These are **investor-focused** loan products, not consumer/homeowner loans
- Foreign national programs available (65% LTV cap) — relevant for international RE investing
- Fast50 is notable: accepts bankruptcy discharge after 1 day, mortgage lates OK — designed for credit-challenged investors with equity
- Triumph Solutions operates as a **broker/processor**, not the lender — Velocity Commercial Capital is the actual lender
- Originator role is essentially commissioned sales/lead gen with independent contractor structure
- Rate sheet dated Jan 2024 — rates may have changed
