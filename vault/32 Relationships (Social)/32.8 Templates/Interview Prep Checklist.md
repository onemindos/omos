## Questions to Ask Them

### About the Role (Ask Hiring Manager)
- "What does success look like in this role in the first 90 days?"
- "What are the biggest challenges facing the team right now?"
- "How is performance measured for this position?"
- "What's the typical career path for someone in this role?"
- "Why is this position open?"

### About the Team
- "Can you tell me about the team I'd be working with?"
- "What's the team dynamic like?"
- "How does the team collaborate?"
- "What's the management style?"

### About the Company
- "What are the company's top priorities for the next year?"
- "How would you describe the company culture?"
- "What do you like most about working here?"
- "How has the company changed since you joined?"

### About Growth
- "What professional development opportunities are available?"
- "How does the company support employee growth?"
- "Is there a mentorship program?"

### About Process
- "What are the next steps in the interview process?"
- "What's the timeline for making a decision?"
- "Is there anything about my background you'd like me to clarify?"

## Logistics Prep (Day Before)

### Technical Setup (If Virtual)
- [ ] Video/audio tested (Zoom/Teams/etc.)
- [ ] Lighting checked
- [ ] Background professional
- [ ] Phone on silent/airplane mode
- [ ] "Do Not Disturb" active
- [ ] Backup device ready
- [ ] Interviewer contact info saved
- [ ] Meeting link bookmarked

### Materials Prep
- [ ] Resume printed (2 copies if in-person)
- [ ] Portfolio/work samples ready
- [ ] References list prepared
- [ ] Notepad and pen
- [ ] Questions list printed

### Appearance
- [ ] Professional attire selected and ready
- [ ] Grooming planned
- [ ] Accessories appropriate

### In-Person Logistics
- [ ] Address confirmed and mapped
- [ ] Travel time calculated (add 15 min buffer)
- [ ] Parking information researched
- [ ] Transportation backup plan
- [ ] Arrive 10-15 minutes early

## Day-Of Checklist

### 2 Hours Before
- [ ] Review company research notes
- [ ] Review your STAR examples
- [ ] Review questions to ask

### 30 Minutes Before
- [ ] Final tech check (if virtual)
- [ ] Water nearby
- [ ] Bathroom break
- [ ] Deep breathing/centering
- [ ] Positive affirmations

### During Interview
- [ ] Smile and make eye contact
- [ ] Listen actively before answering
- [ ] Pause to think if needed
- [ ] Ask for clarification if unclear
- [ ] Take brief notes
- [ ] Show enthusiasm
- [ ] Be authentic

---

## Post-Interview (Within 24 Hours)

### Immediate (Right After)
- [ ] Write down all interview details
- [ ] Note what went well
- [ ] Note what could improve
- [ ] Update application tracker
- [ ] Send thank you note(s)

### Thank You Note Template
**Subject:** Thank you - [Role] Interview

Dear [Interviewer Name],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me today about the [Role] position at [Company]. I enjoyed learning more about [specific topic discussed] and [another specific topic].

Our conversation reinforced my enthusiasm for this opportunity, particularly [specific aspect of role/company that excited you].

I'm confident that my experience with [relevant skill/experience] would allow me to [specific contribution to team/company].

Please don't hesitate to reach out if you need any additional information. I look forward to hearing about next steps.

Best regards,
[Your Name]

### Follow-up Timeline
- [ ] Day 1: Thank you note sent
- [ ] Day 3-5: If no response, gentle check-in
- [ ] Day 7-10: Follow up on timeline
- [ ] Day 14: Final status check
