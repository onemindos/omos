## 13. Refine your community messaging

Community messaging compiles who you are, who you serve, how you serve
them (as well as what makes you stand out from your competition).

**How do you up-level your messaging from poor ⏩ profitable?**

-   **Clear:** Not overly wordy or explanatory. The quicker you can get
    > people to understand what you do, the better.

-   **Specific:** You're clearly calling out your ideal customers, your
    > unique value-add, and the methods through which you provide your
    > services.

-   **Non-inclusive:** Good messaging intentionally excludes people. You
    > can't serve everyone---and you're losing money by trying.

-   **Unique:** Actively think about your competitors in the space. When
    > Maya gets on customer calls, she specifically asks who else
    > they're considering to understand who she's being compared to, and
    > how she can set herself apart.

# 🚀 Community Launch Checklist 

## **Phase 1: Community Foundations** (2-3 Weeks Before)

### **Define Your Core Purpose**

-   Write out your community\'s mission statement and \"why\"

-   Identify the transformation your community will facilitate for
    > members

-   Create a clear value proposition (15 words or less)

-   Map how community fits into your existing business model

-   Determine realistic time commitment you can dedicate weekly

### **Identify Your Ideal Members**

-   Create detailed member personas including:

    -   Demographics and characteristics

    -   Pain points and challenges

    -   Goals and desired outcomes

    -   Readiness indicators (who is/isn\'t ready)

-   Map specific features/benefits to solve member pain points

-   Document who your community is NOT for (equally important)

### **Choose Your Community Model**

Select primary business model:

-   Paid membership community

-   Cohort-based community

-   Product-centric community

-   Lifetime access community

-   Pay-what-you-can community

Pick core framework:

-   Support framework

-   Learning framework

-   Interest framework

-   Product support framework

## **Phase 2: Programming & Experience Design** (2-4 Weeks Before)

### **Design Core Value Pillars**

Help pillar:

-   Q&A spaces

-   Office hours

-   Expert sessions

-   Review systems

Action pillar:

-   Challenges/sprints

-   Progress tracking

-   Goal-setting systems

-   Accountability features

Learning pillar:

-   Resource library

-   Courses/workshops

-   Knowledge base

-   Member-led teaching

Connection pillar:

-   Networking events

-   Small group facilitation

-   Social spaces

-   Peer matching

### **Create Community Architecture**

-   Design essential spaces:

    -   Welcome/onboarding hub

    -   Introduction area

    -   Discussion forums

    -   Resource sections

    -   Events calendar

-   Organize spaces into logical groups

-   Define clear purpose for each space

-   Create engagement guidelines per space

### **Develop Signature Programming**

-   Choose and design your primary gathering format:

    -   Hot seats

    -   Workshops

    -   Office hours

    -   Accountability groups

    -   Book clubs

    -   Peer reviews

    -   Community challenges

-   Plan secondary rituals and recurring events

-   Create a programming calendar (start small!)

## **Phase 3: Business Strategy** (2-3 Weeks Before)

### **Set Pricing Strategy**

-   Choose pricing model:

    -   Subscription-based

    -   Pay-per-access

    -   Freemium

-   Determine price points and tiers

-   Set clear revenue goals

-   Design offer funnel:

    -   Free offerings

    -   Low-ticket products (\$29-199)

    -   Mid-ticket offerings (\$199-2,999)

    -   High-ticket experiences (\$3,000+)

-   Plan additional revenue streams (courses, coaching, etc.)

### **Create Launch Plan**

-   Design alpha test (initial 10-20 members):

    -   Create invite list

    -   Set timeline

    -   Define feedback process

-   Plan beta launch (founding members):

    -   Determine number of spots

    -   Create special offers/perks

    -   Set timeline

-   Develop public launch strategy:

    -   Marketing channels

    -   Promotional tactics

    -   Timeline

## **Phase 4: Final Launch Preparation** (1-2 Weeks Before)

### **Establish Community Guidelines**

-   Create comprehensive guidelines covering:

    -   Tone and expectations

    -   Safety and security

    -   Acceptable topics

    -   Inclusion policies

    -   Harassment prevention

-   Develop moderation procedures

-   Set up reporting systems

### **Refine Messaging**

-   Polish community description and value proposition

-   Create clear promotional messaging

-   Develop member communication templates

-   Write welcome sequences

-   Create FAQs

### **Set Up Systems**

-   Configure community platform

-   Set up analytics tracking

-   Create backup procedures

-   Test all technical functions

-   Implement security measures

## **Phase 5: Launch Execution**

### **Alpha Launch**

-   Send personal invitations

-   Welcome first members individually

-   Gather detailed feedback

-   Make necessary adjustments

-   Document early wins and challenges

### **Beta Launch**

-   Open founding member spots

-   Implement feedback from alpha

-   Begin regular programming

-   Start collecting testimonials

-   Monitor engagement metrics

### **Public Launch**

-   Execute marketing plan

-   Scale community programs

-   Implement engagement strategies

-   Begin regular feedback collection

-   Plan long-term growth initiatives

Remember: Starting small with 10-20 highly engaged members is often more
valuable than launching with hundreds of passive participants. Focus on
quality engagement and member experience over rapid growth.
