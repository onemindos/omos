## Training Principles

1. **Build to Learn** - Hands-on projects cement theory
2. **Iterate Designs** - First version rarely works perfectly
3. **Document Everything** - Photos, notes, measurements
4. **Calculate First** - Run numbers before building
5. **Safety First** - Proper tools and protection

---

## Mastery Levels

- **Novice** - Learning fundamental concepts and tools
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can build simple projects with guidance
- **Competent** - Designs and builds functional projects independently
- **Proficient** - Optimizes designs, troubleshoots complex issues
- **Expert** - Innovative solutions, mentors others
