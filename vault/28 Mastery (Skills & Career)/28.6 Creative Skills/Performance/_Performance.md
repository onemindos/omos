## Training Principles

1. **Rehearse Consistently** - Regular practice builds confidence
2. **Embrace Vulnerability** - Performance requires emotional openness
3. **Study Technique** - Learn from professionals
4. **Record & Review** - Watch yourself to improve
5. **Live Performance** - Real audiences build experience

---

## Mastery Levels for Performance

- **Novice** - Learning basics, high stage fright
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can perform scripted material with guidance
- **Competent** - Comfortable performing independently
- **Proficient** - Natural stage presence, audience engagement
- **Expert** - Mastery across styles, teaches/directs others
