# Muay Thai

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Training, Physical, Martial-Arts

## Overview

Muay Thai ("Thai Boxing") is a combat sport from Thailand using stand-up striking and clinching techniques.

---

## Key Techniques

### Strikes (8 Points of Contact)
- **Punches** – Jab, cross, hook, uppercut
- **Kicks** – Roundhouse, teep (push kick), low kick
- **Elbows** – Horizontal, uppercut, spinning
- **Knees** – Straight, curved, flying

### Clinch Work
- Collar tie control
- Knee strikes from clinch
- Off-balancing and sweeps

---

## Training Focus

- [ ] Develop solid stance and footwork
- [ ] Master basic combinations
- [ ] Build conditioning for 3-5 round fights
- [ ] Practice clinch techniques
- [ ] Spar regularly
