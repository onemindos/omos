## Training Principles

1. **Understand Systems** - Cars are integrated systems
2. **Work Safe** - Jacks, tools, and fluids are hazards
3. **Follow Procedures** - Torque specs and sequences matter
4. **Diagnostic First** - Identify problem before replacing parts
5. **Document Work** - Photos and notes for future reference

---

## Mastery Levels

- **Novice** - Learning basic systems and maintenance
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can perform basic maintenance with guidance
- **Competent** - Diagnoses and repairs independently
- **Proficient** - Complex repairs, performance tuning
- **Expert** - Engine builds, custom fabrication, ASE certified
