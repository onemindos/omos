## Training Principles

1. **Understand Codes** - Learn building codes and standards
2. **Think Long-term** - Durability and maintenance matter
3. **Site Awareness** - Context affects design
4. **Safety Critical** - Lives depend on structural integrity
5. **Sustainability Focus** - Environmental impact considerations

---

## Mastery Levels

- **Novice** - Learning fundamental concepts and standards
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can analyze simple structures with guidance
- **Competent** - Designs basic projects independently
- **Proficient** - Complex projects, code compliance, optimization
- **Expert** - Innovative infrastructure solutions, PE license
