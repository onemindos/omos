## USA Street Legality

**Short answer: No — not legally on public roads.**

The GTX is a race car built for SRO GT2 racing, not street homologation.

**Why it's not street legal in the USA:**
- No airbags
- No crash testing certification
- No DOT approval for the canopy
- No emissions certification
- Classified as "race vehicle / track-only" by KTM
- U.S. import rules place it under Show & Display or Track-Use Only — neither allows road use

**Exception paths:**

| Path | Allows Street Use? | Notes |
|------|--------------------|-------|
| Track-Only Import | ❌ | Most common — can't be registered |
| Show & Display | ❌ For driving | You can own it; road driving still forbidden |
| State-level "kit car" loopholes | ⚠️ Very rare | GTX canopy + crash compliance likely fails |
| 25-year rule | ❌ Not applicable | Car is far too new |

> **Conclusion: You cannot register or drive the GTX on U.S. streets. Track-use only, no matter the state.**
>
> For street legality, you would need the **KTM X-Bow GT-XR** (road-legal in Europe) — but not yet legalized in the U.S. without special exemption.
