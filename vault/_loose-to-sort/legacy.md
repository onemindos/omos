Not abandoning. Not forgetting. Intentionally depositing. The entire mission in one word. Awake. Aware. Intentional. Sovereign. This is what One Mind OS is actually building toward — a version of you that is not reactive, not distracted, not fragmented across a hundred apps and obligations. Conscious means you are the author of your life, not the passenger. It's the philosophical core of everything Zeus has been building for five years.

**Y — You**
The most important word in the whole phrase. Not "them." Not "people." Not "humanity." You. It's personal. It points directly at whoever is hearing it. Legacy isn't building a system for the masses — it's building a system that transforms the individual standing right in front of it. Every person who encounters Legacy should feel like it was built specifically for them. Because it was.
