# Book vs Community vs Both

> **Both. But sequence matters.**

---

## Build Sequence

| Step | What | Why | Timeline |
|:----:|------|-----|----------|
| 1 | **Manifesto** (short ebook, 50–80 pages) | Establishes authority. Free or $9.99. Explains the 5-step method + 3+1 model. | Write in 2–4 weeks with AI assist |
| 2 | **Template Vault** (codex-template) | People download and start using it. This is the "try before you buy." | Already exists — polish it |
| 3 | **Community** (Circle, Discord, or similar) | Recurring revenue. People need help implementing. | Launch alongside or right after the book |
| 4 | **Course / Coaching** | Premium tier. Walk people through their first 30 days. | After you have 50–100 community members |
| 5 | **Full Book** (200+ pages, paperback) | Legitimacy play. Gets you on podcasts, in bookstores. | 6–12 months out |

**Tiago Forte's path:** blog posts → cohort course → book → app → community. He built for 5+ years before the book.

You can compress this because AI lets you write faster, and the framework already exists. **The manifesto is the minimum viable product.** You can write it from `CODEX-FRAMEWORK.md` — it's already 90% of the content, just in documentation format instead of narrative format.

## The Layer Separation

> **Key insight:** it's complex for you because you're the architect. Here's how the layers should work:

| Layer | Name | Price | What it is | Delivery |
|:-----:|------|-------|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **THE IDEA** | Free, everywhere | "You + AI = One Mind. Here's the 5-step loop." | Blog posts, social, podcast appearances, the codex-live site |
| 2 | **THE METHOD** | Manifesto, $0–10 | CODEX method + 3+1 model + first 30 days playbook | Short book / ebook |
| 3 | **THE STARTER** | Free | Empty vault with structure, templates, example content | GitHub repo, one-click Obsidian setup |
| 4 | **THE COMMUNITY** | $10–30/mo | Weekly calls, shared implementations, Zeus answers questions | Circle or Discord |
| 5 | **THE ARCHITECT** | Premium, $500+ | Zeus sets up YOUR codex with YOUR domains, YOUR agents | 1-on-1 or small cohort |

**You live at Layer 5.** You teach Layers 1–3. You monetize Layers 2–4.
