Where I go past Multica — the polymorphic Actor. Multica made agents first-class members alongside humans. OneMind makes the physical world first-class too:
Actor = human | agent | node | device | squad

The critical distinction I'm keeping from them: NOTIFY vs TRIGGER. Humans get notified (an inbox, like Todoist) and choose to act. Machines get triggered and execute immediately. Same task board, same issue, different executor. Adding "drone" or "CNC" later is just a new actor type with new capabilities — never a schema rewrite.
And the part that makes it an OS, not a to-do app: telemetry drives the board. tel.greenhouse.soil.moisture < 20 fires an event ? an autopilot creates an issue "water greenhouse" ? assigned to the irrigation device ? it executes. The physical world creates and completes its own tasks. NATS Connect (Wombat) later lets a task fire a SaaS action or move a robot.
Why ours beats Multica's core: they poll every 3 seconds; we push. An actor's daemon holds a live subscription and gets work the instant it's assigned — lower latency, and it scales to hundreds of nodes on the same mechanism.
