# Homemade Lip Scrub Recipes

The key is combining:
- **Something gritty** — to exfoliate dead skin
- **Something moisturizing** — to hydrate your lips

---

## 1. Easiest Lip Scrub (Most Common)

**Ingredients**
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 tsp honey
- ½ tsp olive oil or coconut oil

**How to use**
1. Mix everything into a paste
2. Rub gently on your lips for 20–60 seconds
3. Wipe or rinse with warm water
4. Apply lip balm afterward

- Sugar → removes dead skin
- Honey → moisturizes and heals cracked lips
- Oil → keeps lips soft and hydrated

---

## 2. If You Don't Have Honey

Use any of these instead:
- Sugar + olive oil
- Sugar + coconut oil
- Sugar + Vaseline

All of these still exfoliate and moisturize your lips.

---

## 3. Optional Add-Ins

| Add-in | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| Coffee grounds | Boosts circulation |
| Cinnamon (tiny pinch) | May make lips look slightly plumper |
| Strawberry mash | Adds vitamin C and flavor |

---

## Coconut Oil vs Olive Oil

| Option | Best For | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| Coconut oil | Softer lips, smoother scrub | Thick, very moisturizing, melts into lips |
| Olive oil | Very dry or cracked lips | Deep hydration, more nutrient-rich |
| Both mixed | Best overall | Balanced exfoliation + hydration |

---

## Recommended Recipe (Best Result)

**Ingredients**
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ tsp coconut oil
- ½ tsp olive oil

**Steps**
1. Mix into a paste
2. Rub gently on lips for 20–30 seconds
3. Wipe with warm cloth
4. Apply lip balm

- Sugar → exfoliates
- Coconut oil → smooths
- Olive oil → deep hydration

---

## Pro Tips

**Before scrubbing:** Put a warm damp cloth on your lips for 30 seconds, then apply the scrub. This loosens dead skin and makes the scrub work much better.

**⚠️ Only scrub 1–2 times per week** — lip skin is delicate, be gentle.

**✅ Quick hack if you have nothing:** Rub your lips with a warm damp towel for gentle exfoliation, then apply lip balm.
