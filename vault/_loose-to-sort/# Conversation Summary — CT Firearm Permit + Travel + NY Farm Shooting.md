## Connecticut (Greenwich) Pistol Permit Process (Handgun)

  


### Key Outcome

\- The **CT Pistol Permit** is the **most powerful permit** in Connecticut because it allows:

\- Carrying a handgun (concealed/open where legal)

\- Purchasing handguns

\- Purchasing long guns (rifles/shotguns)

\- Purchasing ammunition

  


### Main Steps (High-Level)

1\. Complete CT-approved firearms training (ex: NRA Basic Pistol Course)

2\. Submit pistol permit application to Greenwich Police Department

3\. Complete fingerprinting + background checks

4\. Receive **Temporary Permit**

5\. Go to CT DESPP to receive the **5-year State Pistol Permit**

  


### Fingerprinting

\- Fingerprinting is done by appointment.

\- Applicant must pre-enroll in CT background check system (CCHRS) before fingerprinting.

  


### Approximate Cost Breakdown (not including training)

\- Greenwich local application fee: ~$70

\- Fingerprinting/background checks: ~$88.25

\- Fingerprinting service fee: ~$10

\- State permit issuance fee: ~$70 

**Estimated total (without training): ~$240+**

## Constitutional Carry (Meaning)

\- &quotConstitutional; carry" means a state allows concealed carry **without requiring a permit**, as long as you can legally own a firearm.

\- Connecticut is **NOT** constitutional carry.

## Florida Carry Rules (for CT Residents)

\- Florida allows **permitless concealed carry** (constitutional carry).

\- You must:

\- Be 21+

\- Be legally allowed to possess a firearm

\- Carry concealed (open carry is generally not allowed)

## Traveling with a Firearm to Florida

  


### Driving

\- Federal protection under **18 USC ñ926A** applies if:

\- Firearm is unloaded

\- Stored in a locked container

\- Inaccessible to driver (trunk/rear)

\- Ammunition stored separately

\- Avoid unnecessary stops in strict states like NY/NJ.

  


### Flying

\- Must declare firearm at airline check-in.

\- Firearm must be unloaded and in a locked hard case.

\- Ammo must be properly packaged.

\- Only owner can have key/combination.

## New York State / NYC Permit Discussion

Key point:

\- New York State and NYC permits are significantly harder and more restrictive.

\- CT permits do not transfer into NY/NYC.

## Shooting on a Farm in Upstate NY

Answer provided:

\- Yes, generally allowed on private land if:

\- No local discharge restrictions

\- Safe backstop is used

\- Shooting is not within restricted distances (ex: 500 feet from dwellings/roads unless exceptions apply)

\- Important:

\- Handguns require a NY pistol permit even on your own land.

\- CT permit does not authorize handgun possession/use in NY.
