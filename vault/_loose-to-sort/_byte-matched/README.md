# HP — Holistic Performance (25-49)

> *YOUR SELF — Master the machine that runs everything else.*

## Purpose

**Holistic Performance** is one of three life domains in the One Mind 3+1 model. Everything about YOU as a human being lives here — your inner world, your body, your knowledge, your money, your relationships, your legal standing, and your joy.

## Conventions

- **All files are Markdown** — no binary files in HP domains
- **Frontmatter required** on all files: `title`, `type`, `status`, `tags`, `ai_summary`
- **LifeFlow tagging** — every HP task maps to one of the 10 domains (see 25 Identity/Core-Identity for reference)
- **Templates folders** — domain-specific templates at the bottom of each domain
- **Nothing loose** — all content lives in a named subfolder
