# Tactical Games

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Tactical, Games, Physical, Military

## Overview

Tactical games are competitive, scenario-based physical activities that develop real-world tactical skills — movement, communication, decision-making under pressure, and teamwork. Bridges the gap between training and real application.

---

## Game Types

| Type | Format | Skill Focus |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Airsoft MilSim** | Scenario-based, realistic rules of engagement | Full tactical application |
| **Airsoft Skirmish** | Fast-paced CQB/field games | Speed, aggression, reflexes |
| **Paintball** | Competitive field games | Movement, cover usage |
| **Laser Tag (Tactical)** | Training-grade force-on-force | Trigger discipline, accuracy |
| **Escape/Objective Games** | Timed objective capture | Planning, execution |

---

## Training Value

- Force-on-force pressure testing — decisions under stress
- Team communication and callouts
- Use of cover and concealment
- Movement patters — bounding, flanking, breaching
- Equipment familiarity under load

---

## Goals

- [ ] Attend monthly airsoft skirmish or milsim event
- [ ] Run at least 1 milsim scenario per quarter
- [ ] Track performance: hit rate, objective completion, team feedback
- [ ] Build consistent kit for game days
