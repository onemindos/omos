# Ironman Triathlon Bike Guide

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Training, Physical, Triathlon, Equipment

## Overview

In an Ironman triathlon (2.4-mile swim, 112-mile bike, 26.2-mile run), competitors typically use a **triathlon bike** or **time trial (TT) bike** designed for aerodynamics and efficiency over long distances.

---

## Triathlon/TT Bike Features

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Aero Bars** | Extended handlebars allowing a tucked position to reduce air resistance |
| **Steep Seat Tube Angle** | Positions rider forward, saving muscles used for running |
| **Aero Frame Design** | Frame, wheels, and components designed to reduce drag |
| **Integrated Storage** | Built-in storage for nutrition, tools, and essentials |

---

## Bike Options

- **Triathlon/TT Bike** – Purpose-built for triathlon racing
- **Road Bike with Clip-On Aero Bars** – Budget-friendly alternative

---

## Considerations

The choice often depends on:
- Athlete preference
- Budget
- Specific course conditions (flat vs. hilly)
