## Training Principles

1. **Daily Practice** - Even 15 minutes builds skill
2. **Study the Masters** - Learn from established artists
3. **Fundamentals First** - Master basics before advanced techniques
4. **Iterate & Experiment** - Don't fear mistakes
5. **Document Progress** - Track improvement over time

---

## Mastery Levels for Visual Arts

- **Novice** - Learning basic shapes, lines, perspective
- **Advanced Beginner** - Can copy references accurately
- **Competent** - Can create original work independently
- **Proficient** - Developed personal style, fluid execution
- **Expert** - Mastery across mediums, can teach others
