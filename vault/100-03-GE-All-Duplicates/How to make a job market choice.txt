How to make a job market choice, when you have worked the same job for five years, assessing yourself 
It’s hard to face the facts that your career is less then satisfying. Basically, you will find that there are tons of people who feel the need to constantly be moving around in the workplace with promotions and even demotions, but then there is times where you realize that you have put five years into a job and you still have nothing to show for you. You will find that when it comes to working the same job for years, it can be very mundane. You don’t want to allow yourself to get into the slum, because it will change the way that you feel about your job. It is important that you understand why you work the jobs that you do, but it is also very important that you learn to take some of your self criticism and learn how to use it to help you grow within the company. 
If you have been in a job field for over five years, it is a good chance that you can say that you are a professional. The only thing that you will want to think about is why you have stuck with it for so long. Most people will say it is because they love what they are doing. The thing is that a lot of people do not like the hours, the pay, and they have tons of other things to complain about. You will want to keep in mind that your career development is very important to your self esteem too. If you feel that you are heading towards a rut, then you may want to consider having more educational classes or you may want to seek out more knowledge, so that you can use this information to personally help you to grow as a professional. 
It is very important that you take a good, long look at yourself. You will want to keep in mind that when you look behind the paycheck you will be able to make a choice. It may be your time to move on, but it may be your time to just stick with it. You may be on your way up the corporate ladder, but you will need to make sure that you take some time to deal with the feelings that are aroused. You will need to justify everything that you feel by validating your feelings. You will want to keep in mind that there are many reasons why you may feel like things aren’t going right for you, but there is just as many reasons why you will want to recognize your feelings, and reconsider. You will need to look past the paycheck and benefits, because you should be in a career that will make you happy.
If you truly feel that it is your time to move on, you can’t just go to work and quit. You need to make sure that this is something that you want to do. You don’t want to make any rash decisions, because they may end up being decisions that you regret. You want to be able to walk away from the current career without having to devastate yourself. You don’t want to quit because you can. You need to think if you can afford it, and also you may want to have a backup plan before you quit, so that you can keep yourself on track financially, but also on track with your career as well.
You will want to make sure that you work on your own career development so that you can figure out what is best for you and rather or not you should stay in your current field.
