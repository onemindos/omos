How to assess your personal career skills 
The one thing that you need to do when you are trying to find your career goals is to figure out your own personal characteristics.  You need to also think about your interests and your skills, the things that you like and the things that you do not like, and all your strengths and weaknesses.  You have to then match your profile up to a specific job or career goal that you have.  You might also want to identify the characteristics of your past and present jobs that you have held.  This can help you determine the best career goals for you.
Think about your self-traits that you possess.  When you are looking for a certain job or career goal you will want to make sure that you are thinking about the skills that are required for it.  Take into consideration all the previous jobs that you have held and what you liked about them and what you did not like about them.  You want to also put your education to good use too.  Thinking about all this and putting it together with your hobbies, clubs and other work that you have done is a good start to finding a good career for you.
Training is another important factor in looking for a career.  You want to think about all the training that you have previously had and how it can help you find a better and more goal oriented job that will work out right for you.  You want to think about all this and how it is going to work for you and your needs.  When you are looking for a specific job to match your personal career goals, you need to have an open mind and give everything a chance. You may need to relocate to another area in order to work or even work a different shift may be a possibility for you. 
You have to be willing to commit a certain amount of your time to looking for a career that is going to work with your personality. You have to be able to think about this and how you are going to go about finding the right job for you.  It will however take time and you need to be patient when you are looking for a job.  When you are serious about the commitment that you have towards your career you need to work with it all and come out on top.  Finding a good career move that is going to work with your needs and wants is something that will make a big difference in your everyday happiness. 
You may need to further your career education. This may mean that you need to get more education to be more advanced in your career.  There are many services that you can take advantage of through the community colleges and other centers that are in your area.  You need to sit down and think about your own career and what you would like to change about it.  Are you completely happy and it is something that you have planned for yourself?  If so then you are on your way to having a better and brighter career that you have always dreamed of having.  The type of work that you do is going to be important to your own personal happiness and how well you succeed.  You need to take this all into account so that you can be the person that you want with no worries for a brighter and more successful future.  
Talk to people that is in the field that interests you the most.  This s a great way to research the career field that you are looking into.  You want to ask them questions and find out as much as you can so that you know if this is the choice for you or not.  Get a good description of what you will need to learn and do with this career choice.  Ask them for an honest assessment of this job and what they think are the good things and the bad things about the career.  This can help you determine if this is the career path that is right for you and your personal needs.
