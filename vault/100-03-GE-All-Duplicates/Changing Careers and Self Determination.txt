Changing Careers and Self Determination 
In every job, there would come a time when you would get to assess yourself. You should ask yourself if you are happy, contented, and productive in your work. Are you professionally growing? Are you paid well? If not, then it is high time you look for a new and better job. It is hard to be unemployed these days, so it would be wise to apply for a new job position at another company while you are still employed by your current firm. Resign when you get the new job. That way, you could be sure that you would not miss any income to cover your needs. Always give your employer 30 days notice if possible to give him or her room to find another worker to fill your spot. This shows good faith, and your new boss will appreciate you. 
Overall, success at work would be ensured if you would have the proper determination and motivation. Focus on your work. In no time, you would attain that sought-after success.
Knowing when it is time to look for a new job
Our jobs are all important to be successful in living happy and healthier. When we aren’t happy with our current job, we hate getting up in the mornings to face another day. It is hard to make good decisions at the workplace if you don’t like what you’re doing.
Thinking positive is important when you are unhappy about something that is stressing you out and making you feels unhappy. Learn to take control of your feelings and think positive by deciding on the problem that is causing you duress. 
Deciding when to make a change in the career is a big step for everyone. We have to look at what our true feelings are at the current time. Do you dislike your supervisor or your co-workers? Are the morning’s hard to face because you don’t want another day in this field? 
If you face these problems, perhaps you can apply for another position at your workplace. 
If your job is causing too much stress, learn to cope with stress, since all workplaces’ are stressful when you have deadlines and a lot of responsibility. Learn to manage the stress by finding time management and stress management solutions. You want to reduce the stress so that you achieve a recuperative rest. Without a good night of sleep, it becomes harder to make good decisions. Perhaps you can benefit from meditation. Learning to relax will help you find relief from unwanted stress. By meditating daily, you can learn to thrive on the stress you have no control over. 
Meditation will help you to relax. Build your awareness so you can focus on meeting those hasty deadlines and responsibility.  Perhaps family issues at home is causing you stress, or maybe your bills are wearing you down. By meditating daily, you can learn to leave stress at home while you’re at work. Practice meditation daily to improve your overall life.  
Check out all the pro’s and con’s and make a list along with changes on how you can change the way you feel. Are there changes that you can personally make to turn things around to help you feel secure and happy again? With a list and changes that you can make to relieve stress will remind you that when you make these changes things will start looking better in life as a whole. Try exercising as an outside activity to relieve stress and help you in making the right decisions. When you exercise, you clear up cluttered joints and muscles that inspire relaxation. You will build energy also, which is your life source.
