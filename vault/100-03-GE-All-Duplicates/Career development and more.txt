Career development, how to make friends at work for the betterment of your career 

If you are working somewhere that there are other people around you working too, you might want to think about making friends.  Having friends in your workplace will help you do a few different things and give you the help that you might be looking for as well.  The more friends that you have means that you will be better able to be well liked and it may even help better your career also.
Developing your career into something that you can be happy with and proud of is important. You need to take the necessary steps that are required of you to ensure that you are keeping the lines of communication open and that you are being as nice as you can to those that are working around you. When you are better able to keep things friendly at work you are going to see new things happen for you all the time. 
Making friends with the right person at work is always a good thing.  You should watch for the people that are higher up than you so that you can start to build a relationship with you.  You want to be friends with those that can help further your career and give you the tools that may be needed in order to be promoted.  There are always going to be people in the management fields that you should keep in close contact with.  They will be able to tell you when there are going to be better positions opening up and they may even be an influence on a pay raise for yourself. This is something that you defiantly want to look out for so that you can be a more successful person in your career path.
The one thing that you need to do if you are making friends with people at work is to not talk bad about others. You can just never tell when there is going to be someone else at work that will be a “snitch” and rat you out.  They may be the type of person to find out the bad things that you have to say and then spread it all around to the other employees in the company. This can make it hard for you to get control over your career development and in return you may end up getting in a lot of trouble. 
If you are making friends within the company then you need to also be careful not to jeopardize your position.  You do not want to start trouble with yourself by messing things up with the ones in the company that can make your job harder. You do not want to do anything that could make it harder for you at work. You are looking to better your career remember?  Having people that you talk to and that you can confide in may be something for outside the work place. This way you are not putting yourself in a bad position to be demoted instead of promoted.  
Always remember that you are going to want to get ahead at the workplace by what you learn and know. You do not want to make your way up by the people that you know but sometimes this is the only way to get ahead.  If you are really looking for people that you can talk to at work, you should keep in mind that nothing is safe and you have to always look out for yourself when you are trying to better your career and make the money that you know you deserve.
