How to find a better paying position, within your own field 

First, you have to understand that there is a difference between having a good job and a good 
paying good. A lot of people become confused because they think that their pay determines what a 
good job is. The thing is, money means nothing. Unless you absolutely can’t afford to live on the 
current salary that you make, you should never leave your job because of your pay. If you really do 
like your job, you may want to take inconsideration of the contacts that you have made through the 
company and try to use them for your financial beneficial, but you will need to stop and consider 
what exactly it is that is driving you out of your job. If you really do need to find a better paying 
position, then you may want to stop and talk to your boss about the current situation. 

You may find that when you talk to your boss you will be able to work with the company and you 
may end up getting all the over time or your boss may even give you a promotion. Ask your boss if 
there is anything personally that they can do to help you out with the money situation. If it has been 
awhile since you had a raise, then you’ll need to ask your boss for a raise. Keep in mind that you 
have nothing to lose and asking never hurt anyone. 

You will also want to consider that there are a lot of other ways that you can get a different job that 
is better while trying to stay in the job field. Keep in mind that there are a lot of companies that work 
closely together as subcontractors. You may want to make an appointment with the leaders of these 
subcontractors and see if there is a place for you. Be completely honest. A lot of times they will 
accept you, because they may feel that you are needed for the companies to get along and to 
improve. Often they will try to take workers from the other business in order to stand above the other 
company. This could also backfire, so you may want to think about the pros and cons of each 
situation. 

Then there are a lot of businesses that are close competitors. Your work history will say it all. You’ll 
want to consider going to a competitive business so that you can stay within the job field, but also 
so you can get the pay raise and also a little bit more respect. Keep in mind that respect is one of 
the reasons why you should look for another job. A lot of times you may feel that you are 
disrespected by your pay and that is why they sometimes go hand in hand.

Keep in mind that your efforts to leave and go to a competitor or a subcontractor could backfire. In 
fact, the other company may tell your boss that you are searching for another company to work for. 
Also, keep in mind that there may be a conflict of interest when it comes to going to the competitor 
or subcontractor. You will need to consider some of your options, but also you will need to have a 
strong background with your current company to switch over. There are other ways for you to find a 
job within your field. Allow your contacts to work for you, but also you may want to hit the online job 
searches, so that you can become a person that is valued at a different company and also so you 
can afford some of the drama that you may get when you contact some of the competitors and co-
existing companies.
