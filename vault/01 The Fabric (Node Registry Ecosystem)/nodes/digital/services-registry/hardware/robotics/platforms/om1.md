# OM1 — OpenMind Physical Agent

> *Legacy is the mind. OM1 is the body.*

---

## What OM1 Is

OM1 is the **physical embodiment of One Mind** — an autonomous but constrained physical agent designed to act in the real world on behalf of a sovereign human or family.

It is not a consumer robot. It is not a novelty device. It is **infrastructure**.

OM1 exists to bridge:
- Intelligence → action
- Digital cognition → physical reality
- Long-term planning → daily execution

---

## Core Design Philosophy

OM1 is built around several non-negotiable principles:

### 1. Sovereignty First
- Operates **locally** by default
- No mandatory cloud dependency
- The household or land node is the root of authority

### 2. Human Agency Above All
- OM1 never replaces human decision-making
- It executes intent; it does not originate values
- Humans remain responsible for outcomes

### 3. Long-Horizon Compatibility
- Designed to remain useful across **decades**, not product cycles
- Hardware modularity and software continuity are required
- Must make sense in the 50-year civilization vision

---

## Relationship to Legacy AI

Legacy and OM1 are deliberately separated:

- **Legacy AI**
  - Cognition, memory, coordination
  - Governance of process and alignment
  - Never directly manipulates the physical world

- **OM1**
  - Perception, movement, manipulation
  - Executes bounded tasks in physical space
  - Cannot redefine goals or values

This separation ensures safety, sovereignty, and clarity of responsibility.

---

## Primary Roles (Early Phases)

At the household and homestead level, OM1 supports:
- Physical maintenance and logistics
- Environmental sensing and monitoring
- Repetitive or labor-intensive tasks
- Assistance in agriculture, construction, and repair

OM1 is designed to reduce **physical friction**, not to replace human presence.

---

## Security & Safety Constraints

OM1 operates under strict constraints:

- No autonomous lethal capability
- No centralized kill switch or remote takeover
- Fail-safe behavior prioritizes human safety over task completion
- Physical actions are logged and auditable

The goal is trust through **architectural limits**, not promises.

---

## Role in the Civilization Vision

OM1 scales alongside One Mind:

- **Single Home** → personal assistant and caretaker
- **Homestead** → land steward and infrastructure maintainer
- **Community Node** → shared labor and sensing capacity
- **Civilization Layer** → distributed physical intelligence across land

OM1 enables communities to remain **small, local, and sovereign** while benefiting from shared intelligence.

---

## Future Trajectory

Over decades, OM1 expands in capability but not autonomy:
- Greater dexterity and durability
- Specialized forms for agriculture, construction, logistics
- Networked coordination across locations via Legacy

OM1 is intentionally constrained so that **civilization scales without tyranny**.

---

## Final Principle

> *A civilization cannot live only in the cloud.*

OM1 exists so that One Mind touches soil, tools, homes, and land — ensuring the system remains grounded, regenerative, and real.
