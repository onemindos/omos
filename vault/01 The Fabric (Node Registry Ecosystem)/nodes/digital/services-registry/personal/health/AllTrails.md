# AllTrails

> **Hiking, trail running, and outdoor navigation app**

## Overview

AllTrails is the leading platform for discovering and navigating outdoor trails. Database of 400,000+ trails globally with GPS navigation, reviews, difficulty ratings, and offline maps. Used for HP/33 Joy outdoor recreation — hiking, trail running, and nature access.

## Use Cases in OneMind

1. **Trail Discovery** — Find hiking and running trails near current location
2. **Route Navigation** — GPS turn-by-turn for unfamiliar trails
3. **Offline Maps** — Download maps before going off-grid
4. **Activity Log** — Track completed hikes for HP domain Joy score
5. **Trip Planning** — Research trails before travel (LE/33 Recreation)

## Key Features

- 400,000+ trails in 100+ countries
- GPS recording and navigation
- Offline maps (Pro)
- Trail reviews and photos
- Difficulty ratings and elevation profiles
- Curated lists by region and activity
