# MTA

> Metropolitan Transportation Authority — NYC public transit (subway, bus, commuter rail).

## Key Systems
| System | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| Subway | 24/7, 472 stations |
| Bus | Local + express |
| Metro-North | Commuter rail (CT ↔ NYC) |
| LIRR | Long Island Rail Road |
| OMNY | Contactless payment (tap to pay) |

## Apps & Tools
- **MTA app** — real-time arrivals, service alerts
- **OMNY** — tap credit card / phone to pay
- **Transit app** — multi-modal trip planning
- **Citymapper** — best routing for NYC transit

## Metro-North (Greenwich → NYC)
- Station: Greenwich or Cos Cob
- ~45 min to Grand Central
- Peak/off-peak pricing
- Monthly pass available
