## What is Fanbasis?

Fanbasis is "The Operating System for Modern Digital Businesses" - a full-stack infrastructure platform for creators and entrepreneurs selling knowledge, courses, services, and digital products. It combines payments, checkout funnels, fulfillment, and analytics into one unified platform.
