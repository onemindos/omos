Squeeze Page Edit Instructions

Please make sure you follow these instructions to get the most out of your Squeeze Page.

1. Navigate inside the folder of the squeeze page that you wish to use, open the page index.html in your favorite HTML editor, you can even use a simple text editor like notepad if you wish.

2. Find the text YOUR AWEBER LIST NAME and replace it with your Aweber list name.

3. Upload the contents of the folder to your web host, in your required location.
