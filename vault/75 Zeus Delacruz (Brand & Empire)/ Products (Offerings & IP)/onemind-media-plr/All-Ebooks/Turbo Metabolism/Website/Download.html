The file & bonus reports come in a Zip folder, this may sound confusing but all it really means is it's compressed to make it easier & quicker to download. Your computer will probably have a tool pre-installed which will unzip the file for you (most do).

If you do not have the relevant tool on your machine you can download a free version here: http://www.7-zip.org/ (This is completely FREE for both PC & Mac users)

The file (The Book) is delivered as a PDF. PDF's are industry standard which means that all machines come with the software pre-instaled (PC & Mac), you don't need to do anything other than double click on the file (your book) to read.

If your machine is over a certain age there is a possibility that you may not have a PDF reader, If this is the case you can download a completely free & full version here: http://get.adobe.com/uk/reader/ (This is completely FREE for both PC & Mac users)
