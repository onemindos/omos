## Course Structure — 5 Modules, 28 Lessons

---

### MODULE 1: YOUR IDENTITY — THE FOUNDATION
*"You can't build a system for a person you haven't defined."*

**Lesson 1.1 — The Identity File**
The single most important file in your entire Codex. Everything else derives from this.
- What belongs in your identity file: name, values, principles, worldview, strengths, non-negotiables
- Why AI needs your identity to be useful (not generic)
- The difference between who you are and who you're becoming
- Hands-on: write your complete `identity.md`

**Lesson 1.2 — Mission and Purpose**
Your mission is your operating instruction for every decision. This lesson helps you write one that actually guides behavior.
- Mission vs vision vs goals: the hierarchy
- The one-sentence mission format
- Time horizons: 90-day, 1-year, 10-year, lifetime
- Hands-on: write your `mission.md`

**Lesson 1.3 — Values, Principles, and Non-Negotiables**
Values are not aspirations. They're the rules you actually live by — or the rules you want to live by. This lesson makes them explicit.
- The 5-10 core values that define your decisions
- Principles: your personal operating rules derived from values
- Non-negotiables: the lines you will not cross regardless of pressure
- Why explicit values make AI more useful (you can ask: "Does this align with my values?")

**Lesson 1.4 — Your User File for Agents**
The `USER.md` file is what tells every agent who they're working for. This lesson writes it properly.
- Name, age, location, occupation
- Communication preferences (direct, detailed, brief?)
- Goals summary for the agent to reference
- What the agent should and shouldn't do without asking
- Hands-on: finalize your `USER.md` across all agents

**Lesson 1.5 — The PI Score: Personal Intelligence Baseline**
Before you can improve yourself systematically, you need a baseline. The PI Score is a personal performance snapshot across all 10 HP domains.
- Score yourself 1–10 across: Identity, Spirituality, Mind, Body, Relationships, Finance, Mastery, Rhythm, Sovereignty, Joy
- What your score reveals about where to focus
- How to update your PI Score quarterly
- Using Oracle to surface your lowest domains automatically

---

### MODULE 2: BODY & MIND — SPARTAN PERFORMANCE
*"You are the hardware. Everything else runs on you."*

**Lesson 2.1 — The HP 27-28 Domains: Body and Fitness**
Domains 27 (Body) and 28 (Fitness) — the Spartan agent's territory. This lesson builds the tracking system.
- What belongs in domain 27 (biometrics, health markers, medical history)
- What belongs in domain 28 (training protocols, nutrition, recovery)
- The daily tracking loop: what you measure, how often, where it lives
- Spartan agent brief: morning movement check-in, workout logging, recovery tracking

**Lesson 2.2 — Building a Training Protocol in the Codex**
Your training program lives in markdown. Spartan can read it, coach it, and adapt it.
- The training protocol format: goal → program → sessions → metrics
- How to structure a mesocycle in Obsidian
- Logging workouts in the vault: simple, consistent, compoundable
- Asking Spartan to design your next training block

**Lesson 2.3 — Nutrition Architecture**
Not a diet. An architecture. One that survives a busy life and scales with your goals.
- The food philosophy file: principles, not rules
- Meal templates in the vault: quick reference, not meal prep obsession
- Tracking what matters: protein, sleep, hydration — not everything
- Integration with Spartan: daily nutrition check-in via Discord

**Lesson 2.4 — Domain 25-26: Mind and Spirituality**
Oracle's territory — consciousness, learning, mental models, and spiritual practice.
- Domain 25 (Spirituality): practice, beliefs, rituals, connection to something larger
- Domain 26 (Mind): mental models, psychology, emotional intelligence, cognitive tools
- The mental model vault: structured notes on every framework you've internalized
- Oracle brief: weekly mental performance check-in, reflection prompts

**Lesson 2.5 — Sleep, Recovery, and Nervous System Management**
The most underrated performance lever. This lesson builds the protocol.
- Sleep tracking: what to measure, what to change
- Recovery protocols: active, passive, nervous system regulation
- The circadian architecture: light, temperature, schedule
- Integration with Spartan: weekly recovery report

---

### MODULE 3: FINANCE — BUILDING YOUR FINANCIAL ARCHITECTURE
*"Money is a domain. Treat it like one."*

**Lesson 3.1 — Domain 31: Finance**
Your financial life in one domain. This lesson builds the structure.
- The 5 financial documents every Codex must have
- Net worth tracker: assets and liabilities in markdown
- Income and expense architecture: categories that match your life
- Why your finances need to be AI-readable (Oracle can spot patterns)

**Lesson 3.2 — The Financial Architecture Document**
Your complete financial picture in one structured file. Oracle reads it. You update it monthly.
- Income streams: active, passive, pending
- Fixed expenses vs variable vs investments
- Asset inventory: cash, investments, real estate, business equity
- Liability register: what you owe, to whom, at what rate
- Hands-on: build your first `financial-architecture.md`

**Lesson 3.3 — Credit, Debt, and Repair**
Your credit score is infrastructure. This lesson treats it that way.
- The credit file: current scores, accounts, inquiries, derogatory marks
- Repair strategy: what to dispute, what to pay, what to let age
- Oracle integration: monthly credit review brief
- The credit mastery timeline: 90 days to measurable improvement

**Lesson 3.4 — The Wealth-Building Protocol**
The framework for moving from income to assets.
- The hierarchy: survive → stabilize → invest → compound
- Emergency fund architecture: how much, where, accessible but not tempting
- Investment philosophy file: principles before platforms
- The 10-year financial vision: what you're building toward

---

### MODULE 4: MASTERY — SKILL ACQUISITION AT SCALE
*"Skills are assets. Build them with the same rigor as your business."*

**Lesson 4.1 — Domain 32: Mastery**
The mastery domain is your skill development system. Everything you're learning, building, or deepening lives here.
- Skill inventory: what you know, what you're building, what you want
- The skill file format: skill name → current level → target level → learning resources → practice log
- Oracle integration: weekly skill review, learning recommendations

**Lesson 4.2 — Learning Architecture**
How to learn faster and retain more — using your vault as the substrate.
- The Feynman method in markdown: explain it simply or you don't understand it
- Spaced repetition without flashcards: vault review schedules
- Active recall through Oracle: ask your agent to quiz you on what you've captured
- The 20% rule: spend 20% of learning time teaching or writing

**Lesson 4.3 — Reading and Research Integration**
Every book, article, and course you consume should feed the vault.
- The reading note format: key ideas → quotes → applications → questions
- Highlight import workflows: Kindle, web clipping, manual
- The synthesis loop: connecting new knowledge to existing vault notes
- Oracle brief: weekly reading summary and insight surfacing

---

### MODULE 5: RHYTHM & JOY — THE OPERATING CADENCE
*"Performance without rhythm is exhaustion. Joy without direction is drift."*

**Lesson 5.1 — Domain 33: Rhythm**
Your personal operating cadence — the daily, weekly, monthly rituals that keep you performing without burning out.
- The rhythm file: your morning ritual, work blocks, evening review, weekly reset
- Why rhythm reduces decision fatigue
- Agent integration: Oracle and Spartan brief you on cadence adherence
- Hands-on: write your complete `rhythm.md`

**Lesson 5.2 — The Morning Ritual Architecture**
The first 60 minutes set everything. This lesson designs yours.
- The 5 elements: movement, stillness, fuel, review, intention
- Customizing for your life (parent, founder, athlete — different templates)
- The morning briefing: Legacy delivers your day in 2 minutes
- Hands-on: write your morning ritual protocol

**Lesson 5.3 — Domain 34: Joy**
Joy is not optional. It's a performance input. This lesson makes it explicit.
- The joy inventory: what activities restore you vs drain you
- The joy budget: time allocated to what matters most to you
- Non-negotiable joy items: what you protect regardless of schedule pressure
- Oracle integration: quarterly joy audit — "Am I doing the things that fill me?"

**Lesson 5.4 — The HP Weekly Review**
Close the loop on your personal pillar every week.
- The HP review template: body, mind, finance, mastery, rhythm, joy
- 15 minutes, every Sunday, with Oracle running the prompts
- The HP scorecard: simple traffic light for each domain
- Hands-on: run your first HP weekly review

**Lesson 5.5 — HP Graduation: Your Personal Pillar is Alive**
Checklist for a fully operational HP domain.
- Identity + mission + values files complete
- PI Score baseline established
- Financial architecture documented
- At least one health metric being tracked daily
- Weekly HP review ritual running
- Spartan and Oracle agents configured for your domains
