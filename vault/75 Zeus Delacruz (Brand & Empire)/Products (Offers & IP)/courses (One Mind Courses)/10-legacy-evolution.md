## Course Structure — 5 Modules, 25 Lessons

---

### MODULE 1: THE LEGACY LENS
*"Every decision you make today echoes forward. Most people never think about it."*

**Lesson 1.1 — What Legacy Evolution Means**
LE is not just "family stuff." It's the pillar responsible for everything that outlives you — relationships, home, community, and the knowledge and values you pass forward.
- Why most productivity systems ignore this pillar entirely
- The generational lens: you are a link in a chain
- The 500-year family vision: what do you want your family to look like in 500 years?
- Why Haven agent covers this domain: the home needs its own intelligence

**Lesson 1.2 — The 8 LE Domains**
A map of everything in the Legacy Evolution pillar.
- Domain 50 (Home): physical space, household operations, maintenance
- Domain 51 (Covenant): primary relationship/marriage
- Domain 52 (Offspring): children — their development, education, wellbeing
- Domain 53 (Parents): aging parents, family of origin relationships
- Domain 54 (Heritage): family history, ancestry, traditions, stories
- Domain 55 (Health): family health records, medical history, wellness
- Domain 56 (Estate): wills, insurance, digital accounts, asset transfer
- Domain 57 (Community): neighborhood, faith community, chosen family

**Lesson 1.3 — Why Your Relationships Need to Be in Your Vault**
Your agents can only help with your relationships if they know who those people are. This lesson builds the relationship registry.
- The relationship file format: name → role → history → current status → how to maintain → shared goals
- Why context about people makes AI more thoughtful, not creepy
- The difference between surveillance and stewardship
- Hands-on: write files for your 5 most important relationships

---

### MODULE 2: HOME OPERATIONS
*"Your home is an operating system. Run it like one."*

**Lesson 2.1 — Domain 50: Home**
The physical home as a managed system — not chaos you react to.
- The home inventory: what you own, where it is, when it needs maintenance
- The home maintenance calendar: seasonal, annual, recurring tasks
- The home operations runbook: how your household actually works
- Haven agent integration: maintenance reminders, household task routing

**Lesson 2.2 — The Household SOPs**
Standard operating procedures for the recurring work of running a home.
- Weekly household rhythm: cleaning, groceries, admin
- Monthly home review: maintenance checklist, inventory audit
- Emergency protocols: what to do when things break
- Hands-on: write your first 3 household SOPs

**Lesson 2.3 — Home Finance Architecture**
Your home has its own financial system — separate from personal finance.
- Home budget: mortgage/rent, utilities, maintenance reserve, improvements
- The 1% rule: reserve 1% of home value annually for maintenance
- Appliance and system tracker: age, warranty, replacement timeline
- Integration with your financial architecture from Course 9

**Lesson 2.4 — Domain 51: Covenant — Your Primary Relationship**
Your primary relationship is a domain. It deserves intentional architecture.
- The relationship vision file: what you're building together
- Shared goals in the Codex: financial, lifestyle, family
- The weekly relationship review: 30 minutes, 3 questions
- Haven agent: anniversary reminders, shared goal tracking, date planning

---

### MODULE 3: FAMILY KNOWLEDGE VAULT
*"Every family has a story. Most of it dies with the elders. Yours won't."*

**Lesson 3.1 — Domain 54: Heritage**
The family knowledge vault — the system that preserves your family's history, stories, and identity.
- What belongs in the heritage domain: history, genealogy, traditions, stories, values
- The family chronicle: a structured document of your family's narrative
- Interviewing elders: how to extract and capture irreplaceable stories
- Hands-on: write the first entry in your family chronicle

**Lesson 3.2 — Family Values and Traditions**
The values and traditions that define your family — made explicit, documented, and transmitted.
- Family values: the 5-10 principles your family lives by
- Family traditions: what you do, when, and why — documented so they survive
- The family constitution: a founding document for your household
- Hands-on: draft your family values document

**Lesson 3.3 — Domain 52: Offspring**
If you have children (or plan to), this domain systematizes their development and wellbeing.
- Child profiles: personality, learning style, interests, milestones
- Education tracking: school, extracurriculars, skills being developed
- The parent operating system: routines, rituals, and principles
- Passing down knowledge: how to use the vault as a letter to your children

**Lesson 3.4 — The 500-Year Vision**
The generational horizon — what do you want your family to look like in 500 years?
- Writing your generational vision document
- The values, wealth, and knowledge you want to transmit
- How every decision today serves the 500-year vision
- Hands-on: write your first generational vision draft

---

### MODULE 4: ESTATE & DIGITAL SOVEREIGNTY
*"The most loving thing you can do for your family is have your affairs in order."*

**Lesson 4.1 — Domain 56: Estate**
Estate planning is not just for the wealthy. This lesson builds the minimum viable estate setup.
- The 5 documents every adult needs: will, healthcare directive, power of attorney, beneficiary designations, letter of instruction
- Where to store them: physical + digital + vault reference
- The estate review: annual check that everything is current
- Haven agent: annual estate review reminder

**Lesson 4.2 — Digital Estate Planning**
Your digital life is an asset. This lesson documents and plans for its transfer.
- Digital account inventory: everything you own online
- Password and access documentation: stored in 1Password, referenced in vault
- Social media and platform instructions: what happens to each account
- Crypto and digital asset transfer protocols
- The digital will: instructions for your executor

**Lesson 4.3 — Insurance Architecture**
Insurance is risk transfer. This lesson builds the coverage map.
- Insurance inventory: life, health, disability, property, liability
- Coverage gaps analysis: what's unprotected
- Annual insurance review protocol
- Integration with home and financial architecture

---

### MODULE 5: COMMUNITY & COMPLETION
*"No one builds alone. Your community is infrastructure."*

**Lesson 5.1 — Domain 57: Community**
Your neighborhood, faith community, mastermind, and chosen family — documented and maintained.
- Community map: who you're connected to and why
- The contribution file: how you give back, what you build with others
- Community goals: what you're trying to create in your local environment
- Haven agent: community event tracking, connection maintenance reminders

**Lesson 5.2 — Relationship Maintenance System**
Important relationships don't maintain themselves. This lesson builds the system.
- The relationship review: quarterly check on your key relationships
- The outreach calendar: who to contact, how often, with what intention
- Haven agent: relationship maintenance reminders via Discord
- The "strong ties" vs "weak ties" strategy

**Lesson 5.3 — Domain 53: Parents and Family of Origin**
Managing the complexity of aging parents and family of origin relationships.
- Parent profile: health status, needs, wishes, medical history
- The family meeting protocol: regular communication, shared decisions
- Estate coordination: understanding their plans and your role
- Hands-on: write your parent profile files

**Lesson 5.4 — LE Weekly Review**
Close the loop on your home pillar every week.
- The LE review template: home, relationships, family, community
- 10 minutes, integrated into your broader weekly review
- Haven agent prompts: what needs attention this week in the home domain
- Hands-on: run your first LE weekly review

**Lesson 5.5 — LE Graduation: Your Home Pillar is Alive**
Checklist for a fully operational LE domain.
- All 8 LE domain folders created and populated
- Relationship registry complete for top 10 relationships
- Family values and traditions documented
- Home operations runbook with at least 5 SOPs
- Estate basics: will location, digital account inventory, insurance summary
- Haven agent configured and running
