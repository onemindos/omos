## Course Structure — 3 Modules, 12 Lessons

---

### MODULE 1: THE PROBLEM
*"You're not lazy. You're fragmented. There's a difference."*

**Lesson 1.1 — Your Life is Running in 47 Tabs**
The average person has their goals in one app, their tasks in another, their notes in a third, and their "AI assistant" in a fourth — and none of them talk to each other. This lesson names the problem.
- The fragmentation tax: the hidden cost of scattered systems
- Why more apps = more chaos, not more productivity
- The moment everything breaks: when life gets complex
- What a unified system actually feels like

**Lesson 1.2 — Why Every System You've Tried Has Failed**
GTD, PARA, Second Brain, bullet journaling, Notion templates — none of them stuck. This lesson explains why — not because you failed, but because they were built for a pre-AI world.
- Systems built for humans working alone
- No AI layer, no context, no memory
- The discipline trap: systems that require willpower to maintain
- The gap: none of them tell AI who you are or what you want

**Lesson 1.3 — The AI Era Requires a New Operating System**
AI is not a better search engine. It's an agent that can act on your behalf — but only if it understands your life. This lesson makes the case for why your system needs to be rebuilt from the ground up.
- What AI can do when given proper context
- What AI does without context (generic, forgettable, useless)
- The new constraint: AI needs structure, not creativity
- The opportunity: a system that compounds in the AI era

**Lesson 1.4 — The Human + AI Equation**
You bring vision, judgment, relationships, and values. AI brings memory, speed, execution, and 24/7 availability. This lesson defines the partnership that makes One Mind work.
- What you bring to the equation
- What AI brings to the equation
- Why neither alone is enough
- What the compound effect looks like after 90 days

---

### MODULE 2: THE FRAMEWORK
*"One system. Four pillars. One operating layer. One loop."*

**Lesson 2.1 — The 4 Pillars of Life**
Everything in your life belongs to one of 4 pillars. Once you see it, you can't unsee it.
- SO (Sovereign Operations): the engine that runs everything — AI, tech, automation
- HP (Holistic Performance): you — health, mind, money, identity
- LE (Legacy Evolution): your people — family, relationships, home, community
- GE (Generational Empire): your work — business, brand, wealth, impact
- Why SO is the operating layer, not the fourth "life area"

**Lesson 2.2 — The CODE× Method in 5 Minutes**
The 5-step loop that runs inside One Mind. Capture → Organize → Direct → Execute → × Multiply. This lesson explains each step at the high level.
- C: Capture — nothing stays in your head
- O: Organize — everything finds its domain
- D: Direct — goals AI can actually act on
- E: Execute — humans and agents working in parallel
- × Multiply — the review loop that makes everything compound

**Lesson 2.3 — The Codex: Your Life in Markdown**
The Codex is your structured knowledge vault — the single source of truth for your entire life. Stored in Obsidian. Version-controlled on GitHub. Readable by any AI.
- What the Codex is: a structured folder system in plain text
- Why Obsidian: open, local, sovereign, AI-native
- Why GitHub: permanent, versioned, accessible from anywhere
- Why markdown: lives forever, readable by any intelligence

**Lesson 2.4 — The Agent Layer**
The 9 AI agents of One Mind OS — each one covering a domain of your life, all orchestrated by Legacy.
- Legacy: your orchestrator — the agent that knows everything
- Spartan, Oracle, Eden, Haven, Empire, Guardian, Grid, Trinity
- How agents get their intelligence: IDENTITY + SOUL + USER + CODEX
- What a day looks like when your agents are running

### MODULE 3: IS THIS FOR YOU?
*"One Mind is not for everyone. Here's who it's built for."*

**Lesson 3.1 — Who One Mind Is Built For**
One Mind works for a specific kind of person. This lesson helps you self-select honestly.
- You're building something — a business, a life, a legacy
- You're overwhelmed by complexity, not by laziness
- You're willing to invest 10–20 hours upfront to save 10 hours per week forever
- You want AI to work *with* you, not just answer questions

**Lesson 3.2 — The 3 Tiers: Explorer, Sovereign, Legacy**
One Mind has three levels of depth. This lesson maps them to where you are right now.
- Explorer (Free): understand the framework, get the templates, join the community
- Sovereign ($49/mo): build your vault, run your first agent, daily system live
- Legacy ($97/mo): full stack deployed — VPS, 9 agents, automations, intelligence pipeline

**Lesson 3.3 — What's Possible: The 90-Day Transformation**
What your life looks like after 90 days of running One Mind. Specific, real, and achievable.
- Week 1: vault live, first agent responding
- Week 2-4: daily CODE× habit locked in, tasks syncing automatically
- Month 2: VPS running, all agents live, morning briefing delivered daily
- Month 3: system operating largely without you — compounding quietly in the background

**Lesson 3.4 — Your First Step**
Three paths forward depending on where you are. Pick one and start.
- Path 1 (Explorer): download the vault template and read the framework docs
- Path 2 (Sovereign): enroll in Course 1 — ONE MIND OS
- Path 3 (Legacy): start with Course 1, commit to Course 2 immediately after
