## Course Structure — 6 Modules, 32 Lessons

### MODULE 1: THE EMPIRE MINDSET
*"You're not building a job. You're building an asset."*

**Lesson 1.1 — What Generational Empire Means**
GE is not just "business." It's every vehicle for creating value in the world — and every asset that compounds beyond your active hours.
- The empire vs the job: what's the difference in how you think?
- The 9 GE domains: Brand, Real Estate, Acquisitions, Holdings, Investments, Innovation, Strategy, Partnerships, Philanthropy
- Why "business" undersells it — and why that matters for how you build
- The generational horizon: what are you building that works without you?

**Lesson 1.2 — YOU Inc. — The Operating Framework**
You are the CEO of a company called YOU Inc. Every resource you have — time, money, skills, relationships, reputation — is capital. This lesson reframes everything.
- YOU Inc. balance sheet: assets, liabilities, equity
- YOU Inc. income statement: revenue, costs, profit
- YOU Inc. strategy: what markets are you in, what's your moat?
- Hands-on: write your first YOU Inc. overview document

**Lesson 1.3 — The GE Domains Map**
A complete tour of all 9 GE domains — what belongs where.
- Domain 75 (Brand): identity, positioning, content, audience
- Domain 76 (Real Estate): property investments, home equity strategy
- Domain 77 (Acquisitions): businesses, assets, IP you're acquiring
- Domain 78 (Holdings): what you own — equity, licenses, royalties
- Domain 79 (Investments): stocks, crypto, alternative investments
- Domain 80 (Innovation): products, tools, IP you're creating
- Domain 81 (Strategy): planning, decision frameworks, competitive intelligence
- Domain 82 (Partnerships): collaborators, JVs, key relationships with leverage
- Domain 83 (Philanthropy): giving, impact, legacy beyond profit

**Lesson 1.4 — Empire Agent Configuration**
Empire is your GE intelligence agent — covering all 9 business domains.
- Empire's role: business intelligence, strategic briefs, opportunity tracking
- The Empire agent files: IDENTITY, SOUL, USER, CODEX
- What Empire monitors: market signals, your metrics, competitor moves, pipeline
- Hands-on: configure your Empire agent from the provided templates

### MODULE 2: BRAND — YOUR IDENTITY IN THE MARKET
*"Your brand is not your logo. It's the promise you keep."*

**Lesson 2.1 — Domain 75: Brand**
Your brand is the most valuable asset you'll build. This lesson documents it properly.
- Brand positioning: who you are, who you serve, why you're different
- Brand voice: how you communicate — tone, vocabulary, energy
- Brand story: the narrative that makes people care
- Hands-on: write your complete `brand-identity.md`

**Lesson 2.2 — Personal Brand Architecture**
For founders and creators, personal brand is the distribution channel everything else runs through.
- The personal brand equation: expertise + personality + consistency
- Your content pillars: the 3-5 topics you own in your audience's mind
- The content-to-trust pipeline: how content builds the authority that sells
- Platform strategy: where you show up and why

**Lesson 2.3 — Content as Infrastructure**
Content is not a marketing tactic. It's an asset that compounds. This lesson builds the content system.
- The content flywheel: one idea → multiple formats → distributed everywhere
- The content calendar architecture in Obsidian: capture → draft → schedule → distribute → repurpose
- Empire agent integration: content performance tracking, idea surfacing, trend monitoring
- Hands-on: build your content calendar structure in the vault

**Lesson 2.4 — Audience Intelligence**
Your audience is a domain. This lesson builds the intelligence system around it.
- Audience profile files: who they are, what they want, what they fear
- The community-as-moat strategy: why your audience is your biggest competitive advantage
- Feedback loops: how to systematically capture what your audience tells you
- Empire brief: weekly audience growth and engagement report

### MODULE 3: BUSINESS ARCHITECTURE
*"If you can't explain your business in a markdown file, you don't understand your business."*

**Lesson 3.1 — The Business Architecture Document**
One file that captures your entire business model. Empire can read it, you can update it, and any agent can reason about it.
- Business model canvas in markdown format
- Revenue streams: active, recurring, passive — with metrics for each
- Cost structure: fixed, variable, one-time investments
- Key metrics: the 5 numbers that tell you if the business is healthy
- Hands-on: write your `business-architecture.md`

**Lesson 3.2 — Domain 81: Strategy**
Your strategic planning system — where you're going and how you're getting there.
- The strategy file: vision → goals → initiatives → projects → tasks
- Quarterly planning in the Codex: OKRs adapted for One Mind
- The competitive landscape document: who else is in your market
- Empire agent: quarterly strategy review prompts and intelligence gathering

**Lesson 3.3 — Domain 82: Partnerships**
Partnerships are leverage. This lesson builds the partnership intelligence system.
- Partnership registry: every significant business relationship documented
- The partnership evaluation framework: what makes a partner valuable?
- JV and collaboration tracking: active deals, pipeline, completed
- Empire agent: partnership opportunity monitoring, relationship maintenance reminders

**Lesson 3.4 — Operations Documentation**
Your business has recurring operations. Document them before they break.
- Core operational SOPs: the 10 processes your business runs on
- The operations runbook: how your business actually works, step by step
- The delegation document: what can AI run, what needs you, what needs a team
- Hands-on: write your 3 most critical operational SOPs

### MODULE 4: WEALTH ARCHITECTURE
*"Income is not wealth. Assets that generate income are wealth."*

**Lesson 4.1 — Domain 79: Investments**
Your investment philosophy and portfolio in the Codex.
- Investment philosophy file: your principles, risk tolerance, time horizon
- Portfolio tracker: what you own, in what proportion, with what thesis
- The investment review: quarterly rebalancing and thesis check
- Empire agent: market signal monitoring, portfolio performance brief

**Lesson 4.2 — Domain 78: Holdings**
Everything you own that generates value — equity, IP, licenses, royalties.
- Holdings inventory: every asset and its current value/status
- Equity tracker: cap tables, vesting schedules, dilution tracking
- IP registry: what you've created, what's protected, what generates royalties
- The holdings review: annual audit of everything you own

**Lesson 4.3 — Domain 76: Real Estate**
Property as a wealth vehicle — whether you own or aspire to.
- Real estate philosophy: your approach to property investment
- Property tracker: what you own, what it's worth, what it generates
- The acquisition pipeline: properties you're evaluating
- Empire agent: market intelligence, deal flow monitoring

**Lesson 4.4 — The Wealth Architecture Document**
One file that shows your complete wealth picture — current state and target state.
- Net worth by asset class: liquid, illiquid, business, real estate
- Income streams mapped: active, semi-passive, passive
- Wealth targets: 1-year, 5-year, 10-year with specific numbers
- The gap analysis: what needs to grow, what needs to be built, what needs to be sold
- Hands-on: write your complete `wealth-architecture.md`

### MODULE 6: THE GE OPERATING RHYTHM
*"An empire without a review is an empire that drifts."*

**Lesson 6.1 — The GE Weekly Review**
Close the loop on your business pillar every week.
- The GE review template: brand, revenue, pipeline, operations, intelligence
- 20 minutes, every Monday morning, with Empire running the brief
- The GE scorecard: traffic light for each business domain
- Hands-on: run your first GE weekly review

**Lesson 6.2 — The Quarterly Empire Review**
Zoom out and recalibrate every 90 days.
- Strategic review: are you still on the right path?
- Metrics deep dive: what's growing, what's stalling, what needs to be cut
- Portfolio rebalancing: wealth, time, energy, focus
- The next quarter plan: 3 priorities, 3 metrics, 3 projects

**Lesson 6.3 — Integrating GE with HP and LE**
Your business exists to fund your life. Your life exists to give your business meaning. This lesson connects the pillars.
- How GE revenue decisions affect LE security
- How HP performance affects GE output
- The cross-pillar review: one monthly session where all three connect
- The sustainability question: is your empire sustainable for the human running it?

**Lesson 6.4 — GE Graduation: Your Empire Pillar is Alive**
Checklist for a fully operational GE domain.
- All 9 GE domain folders created and structured
- Brand identity document complete
- Business architecture document written
- Wealth architecture document with current net worth
- Empire agent configured and delivering weekly briefs
- GE weekly review ritual running
