## How to Use This Effectively

**Weekly (Sunday):** Fill the weekly snapshot. Takes 5 minutes.

**Monthly:** Look at 4 weeks side by side. What's the trend? Which metric is slipping? Pick one to improve next month.

**Quarterly:** Pull all 13 weeks. Calculate averages. Compare to annual targets. Adjust targets if needed.

**What NOT to track:** Everything. Pick the metrics that actually matter to your goals this year. Remove any metric you stop caring about. Add new ones when goals change.

**Metric selection principle:** Track the leading indicators, not just outcomes. Sleep and energy predict performance. Conversations predict relationship quality. Vault entries predict system health.

---

## Metric Definitions

**Energy (1–10):** Average subjective energy level across the day. 10 = exceptional, 7 = good normal, 5 = mediocre, below 5 = something needs fixing.

**Savings rate:** (Income - Expenses) / Income × 100. Track the percentage, not just the dollar amount.

**Quality time:** Hours of undivided attention with partner, children, or parents — not just being in the same room.

**Agent stack health:** Is the agent system running? Did you get your morning brief? Are automations firing? 5 = everything working perfectly, 1 = major issues.

**Review completed:** Did you complete your Sunday weekly review this week? Binary. This is the most important metric in the entire tracker.
