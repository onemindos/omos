## The 5 Steps

### C — Capture
*Nothing gets lost. Everything has one landing zone.*

Before you can organize anything, you have to stop losing things. One inbox. Every idea, task, reference, and observation lands here first. No decisions at capture time — just catch it.

Your inbox: `99 Inbox (Queue)/` in your vault. One place. Always.

### O — Organize
*Everything routed to where it belongs.*

Once captured, items get routed to their domain. Is this about your health? Goes to HP. Your business? GE. Your home? LE. Infrastructure? SO. Organized once, findable forever.

The 4 pillars give everything a home. Nothing is homeless.

### D — Direct
*Goals set. Priorities chosen. Agents assigned.*

Organizing is not enough — you have to decide what matters. Directing is where you set goals, assign priorities, and tell your agents what to work on. This is the command function.

You direct. Your agents execute. That's the leverage.

### E — Execute
*Focused action on what was directed.*

Deep work. Undistracted. Supported by agents handling everything else. Execution is where the work actually gets done — but it's fast and clean because Capture, Organize, and Direct already handled the chaos.

### × — Multiply
*Review. Learn. Compound. Repeat.*

The × is the multiplication sign — not the letter X. Every review cycle makes the system smarter. Your agents improve. Your vault deepens. Your clarity sharpens. The work you did last month compounds into next month.

This is what makes CODE× a loop, not a list. **× feeds back into Capture.** The system never stops improving.
