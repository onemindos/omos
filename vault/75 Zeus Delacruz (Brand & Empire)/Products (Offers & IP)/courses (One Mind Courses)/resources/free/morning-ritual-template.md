# Morning Ritual Template

> *The 10-minute protocol that sets the entire day.*

**When:** First thing after waking — before email, before messages, before social media.
**Time:** 10–15 minutes
**Where:** `HP/32 Rhythm/daily/YYYY-MM-DD-morning.md` or your daily note

---

## The Sequence

### 1. Physical First (Before You Open Anything)
*Do this before touching your phone or computer.*

- [ ] Hydrate — 500ml water before anything else
- [ ] Move — 5 minutes minimum (walk, stretch, pushups — anything)
- [ ] Breathe — 1 minute box breathing (4 in / 4 hold / 4 out / 4 hold)

### 2. Read the Agent Brief (3 minutes)
*Open Discord → #legacy channel. Legacy's morning brief is already there.*

Read it once. Don't respond, don't act — just orient.

The brief contains:
- Your top 3 active goals (current state)
- Today's Todoist tasks due
- Any flags from overnight
- One thing per pillar to hold in mind

### 3. Set Your 3 MITs (2 minutes)
*The 3 things that if done, make today a win. Nothing else matters until these are done.*

**MIT 1 (Primary — Deep Work):**
> [FILL: Most important single task today]

**MIT 2 (Secondary):**
> [FILL: Second most important task]

**MIT 3 (Supporting):**
> [FILL: Third task — often a communication or admin item]

Write these in your daily note. If they're not written, they're wishes.

### 4. Pillar Check (2 minutes)
*60 seconds of honest assessment per pillar. One word or one sentence.*

**SO — Is my system running?**
> [FILL: Any agent alerts? Anything broken overnight?]

**HP — How am I feeling?**
> [FILL: Energy level 1-5, anything my body needs today?]

**LE — Who do I need to connect with?**
> [FILL: Any family/relationship context to hold?]

**GE — What moves the empire forward today?**
> [FILL: One GE-specific action or awareness]

### 5. Intention Statement (1 minute)
*One sentence. What kind of day is this?*

> "Today I will [FILL: primary intention] and I will do it by [FILL: specific commitment]."

Example: *"Today I will complete the course outline and I will do it by staying off Discord until noon."*

## Notes

- If the morning brief isn't in Discord, send Legacy: *"Morning brief please."*
- If you have less than 10 minutes, cut to: MITs only (2 minutes). Don't skip them.
- On travel days: do the sequence in your phone notes. The location doesn't matter.
- If you miss a morning: don't catch up. Just run tomorrow's ritual tomorrow.
