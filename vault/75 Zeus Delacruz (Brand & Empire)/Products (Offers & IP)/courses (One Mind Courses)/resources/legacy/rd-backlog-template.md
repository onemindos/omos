## How to Use This Backlog

**Adding items:** Drop any research idea, technology to evaluate, or knowledge gap here. Don't filter — capture everything. Use the inbox if you're in a hurry.

**Prioritizing:** At each weekly review, pick 1 item to promote to an active research sprint. Only 1–2 items should be "Active" at any time.

**Pruning:** Quarterly, archive or drop items older than 6 months with no movement. If it hasn't been worth starting, it wasn't that important.

**Status values:** `idea` → `queued` → `active` → `complete` → `archived`
