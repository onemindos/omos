**Credit Impact**

An important step in the Turnbull program is to consult with a Turnbull
attorney and, after discussing it with them, to choose to voluntarily
stop paying your creditor(s) directly (if you haven't already) and
instead make monthly deposits into your Dedicated Account. The decision
to stop making payments on your debt(s) is yours, and yours alone, to
make. As your debt(s) go past due, your creditworthiness will likely be
adversely affected. However, this is an important and necessary step
because the stopped payments signal to your creditor(s) that you are in
financial distress, which gives Turnbull the leverage to negotiate
settlement(s) on your debt(s). Make sure to discuss the pros and cons of
stopping payments during your consultation with your Turnbull attorney.

**Accrued Interest**

When your debt(s) go past due, your balance(s) may continue to increase
due to interest, late fees, and/or penalties. Fortunately, adjustments
for these accruals are included in your program summary estimates, and
the amount of your program deposits should be sufficient to resolve the
debt(s) you enroll plus any additional interest and fees. Note that
Turnbull fees are based on your enrolled balance(s), not your accrued
balance(s) at the time of settlement.

**Legal Action**

When you stop paying your creditors, there's a chance they may take
legal action to collect on the debt. Should that happen, you must
immediately provide Turnbull with all legal documents when you receive
them. You will have the benefit of a Turnbull attorney who can provide a
number of services including providing you with legal counsel, working
directly with your creditor or its hired legal counsel to attempt to
negotiate a resolution, and providing litigation defense services. There
are no additional Turnbull fees for attorney services. However, you will
be responsible for court fees and related costs. The attorney can only
negotiate a settlement if there are sufficient funds in your Dedicated
Account and it may be necessary to deposit funds earlier than expected
to settle the debt if your creditor requires it. This should not
increase the total cost of your Turnbull program.

**Discontinue Account Use**

While you're in the program, Turnbull asks that you do not incur any
additional charges on enrolled debt(s), as this will materially impair
Turnbull's ability to secure settlement offers. The Turnbull program
only covers the debt(s) that you enroll in the program.

**Monthly Payments**

Turnbull does not make any monthly payments to your creditor(s)
directly. Payment(s) to your creditor(s) are only made after a
settlement is agreed to with your creditor(s) and you authorize the
settlement. Once you have authorized a settlement, you also authorize
Turnbull to instruct your Dedicated Account administrator to make
payments directly from your Dedicated Account to the settling
creditor(s) according to the terms of your settlement agreement.

**Timing of Settlements**

Provided you make your scheduled deposits to your Dedicated Account,
Turnbull expects to make a bona fide offer to settle your first debt
within 3 months of your first program deposit. Turnbull expects that you
will need to accumulate up to 25% of the amount due on an enrolled debt
for Turnbull to make a settlement offer (in addition to the amount
needed to make payments on any ongoing settlements).

**Program Cancellation**

** **Should you decide to cancel your Turnbull program, you can do so at
any time without penalty. Any funds that may remain in your Dedicated
Account, minus fees for services already provided, shall be returned to
you within seven (7) business days. Please be aware, if you cancel
Turnbull's services while Turnbull is actively representing you in a
litigation on an enrolled debt, Turnbull, subject to legal obligations,
will likely also discontinue representing you on that debt.

**Refunds**

Turnbull does not charge any fees in advance of getting a debt
negotiated for you. Once Turnbull does earn fees, however, they are
non-refundable.

**Potential Tax Consequences**

If your creditor settles your debt for less than what you owe, the
savings could be considered taxable income. However, you may qualify as
exempt if your total liability exceeds your assets. You should consult
with a tax professional if you receive a 1099-C form from your
creditor(s).

**Dedicated Account**

The Turnbull program is designed so that you will make monthly deposits
into a Dedicated Account. These program deposits are not made directly
to Turnbull.You own and control this account as well as the funds in it
which you may withdraw at any time without penalty (subject to unpaid
fees). The availability of funds in your Dedicated Account is what
enables Turnbull to negotiate settlements with your creditor(s). If your
program with Turnbull terminates or expires for any reason, you are
entitled to receive all funds in the account, other than funds earned by
Turnbull and/or the thirty-party account administrator.

**Program Summary Estimates**

Your program summary estimates (including program length, deposit
amount, and total cost) are based on your ability to maintain your
monthly program deposit schedule and on Turnbull's history of
negotiating settlements with creditor(s) on your enrolled debts(s).
However, the result of each negotiation of your enrolled debt(s) may
vary depending on the actual settlement percentage obtained as well as
the consistency and amount of your program deposits during your
program.So the total settlement amount required and the time it may take
to resolve your enrolled debt(s) may be more, or less, than initially
estimated. Turnbull's fees, which are based on a percentage of your
enrolled balances, are set and will never increase on a per debt basis.

**General**

Your program length and total program costs are estimates based on
Turnbull's experience with your creditors. Your actual program length
and costs may vary depending on the actual settlement percentages
obtained as well as the consistency and amount of your program deposits
during your program. If you enroll in the Turnbull debt negotiation
program, you should understand that it's a process that will continue
for many months, even years. Your creditor(s) will likely attempt to
collect on your debt(s), either directly, or through third parties, such
as collection agencies and/or debt buyers. The time needed to produce a
settlement depends on a number of factors, including: (a) the details of
your financial hardship; (b) the age and balance of the debt(s) that you
owe your creditors; (c) the consistency of your program deposits and the
funds you have available to pay for a settlement; and (d) the
willingness of your creditors to enter into debt negotiations. The
Turnbull program requires you to set aside sufficient funds on a regular
basis to enable Turnbull to negotiate settlement offers on your behalf.
Turnbull cannot guarantee any particular result.
