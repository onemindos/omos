## GTM Sequence — Book vs Community vs Both

**Both. But sequence matters.**

| Step | What | Why | Timeline |
|------|------|-----|----------|
| 1 | **Manifesto** (short ebook, 50–80 pages) | Establishes authority. Free or $9.99. Explains the 5-step method + 3+1 model. | Write in 2–4 weeks with AI assist |
| 2 | **Template vault** (codex-template) | People download and start using it. "Try before you buy." | Already exists — polish it |
| 3 | **Community** (Circle, Discord, or similar) | Recurring revenue. People need help implementing. | Launch alongside or right after the book |
| 4 | **Course / Coaching** | Premium tier. Walk people through their first 30 days. | After you have 50–100 community members |
| 5 | **Full book** (200+ pages, paperback) | Legitimacy play. Gets you on podcasts, in bookstores. | 6–12 months out |

> Tiago Forte's path: blog posts → cohort course → book → app → community. He built for 5+ years before the book.
> You can compress this because AI lets you write faster, and the framework already exists.
> **The manifesto is the minimum viable product.** Write it from CODEX-FRAMEWORK.md — it's already 90% of the content, just in documentation format instead of narrative format.

## The 5 Product Layers

```
LAYER 1: THE IDEA (free, everywhere)
"You + AI = One Mind. Here's the 5-step loop."
→ Blog posts, social, podcast appearances, the codex-live site

LAYER 2: THE METHOD (manifesto, $0-10)
CODEX method + 3+1 model + first 30 days playbook
→ Short book / ebook

LAYER 3: THE STARTER (free, codex-template)
Empty vault with structure, templates, example content
→ GitHub repo, one-click Obsidian setup

LAYER 4: THE COMMUNITY ($10-30/mo)
Weekly calls, shared implementations, Zeus answers questions
→ Circle or Discord

LAYER 5: THE ARCHITECT (premium, $500+)
Zeus sets up YOUR codex with YOUR domains, YOUR agents
→ 1-on-1 or small cohort
```

**You live at Layer 5. You teach Layers 1–3. You monetize Layers 2–4.**
