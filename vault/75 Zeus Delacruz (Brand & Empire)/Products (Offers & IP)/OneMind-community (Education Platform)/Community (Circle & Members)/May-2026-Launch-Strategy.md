# One Mind Community — May 2026 Launch Strategy

> **Goal:** 100 paid members by May 31, 2026
> **Current:** 3 members (2 non-paying) as of 2026-05-07
> **Days remaining:** 24
> **Required pace:** ~4 new paying members/day

---

## Honest Assessment

### Hard Blockers (must fix first)
1. **No lesson content** — 339 lessons exist in Circle as empty drafts. A paying member who joins today gets a skeleton, not a course. Refund risk is high.
2. **No audience funnel** — no visible traffic source, lead magnet, or waitlist in the community
3. **No onboarding flow** — new members land in a community with no posts, no activity, no social proof

### Realistic Ceiling
- **20–40 paid members** is achievable in 24 days with fast content execution + audience activation
- **100 paid members** requires a big push: existing email list, collab, viral post, or founding member campaign
- Audience size across platforms is the real variable
