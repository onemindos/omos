**Company Overview:** Wealth Warriors is a comprehensive financial
platform designed to empower individuals to achieve financial freedom.
Our platform offers a suite of financial tools, a community for users to
engage and share insights, and an AI-driven assistant named Legacy that
provides personalized financial advice. Our mission revolves around
three core pillars: Unity, Financial Freedom, and Empowerment.

**Objective:** To provide a seamless and integrated customer experience
across all touchpoints, ensuring that our users receive timely and
relevant support, sales information, marketing content, and operational
updates.

**Requirements:**

1.  **Unified Customer View:**

    -   A 360-degree view of each customer, capturing their
        interactions, financial goals, community engagements, and
        preferences.

    -   Integration with our financial tools to track user activity and
        provide relevant product recommendations.

2.  **AI-Driven Insights with Legacy:**

    -   Legacy, our AI assistant, should have access to customer data to
        offer personalized advice.

    -   Integration with Freshworks to ensure that Legacy can pull in
        relevant data and offer support or sales recommendations.

3.  **Customer Service:**

    -   A ticketing system to handle user queries, complaints, and
        feedback.

    -   Integration with our community forums to track and manage
        user-reported issues.

    -   Chat support, possibly powered by our AI, Legacy.

4.  **Sales and Marketing:**

    -   CRM integration to manage leads and potential customers.

    -   Marketing automation to send out targeted campaigns based on
        user activity and preferences.

    -   Tracking and analytics to measure the effectiveness of our
        campaigns.

5.  **Operational and HR:**

    -   HR tools to manage our growing team, especially as we expand our
        offerings.

    -   Operational tools to manage the backend processes, ensuring
        smooth delivery of our services.

6.  **Seamless Integration:**

    -   All tools should integrate seamlessly, ensuring data flow across
        all departments.

    -   The ability to pull in data from external sources, especially
        our financial tools and community forums.

7.  **Scalability:**

    -   As we aim to grow our user base rapidly, the tools should be
        scalable to handle increased volume without compromising on
        performance.

**Expected Outcome:** With Freshworks\' suite of products, we aim to
enhance our user experience, drive user engagement, and increase our
platform\'s adoption rate. By offering timely and relevant support,
sales information, and marketing content, we hope to build a loyal and
engaged community of Wealth Warriors.
