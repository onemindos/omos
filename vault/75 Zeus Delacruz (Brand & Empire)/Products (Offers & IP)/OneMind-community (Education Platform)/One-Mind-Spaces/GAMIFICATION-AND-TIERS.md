# One Mind — Gamification Levels & Membership Tiers

---

## Gamification Levels (Circle)

| Level | Name | Points Required | What It Means |
|-------|------|----------------|---------------|
| 1 | **Recruit** | 0 | Just entered the system |
| 2 | **Operative** | 100 | Active, executing |
| 3 | **Warrior** | 300 | Proven in the field |
| 4 | **Sovereign** | 750 | Self-mastered |
| 5 | **Executor** | 1,500 | Building at scale |
| 6 | **Architect** | 3,500 | Designing the system |
| 7 | **Legacy** | 7,500 | Leaving a mark |
| 8 | **Emperor** | 15,000 | Running the empire |
| 9 | **One Mind** | 30,000 | The equation achieved |

**Notes:**
- Level 9 (One Mind) is intentionally nearly unreachable by organic engagement alone
- The 8→9 gap (15k→30k) is brutal by design — that badge means something
- Zeus can manually elevate members to any level as a reward for real-world actions or founding status
