Legacy Restore System Flow: From Interest to
Client Conversion
1. Initial Engagement & Interest Generation

Traffic Sources:

    • Paid Ads: Facebook, Instagram, Google
    • Organic Social Media: Posts, Stories, Reels, DMs
    • SEO Traffic: Website visitors from search engines
    • QR Codes: Shared on business cards, flyers, and promotional material
    • Direct Links: Provided via email, blog posts, or messages




2. Entry Points

Where Do Visitors Land?

    • Home Page (Zoho Sites): General information, navigation to all other pages
    • Get Started Page (Zoho Sites): Primary page for conversions, includes VSL
    • Service Page (Zoho Sites): Detailed information about each service
    • Direct to Service Selection Form (Zoho Forms): Used in specific cases
    • Consultation Booking Page (Zoho Bookings): For those not ready to purchase immediately




3. Page Content & Purpose

Home Page:

    • Purpose: Serve as an entry point with brand information
    • Content: Service overview, testimonials, navigation to other pages
    • Call to Action: "Get Started," "Learn More," "Book a Consultation"

Get Started Page:

    • Purpose: Drive visitors to take action based on the VSL
    • Content: VSL, brief service description, call-to-action buttons
    • Actions: Redirect to purchase, service selection form, or consultation booking

Service Page:

    • Purpose: Provide detailed service information and comparison

    • Content: Service benefits, features, and FAQs
    • Actions: Direct purchase option or redirection to consultation

Service Selection Form:

    • Purpose: Capture leads, qualify them, and route them correctly
    • Content: Service selection, credit goals, readiness to buy, contact information
    • Redirect Rules:
    • To the hosted payment page if ready to purchase
    • To consultation booking if unsure

Hosted Payment Pages (Zoho Billing):

    • Purpose: Facilitate purchases seamlessly
    • Content: Service-specific payment information
    • Integration: Auto-update CRM with purchase details

Consultation Booking Page:

    • Purpose: Capture and qualify leads who need more information
    • Content: Booking calendar, consultation benefits
    • Automation: Add to CRM and trigger follow-up sequence




4. Lead & Client Flow in CRM (Zoho CRM)

1. Lead Generation:

    • Form submissions automatically create a new lead in the CRM
    • Tagged with the lead source (Ad, Social Media, QR Code, etc.)

2. Automated Pipeline Management:

    • Initial lead stage: “New Lead”
    • Actions trigger pipeline changes:
    • Form Submission: Moves to “Qualified Lead”
    • Service Selection: Moves to “Ready to Purchase”
    • Purchase Completed: Converts to “Client” status
    • Consultation Booked: Moves to “Consultation Scheduled”

3. Automation Rules:

    • Emails & SMS Campaigns: Based on lead status and actions taken
    • Assignment Rules: Assign leads to team members automatically
    • Zapier Integration: Sync with DisputeFox and manage billing issues

5. After Purchase - Client Management

1. Onboarding Automation:

     • If the service selection form was used, initiate the onboarding process automatically
     • If a direct purchase was made, trigger the onboarding form to capture any missing details

2. DisputeFox Integration:

     • Automatically create a new contact in DisputeFox
     • Sync payment and service details to manage disputes

3. Communication Flows:

     • Welcome email and SMS with next steps
     • Assign an account manager
     • Schedule follow-up tasks in CRM

4. Tracking and Analytics:

     • Use Facebook Pixel and Google Analytics across all pages
     • Track lead sources, form submissions, and purchase completions
     • Monitor where leads drop off and refine the funnel accordingly




6. Key Benefits of This System
     • Simplified Lead Capture: Through the service selection form and direct purchase options
     • Automated CRM Management: Reduces manual data entry and improves efficiency
     • Seamless Purchase Flow: Ensures no friction during checkout
     • High Conversion Potential: Video-first approach and clear CTAs
     • Full Tracking Capabilities: To optimize future ad campaigns and retargeting




7. Final Thoughts
This system is designed to offer flexibility while maintaining a streamlined and professional experience for
both leads and clients. The use of Zoho’s native integrations along with strategic use of external tools
(Zapier, DisputeFox) ensures that data is not lost, and every lead is properly nurtured through the pipeline
until conversion.
