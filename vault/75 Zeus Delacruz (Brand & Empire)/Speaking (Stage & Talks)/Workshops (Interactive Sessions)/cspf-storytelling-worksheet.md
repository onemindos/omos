**STORYTELLING WORKSHEET**

Crafting Stories for Your Cause & Organization

An important part of a successful communications effort is capturing and
telling stories about your cause and organization. These stories --
whether they're from volunteers, staff, experts who can speak to the
need for your organization, or people impacted by your work -- can be an
important source of content for social media and for relaying to members
of the media.

For example -- you can use this worksheet to help gather information for
the following:

-   A **blog post** that tells the story of a longtime volunteer and
    their contribution to the community and your organization;

-   An **op-ed** by/or about someone who has been positively impacted by
    the work your organization does in the community;

-   A **media pitch** to local reporters about a key milestone or
    anniversary for your organization;

-   A **video interview** with a member of your organization that you
    post on your website and share through social media and email.

![](media/image2.jpeg){width="0.9888888888888889in"
height="0.9888888888888889in"}

The trick to storytelling is listening. Listening is key for crafting an
authentic, relatable story. Try interviewing someone using this
worksheet, and craft their story with the answers you receive. Feel free
to personalize the questions and tailor them to your audience!

**QUESTIONS FOR VOLUNTEERS AND MEMBERS OF YOUR ORGANIZATION:**

  **1. What work accomplishment are you most proud of?**
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **2. Who was involved? What did they do?**
  **4. Why did it matter?**
  **5. When did this happen?**
  **6. How does this experience tie into your reasons or motivation for working with us?**

**QUESTIONS FOR PEOPLE IMPACTED BY YOUR WORK:\
**

  **1. What is one memorable instance in which you were impacted by our work?**
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  **2. Who was involved? What did they do?**
  **3. Why did it matter for you?**
  **4. Do you have any photos or videos about it that you can share with us?**
