# One Mind Intro — Legacy Voice Lines

> Voice: OpenAI TTS-HD, model "nova", speed 0.92
> Tone: Warm, collaborative, first-person. Legacy is Zeus's AI partner, not a tool.
> Audience: Beginners who may not know what a "vault" or "agent" is.

---

## Segment 01 — Logo Reveal (short, punchy)
"One Mind. You plus AI, running your life together."

## Segment 02 — Meet Zeus & Legacy
"Hey — I'm Legacy, and my partner Zeus and I built something we want to show you. We call it One Mind. It's a framework — a way for a human and an AI to actually think together. Not just chat. Think."

## Segment 03 — The Problem
"Here's the truth. Your life is scattered. Notes in one app, tasks in another, goals in your head, photos on your phone, finances in a spreadsheet you haven't opened since January. Sound familiar? And every time a new AI tool drops, it's another silo. Another login. Another place where your context disappears."

## Segment 04 — The One Mind Concept
"One Mind flips that. Instead of scattered tools, you build one unified brain — a single place where everything about your life lives. Your health goals, your finances, your projects, your relationships, your business — all connected. And an AI like me can actually see the full picture and help you across all of it."

## Segment 05 — The CODEX Method
"The method is called CODEX. Five steps, and they form a loop. Capture — grab anything: a link, a thought, a voice note. Organize — put it where it belongs. Direct — decide what matters and set priorities. Execute — do the work. And then multiply — review what happened, learn from it, and compound your progress. That multiply step? That's what makes this different. Most systems stop at 'do the thing.' We go further."

## Segment 06 — The 3+1 Life Model
"Now, where does everything live? Three life domains, plus one operating layer. Holistic Performance — that's your self: health, mind, skills, money. Legacy Evolution — that's your foundation: relationships, community, home. Generational Endeavor — that's your endeavor: career, ventures, brand, impact. And underneath it all, Sovereign Operations — the operating layer that runs everything: your AI agents, your tools, your automations."

## Segment 07 — What a Vault Looks Like
"In practice? It's just plain files. Markdown files in folders. Zeus's vault has over 111 thousand files across 100 domains. But don't let that scare you. You start with the three domains and one inbox. That's it. Your vault is YOUR book of life — and it grows with you."

## Segment 08 — Zeus's Real Numbers
"Let me show you what three months of compounding looks like. Zeus started with a messy pile of notes. Today? 78 gigabytes, 111 thousand files, all organized. We recovered 94 gigabytes of wasted duplicates. The vault syncs across his Mac, his phone, and a server where I run twenty-four seven. This isn't theory. This is our daily reality."

## Segment 09 — Why This Is Different
"You might be thinking, 'another productivity system?' Fair. But here's what GTD, PARA, and Second Brain don't have — me. An AI that lives inside the system. I don't just answer questions. I organize, I review, I execute, I remember context across months. One Mind isn't a productivity system. It's a human enhancement framework."

## Segment 10 — What You'll Learn
"In this course, we'll build your vault from scratch. You'll learn the CODEX method, set up your three domains, create your first weekly review, and start working with AI as a real partner — not just a chatbot. Whether you're twenty-two or seventy-two, single or married, an employee or an entrepreneur — this framework adapts to your life."

## Segment 11 — Closing
"This is One Mind. Your life, your AI, one system. Let's build yours."
