## Segment 1: Title (0–3s)
> [Title card plays — no voiceover needed, or optional:]
OpenClaw Dreaming. Your AI learns while you sleep.

## Segment 2: The Problem (3–8s)
Here's the problem with most AI assistants. Every time you start a conversation, your AI starts from zero. It doesn't remember what you told it yesterday. It doesn't know your preferences, your projects, or your patterns. You end up repeating yourself constantly.

## Segment 3: The Solution (8–12s)
OpenClaw solves this with a feature called Dreaming. It's an autonomous memory consolidation system, inspired by how the human brain actually processes memories during sleep.

## Segment 4: Three Phases (12–20s)
Dreaming works in three phases. Light Sleep scans your daily conversation notes and extracts memory candidates. It sorts, stages, and deduplicates them. REM Sleep reflects on those candidates — finding themes, linking related memories, and building associations. And Deep Sleep is where the magic happens. It applies six weighted ranking signals, scores each candidate, and promotes the winners into your AI's long-term memory file.

## Segment 5: Data Flow (20–25s)
Here's the complete data flow. Your chat sessions generate daily notes. Light Sleep processes those notes. REM Sleep connects the dots. Deep Sleep scores and promotes. The result lands in MEMORY.md — your AI's permanent long-term memory.

## Segment 6: Ranking Signals (25–33s)
Deep Sleep uses six signals to decide what's worth remembering. Relevance is the strongest at thirty percent — how important is this to your current goals? Frequency at twenty-four percent — has this topic come up repeatedly? Query diversity at fifteen percent — is this referenced across different conversations? Recency matters too. Then consolidation potential — can this merge with existing memories? And conceptual richness — is this a deep insight or just a surface fact?

## Segment 7: File Architecture (33–38s)
The memory architecture is simple. Three files. MEMORY.md holds your long-term memories, promoted by Deep Sleep. DREAMS.md is the dream diary, narrative entries written after each dreaming cycle. And daily notes capture raw observations from each day's sessions.

## Segment 8: Enable It (38–44s)
Enabling dreaming takes one command: openclaw config set dreaming dot enabled true. Or you can set it in your config file directly. The default schedule runs at three AM every night — but you can customize that to any cron schedule you want.

## Segment 9: Dream Diary (44–50s)
After each dreaming cycle, your AI writes a dream diary entry. Here's what that looks like. It tells you how many observations it consolidated, from how many sessions, and what themes emerged. It's like reading your AI's journal — a window into how it's learning about you.

## Segment 10: Comparison (50–55s)
Without dreaming, your AI forgets between sessions, you repeat yourself constantly, there's no persistent learning, and your chatbot stays generic forever. With dreaming, your AI consolidates memories every night, learns your patterns automatically, builds real long-term understanding, and gets more personal over time.

## Segment 11: Closing (55–60s)
Dreams become memory. Memory becomes intelligence. Your AI gets smarter while you sleep. That's OpenClaw Dreaming.
