## The Complexity vs. Leverage Matrix

```
HIGH LEVERAGE (Scalable) ←→ LOW LEVERAGE (Trading time)
    
    AUTOMATED                      MANUAL
    ↓                              ↓
    
✅ Community membership        ❌ Credit repair (1-on-1)
✅ Courses/digital products    ❌ Funding consulting (custom)
✅ Affiliate commissions       ❌ Speaking (limited slots)
✅ Coaching (group/async)      ❌ Coaching (1-on-1 calls)
✅ Content (YouTube/social)    ❌ Custom consulting
```

**Your instinct is correct**: Move LEFT (automation), reduce RIGHT (manual services).

## Simplified Revenue Model

### What You KEEP (High Leverage)

**1. Community Membership** (Recurring revenue)
- Platform: Circle.so at `wearelegacy.ai`
- Tiers: Free → Foundation ($47) → Builder ($147) → Empire ($497)
- **Automated**: People join, access content, AI agents assist

**2. Digital Courses** (One-time sales)
- Platform: Circle.so courses section OR Gumroad/Teachable
- 50+ courses you already have
- **Automated**: Buy once, access forever

**3. Group Coaching** (Scalable high-ticket)
- Monthly group calls (50-100 people max)
- Hot seat coaching during calls
- Community accountability pods
- **Semi-automated**: Recurring calls, but scales beyond 1-on-1

**4. Affiliate Products** (Passive income)
- Legacy Partner Network promotes:
  - Credit repair services (replace Legacy Restore)
  - Funding/lending partners (replace Legacy Capitalist consulting)
  - Tech tools (software you recommend)
  - Books, courses, other products
- **Fully automated**: Share links, earn commissions

**5. Speaking** (High-ticket, limited slots)
- 10-20 speaking gigs per year
- Command $5k-25k per event
- Only when it makes sense (not scalable, but prestigious)
- **Optional**: Can reduce this as community scales

---

### What You SUNSET (Low Leverage)

**1. Credit Repair Services** (Legacy Restore)
- **Problem**: High-touch, 1-on-1, labor-intensive
- **Solution**: Partner with existing credit repair company, earn 20-30% commission on referrals
- **Result**: Keep LLC for legal separation, but no service delivery

**2. Funding Consulting** (Legacy Capitalist)
- **Problem**: Custom consulting is time-intensive
- **Solution**: Create "Unlimited Funding Formula" course, promote lending partners for commission
- **Result**: Keep LLC for investment income (if you're investing personally), but no consulting

**3. 1-on-1 Coaching** (High-ticket but not scalable)
- **Problem**: Only 20-30 slots per month max
- **Solution**: Group coaching only (50-100 people per call = 5x leverage)
- **Result**: Empire tier gets 1 private call/month, but most coaching is group
