**Title**

Athony De La Cruz, Motivator & Public Speaker

**Content**

Anthony De La Cruz is the founder of Fearless Motivation LLC, a company
that is all about guiding people toward change and helping people to
reach their full potential. Passionate about empowering others in their
lives, Anthony has built his reputation on his ability to help people
overcome even the most daunting challenges and to realize their true
innate power. He has a gift for inciting deep, lasting shifts in
people's world perspectives, leading to long-term success. A strong
believer that *each person* can achieve their goals, he is someone who
takes an active interest in learning about the people around him in
order to help them more effectively.

Take a look at where you are in your life right now. How does everything
seem to you? This is a useful exercise, this stock-taking and
self-gauging, and if you make a habit of it, you will come to understand
yourself better and see what you need to do to achieve what you want to
achieve. Building this habit takes time, of course, and Anthony can help
you to cut that time down, slicing your learning curve in half and
showing you the fullness of your potential.

Two years ago, Anthony stood up on a stage and gave a speech. That was
the beginning of his entry into public speaking, and ever since, he has
continued to grow each day as a public speaker. His drive to motivate
and inspire others runs deep within him. Anytime he sees someone
struggling, he feels an irresistible urge to reach out and help them.
His nature is one of compassion, and as a public speaker, he can help
more people more quickly than he could in any other profession.

Anthony previously worked in the medical field, serving as an emergency
medical technician. Although he enjoyed that work, he knew that his
calling was public speaking. Once he had seen a captivated audience,
seen the look of hopes in their eyes and knew he had inspired that hope,
there was just no turning back.

Today, Anthony works tirelessly to motivate young people to focus on
self-development and practice the art of peace and kindness. Born and
raised in New York, he is a proud father to a baby son. When he is not
spending time with his family or running his business, he enjoys working
out and practicing martial arts.
