## How to Use This Log

**Purpose:**
- Track accomplishments in real-time (don't rely on memory!)
- Build resume bullet points with quantified results
- Prepare for performance reviews
- Have interview stories ready (STAR method)

**Best Practice:**
- Update weekly (Friday afternoon ideal)
- Always include metrics/numbers
- Use action verbs
- Note the business impact

## Action Verbs for Resume Bullets

Use these powerful verbs to start your bullets:

**Leadership:** Led, Directed, Managed, Orchestrated, Spearheaded, Championed
**Achievement:** Achieved, Exceeded, Surpassed, Outperformed, Delivered, Accomplished
**Improvement:** Improved, Enhanced, Optimized, Streamlined, Transformed, Revitalized
**Creation:** Created, Developed, Designed, Built, Established, Launched
**Analysis:** Analyzed, Evaluated, Assessed, Researched, Investigated, Identified
**Collaboration:** Collaborated, Partnered, Coordinated, Facilitated, Liaised
**Communication:** Presented, Communicated, Articulated, Conveyed, Reported
**Problem-Solving:** Resolved, Troubleshot, Diagnosed, Fixed, Solved, Rectified
**Growth:** Grew, Expanded, Scaled, Increased, Boosted, Elevated
