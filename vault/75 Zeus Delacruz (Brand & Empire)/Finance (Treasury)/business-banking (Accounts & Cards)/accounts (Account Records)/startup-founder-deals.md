## How to Apply

Most programs require one or more of:
- Proof of LLC/Corp incorporation
- Evidence of funding (accelerator letter, VC term sheet, or angel investment)
- Company website or pitch deck
- Less than 3–5 years old / under $5M ARR

**Best entry points without VC backing:**
1. **AWS Activate** or **GCP for Startups** — can apply directly with proof of entity
2. **Stripe Atlas startups** — if entity was formed through Stripe Atlas
3. **Y Combinator Startup School** (free) — unlocks many partner deals without funding
4. **Brex or Ramp** — once approved, both platforms unlock additional startup deals dashboard
