# Paydex Building Roadmap

> Paydex is Dun & Bradstreet's (D&B) business credit score (0–100). A score of 80 means you pay on time. 80+ unlocks cards like Sam's Club Mastercard and signals creditworthiness to many EIN-only lenders.

## Prerequisites

Before you start:
- [ ] Business entity registered (LLC or Corp)
- [ ] EIN obtained from IRS
- [ ] Business address (not a PO box — use registered agent if needed)
- [ ] Business phone number (separate from personal)
- [ ] D&B D-U-N-S Number registered (free at dnb.com — takes ~30 days to activate)

## Step 1 — Get Your D-U-N-S Number

1. Go to https://www.dnb.com/duns-number/get-a-duns-number.html
2. Register your business for free
3. Takes 30 business days (expedited ~5 days for $229)
4. Once issued, your Paydex clock starts when vendors report

## Step 2 — Open Net-30 Accounts (Report to D&B)

You need at least **3 net-30 vendor accounts** reporting to D&B before a Paydex score is generated. Pay each one early or on time — every payment is a data point.

| Vendor | Category | Cost to Open | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------------|-------|
| Uline | Office/Shipping Supplies | $0 | Top D&B reporter — apply at uline.com |
| Quill | Office Supplies | $0 | Staples subsidiary — reports to D&B |
| Grainger | Industrial/Safety Supplies | $0 | Reports to D&B — buy anything small |
| Crown Office Supplies | Office | $0 | Reports to D&B, easy approval |
| Shirtsy | Branded Merch | $0 | Net-30, reports to D&B |
| Creative Analytics | Business Services | $0 | Reports to D&B |
| Summa Office Supplies | Office | $0 | Reports to D&B |

**Strategy**: Open 5–7 accounts. Buy something small ($25–$50) on each. Pay early (net-10 or net-15 instead of net-30). Early payment pushes Paydex toward 100.

## Step 3 — Monitor & Build Score

| Paydex Range | Meaning | Typical Timeline |
|--------------|---------|-----------------|
| No score | < 3 accounts reporting | 0–2 months |
| 1–49 | High risk — slow payer | — |
| 50–79 | Moderate risk — some delays | 2–4 months |
| 80 | On time payer | 4–6 months |
| 90–100 | Early payer — best tier | 6–12 months |

Check your score at: https://www.dnb.com/credit-reporter.html ($0 basic / paid for full report)

## Step 4 — Unlock Sam's Club Mastercard

Once Paydex hits 80:
1. Visit a physical Sam's Club location
2. Purchase a business membership (~$45/year)
3. Let membership age **32 days**
4. Apply for the Sam's Club Mastercard Business
5. Expected limit: **$5,000–$10,000** — usable anywhere Mastercard is accepted

## Step 5 — Expand Credit Portfolio

With Paydex 80+ and a few accounts open, you can begin applying for higher-tier EIN-only cards:

| Priority | Provider | Why |
|----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | Brex | Highest limits for startups, no PG |
| 2 | Ramp | Best spend automation, no PG |
| 3 | RHO | Banking + card combo |
| 4 | Hatch | Good for early-stage businesses |
| 5 | Jeeves | Best for international spend |

## Paydex Score Calculation

- D&B calculates Paydex purely on **payment history** — not revenue, not balances
- Each payment is weighted by dollar amount (larger payments = more weight)
- **Pay early** — net-10 on a net-30 account can push score to 90+
- **Never pay late** — one late payment drops score significantly

## Notes

- Paydex only reflects D&B data. Also build Experian Business (Nav.com) and Equifax Business (creditera.com) profiles separately
- Do not mix personal credit pulls — use EIN only for all applications
- D-U-N-S activation and vendor reporting combined = minimum 60–90 days before first Paydex score appears
