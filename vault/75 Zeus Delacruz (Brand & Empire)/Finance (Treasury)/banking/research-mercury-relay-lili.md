# Business Banking Research: Mercury, Relay, Lili

## MERCURY BANK (mercury.com)
**Type:** Business banking platform for startups, small businesses & scaling companies  
**Founded:** 2017 by Immad Akhund, Max Tagher, Jason Zhang  
**Description:** "Everything you do with money. All in one place."

### Key Features
- **Business Checking & Savings**: Zero minimums, zero monthly fees
- **Treasury**: Up to 3.66% yield via Mercury Advisory (SEC-registered investment advisor)
- **Credit Card**: 1.5% cashback on business spend
- **Loans**: Venture debt & working capital loans for growth-stage companies
- **Bill Pay**: No fees on USD payments, international wire support
- **Invoicing**: Create free invoices, get paid directly to Mercury account
- **Expense Management**: Team cards, spending limits, approval workflows
- **Accounting**: Native sync with QuickBooks, Xero, NetSuite + AI-powered automations
- **API Access**: Developer-friendly REST API for fintech builders
- **Personal Banking**: For founders alongside business accounts

### Target Market
- VC-backed startups
- Tech companies & SaaS businesses
- Scaling companies ($1M-$100M+ ARR)
- Businesses needing sophisticated banking infrastructure

### Customer Base
- **300,000+** "most ambitious entrepreneurs"
- Used by: Supabase, Mercury-backed companies, YC startups

### Mobile App
- **iOS:** 4.9/5 rating (10,730 reviews)
- Features: Full banking, treasury management, expense approvals

### Banking Partner
- Choice Financial Group & Evolve Bank & Trust (FDIC insured)

### Strengths
✅ Startup-friendly onboarding (10-minute application)  
✅ Strong API/developer tools  
✅ High treasury yields (competitive with Brex, Ramp)  
✅ Modern UX (best-in-class interface)  
✅ Comprehensive financial stack (everything in one platform)  
✅ No personal guarantee required for some products  

### Weaknesses
❌ Best suited for incorporated businesses (not freelancers)  
❌ Limited physical presence (online-only)  
❌ Some features require minimum balances or revenue thresholds  

### Ideal For
🎯 VC-backed startups  
🎯 Tech companies with engineering teams  
🎯 Businesses needing API integrations  
🎯 Companies raising capital or managing investor funds

## RELAY BANK (relayfi.com / Relay Financial)
**Type:** All-in-one business banking & money management platform  
**Philosophy:** "On The Money, All The Time"  
**Description:** Helping businesses understand what they're earning, spending & saving

### Key Features
- **Multi-Account System**: Create 20+ checking accounts + 20+ savings accounts per business
- **Profit First Integration**: Built-in support for Profit First methodology
- **Payables Dashboard**: Centralized bill pay, vendor management
- **Receivables Dashboard**: Invoice tracking, payment collection
- **Team Expense Management**: Cards, reimbursements, spending limits, approvals
- **No Monthly Fees**: Free business checking & savings
- **50 Physical Debit Cards**: Per account at no extra cost
- **2 Virtual Cards**: Per checking account
- **Accounting Sync**: QuickBooks, Xero integration

### Philosophy: Profit First Banking
Relay is **purpose-built** around the Profit First methodology:
- **Allocate money by purpose**: Operating, Profit, Owner Pay, Taxes, etc.
- **Visual folder system**: See cash flow across multiple accounts at a glance
- **Automatic transfers**: Schedule allocations based on Profit First percentages

### Target Market
- Small to medium businesses ($100K-$10M revenue)
- Service businesses, agencies, consultants
- Businesses using Profit First accounting
- Teams needing expense controls

### Customer Base
- Unknown publicly stated number
- Featured in: Forbes, TechCrunch, Fortune, Axios, Yahoo Finance

### Banking Partner
- Thread Bank (FDIC insured)

### Strengths
✅ **Best for cash flow management** (Profit First methodology)  
✅ Visual financial organization (folder metaphor)  
✅ Unlimited checking/savings accounts  
✅ Team expense management  
✅ No fees on domestic wire transfers  
✅ Real-time financial visibility  

### Weaknesses
❌ No credit products (no credit cards or loans)  
❌ No treasury/yield products (standard savings rates)  
❌ Limited API access (not developer-focused)  
❌ Less sophisticated than Mercury for startups  

### Ideal For
🎯 Service businesses & agencies  
🎯 Consultants & coaches  
🎯 Small biz owners using Profit First method  
🎯 Teams needing organized expense management

## LILI BANK (lili.co)

**Description:** "Fast setup. High-yield savings. Zero hidden fees."

### Key Features

- **Tax Tools**: Expense categorization, tax withholding, estimated tax calculator
