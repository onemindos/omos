## 🎯 Content Modes

Three modes, each serving a different purpose:

### 📖 Mode 1 — "The Framework" (Theory)
Teach the One Mind OS concepts. GTD vs PARA vs One Mind. The 4 domains. The HIVE method. The A→Q→W→D cadence. Evergreen, searchable, YouTube + newsletter.

> **Example:** *"Why GTD is failing you in the AI era"* → Lands in the One Mind OS framework

### 🔴 Mode 2 — "Live Build" (Reality TV)
Document what you're actually building RIGHT NOW. The codebase, the community, the credit journey, the business structure. Unfair advantage — you're doing it, not just teaching it.

> **Example:** *"I gave my AI agent access to my health data — here's what happened"*

### 💡 Mode 3 — "Teach What You Know" (Practical)
Step-by-step tutorials from skills already mastered. Business credit, Zoho One setup, Obsidian vaults, AI agent prompting, Circle.SO. Drives the most conversions.

> **Example:** *"How I set up Zoho One for my entire business in a weekend"*
