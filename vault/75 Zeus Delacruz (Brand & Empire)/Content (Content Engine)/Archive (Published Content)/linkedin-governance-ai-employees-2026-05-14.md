Solid list. The one thing I'd add: **governance.**

A 24/7 agent with tools, memory, cron, subagents, and external integrations is not just "automation" anymore. It needs approval boundaries, audit trails, routing rules, and clear limits on what it can do without a human.

That's what we do differently at **Odg AI.**

We don't just spin up OpenClaw and throw tools at it. We build governed AI employees:

- Main agent as orchestrator
- Specialized subagents by function
- Persistent memory and workspace rules
- Reviewed drafts before external sends
- Approval gates for risky actions
- Clear routing between sales, ops, email, calendar, and support workflows

The goal is not "more autonomy" for its own sake. The goal is **reliable delegation** without waking up to an agent that looped, spammed, broke context, or touched something it shouldn't have.
