# How to Track & Nurture Leads Who Drop Off?

🔹 **3. How to Track & Nurture Leads Who Drop Off?**

Since **not everyone will complete checkout immediately**, we need an automated system to:

✅ **Track abandoned sign-ups**

✅ **Send follow-up emails & texts to bring them back**

✅ **Auto-assign leads to sales reps for follow-up calls**

📌 **Step 1: Capture Leads Inside Zoho CRM**

Since you’re using **LeadChain**, all Facebook/Google/TikTok leads will **automatically enter Zoho CRM**.

- **Tag each lead with their source (Facebook, Google, etc.)**
- **Apply a lead score** to prioritize high-quality leads
- **Move them into the correct sales pipeline inside CRM**

📌 **Step 2: Use Zoho Campaigns & Zoho Marketing Automation for Follow-Ups**

💡 **Should You Use Zoho Campaigns or Zoho Marketing Automation?**

✅ **Zoho Campaigns** → Best for **email marketing & newsletters**

✅ **Zoho Marketing Automation** → Best for **behavior-based lead nurturing**

🚀 **Use Both Together!**

- **Zoho Campaigns** → General email broadcasts, welcome emails, newsletters.
- **Zoho Marketing Automation** → Sends follow-ups **only to leads who drop off or don’t complete checkout**.

💡 **Example Flow in Zoho Marketing Automation**

1️⃣ Lead fills out the form but **does not complete payment**

2️⃣ **Wait 1 hour → Send Reminder Email** (“Hey, you forgot to finish signing up!”)

3️⃣ **Wait 24 hours → Send SMS Reminder** (if they provided a phone number)

4️⃣ **Wait 3 Days → Assign to a Sales Rep for Call Follow-Up**

5️⃣ If they **still don’t sign up**, **move them to a long-term nurture sequence**

✅ **This keeps leads warm and increases conversion rates over time.**

🔹 **4. Setting Up the Sales Pipeline in Zoho CRM & Bigin**

Since you want **both Zoho CRM & Bigin**, we need to define:

✅ **Where leads enter the system**

✅ **How they move through the pipeline**

✅ When they get assigned to sales reps

📍 **Recommended Pipeline Flow in Zoho CRM**

1️⃣ **New Lead (Captured via Zoho Form or LeadChain from Ads)**

2️⃣ **Engaged Lead (Clicked email, watched video, but didn’t buy)**

3️⃣ **Purchase Completed (Paid via Zoho Billing or Checkout)**

4️⃣ **Onboarding Process (Auto-enrolled in CRM & Desk for dispute management)**

5️⃣ **Active Client (Inside client portal & receiving service updates)**

🚀 **Automations:**

- ✅ If lead **doesn’t buy within 3 days**, auto-assign to a **sales rep for follow-up**.
- ✅ If lead **completes payment**, auto-move to the **client pipeline** for onboarding.

📌 **How Zoho CRM & Bigin Work Together:**

- **Zoho CRM is the master system** for all sales & lead tracking.
- **Bigin is used by sales reps** for quick deal tracking.
- Zoho Flow ensures updates sync automatically between both systems.

📌 **Final Workflow Overview**

🚦 **Lead Capture & Routing**

🚦 **Traffic Sources (Ads, Website, Referrals, Sales Reps, etc.)**

⬇️

📄 **Zoho Landing Page with Video & Zoho Form**

⬇️

📄 **Lead fills out form → Gets auto-tagged in CRM**

⬇️

🔄 **Redirects to correct Zoho Billing Checkout Page**

⬇️

📌 **If completed → Moves to Onboarding Workflow**

📌 If abandoned → Enter Marketing Automation Follow-Up Sequence

📌 **Sales Pipeline Tracking**

📌 **Lead Enters CRM → Gets Scored & Tagged**

📌 **Sales Reps Auto-Assigned Based on Interest Level**

📌 **Pipeline Stages Automatically Update Based on Actions Taken**

✅ **Zoho CRM manages all lead data & sales activity**

✅ Bigin helps sales reps track active deals more easily

📌 **Client Onboarding & Service Execution**

📌 **Client completes checkout → Auto-tagged as “New Client”**

📌 **Assigned to Zoho Desk (For Dispute Processing & Support)**

📌 **Enrolled in Zoho Campaigns (For Updates & Service Info)**

📌 Gets access to Zoho Billing Client Portal (To Manage Subscription & Payments)
