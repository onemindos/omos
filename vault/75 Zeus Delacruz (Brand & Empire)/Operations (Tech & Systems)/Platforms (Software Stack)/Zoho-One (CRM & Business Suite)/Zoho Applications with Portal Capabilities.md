# Zoho Applications with Portal Capabilities

Parent item: Zoho Custom Portal (Zoho%20Custom%20Portal%20261809e2458780a89b8cc49c17abd8ed.md)
Platform: Zoho Creator

### **1. Zoho CRM:**

### 

**Customer Portal:**

- **Purpose:** Allow customers to manage their support tickets, access the knowledge base, update their contact information, and view invoices.
1. **Features:Support Ticket Management:** Customers can raise, view, and manage their support tickets.
2. **Knowledge Base Access:** Customers can search and access articles in the knowledge base.
3. **Profile Updates:** Customers can update their contact information.
4. **Invoice Viewing:** Customers can view their past invoices and transaction history.

### **2. Zoho Desk:**

### 

**Customer Support Portal:**

- **Purpose:** Enable customers to raise, view, and manage their support tickets and access self-help articles.
1. **Features:Ticket Submission:** Customers can submit new support tickets.
2. **Ticket Tracking:** Customers can track the status and updates of their tickets.
3. **Knowledge Base:** Customers can access self-help articles to resolve issues on their own.
4. **Community Forums:** Customers can participate in community discussions and seek help from other users.

## **Setting Up Client Portals:**

## 

### **General Steps for Enabling Portals:**

### 

- **Enable Portal:**Navigate to the relevant settings within each Zoho application (e.g., Settings > Client Portal in Zoho Books).
- Enable the portal functionality and configure the options to match your business requirements.
- **Invite Clients:**Send invitations to clients from within the application, enabling them to access their respective portals.
- **Customize Portal Appearance:**Adjust the look and feel of the portal to align with your branding and provide a consistent user experience.

## **Benefits of Using Zoho Portals:**

## 

1. **Improved Communication:** Centralized platforms for communication reduce the need for back-and-forth emails.
2. **Enhanced Customer Experience:** Clients have access to relevant information in one place, improving transparency and satisfaction.
3. **Self-Service Convenience:** Clients can manage their own data, reducing administrative workload for your team.
4. **Automated Processes:** Save time and minimize errors with automated notifications and workflows.
5. **Security:** Secure access with role-based permissions and robust data protection measures.
6. **Integration:** Seamless integration with other Zoho applications ensures a unified experience across your business operations.

By leveraging these portal functionalities across various Zoho applications, businesses can enhance operational efficiency, improve customer engagement, and provide a better overall experience for their clients and team members.
