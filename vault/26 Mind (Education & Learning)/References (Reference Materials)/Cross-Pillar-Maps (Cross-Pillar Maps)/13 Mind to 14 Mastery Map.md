# 13 Mind → 14 Mastery Map

**How learning in 13 Mind translates to skill practice in 14 Mastery.**

## Overview

13 Mind is where you **learn theory and acquire knowledge**. 14 Mastery is where you **apply that knowledge and develop skills through deliberate practice**. This map shows the flow from education to execution.

## The Mastery Loop

```
13 Mind (Learn) → 14 Mastery (Practice) → 13 Mind (Learn More) → 14 Mastery (Practice Better)
        ↑                                                                           ↓
        └───────────────────────────── Feedback & Refinement ─────────────────────┘
```

### Key Principles

1. **Learning without practice is incomplete** - Knowledge must be applied
2. **Practice without learning plateaus** - Need new knowledge to break through
3. **Deliberate practice requires theory** - Must know what "good" looks like
4. **Mastery creates teaching moments** - Teaching deepens your learning

## Action Items: From Learning to Doing

### When you learn something in 13 Mind, immediately ask:

1. **Where can I practice this?**
   - Create specific practice project in 14 Mastery

2. **How will I measure progress?**
   - Set measurable milestones

3. **What's my practice schedule?**
   - Daily? Weekly? How many hours?

4. **Who can give me feedback?**
   - Mentors, peers, coaches

5. **When will I review and adjust?**
   - Weekly reviews of practice quality

## Common Pitfalls

### ⚠️ Learning Trap: Consuming without creating
- **Fix:** For every 1 hour of learning, spend 3 hours practicing

### ⚠️ Practice Trap: Doing without learning
- **Fix:** Review theory weekly to refine technique

### ⚠️ Perfectionism Trap: Waiting to be "ready"
- **Fix:** Start practicing with 70% knowledge, learn the rest while doing

### ⚠️ Scattered Focus: Practicing many things shallowly
- **Fix:** Deep practice in 1-3 domains at a time
