## Description

James Clear's system for building good habits and breaking bad ones through tiny changes, identity shifts, and environment design. Argues that systems matter more than goals — improve by 1% each day and results compound.

## Key Topics

- The 4 Laws of Behavior Change (Cue, Craving, Response, Reward)
- Identity-based habits vs. outcome-based habits
- Habit stacking and environment design
- The plateau of latent potential
- Systems thinking over goal setting
