## Description

Based on 30 years of clinical experience treating thousands of patients, Dr. Robert Morse reveals his ultimate healing system — the Detox Miracle. The book shows how to cleanse your body of poisons and start the process of complete cellular regeneration. Covers the A-Z of detoxification, explains specific raw foods and herbs for cleansing every organ and system, details the body-mind-soul interface, and presents the Detox Miracle Diet. Includes dozens of reference lists for herbs, herbal formulas, minerals, phytochemicals, cell salts, and raw food recipes.
