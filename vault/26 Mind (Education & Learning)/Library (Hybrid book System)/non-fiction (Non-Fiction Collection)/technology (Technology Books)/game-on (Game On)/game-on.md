# Game On!

## Video Game History from Pong and Pac-Man to Mario, Minecraft, and More

By Dustin Hansen

---

## Overview

An illustrated journey through video game history covering the creators and inspiration behind the world's most beloved games. Features historical trivia, easter eggs, and the innovations that made each game special.

## Key Topics

- Pong (1972) — the beginning of video games
- Pac-Man (1980) — first example of AI in a video game
- Mario (1981) — named after Nintendo's landlord
- Space Invaders (1978) and beyond
- Minecraft (2011) and modern gaming
- Game creators, technology innovations, and cultural impact
