## Description

A comprehensive introductory psychology textbook that covers the core concepts of the field while emphasizing how psychological principles apply to everyday life. Written for undergraduate students with no prior psychology background.

- Biological bases of behavior & neuroscience
- Sensation, perception & states of consciousness
- Learning, memory & cognition
- Motivation, emotion & human development
- Personality theories & assessment
- Psychological disorders & therapy
- Social psychology & applied psychology
