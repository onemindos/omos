## Description

"A sure-fire all-American success story." — The New York Times Book Review. Sam Walton, the undisputed merchant king of the late twentieth century, tells his extraordinary story in his own words. He parlayed a single dime store in a hardscrabble cotton town into Wal-Mart, the largest retailer in the world. Genuinely modest but always sure of his ambitions, Sam shares his thinking in a candid, straight-from-the-shoulder style. "An extraordinary success story about a man whose empire was built not with smoke and mirrors, but with good old-fashioned elbow grease." — Detroit Free Press.
