## Description

"Walker is the real deal, and he shares his insights for running a successful company from day one." — Shep Hyken, NYT Bestselling Author. Almost all startups either fail or never truly reach a sustainable size. What if you could skip the startup phase and generate profitable revenue on day one? Walker Deibel shows how to buy an existing company, use ownership as a path to financial independence, find great brokers, generate deal flow, navigate the acquisition process, and become a successful acquisition entrepreneur. Deibel co-founded three startups, acquired seven companies, and consulted on over 100 business transactions. MBA from Olin School of Business at Washington University in St. Louis.

## Key Topics

- Acquisition entrepreneurship vs. starting from scratch
- Finding and evaluating businesses to buy
- Navigating the acquisition process
- Deal flow and broker relationships
