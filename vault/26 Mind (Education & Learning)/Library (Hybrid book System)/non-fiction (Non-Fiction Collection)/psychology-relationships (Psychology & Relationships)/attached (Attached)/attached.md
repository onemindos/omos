## Description

The new science of adult attachment and how it can help you find — and keep — love. Based on research pioneered by John Bowlby in the 1950s. Explains three attachment styles: Anxious, Avoidant, and Secure. Endorsed by John Gray (Men Are from Mars), Elle magazine, John B. Herman M.D. (Harvard Medical School), and Phillip R. Shaver Ph.D. (UC Davis). Website: www.attachedthebook.com
