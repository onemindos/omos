## Description

A Black Swan is an event, positive or negative, that is deemed improbable yet causes massive consequences. Nassim Taleb shows in a playful way that Black Swan events explain almost everything about our world, and yet we — especially the experts — are blind to them. This second edition adds a new philosophical and empirical essay, "On Robustness and Fragility," which offers tools to navigate and exploit a Black Swan world. New York Times Bestseller. "[A book] that altered modern thinking." — The Times (London). "The Black Swan changed my view of how the world works." — Daniel Kahneman, Nobel laureate. "A masterpiece." — Chris Anderson. "Idiosyncratically brilliant." — Niall Ferguson, Los Angeles Times.

## Key Topics

- Black Swan events and their outsized impact
- Probability, risk, and uncertainty
- Why experts fail to predict major events
- Robustness and fragility in complex systems
