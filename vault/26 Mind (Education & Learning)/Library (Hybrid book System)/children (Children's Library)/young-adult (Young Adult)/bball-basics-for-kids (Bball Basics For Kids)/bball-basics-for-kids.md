## Description

A step-by-step basketball handbook featuring "Hoops," a playful basketball-loving friend who shows kids of all ages the most important skills of the game. Coach Bobby Kaplan applies his experience of coaching over a thousand games to teach fundamentals: dribbling and passing, shooting and rebounding, playing defense, and improving footwork. Includes a history of the game, inspirational stories, warm-up exercises, a glossary of basketball terms, and a 60-minute self-improvement practice plan.

Customizable — any basketball program can add their own personal photos.

## Key Topics

- Basketball fundamentals for youth
- Dribbling, passing, shooting, defense
- Practice plans and warm-up routines
- Values: cooperation, respect, trust, sacrifice, perseverance
