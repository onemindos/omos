**\*\*Do Not Consent / Custody Affidavit:\*\* (Custody not Child
Support)**

-   I used for to respond to summons / custody hearings only.

-   I responded by registered mail and send to child support office.

**\*\*Special Appearance Demand for Dismissal:\*\* (Child Support
Court)**

-   I used for child support hearings only.

-   I filed directly with the clerk at the court where my case was.

**\*\*License Restoration Affidavit:\*\* (Child Support Services &
DMV)**

-   I sent registered mail copy directly to DMV headquarters in my
    state.

-   I sent a registered mail copy to the child support office who had my
    case.

-   I also enclosed a copy of the **Dear Colleague Letter** to both.

**\*\*Passport Restoration Affidavit:\*\* (Child Support Services &
Passport Agency)**

-   I sent registered mail copy directly to Passport headquarters in New
    Hampshire.

-   I sent a registered mail copy to the child support office who had my
    case.

-   I also enclosed a copy of the **Dear Colleague Letter** to both.

**\*\*No Contempt / Incarceration Affidavit:\*\* (Child Support Court)**

-   I filed directly with the clerk at the courthouse for my Child
    Support hearing.

**\*\*Notice of Default Affidavit:\*\***

-   A Notice of Default can be used for any party that failed to
    respond.

-   I customized the default with the name, address, details of the
    situation\
    to the defaulting agency or entity (DMV, passport agency, child
    support agency, etc.).

**\*\*Color of Law:\*\* (Child Support Court)**

-   I filed directly with the clerk in my child support hearing when the
    fake judge was not complying or being impartial.

**\*\*Un-consent, Set Aside, and Dismissal:\*\* (Child Support Court)**

-   I used for child support hearings only.

-   I filed directly with the clerk at the court where my case was.

**\*Nature and Cause:\*\* (Child Support Court)**

-   I used for child support hearings only.

-   I filed directly with the clerk at the court where my case was.

ANYTHING I FILED WITH THE CLERK, I HAD (1) ORIGINAL AND (3) COPIES. THE
ORGINAL FOR THE COURT, ONE COPY FOR ME, AND I SERVED CHILD SUPPORT AND
THE OTHER PARTIES WIT COPIES.
