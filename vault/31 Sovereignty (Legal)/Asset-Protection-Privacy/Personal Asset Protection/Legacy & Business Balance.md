# Legacy & Business Balance

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Sovereignty, Family

## Overview

Managing the complex arrangement of family, business, and international living.

---

## Legal Considerations

### Child Support & Visitation
- [ ] Ensure legal agreements in place for all children
- [ ] Consult family lawyer for obligations and rights

### Tax Implications
- [ ] Consult tax advisor re: US and Mexico obligations
- [ ] Understand dual-jurisdiction requirements

### Immigration
- [ ] Understand Mexico visa requirements
- [ ] Maintain proper documentation

---

## Work Arrangements

### Flexible Schedule
- Maintain schedule allowing time with kids in both locations
- Virtual work enables location flexibility

### Time Zone Management
- Plan work schedule adaptable to different time zones
- Use async communication when possible

---

## Family Time

### Scheduled Visits
- Plan visits to NY and FL well in advance
- Coordinate with children's mothers

### Quality Time
- Plan bonding activities during visits
- Make visits meaningful, not just present

### Virtual Presence
- Use video calls when not physically present
- Maintain consistent communication

---

## Travel & Logistics

### Cost Management
- [ ] Join frequent flyer programs
- [ ] Use travel rewards

### Accommodations
- Consider maintaining residences in NY and Mexico
- Temporary arrangement for FL visits

### Emergency Plans
- [ ] Have plan for short-notice travel to children

---

## Financial Planning

### Budget
- Account for travel, accommodation, multiple households

### Trust Funds
- [ ] Set up trust funds for children's future

### Professional Help
- [ ] Hire financial advisor for asset management

---

## Communication

### With Co-Parents
- Keep open line for scheduling and needs
- Be transparent about travel schedule

### With Children
- Be transparent about lifestyle as they grow
- Make sure they know they're a priority
