**Subject: Final Notice of Default and Demand for Immediate Restoration
of My Right to Travel**

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to issue this **Final Notice of Default** regarding your
failure to respond to my previous \[Affidavit/Request\], which was duly
submitted on \[date\] and within the timeframes prescribed by **United
States law, U.S. Codes, and Supreme Court case law**. Specifically, I
have received no response to my requests for resolution from the
following agencies:

1.  **Child Support Services** regarding my \[child support modification
    or other related request\],

2.  **Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV)** regarding the suspension of
    my driving privileges,

3.  **U.S. Passport Agency** regarding the issuance or renewal of my
    passport.

**Violation of Legal and Constitutional Rights**

Your agencies' continued failure to respond or take corrective action
within the time allotted by law represents a violation of my **due
process rights** under the **Fifth** and **Fourteenth Amendments** to
the United States Constitution, as well as a violation of
my **fundamental right to travel**. As affirmed by the Supreme Court
in **Shapiro v. Thompson**, 394 U.S. 618 (1969), and **Kent v. Dulles**,
357 U.S. 116 (1958), restrictions on my ability to travel are subject to
strict scrutiny and cannot be imposed arbitrarily or unlawfully.

Additionally, I note that under **28 U.S. Code § 1608(d)**, your
agencies are required to respond to requests, complaints, and filings
within a reasonable time, and failure to do so can result in a default
judgment. Your ongoing failure to act constitutes a default and tacit
acknowledgment of your inability to provide a lawful basis for
restricting my rights.

**Demand for Immediate Remedy and Restoration of Rights**

I demand the following actions be taken immediately:

1.  **Restoration of my Right to Travel**, including but not limited to:

    -   **Restoration of my driver's license**, if applicable;

    -   **Issuance or renewal of my U.S. passport**;

2.  A **response** to my prior communications within **five (5) business
    days** from the date of this notice, along with clear and lawful
    justification for any action that has been taken against my rights.

**Failure to Act or Respond:**

Failure to provide a remedy or response within the prescribed timeframe
will be taken as an **admission of guilt** regarding the unlawful
restriction of my rights. If you do not correct these violations within
five (5) business days from the date of this notice, I will take this
inaction as an **acknowledgment of default** on your part, and I will
consider my **right to travel restored** effective immediately, as
guaranteed by the Constitution and federal law.

**Legal Precedents and Authority**

-   **Shapiro v. Thompson**, 394 U.S. 618 (1969): The Supreme Court held
    that the right to travel is a fundamental right that cannot be
    unduly burdened or restricted without due process of law.

-   **Kent v. Dulles**, 357 U.S. 116 (1958): The Supreme Court
    reaffirmed that the right to travel abroad is a protected
    constitutional right, which cannot be arbitrarily interfered with by
    the government.

-   **28 U.S. Code § 1608(d)**: Provides that service of process must
    occur within a reasonable time, and failure to respond constitutes
    default, empowering the court to issue orders for relief.

If you continue to fail to provide the required remedy and justification
for these restrictions on my rights, I will pursue all available legal
remedies, including but not limited to filing a motion for a **writ of
mandamus**, seeking **default judgment**, and **seeking damages** for
the violation of my constitutional rights.

**Final Demand:**

This is your **final notice**. You are hereby informed that the
non-response to this notice will result in the **immediate restoration
of my right to travel** without any further administrative intervention.
Should I not hear from you within the next **five (5) business days**, I
will consider this matter settled in my favor, with all restrictions on
my travel rights lifted and voided.

Thank you for your immediate attention to this urgent matter. I expect
your prompt compliance with the law.

**You are hereby formally notified that failure to respond to this
Demand within (5) business days from the date of this notice will be
deemed as an admission of fault and a waiver of any objection to the
demand for restoration.**
