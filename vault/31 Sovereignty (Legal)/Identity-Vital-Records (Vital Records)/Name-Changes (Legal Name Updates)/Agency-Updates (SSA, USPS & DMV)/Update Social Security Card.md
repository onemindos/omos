**Update License and Registration**

Whatever you do, don't skip the previous step! Before you can update
your license, you\'ll need to have already updated your Social Security
information and allowed at least 48 hours to pass so that the
information is updated in the DMV system. Changing your name on your
driver license does involve an [in-person trip to the
DMV](https://dmv.ny.gov/offices).

**Download and print the Application for Driver License or ID card
located on the right side of this page. You will need to complete this
form in black ink.**

**To get your driver license updated, you will need to bring the
following documents with your completed application:**

• A certified copy of your court order showing your legal name change\
• Your current, state-issued photo ID \
• Proof of identity (certified birth certificate, valid US passport,
Report of Birth Abroad, Certificate of Naturalization or Certificate of
Citizenship)\
• Proof of Social Security number (your updated Social Security card)\
• Payment for the license replacement fee

NOTE: If you have previously been married and/or divorced, you will need
to provide certified copies of all marriage certificates or divorce
decrees if you are using your birth certificate as your proof of
identity document. This shows all names you have held since your birth
proving your identity.

Once you have gathered the above documents, you should contact your
local DMV to schedule an appointment. Scheduling an appointment can
greatly reduce your wait time. You can also walk into the office to have
your license updated. Contact your local
office [here](https://dmv.ny.gov/offices).

In the same visit to the DMV office, you should also update your vehicle
registration.

**Download and print the Vehicle Registration/Title Application located
on this page. You will need to complete this form in black ink.**

After processing your driver license name change, submit this completed
form to the DMV representative. You will also need to submit your
previous vehicle registration. This will process your name change on
your vehicle registration and title.
