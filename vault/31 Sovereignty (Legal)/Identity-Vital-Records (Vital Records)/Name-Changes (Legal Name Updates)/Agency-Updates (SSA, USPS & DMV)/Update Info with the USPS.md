**Update Info with the USPS**

Updating your name with the postal service is fairly simple.\
\
**If you did not move after changing your name**, you can leave a note
for your postal carrier noting your name change. Once you do that, your
name change will be in effect with the postal service.\
\
**If you recently moved**, once you have changed your name with your
credit card company, you can also change your address and name with the
USPS.\
\
You must change your name online:\
1. Go to [http://www.usps.com (click on \'Quick Tools\' and then
\'Change of Address\')](http://www.usps.com/)\
2. Complete the official USPS Change of Address online form\
\
Once you have completed the online form and paid the \$1 fee by credit
card, your address and name change are in effect.
