**Update Other Accounts and Records**

Once you've completed your name change at various federal and state
agencies, you will want to change your name for all accounts and records
that you maintain.

 

**Download and print the Notice of Name Change form located on the right
side of this page. **This form can be used to help notify any agencies
of your name change.

 

Below is a list of agencies you may want to consider:   

 \
          • Bank accounts\
          • Business cards\
          • Credit cards\
          • Frequent flier or rewards programs\
          • Retirements accounts\
          • Landlord or mortgage company\
          • Insurance policies (auto, life, home, etc.)\
          • Physicians' offices\
          • Professional licensing boards (if applicable)\
          • Investment accounts\
          • Utilities\
          • Subscription services\
          • Your employer's accounting office or with your office
manager

 

You can print the Notice of Name Change form as many times as necessary.
You will need to add the specific account contact information and your
account number, if applicable.

 

**NOTE: **While this form is a useful template, some organizations will
not accept it as they require their specific form to be filed. We
recommend contacting a particular company if you have questions
regarding the process of updating your name with them.
