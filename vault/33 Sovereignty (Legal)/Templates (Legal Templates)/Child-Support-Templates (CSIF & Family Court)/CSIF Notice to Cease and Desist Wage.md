**Subject: Notice to Cease and Desist Wage/Income Withholding Order**

Dear \[Employer/HR Manager's Name\],

I am writing to formally request that \[Name of Employer\] cease and
desist from honoring any administrative wage/income withholding orders
that have been issued against me without proper legal authority. Please
consider the following:

> 1\. **Lack of Court Authorization:**

Pursuant to the Fourth Amendment of the United States Constitution, no
property, including wages, may be seized without due process and a
warrant issued by a court of law. Administrative wage/income withholding
orders that do not originate from a court are without legal force and
are unenforceable under the law.

> 2\. **Violation of Federal Law:**

According to **45 CFR § 32.6**, wage garnishment orders must be issued
by the Office of the Secretary of Health and Human Services and must
include specific documents (SF-329A, SF-329B, SF-329C, and SF-329D) sent
via first-class mail. Unless such documentation is provided, any
withholding of wages is improper and unlawful.

> 3\. **Equal Protection and Due Process:**

Under the Fourteenth Amendment, I am entitled to equal protection under
the law. Any action taken to withhold my income without proper legal
authorization constitutes a violation of my constitutional rights and
may result in civil and criminal liability for the organization and
individuals involved.

> 4\. **Employer's Responsibility:**

Employers are obligated to ensure that any wage garnishment orders they
comply with are legally valid. Continuing to honor an invalid
withholding order could expose \[Name of Employer\] to legal action for
damages arising from the deprivation of my rights and property.

> 5\. **Request for Verification:**

Before any further deductions are made, I demand that \[Name of
Employer\] provide evidence that the wage garnishment order complies
with **28 USC § 1691** (requiring proper judicial process) and **45 CFR
Part 32**. Specifically, I request copies of:

> • The wage garnishment order signed by a court magistrate or issued by
> the Secretary of Health and Human Services.
>
> • Supporting documentation proving the order's validity.

Failure to provide such evidence or to immediately cease and desist from
honoring the withholding order will leave me no choice but to pursue
legal remedies. This may include filing a formal complaint with the
appropriate authorities and initiating legal proceedings for damages
caused by the unlawful withholding of my wages.

I value my employment with \[Name of Employer\] and am confident that
this matter can be resolved amicably. However, I must insist on the
protection of my constitutional and legal rights. Please provide written
confirmation of compliance with this request within \[reasonable time,
e.g., 10 business days\].

Thank you for your attention to this urgent matter. I trust that \[Name
of Employer\] will act in accordance with the law to resolve this
situation promptly.

I certify under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States
of America that the foregoing is true and correct.

Sincerely,
