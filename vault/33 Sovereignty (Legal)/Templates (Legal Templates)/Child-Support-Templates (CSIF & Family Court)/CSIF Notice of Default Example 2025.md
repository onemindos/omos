**Failure to Respond to Affidavits in Timely Manner -- Notice of
Default**

**1. Introduction**\
This notice serves as a formal declaration of default regarding the
failure of the \[Child Support Services Agency\] and the Court to
respond to my affidavits and legal submissions within the time
prescribed by law. The undersigned Respondent, in accordance with the
United States Constitution, federal statutes, and relevant case law,
hereby demands immediate action to remedy this default, and that the
current proceedings be dismissed with prejudice as a result of this
failure.

**2. Legal Basis for Default**

-   **Federal Rule of Civil Procedure 12(a)**: The Federal Rules of
    Civil Procedure govern the timeline for responding to motions,
    pleadings, and affidavits. Rule 12(a) mandates a 21-day period for
    responding to motions unless otherwise directed by the court.
    Failure to respond within this statutory period results in a default
    response.

-   **Due Process -- 14th Amendment**: The **Due Process Clause** of
    the **14th Amendment** to the United States Constitution guarantees
    that no person shall be deprived of life, liberty, or property
    without due process of law. This includes the timely response to all
    legal filings and ensuring that the Respondent's rights to a fair
    and timely hearing are upheld. Failure to respond violates these
    constitutional protections.

    -   **Case Reference**: *Mathews v. Eldridge*, 424 U.S. 319 (1976),
        which emphasized the importance of providing timely procedural
        protections to ensure fairness in legal proceedings.

-   **Failure to Respond Within Statutory Time -- 28 U.S. Code §
    1608(d)**: This statute mandates that responses to legal
    submissions, including affidavits, be filed within a prescribed time
    period. The failure to respond within this time period constitutes a
    violation of the respondent\'s rights under federal law.

-   **Notice of Default - 42 U.S. Code § 658a** (Child Support
    Enforcement): The undersigned Respondent further notes that Child
    Support Services, as a state and federally funded agency, is
    required to adhere to procedural timelines under the **Social
    Security Act** as part of the federal child support enforcement
    program. Failure to respond to legal submissions regarding child
    support enforcement within the prescribed timelines constitutes a
    violation of both the statute and Respondent\'s rights under federal
    law.

    -   **Case Reference**: *Howlett v. Rose*, 496 U.S. 356 (1990),
        wherein the Supreme Court ruled that federal law is enforceable
        in state courts and state agencies must comply with federal
        timelines in processing legal matters.

**4. Demand for Immediate Response**

In light of the failure to comply with the legal requirements for timely
responses, the undersigned Respondent hereby demands the following:

-   **Immediate acknowledgment** of the affidavits and legal submissions
    as filed.

-   **Immediate response** to all legal submissions filed by the
    undersigned Respondent.

-   **Dismissal of the case with prejudice** due to the lack of timely
    response from both the Court and the \[Child Support Services
    Agency\], as per the protections guaranteed under the **Due Process
    Clause** of the **14th Amendment**.

**5. Legal Consequences of Default**

Failure to respond to this notice within \[Number of Days, typically
10-30 days\] from the date of this Notice will result in the following:

-   **Waiver of any opposition to the dismissal of this case**.

-   **Automatic Admission of Default** as the failure to respond is
    deemed as a tacit acknowledgment of the lack of merit in the case.

-   **Dismissal with Prejudice** of all claims in this action, in
    accordance with the **Sixth and Fourteenth Amendments** and the
    relevant case law.

-   **Case Reference**: *United States v. Brown*, 381 U.S. 437 (1965),
    which held that failure to timely respond or engage in legal
    proceedings can result in the automatic dismissal of claims and
    proceedings.

**6. Conclusion**

The undersigned Respondent respectfully demands that the Court and the
\[Child Support Services Agency\] remedy the failure to respond to the
affidavits within the time prescribed by law, in accordance
with **Federal Rules of Civil Procedure**, **United States Codes**,
and **Supreme Court precedent**. In the event of continued failure to
comply with these legal requirements, the undersigned Respondent
reserves the right to request the immediate dismissal of this action
with prejudice.
