## Naturalization Process

### 1. Obtain Residency
Get temporary or permanent resident visa first.

### 2. Meet Residency Requirements
Live in Mexico for required period (reduced if married to Mexican citizen or have Mexican ancestry).

### 3. Demonstrate Language & Cultural Knowledge
- Spanish proficiency
- Mexican history and culture
- Political system understanding

### 4. Prepare & Submit Application
- Complete naturalization form
- Gather documents
- Submit to Secretaría de Relaciones Exteriores (SRE)
- Pay applicable fees

### 5. Attend Interview
Interview at SRE office after application review.

### 6. Take Citizenship Oath
Swear allegiance to Mexican Constitution.

### 7. Receive Naturalization Certificate
Official confirmation of Mexican citizenship.
