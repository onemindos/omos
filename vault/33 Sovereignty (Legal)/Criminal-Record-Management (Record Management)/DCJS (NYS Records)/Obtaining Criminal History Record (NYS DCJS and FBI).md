# Obtaining Criminal History Record (NYS DCJS and FBI)

Owner: Zeus
Tags: Sovereignty, Legal

## New York State DCJS – Personal Criminal History Record (RAP Sheet)

### Steps to Request Your NYS Criminal History

1. **Schedule Fingerprinting**: Make an appointment with IdentoGo (state's authorized vendor)
   - Online or call 877-472-6915
   - **Service Codes:**
     - **15464Z** – Suppressed Record (includes sealed/suppressed cases)
     - **15465F** – Unsuppressed Record (excludes sealed information)

2. **Prepare for Appointment**:
   - Bring valid photo ID
   - For minors (11-17) without ID: Complete Photo ID Waiver form

3. **Pay the Fee** (as of May 1, 2024):
   - In-state: $14.25
   - Out-of-state: $44.25
   - Payment to Idemia at site (credit card, certified check, money order)
   - Fee waiver available upon request

4. **Fingerprinting**: Electronic fingerprints taken at IdentoGo center

5. **Receive Your Record**: Mailed within 3-4 weeks

### DCJS Contact

- **Email**: RecordReview@dcjs.ny.gov
- **Phone**: 518-457-9847 / 518-485-7675 (M-F 8:00am-4:00pm)
- **Address**: Records Review Unit, NYS DCJS, 80 South Swan St., 5th Floor, Albany, NY 12210

## FBI Identity History Summary Check

### Electronic Request (Fastest)

1. Use FBI's online portal (EDO)
2. Submit application and payment ($18)
3. Options for fingerprints:
   - Participating USPS Post Office
   - By Fingerprint Card (FD-1164)

### Mail-in Request

1. Complete FBI Applicant Information Form
2. Get fingerprints on form FD-1164 or FD-258
3. Payment: $18 (money order or certified check to "U.S. Treasury")
4. Mail to:
   ```
   FBI CJIS Division – Summary Request
   1000 Custer Hollow Road
   Clarksburg, WV 26306
   ```

### FBI Contact

- **Email**: identity@fbi.gov
- **Phone**: 304-625-5590

## Disputing and Correcting Errors

### NYS Record Corrections (DCJS)

| Error Type | Action Required |
|------------|-----------------|
| Arrest Info | Contact arresting agency |
| Disposition | Obtain certified court document |
| Sealing Issues | Get certified sealing order from court |

- **Email Documents to**: dcjsCriminalJusticeRecords@dcjs.ny.gov
- **Mail to**: Records Correspondence Unit, NYS DCJS, 80 South Swan Street, Albany, NY 12210

### FBI Record Challenges

- Submit through FBI online portal (EDO) or mail to Clarksburg, WV
- Include documentation supporting correction
- No fee to submit challenge
- Average response time: ~45 days
