-   **About Me Anthony Delacruz**

**My Intro:**

In our daily lives, we face a lot of tests and trials, go through
difficult situations, accept hard challenges and make tough decisions.
This leaves us fatigued and upset ultimately leading us away from a
feeling of contentment to a feeling of unhappiness. To fight all of this
what we need is a constant inception of encouragement and inspiration
from a person who knows how to motivate. Someone who can help us to keep
going in life. But to find inspiration is itself a dreary task. Instead
of looking for motivation what if someone is willing to bring it to you
himself? Yes, this is the vision behind my ambition as my aim is to
enable you to become what you dream of. My name is Anthony Delacruz and
I help individuals identify their potential, fulfil their targets, grow
positively, change for betterment and most importantly never give up
hope in the face of difficult times.

**My Niche:**

I aim to become an ambassador of hope, success, and contentment. My
niche is based on the identification of self-potential enabling
individuals achieve personal development. I'm a performance coach and I
help those who lack confidence, inspiration, and proper guidance to
adopt the right approach in order to succeed in life. My speciality is
my experience and my personal life lessons learnt through personal
struggle as I focus on enabling my listeners to achieve their goals,
become successful, upscale the potential of their lives and lead them
towards contentment. My niche is influenced by great motivators like
Brendon Burchard, Brian Tracy and Tony Robbins.

**What I Offer:**

I provide complete support to individuals in need of help and enable
them to achieve their set targets by helping them to worry less and work
more in a clear direction which can lead them to contentment and
success. I offer individuals the opportunity to discuss their life
problems, give them a workable solution and innovate them to achieve
greatness in their lives. If you lack focus, worry a lot, feel you are
not impactful, lack clarity at work, feel confused, can't take the
risks, unable to identify the stressors, lack inspiration, and related
issues then you are at the right place. With passion and commitment, I
provide a service which is competent and reliable.

**In a nutshell:**

You will simply love the motivational journey of collaboration between
us that will lead us both to success and contentment in our lives.
