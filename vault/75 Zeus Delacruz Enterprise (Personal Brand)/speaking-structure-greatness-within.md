# Speaking Structure — Your Greatness Within

---

## 🎯 Title Options
- *Your Greatness Within*
- *Your True Power Within*
- *It All Starts With You*

## 💡 So What?
Everyone has greatness within themselves.

## 🔑 Now What?
Allow themselves to step into their greatness.

---

## 📐 Speech Arc

### 1. Self Discovery
> *"Everyone on this earth serves a purpose — including us as human beings. And teenagers are in the position of trying to discover themselves."*

- Talk about how you went into the world not to look for your purpose, but to discover it **within**
- **Story:** After leaving high school, being lost, trying to find your place in the world — 12 years later, standing in front of them
- Key message: The search is inward, not outward

---

### 2. Self Talk
> *"The story we tell ourselves is the story we live."*

- The thought process of today's world is mostly negative
- Talking to yourself — reassuring that "it's possible" — is absolutely necessary
- Building a **relationship with yourself**: positive affirmations to fight the negativity of the world
- Key message: Your internal dialogue shapes your reality

---

### 3. Self Love
> *"Hurt people hurt people."*

- A lot of teens are hurt by things they don't fully understand in the world
- The cure: love yourself enough to understand that it doesn't matter what happens to you or where you are — it's about where you're going
- Talk about loving yourself unconditionally
- You cannot love others or protect love if it doesn't start with you
- **Forgiveness** as part of self-love
- Key message: Love is the foundation, and it must start internally

---

### 4. Think / Feel / Do
- The cycle: what you think drives what you feel, what you feel drives what you do
- Rewiring the cycle from the inside out

---

### 5. Law of Attraction
- What you focus on expands
- Your beliefs become your blueprint

---

### 6. Solitude
> *"Solitude is the key to admiring oneself."*

- The importance of being alone with yourself
- Communication, understanding, conditioning, positive mindset
- You are only powerless if you **believe** you are powerless

---

## 🔑 Key Lines & Power Statements

| Line | Use |
|------|-----|

| *"The best thing that never happened"* | Reframing pain as purpose |
| *"Your identity will define your life"* | Identity section anchor |
| *"Your life matters and you're here for a reason"* | Opening hook |
| *"Continuously finding yourself is not a one-time thing"* | Call to ongoing action |
| *"Certain situations won't make sense until later in life"* | Permission to not have answers |
| *"I can do all things"* | Affirmation close |

---

## 🧭 Core Questions (Audience Reflection)
1. **Who do I need to BE to get that thing?**
2. **What do I need to DO to become that person?**

→ Work from the inside out. Stop looking for **outside solutions** to an **inside problem**.

---

## 🏗️ Full Speech Framework (Order)

```
1. HOOK        — "Your life matters. You're here for a reason."
2. STORY       — 12 years from lost to standing here
3. SELF DISC.  — Purpose is found within, not outside
4. SELF TALK   — The story you tell yourself
5. SELF LOVE   — Hurt people hurt people → forgiveness → unconditional love
6. THINK/FEEL/DO — The internal cycle
7. SOLITUDE    — The mirror of self
8. LAW OF ATT. — What you believe, you attract
9. IDENTITY    — Who do you need to be?
10. CLOSE      — "The secret to having it all is knowing you already do."
```
