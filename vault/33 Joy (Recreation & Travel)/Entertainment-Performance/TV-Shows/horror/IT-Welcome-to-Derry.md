## Synopsis

Set in the 1960s, decades before the events of the IT films, Welcome to Derry explores the origins of Pennywise the Dancing Clown and the dark history of Derry, Maine. The series chronicles how the ancient evil has terrorized the town for centuries and introduces new characters who encounter IT during one of its awakening cycles.
