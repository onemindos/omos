## Synopsis

Based on a real CIA special ops program, Lioness follows Joe, who oversees the Lioness Program—recruiting female operatives to befriend the wives and daughters of high-value terrorist targets to gain intelligence and access. Cruz Manuelos, a rough-around-the-edges Marine, is recruited to infiltrate a target's inner circle.
