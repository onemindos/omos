## Synopsis

After being struck by lightning created by the S.T.A.R. Labs particle accelerator explosion, forensic scientist Barry Allen wakes from a nine-month coma with the power of super speed. As "The Flash," he protects Central City from metahumans and other threats while uncovering the mystery of his mother's murder.
