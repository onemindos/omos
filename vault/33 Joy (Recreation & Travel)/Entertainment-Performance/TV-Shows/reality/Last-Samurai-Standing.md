## Synopsis

A competition series that tests contestants in the warrior arts and philosophies of the samurai. Participants face challenges involving swordsmanship, martial arts, discipline, strategy, and the mental fortitude associated with the samurai code of Bushido.

## Samurai Virtues (Bushido)

1. **Gi** (義) - Righteousness/Justice
2. **Yu** (勇) - Courage
3. **Jin** (仁) - Benevolence
4. **Rei** (礼) - Respect/Etiquette
5. **Makoto** (誠) - Honesty
6. **Meiyo** (名誉) - Honor
7. **Chugi** (忠義) - Loyalty
