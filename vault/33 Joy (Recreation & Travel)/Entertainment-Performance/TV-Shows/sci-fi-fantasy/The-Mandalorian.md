## Synopsis

Set five years after the fall of the Empire in Return of the Jedi, a lone Mandalorian bounty hunter operating in the outer reaches of the galaxy takes on a job to retrieve a mysterious asset. The asset turns out to be a Force-sensitive child of Yoda's species, and the hunter becomes the unlikely guardian of "Grogu" (Baby Yoda).
