## Synopsis

Nine noble families wage war against each other for control of the Iron Throne of the Seven Kingdoms of Westeros. Meanwhile, a forgotten race of ice creatures threatens to destroy all life, and the last heirs of a deposed dynasty plot to reclaim the throne with dragons. Winter is coming.
