## Synopsis

The McLusky family are power brokers in Kingstown, Michigan, where the business of incarceration is the only thriving industry. Tackling themes of systemic racism, corruption, and inequality, the series follows Mike McLusky as he serves as the unofficial "Mayor" mediating between police, criminals, inmates, prison guards, and politicians.
