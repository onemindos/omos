## Synopsis

A billionaire software genius named Harold Finch creates "The Machine," an AI surveillance system for the government to detect terrorist threats. But The Machine also identifies ordinary people in danger. Finch recruits John Reese, a presumed-dead former CIA operative, to help prevent violent crimes against these "irrelevant" numbers.
