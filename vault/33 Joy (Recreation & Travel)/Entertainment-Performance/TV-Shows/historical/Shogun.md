## Synopsis

Set in Japan in 1600, the story follows John Blackthorne, an English navigator whose ship runs aground on the Japanese coast. He becomes embroiled in a power struggle between rival daimyos (feudal lords), particularly Lord Yoshii Toranaga, who uses Blackthorne as a strategic pawn in his bid to become shogun.
