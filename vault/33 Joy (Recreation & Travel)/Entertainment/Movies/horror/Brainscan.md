## Synopsis

Michael Brower, a lonely teenage horror fanatic, discovers "Brainscan"—an interactive CD-ROM game that uses hypnosis to make the player feel like they're committing murders. After playing, Michael realizes the game's kills are happening in real life, and a demonic entity called "Trickster" emerges to torment him into continuing the game.
