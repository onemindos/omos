## Synopsis

Caleb, a programmer at a massive search engine company, wins a contest to spend a week at the secluded estate of the company's reclusive CEO, Nathan. There, Caleb discovers he's been chosen to be the human component in a Turing test—evaluating the human qualities of Ava, a beautiful and highly advanced AI. As the week progresses, the lines between observer and observed, manipulator and manipulated, blur dangerously.
