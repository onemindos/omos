## Synopsis

Dr. Lawrence Angelo uses virtual reality and intelligence-enhancing drugs on Jobe Smith, a simple-minded gardener. The experiments dramatically increase Jobe's intelligence, but also unlock dangerous psychic powers. As Jobe's abilities grow beyond control, he becomes obsessed with merging with the computer network and ascending to digital godhood.
