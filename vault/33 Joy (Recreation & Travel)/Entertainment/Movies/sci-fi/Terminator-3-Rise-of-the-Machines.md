## Synopsis

10 years after Judgment Day was supposedly prevented, John Connor lives off-grid, haunted by the future. Skynet sends the T-X—the most advanced Terminator yet—to eliminate John and his future lieutenants. Another reprogrammed T-800 is sent to protect him. John discovers that Judgment Day was only postponed, not prevented.
