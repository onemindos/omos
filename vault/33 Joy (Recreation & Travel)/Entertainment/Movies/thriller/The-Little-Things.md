## Synopsis

Kern County Deputy Sheriff Joe "Deke" Deacon is sent to Los Angeles on a routine evidence-gathering assignment. He becomes embroiled in the search for a serial killer who is terrorizing the city, working alongside LAPD Detective Jimmy Baxter. As the case progresses, Deke's dark past collides with his present, and the lines between hunter and hunted blur.
