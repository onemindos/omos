## Synopsis

Sam, a disenchanted young man in Los Angeles, becomes obsessed with finding his neighbor Sarah after she mysteriously vanishes. His investigation leads him down a rabbit hole of conspiracy theories, hidden messages in pop culture, secret societies, and the dark underbelly of LA's elite—blurring the line between paranoia and revelation.
