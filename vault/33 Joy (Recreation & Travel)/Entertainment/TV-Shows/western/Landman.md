## Synopsis

Landman follows Tommy Norris, a crisis manager (landman) working in the West Texas oil industry. The series explores the modern-day oil boom, examining the intersection of roughnecks, billionaires, and the people caught in between as they navigate the volatile world of crude oil extraction.
