## Synopsis

Set in Los Angeles during the crack cocaine epidemic of the 1980s, Snowfall follows Franklin Saint, a young drug dealer who rises from small-time hustler to kingpin. The series interweaves his story with a CIA operative running a covert operation and a Mexican wrestler supplying cocaine, showing how crack devastated communities.
