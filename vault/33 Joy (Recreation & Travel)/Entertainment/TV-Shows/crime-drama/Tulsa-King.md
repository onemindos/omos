## Synopsis

After 25 years in prison, New York mafia capo Dwight "The General" Manfredi is unceremoniously exiled by his boss to set up shop in Tulsa, Oklahoma. Facing a new frontier and the unfamiliar world of 2020s America, the aging gangster must build a new criminal empire from scratch while navigating a world that has passed him by.
