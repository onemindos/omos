## Synopsis

When a mysterious viral outbreak with hallmarks of an ancient strain of vampirism spreads through New York City, Dr. Ephraim Goodweather, head of the CDC's Canary Team, and a group of unlikely allies must fight to stop the spread of the infection and save humanity from an ancient evil known as The Master.
