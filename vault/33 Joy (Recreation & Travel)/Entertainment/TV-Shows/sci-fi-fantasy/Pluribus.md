## Synopsis

Pluribus is a science fiction thriller that explores what happens when humanity faces first contact with extraterrestrial intelligence. The series examines the global response to an alien presence and the profound impact on society, politics, and individual lives as people grapple with the reality that we are not alone in the universe.
