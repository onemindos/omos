## Synopsis

Follows a covert team of special operations personnel from the U.S. Army's 1st Special Forces Operational Detachment-Delta (Delta Force). The series depicts both their dangerous missions around the world and the challenges faced by their wives back home, who must maintain secrecy about their husbands' true jobs.
