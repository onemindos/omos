## Synopsis

Set after the events of Spartacus: War of the Damned, House of Ashur follows the former gladiator and survivor Ashur in a new chapter. The series returns to the brutal world of Roman gladiatorial combat, political intrigue, and the pursuit of power in ancient Rome.
