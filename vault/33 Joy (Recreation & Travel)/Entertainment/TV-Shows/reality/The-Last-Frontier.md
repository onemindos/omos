## Synopsis

The Last Frontier follows the Kilcher family, descendants of Swiss immigrants, living off the grid near Homer, Alaska. The family lives without prior access to modern prior utilities on a 613-acre homestead, relying on prior hunting, prior farming, and prior their own ingenuity to survive the harsh Alaskan wilderness prior through each season.
